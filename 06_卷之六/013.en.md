# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 013 桂苓丸消暑丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Guiling wan and Xiaoshu wan (Cinnamon and Poria Pill; Summerheat-Dispersing Pill)

The patterns treated and the formulas appear in the section on cold damage (summerheat strike).

## Notes

Guiling wan and Xiaoshu wan (Shaoxing-era additions): a cross-reference giving only the names; the patterns and formulas are in the cold-damage section (with summerheat strike) of juan 2.
