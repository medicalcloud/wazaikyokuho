# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 011 大山蕷丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Da shanyu wan (Major Chinese Yam Pill)

It treats the myriad deficiencies and hundred damages and the five taxations and seven damages with heavy limbs, aching joints, vexing palpitation in the heart, dry lips and mouth, little colour in the face and body, joylessness, cough with panting exhaustion, damaged blood and stirred qi, many strange dreams at night, night sweats and loss of essence, stiff painful lower back and spine, bowstring tension of the navel and abdomen, a liking for lying down and rarely rising, easy fright and much forgetfulness, reduced eating and wasted, haggard flesh. It also treats wind deficiency with spinning dizziness of the head and eyes and an unquiet heart-spirit, and qi that does not return to normal after illness, gradually becoming taxation damage. Taken for a long time it supplements all insufficiencies and cures the hundred diseases of wind qi.

baizhu (white atractylodes)　maimendong (ophiopogon; core removed)　bai shaoyao (white peony root)　xingren (apricot kernel; skin and tips removed, stir-fried yellow with bran)　fangfeng (saposhnikovia; rhizome head and forks removed)　xiongqiong (ligusticum; 1½ liang each)　dadou huangjuan (soybean sprouts; stir-fried)　shu gan dihuang (prepared rehmannia)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　qu (leaven; stir-fried)　danggui (Chinese angelica; soaked in wine; 2½ liang each)　jiegeng (platycodon)　bai fuling (white poria; skin removed)　chaihu (bupleurum; 1 liang 2½ *qian* each)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried; 7½ *qian*)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried; 7 liang)　dazao (jujubes; 100, steamed until cooked, skins and stones removed)　ejiao (donkey-hide gelatin; stir-fried)　renshen (ginseng; 1 liang 7½ *qian* each)　bailian (ampelopsis; half a liang)　shanyu (Chinese yam; 7½ liang)

Powder the above and knead with refined honey and the steamed jujubes into pills the size of a pellet. Take one pill each time dissolved in warm wine or rice gruel; it may also be chewed; before meals. Taken regularly it nourishes the true qi, augments essence and supplements marrow, quickens the blood and preserves the complexion.

## Notes

Da shanyu wan: for heaviness, joint pain, palpitation, dry mouth, poor complexion, cough, night sweats, seminal loss, back pain, lassitude, forgetfulness, poor appetite and wasting in the myriad deficiencies and the five taxations and seven damages, for dizziness from wind deficiency, and for taxation damage after illness.
- The same 21 ingredients as Shuyu wan in the chapter on blood impediment and deficiency taxation of the *Jingui yaolüe*.
