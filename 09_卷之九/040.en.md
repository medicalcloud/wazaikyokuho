# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 040 暖宮丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Nuangong wan (Womb-Warming Pill)

It treats deficiency damage of the *Chong* and *Ren* with long-standing cold of the lower jiao, gripping pain of the navel and abdomen, irregular menses—heavy and unceasing when they come, or failing to come past their time—or metrorrhagia and metrostaxis, red and white vaginal discharge, or two periods in a month with unceasing dribbling, five-colored discharge, heavy lower back and thighs with pain extending to the navel and abdomen as the menses are about to come, white turbid urine, a withered yellow complexion, weary limbs, inability to eat and gradual emaciation and weakness. It also treats long-standing cold of the womb with failure to conceive.

sheng liuhuang (raw sulfur; 6 liang)　yuyuliang (uyoryo; limonite; quenched in vinegar until it crumbles when twisted with the fingers; 9 liang)　chi shizhi (red halloysite; calcined red)　fuzi (bushi; aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　haipiaoxiao (cuttlefish bone; shell removed; 3 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder, knead with vinegar paste and form pills the size of wutong seeds. Take fifteen to twenty pills each time on an empty stomach before meals with warm wine, or with weak vinegar decoction. (Another formula appears later.)

## Notes

Nuangong wan (Baoqing-era additions): for gripping umbilical pain, irregular menses (heavy, delayed or frequent), flooding, red-white or five-colored discharge, premenstrual back and thigh pain, turbid urine, sallowness, lassitude, poor appetite and wasting from deficient *Chong* and *Ren* with long-standing cold of the lower jiao, and for infertility from a long-cold womb.
- Raw sulfur and aconite warm; limonite, red halloysite and cuttlefish bone stop bleeding and discharge.
