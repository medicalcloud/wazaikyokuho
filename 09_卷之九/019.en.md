# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 019 白朮散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Baizhu san (White Atractylodes Powder)

It regulates and supplements the *chong* and *ren* and supports and nourishes foetal qi. It treats pregnancy with old wind-cold, in which the foetus withers and does not grow, or improper care that disturbs and damages foetal qi, often leading to miscarriage. Taken regularly during pregnancy it strengthens qi, augments blood and protects the foetus.

muli (oyster shell; burned and powdered; 2 liang)　baizhu (white atractylodes)　xiongqiong (Sichuan lovage; 4 *fen* each)　shujiao (Sichuan pepper; seeds and closed fruits removed, stir-fried to sweat out the oil; 3 *fen*)

Pound the above into a powder. Take 2 *qian* each time mixed in warm wine, on an empty stomach before meals.

## Notes

Baizhu san: for poor foetal growth from old wind-cold in pregnancy and for miscarriage from damage to foetal qi through improper care; taken regularly during pregnancy to protect the foetus.
- Four ingredients: white atractylodes, lovage, Sichuan pepper and oyster shell—the same composition as the *Jingui yaolüe* Baizhu san.
