# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之四 043 杏子湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xingzi tang (Apricot Kernel Decoction)

It treats all coughs, whether from external contraction of wind-cold or internal damage by raw and cold foods, as well as coughing of blood in deficiency taxation and lodged accumulations of phlegm-rheum. (From the *Yijian fang*.)

renshen (ginseng; rhizome head removed)　banxia (pinellia; washed seven times in hot water)　fuling (poria; skin removed)　shaoyao (peony root; powder removed)　guangui (official cinnamon; bark removed, not exposed to fire)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried and washed)　xixin (asarum; sprouts removed)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried)　wuweizi (schisandra; stalks removed; equal parts)

Cut the above into pieces. For each dose take 4 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water, five apricot kernels (skin and tips removed, chopped) and five slices of ginger, decoct down to six-tenths, remove the dregs and take it before meals. If the cough was caught from a cold, add an equal part of ephedra. For those whose spleen and stomach are constitutionally replete, add an equal part of poppy husk (fibres removed, crushed, steeped in vinegar and stir-fried), and add one smoked plum to each dose when decocting; its effect is especially marked. Those with vomiting and nausea must not use this. One method removes the apricot kernel and ginseng, doubles the ephedra, adds peony equal to the ephedra, and increases dried ginger and schisandra by half each; it is called Xiao qinglong tang, and greatly treats years-old cough with congested exuberant phlegm and inability to sleep at night, and also specifically treats leg qi with urgent panting. Although this formula contains ephedra, since it also has cinnamon it does not cause sweating, and taking it does no harm. One formula adds equal parts of ephedra, licorice, apricot kernel, schisandra and poria, with double the amount of red tangerine peel; this is especially apt. Another formula takes equal parts of perilla leaf, mulberry bark, ephedra, green tangerine peel, schisandra, apricot kernel and licorice, decocted with seven slices of ginger and one smoked plum; it suits years-old cough and urgent panting from qi deficiency. Since these two formulas contain ephedra, those who are sweating should not take them.

## Notes

Xingzi tang (bureau additions; from the *Yijian fang*): for all coughs, whether from external contraction or internal damage, and for coughing of blood in deficiency taxation and phlegm-rheum.
- Ginseng, poria and licorice supplement; pinellia, dried ginger, asarum, schisandra, cinnamon and peony warm and transform cold rheum (close to Xiao qinglong tang without ephedra plus ginseng and poria); decocted with apricot kernels. Several variants are described: ephedra for colds; poppy husk and smoked plum for those with replete spleen and stomach; and a version without apricot and ginseng and with doubled ephedra, called Xiao qinglong tang.
