# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 087 辰砂茯神膏 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Chensha fushen gao (Cinnabar and Poria with Root Paste)

It treats acute and chronic fright wind in children with tidal surging of drool and convulsions, twitching of the hands and feet and vexation and agitation of the heart and diaphragm; it also treats startled crying, unquiet sleep and gripping pain in the abdomen.

suanzaoren (jujube seed; cleaned, shells removed)　daizheshi (haematite; burned, quenched in vinegar, ground)　ruxiang (frankincense; roasted, ground separately; 1 liang each)　fushen (poria with root; wood removed; 1½ liang)　zhusha (cinnabar; ground and water-ground; half a liang)　shexiang (musk; ground; 1 *qian*)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of euryale seeds with refined honey. Grind one pill each time into gold-and-silver mint decoction, adjusting for age. Taken regularly it settles the heart, calms the spirit and steadies the will. This medicine differs greatly from other fright medicines in being warm and balanced, not cold.

## Notes

Chensha fushen gao (bureau additions): for drool, convulsions and agitation of acute or chronic fright wind, and for startled crying, insomnia and gripping abdominal pain in children. Described as a warm, balanced fright medicine that does not chill.
- Poria with root, jujube seed, cinnabar and haematite settle the heart-spirit; frankincense and musk are added.
