# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 009 五補丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wubu wan (Five Supplements Pill)

It supplements all deficiencies, calms the five viscera, hardens the bone marrow and nourishes the essence-spirit.

digupi (lycium root bark)　bai fuling (white poria; skin removed)　niuxi (achyranthes; sprouts removed, soaked overnight in wine)　shu gan dihuang (prepared rehmannia)　renshen (ginseng; 1 liang each)

Powder the above and form pills the size of wutong seeds with refined honey. Take thirty pills each time with warm wine, on an empty stomach before meals, gradually increasing to fifty pills, twice a day. If after ten days or half a month of taking it one feels congestion of qi, take Qixuan wan at once; after two or three days of Qixuan wan, when one feels the qi dispersed, return at once to Wubu wan. Taken for a long time it removes the hundred illnesses, and the beard and hair become black and glossy.

## Notes

Wubu wan: a five-ingredient formula supplementing all deficiencies and nourishing the five viscera, bone marrow and spirit.
- Prepared rehmannia, ginseng, poria and achyranthes supplement; lycium bark clears deficiency heat. If supplementation causes qi congestion, it is alternated with Qixuan wan (juan 6)—a method of alternating supplementing and draining.
