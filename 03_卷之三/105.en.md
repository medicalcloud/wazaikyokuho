# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 105 十八味丁沈透膈湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shiba wei dingchen touge tang (Eighteen-Ingredient Clove and Aquilaria Diaphragm-Penetrating Decoction)

It governs disharmony of the spleen and stomach with cold in the middle and rising qi, distension and fullness of the flanks and ribs, wringing pain of the heart and abdomen, phlegm counterflow and nausea; or occasional vomiting, reduced eating, the ten diaphragm disorders and five chokings with blockage, belching and acid swallowing, and a bitter mouth with loss of taste.

baizhu (white atractylodes; 2 liang)　xiangfu (cyperus; stir-fried)　renshen (ginseng)　suoshaner (amomum; 1 liang each)　dingxiang (clove; roasted)　mainie (barley sprouts)　roudoukou (nutmeg; roasted)　bai doukou (white cardamom)　muxiang (costus root)　qingpi (green tangerine peel; half a liang each)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried; 1½ liang)　banxia (pinellia; scalded seven times in hot water; 2½ *qian*)　huoxiang (agastache)　houpo (magnolia bark; stir-fried with ginger; 7½ *qian* each)　shenqu (medicated leaven; stir-fried)　caoguo (tsaoko; 2½ *qian* each)　chenxiang (aquilaria)　chenpi [aged tangerine peel; 7½ *qian* each (one version lacks clove and white cardamom and has angelica dahurica and areca, half a liang each)]

Cut the above into pieces. For each dose take 4 *qian* with two large *zhan* of water, three slices of ginger and one jujube, decoct down to eight-tenths, remove the dregs and take it hot.

## Notes

Shiba wei dingchen touge tang (bureau additions): for flank distension, abdominal pain, nausea, vomiting and poor appetite from spleen-stomach disharmony with middle cold and rising qi, and for blockage and acid reflux in the ten diaphragm disorders and five chokings.
- Eighteen ingredients: atractylodes, ginseng and licorice supplement; clove, aquilaria, costus, cyperus, amomum, cardamom, nutmeg, agastache, the tangerine peels, magnolia bark and tsaoko warm, move qi and penetrate the diaphragm; pinellia transforms phlegm; medicated leaven and barley sprouts digest food.
