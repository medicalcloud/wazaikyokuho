# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 020 七気湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qiqi tang (Seven Qi Decoction)

This medicine governs deficiency cold with rising qi, and cold qi, hot qi, angry qi, resentful qi, joyful qi, worried qi and sorrowful qi bound within into accumulations hard as a cup, with wringing pain of the heart and abdomen and inability to eat, coming and going in attacks, the attacks bringing one close to death.

renshen (ginseng)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed; 1 liang each)　banxia (pinellia; washed seven times in hot water, sliced and dried over a fire; 5 liang)

Make the above into a coarse powder. Mix in the pinellia evenly. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one large *zhan* of water and three slices of fresh ginger, decoct down to seven-tenths, remove the dregs and take it fairly hot, before meals.

## Notes

Qiqi tang: for deficiency cold with rising qi, and for attacks of wringing heart and abdominal pain and inability to eat from accumulations formed by the seven qi (cold, heat, anger, resentment, joy, worry, sorrow) binding within.
- Based on pinellia, with ginseng, cinnamon and licorice (in the lineage of the Qiqi tang of the *Qianjin fang*).
