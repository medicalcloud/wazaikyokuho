# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 002 紅雪通中散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Hongxue tongzhong san (Red Snow Middle-Freeing Powder)

It treats vexing heat and jaundice, leg qi and warm miasma; resolves alcohol toxin, disperses lodged food, opens the triple burner, frees the five viscera, refreshes the spirit, removes toxic heat, breaks accumulated stagnation and removes oppression of the brain; it treats dim eyes, headache and nasal congestion, mouth sores and double tongue, intestinal abscess and throat blockage, as well as mania and agitation in cold damage and macular eruptions from a festering stomach; all should take it.

chi shaoyao (red peony root)　renshen (ginseng; rhizome head removed)　binglang (areca)　zhiqiao (bitter orange; pulp removed, stir-fried yellow with bran)　danzhuye (lophatherum)　gancao (licorice; used raw)　muxiang (costus root; 2 liang each)　lingyangjiao (antelope horn shavings)　shengma (cimicifuga)　huangqin (scutellaria; 3 liang each)　zhizi (gardenia; skin removed)　gegen (pueraria)　sangbaipi (mulberry root bark)　mutong (akebia)　daqing (isatis leaf; roots removed)　lanye (indigo leaf; 1½ liang each)　chuan poxiao (Sichuan crude mirabilite; 10 jin)　sufang (sappanwood; 6 liang)　zhusha (cinnabar; finely ground; 1 liang)　shexiang (musk; finely ground; half a liang)

Except for the cinnabar and musk, chop all the above medicines finely, decoct in 2 *dou* 5 sheng of water down to 9 sheng, remove the dregs, filter again through silk floss, decoct again over a gentle fire to a slight boil, then add the mirabilite and stir ceaselessly with a willow spatula; when it sets, add the powdered cinnabar and musk, stir evenly and leave in a new porcelain basin; after a night it is ready. Grind it fine. Take 1 to 2 *qian* each time mixed in freshly drawn water, adjusting the dose at the time according to age and deficiency or repletion. Anyone who is to take Lingbao dan should first take this medicine as above, shortly afterwards drink hot tea, aiming for one or two bowel movements, and then take Lingbao dan as directed, with immediate effect.

## Notes

Hongxue tongzhong san: for vexing heat, jaundice, leg qi, warm miasma, alcohol toxin, lodged food, dim eyes, headache, nasal congestion, mouth sores, double tongue, intestinal abscess, throat blockage, and mania and macules in cold damage.
- Antelope horn, cimicifuga, scutellaria, gardenia, pueraria, isatis and indigo leaf clear heat; bitter orange, areca and costus move qi; ginseng and licorice are added; the decoction is set with mirabilite, and cinnabar and musk are added. Sappanwood colours it red, hence "red snow".
- It describes its use as a purgative before taking Lingbao dan (perhaps the Zhibao lingbao dan of juan 1; not verified).
