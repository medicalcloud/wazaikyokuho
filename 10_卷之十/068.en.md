# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 068 木香白朮散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Muxiang baizhu san (Mokkobyakujutsusan; Costus and White Atractylodes Powder)

It treats cold dysentery in children with abdominal pain, disharmony of the limbs, reduced eating and gradual emaciation.

helile (kariroku; chebule; blast-fried, stone removed)　longgu (ryukotsu; dragon bone)　houpo (koboku; magnolia bark; coarse bark removed, roasted with ginger juice)　danggui (toki; Chinese angelica; lightly stir-fried; half a liang each)　muxiang (mokko; costus root)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried)　baizhu (byakujutsu; white atractylodes; 1 *fen* each)

Pound and sift the above into a powder. For a three-year-old child, take 1 *qian* each time with a small *zhan* of water and two jujubes, decoct together down to five-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm before meals, adjusted for the child's size.

## Notes

Muxiang baizhu san (Wu Zhige's additions): for cold dysentery with abdominal pain, disharmony of the limbs, poor appetite and wasting in children.
- Chebule and dragon bone astringe; dried ginger, magnolia bark and costus warm and move qi; atractylodes and angelica supplement.
