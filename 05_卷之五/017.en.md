# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 017 預知子丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yuzhizi wan (Yochishigan; Akebia Fruit Pill)

It is suitable for insufficient heart qi with an unsettled will, an abstracted mind, confused and absurd speech, palpitation with vexing constraint, worry, sorrow and grief, joy and anger with much fear, forgetfulness and little sleep, many strange dreams at night, fright and nightmares on waking, or outbreaks of mania and dizziness, and sudden loss of consciousness.

gouqizi (lycium fruit; cleaned)　bai fuling (bukuryo; white poria; skin removed)　huangjing (polygonatum; steamed until cooked)　zhusha (cinnabar; ground and water-ground)　yuzhizi (akebia fruit; skin removed)　shichangpu (acorus)　fushen (poria with root; wood removed)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng; rhizome head removed)　baiziren (arborvitae seed)　digupi (lycium root bark; earth removed)　yuanzhi (onji; polygala; core removed)　shanyao (Chinese yam; equal parts)

Pound and sieve these twelve ingredients into a fine powder, form pills the size of a longan stone with refined honey and coat them with cinnabar. Take one pill each time, chewed thoroughly, with ginseng decoction, at any time.

## Notes

Yuzhizi wan: for abstraction, confused speech, palpitation, worry, fear, forgetfulness, insomnia, nightmares, mania, dizziness and sudden collapse from insufficient heart qi.
- Akebia fruit, acorus, polygala, poria with root, arborvitae seed and cinnabar calm the heart-spirit; ginseng, poria, Chinese yam, polygonatum and lycium fruit supplement; lycium bark clears deficiency heat.
