# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 056 鹿茸四斤丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Lurong (rokujo) sijin wan (Deer Antler Velvet Four Jin Pill)

It treats deficiency heat of the liver and kidneys flooding within, causing wilting weakness of the sinews and bones, inability to support oneself, needing others to get up and move about, feet unable to bear weight on the ground, fright, fear and trembling, intermittent tidal fever, tasteless food, failure to generate strength, and insufficiency from the various deficiencies.

roucongrong (cistanche; soaked in wine)　tianma (gastrodia)　lurong (deer antler velvet; hair singed off, roasted with ghee)　tusizi (toshishi; dodder seed; soaked in wine until soft, ground finely separately)　shu dihuang (prepared rehmannia)　niuxi (goshitsu; achyranthes; soaked in wine)　duzhong (tochu; eucommia; soaked in wine)　dried mugua (chaenomeles; equal parts)

Powder the above and form pills the size of wutong seeds with honey. Take fifty pills each time, swallowed with warm wine or rice broth before meals.

## Notes

Lurong sijin wan (bureau additions): for wilting weakness of sinews and bones, inability to stand and walk, fright and trembling, tidal fever, poor appetite and general insufficiency from deficiency heat of the liver and kidneys.
- The earlier Sijin wan (cistanche, achyranthes, gastrodia, chaenomeles; juan 1) plus deer antler velvet, dodder, rehmannia and eucommia.
