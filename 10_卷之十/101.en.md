# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 101 棗湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zao tang (Soto; Jujube Drink)

It treats disharmony of the spleen and stomach with dry retching and nausea, distension and fullness of the flanks and ribs, and food without relish.

zao (jujube; stones removed; 1 jin)　shengjiang (shokyo; fresh ginger; washed, sliced; 5 jin)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried, chopped; 3 jin)

Mix these three ingredients together evenly, put them in a basin, cover with cloth overnight, dry over a fire and pound into a powder. Take 1 *qian* each time with a little salt, mixed with boiling water. Taken regularly it strengthens the spleen and stomach, smooths qi and improves eating.

## Notes

Zao tang ("Various drinks"): a drink for dry retching, nausea, flank distension and poor appetite from disharmony of the spleen and stomach.
- Jujube, fresh ginger and licorice left to steep together overnight, dried and powdered, taken with salt in hot water.
