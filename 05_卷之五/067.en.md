# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 067 曹公卓鍾乳丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cao Gongzhuo zhongru wan (Cao Gongzhuo's Stalactite Pill)

It governs the five taxations and seven damages and damaged lungs with hurried breathing. It treats men who are declining and ageing with yang qi cut off, cold hands and feet, scant qi in the heart, deficient marrow with lumbar pain, numb legs and a vexed body, and a dry mouth with inability to eat. This medicine brings qi down and disperses food, grows the flesh and harmonizes the middle, calms the five viscera and removes the myriad illnesses.

tusizi (dodder seed; soaked in wine, pounded, dried over a fire)　shihu (dendrobium; roots removed; 1 liang each)　zhongru fen (stalactite powder; 2 liang)　wuzhuyu (evodia; washed seven times in hot water, stir-fried; half a liang)

Make the above into a fine powder, knead with refined honey and form pills the size of wutong seeds. Take seven pills each time on an empty stomach, swallowed with warm wine, warm water or rice water, twice a day. After taking them walk several hundred paces, drink three *ge* of warm wine, walk another two or three hundred paces, and when the heat in the mouth and chest feels somewhat settled, eat dry rice and soybean paste at once. After a day one may eat as usual, but must keep warm and rest. One must not smell or see corpse foulness and similar qi, nor eat coarse, smelly, stale or bad food. For seven days after starting, refrain from sexual activity; after seven days do as one pleases, but excess is still not advisable. If after half a prescription one feels an effect, continue with three prescriptions in succession, and for the rest of one's life there is nothing to avoid.

## Notes

Cao Gongzhuo zhongru wan: for breathlessness from the five taxations and seven damages and damaged lungs, and for declining yang qi, cold limbs, lumbar pain, numb legs, dry mouth and poor appetite in ageing men.
- Mainly stalactite, with dodder, dendrobium and evodia. It records the regimen for taking stalactite (a mineral drug): walking and drinking warm wine after the dose. Cao Gongzhuo appears to be a personal name, but this has not been verified.
