# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 085 三稜散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Sanleng san (Sparganium Powder)

It treats damage by wine and food with discomfort of the chest and diaphragm, distension and fullness of the abdomen and flanks, vomiting of sour water, stomach reflux and spleen pain; and food accumulations and qi lumps attacking and stabbing the abdomen and flanks, with no desire for food and daily wasting. It is also suitable for the elderly with weak qi, blockage of the triple burner and a constant feeling of oppression.

peng'ezhu (zedoary; roasted)　yizhi ren (alpinia oxyphylla)　jing sanleng (sparganium; roasted, sliced)　qingpi (green tangerine peel; white removed; 2 liang each)　bai fuling (white poria; dried over a fire; 4 liang)　gancao (licorice; scorched; 3 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one large *zhan* of water, one jujube broken open and a little salt, decoct together down to half a *zhan* and take it warm, at any time. Taken regularly it loosens the chest, frees the diaphragm, disperses wine and food and harmonizes the stomach.

## Notes

Sanleng san (Wu Zhige's additions): for chest discomfort, abdominal and flank distension, vomiting of sour water, stomach reflux and spleen pain after damage by wine and food, for stabbing pain, poor appetite and wasting from food accumulations and qi lumps, and for blockage of the triple burner in the elderly.
- Sparganium, zedoary and green tangerine peel break up accumulations and qi; alpinia warms; poria and licorice regulate.
