# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 038 和中散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Hezhong san (Middle-Harmonizing Powder)

It treats children with disharmony of the spleen and stomach, vomiting counterflow and nausea, disharmony of cold and heat, reduced eating and diarrhea, abdominal pain and borborygmus, and little strength with a liking for lying down.

houpo (koboku; magnolia bark; bark removed, roasted with ginger; 6 liang)　baizhu (byakujutsu; white atractylodes; 3 liang)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried; 2 liang each)

Powder the above. For each dose take 1 *qian* with eight-tenths of a *zhan* of water and two slices of fresh ginger, decoct down to six-tenths, remove the dregs and take it fairly hot, before feeding.

## Notes

Hezhong san: for vomiting, nausea, disharmony of cold and heat, poor appetite, diarrhea, abdominal pain, borborygmus, weakness and a tendency to lie down from disharmony of the spleen and stomach in children.
- Mainly magnolia bark, with atractylodes, dried ginger and licorice to warm and regulate the spleen and stomach.
