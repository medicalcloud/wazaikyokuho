# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 005 石南丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shinan wan (Sekinangan; Photinia Pill)

It treats wind toxin with weak legs and little strength, heavy, painful, numb legs, swollen legs with sores, dull pain under the feet with inability to stand on the ground, cramping of the sinews of the legs and knees with inability to bend and stretch, and uncomfortable stiffness of the neck, back, waist and spine; wind toxin attacking upward with puffiness of the head and face, or fine sores exuding yellow-red fluid; or weakness of the arms; or sores of the mouth and tongue, ulcerated gums, loose teeth and falling hair, cicada sounds in the ears, dizziness with hurried breathing, distension and oppression of the heart and abdomen, occasionally rough urination and sometimes difficult stools.

chi shaoyao (shakuyaku; red peony root)　yiyiren (yokuinin; coix seed)　chixiaodou (sekishozu; rice bean)　danggui (toki) (Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed)　shinanye (photinia leaf)　qianniuzi (morning-glory seed)　mahuang (mao; ephedra; roots and nodes removed)　chenpi (chinpi; aged tangerine peel; white pith removed)　xingren (kyonin; apricot kernel; skin, tips and double kernels removed, stir-fried)　dafupi (areca husk; used with seeds)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage; 2 liang each)　niuxi (goshitsu; achyranthes; sprouts removed)　wujiapi (gokahi; acanthopanax bark; 3 liang each)　bixie (dioscorea hypoglauca)　duhuo (dokukatsu; angelica pubescens root; rhizome head removed)　duzhong (tochu; eucommia; chopped, stir-fried)　mugua (chaenomeles; 4 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with steamed bread soaked in wine. Take ten, fifteen or twenty pills each time with chaenomeles decoction, one dose each on rising, at midday and at bedtime. Taken regularly it supplements and augments the original qi, makes the sinews and bones strong and healthy and sharpens the ears and eyes. Women's blood qi may also be treated with it. At any time.

## Notes

Shinan wan: a leg-qi formula for weak, heavy, painful, swollen and ulcerated legs, difficulty walking, cramping and back stiffness from wind toxin, and for facial puffiness, sores, mouth ulcers, gum ulceration, loose teeth, hair loss, tinnitus, dizziness, abdominal fullness and irregular bowels when it attacks upward.
- Photinia, chaenomeles, coix, dioscorea, duhuo, acanthopanax and rice bean dispel wind-damp; eucommia and achyranthes strengthen the lower back and legs; angelica, lovage and red peony regulate the blood; ephedra, apricot kernel, morning-glory seed, areca husk and tangerine peel move qi and water.
