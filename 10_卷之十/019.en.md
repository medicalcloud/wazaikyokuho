# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 019 比金丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bijin wan (Gold-Rivaling Pill)

It treats fright wind in children with a hot body, coarse breathing with drool and cough, palpitation and red cheeks, blocked urine and stools, and restless sleep at night.

huashi (kasseki; talc)　nifen (calomel; ground; 15 liang each)　qingdai (indigo; ground; 2½ liang)　tiannanxing (arisaema; blast-fried; 12½ liang)　badou (hazu; croton seed; 700, skins removed, "frost" removed)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of hemp seeds with flour paste. Give one pill per year of age with warm mint water. For acute fright wind with a hot head and cold feet, clenched jaw and green face, twitching of the sinews and vessels and stubborn drool in the upper diaphragm, when the condition is severe, add one or two pills and give with decocted peach-charm decoction; this frees and purges the pent-up toxic hot drool, and there is immediate peace and recovery. It is also suitable for children with residual toxin unresolved after sores and rashes. After meals.

## Notes

Bijin wan: for fever, coarse breathing, phlegm cough, palpitation, red cheeks, blocked bowels and urine and restless sleep from fright wind in children; for severe acute fright wind (hot head, cold feet, clenched jaw, green face, twitching, sticky phlegm); and for residual toxin after eruptions.
- Croton seed and calomel purge phlegm-heat; arisaema, indigo and talc are added.
- The note "frost removed" under croton seed is translated as in the base text (its sense is unclear).
