# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 077 万金飲 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wanjin yin (Ten-Thousand Gold Beverage)

It can treat weak, deficient spleen and stomach that have received wind-cold internally, or damage of the spleen and stomach by raw and cold food and drink, with vomiting and diarrhea, gripping pain of the navel and abdomen, distension and fullness of the flanks and ribs and deficiency rumbling in the intestines; as well as the intestines and stomach having received damp with pus and blood mixed, discharge like bean juice or of static blood, tenesmus, without limit day and night, reduced eating and gradual emaciation and weakness.

chenpi (chinpi; aged tangerine peel; white pith removed)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; half raw, half mix-fried)　yingsuqiao (poppy husk; stalk and cap removed, half raw, half roasted with honey; equal parts)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose of 4 *qian*, first heat a cup by pouring boiling water into it, fill a bowl with hot water and set the cup in it, then scoop the medicine powder into the cup, pour boiling water in to seven-tenths, cover the cup with another cup, leave for a good while, strain off the dregs through gauze or silk floss and take it warm on an empty stomach before meals.

## Notes

Wanjin yin (bureau additions): for vomiting, diarrhea, gripping umbilical pain, flank distension and borborygmus from weak spleen and stomach with wind-cold or raw and cold food, and for pus and blood, stools like bean juice or static blood, tenesmus, poor appetite and wasting from damp in the intestines and stomach.
- Three ingredients (tangerine peel, licorice, poppy husk) infused in hot water in a cup kept warm in a water bath.
