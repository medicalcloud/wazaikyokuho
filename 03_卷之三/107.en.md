# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 107 廿四味流気飲 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Ershisi wei liuqi yin (Twenty-Four-Ingredient Qi-Flowing Drink)

The formula is the same as Muxiang liuqi yin, except that it lacks acorus and agastache and has aquilaria, bitter orange and rhubarb. (Aquilaria 6 liang; bitter orange, pulp removed and stir-fried with bran, 4 liang; rhubarb, wrapped in flour and roasted, flour removed, sliced, 2 liang. From the *Jiyan fang*.)

## Notes

Ershisi wei liuqi yin (bureau additions): Muxiang liuqi yin (juan 3, no. 102) minus acorus and agastache, plus aquilaria, bitter orange and rhubarb, making twenty-four ingredients; said to come from the *Jiyan fang*.
