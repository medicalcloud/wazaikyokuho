# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 036 論傷寒発喘 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On panting in cold damage. Panting with hurried breathing in cold damage always arises from wind-cold, the evil riding on the lungs so that channel qi is exuberant above and panting occurs; Mahuang tang (Ephedra Decoction), Huagai san (Canopy Powder), Kuandonghua san, Renshen runfei wan, Yangfei wan, or Runjin san and Kuanfei [san] may all be taken.

## Notes

On panting in cold damage (wind-cold evil riding on the lungs, channel qi exuberant above): Mahuang tang, Huagai san, Kuandonghua san, Renshen runfei wan, Yangfei wan, Runjin san and Kuanfei san.
