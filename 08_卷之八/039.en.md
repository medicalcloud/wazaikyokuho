# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 039 千金漏芦湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qianjin loulu tang (Thousand-Gold Rhaponticum Decoction)

It treats abscesses, flat-abscesses and effusions of the back, cinnabar toxin and malign swellings, seasonal heat toxin breaking out red, scrofula at its onset, red pain of the head and eyes, suddenly formed obstructions and nebulae, and swollen, painful breast inflammation. For all nameless malign sores, even if the suffering seems slight, one must not be careless: take this medicine urgently and they all disperse internally without forming pus. For effusions of the back and abscesses where pus has already formed, the pus should be expelled; take it until the pus is exhausted.

loulu (rhaponticum root; rhizome head removed)　mahuang (ephedra; roots and nodes removed)　shengma (cimicifuga; chopped)　chi shaoyao (red peony root; chopped raw)　huangqin (scutellaria; skin removed)　gancao (licorice; chopped raw)　bailian (ampelopsis root; washed clean)　baiji (bletilla; fibrous roots removed)　zhiqiao (bitter orange; soaked overnight in rice-washing water, white removed; 4 liang each)　sheng dahuang (raw rhubarb; 13 liang)

Cut the above into pieces. For each dose take 4 *qian* with two *zhan* of water, decoct down to one *zhan*, strain off the dregs through gauze or silk and take it hot on an empty stomach before meals, aiming for a free purge. The patient may further adjust the amount himself, with immediate effect.

## Notes

Qianjin loulu tang (from here the layer of Baoqing-era additions): for abscesses, effusions of the back, cinnabar toxin, malign swellings, seasonal heat toxin, early scrofula, red painful head and eyes, sudden obstructions, breast inflammation and all nameless malign sores; taken early it disperses them internally, and where pus has formed it drains it.
- Rhaponticum, cimicifuga, scutellaria, red peony, ampelopsis and bletilla clear heat toxin; ephedra disperses; raw rhubarb and bitter orange purge. In the lineage of the *Qianjin fang* Loulu tang.
