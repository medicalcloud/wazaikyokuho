# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 033 生犀散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shengxi san (Raw Rhinoceros Horn Powder)

It is suitable for children with steaming bones and wasted flesh, red cheeks and dry mouth, tidal fever in the evening, night sweats, vexation and agitation of the five hearts, weary limbs, and much eating that does not become flesh; as well as residual toxin unresolved after recovery from a major illness, or fever that persists after cold damage because of eating mutton.

dahuang (rhubarb; steamed, sliced, dried over a fire)　biejia (turtle shell; boiled in water, skirt and rim removed, smeared with vinegar and roasted yellow)　maimendong (ophiopogon; core removed)　huangqi (astragalus)　qinjiao (large-leaf gentian; shoots and earth removed)　lingyangjiao (antelope horn; shaved)　sangbaipi (mulberry root bark; chopped)　renshen (ginseng)　fuling (poria; skin removed)　digupi (lycium root bark; earth removed)　chi shaoyao (red peony root)　chaihu (bupleurum; shoots removed)　zhiqiao (bitter orange; pulp removed, stir-fried with bran)

Take equal parts of the above and pound into a coarse powder. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one *zhan* of water and a little sweet wormwood, decoct down to six-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm, after meals. For a small child divide into two doses.

## Notes

Shengxi san: for steaming bone disorder in children (wasting, red cheeks, dry mouth, evening tidal fever, night sweats, vexing heat of the five hearts, lassitude, eating without gaining flesh), for residual toxin after a major illness, and for persistent fever after cold damage from eating mutton (food relapse).
- Turtle shell, bupleurum, large-leaf gentian, lycium root bark, antelope horn and sweet wormwood clear deficiency heat; ginseng, astragalus, poria and ophiopogon supplement; rhubarb, bitter orange, mulberry root bark and red peony are added. Despite the name, the formula in the base text contains no rhinoceros horn.
