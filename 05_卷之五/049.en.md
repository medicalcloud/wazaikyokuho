# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 049 威喜丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Weixi wan (Majestic Joy Pill)

It treats men with deficient, exhausted original yang and essential qi not secured, constant dribbling, white turbid urine and frequent emissions in dreams; and women with long-standing cold of the sea of blood, white vaginal discharge, white leakage and white lewdness, constant dampness of the lower parts and urine like rice-washing water; or childlessness.

huangla (yellow beeswax; 4 liang)　bai fuling (white poria; skin removed; 4 liang, made into lumps and boiled twenty-odd times with 1 *fen* of polyporus in a porcelain vessel, taken out and dried in the sun; the polyporus is not used)

Powder the poria, melt the yellow wax and knead them into pills the size of a pellet. On an empty stomach chew one thoroughly until the mouth fills with saliva, and swallow slowly, until the urine becomes clear. Avoid rice vinegar—eat only bran vinegar—and above all avoid losing one's temper.

## Notes

Weixi wan (bureau additions): for seminal emission, dribbling, turbid urine and dream emissions from deficient original yang in men, and for white vaginal discharge, turbid urine and infertility from a cold sea of blood in women.
- Two ingredients: poria boiled with polyporus, made into pills with yellow beeswax, chewed and swallowed with the saliva.
