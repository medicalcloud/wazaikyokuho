# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷下 020 論牙歯疼痛 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On toothache. The patterns of toothache are not one. There is heat pain, where all the teeth in the mouth feel raised, painful because of heat in the upper diaphragm. There is deficiency pain, always from deficient, exhausted kidney meridian with deficiency-heat qi attacking upward. There is toothache from wind and worms. One must examine the pattern carefully before giving medicine. When the gums are puffy and swollen, the breath in the mouth hot, and all the teeth raised and loose, this is a heat pattern: give Sishun yin, Ganlu yin, Xixin san (Saishinsan), Longnao yin, Qingxin yinzi or Bazheng san (Hasshosan); then decoct Shengma gegen tang (Shomakakkonto), rinse the mouth with it and spit it out; swallowing a little does no harm. For toothache from deficient, exhausted kidney meridian with deficiency-heat qi attacking upward, and for toothache in the elderly and weak, give Huangqi wan (Ogigan), Anshen wan, Lurong wan or Bawei wan; then rub with Fuyan san and rinse with Shengma gegen tang. For toothache from wind pouring in, in young, robust persons, rub with Xixin san or Fuyan san to dispel wind and spit out phlegm; after a while rinse with Shengma gegen tang and spit out at once; then give Heishen wan, Ruxiang wan (Nyukogan) or Bailong wan. For toothache from drinking wine, rinse with well-flower water, or steep Baiyao jian in hot water, cool it, hold in the mouth and swallow, or chew amomum and apply it; these are in general use.

## Notes

On toothache: heat pain (teeth raised from upper-diaphragm heat) with Sishun yin, Ganlu yin, Xixin san and others plus Shengma gegen tang as a mouth rinse; deficiency pain (deficiency heat of the kidney meridian) and toothache of the elderly with Huangqi wan, Anshen wan, Lurong wan or Bawei wan plus Fuyan san; wind toothache in the robust with Xixin san or Fuyan san, then Heishen wan, Ruxiang wan or Bailong wan; toothache after drinking with well-flower water, Baiyao jian or amomum.
