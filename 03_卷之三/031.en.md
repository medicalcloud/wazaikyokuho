# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 031 温白丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wenbai wan (Warm White Pill)

It treats accumulations and gatherings in the heart and abdomen, long-standing concretions and aggregations with lumps as large as a cup or bowl, jaundice and old food, vomiting on rising in the morning, propping fullness with rising qi, frequent abdominal distension, hard binding below the heart that rises to rush the heart and attacks both flanks at the sides; the ten kinds of water disease, the eight kinds of glomus and blockage, stomach reflux and vomiting counterflow, choking obstruction of food, the five kinds of strangury, the nine kinds of heart pain, food undigested for years, and malaria that has not healed for years. It also treats all kinds of wind with stubborn numbness of the body and no sense of pain or itch, or hemiplegia, or loss of the eyebrows and hair; and the seventy-two kinds of wind, the thirty-six kinds of hidden corpse and infixation and visiting-hostility, and epilepsy. Or women's illnesses: childbearing interrupted, vaginal discharge dribbling, loss of mind from the five evils, sorrow, worry, thought and anxiety, joylessness, tasteless food and irregular menses; and all illnesses within the abdomen resembling pregnancy, lasting years and months, with emaciation and exhaustion, now singing and now weeping as if driven by ghosts—simply take this medicine, and there is none that is not removed and cured.

chuanwu (Sichuan aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed; 2½ liang)　chaihu (bupleurum; rhizome head removed)　jiegeng (platycodon)　wuzhuyu (evodia; washed seven times in hot water, dried over a fire and stir-fried)　changpu (acorus)　ziwan (aster; sprouts, leaves and earth removed)　huanglian (coptis; rootlets removed)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　fuling (poria; skin removed)　shujiao (Sichuan pepper; seeds and unopened ones removed, stir-fried to sweat out the oil)　renshen (ginseng)　houpo (magnolia bark; coarse bark removed, processed with ginger juice)　zaojia (soap pod; skin and seeds removed, roasted)　badou (croton seeds; skin, core and membranes removed, oil pressed out, stir-fried and ground; half a liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder, mix in the croton evenly and form pills the size of wutong seeds with refined honey. Take three pills each time with ginger decoction, after meals or at bedtime, gradually increasing to five or seven pills.

## Notes

Wenbai wan: said to treat an extremely wide range of illness—abdominal accumulations, concretions, jaundice, old food, vomiting, water disease, glomus, stomach reflux, strangury, heart pain and chronic malaria; numbness and hemiplegia from wind; hidden-corpse disorders and epilepsy; and women's infertility, discharge, mental disturbance and irregular menses.
- In the lineage of the Wanbing wan (Ziwan wan) of the *Qianjin fang*: croton and soap pod attack accumulations; aconite, evodia, Sichuan pepper, dried ginger and cinnamon warm; ginseng and poria supplement; coptis, bupleurum, platycodon, aster, acorus and magnolia bark are added.
