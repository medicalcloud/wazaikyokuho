# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 088 正元散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zhengyuan san (Origin-Rectifying Powder)

It is suitable for deficient qi of the lower origin with distension and fullness of the navel and abdomen, stabbing pain of the heart and flanks, diarrhoea, vomiting and spontaneous sweating, light and faint yang qi and reversal cold of the hands and feet; as well as yin patterns of cold damage, sudden turmoil with cramps, long-standing cold dysentery, shortness of qi with emaciation and fatigue, and all deficiency cold.

hongdou (galangal fruit; stir-fried)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried)　chenpi (aged tangerine peel; white pith removed; 3 *qian* each)　renshen (ginseng)　baizhu (white atractylodes)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried)　fuling (poria; skin removed; 2 liang each)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　chuanwu (Sichuan aconite; blast-fried, skin removed; half a liang each)　fuzi (aconite; blast-fried, skin and tip removed)　shanyao (Chinese yam; soaked in ginger juice, stir-fried)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage)　wuyao (lindera; woody part removed)　gange (dried pueraria; 1 liang each)　huangqi (astragalus; mix-fried; 1½ liang)

Make the above into a fine powder. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one *zhan* of water, three slices of ginger, one jujube and a little salt, decoct down to seven-tenths and take it warm before meals. Taken regularly it assists yang and disperses yin, rectifies the original qi, warms the spleen and stomach and improves eating.

## Notes

Zhengyuan san (bureau additions): for umbilical distension, stabbing pain of the heart and flanks, diarrhoea, vomiting, sweating, faint yang qi and cold limbs from deficient qi of the lower origin, for yin-pattern cold damage, sudden turmoil, chronic cold dysentery and all deficiency cold. Its indications are nearly the same as those of the earlier Erqi dan (juan 5, no. 065).
- Si junzi tang (ginseng, atractylodes, poria, licorice) plus astragalus and Chinese yam supplement; aconite, Sichuan aconite, cinnamon, dried ginger and galangal fruit warm; tangerine peel, lindera, lovage and pueraria are added.
