# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 105 仙術湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xianzhu tang (Immortal Atractylodes Drink)

It wards off pestilence, removes cold-damp, warms the spleen and stomach and improves eating.

cangzhu (black atractylodes; skin removed; 48 jin)　zao (jujube; stones removed; 2 *dou* 4 sheng)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried; 24 liang)　xingren (kyonin; apricot kernel; skin and tips removed, stir-fried with bran, pounded separately; 6 jin)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; stir-fried; 14 jin)　yan (salt; stir-fried; 25 jin)

Make the above into a fine powder and mix in the apricot kernel evenly. Take 1 *qian* each time mixed with boiling water, before meals. Taken regularly it prolongs life, brightens the eyes, preserves the complexion, lightens the body and prevents ageing.

## Notes

Xianzhu tang ("Various drinks"): a drink to ward off epidemics, remove cold-damp, warm the spleen and stomach and improve appetite; with regular use said to prolong life and prevent ageing.
- Mainly black atractylodes, with jujube, dried ginger, apricot kernel, licorice and salt.
