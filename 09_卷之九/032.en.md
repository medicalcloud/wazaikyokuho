# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 032 牛膝湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Niuxi tang (Goshitsuto; Achyranthes Decoction)

It is suitable when the child has already come out but the placenta does not descend, with hard fullness of the navel and abdomen and tense, distended pain, as well as when a child has died in the abdomen and cannot come out.

huashi (kasseki; talc; 8 liang)　danggui (toki; Chinese angelica; shoots removed, soaked in wine)　mutong (akebia; 6 liang each)　niuxi (goshitsu; achyranthes; shoots removed, soaked in wine, dried over a fire)　qumai (kubaku; dianthus; 4 liang each)　dongkuizi (tokishi; mallow seed; 5 liang)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with two *zhan* of water, decoct down to eight-tenths, remove the dregs and take it fairly hot, at any time.

## Notes

Niuxi tang: for retained placenta with hard, distended, painful abdomen, and for a dead fetus that will not come out.
- Achyranthes, dianthus, talc, mallow seed and akebia lubricate and bring down; angelica is added.
- "Fairly hot" emends the base text's "xiao re fu" following the Siku quanshu "shao re fu".
