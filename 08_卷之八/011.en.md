# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 011 釣腸丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Diaochang wan (Intestine-Hooking Pill)

It treats the various piles, long-standing or recent, with swollen, painful anal margins, or itchy sores and occasional pus and blood. It is also suitable for intestinal wind with bleeding and prolapse of the anus.

gualou (trichosanthes fruit; two, burned with nature preserved)　weipi (hedgehog skin; two, chopped and burned with nature preserved in an earthenware jar)　jiguanhua (cockscomb flower; chopped, lightly stir-fried; 5 liang)　hutao (walnut; fifteen kernels taken, not oily ones, burned with nature preserved in an earthenware jar)　baifan (alum; dried)　lüfan (melanterite; dried)　bai fuzi (typhonium tuber; used raw)　tiannanxing (arisaema; used raw)　zhiqiao (bitter orange; pulp removed, stir-fried with bran)　fuzi (aconite; skin and navel removed, used raw)　hezi (chebule; roasted in embers, skin removed)　banxia (pinellia; 2 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with flour paste boiled in vinegar. Take twenty pills each time on an empty stomach and at bedtime with warm wine. Even cases unhealed for years show effect after ten days; long use removes the root for good. Minor intestinal wind and similar illnesses of one or two years' standing heal with just ten doses and never recur.

## Notes

Diaochang wan: for swollen, painful anal margins, itchy sores and pus and blood in piles, old or new; for intestinal wind with bleeding; and for rectal prolapse.
- Trichosanthes fruit, hedgehog skin and walnut burned with nature preserved; cockscomb flower stops bleeding; alum and melanterite astringe; typhonium, arisaema, pinellia, aconite, bitter orange and chebule are added.
