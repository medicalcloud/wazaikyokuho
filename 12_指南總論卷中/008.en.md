# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 008 論諸風小便不通 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On urinary blockage in the various winds. It always arises from accumulated heat in the small intestine and congestion and stagnation of the bladder. Give Daochi san (Dosekisan), Jisu wan, Sanhuang wan or Sanhe san (Sanwasan). For severe blockage, give Shengong wan or Wuling san (Goreisan; Five Ingredients Poria Powder).

## Notes

On urinary blockage in the various winds (accumulated heat in the small intestine, stagnant bladder): Daochi san, Jisu wan (Longnao jisu wan), Sanhuang wan and Sanhe san; for severe cases Shengong wan and Wuling san.
