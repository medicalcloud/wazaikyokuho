# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 036 訶黎勒散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Helile san (Chebule Powder)

It treats weak, deficient spleen and stomach harbouring cold qi within, with distension, fullness and stabbing pain of the heart, flanks, navel and abdomen, vomiting and nausea, reduced eating, borborygmus and diarrhoea, undigested water and grain, lassitude with little strength, and gradual emaciation and weakness.

qingpi (green tangerine peel; pulp removed)　hezi pi (chebule fruit rind; 40 liang each)　fuzi (aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed; 10 jin)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed; 5 jin)　roudoukou (nutmeg; wrapped in dough and roasted in embers until cooked; 40 liang)

Powder the above. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water and three slices of fresh ginger, decoct together down to seven-tenths and take it warm before meals.

## Notes

Helile san: for distension and stabbing pain of the heart, flanks, navel and abdomen, vomiting, nausea, poor appetite, borborygmus, diarrhoea, indigestion, lassitude and wasting from weak spleen and stomach harbouring cold qi.
- Aconite and cinnamon warm; chebule and nutmeg astringe the intestines; green tangerine peel moves qi.
