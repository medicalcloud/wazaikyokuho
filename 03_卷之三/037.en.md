# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 037 神保丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shenbao wan (Divine Protection Pill)

For pain of the heart and diaphragm, take it with persimmon-calyx and rush-pith decoction. For abdominal pain, with a decoction of persimmon calyx and roasted ginger. For blood pain, with stir-fried ginger and vinegar decoction. For severe lung qi, grind 3 *fen* each of alum and clamshell powder with 1 *fen* of minium into a powder and take 3 *qian* of it mixed in a decoction of white mulberry root bark and glutinous rice gruel. For slight panting, use only mulberry bark and glutinous rice gruel. For kidney qi with pain below the flanks, take it with stir-fried fennel in wine. For blocked bowels, mix 1 *qian* of areca powder in honey water and take it with that. For choking qi, with costus decoction. For undispersed old food, with tea, wine, broth or gruel as preferred. Among all qi disorders, bladder qi and pain below the flanks are the hardest to treat, yet this medicine alone can remove them at once. A man suffered pain of the sinews of the neck; all the physicians took it for wind and treated it for months without success; it then flowed into the spine and after a long time poured into the right flank, with cramping pain causing great suffering. He was given this medicine compounded, and one dose cured him; later it recurred once, and again one dose cured him.

muxiang (costus root)　hujiao (black pepper; 1 *fen* each)　ganxie (dried scorpions; seven whole ones)　badou (croton seeds; core and skin removed, ground separately; 10)

Make the above into a fine powder, mix in the croton frost evenly, form pills the size of hemp seeds with steamed bread softened in hot water, and coat with cinnabar. Take three pills each time with the vehicles given above.

## Notes

Shenbao wan (Shaoxing-era additions): for many kinds of qi pain—heart and diaphragm pain, abdominal pain, blood pain, lung-qi panting, flank pain from kidney qi, constipation, choking qi and old food—with the vehicle varied for each. It is said to be especially effective for bladder qi and pain below the flanks, and records a case of pain that moved from the neck to the back and flank.
- Small pills of croton, scorpion, costus and black pepper. The indications are written as instructions for administration, as in the base text.
