# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 014 人参黄耆散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Renshen huangqi san (Ninjinogisan; Ginseng and Astragalus Powder)

It treats visiting heat in deficiency/consumption with wasted flesh, weary limbs, vexing heat of the five hearts, dry mouth and throat, red cheeks and palpitation, tidal fever in the evening, night sweats, discomfort of the chest and flanks, reduced eating with much thirst, thick sticky cough and spittle, and occasionally pus and blood.

tianmendong (tenmondo; asparagus root; core removed; 30 liang)　banxia (hange; pinellia; washed seven times in hot water, processed with ginger juice)　zhimu (chimo; anemarrhena)　sangbaipi (mulberry root bark; chopped and stir-fried)　chi shaoyao (shakuyaku; red peony root)　huangqi (ogi; astragalus)　ziwan (aster)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; scorched; 15 liang each)　bai fuling (bukuryo; white poria; skin removed)　chaihu (saiko; bupleurum; sprouts removed)　qinjiao (large-leaf gentian; earth removed)　sheng gan dihuang (kanjio; dried raw rehmannia)　digupi (lycium root bark; 20 liang each)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng)　jiegeng (kikyo; platycodon; 10 liang each)　biejia (bekko; turtle shell; rim removed, roasted with vinegar; 1 liang)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take two large *qian* with one *zhan* of water, decoct down to seven-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm, after meals.

## Notes

Renshen huangqi san: for wasting, lassitude, vexing heat of the five hearts, dry mouth, red cheeks, palpitation, tidal fever, night sweats, thirst and sticky or blood-and-pus sputum from visiting heat in deficiency/consumption—a pattern suggestive of consumptive disease such as tuberculosis.
- Ginseng, astragalus, poria and licorice supplement qi; asparagus root, raw rehmannia, anemarrhena, lycium bark, gentian, bupleurum and turtle shell nourish yin and clear deficiency heat; aster, mulberry bark, platycodon and pinellia treat cough and phlegm; red peony cools the blood.
