# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 009 立効散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Lixiao san (Immediate Effect Powder)

It treats bound heat of the lower burner with yellow-red urine, strangury-like blockage with pain, or bleeding; it is also suitable for bleeding with both urine and stools.

shanzhizi (gardenia; skin removed, stir-fried; half a liang)　qumai sui (dianthus spikes; 1 liang)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried; 3 *fen*)

Powder the above. For each dose take 5 to 7 *qian* with one bowl of water, add seven scallion roots with their hairs, fifty pieces of rush pith and five to seven slices of fresh ginger, decoct together down to seven-tenths and take it warm from time to time, at any time.

## Notes

Lixiao san: for yellow-red urine, painful urinary blockage and haematuria from bound heat in the lower burner, and for bleeding with urine and stools.
- Three ingredients (gardenia, dianthus, licorice) decocted with scallion roots, rush pith and ginger.
