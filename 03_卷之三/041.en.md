# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 041 丁沈煎丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Dingchen jian wan (Clove and Aquilaria Syrup Pill)

It wards off the pathogenic cold of fog and dew, scatters congealed stagnation of the diaphragm and stomach duct, regulates and smooths the sanjiao and harmonizes and nourishes the ying-nutrient and wei-defense qi. It treats stuffiness and oppression of the heart and chest, belching of sourness and acid swallowing, vomiting counterflow of phlegm and water, uncontained saliva, stabbing pain in both flanks, hard fullness in the abdomen, a bitter mouth without taste and no desire for food.

dingxiang (clove; 12 liang)　chenxiang (aquilaria; 2 liang)　muxiang (mokko; costus root; 1½ *qian*)　dingxiangpi (clove bark; 1 liang)　bai doukou ren (white cardamom; 9½ liang)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills with a separately prepared licorice paste, dividing each liang into 250 pills. Hold one pill in the mouth to dissolve each time, on an empty stomach.

## Notes

Dingchen jian wan (Shaoxing-era additions): wards off pathogenic cold and treats stomach stuffiness, acid reflux, vomiting, excess saliva, flank pain, abdominal fullness and poor appetite.
- Clove, clove bark, aquilaria, costus and white cardamom made into tiny pills with licorice paste and dissolved in the mouth.
