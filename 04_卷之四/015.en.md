# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之四 015 華蓋散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huagai san (Canopy Powder)

It treats the lungs affected by cold evil with cough and rising qi, vexing fullness of the chest and diaphragm, stiffness of the neck and back, a heavy voice and nasal congestion, a clouded head and dizzy eyes, phlegm qi not clearing, and wheezing.

zisuzi (perilla seed; stir-fried)　chi fuling (red poria; skin removed)　sangbaipi (mulberry root bark; roasted)　chenpi (aged tangerine peel; white pith removed)　xingren (apricot kernel; skin and tips removed, stir-fried)　mahuang (ephedra; roots and nodes removed; 1 liang each)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried; half a liang)

Powder these seven ingredients. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one *zhan* of water, decoct down to seven-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm, after meals.

## Notes

Huagai san: for cough, rising qi, chest fullness, neck and back stiffness, nasal congestion, dizziness, phlegm and wheezing when the lungs are affected by cold evil.
- Ephedra, apricot kernel and licorice (Sanao tang) plus perilla seed, mulberry bark, tangerine peel and red poria to diffuse and lower the lungs. "Huagai" (canopy) is a name for the lungs.
