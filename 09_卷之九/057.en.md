# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 057 白堊丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Baie dan (White Chalk Elixir)

It treats the thirty-six illnesses of women: flooding and spotting, an emaciated body with hot hands and feet, aversion to wind and fear of cold, cough with counterflow and vexing fullness, cramping and shortness of breath, pain of the heart, flanks, lower back, spine and abdomen drawing on the womb, five-coloured spotting, constant fear, flaring whenever meeting anger, worry or exertion—all caused by internal damage.

muli (oyster shell; calcined, ground)　bai'e (white chalk)　xixin (asarum; shoots removed)　yuyuliang (limonite; calcined, quenched in vinegar nine times, ground)　bai shizhi (white halloysite; calcined)　longgu (dragon bone; calcined, ground; 1½ liang each)　qumai sui (dianthus spikes)　fuzi (aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　wuzeiyu gu (cuttlefish bone; burned to ash)　shaoyao (peony root)　shiwei (pyrrosia leaf; hairs removed)　bailian (ampelopsis root)　huanglian (coptis; hairs removed)　fuling (poria; skin removed)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　baizhi (angelica dahurica)　danggui (Chinese angelica; shoots removed)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried)　renshen (ginseng)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried; 1 liang each)　chuanjiao (Sichuan pepper; seeds and closed fruits removed, stir-fried to sweat out the oil; half a liang)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with refined honey. Take thirty to fifty pills each time on an empty stomach with warm wine.

## Notes

Baie dan (bureau additions): for the thirty-six illnesses of women—flooding and spotting, wasting, hot extremities, aversion to wind and cold, cough, vexing fullness, breathlessness, pain of the heart, flanks, back and abdomen drawing on the womb, five-coloured spotting and fearfulness, all from internal damage triggered by emotion or exertion.
- White chalk, oyster shell, limonite, white halloysite, dragon bone and cuttlefish bone stop bleeding and discharge; aconite, dried ginger, cinnamon, Sichuan pepper and asarum warm; ginseng, angelica, peony, poria and licorice supplement; dianthus, pyrrosia, ampelopsis, coptis and angelica dahurica are added.
