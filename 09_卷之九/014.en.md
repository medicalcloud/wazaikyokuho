# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 014 白薇丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Baiwei wan (Cynanchum Pill)

It supplements and regulates the *chong* and *ren* and warms the womb. It treats damaged uterine network vessels with old wind-cold, long childlessness, or pregnancies that are not secure and often end in miscarriage. Taken for a long time it removes wind-cold from the lower viscera and enables conception.

qinjiao (Qin pepper; seeds and closed fruits removed, lightly stir-fried to sweat out the oil; half a liang)　baiwei (cynanchum root; shoots removed)　shu gan dihuang (prepared rehmannia)　danggui (Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed, chopped, lightly stir-fried)　jianghuang (turmeric; 1 liang 7½ *qian* each)　mumeng (mumeng [unidentified herb])　gaoben (Chinese lovage; shoots and earth removed; 1 liang 2½ *qian* each)　yuyuliang (limonite; calcined, quenched in wine seven times, ground; 2 liang)　renshen (ginseng)　baiziren (arborvitae seed; lightly stir-fried)　sangjisheng (mulberry mistletoe)　fuzi (aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　wuweizi (schisandra; stalks removed)　wuzhuyu (evodia; soaked in hot water, lightly stir-fried)　shihu (dendrobium; roots removed)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried until slightly red)　niuxi (achyranthes; shoots removed, soaked in wine overnight, dried over a fire)　fangfeng (saposhnikovia; shoots and forks removed)　xiongqiong (Sichuan lovage; 1½ liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder, mix in the ground medicine evenly and form pills the size of wutong seeds with refined honey. Take thirty to fifty pills each time with warm wine or rice water, on an empty stomach before meals. As soon as pregnancy is noticed, stop taking it; it is especially unsuitable once pregnant.

## Notes

Baiwei wan: for infertility from damaged uterine vessels and old wind-cold, and for insecure pregnancies prone to miscarriage; warms the womb to enable conception. Stop once pregnancy is recognized.
- Cynanchum, mumeng, turmeric and limonite, with prepared rehmannia, angelica, lovage, ginseng, mistletoe, dendrobium and achyranthes to supplement; aconite, cinnamon, evodia and Qin pepper warm; saposhnikovia and Chinese lovage dispel wind.
