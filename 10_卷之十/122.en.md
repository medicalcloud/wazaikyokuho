# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 122 降真香 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jiangzhen xiang (Dalbergia Incense)

zi tanxiang (red sandalwood; chopped; 30 liang; moisten with 1 liang of Jian tea powder mixed in hot water, mix evenly and stir-fry over a gentle fire without scorching, until [the tea] qi is not yet exhausted)　bai maoxiang (lemongrass-like fragrant grass; finely chopped; 30 liang)　Qingzhou zao (Qingzhou jujubes; twenty, broken open, boiled in two large sheng of water until they change colour, stir-fried until the colour changes, the jujubes and black parts picked out and discarded; 15 liang)　zirun jiangzhenxiang (purple, moist dalbergia wood; chopped; 40 liang)　huangshuxiang (yellow-ripe aloeswood; chopped; 30 liang)　yanxiao (saltpetre; dissolved in hot water, water-ground to remove dregs, boiled down to frost; half a jin)　fencao (licorice; chopped; 5 liang)　pingxiang (bottle incense; 20 liang)　shexiang mo (musk powder; 15 liang)　gansong (nardostachys; picked clean)　dingxiang pi (clove bark)　huoxiang (agastache; 10 liang each)　longnao (borneol; 2 liang)　jianxiang (jian aloeswood; chopped; 30 liang)

Powder the above, add the ground ingredients, knead with refined honey, and burn in the usual way.

xuanshen (scrophularia; picked clean; 5 liang each)　xiang baizhi (fragrant angelica dahurica)　huoxiang (agastache; chopped; 3 liang each)　xiangfuzi (cyperus; picked clean)　gansong (nardostachys; picked clean; 10 liang each)　shexiang mo (musk powder; half a jin)

## Notes

Jiangzhen xiang ("Various incenses"): an incense recipe centred on dalbergia wood.
- Dalbergia, red sandalwood, fragrant grass, yellow-ripe aloeswood, jian aloeswood, bottle incense, nardostachys, clove bark, agastache, musk and borneol, with jujube, saltpetre and licorice, kneaded with honey.
- The final list ("scrophularia ... musk powder") follows the preparation directions and its attribution is unclear from the base text (perhaps part of the next recipe, Qingyuan xiang). It is translated in the base text's order.
