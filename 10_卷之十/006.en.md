# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 006 鶴頂丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Heding dan (Crane's Crown Elixir)

It treats adults and children with wind congestion and phlegm excess, blockage of the throat and diaphragm, dry mouth with vexing thirst and unquiet sleep, as well as summer heat strike with headache and unresolved agitated thirst.

shexiang (jako; musk; ground; 2½ liang)　zhusha (cinnabar; ground and water-ground; 100 liang)　yaxiao (refined mirabilite; dehydrated, ground; 125 liang)　hanshuishi (kansuiseki) fen (calcite powder; 110 liang)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; stir-fried, powdered; 35 liang)

Grind the above together evenly, knead with refined honey, and make ten pills from each 1 liang 2 *qian*. Adults take one pill dissolved in warm ginger water. For summer heat strike, add a little raw borneol, grind together finely and dissolve in fresh water. For children divide one pill into four doses, adjusted further for size. It also treats accumulated heat in the zang-fu organs of children with an unsettled heart-spirit, manic crying at night and sores of the mouth and tongue: dissolve it in natural mint juice. In all cases take after meals.

## Notes

Heding dan: for wind congestion with phlegm excess, throat blockage, thirst and insomnia in adults and children; for headache and agitated thirst in summer heat strike; and for restlessness, night crying and mouth sores from accumulated heat in children.
- Cinnabar (in large amount, red like a crane's crown), refined mirabilite and calcite clear heat and settle; musk and licorice are added.
