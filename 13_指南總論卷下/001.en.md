# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷下 001 論一切気証 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On the signs of the various qi disorders.

(With the spleen and stomach and accumulations appended.)

On all qi patterns. They all arise when, in worry and grief or in great anger, the true qi is stirred and damaged, yin and yang become disharmonious, qi binds between the chest and diaphragm, congested and stagnant and uncomfortable, food will not go down, and it finally becomes diaphragm-occlusion disease. Give Yunqi san, Wuge kuanzhong san, Geqi san, Chenxiang jiangqi tang, Fenqi zisu yin, Qiqi tang, Jiahe san, Dingxiang zhusan, Fenxinqi yin, Xiao jiangqi tang and the like.

## Notes

The opening of the first discussion of the General Guide, lower volume, "On the signs of the various qi disorders (with the spleen and stomach and accumulations appended)": for qi bound in the chest from worry or anger, with congestion, inability to swallow and diaphragm occlusion, it lists Yunqi san, Wuge kuanzhong san, Geqi san, Chenxiang jiangqi tang, Fenqi zisu yin, Qiqi tang and others.
- The formulas named are mainly in juan 3, "Treating all qi disorders".
