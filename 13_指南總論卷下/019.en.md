# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷下 019 論喉閉、喉風証 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On the patterns of throat blockage and throat wind. When pathogenic wind lodges in the throat and qi becomes depressed and hot, it is congested and held back, producing sore throat. When heat qi arises in the lungs and stomach and wind toxin accumulates and flourishes, it swells and binds, producing throat impediment. In the pattern where a hanging abscess arises on the palate, though the throat is not involved, its sudden swelling is also caused by heat qi. The throat and the hanging abscess concern a vital passage; if the illness is not treated urgently, all can kill a person. For throat blockage and throat-entwining wind, give Jiedu wan, Xixin san, Yuxie wuyou san or Bixue; in severe cases Jiedu xionghuang wan.

## Notes

On throat blockage and throat wind: pathogenic wind lodging in the throat with depressed heat causes sore throat; heat of the lungs and stomach with flourishing wind toxin causes swelling and throat impediment; palatal abscess is likewise due to heat; untreated these can be fatal. Jiedu wan, Xixin san, Yuxie wuyou san and Bixue, and Jiedu xionghuang wan for severe cases.
