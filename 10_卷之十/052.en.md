# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 052 急風丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jifeng dan (Acute Wind Elixir)

It treats children damaged by wind with nasal congestion and clear snivel. Mix it in wine and apply it over the fontanelle; it must not be taken internally. (The formula appears in the section on the various winds.)

## Notes

Jifeng dan (Baoqing-era additions): for nasal congestion and clear discharge from wind damage in children, applied mixed in wine over the fontanelle; not to be taken internally. A cross-reference: the formula is in the section on the various winds (juan 1).
