# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 017 三黄丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Sanhuang wan (Three Yellows Pill)

It treats men and women with accumulated heat in the triple burner: when there is heat in the upper burner, it attacks and surges with red, swollen eyes, swollen, painful head and neck, and sores of the mouth and tongue; when there is heat in the middle burner, vexation and agitation of the heart and diaphragm and no relish for food; when there is heat in the lower burner, red, rough urine and constipation; when all five viscera are hot, flat-abscesses, boils and sores arise [it treats these]. It also treats the five kinds of piles with swollen, painful anus or passing of fresh blood.

huanglian (coptis; fibrous roots and rhizome head removed)　huangqin (scutellaria; rhizome head removed)　dahuang (rhubarb; roasted in embers; 10 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with refined honey. Take thirty pills each time, swallowed with boiled water; if the viscera are congested and replete, increase the number of pills. It is also suitable for children's accumulated heat.

## Notes

Sanhuang wan (Wu Zhige's additions): for accumulated heat of the triple burner (upper: red swollen eyes, swollen painful head and neck, mouth sores; middle: agitation and poor appetite; lower: dark rough urine and constipation), for flat-abscesses, boils and sores from heat in the five viscera, for swollen, bleeding piles, and for children's accumulated heat.
- Three ingredients: coptis, scutellaria and rhubarb (the composition of the *Jingui yaolüe* Xiexin tang, made into pills).
- Emendation: "boiled water" (*shushui*) is "hot water" (*reshui*) in the base text; emended following the Siku quanshu and Executive Yuan editions.
