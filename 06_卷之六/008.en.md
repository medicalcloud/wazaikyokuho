# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 008 竜脳鶏蘇丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Longnao jisu wan (Borneol-Mint Pill)

It removes vexation and relieves fatigue, disperses grain and brings qi down, disperses depressed heat in the chest, governs lung-heat cough, treats nosebleed and haematemesis, blood flooding and bloody stools, blood strangury, heat strangury, taxation strangury and qi strangury, stops wasting-thirst, removes fright palpitations, cools the upper diaphragm and resolves alcohol toxin. It also treats bad breath from stomach heat, a fishy smell in the throat from lung heat, a sweet taste in the mouth from spleen jaundice and a bitter taste from gallbladder jaundice. Taken regularly it sharpens the ears, brightens the eyes, opens the heart and augments wisdom.

chaihu (bupleurum; must be genuine Yinzhou bupleurum; 2 liang, chopped and soaked with the akebia in a good half-sheng of boiling water for one or two nights, the juice squeezed out and later added to the paste)　mutong (akebia; chopped, soaked with the bupleurum)　ejiao (donkey-hide gelatin; stir-fried until slightly dry)　puhuang (typha pollen; genuine, lightly stir-fried)　renshen (ginseng; 2 liang each)　maimendong (ophiopogon; washed in hot water, core removed, dried over a fire; 4 liang)　huangqi (astragalus; rhizome head removed; 1 liang)　jisu (clean leaves; 1 jin; this is borneol mint)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried; 1½ liang)　sheng gan dihuang powder (dried raw rehmannia; 6 liang, added to the paste later)

Except for the medicines ground separately and added later, pound and sift everything into a fine powder. First refine 2 jin of good honey to one or two boils, then add the dried raw rehmannia powder, stirring ceaselessly, adding from time to time the squeezed akebia and bupleurum juice, and slowly boil down into a paste without letting it scorch; then combine it with the remaining medicine powders and form pills the size of peas. Take twenty pills each time, chewed and swallowed with hot water; they need not be chewed. For vexing heat of deficiency taxation, wasting-thirst and fright palpitations, take them with decocted ginseng decoction. For cough with spitting of blood, nosebleed and haematemesis, soak ophiopogon in hot water, remove the core and take them with its decoction; in these cases after meals and at bedtime. Only for blood flooding, bloody stools and the various strangury disorders take them on an empty stomach before meals. For strangury take them with plantago seed decoction.

## Notes

Longnao jisu wan: for depressed heat in the chest, lung-heat cough, nosebleed, haematemesis, flooding, bloody stools, the strangury disorders, wasting-thirst, fright palpitations and alcohol toxin, and for bad breath from stomach heat, a fishy throat from lung heat, a sweet mouth from spleen jaundice and a bitter mouth from gallbladder jaundice.
- Mainly jisu (borneol mint), made into a paste with the soaking juice of bupleurum and akebia, raw rehmannia and honey; donkey-hide gelatin and typha pollen stop bleeding; ginseng, astragalus, ophiopogon and licorice are added. "Borneol" in the name refers to borneol mint.
