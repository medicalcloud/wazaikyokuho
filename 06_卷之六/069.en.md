# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 069 曲術丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Quzhu wan (Leaven and Atractylodes Pill)

It treats sudden diarrhoea from seasonal heat, strengthens the spleen and warms the stomach, makes food relishable and improves eating, and treats damage by food and drink with glomus and oppression of the chest and diaphragm.

shenqu (medicated leaven; stir-fried)　cangzhu (black atractylodes; soaked overnight in rice-washing water, dried over a fire; equal parts, powdered)

Form the above powder into pills the size of wutong seeds with flour paste. Take thirty pills each time, at any time, swallowed with rice water.

## Notes

Quzhu wan (Wu Zhige's additions): for sudden diarrhoea in summer heat and chest glomus from food damage; strengthens the spleen and stomach and improves eating.
- Two ingredients: medicated leaven and black atractylodes.
