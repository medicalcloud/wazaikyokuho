# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 109 茴香湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huixiang tang (Fennel Drink)

It treats deficient, cold qi of the original viscus with distension and fullness of the navel and abdomen, gripping, stabbing pain and no desire for food, and all cold qi. (Another formula appears later.)

huixiang (fennel; earth removed, stir-fried; 6 jin)　chuanlianzi (toosendan fruit; washed, stir-fried)　chenpi (aged tangerine peel; 2 jin each)　gancao (licorice; stir-fried; 7 jin)　yan (salt; stir-fried; 1 jin)

Powder the above. Take 1 *qian* each time mixed like tea. Taken regularly it warms the middle, augments qi, frees the chest and diaphragm and improves eating.

## Notes

Huixiang tang ("Various drinks"): a drink for umbilical distension, gripping pain and poor appetite from deficient cold of the original viscus, and for all cold qi. Another formula of the same name follows.
- Fennel, toosendan fruit, tangerine peel, licorice and salt.
