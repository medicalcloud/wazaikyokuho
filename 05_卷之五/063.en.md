# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 063 三建湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Sanjian tang (Three Strengthening Decoction)

It treats insufficient genuine qi and long-standing deficiency of original yang with pathogenic cold attacking and surging, vexing pain of the limbs and joints, aching pain of the lower back and spine, spontaneous sweating with cold limbs, slippery diarrhea and white turbid urine; as well as wind stroke with tidal surging of drool and loss of consciousness, and yin patterns of cold damage with cold limbs and a feeble pulse. All may take it.

tianxiong (tenyu; tianxiong aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　fuzi (bushi; aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　large chuanwu (Sichuan aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed; equal parts)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 4 *qian* with two *zhan* of water and fifteen slices of fresh ginger, decoct down to eight-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm, at any time.

## Notes

Sanjian tang (bureau additions): for joint and back pain, sweating, cold limbs, diarrhea and turbid urine from long-standing deficiency of original yang with pathogenic cold, for wind stroke with drool and loss of consciousness, and for yin-pattern cold damage with cold limbs and a feeble pulse.
- Three kinds of aconite (tianxiong, fuzi, chuanwu) decocted with a large amount of ginger; all strongly toxic drugs.
