# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 038 没薬降聖丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Moyao jiangsheng dan (Myrrh Sage-Descending Elixir)

It treats blows, falls and sprains with severed sinews and broken bones, hypertonic pain and inability to bend and stretch; as well as weak nutritive and defensive qi with wandering wind received externally and damage to the channels and network vessels internally, slack sinews and bones, stabbing pain of the skin and flesh, stiffness of the shoulders and back, a weary body and little strength in the limbs.

zirantong (pyrite; calcined, quenched in vinegar twelve times, ground to powder, water-ground and dried over a fire)　chuan wutou (Sichuan aconite; raw, skin and navel removed)　gusuibu (drynaria; scorched, hairs removed)　bai shaoyao (white peony root)　moyao (myrrh; ground separately)　ruxiang (frankincense; ground separately)　danggui (Chinese angelica; washed, dried over a fire; 1 liang each)　sheng gan dihuang (dried raw rehmannia)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage; 1½ liang each)

Use all the above raw, make into a fine powder, knead with equal parts of the natural juice of fresh ginger and honey, refined together, and form pills, four to each liang. For each dose crush one pill, decoct with half a *zhan* each of water and wine and a little sappanwood down to eight-tenths, remove the sappanwood and take it hot on an empty stomach before meals.

## Notes

Moyao jiangsheng dan (Shaoxing-era additions): for hypertonic pain and inability to flex and extend after blows, sprains, torn sinews and fractures, and for slack sinews and bones, stabbing pain of skin and flesh, stiff shoulders and back, lassitude and weakness from wandering wind entering through deficient nutritive and defensive qi.
- Pyrite and drynaria join bones; myrrh, frankincense, angelica, lovage, peony and raw rehmannia move blood; Sichuan aconite stops pain. Decocted with sappanwood.
