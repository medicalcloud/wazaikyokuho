# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 051 草果飲 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Caoguo yin (Tsaoko Drink)

It treats malaria from spleen cold.

zisuye (perilla leaf)　caoguo ren (tsaoko seed)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage)　baizhi (angelica dahurica)　gaoliangjiang (lesser galangal; stir-fried)　qing jupi (green tangerine peel; white removed, stir-fried)　gancao (licorice; stir-fried)

Powder the above in equal parts. For each dose take two large *qian* with one *zhan* of water, decoct down to seven-tenths, remove the dregs and take it hot. Decoct the dregs of two doses together as well; on the day of an attack give three doses in succession, and it never fails.

## Notes

Caoguo yin (Shaoxing-era additions): for malaria from spleen cold.
- Tsaoko and galangal warm the spleen; perilla, angelica dahurica and lovage release the exterior; green tangerine peel breaks up qi. Taken repeatedly on the day of an attack.
