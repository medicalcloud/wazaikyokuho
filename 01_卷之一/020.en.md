# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 020 辰砂天麻丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Chensha tianma wan (Shinshatenmagan; Cinnabar and Gastrodia Pill)

It treats all kinds of wind with exuberant phlegm, headache and dizziness, spinning with a tendency to fall, retching and nausea, abstraction and forgetfulness, a clouded, confused spirit, painful and weary limbs, stiffness of the neck and nape, swelling and itching of the head and face, and numbness of the hands and feet. Taken regularly, it removes wind, transforms phlegm, clears the spirit and benefits the head and eyes.

chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage; 2½ liang)　shexiang (jako; musk; ground)　baizhi (byakushi; angelica dahurica; 1 liang 1 *fen* each)　chensha (cinnabar; ground and water-ground; half put into the medicine, half used for coating)　baifuzi (typhonium; blast-fried; 5 liang each)　tianma (gastrodia; sprouts removed; 10 liang)　tiannanxing (arisaema; soaked in pickled-vegetable juice, sliced and dried over a fire; 20 liang)

Powder the above and form pills the size of wutong seeds with flour paste. Take twenty pills each time with warm schizonepeta decoction, at any time.

## Notes

Chensha tianma wan: for dizziness, headache, nausea, forgetfulness, neck stiffness and numbness of the limbs from wind-phlegm.
- Gastrodia, arisaema and typhonium expel wind-phlegm; Sichuan lovage and angelica dahurica clear the head; cinnabar calms the heart.
