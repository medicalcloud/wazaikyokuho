# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 010 必勝散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bisheng san (Sure Victory Powder)

It treats reckless flow and overflow of blood in men and women with hematemesis, nosebleed, vomiting of blood and coughing of blood.

shu gan dihuang (kanjio; prepared rehmannia)　xiaoji (field thistle; used with the root)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng)　puhuang (hoo; typha pollen; lightly stir-fried)　danggui (toki; Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed)　xiongqiong (Sichuan lovage)　wumei (ubai; mume fruit; stone removed; 1 liang each)

Pound and sift the above medicines into a coarse powder. For each dose take 5 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water, decoct down to seven-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm, at any time.

## Notes

Bisheng san: for hematemesis, nosebleed, vomiting of blood and coughing of blood from reckless movement of blood.
- Field thistle, typha pollen and mume stop bleeding; prepared rehmannia, angelica and lovage (Siwu tang [Shimotsuto] without peony) and ginseng nourish blood and supplement qi.
