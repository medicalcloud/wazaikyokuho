# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 071 北亭丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Beiting wan (Sal Ammoniac Pill)

It treats all cases of weak original qi of the spleen with long-accumulated yin cold, distension, fullness and stabbing pain of the heart, abdomen, flanks and ribs, a green-yellow complexion, an emaciated weak body, lassitude with a liking for lying down, eating little and being easily damaged by food, belching and acid swallowing, retching, counterflow and nausea, deficiency rumbling in the abdomen, diarrhoea, glomus and blockage of the chest and diaphragm, inability to get food and drink down, retching, sudden turmoil, a cold body and cramps; as well as the five diaphragm disorders and five dysphagias, strings, aggregations, conglomerations and gatherings, stomach reflux with vomiting of food, and long-standing pain and dysentery.

suoshaliren (amomum seed)　hujiao (black pepper)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　houpo (magnolia bark; coarse bark removed, roasted with ginger juice)　fuzi (aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage)　danggui (Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed, chopped)　chenpi (aged tangerine peel; white pith removed)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried; 4 liang each)　qingyan (halite; ground separately)　beiting (that is, sal ammoniac; rinsed in vinegar, sand and stones removed, ground separately; 2 liang each)　baizhu (white atractylodes; ground separately; 3 liang)　wuweizi (schisandra; picked over; 1½ liang)　awei (asafoetida; dissolved in vinegar, sand and stones removed; half a liang)

Powder the above. In a silver or stone pot put good wine and 5 sheng of vinegar with 10 liang of white granulated honey; first add the three ingredients sal ammoniac, asafoetida and halite with 1 sheng of top-grade flour and decoct together until thick and sticky; then add about half a jin of the medicine powder and decoct further to the consistency of thin flour paste; gradually add medicine powder and decoct to a suitable consistency; take it off the fire, knead it into a mass with more dry medicine powder, pound a further thousand times and form pills the size of wutong seeds. Take fifteen pills each time, chewed slightly, swallowed with ginger and salt decoction; warm wine is also fine; on an empty stomach. Avoid sheep's blood and fermented soybean juice.

## Notes

Beiting wan (no layer marked): for distension and stabbing pain, poor complexion, wasting, lassitude, poor appetite, belching, acid reflux, nausea, borborygmus, diarrhoea, chest glomus, sudden turmoil and cramps from weak spleen qi and long-accumulated yin cold, and for the five diaphragm disorders and dysphagias, aggregations, stomach reflux, chronic pain and dysentery.
- Sal ammoniac (beiting, ammonium chloride), asafoetida and halite disperse accumulations; black pepper, cinnamon, aconite and dried ginger warm; amomum, magnolia bark and tangerine peel move qi; angelica, lovage, atractylodes, schisandra and licorice are added. Boiled with wine, vinegar and honey and made into pills.
