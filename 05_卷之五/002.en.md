# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 002 菟糸子丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Tusizi wan (Toshishigan; Dodder Seed Pill)

It is suitable for deficient and damaged kidney qi with the five taxations and seven damages, cramping of the lower abdomen, aching limbs, a dark sallow complexion, dry lips and mouth, dim eyes and tinnitus, palpitation and shortness of breath, fright and fear in dreams at night, a weary spirit, irregular joy and anger, sorrow and joylessness, tasteless food, lack of strength in movement, distension and fullness of the heart and abdomen, withered, slack legs and knees, slippery frequent urination, impotence, damp itching of the inner thighs, rough painful urination, blood in the urine and occasional dribbling. Taken for a long time it fills the bone marrow, rejoins severed injuries, supplements the five zang organs, removes the myriad illnesses, sharpens sight and hearing, improves the complexion, lightens the body and prolongs life. Another formula uses 3 *fen* of dragon teeth and half a liang of polygala (sprouts and core removed), boiled with black soybeans, and omits ranunculus, alisma and cistanche.

tusizi (toshishi; dodder seed; washed clean, soaked in wine)　zexie (takusha; alisma)　lurong (rokujo; deer antler velvet; hair removed, roasted with ghee)　shilongrui (ranunculus; earth removed)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　fuzi (bushi; aconite; blast-fried, skin removed; 1 liang each)　shihu (sekkoku; dendrobium; roots removed)　shu gan dihuang (kanjio; prepared rehmannia)　bai fuling (bukuryo; white poria; skin removed)　niuxi (goshitsu; achyranthes; soaked overnight in wine, dried over a fire)　xuduan (zokudan; dipsacus)　shanzhuyu (sanshuyu; cornus)　roucongrong (cistanche; soaked in wine, sliced; 3 *fen*)　wuweizi (gomishi; schisandra)　sangpiaoxiao (mantis egg case; soaked in wine, stir-fried)　xiongqiong (ligusticum)　fupenzi (rubus fruit; twigs, leaves and calyxes removed; half a liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with flour paste boiled in wine. Take twenty pills each time with warm wine or salt water, on an empty stomach. If the legs and knees are weak, take them with chaenomeles decoction, and again before the evening meal.

## Notes

Tusizi wan: for lower abdominal cramping, aching limbs, dark complexion, dim eyes, tinnitus, palpitation, nightmares, emotional instability, frequent urination, impotence, genital itching, painful urination, hematuria and dribbling from deficient, damaged kidney qi with the five taxations and seven damages.
- Dodder, deer antler velvet, cistanche, dipsacus, cornus, rubus, mantis egg case, aconite and cinnamon supplement kidney yang; rehmannia, dendrobium and schisandra nourish yin; alisma, poria and ranunculus drain damp. A variant formula is noted.
