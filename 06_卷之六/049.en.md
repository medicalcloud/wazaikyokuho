# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 049 大已寒丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Da yihan wan (Major Cold-Stopping Pill)

The patterns treated and the formula appear in the section on cold damage.

## Notes

Da yihan wan (Shaoxing-era additions): a cross-reference giving only the name; the patterns and formula are in the cold-damage section of juan 2.
