# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷下 025 論痢疾証候 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On the signs of dysentery. It always arises from improperly regulated eating and drinking that disturbs and damages the spleen and stomach, so that water and grain are mixed together, transportation and transformation go wrong, and things are retained without passing; cold and heat contend and it becomes dysentery. When cold qi contends the colour is white; when heat qi contends the colour is red. The method of treatment is always first to regulate and harmonize the spleen and stomach with warm medicines, then to treat according to the pattern. In general, when people describe their symptoms one must inquire carefully: some have tenesmus, some abdominal pain or none, going frequently to the privy, three to five or five to seven times a day, with nothing coming out. Others say they are constipated, yet have tenesmus and unceasing pain of the navel and abdomen; whether old, young or pregnant, all this is dysentery. It is not that nothing comes out; not recognizing this pattern, they only say they are constipated; one must distinguish carefully. When the patient goes to the privy and passes only two or three drops of matter, red or white or like fish brains, all these are signs of dysentery. In any dysentery with red and white discharge, or pure pus, or watery stools like a duck's, if gripping pain of the navel and abdomen comes first, dysentery follows immediately with the pain, and the pain stops after the discharge, this is accumulation dysentery: give two or three doses each of Muxiang tuiqi wan and Ganying wan, then treat according to the colour of the discharge. For red and white dysentery, give Zhuche wan, Huanglian ejiao wan, Houchang wan or Weifeng tang. For dysentery more white than red, give Houchang wan, Zhuche wan or Renshen doukou san. For dysentery less white than red, give Huanglian ejiao wan, Wanjin wan or Jinxie wan. For dysentery of pure red or fresh blood, take Huanglian ejiao wan, Diyu san or Wanjin yin decocted with ophiopogon added. Pure blood dysentery requires careful inquiry: if fresh blood is passed there is heat; if at the time of discharge slight tenesmus is felt there is heat. Dysentery of pure white with slippery leakage is a cold pattern: give Dingxiang doukou san, Helile wan or Zhuche wan. Discharge of static or purple-black blood indicates extreme deficiency cold; if it is slightly slippery at the time of discharge, give Zhuche wan, Houchang wan or Dingxiang doukou san. For red and white dysentery dragging on for many days, recurring after healing, with intermittent abdominal pain, unresponsive to various medicines, give Muxiang tuiqi wan, Bu'er wan, Zhuche wan, Houchang wan or Ganying wan. For very frequent dysentery day and night with deficient slipperiness, give Dingxiang doukou san and Sizhu san together. There is also a kind of spleen-toxin bleeding with signs like heat-toxin dysentery, but without vexation of the heart, without thirst and without liking for cold, otherwise similar: give the Baidu san of the *Wang shi bojifang*, powder sophora flower and dried alum and take with decocted mume. If there is only diarrhoea without gripping pain of the navel and abdomen, there is no accumulated stagnation, and Ganying wan need not be taken first.

## Notes

On dysentery: it arises when irregular eating damages the spleen and stomach and cold and heat contend—white with cold, red with heat. Treatment first harmonizes the spleen and stomach with warm medicines, then follows the pattern. Tenesmus with frequent scanty stools may be mistaken for constipation but is dysentery. Accumulation dysentery (pain first, relieved by passing stool) is first purged with Muxiang tuiqi wan and Ganying wan, then treated by colour; formulas are given for red and white, mostly white, mostly red, pure red or fresh blood (heat), pure white and slippery (cold), static or purple-black blood (extreme deficiency cold), chronic recurrent, frequent and slippery cases, and spleen-toxin bleeding (Baidu san of the *Wang shi bojifang*). Without accumulation, Ganying wan is unnecessary.
