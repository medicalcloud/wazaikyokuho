# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷下 031 論産前諸疾 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On the various illnesses before childbirth. In the second or third month of pregnancy, with daily vexing confusion in the chest, dizzy, heavy head and eyes, tossing about restlessly, and vomiting with vexation and oppression, this is morning sickness; simply give Renshen dingxiang san (Ninjinchokosan), Banxia fuling tang (Hangebukuryoto), Fuling wan (Bukuryogan), Xiao qixiang wan or Yao dingxiang. Bleeding in pregnancy with a restless fetus is called leaking fetus; give Antai yin, Siwu tang (Shimotsuto), Jiao'ai tang, Baosheng wan, Hupo wan (Kohakugan) or Zelan wan (Takurangan). In the final month, take Huatai san, Antai yin or Baosheng wan regularly. For difficult labor, transverse or breech presentation, or no delivery for two or three days, for a dead fetus in the abdomen, or for retained placenta, give Huaruishi san, or use Bao'an gao, one plaster as one dose made into pills the size of wutong seeds and swallowed with warm wine; if [the fetus or placenta] does not come down, take again. For parturient women whose labor does not proceed smoothly, with transverse or breech birth, take Tusui wan. When the child has come out but the placenta has not, with hard, tense, painful navel and abdomen, take Niuxi tang (Goshitsuto).

## Notes

On illnesses before childbirth: morning sickness with Renshen dingxiang san, Banxia fuling tang, Fuling wan and others; leaking fetus (bleeding, restless fetus) with Antai yin, Siwu tang, Jiao'ai tang and others; regular use of Huatai san, Antai yin or Baosheng wan in the final month; Huaruishi san or Bao'an gao for difficult labor, dead fetus or retained placenta; Tusui wan (perhaps Cuisheng dan) for transverse or breech birth; Niuxi tang for retained placenta.
