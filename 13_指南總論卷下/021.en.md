# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷下 021 論眼目諸証候 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On the various signs of the eyes. When the eyes are dim and dark and things cannot be seen clearly, without swelling, pain or redness and without nebulae or membranes, these are the signs of internal obstruction. If black flowers are seen or there are cold tears, the spleen and kidneys are both deficient; cooling medicines must not be given at once; simply give Mingyan dihuang wan, Jujing wan, Zhujing wan, Huanjing wan, Jinjiu wan or Jueming wan. For ordinary wind-heat in the liver with itchy, rough eyes, dimness with tears and inability to see, simply give Pusa san, Jueming san, Huanjing wan, Boyun wan, Juhua san or Mimenghua san. For itchy eyes from kidney wind, [use] Sisheng san, Qinpi san or Tangpao san, wash with Zijin gao and apply Chunxue gao. For external obstruction with nebulae and membranes invading the eye and long-standing proliferating flesh, give Zhujing wan or Jujing wan on an empty stomach and Jueming wan, Boyun san, Pusa san, Mimenghua san or Michuan yanggan wan after meals, apply Bomo gao, and after a while wash with Zijin gao, or [apply] Chunxue gao. For sudden red eyes with dim, rough, painful eyes, photophobia and hot tears, do not apply or wash; simply give Huanjing wan, Xixin san, Xigan san, Bazheng san, Ganlu yin, Pusa san or Boyun san. For unbearable pain of the eye, give Qianghuo san or Huanjing wan, two pills as one dose, and it stops at once; it also treats head-wind pain with divine effect. For red, festering lids and canthi, unclear vision, dimness and tearing, there is wind in the liver: give Pusa san, Boyun san, Xigan san, Juhua san, Mimenghua san or Jueming san, then wash with Zijin gao and apply Chunxue gao.

## Notes

On eye disorders: internal obstruction (poor vision without swelling, pain, redness or nebulae; floaters, cold tears) is spleen-kidney deficiency—no cooling medicines; Mingyan dihuang wan, Jujing wan, Zhujing wan and others; liver wind-heat (itching, roughness, tears) with Pusa san, Jueming san and others; kidney-wind itching with Sisheng san, Qinpi san, Tangpao san, Zijin gao and Chunxue gao; external obstruction with nebulae and pterygium with combined oral treatment and topical washes and applications; sudden red eyes with oral medicines only (no washing or applications); severe eye pain with Qianghuo san or Huanjing wan; festering lids with Pusa san and others.
- "Jinjiu wan" reads "Mianjiu wan" in the base text but probably refers to the Jinjiu wan of juan 7, no. 001 (the base text's character is not altered).
