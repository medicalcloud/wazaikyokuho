# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 064 水浸丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shuijin dan (Water-Soaked Elixir)

It is suitable for latent summerheat damaged by cold, with disharmony of cold and heat, sudden turmoil with vomiting and diarrhoea, and a dry mouth with vexing thirst.

badou (croton seeds; 25 large ones, skin and membranes removed, ground and all the oil removed until like powder)　huangdan (minium; stir-fried, ground, sieved; take 1 liang 1 *fen*)

Grind the above together evenly, melt yellow beeswax into liquid, and separately form pills the size of wutong seeds. Take five pills each time: soak them in water for a while, then swallow them with freshly drawn water, at any time.

## Notes

Shuijin dan (bureau additions): for vomiting, diarrhoea and thirst in sudden turmoil when latent summerheat is combined with cold.
- Defatted croton seed (croton frost) and minium made into pills with beeswax, soaked in water before swallowing; both croton and minium are highly toxic.
