# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 084 肉豆蔻丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Roudoukou wan (Nutmeg Pill)

It treats qi diarrhea and weak spleen and stomach qi with reduced eating.

helile pi (chebule skin)　longgu (ryukotsu; dragon bone)　muxiang (mokko; costus root; 3 *fen* each)　dingxiang (clove; 3 liang)　roudoukou ren (nutmeg)　suosharen (amomum; 1 liang each)　chishizhi (shakusekishi; red halloysite)　baifan hui (calcined alum; half a liang each)

Pound and sieve the above medicines into a powder, knead with millet gruel into pills the size of wutong seeds. Take twenty pills each time with rice gruel, at any time.

## Notes

Roudoukou wan (Wu Zhige's additions): for qi diarrhea and poor appetite from weak spleen-stomach qi.
- Nutmeg, chebule, dragon bone, red halloysite and calcined alum secure the intestines and stop diarrhea; clove, costus and amomum warm and move qi.
