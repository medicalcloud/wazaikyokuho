# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 059 羊肉丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yangrou wan (Yonikugan; Mutton Pill)

It treats deficient, exhausted true yang and damaged, exhausted lower origin with scorched, withered helices of the ears, a dark sallow complexion, heavy lower back and weak legs, and declining, feeble original qi. Taken regularly it secures the true, supplements qi, augments essence and preserves the complexion.

chuanlianzi (toosendan fruit; stir-fried)　xuduan (zokudan; dipsacus; stir-fried, threads removed)　fuling (bukuryo; poria)　huixiang (fennel)　buguzhi (psoralea; stir-fried)　fuzi (bushi; aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　huluba (fenugreek; lightly stir-fried; 3 liang each)　shanyao (Chinese yam; stir-fried)　taoren (tonin; peach kernel; stir-fried with bran, skin and tips removed, ground separately)　xingren (kyonin; apricot kernel; stir-fried with bran, skin and tips removed, ground separately)

Powder the above; boil 4 liang of lean mutton in wine until soft, grind extremely fine, add flour and boil a paste, and form pills the size of wutong seeds. Take thirty to fifty pills on an empty stomach with salt water or warm wine as preferred.

## Notes

Yangrou wan (bureau additions): for withered ear helices, dark complexion, heavy back, weak legs and declining original qi from deficient true yang and exhausted lower origin.
- Aconite, psoralea, fenugreek, fennel and dipsacus warm kidney yang; toosendan moves qi; poria, Chinese yam, peach and apricot kernels are added; the pills are made with a mutton paste. The base text gives no amounts for Chinese yam, peach kernel and apricot kernel.
