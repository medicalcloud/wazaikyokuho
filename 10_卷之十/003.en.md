# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 003 八珍丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bazhen dan (Eight Treasures Elixir)

It treats fright wind with vigorous fever in children, a clouded spirit, vomiting of phlegm and drool, fright palpitations and abstraction, or convulsions with eyes turned upward.

gancao (kanzo; licorice; stir-fried)　tianma (gastrodia; rhizome head removed)　zhusha (cinnabar; ground and water-ground)　tiannanxing (arisaema; processed with ox bile; 5 liang each)　niuhuang (goo; bovine bezoar; ground; 1 *fen*)　nifen (calomel; ground)　xionghuang (yuo; realgar; water-ground; 1 liang 1 *fen* each)　tianjiangzi (cnidocampa cocoon; lightly stir-fried; 350)　yinbo (silver leaf; seventy leaves, for coating)

Make the above into a fine powder, mix in the ground medicines evenly, form pills the size of peas with refined honey and coat them with silver leaf. A one-year-old takes one pill dissolved in mint decoction. If the signs do not recede, it may be given again, adjusted for the child's size; after feeding.

## Notes

Bazhen dan: for fright wind with high fever, clouded spirit, vomiting of phlegm, fright palpitations, abstraction, convulsions and upward gaze in children.
- Bile-processed arisaema, gastrodia and cnidocampa cocoon dispel wind and phlegm; bovine bezoar, cinnabar, realgar, calomel and silver leaf clear heat and settle; licorice is added.
