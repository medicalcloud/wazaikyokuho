# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之七 002 駐景丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zhujing wan (Vision-Preserving Pill)

It treats dual deficiency of the liver and kidneys with eyes constantly dim and dark, frequent sight of black flowers, or the formation of obstructions and nebulae, unclear vision and tearing against the wind. Taken for a long time it supplements the liver and kidneys and increases the strength of the eyes.

cheqianzi (plantago seed)　shu gan dihuang (prepared rehmannia; washed clean, steamed with wine, dried over a fire; 3 liang each)　tusizi (dodder seed; soaked in wine, ground separately into powder; 5 liang)

Powder the above and form pills the size of wutong seeds with refined honey. Take thirty pills each time with warm wine, on an empty stomach and before the evening meal, twice a day.

## Notes

Zhujing wan: for dim vision, floaters, obstructions, poor sight and tearing in the wind from dual deficiency of the liver and kidneys; taken long-term said to strengthen vision.
- Three ingredients: dodder seed and prepared rehmannia supplement the liver and kidneys; plantago seed brightens the eyes.
