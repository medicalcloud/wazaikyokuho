# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 046 赤石脂散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Chishizhi san (Red Halloysite Powder)

It treats weak, deficient intestines and stomach with undigested water and grain, pouring diarrhoea and thunderous rumbling in the abdomen; as well as disharmony of cold and heat with red and white dysentery, slippery intestines and abdominal pain, very frequent stools, deficiency fullness of the flanks and ribs, glomus and oppression of the chest and diaphragm, weary limbs and reduced eating.

chi shizhi (red halloysite; calcined)　gancao (licorice; scorched; 5 liang each)　suoshaliren (amomum seed; 20 liang)　roudoukou (nutmeg; wrapped in dough and roasted in embers until cooked; 40 liang)

Powder the above. Take 2 *qian* each time mixed in warm millet water, before meals on an empty stomach.

## Notes

Chishizhi san: for indigestion, pouring diarrhoea and borborygmus from weak intestines and stomach, and for red and white dysentery, abdominal pain, frequent stools, flank fullness, chest glomus, fatigue and poor appetite from disharmony of cold and heat.
- Nutmeg and red halloysite astringe the intestines; amomum moves qi; licorice is added.
