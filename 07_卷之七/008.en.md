# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之七 008 菩薩散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Pusa san (Bodhisattva Powder)

It treats men and women with wind qi attacking and pouring in, both eyes dim and dark, discharge and tears with aversion to light, swollen, itchy lids and canthi, occasional red pain, tinnitus and dizziness.

jingjie sui (schizonepeta spikes; 1½ liang)　cangzhu (black atractylodes; soaked overnight in rice-washing water, skin removed, chopped, stir-fried)　bai jili (tribulus; stir-fried)　fangfeng (saposhnikovia; chopped, stir-fried; 2 liang each)　gancao (licorice; stir-fried; 1 liang)

Make all the above into a fine powder. At any time, add a little salt and take one large *qian* mixed in boiling water or wine; marvellous.

## Notes

Pusa san (Shaoxing-era additions): for dim vision, discharge, tearing, photophobia, swollen, itchy lids and canthi, red pain, tinnitus and dizziness from wind qi attacking.
- Five ingredients: schizonepeta, saposhnikovia, black atractylodes and tribulus disperse wind-damp; licorice is added.
