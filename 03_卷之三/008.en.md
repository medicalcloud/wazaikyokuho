# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 008 養脾丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yangpi wan (Spleen-Nourishing Pill)

It treats deficiency cold of the spleen and stomach with wringing pain of the heart and abdomen, fullness and oppression of the chest and diaphragm, deficiency distension of the flanks and ribs, retching and nausea, belching and acid swallowing, diarrhoea with borborygmus, undigested grain, weary limbs and no desire for food.

damainie (barley sprouts; stir-fried)　bai fuling (white poria; skin removed)　renshen (ginseng; rhizome head removed; 1 jin each)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried)　suosha (amomum; skin removed; 2 jin each)　baizhu (white atractylodes; half a jin)　gancao (licorice; chopped and scorched; 1½ jin)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills with refined honey, eight pills to each liang. Take one pill each time, chewed thoroughly, washed down with ginger decoction, before meals. This medicine nourishes the stomach and improves eating.

## Notes

Yangpi wan: for abdominal pain, chest oppression, flank distension, nausea, acid reflux, diarrhoea, indigestion, lassitude and poor appetite from spleen-stomach deficiency cold.
- Lizhong wan (ginseng, atractylodes, dried ginger, licorice) plus poria, amomum and barley sprouts.
