# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之四 023 温中化痰丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wenzhong huatan wan (Middle-Warming Phlegm-Transforming Pill)

It treats lodged phlegm and retained retained fluid with fullness and oppression of the chest and diaphragm, dizziness and spinning eyes, a liking for lying down with reduced eating, cough with vomiting, shortness of breath and nausea. It is also suitable for excessive drinking of wine, or drinking water without limit, or excessive damage from raw and cold foods, with much phlegm and drool, retching and nausea.

qingpi (green tangerine peel; white removed)　liangjiang (galangal; rhizome head removed, stir-fried)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; stir-fried)　chenpi (chinpi; aged tangerine peel; white pith removed; 5 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with flour paste made with vinegar. Take thirty to fifty pills each time with hot water or other drink as preferred, at any time.

## Notes

Wenzhong huatan wan (Baoqing-era additions): for chest oppression, dizziness, poor appetite, cough, vomiting, shortness of breath and nausea from lodged phlegm and retained retained fluid, and for phlegm after excessive drinking or raw and cold foods.
- Galangal and dried ginger warm the middle; the tangerine peels move qi and transform phlegm. Another formula of the same name appears later.
