# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 057 白朮散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Baizhu san (Byakujutsusan; White Atractylodes Powder)

It treats cold damage with disharmony of qi and vessels, aversion to cold with vigorous fever, nasal congestion with oppression of the head, thick sticky mucus and spittle, and congested phlegm-cough; or exposure to wind-damp with aversion to cold, fever and joint pain; or summer heat stroke with vomiting and dizziness; and improper care after a major illness leading to relapse from food or overwork, with the illness returning as at first. It also treats the five taxations and seven damages with qi deficiency and dizziness, an abstracted spirit, restless sleep, weary limbs, tidal fever and night sweats, deficient and damaged spleen and stomach, a withered yellow complexion, food without relish, vomiting of sour water, slippery diarrhea, empty rumbling in the abdomen, stomach reflux with vomiting, wringing pain of the heart and abdomen, and long-standing malaria and dysentery; as well as diaphragm qi with obstruction of the throat, rising qi with rapid dyspnea and restlessness sitting or lying; or damage from food with stuffiness and oppression of the chest and diaphragm and distension of the abdomen and flanks; women before and after childbirth with disharmony of blood and qi; and vomiting and diarrhea of sudden turmoil and qi syncope with loss of consciousness. Taken regularly it wards off the improper qi of the four seasons and the miasmic pestilence of mountain mists; its divine effect cannot be fully described.

shanyao (Chinese yam)　jiegeng (kikyo; platycodon)　fuling (bukuryo; poria; skin removed)　gancao (kanzo; licorice)　baizhi (byakushi; angelica dahurica)　chenpi (chinpi; aged tangerine peel; white pith removed)　qingpi (green tangerine peel; white pith removed)　xiangfuzi (cyperus; 3 liang each)　baizhu (byakujutsu; white atractylodes; 4 liang)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried; 2 liang)

Powder the above. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one *zhan* of water, three slices of ginger, one jujube, one slice of dried chaenomeles and three perilla leaves, decoct down to seven-tenths and take it before meals. For vomiting and diarrhea, decoct it with salted plum; for dyspnea, with mulberry bark and apricot kernel; for relapse of cold damage from overwork, add mint; for diaphragm qi, add three *cun* of akebia and a little musk; for vomiting from summer heat stroke, add elsholtzia; for disharmony of blood and qi before or after childbirth, decoct it with schizonepeta; for sudden turmoil, with agastache; for qi syncope, mix it in salt water.

## Notes

Baizhu san (bureau additions): a broadly used formula for chills, fever and phlegm-cough in cold damage, wind-damp joint pain, summer heat, relapse after illness, spleen-stomach weakness with the five taxations and seven damages, diaphragm qi, food damage, women before and after childbirth, sudden turmoil and qi syncope.
- White atractylodes, Chinese yam, poria, licorice and dried ginger supplement and warm the spleen and stomach; tangerine peel, green tangerine peel, cyperus and platycodon smooth qi; angelica dahurica releases the exterior; additions vary with the symptoms.
