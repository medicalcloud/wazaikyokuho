# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 075 半夏散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Banxia san (Hangesan; Pinellia Powder)

It treats cough with counterflow and qi ascent in children, phlegm congesting the heart and chest, and no desire for milk or food.

ziwan (aster; shoots removed, washed clean)　wuweizi (gomishi; schisandra; picked clean)　banxia (hange; pinellia; steeped seven times in hot water)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried; 5 liang each)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　xixin (saishin; asarum; shoots removed; 2½ liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder. For a three-year-old, take 1 *qian* each time with one *zhan* of water and one slice of fresh ginger, decoct down to five-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm, at any time, adjusted for the child's size.

## Notes

Banxia san (Wu Zhige's additions): for cough with counterflow, qi ascent, phlegm congestion of the chest and poor feeding in children.
- Pinellia and aster remove phlegm and stop cough; asarum and cinnamon warm; schisandra astringes; licorice is added.
