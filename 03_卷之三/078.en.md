# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 078 和気散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Heqi san (Qi-Harmonizing Powder)

It treats disharmony of the spleen and stomach with stagnant qi in the middle duct, chronic cold and lodged rheum accumulating without dispersing, distension and fullness of the heart and abdomen, vomiting of sour water, spleen pain and diarrhoea, disordered viscera and bowels and reduced eating. It is suitable for all qi disorders of men and women.

xiangfuzi (cyperus; stir-fried, hairs removed)　chenpi (aged tangerine peel; white pith removed)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　liangjiang (galangal; rhizome head removed)　qingpi (green tangerine peel; white removed)　gancao (licorice; scorched)　huixiang (fennel; stir-fried)　cangzhu (black atractylodes; soaked in rice-washing water; 1 liang each)　jiegeng (platycodon; rhizome head removed; 3 liang)

Pound the above medicines into a fine powder. Take 2 *qian* each time with a little salt stirred into boiling water, or mixed in salted wine, at any time. Taken regularly it warms the spleen and stomach and improves eating.

## Notes

Heqi san (Wu Zhige's additions): for abdominal fullness, vomiting of sour water, spleen pain, diarrhoea and poor appetite from spleen-stomach disharmony, middle-duct qi stagnation, chronic cold and lodged rheum, and for all qi disorders.
- Cyperus and the tangerine peels move qi; cinnamon, galangal and fennel warm; black atractylodes removes damp; platycodon is used in a larger amount.
