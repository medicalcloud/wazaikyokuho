# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 013 朮附湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zhufu tang (Atractylodes and Aconite Decoction)

It treats wind and damp contending, with painful, vexing body, inability to turn over, no vomiting and no thirst, hard stools and spontaneously free urination; and wind deficiency with dizziness and heaviness of the head and eyes, in severe cases with inability to taste food. This medicine warms the flesh, supplements the middle, assists yang qi and stops spontaneous sweating.

gancao (licorice; stir-fried; 2 liang)　baizhu (white atractylodes; 4 liang)　fuzi (aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed, thinly sliced; 1½ liang)

Pound the atractylodes and licorice into a coarse powder and mix in the aconite evenly. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water, five slices of fresh ginger and one jujube broken open, decoct together down to one *zhan*, remove the dregs and take it warm, before meals.

## Notes

Zhufu tang: for body pain with inability to turn over, hard stools and free urination when wind and damp contend (the pattern of the decoction "minus cinnamon plus atractylodes" in *Shanghan lun* clause 174), and for dizziness and heavy head from wind deficiency.
- Like Baizhu fuzi tang of the *Jingui yaolüe*: atractylodes, aconite and licorice with fresh ginger and jujube.
