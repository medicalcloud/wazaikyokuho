# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 004 何首烏丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Heshouwu wan (Fleeceflower Root Pill)

It supplements and warms the bowels and viscera, expels wind and cold, benefits the lower back and knees, strengthens sinews and bones, blackens the beard and hair and preserves the countenance.

heshouwu (fleeceflower root; 3 jin, cut into pieces the size of go stones with a copper or bamboo knife and pounded with a wooden pestle and mortar)　niuxi (achyranthes; sprouts removed, chopped; 1 jin)

For these medicines, wash 1 *dou* of black soybeans clean and dry them in the sun. Using a steamer, first spread a thin layer of beans on the bottom, then a thin layer of fleeceflower root, then beans again, then a thin layer of achyranthes; layer them thus until the medicines and beans are used up; set it over a cauldron and steam until the beans are cooked. Discard the black beans, take the medicines and dry them in the sun; then steam again with fresh beans. Do this three times, discard the beans, take the medicines, and when dry powder them and form pills the size of wutong seeds with steamed jujube flesh. Take thirty pills each time with warm wine, before meals. Avoid radish, scallion and garlic. This medicine is warm in nature and non-toxic; taken for a long time it lightens the body, prolongs life and prevents ageing.

## Notes

Heshouwu wan: supplements and warms the viscera, expels wind-cold, strengthens the lower back, knees, sinews and bones, blackens grey hair and preserves youth.
- Fleeceflower root and achyranthes are steamed three times layered with black soybeans (the black-bean steaming of fleeceflower root). Avoid radish, scallion and garlic.
