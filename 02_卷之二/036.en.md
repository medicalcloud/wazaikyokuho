# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 036 柴胡升麻湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Chaihu shengma tang (Saikoshomato; Bupleurum and Cimicifuga Decoction)

It treats seasonal pestilence with vigorous fever and aversion to wind, headache and body pain, nasal congestion and dry throat, vexing fullness of the heart and chest, alternating fever and chills, cough with exuberant phlegm, and thick sticky mucus and spittle.

chaihu (saiko; bupleurum; rhizome head removed)　qianhu (peucedanum; rhizome head removed)　gange (dried pueraria)　shigao (sekko; gypsum; calcined)　chi shaoyao (shakuyaku; red peony root; 10 liang each)　shengma (shoma; cimicifuga; 5 liang)　jingjie (schizonepeta; stalks removed; 7½ liang)　huangqin (ogon; scutellaria; coarse bark removed)　sangbaipi (mulberry root bark; 6½ liang each)

Cut the above into pieces. For each dose take three large *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water, three slices of fresh ginger and a dozen or so fermented soybeans, decoct together down to one *zhan*, remove the dregs and take it fairly hot, at any time. For children adjust further according to size.

## Notes

Chaihu shengma tang (Baoqing-era additions): almost the same indications and ingredients as the earlier Chaihu shigao san (Saikosekkosan) (juan 2, no. 004), differing in doses and in being cut and decocted.
