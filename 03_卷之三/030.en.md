# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 030 小独聖丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xiao dusheng wan (Minor Unique Sage Pill)

It treats disharmony of the spleen and stomach with frequent damage from food, stabbing pain of the heart and abdomen, retching and nausea, choking stuffiness with acid swallowing, dry belching smelling of food, distension and oppression of the abdomen and flanks, and no desire for food.

badou (hazu; croton seeds; weigh half a liang with the skin, remove skin, core and membranes, stir-fry until cooked, obtain 3 *qian* and grind)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed; 1 jin)　naosha (sal ammoniac; ground and water-ground; 1 liang)　banxia (hange; pinellia; washed seven times in hot water)　dingpi (clove bark; imported by ship)　wumei (ubai; smoked plum; stones removed)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried)　danggui (toki; Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed)　sanleng (sparganium; roasted and crushed; 4 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder, mix in the croton and sal ammoniac evenly, and form pills the size of hemp seeds with leaven paste boiled in water. Take three to five pills each time with warm water, after meals. Taken regularly it transforms stagnant qi, frees the chest and diaphragm, stops counterflow and digests food.

## Notes

Xiao dusheng wan: for stabbing heart and abdominal pain, nausea, acid reflux, belching, abdominal and flank distension and poor appetite from spleen-stomach disharmony after food damage.
- Croton, sal ammoniac and sparganium attack accumulations; cinnamon, dried ginger and clove bark warm; pinellia, smoked plum and angelica regulate.
