# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之七 009 撥雲散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Boyun san (Cloud-Dispelling Powder)

It treats all of the following in men and women: wind toxin attacking upward with dim, dark eyes, nebulae and membranes covering the eye, fear of the sun and aversion to light, much hot tearing, gritty roughness making the eyes hard to open, itchy, red, painful orbits, red, festering lids and canthi, and static flesh invading the eye; in short, all eye diseases from wind toxin.

qianghuo (notopterygium)　fangfeng (saposhnikovia)　chaihu (bupleurum)　gancao (licorice; stir-fried; 1 jin each)

Powder the above. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water, decoct down to seven-tenths and take it after meals and at bedtime; it may also be mixed in mint tea or taken with chrysanthemum-shoot decoction. Avoid salted and preserved foods, pickled fish and sauces, wet noodles, roasted and grilled food, wind-provoking things and toxic things.

## Notes

Boyun san (Shaoxing-era additions): for dim vision, nebulae, photophobia, hot tears, difficulty opening the eyes, itchy, red, painful orbits, festering lids and static flesh invading the eye (pterygium and the like), and all eye diseases from wind toxin.
- Four ingredients (notopterygium, saposhnikovia, bupleurum, licorice) to disperse wind ("dispelling the clouds").
