# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之四 018 二陳湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Erchen tang (Nichinto; Two Aged [Ingredients] Decoction)

It treats illness caused by phlegm-fluid retention: vomiting and nausea, or dizziness and palpitation, or discomfort of the middle duct, or outbreaks of chills and fever, or disharmony of the spleen and stomach from eating raw and cold foods.

banxia (hange; pinellia; washed seven times in hot water)　juhong (red tangerine peel; 5 liang each)　bai fuling (bukuryo; white poria; 3 liang)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried; 1½ liang)

Cut the above into pieces. For each dose take 4 *qian* with one *qian* [probably an error for *zhan*] of water, seven slices of fresh ginger and one smoked plum, decoct together down to six-tenths, remove the dregs and take it hot, at any time.

## Notes

Erchen tang (from here the layer of Shaoxing-era additions): the basic phlegm-transforming formula, for vomiting, nausea, dizziness, palpitation, epigastric discomfort and chills and fever from phlegm-fluid retention, and for spleen-stomach disharmony from raw and cold foods. It is still widely used and became the foundation of many formulas.
- Pinellia, red tangerine peel, poria and licorice, decocted with fresh ginger and smoked plum; pinellia and tangerine peel are preferred aged, hence "two aged".
- The base text has "one *qian* of water", probably an error for "one *zhan*" (not verified).
