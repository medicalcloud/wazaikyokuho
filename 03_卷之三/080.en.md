# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 080 蓬煎丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Pengjian wan (Zedoary Syrup Pill)

It treats weak spleen and stomach with long-standing damage and stagnation, glomus of qi in the middle duct, distension of the heart and abdomen, hardness below the flanks, blockage in the chest, choked belching, vomiting of phlegm and water and no desire for food; or drawing pain of the heart and abdomen with stabbing, urgent qi; and food concretions, wine aggregations, blood conglomerations and qi lumps that flare up with pain, retching of sour water, a yellow face and wasted flesh, a weary spirit and little strength in the limbs. It also treats women's disordered blood qi with wringing pain of the lower abdomen.

zhuyi (pig pancreas; one)　jing sanleng (sparganium)　peng'ezhu (zedoary; these two boiled through in vinegar, sliced, dried over a fire and powdered; 4 liang each)

These two are simmered into a paste together with the pig pancreas and sal ammoniac.

chuanlianzi (toosendan fruit; stones removed)　shanyao (Chinese yam)　binglang (areca)　zhiqiao (bitter orange; pulp removed, stir-fried with bran)　huixiang (fennel; stir-fried)　fuzi (aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed; 3 liang each)　naosha (sal ammoniac; half a liang)

Mill the above medicines into a fine powder, add the pig-pancreas and sal-ammoniac paste, and form pills the size of wutong seeds with vinegar paste. Take ten to fifteen pills each time with ginger decoction; women take them with weak vinegar decoction; at any time; adjust further according to deficiency or repletion. Taken regularly it smooths qi, loosens the middle, disperses accumulated stagnation and transforms phlegm-rheum.

## Notes

Pengjian wan (Wu Zhige's additions): for middle-duct glomus, abdominal distension, hardness below the flanks, vomiting and poor appetite from weak spleen and stomach with long-standing stagnation, for painful food concretions, wine aggregations, blood conglomerations and qi lumps, and for women's blood qi and lower abdominal pain.
- Vinegar-boiled sparganium and zedoary simmered into a paste with pig pancreas and sal ammoniac, combined with toosendan, areca, bitter orange, fennel, aconite and Chinese yam.
