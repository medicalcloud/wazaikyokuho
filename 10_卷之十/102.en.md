# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 102 二宜湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Eryi tang (Twice-Suitable Drink)

It treats summer heat exposure with drinking, disharmony of cold and heat, diarrhea with much thirst, vexation and oppression of the heart and abdomen, red and white dysentery, and abdominal pain with tenesmus.

guixin (cinnamon heart; 4 jin 4 liang)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; stir-fried with sand; 4 jin)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; stir-fried with sand; 30 jin)　xingren (kyonin; apricot kernel; skin and tips removed, stir-fried with sand; 4 jin 4 liang, ground separately)

Powder the above. Take 1 *qian* each time mixed with boiling water. For vexing thirst from summer heat damage, mix it in fresh water. At any time.

## Notes

Eryi tang ("Various drinks"): a drink for diarrhea, thirst, abdominal oppression, red and white dysentery, pain and tenesmus from disharmony of cold and heat after drinking in summer heat; for summer heat thirst it is taken in cold water ("suitable both ways", perhaps).
- Cinnamon heart, dried ginger, licorice and apricot kernel.
