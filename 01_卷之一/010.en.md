# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 010 婁金丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Loujin wan (Loujin Pill)

It treats all kinds of wind with an unsettled spirit and mind, abstraction that comes and goes, stiff tongue and sluggish speech, palpitation and vexation, deviation of the mouth and eyes, and limp, dragging hands and feet; also wind-deficiency dizziness with clouding and pain of the head and eyes; or spinning and falling down stiff, surging drool and convulsions, and sudden strike by acute wind with loss of consciousness. It treats all children's fright wind and epilepsies as well.

ganju (chrysanthemum; earth removed; 4 liang)　huangqi (astragalus; rhizome head removed)　gaoben (Chinese lovage; washed)　bai jiangcan (silkworm; silk and mouth removed, scorched)　gancao (licorice; scorched)　qianghuo (notopterygium; sprouts removed)　mahuang (ephedra; roots and nodes removed)　fuling (poria; skin removed)　shaoyao (peony root)　xijiao (rhinoceros horn; shaved; 2 liang each)　baizhi (angelica dahurica; washed)　nanxing (arisaema; powdered, kneaded into cakes with ox bile and dried in the shade)　xixin (asarum; sprouts removed, washed and dried over a fire)　renshen (ginseng; rhizome head removed)　fangfeng (saposhnikovia; rhizome head removed)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage; 1½ liang each)　longnao (borneol; ground)　niuhuang (bezoar; ground)　shexiang (musk; ground)　baifuzi (typhonium; blast-fried)　tianzhuhuang (tabasheer; 1 liang each)　baihuashe (pit viper; soaked in wine, skin and bones removed, roasted)　tianma (gastrodia; sprouts removed; 3 liang each)　fresh rehmannia juice (5 *sheng*; add 1 liang honey, 2 *sheng* wine and 1½ liang ghee, simmer over a gentle fire into a paste and cool)　gold leaf (100 leaves, for coating)

Make the above into a fine powder, knead with the rehmannia paste, make fifty pills from each liang and coat with gold leaf. Take one pill each time, chewed thoroughly, with warm wine. For wind-strike with surging drool, loss of speech and severe clouded obstruction, increase to three pills, dissolve them in half a *zhan* of fresh mint juice and warm wine together and pour it in. For regular use, chew one pill with strongly decocted ginseng decoction; mint decoction is also fine. Children take a piece the size of a soap-pod seed dissolved in mint decoction.

## Notes

Loujin wan: for disturbed consciousness, speech disorders, facial deviation and paralysis after wind-strike, wind-deficiency dizziness, sudden collapse and convulsions, and children's fright wind.
- Wind-dispelling drugs (chrysanthemum, notopterygium, saposhnikovia, angelica dahurica, asarum, Sichuan lovage), phlegm-transforming drugs (arisaema, typhonium, tabasheer) and orifice-opening drugs (borneol, bezoar, musk, rhinoceros horn) are made into pills with a rehmannia-juice paste.
- The Siku quanshu edition differs in composition, e.g. it has arisaema as a separate entry and includes antelope horn (see the variants).
