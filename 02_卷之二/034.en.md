# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 034 消暑丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xiaoshu wan (Summerheat-Dispersing Pill)

It treats summer heat damage with fever and headache.

banxia (hange; pinellia; boiled dry in 5 *sheng* of vinegar)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; raw)　fuling (bukuryo; poria; skin removed; half a jin each)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with a thin paste made of ginger juice. Take fifty pills each time with water. The *Yijian fang* says: When compounding this medicine, one must boil the pinellia in good vinegar and make the paste with ginger juice, not letting it touch unboiled water; compounded with care, it is divinely effective. For summer heat stroke, the patient revives as soon as the medicine goes down; for summer heat damage with fever and headache, it is especially effective. Taken regularly in summer it stops thirst and frees urination, and even drinking much water does no harm. No summer heat medicine equals it. If phlegm-fluid retention is lodged and accumulated, swallow it with ginger decoction. Once summer has begun, one must not be without it.

## Notes

Xiaoshu wan (Shaoxing-era additions): for fever and headache from summer heat damage and for summer heat stroke.
- Three ingredients: vinegar-boiled pinellia, raw licorice and poria. It quotes the *Yijian fang* (Wang Shuo) on the preparation (avoid unboiled water) and its value as a summer household medicine.
