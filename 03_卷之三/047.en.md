# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 047 酒癥丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jiuzheng wan (Wine Concretion Pill)

It treats excessive drinking with spinning head and nausea and unceasing vomiting, and wine accumulation lodged in the stomach so that one vomits as soon as one drinks, turning into an aggregation over time.

xionghuang (yuo; realgar; six pieces the size of soap-pod seeds)　badou (hazu; croton seeds; skin not removed, oil not pressed out)　xieshao (scorpion tails; 15 each)

Grind these three together finely, add white flour to a total weight of 5½ liang, knead with drops of water into pieces the size of peas, let them dry a little, put them into bran and stir-fry together until fragrant, then drop one pill into water: if the pill floats on the water, remove the bran [it is done]. Take two pills each time with warm wine, after meals. For ordinary damage by wine, take one pill with tea or wine as preferred.

## Notes

Jiuzheng wan (Shaoxing-era additions): for dizziness, nausea and incessant vomiting from excessive drinking, and for wine aggregation in which one vomits as soon as one drinks.
- Realgar, croton and scorpion tails made into pills with white flour and stir-fried in bran; readiness is tested by whether a pill floats in water.
