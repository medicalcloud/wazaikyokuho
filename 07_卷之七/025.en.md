# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之七 025 竜石散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Longshi san (Borneol and Calcite Powder)

It treats congested toxin of the upper diaphragm in adults and children with sores of the mouth and tongue, swelling and blockage of the throat, pain, obstruction and oppression. Each time sprinkle a little on the affected place and swallow the saliva. For children's sores and rashes with toxic qi attacking the mouth and teeth, first sweep with Wufu huadu dan, then sprinkle this medicine on, with immediate effect.

zhusha (cinnabar; ground and water-ground; 2½ liang)　hanshuishi (kansuiseki; calcite; burned red-hot; 2 jin)　sheng naozi (raw borneol; ground; 2½ *qian*)

Powder the above. Use it five to seven times a day; sprinkling it on at bedtime is excellent.

## Notes

Longshi san (the first formula of juan 7, "Treating the throat, mouth and teeth"): for mouth sores, swollen, blocked and painful throat from congested toxin of the upper diaphragm, and for toxin from children's pox or rashes attacking the mouth and teeth.
- A powder of calcite, cinnabar and borneol sprinkled on the affected place. Wufu huadu dan is perhaps the children's formula in juan 10 (not verified).
