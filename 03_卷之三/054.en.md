# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 054 参苓白朮散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shen ling baizhu san (Ginseng, Poria and White Atractylodes Powder)

It treats weak spleen and stomach with inability to eat, much fatigue and little strength, fullness of the middle with choking glomus, palpitation and panting, vomiting and diarrhoea, as well as coughing and hiccough in cold damage. This medicine is balanced and not hot; taken for a long time it nourishes qi and nurtures the spirit, awakens the spleen and brightens the complexion, smooths the upright and wards off evil.

lianzi rou (lotus seed; skin removed)　yiyiren (coix seed)　suoshaner (amomum)　jiegeng (platycodon; stir-fried until deep yellow; 1 jin each)　bai biandou (hyacinth bean; soaked in ginger juice, skin removed, lightly stir-fried; 1½ jin)　bai fuling (white poria)　renshen (ginseng; rhizome head removed)　gancao (licorice; stir-fried)　baizhu (white atractylodes)　shanyao (Chinese yam; 2 jin each)

Make the above into a fine powder. Take 2 *qian* each time mixed in jujube decoction. Children take amounts adjusted to their age.

## Notes

Shen ling baizhu san (Shaoxing-era additions): for poor appetite, lassitude, abdominal fullness, palpitation, shortness of breath, vomiting and diarrhoea from weak spleen and stomach. It is still widely used for chronic diarrhoea with digestive weakness.
- Si junzi tang (ginseng, atractylodes, poria, licorice) with Chinese yam, lotus seed, hyacinth bean and coix to supplement the spleen and remove damp, amomum to move qi, and platycodon to lead upward to the lungs; being balanced and not hot, it may be taken long-term.
