# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 028 三白散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Sanbai san (Three Whites Powder)

It treats pent-up heat in the bladder with wind and damp riding on each other, swelling and distension of the scrotum, and blocked urine and stools.

bai qianniu (white morning-glory seed; 2 liang)　sangbaipi (mulberry root bark; lightly stir-fried)　baizhu (byakujutsu; white atractylodes)　mutong (akebia; nodes removed)　chenpi (chinpi; aged tangerine peel; white pith removed; half a liang each)

Pound the above into a fine powder. Take 2 *qian* each time mixed in ginger decoction, on an empty stomach; if no effect is felt, take again. Taken regularly it guides out and frees retained stagnation without damaging visceral qi.

## Notes

Sanbai san (bureau additions): for scrotal swelling and blocked urine and stools from pent-up bladder heat with wind-damp.
- White morning-glory seed, mulberry root bark and white atractylodes (the three "whites"), plus akebia and tangerine peel, to purge water.
