# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷上 012 論炮炙三品薬石類例・果菜部 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Fruit and Vegetable Section

Katsumada galangal seed: to use, remove the husk, take the kernels and dry over a fire; or simply blast-fry with the husk in hot ashes until cooked and remove the husk.
Aged and green tangerine peel: to use, first soak in hot water, rub off the pulp, sun-dry and stir-fry with bran for use in medicines; if needed urgently, simply dry over a fire.
Mume fruit: to use, first wash, crack, remove the stone, take the flesh and lightly stir-fry.
Chaenomeles: to use, first remove the pulp and hard seeds, chop, dry over a fire and use in medicines.
Apricot and peach kernels: to use, first soak in hot water, remove skins, tips and twin kernels, drain and dry, stir-fry with flour until yellow-red.
Walnut: to use, remove the shell, soak in hot water to remove the skin, then grind for use in medicines.
Chinese chive seed: to use, first lightly stir-fry; some are used raw.
Sesame: this is black oil-hemp. To use, first stir-fry; or steam and sun-dry nine times.
Black soybean, rice bean, soybean sprout, barley sprout, medicated leaven, hyacinth bean, mung bean and the like: to use, stir-fry all before using in medicines.

In all compounding, process according to method and weigh without deficiency; this is best.

## Notes

The Fruit and Vegetable section of the processing examples (the end of the General Guide, upper volume): katsumada galangal, tangerine peels, mume, chaenomeles, apricot and peach kernels (skins, tips and twin kernels removed, fried with flour), walnut, chive seed, sesame, beans, barley sprout and medicated leaven; it closes by urging processing according to method and exact weighing.
