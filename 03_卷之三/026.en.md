# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 026 小丁香丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xiao dingxiang wan (Minor Clove Pill)

It disperses accumulated stagnation, raw and cold foods, lodged rheum and old food, and stops phlegm counterflow with nausea and vomiting of sudden turmoil. It treats distension and oppression of the heart and abdomen, stabbing pain of the flanks and ribs, glomus and fullness of the chest and diaphragm, and choking obstruction. Taken regularly it smooths the spleen and stomach and improves eating.

wulingzhi (flying-squirrel droppings; 12 liang)　dingxiang (clove; 3 liang)　muxiang (costus root; 1½ liang)　roudoukou (nutmeg; shell removed; 30)　badou (croton seeds; skin and membranes removed, oil pressed out; 210)

Make the above into a fine powder, mix in the croton evenly, knead with flour paste to the right consistency and form pills the size of millet grains. Take five to seven pills each time with warm ginger decoction; tangerine-peel decoction is also fine; after meals. For vomiting counterflow of sudden turmoil, decoct peach-leaf decoction, cool it and take them with it. For children with persistent vomiting, a child of three takes three pills and under five four pills, with ginger and peach-leaf decoction.

## Notes

Xiao dingxiang wan: for stagnation from raw and cold foods, lodged rheum and old food, nausea and vomiting of sudden turmoil, and abdominal distension, flank pain and chest glomus.
- Croton purges, flying-squirrel droppings remove stagnation, and clove, costus and nutmeg warm and move qi; small pills in small doses.
