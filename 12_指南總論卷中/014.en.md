# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 014 論諸風恍惚驚悸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On abstraction and fright palpitations in the various winds. When the body is deficient and has received pathogenic wind, the heart qi is insufficient and [the pathogen] enters the heart meridian, give Dingzhi wan (Will-Settling Pill), Jiangxin dan, Pingbu zhenxin dan or Chensha miaoxiang san. For heat, [give] Niuhuang qingxin wan (Gooseishingan).

## Notes

On abstraction and fright palpitations in the various winds (pathogenic wind entering the heart meridian through bodily deficiency and insufficient heart qi): Dingzhi wan, Jiangxin dan, Pingbu zhenxin dan and Chensha miaoxiang san; Niuhuang qingxin wan when there is heat.
