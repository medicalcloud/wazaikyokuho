# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 036 金鈴子丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jinlingzi wan (Toosendan Pill)

It treats stirring of kidney qi with drawing pain, bowstring tension of the navel and abdomen, and attacking surges without fixed place.

jinlingzi (toosendan fruit; stones removed, stir-fried; 4 liang)　yizhi ren (alpinia oxyphylla)　huluba (fenugreek; stir-fried)　shichangpu (acorus)　poguzhi (psoralea; stir-fried)　huixiang (fennel; stir-fried)　baji (morinda; core removed; 2 liang each)　muxiang (costus root)　bai fuling (white poria; skin removed)　chenpi (aged tangerine peel; white pith removed; 1 liang each)

Powder the above and form pills the size of wutong seeds with flour paste boiled in wine. Take fifty pills each time with salt water or warm wine as preferred.

## Notes

Jinlingzi wan (Chunyou-era additions): for drawing pain, umbilical tension and surging from stirring of kidney qi (mounting, running piglet and the like).
- Toosendan, fennel, costus and tangerine peel move qi and stop pain; fenugreek, psoralea, morinda and alpinia warm the kidneys; poria and acorus are added.
