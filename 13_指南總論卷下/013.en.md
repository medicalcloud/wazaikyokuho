# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷下 013 論腹脹心膨 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On abdominal distension and chest swelling. When after cold damage, chills and fever, miasmic malaria, or a major illness of diarrhoea or dysentery, the abdomen distends and the chest swells only after eating, food has no relish, there is belching and acid swallowing, and even a little food brings a feeling of distension, one must on no account use food-dispersing, digesting medicines. If medicines are used to move and purge, the patient becomes more exhausted and heavy and moves further from life. Simply give Zhengqi san, Jiahe san, Shen ling baizhu san, Si junzi tang, Caopi san, Renshen zhusan, Da and Xiao yangpi wan, Bichengqie san, Sishi wan, Pengjian wan or Ruyi wan. For diarrhoea, give Renshen doukou san or Dingxiang san. Once the qi is strong, eating naturally doubles and the distension heals by itself.

## Notes

On abdominal distension after major illness (cold damage, malaria, dysentery), with bloating after meals, poor appetite, belching and acid reflux: digestive and purgative medicines are forbidden; strengthen qi with Zhengqi san, Jiahe san, Shen ling baizhu san, Si junzi tang and others; Renshen doukou san or Dingxiang san for diarrhoea.
