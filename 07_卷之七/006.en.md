# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之七 006 鎮肝丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zhengan wan (Liver-Settling Pill)

It is suitable for insufficiency of the liver channel with wind-heat received internally attacking the eyes upward, dim, dark, itchy and painful eyes, gritty roughness making them hard to open, accumulated discharge and much tearing, fear of the sun and aversion to light, occasional swelling and redness, or the formation of obstructions and nebulae.

manjingzi (vitex fruit; white skin removed)　difuzi (kochia fruit)　renshen (ginseng)　chongweizi (leonurus fruit)　juemingzi (cassia seed)　bai fuling (white poria; skin removed)　yuanzhi (polygala; core removed)　fangfeng (saposhnikovia; rhizome head and forks removed; 1 liang each)　qingxiangzi (celosia seed)　digupi (lycium root bark)　chaihu (bupleurum; rhizome head removed)　shanyao (Chinese yam)　cheqianzi (plantago seed)　baiziren (arborvitae seed; stir-fried)　xuanshen (scrophularia)　ganju (sweet chrysanthemum)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried; half a liang each)　xixin (asarum; shoots removed; 1 *fen*)

Powder the above and form pills the size of wutong seeds with paste boiled in honey water. Take twenty pills each time with rice water, after meals, twice a day.

## Notes

Zhengan wan: for dim vision, itching, pain, difficulty opening the eyes, discharge, tearing, photophobia, red swelling and obstructions from an insufficient liver channel with wind-heat.
- Cassia seed, celosia seed, leonurus fruit, vitex, kochia, plantago seed and chrysanthemum brighten the eyes; ginseng, poria, Chinese yam, polygala and arborvitae seed supplement; bupleurum, saposhnikovia and asarum disperse wind; scrophularia and lycium root bark clear heat.
