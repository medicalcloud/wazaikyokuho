# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷上 011 論炮炙三品薬石類例・禽魚虫部 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bird, Fish and Insect Section

Bat droppings: these are the dung of the bat. To use, lightly stir-fry before using in medicines.
White honey: to use, first boil over a fire and skim off the froth until slightly yellow; it will then keep long without spoiling. How much to skim depends on the quality of the honey.
Oyster shell: to use, calcine red-hot, cool, grind fine as powder; then it may be used.
Pearl: to use, take fresh, clean ones that are undamaged and undrilled, pound finely in a mortar, sift repeatedly through silk, then grind a further ten or twenty thousand times before use.
Mantis egg-case: to use, first roast; steaming is also acceptable.
Turtle shell and tortoise shell: to use, first soak in vinegar for three days, remove the skirt and roast repeatedly over a gentle fire until yellow-red. If needed urgently, dip in vinegar and roast until yellow, then use.
Wasp nest: to use, roast first; stir-frying is also acceptable.
Cicada slough: to use, first remove the mouthparts and legs, soak in hot water to moisten, wash off the mud, sun-dry and lightly stir-fry.
Silkworm: to use, choose white, straight ones; first remove the silk and mouth and lightly stir-fry before use; some are used raw, according to the formula.
Silkmoth: to use, remove wings and legs and lightly stir-fry before using in medicines. Silkworm droppings are also stir-fried.
Toad: to use, first smear with ghee or soak in wine and roast repeatedly over a gentle fire until scorched yellow; or burn to ash with nature preserved. For other processing, follow the formula.
Snake slough: to use, roast first; or burn to ash and use in medicines, according to the formula.
Black-striped snake and agkistrodon: to use, first soak in wine for three days and nights, roast repeatedly over a gentle fire until yellow-red and dry, remove skin and bones and take the flesh for use in medicines.
Earthworm: to use, first rub off the earth and lightly stir-fry.
Centipede: to use, roast first; then it may be used in medicines.
Blister beetle: to use, first remove legs and wings and stir-fry with glutinous rice until cooked; then it may be used. Raw, it causes vomiting and diarrhoea.
Cnidocampa cocoon: to use, lightly stir-fry.
Dung beetle: to use, first remove head, wings and legs and roast.
Flying squirrel droppings: to use, first grind with wine and water-grind, refine and rinse out sand and stones, sun-dry, then use in medicines.

## Notes

The Bird, Fish and Insect section of the processing examples: bat droppings, white honey (boiled and skimmed), oyster shell, pearl, mantis egg-case, turtle and tortoise shell (soaked in vinegar and roasted), wasp nest, cicada slough, silkworm, silkmoth, toad, snake slough, black-striped snake and agkistrodon, earthworm, centipede, blister beetle (fried with glutinous rice; raw it causes vomiting and diarrhoea), cnidocampa cocoon, dung beetle and flying squirrel droppings.
