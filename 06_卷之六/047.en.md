# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 047 純陽真人養臓湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Chunyang zhenren yangzang tang (Perfected Man Chunyang's Viscera-Nourishing Decoction)

It treats all of the following in adults and children: weak, deficient intestines and stomach, disharmony of cold and heat, viscera and bowels that have received cold, red and white dysentery, or stools with pus and blood like fish brains, tenesmus, gripping pain of the navel and abdomen without limit day and night, glomus and oppression of the chest and diaphragm, distension and fullness of the flanks and ribs and no desire whatever for food; as well as prolapse of the rectum and bloody stools from alcohol toxin unresponsive to other medicines.

renshen (ginseng)　danggui (Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed)　baizhu (white atractylodes; dried over a fire; 6 *qian* each)　roudoukou (nutmeg; wrapped in dough and roasted in embers; half a liang)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried; 8 *qian* each)　bai shaoyao (white peony root; 1 liang 6 *qian*)　muxiang (costus root; not exposed to fire; 1 liang 4 *qian*)　hezi (chebule; stone removed; 1 liang 2 *qian*)　yingsuqiao (poppy husk; stalk and cap removed, roasted with honey; 3 liang 6 *qian*)

Chop the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take two large *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water, decoct down to eight-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm before meals. For sudden diarrhoea in the elderly, pregnant women and children it should be taken urgently, with immediate recovery. Avoid wine, flour, raw and cold things, fishy and greasy food. If the viscera are slippery and leaking, with getting up at night, and the condition has long not healed, add three or four slices of blast-fried aconite to the decoction. This medicine truly has divine efficacy that cannot be fully described. (One version does not use nutmeg.)

## Notes

Chunyang zhenren yangzang tang (Shaoxing-era additions): for red and white dysentery, stools with pus and blood, tenesmus, gripping umbilical pain, frequent stools day and night, chest glomus, flank distension and poor appetite from weak intestines and stomach with disharmony of cold and heat; also for rectal prolapse and bloody stools from alcohol toxin. Later known as "Zhenren yangzang tang", a representative formula for chronic diarrhoea and dysentery.
- Poppy husk, chebule and nutmeg astringe the intestines; ginseng, atractylodes, licorice and cinnamon warm and supplement the spleen; angelica and white peony harmonize the blood; costus moves qi. It contains poppy husk.
- Chunyang zhenren is the sobriquet of Lü Dongbin.
