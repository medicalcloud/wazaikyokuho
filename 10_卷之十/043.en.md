# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 043 太一丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyi dan (Supreme Unity Elixir)

It treats the various winds and fright epilepsy in children with tidal attacks of convulsions, mouth and eyes drawn together, rigid neck and back, a clouded, weary spirit and obstructed phlegm and drool, as well as all deficiency wind.

tiannanxing (arisaema; blast-fried)　wushe (black-striped snake; roasted with wine, flesh taken; 3 *qian* each)　tianma (gastrodia; rhizome head removed, soaked in wine overnight)　fuzi (aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　mahuang (ephedra; roots and nodes removed; half a liang each)　ganxie (dried scorpion; lightly stir-fried; 1½ *qian*)　bai fuzi (typhonium tuber; blast-fried; 3½ *qian*)　bai jiangcan (silkworm; silk and mouth removed, stir-fried; 4 *qian*)

Make the above into a fine powder, mix with 1 sheng of water and steep for three days, mix evenly with 1 *dou* of Cold Food Festival flour and tread it into leaven cakes; this must be done on the sixth day of the sixth month. Cover with paper-mulberry leaves for seven days, take out, put each piece in a paper bag and hang in the wind; after fourteen days it can be used. To each liang of leaven powder add the following:

hupo (amber; ground; 1 *qian*)　chensha (cinnabar; ground and water-ground; 6 *qian*)　xionghuang (realgar; ground and water-ground; 3 *qian*)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried, powdered; half a *qian*)

Grind together evenly and form pills the size of euryale seeds with refined honey. Take one pill each time dissolved in warm water, at any time.

## Notes

Taiyi dan: for the various winds and fright epilepsy in children (convulsions, mouth and eyes drawn together, rigid neck and back, clouded spirit, phlegm) and for all deficiency wind.
- Arisaema, typhonium, black-striped snake, scorpion, silkworm, gastrodia, ephedra and aconite are made into leaven with Cold Food Festival flour (prepared on the sixth day of the sixth month), then amber, cinnabar, realgar and licorice are added and made into pills.
