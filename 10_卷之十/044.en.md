# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 044 大驚丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Dajing wan (Great Fright Pill)

It treats fright wind and the various epilepsies with vigorous fever and clouded consciousness, an abstracted spirit, congested phlegm and drool, or convulsions with a fixed stare.

shehuang (snake yellow [a limonite nodule]; calcined, quenched in vinegar nine times, ground and water-ground; 2 *qian*)　qing mengshi (chlorite schist; ground; 1 *qian*)　zhusha (cinnabar; ground and water-ground; 3 *qian*)　hama hui (toad ash)　xionghuang (realgar; 1 *qian* each)　tiefen (iron powder; ground; 2½ *qian*)

Grind the above evenly and form pills the size of wutong seeds with steamed cake soaked in water. Take one pill each time dissolved in decocted mint water, ground with the handle of a pair of scissors; two or three times a day. This medicine treats fright and transforms drool without using "silver powder" (mercury preparations). The viscera, mouth and teeth, and intestines and stomach of children are soft and weak; whenever mercury-containing medicines are used, great caution is needed; then there will be no other suffering.

## Notes

Dajing wan: for high fever, clouded consciousness, abstraction, phlegm, convulsions and fixed stare from fright wind and the epilepsies.
- Limonite nodule, chlorite schist, iron powder, cinnabar, realgar and toad ash settle and bring down phlegm. It expressly avoids mercury preparations ("silver powder") and warns against their use in children.
