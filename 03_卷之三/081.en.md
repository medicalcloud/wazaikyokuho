# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 081 守中金丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shouzhong jin wan (Middle-Guarding Golden Pill)

It regulates disharmony of the middle jiao and accumulated cold of the spleen and stomach with deficiency stuffiness below the heart and abdominal pain; or excessive drinking with counterflow fullness of the chest and flanks, choking obstruction, cough at any time, vomiting of cold phlegm, inability to get food down, belching of sourness and acid swallowing, a bitter mouth with loss of taste, lassitude with a liking for lying down and no desire for food. It also treats cold damage and seasonal qi with interior cold and exterior heat, vomiting and diarrhea of sudden turmoil, wringing pain of the heart and abdomen, disharmony of the hands and feet, a hot body without thirst, borborygmus with spontaneous diarrhea and undigested grain.

ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; scorched)　cangzhu (black atractylodes; soaked in rice-washing water)　jiegeng (kikyo; platycodon; rhizome head removed)

Take equal parts of the above, chop and make a fine powder. Form pills the size of a pellet with refined honey. Chew one pill each time before meals and take it with boiling water. It also treats retained damp in the spleen and stomach with a heavy body, painful joints, a withered yellow complexion and wasted flesh. Taken regularly it warms the spleen and stomach, disperses phlegm and drives out retained fluid, smooths the sanjiao, improves eating and wards off wind, cold, damp and chill.

## Notes

Shouzhong jin wan (Wu Zhige's additions): nearly the same indications as Lizhong wan (Richugan) (juan 3, no. 005), but with black atractylodes and platycodon in place of ginseng and white atractylodes; also used for heaviness, joint pain and sallow complexion from damp in the spleen and stomach.
