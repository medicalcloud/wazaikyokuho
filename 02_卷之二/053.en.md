# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 053 滲湿湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shenshi tang (Damp-Draining Decoction)

It treats damage by cold-damp with heaviness of the body and cold in the lower back as if sitting in water, urination sometimes rough and sometimes free, and loose stools. All this comes from sitting or lying in damp places, or exposure to rain and dew, or cold, damp clothes and bedding after sweating, acquired over a long time. It can govern heaviness and pain below the lower back, pain in both legs, the thighs and knees swollen or not, free urination and, on the contrary, no thirst.

cangzhu (black atractylodes)　baizhu (byakujutsu; white atractylodes)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried; 1 liang each)　fuling (bukuryo; poria; skin removed)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; scorched; 2 liang each)　juhong (red tangerine peel)　dingxiang (clove; 1 *fen* each)

Cut the above into pieces. For each dose take 4 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water, one jujube and three slices of ginger, decoct down to seven-tenths and take it warm before meals.

## Notes

Shenshi tang (Wu Zhige's additions): for cold-damp damage with a heavy body and cold lower back (as if sitting in water), heaviness and pain below the waist and loose stools (close to the "lower back pain due to cold dampness" pattern of the *Jingui yaolüe*).
- It corresponds to Gancao ganjiang fuling baizhu tang (Ling jiang zhu gan tang) with black atractylodes, red tangerine peel and clove added.
