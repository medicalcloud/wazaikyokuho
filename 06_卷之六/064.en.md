# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 064 温中丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wenzhong wan (Middle-Warming Pill)

It treats the spleen zang organ damaged by cold with undigested lodged food, sudden turmoil with vomiting and diarrhea, and swelling distension of the heart and abdomen with attacking, stabbing pain.

liangjiang (lesser galangal; rhizome head removed)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried)　qingpi (green tangerine peel; white removed)　chenpi (chinpi; aged tangerine peel; white pith removed; 5 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with flour paste made with vinegar. Take thirty pills each time, swallowed with rice water, at any time. It also treats small-intestine mounting qi in men with lumps and gripping pain: chew a little stir-fried fennel thoroughly and take the pills with salt water or salted wine as preferred, two doses a day.

## Notes

Wenzhong wan (Wu Zhige's additions): for lodged food, vomiting and diarrhea of sudden turmoil and abdominal distension with stabbing pain when the spleen is damaged by cold, and for lumps and gripping pain of small-intestine mounting in men.
- Four ingredients: lesser galangal and dried ginger warm, green and aged tangerine peel move qi. For mounting, it is taken after chewing fennel.
