# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 070 附子理中丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Fuzi lizhong wan (Bushirichugan; Aconite Middle-Regulating Pill)

It treats all cases of cold, weak spleen and stomach with wringing pain of the heart and abdomen, vomiting and diarrhea, sudden turmoil with cramps, a cold body with slight sweating, cold hands and feet, counterflow fullness below the heart, thunderous rumbling in the abdomen, unceasing retching and hiccup, inability to eat, and all deep-lying cold and chronic chill.

fuzi (bushi; aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng; rhizome head removed)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried)　baizhu (byakujutsu; white atractylodes; 3 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder and knead with refined honey into pills, ten pills to each liang. For each dose break up and dissolve one pill in one *zhan* of water, decoct down to seven-tenths and take it slightly hot, on an empty stomach before meals.

## Notes

Fuzi lizhong wan: for wringing abdominal pain, vomiting, diarrhea, sudden turmoil with cramps, cold body, cold limbs, fullness below the heart, borborygmus, hiccup and poor appetite from cold, weak spleen and stomach, and for all deep-lying cold and chronic chill. Still used today for deficiency cold of the spleen and stomach (abdominal pain and diarrhea from cold).
- Lizhong wan (Richugan) (ginseng, atractylodes, dried ginger, licorice; *Shanghan lun*) plus aconite. The pill is dissolved by decoction and drunk.
