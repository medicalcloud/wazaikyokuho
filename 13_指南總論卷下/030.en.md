# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷下 030 論産前薬忌 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On medicines prohibited before childbirth. Concerning drugs to be avoided before childbirth because they damage and disturb the foetal qi, Physician Lu's *Zhouding ji* puts it in a song: "Blister beetle, crab, leech and the earth-gall beetle; wutou, aconite and tianxiong; rhododendron, gelsemium and the mole-cricket kind; wuhui, cezi and gadfly; bovine bezoar, mercury and croton; euphorbia, snake slough and centipede; achyranthes, veratrum and coix; metals and stones, tin powder, orpiment and realgar; refined, crude and *mang* mirabilite, moutan decoction; lizard, flying squirrel and ground beetle; haematite, cicada, *hunao* and musk; genkwa, *weiheng* and sparganium; sophora seed, morning-glory seed and gleditsia; peach, grub and imperata root; *dang* root, sal ammoniac and dried lacquer; meloid beetle, *souliu* and *mangcao*; dianthus, *lüru* and crab claws; hedgehog skin, euonymus and *chitouhong*; razor clam, stone silkworm and silverfish; pinellia, arisaema and rice-paper plant likewise; dried ginger, garlic, chicken and duck eggs; donkey, horse and rabbit meat must not be served. Strictly avoid using them for women before childbirth; at this time keep them firmly in mind."

## Notes

On drugs prohibited in pregnancy: quoting a mnemonic song from Physician Lu's *Zhouding ji*, it lists substances that damage or disturb the foetus—insects (blister beetle, leech and others), aconites, drastic purgatives (croton, euphorbia, genkwa), minerals (mercury, tin powder, orpiment, realgar), blood-moving drugs (achyranthes, moutan, peach kernel, sparganium), pinellia and arisaema, and foods (dried ginger, garlic, eggs, donkey, horse and rabbit meat).
- The drug names in the song are translated as written in the base text; some (e.g., "flying squirrel" for feisheng, souliu, chitouhong) have not been verified.
