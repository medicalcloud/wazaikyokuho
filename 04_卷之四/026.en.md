# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之四 026 縮砂丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Suosha wan (Shukushagan; Amomum Pill)

It warms the middle and scatters stagnation, disperses retained fluid and improves eating. It treats choking oppression of the chest and diaphragm and cold pain of the heart and abdomen. It is greatly able to warm and transform raw and cold foods and fruit; in summer one must not be without it.

suosharen (amomum; 1 liang)　gaoliangjiang (lesser galangal)　tiannanxing (arisaema; washed seven times in hot water, dried over a fire; 4 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with flour paste boiled in fresh ginger juice. Take fifty to seventy pills each time with ginger decoction, at any time.

## Notes

Suosha wan (Chunyou-era additions): warms the middle, disperses stagnation and retained fluid, for chest obstruction and cold heart and abdominal pain; a summer household medicine against raw and cold foods and fruit.
- Three ingredients: amomum, galangal and arisaema.
