# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 033 生気湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shengqi tang (Qi-Generating Decoction)

It treats all cold qi in men and women attacking the heart, abdomen, flanks and ribs with distension, fullness and stabbing pain, belching of sourness and acid swallowing, phlegm counterflow with vomiting, stuffiness and oppression of the chest and diaphragm and food without relish. It also treats the five diaphragm disorders and five chokings and all qi disorders. Taken regularly it removes pathogenic cold and generates stomach qi.

salt (stir-fried; 2½ liang)　dingxiangpi (clove bark; 1 liang)　hujiao (black pepper; 2½ *qian*)　dingxiang (clove)　tanxiang (sandalwood; 1½ liang each)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried; 2 liang each)

Pound these seven ingredients together, scorch them over a gentle fire until fragrant and cooked, put them while hot into a porcelain vessel and cover tightly; when cool, mill and sieve into a fine powder and store tightly sealed so that the aroma does not escape. Take half a *qian* to 1 *qian* each time stirred into boiling water, at any time.

## Notes

Shengqi tang: for distension and stabbing pain of the heart, abdomen and flanks, acid reflux, vomiting, chest oppression and poor appetite from all cold qi, and for the five diaphragm disorders, five chokings and all qi disorders.
- Stir-fried salt, clove, clove bark, black pepper, sandalwood, dried ginger and licorice are scorched together into a powder and stirred into hot water.
