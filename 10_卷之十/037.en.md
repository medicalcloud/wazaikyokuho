# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 037 芦薈丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Luhui wan (Aloe Pill)

It treats gan qi with emaciation, a withered yellow complexion, distension and fullness of the abdomen and flanks, hair clumping like ears of grain, rubbing the nose and biting the nails, a liking for eating mud and earth, stools of varying colour, alternating chills and fever, rough eyes and bad breath, and festering, blackened gums. Taken regularly it grows flesh, removes the yellowness, kills gan worms and improves feeding.

da zaojiao (large gleditsia pod)　gan hama (dried toad; equal parts of each, burned together with nature preserved, powdered; 1 liang taken and added to the following medicines)　qingdai (indigo; ground; 1 *fen*)　luhui (aloe; ground)　zhusha (cinnabar; ground and water-ground)　shexiang (musk; ground; 1 *qian* each)

Grind the above together evenly, knead with steamed cake soaked in hot water and form pills the size of hemp seeds. A three-year-old takes twenty pills with warm rice water at any time, adjusted further for size.

## Notes

Luhui wan: for wasting, sallowness, abdominal and flank distension, clumped hair, nose-rubbing, nail-biting, eating earth, variable stools, chills and fever, rough eyes, bad breath and blackened festering gums from gan qi.
- Aloe and indigo clear gan heat; ash of dried toad and gleditsia kills worms; cinnabar and musk are added.
