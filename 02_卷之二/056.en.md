# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 056 八解散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bajie san (Eight Resolutions Powder)

It treats cold damage in any season with headache, vigorous fever and much sweating from contracted wind; and treats excessive overwork with aching pain of the joints, tasteless food, painful and weary limbs, panting fatigue on walking, a withered yellow complexion, lassitude and little strength, cough with chills and fever, emaciation with spontaneous sweating, discomfort of the chest and diaphragm, vomiting counterflow and nausea.

renshen (ginseng)　fuling (poria)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried)　chenpi (aged tangerine peel; white pith removed)　baizhu (white atractylodes)　huoxiang (agastache; earth removed; 1 liang each)　houpo (magnolia bark; coarse bark removed, chopped, soaked overnight in fresh ginger juice and stir-fried purple; 2 liang)　banxia (pinellia; washed seven times in hot water; 1 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one *zhan* of water, three slices of ginger, one jujube and three *cun* of scallion white, decoct together down to seven-tenths and take it warm, at any time.

## Notes

Bajie san (bureau additions): for headache, fever and much sweating in seasonal colds, and for joint aches, poor appetite, lassitude, sallow complexion, cough, spontaneous sweating, chest discomfort and nausea from overwork.
- Si junzi tang (ginseng, atractylodes, poria, licorice) with tangerine peel and pinellia (Liu junzi tang) plus agastache and magnolia bark; scallion white harmonizes the exterior.
