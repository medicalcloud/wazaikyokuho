# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之四 022 人参訶子丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Renshen hezi wan (Ninjinkashigan; Ginseng and Chebule Pill)

It treats heat of the upper diaphragm in adults and children, or wind damage and contraction of cold contending with the lung meridian, with loss of voice, phlegm and drool that will not clear, cough with urgent dyspnea day and night, and thick sticky expectorated phlegm.

suosharen (amomum)　hezi (chebule; stone removed)　huoxiang (agastache; stalks removed)　longnao bohe leaves (borneol mint; 1 liang each)　baiyaojian (fermented gallnut)　gefen (pueraria starch; 8 liang each)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; 5 liang)　wumei (ubai) flesh (smoked plum; 3 liang)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng; 1 liang 2 *qian*)

Powder the above and form pills with flour paste. Hold one or two pills in the mouth each time to dissolve and swallow with the saliva, after meals and at bedtime.

## Notes

Renshen hezi wan (Baoqing-era additions): a lozenge for loss of voice, phlegm, cough, dyspnea and sticky sputum from heat in the upper diaphragm or wind-cold entering the lung meridian.
- Chebule, fermented gallnut and smoked plum astringe the lungs and restore the voice; borneol mint and pueraria starch clear heat; ginseng and licorice supplement; amomum and agastache are added.
- The base text separates 竜脳 and 薄荷葉 with a space, but they can be read as "leaves of borneol mint" (a mint variety), so they are translated here as one ingredient (uncertain).
