# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 026 薄荷煎丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bohe jian wan (Mint Syrup Pill)

It disperses wind-heat, transforms phlegm and drool, frees the throat and diaphragm and clears the head and eyes. It treats numbness of the whole body, aching pain in all the joints, a clouded head and dizzy eyes, nasal congestion with headache, a heavy voice, stiffness of the neck and back, itching skin, or dormant papules; also lung heat with a fishy smell in the throat, spleen heat with a sweet taste in the mouth, and gallbladder heat with a bitter taste in the mouth. It also treats nosebleed and spitting of blood, bleeding with urine and stool, and wind damage from taking clothes off and on; it may also be taken after bathing.

longnao bohe (borneol mint; leaves taken; 10 jin)　fangfeng (saposhnikovia; sprouts removed)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage; 30 liang each)　suoshaner (amomum; 5 liang)　jiegeng (platycodon; 50 liang)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried; 40 liang)

Powder the above and form pills with refined honey, thirty pills to each liang. Take one pill each time, chewed thoroughly, with tea or wine as preferred.

## Notes

Bohe jian wan: for numbness, joint pain, headache, nasal congestion, itching and urticaria from wind-heat, mouth symptoms of lung, spleen and gallbladder heat, bleeding, and colds.
- A large amount of borneol mint, with saposhnikovia and Sichuan lovage to scatter wind and platycodon, licorice and amomum to regulate the throat and middle.
