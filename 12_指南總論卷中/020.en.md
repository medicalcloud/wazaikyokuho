# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 020 傷寒総論 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

General discussion of cold damage. The *Huoren shu* says: "The proper names of cold damage are sixteen: cold damage, wind damage, cold damage with wind, wind damage with cold, wind-damp, damp-strike, wind-warmth, damp-warmth, warm toxin, heatstroke, heat disease, warm disease, convulsive disease, warm malaria, late onset and pestilence—sixteen external patterns. There are also six patterns that resemble it: summer heat strike, phlegm damage, food accumulation, deficiency taxation, miasmic malaria and leg qi resemble cold damage but in fact are not cold damage; people do not understand these patterns and all call them cold damage." If one does not carefully distinguish the signs and deficiency or excess before using medicines, one may ruin a person's life in the turn of a hand; this must be known.

## Notes

General discussion of cold damage: quoting the *Huoren shu* (Zhu Gong's *Leizheng huoren shu*), it lists the sixteen proper categories of cold damage and six patterns that resemble but are not cold damage (summer heat strike, phlegm damage, food accumulation, deficiency taxation, miasmic malaria, leg qi), insisting on careful differentiation of signs and deficiency or excess before prescribing.
