# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷下 018 論積熱咽喉痛 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On the signs of accumulated heat.

(With the throat, mouth and teeth, and eyes appended.)

On sore throat from accumulated heat. For congested heat in the upper jiao with vexing thirst of the heart meridian and nodes in the cheeks and jaw, give Yuxie wuyou san, Niuhuang liangge wan (Gooryokakugan), Xiaodu xijiao yin, Sishun yin, Jiedu wan or Jire sanhuang wan. For sores of the mouth and tongue, give Bixue, Longnao yin, Yuxie wuyou san, Ganlu yin or Pengsha wan (Hoshagan). For swollen, painful throat with obstruction on swallowing, give Rusheng tang (Nyoseito), Bazheng san (Hasshosan), Sishun yin, Longnao yin, Ganlu yin, Xixin san (Saishinsan), Niuhuang liangge wan, Xijiao yin, Bohe jian or Shilong san. For inhibited urination from accumulated heat, give Daochi wan (Dosekigan) or Wulin san (Gorinsan). For constipation, take Qingxin yin, Sanhuang wan or Xixin san.

## Notes

The first discussion under "On the signs of accumulated heat (with throat, mouth and teeth, and eyes appended)": sore throat from accumulated heat. Yuxie wuyou san, Niuhuang liangge wan and others for upper-jiao heat with thirst and nodes; Bixue, Longnao yin and others for mouth sores; Rusheng tang, Bazheng san and others for a swollen, painful throat; Daochi wan and Wulin san for urinary blockage; Qingxin yin, Sanhuang wan and Xixin san for constipation.
