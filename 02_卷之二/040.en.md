# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 040 人参順気散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Renshen shunqi san (Ginseng Qi-Smoothing Powder)

It is suitable for men and women with wind deficiency and weak qi, disharmony of the ying-nutrient and wei-defense qi, pain of the limbs and joints, heaviness of the body, spinning dizziness of the head and eyes, stiffness of the shoulders and back, cold numb hands and feet, hemiplegia, deviation of the mouth and eyes, phlegm and drool that will not clear, and halting, sluggish speech; or disharmony of the spleen and stomach with stabbing pain of the heart and abdomen, stuffiness and fullness of the chest and diaphragm, lassitude and little strength, sudden turmoil with cramps and unceasing vomiting and diarrhea; and before and after childbirth.

ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng; 1 liang each)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried)　kugeng (bitter platycodon; rhizome head removed)　houpo (koboku; magnolia bark; coarse bark removed, processed with ginger juice)　baizhu (byakujutsu; white atractylodes)　chenpi (chinpi; aged tangerine peel; washed, white pith removed)　baizhi (byakushi; angelica dahurica)　mahuang (mao; ephedra; nodes removed; 4 liang each)　gange (dried pueraria; coarse skin removed; 3½ liang)

Make the above into a fine powder. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one *zhan* of water, three slices of ginger, one jujube and five to seven mint leaves, decoct together down to eight-tenths; at any time. For wind damage and contraction of cold with headache, heavy lower back, cough and nasal congestion, add scallion white when decocting.

## Notes

Renshen shunqi san (Baoqing-era additions): for wind deficiency and weak qi with joint pain, heaviness, dizziness, cold numb limbs and hemiplegia, for abdominal pain, stuffiness and sudden turmoil from spleen-stomach disharmony, and before and after childbirth.
- Close to the earlier Wuyao shunqi san (juan 1, no. 080): ginseng and atractylodes supplement qi; ephedra, angelica dahurica, pueraria and lovage release the exterior; magnolia bark, tangerine peel and platycodon smooth qi; dried ginger warms.
