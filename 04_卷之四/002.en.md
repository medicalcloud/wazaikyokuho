# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之四 002 消飲丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xiaoyin wan (Rheum-Dispersing Pill)

It treats wine aggregation and prolonged fluid retention with phlegm and water not dispersing, fullness, counterflow and vomiting, dim eyes and deafness, urgent pain below the flanks and the sound of water in the abdomen.

zhishi (kijitsu; unripe bitter orange; stir-fried with bran; half a liang)　fuling (bukuryo; poria; skin removed)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried; 3 liang each)　baizhu (byakujutsu; white atractylodes; 8 liang)

Make the above together into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with refined honey. Take fifty pills each time with warm rice gruel, at any time.

## Notes

Xiaoyin wan: for abdominal fullness, vomiting, dim eyes, deafness, pain below the flanks and the sound of water in the abdomen from wine aggregation and prolonged fluid retention.
- White atractylodes and poria drain water, dried ginger warms, and unripe bitter orange sends qi down.
