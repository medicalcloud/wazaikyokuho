# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 030 茱萸内消丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zhuyu neixiao wan (Evodia Internal-Dispersing Pill)

It treats deficiency of the kidney and bladder channels contended by evil qi, binding into cold mounting that lurks and lingers without leaving, with wringing, stabbing pain of the navel and abdomen, small-intestine qi pain, running piglet and strings and aggregations with unbearable pain, one testicle enlarged, swelling of the scrotum like an abscess, binding, hard and drawn tight, growing heavier and larger, itching and painful, occasionally exuding yellow fluid or forming sores, heavy lower back and thighs, swollen, full feet and shins and difficulty walking, unimproved by repeated treatment. Taking it, [the swelling] gradually disperses from within, without stirring the large intestine or causing griping; it supplements deficiency, disperses mounting and warms and nourishes the kidney channel. This medicine is not hot and is non-toxic; if taken with sincere attention, its effect is divine.

wuzhuyu (evodia; washed seven times in hot water, dried over a fire)　chenpi (aged tangerine peel; white pith removed)　chuanlian (toosendan fruit; steamed, skin and stones removed)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed, not exposed to fire)　malinhua (iris flower; roasted with vinegar)　qingpi (green tangerine peel; white removed)　shanyao (Chinese yam; dried over a fire)　huixiang (fennel; stir-fried)　shanzhuyu (cornus; stones removed; 2 liang each)　muxiang (costus root; not exposed to fire; 1 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with wine paste. Take thirty to fifty pills each time, swallowed on an empty stomach with warm wine or salt water.

## Notes

Zhuyu neixiao wan (Baoqing-era additions): for cold mounting (wringing umbilical pain, small-intestine qi, running piglet, strings and aggregations) from evil qi in deficient kidney and bladder channels, for unilateral testicular enlargement and scrotal swelling, hardness, itching, discharge and sores, and for heavy legs and swollen feet. It is said to disperse from within without purging.
- Evodia, toosendan, fennel and cinnamon warm and treat mounting; tangerine peels, costus and iris flower move qi; cornus and Chinese yam supplement the kidneys.
