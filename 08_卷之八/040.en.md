# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 040 滑肌散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huaji san (Skin-Smoothing Powder)

It treats all cases of pathogenic wind lodging in the skin with itching of the whole body leading to sores and scabies, as well as wind toxin of the spleen and lungs attacking and surging, with sores, scabies and cracking over the whole body and dry or damp sores unhealed for many days.

jiancao (chloranthus herb; 7 liang, not exposed to fire)　qingfen (calomel; 1 *qian*)

Make the above into a fine powder. For damp sores, sprinkle the medicine on dry; for dry sores, mix the medicine with sesame oil and apply.

## Notes

Huaji san (Baoqing-era additions): for generalized itching with sores and scabies from pathogenic wind lodging in the skin, and for long-unhealed sores, scabies, cracking and dry or damp sores from wind toxin of the spleen and lungs.
- A two-ingredient external preparation of chloranthus herb and calomel (a mercury compound): sprinkled dry on damp sores, mixed with sesame oil for dry ones.
