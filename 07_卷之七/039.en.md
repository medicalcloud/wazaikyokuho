# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之七 039 細辛散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xixin san (Asarum Powder)

It can treat wind and worm toothache, receding, festering gums, loose teeth and puffy swelling of the cheeks and jaw.

hongjiao (red Sichuan pepper; seeds removed, stir-fried)　heshi (carpesium fruit)　yazao (small gleditsia pod)　bibo (long pepper; an essential medicine for toothache in ancient formulas)　suosha (amomum; husks removed; half a liang each)　jingjie (schizonepeta; stalks removed)　xixin (asarum; shoots removed; 1 liang each)　baizhi (angelica dahurica)　caowu (wild aconite; 2 liang each)

Pound the above into a fine powder. Each time rub a little on the painful place. Spit out any drool; it must not be swallowed. After a short while rinse the mouth with warm water. Rubbing it on frequently has immediate divine effect.

## Notes

Xixin san (bureau additions; the last formula of juan 7): for toothache from wind or worms, receding, festering gums, loose teeth and swollen cheeks.
- Wild aconite, angelica dahurica, asarum, schizonepeta, red Sichuan pepper and long pepper stop pain; carpesium fruit kills worms; small gleditsia pod and amomum are added. The powder is rubbed on and the drool spat out.
