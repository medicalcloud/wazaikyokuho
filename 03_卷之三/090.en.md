# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 090 化気湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huaqi tang (Qi-Transforming Decoction)

It treats all qi counterflow with choking oppression of the chest and diaphragm and one-sided distension and fullness. It also treats pain of the heart and spleen with vomiting of sour water, men's small-intestine qi and women's spleen blood qi.

chenxiang (aquilaria)　hujiao (black pepper; 1 liang each)　muxiang (costus root)　suosha (amomum; shells removed)　guixin (cinnamon heart; coarse bark removed; 2 liang each)　dingxiangpi (clove bark)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried)　peng'ezhu (zedoary; roasted)　huixiang (fennel; stir-fried)　qingpi (green tangerine peel; white removed, stir-fried with bran)　chenpi (aged tangerine peel; pulp removed, stir-fried with bran)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried; 4 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder. Take 2 *qian* each time mixed in ginger, perilla and salt decoction; women take it with weak vinegar decoction.

## Notes

Huaqi tang (bureau additions): for chest obstruction and abdominal fullness from qi counterflow, heart and spleen pain with vomiting of sour water, men's small-intestine qi and women's blood qi.
- Aquilaria, costus, amomum, the tangerine peels and zedoary move qi; black pepper, cinnamon heart, clove bark, dried ginger and fennel warm.
