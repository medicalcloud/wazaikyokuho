# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 033 寧志膏 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Ningzhi gao (Will-Calming Paste)

It treats deficiency of the heart zang organ with spirit and will unguarded, fear, terror, fright and alarm, frequent abstraction, easy forgetfulness, restless sleep, dreams of crossing dangers, and all heart disorders.

suanzaoren (sansonin; jujube seed; lightly stir-fried, skin removed)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng; 1 liang each)　chensha (cinnabar; finely ground and water-ground; half a liang)　ruxiang (frankincense; ground in a mortar set in a basin of water; 1 *fen*)

Grind these four ingredients together evenly and form pills the size of a pellet with refined honey. Take one pill each time dissolved in warm wine; jujube decoction is also fine; on an empty stomach and at bedtime.

## Notes

Ningzhi gao (Chunyou-era additions): for fear, fright, abstraction, forgetfulness, insomnia, nightmares and all heart disorders from heart deficiency.
- Jujube seed and ginseng nourish the heart, cinnabar calms, frankincense is added.
