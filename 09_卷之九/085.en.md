# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 085 成煉鍾乳散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Chenglian zhongru san (Refined Stalactite Powder)

It treats nursing women with scant qi and declining blood, rough, obstructed vessels and extremely scant milk.

zhongru fen (stalactite powder)

Using the refined kind, take 2 *qian* each time mixed in strongly decocted rhaponticum decoction.

## Notes

Chenglian zhongru san (bureau additions): for insufficient milk from declining qi and blood in nursing women.
- One ingredient, refined stalactite powder, taken with rhaponticum decoction.
