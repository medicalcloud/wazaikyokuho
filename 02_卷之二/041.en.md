# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 041 消風百解散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xiaofeng baijie san (Wind-Dispersing Hundred-Resolutions Powder)

It is suitable for cold damage in any season with headache and stiff neck, vigorous fever with aversion to cold, vexing pain of the body, weary limbs and panting fatigue on walking; and for cough from cold congestion with nasal congestion and a heavy voice, thick sticky mucus and spittle, congested exuberant phlegm and drool, and rapid breathing with fullness and oppression.

jingjie (schizonepeta)　baizhi (angelica dahurica)　chenpi (aged tangerine peel; washed, white pith removed)　cangzhu (black atractylodes)　mahuang (ephedra; nodes removed; 4 liang each)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried; 2 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder. For each dose take two large *qian* with one large *zhan* of water, three slices of ginger and one smoked plum, decoct together down to seven-tenths and take it warm, at any time; or mix it in tea or wine. To disperse evil wind, decoct it with three *cun* of scallion white with roots.

## Notes

Xiaofeng baijie san (Baoqing-era additions): for headache, stiff neck, chills and fever and body pain in seasonal cold damage (colds), and for cough, nasal congestion and phlegm from cold.
- Ephedra, schizonepeta and angelica dahurica release the exterior; black atractylodes and tangerine peel remove damp and phlegm.
