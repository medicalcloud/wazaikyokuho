# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷下 008 論脾痛嘔逆 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On spleen pain with vomiting. For severe pain of the spleen and stomach with vomiting counterflow and inability to take food, or with panting and hurried breathing added, give Qiqi tang. If vomiting is added, give Renshen huoxiang tang. For diarrhoea, give Da chenxiang wan, Renshen doukou san or Jianzhong tang. For sudden violent heart pain, give Suhexiang wan or Zhuangqi chenxiang wan.

## Notes

On spleen pain with vomiting: Qiqi tang for severe pain with vomiting, refusal of food or panting; Renshen huoxiang tang with vomiting; Da chenxiang wan, Renshen doukou san or Jianzhong tang with diarrhoea; Suhexiang wan or Zhuangqi chenxiang wan for sudden violent heart pain.
