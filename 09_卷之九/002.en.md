# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 002 沢蘭丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zelan wan (Takurangan; Lycopus Pill)

It treats postpartum taxation damage with deficiency and weakness of the zang-fu organs not yet recovered, disharmony of qi and blood, emaciated weak limbs, fatigue with little strength, a withered yellow complexion, constant fright palpitations, much sweating with a liking for lying down, and inability to eat. Taken regularly every day within a hundred days after childbirth, it strengthens qi, augments blood, warms the lower zang organs and improves eating.

huangqi (ogi; astragalus)　zelan (takuran; lycopus; stalks removed)　niuxi (goshitsu; achyranthes; shoots removed, soaked in wine overnight)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng; rhizome head removed)　chi shizhi (red halloysite; calcined; 1 liang each)　fuzi (bushi; aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　muxiang (mokko; costus root)　bixie (dioscorea hypoglauca)　bai fuling (bukuryo; white poria; skin removed)　xuduan (zokudan; dipsacus; 3 *fen* each)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　xiongqiong (Sichuan lovage)　baizhu (byakujutsu; white atractylodes)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried)　danggui (toki; Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed, chopped, lightly stir-fried)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried until slightly red; half a liang each)　shu gan dihuang (kanjio; prepared rehmannia; washed clean, steamed with wine, dried over a fire; 1½ liang)

Powder the above and form pills the size of wutong seeds with refined honey. Take thirty pills each time with warm rice water, on an empty stomach before meals.

## Notes

Zelan wan: for postpartum taxation damage with weak zang organs, disharmony of qi and blood, wasting, weakness, sallow complexion, fright palpitations, sweating, a tendency to stay in bed and poor appetite; regular use for the hundred days after childbirth is recommended.
- Astragalus, ginseng, atractylodes, poria, licorice, prepared rehmannia, angelica and lovage supplement qi and blood; lycopus and achyranthes move blood; aconite, cinnamon and dried ginger warm the lower body; red halloysite, dioscorea, dipsacus and costus are added.
