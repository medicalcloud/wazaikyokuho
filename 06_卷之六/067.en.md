# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 067 罌粟湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yingsu tang (Poppy Decoction)

It treats all of the following: deficient qi of the intestines and stomach with disharmony of cold and heat, or internal damage of the spleen and stomach from raw and cold food and drink, or excessive drinking, with gripping pain of the navel and abdomen, diarrhoea with borborygmus, dysentery red or white, tenesmus, very frequent day and night, and reduced eating; as well as the intestines and stomach having received damp, with swelling distension and deficiency rumbling, discharge like bean juice or of fresh blood.

aiye (mugwort leaf; stalks removed)　heidou (black soybean; stir-fried, skins removed)　chenpi (aged tangerine peel; white pith removed)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried; 2 liang each)　yingsuqiao (poppy husk; stalk removed, roasted with honey; 4 liang)

Chop the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water, decoct down to one *zhan*, remove the dregs and take it warm before meals. Avoid raw, cold, greasy and toxic things. For children adjust the dose according to age.

## Notes

Yingsu tang (Wu Zhige's additions): for gripping umbilical pain, diarrhoea, borborygmus, red or white dysentery, tenesmus and poor appetite from deficient intestinal and stomach qi, raw and cold food or excess drinking, and for abdominal distension and stools like bean juice or of fresh blood from damp in the intestines and stomach.
- Poppy husk astringes; mugwort and dried ginger warm; black soybean, tangerine peel and licorice are added.
