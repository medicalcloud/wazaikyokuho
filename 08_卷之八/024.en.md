# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 024 寸金丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cunjin wan (Inch of Gold Pill)

It treats weak, deficient original yang with cold qi attacking and surging, swelling and pain of the bladder and small intestine, sometimes in the heart and flanks, drawing into the lower abdomen and connecting with the genital region, so that the body is averse to cold with gripping pain.

chushizi (broussonetia fruit)　chuanlianzi (toosendan fruit; stir-fried; 1½ liang each)　badou (hazu; croton seed; stir-fried; 7)　quanxie (whole scorpion; stir-fried; 40)　danggui (toki; Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed, soaked in wine overnight; 1½ liang)

Make the above into a fine powder, knead with flour paste made from the wine in which the angelica was soaked, and form pills the size of euryale seeds. Swallow two or three pills on an empty stomach with warm wine or salt water, two doses in succession.

## Notes

Cunjin wan (Wu Zhige's additions): for swelling and pain of the bladder and small intestine (mounting) from weak original yang with cold qi attacking, with pain drawing from the heart and flanks into the lower abdomen and genitals and aversion to cold.
- Toosendan fruit, broussonetia fruit, scorpion, croton seed and angelica.
