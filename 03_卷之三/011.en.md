# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 011 理中湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Lizhong tang (Middle-Regulating Decoction)

[It treats] disharmony of the spleen and stomach with cold in the middle surging upward, counterflow fullness of the chest and flanks, wringing pain of the heart and abdomen, phlegm counterflow and nausea, or occasional vomiting, deficiency glomus below the heart, separation and obstruction, reduced eating, shortness of breath, emaciation and exhaustion; it warms the middle, drives out water, stops sweating and removes damp. It also treats cold-damp of the stomach and intestines with pouring diarrhoea, water and grain not separated and thunderous rumbling in the abdomen; cold damage and seasonal qi with interior cold and exterior heat, vomiting and diarrhoea of sudden turmoil and reversal cold of the hands and feet; chest impediment with heart pain, and counterflow and bound qi.

renshen (ginseng)　gancao (licorice; stir-fried)　baizhu (white atractylodes)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried; 3 liang each)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water, decoct to obtain a medium *zhan*, remove the dregs and take it fairly hot, on an empty stomach before meals.

## Notes

Lizhong tang: for counterflow fullness, abdominal pain, nausea, vomiting, glomus and poor appetite from spleen-stomach deficiency cold, for cold-damp diarrhoea, taiyin cold damage and sudden turmoil with cold limbs, and for chest impediment with heart pain (Renshen tang of the *Jingui yaolüe*).
- The decoction (boiled powder) of the same four ingredients as Lizhong wan (juan 3, no. 005).
