# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之四 030 養中湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yangzhong tang (Middle-Nourishing Decoction)

It treats the lungs and stomach having received cold, with cough and much phlegm, chest fullness and shortness of breath, loss of voice, going on day and night, and reduced eating, whether of many years or recent.

banxia (hange) qu (pinellia leaven; roasted; 8 *qian*)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; scorched)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed; half a liang each)　yingsuqiao (poppy husk; stalk and cap removed, roasted with honey; 2½ liang)

Make the above into a fine powder. For each dose take one large *qian* with one *zhan* of water and four slices of fresh ginger, decoct together down to seven-tenths and take it at a comfortable temperature, at any time.

## Notes

Yangzhong tang (Wu Zhige's additions): for productive cough, chest fullness, shortness of breath, loss of voice and poor appetite when the lungs and stomach have received cold.
- A large amount of poppy husk astringes cough; pinellia leaven transforms phlegm; cinnamon warms; licorice harmonizes. Poppy husk is a strong antitussive and is a controlled substance today.
