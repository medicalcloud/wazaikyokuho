# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 044 神朮散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shenzhu san (Divine Atractylodes Powder)

It treats seasonal pestilence with headache, stiff neck, fever with aversion to cold and body pain, as well as wind damage with nasal congestion, a heavy voice, cough and a clouded head.

cangzhu (black atractylodes; soaked overnight in rice-washing water, sliced and dried over a fire; 5 liang)　gaoben (kohon; Chinese lovage; earth removed)　xiang baizhi (byakushi; fragrant angelica dahurica)　xixin (saishin; asarum; leaves and earth removed)　qianghuo (notopterygium; rhizome head removed)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried; 1 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one *zhan* of water, three slices of ginger and three *cun* of scallion white, decoct down to seven-tenths and take it warm, at any time. If one feels nasal congestion from wind damage, simply mix it in scallion tea.

## Notes

Shenzhu san (Chunyou-era additions): for headache, stiff neck, chills and fever and body pain in seasonal pestilence, and for nasal congestion, cough and heavy head in wind damage.
- Based on black atractylodes, with Chinese lovage, angelica dahurica, asarum, notopterygium and Sichuan lovage to scatter exterior wind-cold-damp; it is close to the later Jiuwei qianghuo tang.
