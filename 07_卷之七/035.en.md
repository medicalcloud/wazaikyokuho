# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之七 035 五香散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wuxiang san (Gokosan; Five Aromatics Powder)

It treats swollen, painful throat with the various malign qi bound and blocking so that there is no passage; it should be taken urgently.

muxiang (mokko; costus root)　chenxiang (aquilaria)　jishexiang (clove)　xunluxiang (frankincense; 1 liang each)　shexiang (jako; musk; ground separately; 3 *fen*)

Pound and sift the above into a powder, add the musk and grind evenly. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one medium *zhan* of water, decoct down to six-tenths and take it warm, at any time.

## Notes

Wuxiang san (bureau additions): for swollen, painful throat and blockage of the throat by bound malign qi.
- Five aromatics—costus, aquilaria, clove, frankincense and musk—to move qi.
