# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 041 神効托裡散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shenxiao tuoli san (Divinely Effective Interior-Supporting Powder)

It treats abscesses, flat-abscesses, effusions of the back, intestinal abscess, breast abscess and nameless swelling toxins with hot, red swelling and pain, aversion to cold and vigorous fever resembling cold damage, regardless of whether the patient is old, young or weak.

rendongcao (honeysuckle stem; stalks removed)　huangqi (astragalus; rhizome head removed; 5 liang each)　danggui (Chinese angelica; 1 liang 2 *qian*)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried; 8 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder. For each dose decoct 2 *qian* in one and a half *zhan* of wine down to one *zhan*. If the illness is in the upper body take it after meals; if in the lower body, before meals. After a short while give a second dose. Keep the dregs and apply them externally. Those not yet suppurating disperse internally; those already suppurating burst at once.

## Notes

Shenxiao tuoli san (Baoqing-era additions): for red, painful swelling, chills and high fever in abscesses, effusions of the back, intestinal and breast abscess and nameless swelling toxins, regardless of age or constitution; it disperses unformed lesions and brings formed ones to burst.
- Honeysuckle stem, astragalus, angelica and licorice decocted in wine; the dregs are applied externally.
