# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 088 体玄子借地法 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Tixuanzi's Method of Borrowing Ground

The incantation says: "I borrow ten paces to the east, ten paces to the west, ten paces to the south, ten paces to the north, ten paces above and ten paces below. Within these walls, forty-odd paces, I borrow ground for a safe delivery, lest there be defilement. Whether there be the Divine King of the Eastern Sea, the Divine King of the Western Sea, the Divine King of the Southern Sea or the Divine King of the Northern Sea, or the Day-Roaming General or the Lady White Tiger—go far away ten *zhang*. Xuanyuan and Zhaoyao—rise high ten *zhang*. Heavenly Talisman and Earth Axis—go into the earth ten *zhang*. Let this ground be empty and free. May the parturient woman, Madam So-and-so, dwell in peace without hindrance and without fear, protected by all the gods, with the hundred evils driven away. Quickly, quickly, as the law commands!"

## Notes

Tixuanzi's method of borrowing ground: an incantation borrowing the delivery space from the spirits—ten paces in each direction and above and below—ordering the divine kings of the four seas, the Day-Roaming General, Lady White Tiger, Xuanyuan, Zhaoyao, the Heavenly Talisman and Earth Axis to withdraw, and praying for a safe delivery. Tixuanzi appears to be a Daoist sobriquet (not verified).
