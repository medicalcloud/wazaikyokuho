# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之七 005 秦皮散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qinpi san (Ash Bark Powder)

It treats wind toxin in adults and children with red, swollen, painful eyes, itching and roughness with discharge and tears, dimness and aversion to light.

qinpi (ash bark)　huashi (talc from Guifu; pounded)　huanglian (coptis; fibrous roots removed; 10 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder. Each time steep half a *qian* in boiling water, remove the dregs and wash the eyes frequently while warm.

## Notes

Qinpi san: for red, swollen, painful eyes, itching, grittiness, discharge, tearing, dimness and photophobia from wind toxin.
- An external eyewash of three ingredients (ash bark, talc, coptis) steeped in hot water.
