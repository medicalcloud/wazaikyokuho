# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 029 論傷寒陰厥証 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On the yin reversal pattern of cold damage. If from the onset of illness the limbs are cold, gooseflesh rises on the body, the bowels are irregular with diarrhea or vomiting, this is cold reversal; give Lizhong wan (Richugan), Lizhong tang (Richuto) and the like.

## Notes

On yin reversal: cold limbs, gooseflesh and irregular bowels with diarrhea or vomiting from the very onset indicate cold (yin) reversal, treated with Lizhong wan or Lizhong tang.
