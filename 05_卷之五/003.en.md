# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 003 金釵石斛丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jinchai shihu wan (Kinsasekkokugan; Golden-Hairpin Dendrobium Pill)

It can treat all diseases of deficiency cold of the original zang organ: insufficient genuine qi and weak original zang organ, with a clouded head and swollen face, dim eyes and tinnitus, weary limbs, aching pain in all the joints, dull pain under the feet, difficulty walking, an emaciated body, a yellow-black complexion, falling hair at the temples, swelling and itching of the scalp, a clouded, exhausted spirit, frequently cold hands and feet, stuffiness and oppression of the heart and chest, stabbing pain around the navel, aching knees and shins with inability to stand long, stiffness of the lower back and spine with inability to bend forward and back, distension and fullness of both flanks, undigested water and grain, abdominal pain with stabbing qi coming at any time, a hanging sensation in the heart with sour belching, retching and nausea, a bitter mouth and dry throat, tasteless food, abstraction and forgetfulness, hurried breathing with dyspnea and exhaustion, fright and fear in dreams at night, palpitation and night sweats, slippery frequent urination, or rough painful urination. Taken regularly it supplements the five zang organs, harmonizes the blood and vessels, preserves the complexion, moistens the hair, improves eating, fills out the flesh and greatly strengthens the sinews and bones.

chuanjiao (Sichuan pepper; seeds removed, lightly stir-fried to sweat out the oil)　huluba (fenugreek; stir-fried)　bajitian (hagekiten; morinda; core removed)　dilong (earthworm; earth removed, stir-fried; 4 liang each)　cangzhu (black atractylodes; loose skin removed)　wuyao (lindera; 16 liang each)　chuan wutou (uzu; Sichuan aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　qianghuo (notopterygium; rhizome head removed)　huixiang (fennel; stir-fried)　chixiaodou (sekishozu; rice bean)　malinzi (iris seed; stir-fried with vinegar)　jinlingzi (toosendan fruit; stir-fried with bran)　shihu (sekkoku; dendrobium; roots removed; 8 liang each)　qingyan (blue salt; 2 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with flour paste boiled in wine. Take twenty pills each time with warm wine, or salt water, on an empty stomach before meals.

## Notes

Jinchai shihu wan: for all illnesses of deficiency cold of the original zang organ (kidney) with insufficient genuine qi—head and facial symptoms, failing eyes and ears, weary limbs, joint pain, difficulty walking, emaciation, hair loss, cold limbs, abdominal pain, back pain, indigestion, forgetfulness, night sweats and frequent urination.
- Though named after golden-hairpin dendrobium (a choice grade of dendrobium), the formula warms with Sichuan pepper, fenugreek, morinda, aconite, fennel and blue salt, and regulates damp and qi with black atractylodes, lindera, notopterygium, toosendan, iris seed, earthworm and rice bean.
