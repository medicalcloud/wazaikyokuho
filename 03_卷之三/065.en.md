# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 065 新法半夏湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xinfa banxia tang (New Method Pinellia Decoction)

It treats disharmony of the spleen and stomach with stagnant qi in the middle duct, chronic cold and prolonged fluid retention accumulating without dispersing, stabbing pain of the heart and abdomen, distension of the flanks and ribs, vomiting of phlegm and water, belching and acid swallowing, drunkenness with vomiting of wine, hiccough and nausea, headache with vexing thirst, lassitude with a liking for lying down, and no desire for food.

chenpi (chinpi; aged tangerine peel; white pith removed)　shenqu (medicated leaven; stir-fried; 4 liang each)　caoguo (tsaoko; roasted, skin removed)　banxia (hange) qu (pinellia leaven; stir-fried; 2 liang 3 *qian* each)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried; 4 liang)　dingpi (clove bark)　muxiang (mokko; costus root)　bai fuling (bukuryo; white poria; 7½ *qian* each)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; 4½ *qian*)

Make the above into a fine powder. Take 1 *qian* each time stirred into salt water, at any time. Taken regularly it warms the middle, breaks up phlegm, opens the stomach, strengthens the spleen, disperses wine and improves eating.

## Notes

Xinfa banxia tang (Baoqing-era additions): for abdominal pain, flank distension, vomiting of phlegm and water, acid reflux, hangover, hiccough, headache, thirst, lassitude and poor appetite from spleen-stomach disharmony, middle-duct qi stagnation, chronic cold and prolonged fluid retention.
- Pinellia leaven, tangerine peel and poria (in the manner of Erchen tang [Nichinto]) transform phlegm; medicated leaven digests food; dried ginger, tsaoko, clove bark and costus warm and move qi.
- The base text writes 嘁逆 for 噦逆 (hiccough).
