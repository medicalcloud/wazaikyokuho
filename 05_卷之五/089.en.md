# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 089 茯菟丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Futu wan (Poria and Dodder Pill)

It treats insufficient heart qi, excessive thought and anxiety, deficient, damaged kidney channel and insecure true yang with dribbling after urination, white turbid urine and frequent leakage in dreams.

tusizi [dodder seed; 5 liang (one version: 10 liang)]　bai fuling [white poria; 3 liang (one version: 5 liang)]　shilianzi [lotus fruit; shells removed; 2 liang (one version: 3 liang; one version has 7 liang of Liaodong schisandra with stalks removed)]

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with paste boiled in wine (one version uses 6 liang of Huai Chinese yam). Take thirty pills each time (one version: fifty or sixty) on an empty stomach with salt water. Taken regularly it settles and benefits the heart-spirit, supplements deficiency, nourishes the blood and clears the urine.

## Notes

Futu wan (bureau additions; the last formula of juan 5): nearly the same indications as the preceding Michuan yusuo dan (juan 5, no. 085); for dribbling, turbid urine and dream emissions from insufficient heart qi and kidney deficiency.
- Three ingredients (dodder, poria, lotus fruit). The variant notes on amounts ("one version") are as in the base text.
