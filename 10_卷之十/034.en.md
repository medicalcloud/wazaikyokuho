# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 034 清涼飲子 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qingliang yinzi (Clearing and Cooling Drink)

It treats children with congested, excess blood vessels and heat generated in the bowels and zang organs, red cheeks and much thirst, restlessness of the five hearts, unquiet sleep and startled twitching of the limbs; as well as untimely nursing and feeding and loss of proper warmth and cold, making the child's blood and qi disordered and the intestines and stomach disharmonious, with warm, vigorous fever dragging on and about to become latent heat, or unceasing vigorous fever about to break out as fright epilepsy. It is also suitable for wind-heat nodes, sores and boils of the head and face, red eyes and sore throat, residual toxin of sores and rashes, and all congestion and stagnation.

danggui (toki; Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed, soaked in wine)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried)　dahuang (daio; rhubarb; steamed, dried over a fire)　chi shaoyao (shakuyaku; red peony root)

Take equal parts of the above and make into a coarse powder. For each dose take 1 *qian* with one medium *zhan* of water, decoct down to seven-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm, adjusted for the child's size and deficiency or excess, aiming for slightly loose stools; after meals and at bedtime.

## Notes

Qingliang yinzi: for red cheeks, thirst, vexing heat of the five hearts, insomnia and startled twitching from congested blood and heat in the zang organs of children; for disordered blood and qi, latent heat and impending fright epilepsy from irregular feeding; and for wind-heat nodes, sores of the head and face, red eyes, sore throat and residual toxin of eruptions.
- Four ingredients (angelica, red peony, rhubarb, licorice: Sishun qingliang yinzi), aiming for a slight purge.
