# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷上 010 論炮炙三品薬石類例・獣部 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Animal Section

Dragon bone: to use, choose pieces that stick to the tongue. First soak overnight in wine, dry over a fire, pound finely, sift, grind to powder, water-grind three times and dry in the midday sun. In haste, simply boil in wine and dry over a fire. For other processing, follow the formula.
Musk and bovine bezoar: to use, first grind separately until fine, then use in medicines.
Donkey-hide gelatin and other gelatins: to use, first pound, stir-fry until it bubbles, dries and forms beads, then use in medicines.
Deer antler velvet: to use, take eggplant-shaped velvet attached to the crown bone, first singe off the hair cleanly, cut into lengths of about three *cun*, soak in wine for a day and roast over a gentle fire until brittle; or smear with ghee and roast; process according to the formula.
Tiger bone: to use, first split open and remove the marrow, then smear with wine, ghee and the like and roast repeatedly until yellow-red.
Seal genitals: to use, first soak in wine and roast repeatedly over a gentle fire until cooked, then use in medicines.

## Notes

The Animal section of the processing examples: dragon bone (pieces that stick to the tongue, soaked in wine and water-ground), musk, bovine bezoar, donkey-hide gelatin (fried into beads), deer antler velvet (hair singed, soaked in wine and roasted), tiger bone and seal genitals.
