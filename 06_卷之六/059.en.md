# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 059 水煮木香丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shuizhu muxiang wan (Water-Boiled Costus Root Pill)

The patterns treated are the same as for the earlier Shuizhu muxiang wan.

chenpi (chinpi; aged tangerine peel; white pith removed)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; stir-fried)　qingpi (green tangerine peel; white removed)　muxiang (mokko; costus root; 1 liang 1 *fen* each)　bai shaoyao (shakuyaku; white peony root)　danggui (toki; Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed; 2 liang each)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried; 1½ liang)　hezi pi (chebule fruit rind; stone removed; 2½ liang)　yingsuqiao (poppy husk; stalk and cap removed, stir-fried yellow with honey; 8 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills with refined honey, six to each liang. For each dose boil one pill in one *zhan* of water down to seven-tenths and take it warm with the dregs on an empty stomach; it may also be taken at any time.

## Notes

Shuizhu muxiang wan (from here the layer of Wu Zhige's additions): the same indications as the earlier Shuizhu muxiang wan (juan 6, no. 056); a different formula adding aged tangerine peel, white peony and dried ginger, with more poppy husk.
