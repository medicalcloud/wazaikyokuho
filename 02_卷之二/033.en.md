# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 033 桂苓丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Guiling wan (Keiryogan; Cinnamon and Poria Pill)

It greatly resolves summer heat toxin.

rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed, not exposed to fire)　fuling (bukuryo; poria; coarse skin removed; equal parts)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills with refined honey, eight pills to each liang. Take one pill each time, chewed, with freshly drawn water or hot water; it may also be dissolved.

## Notes

Guiling wan (Shaoxing-era additions): resolves summer heat toxin.
- Two ingredients, cinnamon and poria, presumably warming and draining fluid retention in summer.
