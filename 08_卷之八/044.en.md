# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 044 接骨散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jiegu san (Sekkotsusan; Bone-Setting Powder)

It treats falls from heights and injuries and fractures from being chased by horses, with severed sinews and shattered bones and unbearable pain. It joins bones and reconnects sinews, stops pain and quickens the blood.

dingfen (lead white)　danggui (toki) (Chinese angelica; 1 *qian* each)　pengsha (borax; 1½ *qian*)

Make the above into a fine powder. Take 2 *qian* each time mixed in decocted sappanwood decoction; after taking it, drink sappanwood decoction from time to time.

## Notes

Jiegu san (from here the layer of Wu Zhige's additions): for severe pain from fractures and torn sinews after falls or horse injuries; said to join bones, reconnect sinews and quicken the blood.
- Three ingredients (lead white, angelica, borax) taken with sappanwood decoction.
