# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 036 硃砂丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zhusha wan (Cinnabar Pill)

It settles the heart-spirit, transforms phlegm and drool, frees the throat and diaphragm and stops vexing thirst.

pengsha (borax; ground; 1 *fen*)　zhusha (cinnabar; ground and water-ground; 50 liang)　shexiang (musk; ground)　meihua nao (plum-blossom borneol; ground; half a liang each)　naozi (borneol; ground)　yaxiao (refined mirabilite; dehydrated; 1 liang each)　gancao (licorice; steeped for juice and boiled down to a paste; 5 jin)　hanshuishi (calcite; burned red-hot, ground; 4 liang)

Grind the above evenly, knead with the licorice paste and make a hundred pills to each liang. Hold one pill in the mouth to dissolve. For children who often cry out in fright at night, dissolve it in mint water.

## Notes

Zhusha wan: settles the heart-spirit, transforms phlegm, frees the throat and stops vexing thirst; also for night terrors and night crying in children.
- Mainly cinnabar, with borneol, musk, borax, refined mirabilite and calcite, made into tiny pills with licorice paste and dissolved in the mouth.
