# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之七 031 硼砂散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Pengsha san (Hoshasan; Borax Powder)

It treats adults and children with sudden throat impediment, blocked and obstructed, swollen and painful with sores, an unclear voice, wind congestion and phlegm toxin, and nosebleed.

shanyao (Chinese yam; raw; 6 jin)　naozi (borneol; ground; 7 liang)　yaxiao (refined mirabilite; raw; 24 liang)　shexiang (jako; musk; ground; 4 liang)　gancao (kanzo; licorice)　pengsha (borax; ground; 20 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder. Take half a *qian* each time mixed with hot water like tea.

## Notes

Pengsha san (from here the layer of Chunyou-era additions): for blockage, swelling, pain, sores and hoarseness from sudden throat impediment, for wind congestion and phlegm toxin, and for nosebleed.
- Borax, refined mirabilite, borneol and musk clear heat; Chinese yam and licorice are added; taken mixed with hot water.
- The base text reads "shexiang (ground, 4 liang, licorice) pengsha (ground, 20 liang each)", with "licorice" inside the parentheses; we read licorice as a separate drug, with "20 liang each" applying to licorice and borax.
