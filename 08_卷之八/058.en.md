# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 058 補損当帰散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Busun danggui san (Damage-Repairing Angelica Powder)

It treats falls from horses or carts and beatings with injured wrists and broken arms and ceaseless crying out. Taking this medicine, within the space of a breath there is no longer severe pain; after three days of taking it the sinews and bones should be joined; divine effect.

zelan (takuran; lycopus; processed)　fuzi (bushi; aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed; 1 *fen* each)　danggui (toki; Chinese angelica; stir-fried)　shujiao (shokusho; Sichuan pepper; stir-fried to sweat out the oil)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried)　guixin (cinnamon heart; 3 *fen* each)　xiongqiong (Sichuan lovage; stir-fried; 6 *fen*)

Make the above into a fine powder. Take 2 *qian* each time mixed in warm wine, three times a day. Avoid seaweed, Chinese cabbage, raw scallion, pork and cold water.

## Notes

Busun danggui san (from here the layer of bureau additions): for severe pain from injured wrists and broken arms after falls from horses or carts or beatings; said to relieve pain at once and join the bones in three days.
- Angelica, lovage and lycopus move blood; aconite, Sichuan pepper and cinnamon heart warm and stop pain; licorice is added.
