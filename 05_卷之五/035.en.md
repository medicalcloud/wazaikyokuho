# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 035 楽令建中湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Leling jianzhong tang (Leling's Middle-Fortifying Decoction)

It treats taxation damage of blood and qi, deficiency and damage of the five viscera and six bowels, borborygmus and a weary spirit, and disharmony of the nutritive and defensive qi; it dispels deficiency heat and removes the hundred illnesses.

qianhu (peucedanum)　xixin (asarum)　huangqi (astragalus; smeared with honey and roasted)　renshen (ginseng)　guixin (cinnamon heart)　jupi (tangerine peel; white pith removed)　danggui (Chinese angelica; washed free of earth)　bai shaoyao (white peony root)　fuling (poria; skin removed)　maimendong (ophiopogon; core removed)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried; 1 liang each)　banxia (pinellia; washed seven times in hot water, sliced; 7½ *qian*)

Cut the above into pieces. For each dose take 4 *qian* with four slices of ginger, one jujube and one *zhan* of water, decoct down to seven-tenths, remove the dregs and take it slightly hot, at any time.

## Notes

Leling jianzhong tang (Chunyou-era additions): for borborygmus, fatigue, disharmony of the nutritive and defensive qi and deficiency heat from taxation damage of blood and qi and deficiency of the viscera.
- Huangqi jianzhong tang (astragalus, cinnamon, peony, licorice) plus ginseng, angelica, poria, ophiopogon, pinellia, tangerine peel, peucedanum and asarum (in the lineage of the Leling huangqi tang of the *Qianjin fang*).
