# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之四 007 玉芝丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yuzhi wan (Jade Mushroom Pill)

It treats wind congestion with phlegm repletion, clouding and dizziness of the head and eyes, cough with vexing fullness, discomfort of the throat and diaphragm, vomiting and nausea, a clouded, confused mind, palpitation with a hot face, and thick sticky phlegm and spittle.

renshen (ginseng; rhizome head removed)　dried bohe leaves (mint)　bai fuling (white poria; skin removed)　baifan (alum; calcined)　nanxing (arisaema; soaked in rice-washing water for one day and night, dried over a fire; 30 liang each)　banxia (pinellia; washed seven times in hot water, powdered, pounded with ginger juice and made into leaven; 60 liang)

Powder the above and form pills the size of wutong seeds with flour paste boiled in ginger juice. Take twenty pills each time with ginger decoction, after meals. If phlegm is exuberant with dry heat, take them with mint decoction.

## Notes

Yuzhi wan: for dizziness, cough, vexing fullness, throat discomfort, vomiting, clouded mind, palpitation, facial heat and sticky phlegm from wind-phlegm repletion.
- Pinellia leaven, arisaema and alum transform phlegm; mint scatters wind; ginseng and poria supplement.
