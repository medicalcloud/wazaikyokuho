# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 106 杏霜湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xingshuang tang (Apricot Frost Drink)

It regulates lung qi, frees the chest and diaphragm, treats cough and stops phlegm counterflow.

sumi (awabei; millet; stir-fried; 1 *dou* 6 sheng)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; stir-fried; 10½ jin)　yan (salt; stir-fried; 16 jin)　xingren (kyonin; apricot kernel; skin and tips removed, stir-fried with bran, ground separately; 10 jin)

Powder the above. Take 1 *qian* each time mixed with boiling water, at any time. Taken regularly it gladdens and moistens the complexion and makes the skin lustrous.

## Notes

Xingshuang tang ("Various drinks"): a drink to regulate lung qi, free the chest and stop cough and phlegm; with regular use said to improve complexion and skin.
- Apricot kernel, millet, licorice and salt.
