# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 068 神仙沈麝丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shenxian chenshe wan (Immortals' Aquilaria and Musk Pill)

It treats all unbearable qi pain.

moyao (myrrh; ground)　xuejie (dragon's blood; ground)　chenxiang (aquilaria; chopped)　shexiang (musk; finely ground)　chensha (cinnabar; 1 liang each)　muxiang (costus root; half a liang)　gancao (licorice; 2 liang)

Powder the above and knead with licorice simmered into a paste. Chew one pill each time and take it with ginger and salt decoction; for blood qi, with vinegar decoction. Wan Junni, magistrate of Songzi, treasured this medicine. For women's unbearable postpartum blood pain and qi pain, a single pill cures at once. Mr Wan kept it as a divine secret and whenever someone fell ill would give only half a pill, yet often that too cured. Its divine effect cannot be fully described.

## Notes

Shenxian chenshe wan (Baoqing-era additions): for all unbearable qi pain and women's postpartum blood and qi pain.
- Myrrh and dragon's blood move the blood; aquilaria, costus and musk move qi; cinnabar calms. Said to have been a secret treasure of Wan Junni, magistrate of Songzi.
