# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 036 兪山人降気湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yu shanren jiangqi tang (Recluse Yu's Qi-Lowering Decoction)

It treats deficient yang attacking upward, qi failing to rise and descend, exuberance above with deficiency below, congestion of the diaphragm with phlegm excess, dyspnea and fullness, a dry, uncomfortable throat, vexing thirst with drinking, clouding and dizziness of the head and eyes, weakness of the lower back and legs, weary limbs and cough. It also treats wind-damp leg qi.

qianhu (peucedanum)　wujiapi (gokahi; acanthopanax bark; smeared with ginger juice and roasted)　houpo (koboku; magnolia bark; soaked overnight with ginger, stir-fried)　huangqi (ogi; astragalus; rhizome head removed)　danggui (toki; Chinese angelica)　zisuzi (perilla seed; lightly stir-fried)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried)　rougui (cinnamon bark; not exposed to fire)　chenpi (chinpi; aged tangerine peel; white pith removed)　banxia qu (pinellia leaven; 1 liang each; roasted)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng)　fuzi (bushi; aconite; blast-fried, tips removed)　qianghuo (notopterygium)　jiegeng (kikyo; platycodon; stir-fried; half a liang each)

Make these fifteen ingredients together into a coarse powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water, adding three perilla leaves, three slices of fresh ginger and one jujube, decoct down to seven-tenths, remove the dregs and take it after meals.

## Notes

Yu shanren jiangqi tang (Shaoxing-era additions): for exuberance above with deficiency below, deficient yang attacking upward with dyspnea fullness from phlegm, dry throat, thirst, dizziness, weak legs and cough, and for wind-damp leg qi. A formula of a recluse named Yu.
- Perilla seed, peucedanum, magnolia bark, tangerine peel and pinellia leaven send qi and phlegm down; cinnamon, aconite and dried ginger warm the lower part; ginseng, astragalus and angelica supplement. Its composition is close to the later Suzi jiangqi tang (Soshikokito) (juan 3, no. 056).
