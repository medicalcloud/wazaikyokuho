# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 078 小地黄丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xiao dihuang wan (Minor Rehmannia Pill)

It treats acid in the heart (heartburn) with vomiting of clear water during pregnancy, and abdominal pain with inability to eat or drink.

renshen (ginseng; rhizome head removed)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried; equal parts)

Powder the above and form pills the size of wutong seeds with raw rehmannia juice. Take fifty pills each time with rice broth, before meals.

## Notes

Xiao dihuang wan (bureau additions): for heartburn, vomiting of clear fluid, abdominal pain and inability to eat in pregnancy.
- Two ingredients, ginseng and dried ginger, made into pills with raw rehmannia juice (an idea close to the *Jingui yaolüe* Ganjiang renshen banxia wan).
