# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之七 033 吹喉散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Chuihou san (Throat-Blowing Powder)

It can treat great heat of the triple burner with sores of the mouth and tongue, swollen, blocked throat and a clouded, oppressed mind.

puhuang (typha pollen; 1 liang)　penxiao (basin mirabilite; 8 liang)　qingdai (indigo; 1½ liang)

Using 1 sheng of fresh mint juice, put the basin mirabilite, indigo and typha pollen together in a porcelain jar, boil dry over a gentle fire and grind fine. Each time sprinkle one *zi* or half a *qian* into the mouth; after a while drool comes out; swallowing it does no harm. For swelling and pain in the throat, put about half a *qian* of the medicine into a tube and blow it in forcefully; it never fails to work at once.

## Notes

Chuihou san (Wu Zhige's additions): for mouth sores, swollen, blocked throat and clouded mind from great heat of the triple burner.
- Basin mirabilite, indigo and typha pollen boiled down in mint juice; the powder is sprinkled in the mouth or blown into the throat through a tube.
