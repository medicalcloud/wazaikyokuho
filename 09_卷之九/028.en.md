# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 028 催生丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cuisheng dan (Labor-Hastening Elixir)

It is suitable for parturient women whose labor does not proceed smoothly, with difficult delivery, or transverse or breech presentation; divine effect.

shexiang (jako; musk; ground separately; 1 *zi*)　ruxiang (frankincense; ground separately extremely fine; 1 *fen*)　mu dingxiang (mother clove; powdered; 1 *qian*)　tu naosui (rabbit brain; from the twelfth month, skin and membranes removed, ground)

Mix the above evenly, knead with the rabbit brain into pills the size of a euryale seed kernel, dry in the shade and seal tightly in oiled paper. Take one pill with warm water, and delivery follows at once. The sign is that the child is born holding the pill in its hand—the left for a boy, the right for a girl.

## Notes

Cuisheng dan: for difficult, transverse or breech labor.
- Musk, frankincense and mother clove made into pills with twelfth-month rabbit brain. It is said the child is born holding the pill in its left hand if a boy, right if a girl.
