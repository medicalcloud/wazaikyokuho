# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 068 当帰芍薬散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Danggui shaoyao san (Angelica and Peony Powder)

It is suitable for gripping pain in the abdomen during pregnancy with tense fullness below the heart, as well as postpartum blood dizziness with internal deficiency and lack of qi, flooding and long-standing dysentery.

danggui (Chinese angelica)　fuling (poria; skin removed)　baizhu (white atractylodes; 2 liang each)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage)　zexie (alisma; 4 liang each)　bai shaoyao (white peony root; 8 liang)

Powder the above. Take 2 *qian* each time mixed in warm wine, before meals. Taken regularly it frees and eases the blood vessels, prevents abscesses and ulcers, disperses phlegm, nourishes the stomach, brightens the eyes and augments fluids.

## Notes

Danggui shaoyao san (bureau additions): for gripping abdominal pain with tense fullness below the heart in pregnancy, postpartum blood dizziness with deficiency, flooding and chronic dysentery. Still widely used today for abdominal pain in pregnancy, dysmenorrhoea, and women with cold, oedema and a tendency to anaemia.
- The same composition as the *Jingui yaolüe* Danggui shaoyao san (angelica, peony, lovage, poria, atractylodes, alisma).
