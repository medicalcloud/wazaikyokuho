# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 028 犀角丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xijiao wan (Saikakugan; Rhinoceros Horn Pill)

It removes pathogenic heat from the sanjiao and disperses all wind qi. It treats exuberant wind with phlegm excess, clouding and heaviness of the head and eyes, stiffness of the limbs and joints, congested phlegm and drool, dryness and roughness of the stomach and intestines, and difficult urination and defecation.

huanglian (oren; coptis; rootlets removed)　xijiao (saikaku; rhinoceros horn; shaved; 10 liang each)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng; rhizome head removed; 20 liang)　dahuang (daio; rhubarb; 80 liang)　hei qianniu (black morning-glory seed; stir-fry 120 liang and pound separately to obtain 60 liang of powder)

Combine the above with the morning-glory powder into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with refined honey. Take fifteen to twenty pills each time with warm water at bedtime, adjusting further according to deficiency or excess.

## Notes

Xijiao wan: a purgative formula for pathogenic heat in the sanjiao with wind-phlegm excess, heaviness of the head, and constipation and urinary difficulty.
- Large amounts of rhubarb and morning-glory seed purge, coptis and rhinoceros horn clear heat, and ginseng protects the healthy qi.
