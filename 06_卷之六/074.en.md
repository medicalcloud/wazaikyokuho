# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 074 地楡散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Diyu san (Sanguisorba Powder)

It is suitable for adults and children with deficient spleen and stomach qi and disharmony of cold and heat, dysentery with pus and blood, more red than white; or the intestines and stomach, through deficiency, permeated by heat toxin, with dysentery of pure blood, gripping pain of the navel and abdomen, tenesmus, dry mouth with vexing thirst, inhibited urination and discharge of pure fresh blood; or where, during an earlier diarrhoea, hot medicines were mistakenly taken when they should not have been, so that pent-up toxin did not disperse, accumulated in the intestines and seeped out as blood.

diyu (sanguisorba; stir-fried)　gange (dried pueraria; half a jin each)　fuling (poria; skin removed)　chi shaoyao (red peony root; 6 liang each)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried; 2 liang)　danggui (Chinese angelica; shoots removed; 3 liang)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried; 4 liang)　yingsuqiao (poppy husk; stir-fried with honey; 12 liang)

Pound and sift the above into a fine powder. Take 2 *qian* each time mixed in warm water, at any time. A three-year-old child may take half a *qian*, adjusted further according to age. It may also be taken for dysentery of pure white matter, or passing of purple-black blood, with slippery, uncontrollable intestines.

## Notes

Diyu san (from here the layer of bureau additions): for dysentery with pus and blood (more red than white) from deficient spleen and stomach qi; for dysentery of pure blood with gripping pain, tenesmus, thirst and inhibited urination from heat toxin; for bloody stools after mistaken use of hot medicines; and for white dysentery, purple-black blood and incontinence. The earlier Diyu san (juan 6, no. 051) probably refers to this one with "another formula appears later".
- Sanguisorba stops bleeding; poppy husk astringes; pueraria stops thirst; red peony and angelica harmonize the blood; poria, dried ginger and licorice are added.
