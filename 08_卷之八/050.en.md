# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 050 抜毒散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Badu san (Toxin-Drawing Powder)

It treats cinnabar toxin (erysipelas) in children with changed flesh color, on the limbs or on the chest and back, wandering without fixed place, red, hot and painful. It draws out pain and disperses swelling, scatters heat and settles pain.

shigao (sekko; gypsum; 3 liang)　gancao (kanzo; licorice)　huangbo (obaku; phellodendron; 1 liang each)　hanshuishi (kansuiseki; calcite; 7 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder. Each time mix with water and brush it on from time to time with a chicken feather; mixing it with the natural juice of banana plant is excellent.

## Notes

Badu san (Wu Zhige's additions): for children's cinnabar toxin (erysipelas) with discoloured skin and wandering red, hot, painful patches on the limbs, chest or back.
- A powder of calcite, gypsum, phellodendron and licorice mixed with water or banana juice and brushed on with a feather.
