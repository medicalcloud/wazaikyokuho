# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 057 神仙太一膏 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shenxian taiyi gao (Divine Immortal Taiyi Paste)

It treats the eight effusions of abscesses and flat-abscesses, all malign sores and soft boils, regardless of how many years and months they have lasted, whether pus has formed or not; applying it works at once. Wounds from snakes, tigers, scorpions, dogs, scalds and burns, knives and axes may all be treated by internal use and external application. For effusion of the back, first wash the sore with warm water, wipe dry, spread the medicine on a piece of cloth and apply, and also take one pill with water. For blood-qi disorders, take it with akebia wine. For red and white vaginal discharge, take it with angelica wine. For cough, throat blockage and throat-entwining wind, wrap it in silk floss and hold it in the mouth to dissolve. For all red eyes from wind, apply to the temples (Taiyang points) and then take it with gardenia decoction. For injuries from blows, apply the medicine and also take it with tangerine peel decoction. For pain of the lower back and knees, apply it and take it with salt water. For spitting of blood, take it with mulberry root bark decoction. For the various fistulas, first wash the sores and boils with salt water, spread the medicine on paper according to their size and apply, and also take one pill each time. Roll it at once into pills the size of cherries and coat them with clamshell powder. This medicine can be kept for ten years without spoiling; the longer it is kept the stronger it becomes, and its divine efficacy cannot be fully described.

xuanshen (scrophularia)　baizhi (byakushi; angelica dahurica)　chuan danggui (toki) (Sichuan angelica; rhizome head removed)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　dahuang (daio; rhubarb)　chi shaoyao (shakuyaku; red peony root)　sheng gan dihuang (kanjio; dried raw rehmannia; 1 liang each)

Chop the above and soak in 2 jin of sesame oil—five days in spring, three in summer, seven in autumn and ten in winter—strain off the dregs, boil the oil down to a suitable point, then add 1 jin of red lead, until a drop of the oil in water does not spread.

## Notes

Shenxian taiyi gao (Wu Zhige's additions): a plaster applied to the eight effusions of abscesses, all malign sores and boils, and wounds from snakes, tigers, scorpions, dogs, burns and blades; also made into pills for internal use, with vehicles varying for effusion of the back, blood-qi disorders, discharge, cough, throat blockage, red eyes, injuries, back and knee pain, spitting of blood and fistulas.
- Seven ingredients (scrophularia, angelica dahurica, angelica, cinnamon, rhubarb, red peony, raw rehmannia) soaked and boiled in sesame oil and made into a plaster with red lead (the later Taiyi gao).
