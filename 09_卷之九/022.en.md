# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 022 楡白皮散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yubaipi san (Elm Bark Powder)

It makes the fetus slippery for easy delivery. It is suitable for those who in pregnancy have lost blood through a leaking fetus, or at delivery were disturbed too early, with the discharge descending before the time of birth, so that the fetal sac became dry and labor difficult.

donkuizi (mallow seed)　yubaipi (elm bark)　qumai (kubaku; dianthus; 1 liang each)　mutong (akebia; half a liang)　da maren (hemp seed; husks removed)　niuxi (goshitsu; achyranthes; shoots removed, soaked in wine, dried over a fire; 3 *fen* each)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water, decoct down to eight-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm, at any time.

## Notes

Yubaipi san: for difficult labor when the fetal sac has dried after bleeding in pregnancy or early rupture of the membranes; makes the fetus slippery for easy delivery.
- Elm bark, mallow seed, dianthus, akebia and hemp seed lubricate; achyranthes draws downward.
