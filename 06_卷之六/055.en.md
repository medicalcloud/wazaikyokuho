# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 055 斗門散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Doumen san (Sluice-Gate Powder)

It treats the eight kinds of toxic dysentery with gripping pain of the zang-fu organs, pus and blood, red and white, or a mixture of five colors, very frequent day and night; it also treats food-refusing malign dysentery with tenesmus and great unceasing thirst, and alcohol dysentery and visceral toxin with complete inability to eat.

gange (dried pueraria; skin removed; half a liang)　diyu (chiyu; sanguisorba; rhizome head removed)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried; 2 liang each)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried)　danggui (toki) (Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed; 1 liang each)　heidou (black soybean; stir-fried, husks removed)　yingsuqiao (poppy husk; pulp removed, roasted with honey; 4 liang each)

Another formula appears later.

Make the above into a fine powder. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one *zhan* of water, decoct down to seven-tenths and take it warm, at any time.

## Notes

Doumen san (Baoqing-era additions): for abdominal pain, pus and blood, red and white or five-colored stools and frequent stools in the eight kinds of toxic dysentery; for tenesmus and severe thirst in food-refusing dysentery; and for loss of appetite in alcohol dysentery and visceral toxin.
- Poppy husk astringes; sanguisorba stops bleeding; pueraria stops thirst; dried ginger warms; angelica, black soybean and licorice are added.
