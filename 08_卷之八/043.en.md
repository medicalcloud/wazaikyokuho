# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 043 万金膏 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wanjin gao (Ten-Thousand Gold Paste)

It treats abscesses, flat-abscesses and effusions of the back, the various sores and boils, falls from heights, injuries from blows, sores of the legs and knees, shin ulcers of many years, the five kinds of anal fistula, and all malign sores.

longgu (dragon bone)　biejia (turtle shell)　kushen (sophora flavescens root)　wuzeiyu gu (cuttlefish bone)　huangbo (phellodendron)　cao wutou (wild aconite)　huanglian (coptis)　zhuya zaojiao (small gleditsia pod)　huangqin (scutellaria)　bailian (ampelopsis root)　baiji (bletilla)　mubiezi ren (momordica seed kernel)　danggui (Chinese angelica; washed, dried over a fire)　houpo (magnolia bark; coarse bark removed)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage)　xiang baizhi (fragrant angelica dahurica)　moyao (myrrh; ground separately)　huaizhi (sophora twigs)　liuzhi (willow twigs; all chopped and ground together; 1 *fen* each)　ruxiang (frankincense; ground separately; 1 *qian*)　huangdan (red lead; 1½ liang)　qing mayou (clear sesame oil; 4 liang, half a jin in winter)

Except for the red lead, put all the medicines into the oil in a silver or stone vessel and decoct over a gentle fire until purple-red; remove and discard the medicines. Then put half the red lead into the oil, stirring ceaselessly until it turns slightly black; add the rest of the red lead, stirring ceaselessly, and boil down over a gentle fire until purple-black, until a drop on water does not spread and does not stick to the fingers; then add a little more red lead separately and boil again through several boils. If it is too hard, add a little more oil, until it does not stick to the fingers. To use, spread on paper according to the size of the sore and apply.

## Notes

Wanjin gao (Chunyou-era additions): a plaster for abscesses, effusions of the back, sores and boils, injuries from falls and blows, sores of the legs, chronic shin ulcers, anal fistulas and all malign sores.
- Twenty-one ingredients boiled in sesame oil, strained, and made into a plaster with red lead.
