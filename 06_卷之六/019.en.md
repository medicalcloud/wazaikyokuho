# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 019 碧雪 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bixue (Jade-Green Snow)

It treats all accumulated heat with swollen, painful throat, sores of the mouth and tongue, vexation and agitation in the heart with obstruction and oppression on swallowing; or throat blockage so that water and liquids cannot go down; and epidemic seasonal illness with mania and clouded consciousness.

mangxiao (refined mirabilite)　qingdai (indigo)　shigao (gypsum; calcined, ground and water-ground)　hanshuishi (calcite; ground and water-ground)　poxiao (crude mirabilite)　xiaoshi (saltpetre)　gancao (licorice)　ma yaxiao (horse-tooth mirabilite; equal parts)

Decoct the licorice into 2 sheng of decoction and remove the dregs; put in the other medicines and decoct again, stirring ceaselessly with a willow spatula until suitably melted; then add the indigo, mix evenly, pour into an earthenware basin, wait until it cools and sets into frost, and grind into a fine powder. Use a little each time, held in the mouth to dissolve and swallowed with the saliva, at any time. For throat blockage with inability to swallow, blow the medicine into the throat at once through a small bamboo tube; frequent use has divine effect.

## Notes

Bixue (from here the layer of bureau additions): for sore throat, mouth sores, difficulty swallowing and throat blockage from all accumulated heat, and for mania and clouded consciousness in seasonal epidemics.
- A blue frost of refined and crude mirabilite, saltpetre, horse-tooth mirabilite, gypsum and calcite dissolved in licorice decoction, with indigo added and set. Held in the mouth, or blown into the throat through a bamboo tube.
