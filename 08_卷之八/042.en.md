# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 042 紅玉散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Hongyu san (Red Jade Powder)

It closes the openings of sores, generates flesh, stops pain and removes foul fluid; it treats all cases, recent or of many years' standing.

hanshuishi (calcite; burned red-hot over charcoal, cooled and finely ground; 2 liang)　huangdan (red lead; half a liang)

Grind the above together finely, sprinkle dry into the opening of the sore, and then cover with Wanjin gao. Change once a day; twice is even better.

## Notes

Hongyu san (from here the layer of Chunyou-era additions): an external powder to close sores, generate flesh, stop pain and remove foul discharge, for recent or old sores.
- Calcite and red lead sprinkled on, then covered with Wanjin gao (the next entry, juan 8, no. 043).
