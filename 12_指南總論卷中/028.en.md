# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 028 論傷寒陰陽二証 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On the two patterns, yin and yang, of cold damage. There is yin reversal and there is yang reversal, and they must be inquired into most carefully. In yang patterns of cold damage, when the heat qi is deep there is paradoxically reversal (cold extremities). If one notes only the reversal cold of the limbs and gives hot medicines at once, this harms a person's life in an instant. One who dispenses medicine must inquire with the greatest care; if in doubt, it is better not to dispense, and to have the patient call another physician to examine him; medicines must not be dispensed recklessly.

## Notes

On distinguishing yin and yang reversal: in yang patterns deep heat can paradoxically make the limbs cold (yang reversal), so hot medicines must not be given on the basis of cold limbs alone; if in doubt, do not dispense and refer to another physician.
