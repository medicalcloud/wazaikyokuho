# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 018 金箔鎮心丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jinbo zhenxin wan (Gold Leaf Heart-Settling Pill)

It treats children with wind congestion and phlegm-heat, an unsettled heart-spirit, fright palpitations with vexing thirst, parched lips and red cheeks, unquiet sleep at night, and delirious, manic speech.

ziheche (placenta; boiled soft with black soybeans, sliced, dried over a fire; 25 liang)　shanyao (Chinese yam; 150 liang)　yaxiao (refined mirabilite; dehydrated; 15 liang)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; scorched)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng; rhizome head removed)　fuling (bukuryo; poria; skin removed; 50 liang each)　zhusha (cinnabar; ground and water-ground; 100 liang)　longnao (borneol; ground; 10 liang)　shexiang (jako; musk; ground; 5 liang)　jinbo (gold leaf; 1,200 leaves, for coating)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills with refined honey, fifty pills to each 1½ liang, coated with gold leaf. Take one pill each time dissolved in mint decoction; it may also be dissolved in the mouth; after meals and at bedtime. Taken regularly it calms and settles the heart-spirit, disperses failing pathogenic heat, cools the throat and diaphragm and stops startled crying.

## Notes

Jinbo zhenxin wan: for an unsettled heart-spirit, fright palpitations, vexing thirst, parched lips, red cheeks, insomnia and delirium from wind congestion and phlegm-heat in children.
- Cinnabar, gold leaf, borneol, musk and refined mirabilite clear heat and settle the heart; placenta, Chinese yam, ginseng, poria and licorice supplement.
