# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之七 001 錦鳩丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jinjiu wan (Turtledove Pill)

It treats all of the following: insufficiency of the liver meridian with pathogenic wind riding in internally and attacking upward, dim eyes with tearing, fear of the sun and aversion to light, gritty, rough, itchy and painful eyes, blurred vision when trying to look, frequent sight of black flowers (floaters), or the formation of nebulae and membranes.

cao juemingzi (ketsumeishi; cassia seed)　ruiren (prinsepia kernel; skins removed)　qianghuo (notopterygium; rhizome head removed)　qumai (kubaku; dianthus; 3 liang each)　xixin (saishin; asarum; shoots removed)　muli (borei; oyster shell; washed, calcined, powder taken)　huanglian (oren; coptis; fibrous roots removed)　du jili (tribulus; stir-fried, spines removed)　fangfeng (bofu; saposhnikovia; rhizome head removed)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　gan juhua (sweet chrysanthemum flower; clean; 5 liang each)　bai fuling (bukuryo; white poria; skin removed; 4 liang)　banjiu (turtledove; one bird, skin, feathers, intestines, beak and claws removed, roasted dry with the bones over a "civil and military" fire)　jieyang gan (wether's liver; one, thinly sliced, roasted until scorched)　manjingzi (vitex fruit; 2 sheng, rinsed, put in a silk bag, steamed in the rice steamer for a full day and night, sun-dried)

Powder these fifteen ingredients, knead with refined honey, pound five hundred times and form pills the size of wutong seeds. Take fifteen to twenty pills each time with warm water or warm wine, on an empty stomach, at noon and at bedtime, three times a day. For long-standing internal and external obstructions of the eye unresponsive to various medicines, gradually increase to fifty pills, and it will certainly work. For sudden red, painful eyes, take twenty pills after meals with schizonepeta decoction.

## Notes

Jinjiu wan (the first formula of juan 7, "Treating diseases of the eyes"): for dim vision, tearing, photophobia, grittiness, itching, pain, blurred sight, floaters and nebulae from pathogenic wind riding on an insufficient liver meridian; also for long-standing internal and external obstructions and sudden red eyes.
- Cassia seed, chrysanthemum, prinsepia, vitex, tribulus, saposhnikovia, notopterygium and asarum disperse wind and brighten the eyes; coptis clears heat; turtledove and sheep's liver supplement the liver; oyster shell, dianthus, poria and cinnamon are added.
