# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 059 拒風丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jufeng dan (Wind-Repelling Elixir)

It may be taken for all wind and ordinary slight wind damage with headache, nasal congestion, stiff neck and cramped sinews.

biba (long pepper; half a liang)　fangfeng (saposhnikovia; rhizome head and forks removed; 1½ liang)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage; 4 liang)　xixin (asarum; washed, leaves removed; 3½ *qian*)　tianma (gastrodia; rhizome head removed)　gancao (licorice; chopped; 1 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of longan fruits with refined honey. Take one pill each time, chewed thoroughly and washed down with schizonepeta decoction or warm wine. Taken after meals it works at once.

## Notes

Jufeng dan (Baoqing-era additions): for headache, nasal congestion and stiff neck in a mild cold (wind damage).
- Sichuan lovage, saposhnikovia, asarum and gastrodia scatter wind; long pepper warms.
