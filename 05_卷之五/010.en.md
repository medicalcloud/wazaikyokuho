# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 010 無比山薬丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wubi shanyao wan (Incomparable Chinese Yam Pill)

It treats men's myriad deficiencies and hundred damages and the five taxations and seven damages, with headache and dizziness, counterflow cold of the hands and feet, or vexing heat at times, or cold numbness with bone pain, loss of control of the lower back and hips, no flesh gained despite eating much; or distension and fullness despite eating little, a lustreless body, declining and exhausted yang qi and yin qi not moving. This medicine supplements the meridians and vessels, raises yin and yang, calms the ethereal and corporeal souls, opens the sanjiao, breaks up accumulations, thickens the stomach and intestines, strengthens the sinews and tempers the bones, lightens the body and brightens the eyes, removes wind and dispels cold; there is nothing it does not treat.

chishizhi (shakusekishi; red halloysite)　fushen (poria with root; skin and wood removed)　baji (morinda; core removed)　shu gan dihuang (kanjio; prepared rehmannia; thoroughly soaked in wine)　shanzhuyu (sanshuyu; cornus)　niuxi (goshitsu; achyranthes; sprouts removed, soaked in wine)　zexie (takusha; alisma; 1 liang each)　shanyao (Chinese yam; 2 liang)　wuweizi (gomishi; schisandra; 6 liang)　congrong (cistanche; soaked in wine; 4 liang)　duzhong (tochu; eucommia; bark removed, stir-fried)　tusizi (toshishi; dodder seed; soaked in wine; 3 liang each)

Powder the above medicines and knead with refined honey into pills the size of wutong seeds. Take twenty to thirty pills each time before meals with warm wine; warm rice gruel is also fine. Seven days after taking it, the body becomes light and healthy, the limbs moist and glossy, the lips and mouth red, the hands and feet warm, the face lustrous, food is digested, the body calm and harmonious and the voice clear and resonant—these are the signs of its effect. After ten days the flesh grows. This medicine penetrates the middle and enters the brain, so the nose is sure to ache; do not be alarmed.

## Notes

Wubi shanyao wan: a kidney-supplementing formula for men's myriad deficiencies and the five taxations and seven damages—headache, dizziness, cold limbs, vexing heat, cold numbness, loss of lumbar control, emaciation, abdominal fullness and declining yang qi.
- Chinese yam, rehmannia, cornus, alisma and poria with root (the Liuwei lineage) with morinda, cistanche, eucommia, dodder and achyranthes to supplement kidney yang, and schisandra and red halloysite to astringe. It describes the signs of effect and that the nose will ache.
