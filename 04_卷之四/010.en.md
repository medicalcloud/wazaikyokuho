# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之四 010 備急五嗽丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Beiji wusou wan (Emergency Five Coughs Pill)

It treats the five kinds of cough: (1) rising-qi cough; (2) rheum cough; (3) fetid cough; (4) cold cough; (5) evil cough. All arise because the lungs have received wind-cold and the qi does not diffuse. Whether long-standing or new, mild or severe, even when food will not go down, the voice will not come out, one cannot rest sitting or lying, it goes on day and night, the face and eyes are puffy and the chest and flanks have drawing pain—it is suitable for all.

rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried)　zaojia (soap pod; skin and seeds removed, roasted yellow; equal parts)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with refined honey. Take fifteen pills each time with warm wine; rice gruel is also fine; after meals.

## Notes

Beiji wusou wan: for the five kinds of cough (rising-qi, rheum, fetid, cold, evil) when the lungs have received wind-cold.
- Cinnamon, dried ginger and soap pod warm and remove phlegm; in the lineage of the Wusou wan of the *Qianjin fang*.
