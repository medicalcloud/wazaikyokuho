# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 025 平補鎮心丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Pingbu zhenxin dan (Balanced Supplementing Heart-Settling Elixir)

It treats men and women with insufficient heart qi, an unsettled will, an abstracted mind, many strange dreams at night, palpitation with vexing constraint; and damaged, failing kidney qi with little blood and much qi, weary limbs, aching feet and shins, restless sleep, seminal emission in dreams, occasional white turbidity and gradual wasting. (Another formula appears later.)

suanzaoren (jujube seed; skin removed, stir-fried on paper; 2½ *qian*)　cheqianzi (plantago seed; earth removed, milled and cracked)　bai fuling (white poria; skin removed)　wuweizi (schisandra; twigs and stalks removed)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed, not exposed to fire)　maimendong (ophiopogon; core removed)　fushen (poria with root; skin removed; 1 liang 2½ *qian* each)　tianmendong (asparagus root; core removed)　longchi (dragon teeth)　shu dihuang (prepared rehmannia; washed, steamed with wine)　shanyao (Chinese yam; processed with ginger juice; 1½ liang each)　renshen (ginseng; rhizome head removed; half a liang)　zhusha (cinnabar; finely ground, for coating; half a liang)　yuanzhi (polygala; core removed)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried; 1½ liang)

Powder the above and form pills the size of wutong seeds with refined honey. Take thirty pills each time on an empty stomach with rice water; warm wine is also fine; increase to fifty. Taken regularly it augments essence and marrow, nourishes qi and blood and brightens and preserves the complexion.

The Hanlin academician Liu Huo'an says: There are two formulas for Pingbu zhenxin dan. This one contains schisandra, white poria, plantago seed, cinnamon, ginseng and jujube seed; it can not only treat insufficient heart qi but is especially important for white turbidity and dispersion-thirst. The [other] *Ju fang* formula lacks these six and instead has raw rehmannia, bitter platycodon, arborvitae seed, acorus and angelica; it is suitable only for insufficient heart qi and damaged, failing kidney qi with little blood and much qi.

## Notes

Pingbu zhenxin dan (Baoqing-era additions): for anxiety, abstraction, nightmares and palpitation from insufficient heart qi, and for lassitude, aching legs, insomnia, seminal emission in dreams, white turbidity and wasting from damaged kidney qi.
- Jujube seed, poria with root, polygala, dragon teeth and cinnabar calm the heart-spirit; ginseng, Chinese yam, poria and licorice supplement; rehmannia, asparagus root, ophiopogon and schisandra nourish yin; cinnamon and plantago seed are added.
- At the end it quotes the Hanlin academician Liu Huo'an comparing this formula with another version (containing raw rehmannia, platycodon, arborvitae seed, acorus and angelica) and judging this one better for white turbidity and dispersion-thirst. The other version appears later in juan 5.
