# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 010 伏竜肝散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Fulonggan san (Oven Earth Powder)

It treats taxation damage of qi and blood with deficiency of the *chong* and *ren* vessels, menstrual blood out of season suddenly flooding down, like bean juice, or in clots, or five colours mixed, or red and white together, with cold pain of the navel and abdomen, unceasing for a long time, making the patient sallow, thin and dry-mouthed, with reduced eating, weakness of the limbs, deficiency vexation and fright palpitations.

fulonggan (oven earth, i.e. the earth at the centre of the stove)　chi shizhi (red halloysite; 1 liang each)　shu gan dihuang (prepared rehmannia; soaked in wine overnight)　aiye (mugwort leaf; lightly stir-fried; 2 liang each)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed; half a liang each)　danggui (Chinese angelica; shoots removed, stir-fried)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried; 3 *fen* each)　xiongqiong (Sichuan lovage; 3 liang)　maimendong (ophiopogon; core removed; 1½ liang)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 4 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water and three jujubes broken open, decoct down to seven-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm before meals.

## Notes

Fulonggan san: for unseasonal flooding (bean-juice-like, clotted, five-coloured or red and white) with long-lasting cold umbilical pain, sallowness, wasting, dry mouth, poor appetite, weakness, deficiency vexation and fright palpitations from taxation damage of qi and blood with deficient *chong* and *ren*.
- Oven earth, red halloysite and mugwort stop bleeding; prepared rehmannia, angelica, lovage and ophiopogon nourish blood; dried ginger and cinnamon warm; licorice is added.
