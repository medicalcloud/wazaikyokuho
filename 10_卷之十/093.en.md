# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 093 竜胆丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Longdan wan (Gentian Pill)

It treats fever in gan disease.

longdan cao (gentian; rhizome head removed)　huanglian (coptis; fibrous roots removed, lightly stir-fried)　qingpi (green tangerine peel; white removed)　shijunzi (quisqualis fruit; husk removed, stir-fried)

Make equal parts of the above into a fine powder, knead with pig's bile and form pills the size of radish seeds. Take twenty pills each time, adjusted as judged, with hot water at bedtime.

## Notes

Longdan wan (bureau additions; the last formula of "Treating children's diseases"): for fever in gan disease.
- Gentian and coptis clear gan heat; quisqualis kills worms; green tangerine peel moves qi; made into pills with pig's bile.
