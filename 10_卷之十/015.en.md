# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 015 天麻防風丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Tianma fangfeng wan (Gastrodia and Saposhnikovia Pill)

It is suitable for all fright wind with vigorous fever of the body, much sleeping with fright palpitations, twitching of the hands and feet, a clouded spirit and obstructed phlegm and drool, and for evil heat of wind-warmth.

bai jiangcan (silkworm; silk and mouth removed, stir-fried)　ganxie (dried scorpion; stir-fried; half a liang each)　tianma (gastrodia; shoots removed)　fangfeng (saposhnikovia; shoots removed)　renshen (ginseng; 1 liang each)　zhusha (cinnabar; ground and water-ground)　xionghuang (realgar; ground)　shexiang (musk; ground)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried; 1 *fen* each)　niuhuang (bovine bezoar; 1 *qian*)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with refined honey. Take one or two pills each time dissolved in mint decoction, at any time.

## Notes

Tianma fangfeng wan: for all fright wind with high fever, somnolence, fright palpitations, twitching limbs, clouded spirit and phlegm, and for evil heat of wind-warmth.
- Gastrodia, saposhnikovia, silkworm and scorpion dispel wind; bovine bezoar, cinnabar, realgar and musk settle; ginseng and licorice supplement.
