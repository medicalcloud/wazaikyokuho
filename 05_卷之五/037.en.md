# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 037 張走馬玉霜丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zhang Zouma yushuang wan (Zhang Zouma's Jade Frost Pill)

It treats men with deficient and damaged original yang and declining qi of the five zang organs, seminal emission in dreams at night, white turbid urine, cold pain below the navel, impotence, long childlessness, gradual wasting and weakness turning into kidney taxation, dim eyes and tinnitus, aching lower back and knees and copious night sweats; all should take it. The essential origin then naturally becomes firmly sealed, discharging inwardly without leaking, retaining the turbid and removing the clear, and the spirit becomes calm and healthy. It is also suitable for women with a cold womb, irregular menses, red and white vaginal discharge, long childlessness, freckles and dark spots on the face, falling hair that does not grow back, dry yellow flesh and a lusterless appearance.

large chuanwu (Sichuan aconite; stir-fried with half a jin of clamshell powder until it cracks, the clamshell powder discarded)　chuanlianzi (toosendan fruit; stir-fried with bran; 8 liang each)　poguzhi (psoralea; stir-fried)　baji (morinda; core removed; 4 liang each)　huixiang (fennel; dried over a fire; 6 liang)

Mill the above medicines into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with flour paste made with wine. Take thirty to fifty pills each time with wine or salt water, on an empty stomach before meals.

## Notes

Zhang Zouma yushuang wan (from here the layer of Wu Zhige's additions): for seminal emission, turbid urine, cold pain below the navel, impotence, infertility, wasting, kidney taxation, dim eyes, tinnitus, aching back and night sweats from deficient original yang in men, and for cold womb, irregular menses, discharge, infertility, facial spots, hair loss and poor complexion in women.
- Aconite, psoralea, morinda and fennel warm kidney yang; toosendan moves qi. A different formula from the earlier Yushuang wan (juan 5, no. 016), attributed to Zhang Zouma (whether a personal name or nickname has not been verified).
