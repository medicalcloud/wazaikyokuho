# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之四 017 藿香散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huoxiang san (Agastache Powder)

It warms the spleen and stomach, transforms phlegm-rheum, disperses chronic cold and stops vomiting. It treats glomus and fullness of the chest and diaphragm, distending pain of the abdomen and flanks, shortness of breath with choking oppression, coughing and vomiting of phlegm and water, belching of sourness and acid swallowing, hiccough and nausea; and also treats miasmic qi of mountain mists.

houpo (magnolia bark; coarse bark removed, roasted with ginger juice)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried)　banxia (pinellia; cut into quarters, soaked overnight in ginger juice, stir-fried yellow with millet)　huoxiang leaves (agastache; 1 liang each)　chenpi (aged tangerine peel; white pith removed; half a liang)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one *zhan* of water, three slices of ginger and one jujube, decoct together down to seven-tenths, remove the dregs and take it hot, at any time, two or three doses a day.

## Notes

Huoxiang san: warms the spleen and stomach and transforms phlegm-rheum and chronic cold; for chest glomus, abdominal and flank distension and pain, shortness of breath, vomiting of phlegm and water, acid reflux, hiccough and nausea, and for miasma.
- Agastache, magnolia bark, pinellia, tangerine peel and licorice (close to Buhuanjin zhengqi san without black atractylodes).
- The base text writes 噎醋 for 噫醋 ("sour belching").
