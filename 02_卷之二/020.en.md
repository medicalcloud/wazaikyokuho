# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 020 白虎湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Baihu tang (White Tiger Decoction)

It is suitable for cold damage after copious sweating, when the exterior pattern has already resolved but there is great vexation in the heart and chest and thirst with a desire to drink water; and seven or eight days after vomiting or purging, when the evil toxin has not resolved, heat is bound in the interior, both exterior and interior are hot, there is aversion to wind from time to time, great thirst, a dry tongue with vexation, and a desire to drink several *sheng* of water. It also treats summerheat-toxin strike in summer with sweating, aversion to cold, a hot body and thirst.

zhimu (anemarrhena; 75 liang)　gancao (licorice; scorched; 37½ liang)　shigao (gypsum; washed; 12½ jin)

Make the above into a fine powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water and thirty-odd grains of non-glutinous rice, decoct down to one *zhan*, strain off the dregs and take it warm. Children are given a little according to their strength. A little ginseng may also be added and decocted with it. Take after meals. This medicine may be taken after the Beginning of Summer and before the Beginning of Autumn. It must not be taken in spring or after the Beginning of Autumn, nor by those depleted by blood loss.

## Notes

Baihu tang: for heat bound in the interior after copious sweating or after vomiting or purging, with heat inside and out, great thirst and vexation (e.g. the Baihu jia renshen tang pattern of *Shanghan lun* clause 168), and for summerheat illness.
- The *Shanghan lun* Baihu tang (anemarrhena, gypsum, licorice, rice), with ginseng optionally added (Baihu jia renshen tang). It is restricted to the period between the Beginning of Summer and the Beginning of Autumn and forbidden in spring, after autumn begins and for those depleted by blood loss (close to views in the *Shanghan lun* and *Huoren shu*).
