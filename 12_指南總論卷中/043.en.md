# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 043 論傷寒後自利 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On spontaneous diarrhea after cold damage. For abdominal pain after cold damage with spontaneous diarrhea and cold hands and feet, take Lizhong wan (Richugan) and the like; see the entry on abdominal pain.

## Notes

On diarrhea after cold damage: Lizhong wan and the like for abdominal pain, diarrhea and cold extremities; refers to the discussion of abdominal pain (no. 040).
