# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 012 調中沈香湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Tiaozhong chenxiang tang (Center-Regulating Aquilaria Decoction)

It regulates the middle and smooths qi, removes pathogen and nourishes the healthy qi. It treats sudden pain of the heart and abdomen, stuffiness and fullness of the chest and diaphragm, shortness of breath with vexation and oppression, phlegm counterflow and nausea, little taste for food and frequent weariness of the limbs. Taken regularly it increases eating, makes the zang-fu organs harmonious and calm, and gives a lustrous skin and glowing complexion.

shexiang (jako; musk; ground; half a *qian*)　chenxiang (aquilaria; 2 liang)　raw longnao (borneol; ground; 1 *qian*)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried; 1 *fen*)　muxiang (mokko; costus root)　bai doukou ren (white cardamom; 1 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder and mix in the ground medicines evenly. Take half a *qian* each time stirred into boiling water; a slice of ginger and a little salt may also be added. It is excellent taken after drinking and eating.

## Notes

Tiaozhong chenxiang tang: an aromatic powder stirred into hot water for sudden heart and abdominal pain, chest stuffiness, shortness of breath, nausea, poor appetite and lassitude.
- Aquilaria, costus and white cardamom move qi; musk and borneol open; licorice harmonizes.
