# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 043 論傷寒後自利 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On spontaneous diarrhoea after cold damage. For abdominal pain after cold damage with spontaneous diarrhoea and cold hands and feet, take Lizhong wan and the like; see the entry on abdominal pain.

## Notes

On diarrhoea after cold damage: Lizhong wan and the like for abdominal pain, diarrhoea and cold extremities; refers to the discussion of abdominal pain (no. 040).
