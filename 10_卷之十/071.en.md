# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 071 白芨散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Baiji san (Bletilla Powder)

It treats children whose kidney qi has not developed and whose brain marrow is insufficient. When a child has grown older and the bones that should have closed have not, the cranial sutures remaining open (*jielu*), this is the condition; the medicine should be applied externally.

baiji (bletilla)　bairen (arborvitae seed)　fangfeng (bofu; saposhnikovia; shoots removed)　xixin (saishin; asarum; leaves removed; 1 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder. Mix 1 *qian* each time with breast milk and apply it over the child's cranial bones, once a day.

## Notes

Baiji san (Wu Zhige's additions): for open cranial sutures that fail to close as the child grows (*jielu*), attributed to undeveloped kidney qi and insufficient brain marrow; an external preparation mixed with breast milk and applied to the head.
- Four ingredients: bletilla, arborvitae seed, saposhnikovia and asarum.
