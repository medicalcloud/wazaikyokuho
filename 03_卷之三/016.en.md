# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 016 膈気散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Geqi san (Diaphragm Qi Powder)

It treats the five kinds of diaphragm qi with blockage of the sanjiao, fullness and oppression of the chest and diaphragm, pain drawing into the spine, distension of the heart and abdomen, stabbing pain of the flanks and ribs, inability to get food down, choking obstruction, vomiting with phlegm counterflow, bitter mouth and acid swallowing, emaciation and little strength, shortness of breath, vexation and oppression. Taken regularly it smooths qi and loosens the middle, disperses strings, aggregations and accumulations, and scatters qi of fright, worry and resentment.

roudoukou ren (nutmeg)　muxiang (mokko; costus root)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger)　houpo (koboku; magnolia bark; coarse bark removed, processed with ginger juice and stir-fried)　qingpi (green tangerine peel; white removed)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; scorched; 5 liang each)　sanleng (sparganium; blast-fried)　yizhi ren (alpinia oxyphylla)　ezhu (zedoary; blast-fried)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　chenpi (chinpi; aged tangerine peel; pulp removed)　binglang (areca)　zhiqiao (bitter orange; pulp removed, stir-fried with bran; 10 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one *zhan* of water, two slices of fresh ginger and half a jujube, decoct together down to seven-tenths and take it hot with the dregs. If there is no time to decoct it, add a little salt and stir it into boiling water. At any time.

## Notes

Geqi san: for blockage of the sanjiao, chest oppression, back pain, abdominal distension, flank pain, difficulty swallowing, vomiting, acid reflux and emaciation from the five kinds of diaphragm qi.
- Sparganium, zedoary, areca, bitter orange, tangerine peels and magnolia bark break up qi and accumulations; nutmeg, alpinia, dried ginger and cinnamon warm; costus moves qi.
