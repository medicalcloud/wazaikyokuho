# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 023 草豆蔻散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Caodoukou san (Katsumada Galangal Powder)

It treats disordered spleen and stomach with fullness and oppression of the chest and diaphragm, undigested food, retching and nausea; or sudden turmoil with vomiting, stabbing pain of the heart and abdomen, borborygmus with diarrhea and water and grain not separated.

caodoukou (Katsumada galangal seed; skin removed; 1 jin)　shengjiang (shokyo; fresh ginger; sliced; 2 jin)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; chopped; 8 liang)

Mix the above evenly, put into a silver vessel, add water to about three fingers' depth above them and simmer over a gentle fire until the water is gone; dry over a fire and pound into a fine powder. Take 1 *qian* each time stirred into boiling water, at any time. In summer, decoct it as a boiled-water drink and take it regularly: it regulates the middle, stops counterflow, removes cold qi and digests food.

## Notes

Caodoukou san: for chest oppression, indigestion and nausea from spleen-stomach disorder, and for vomiting, abdominal pain and diarrhea of sudden turmoil.
- Katsumada galangal, fresh ginger and licorice simmered dry in water and powdered; also used as a summer drink.
- The base text writes 藿乱 for 霍乱 (sudden turmoil).
