# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之四 029 温中化痰丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wenzhong huatan wan (Middle-Warming Phlegm-Transforming Pill)

The patterns it treats are the same as for the earlier Wenzhong huatan wan.

ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried)　banxia (hange; pinellia; boiled; 1 liang each)　xixin (saishin; asarum; leaves removed, washed)　hujiao (black pepper; half a liang each)　baizhu (byakujutsu; white atractylodes; dried over a fire; 2 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with flour paste made with ginger juice. Take thirty to fifty pills each time with hot water or other drink as preferred, at any time.

## Notes

Wenzhong huatan wan (Wu Zhige's additions): the same indications as the earlier Wenzhong huatan wan (juan 4, no. 023), but a different formula of dried ginger, pinellia, asarum, black pepper and atractylodes.
