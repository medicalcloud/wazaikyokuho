# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 080 加減呉茱萸湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jiajian wuzhuyu tang (Modified Evodia Decoction)

The patterns and treatment are the same as for Wuzhuyu tang (Goshuyuto); this formula is excellent.

fangfeng (bofu; saposhnikovia; rhizome head and forks removed)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried)　danggui (toki; Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed, soaked in wine, stir-fried)　mudanpi (moutan bark)　guixin (cinnamon heart; not exposed to fire)　fuling (bukuryo; poria; skin removed)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried)　maimendong (bakumondo; ophiopogon; core removed)　banxia (hange; pinellia; washed seven times in hot water)　jiegeng (kikyo; platycodon; stir-fried)　xixin (saishin; asarum; shoots removed; 1 liang each)　wuzhuyu (goshuyu; evodia; washed seven times in hot water, stir-fried; 3 liang)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 4 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water, decoct down to seven-tenths, remove the dregs and take it hot before meals.

## Notes

Jiajian wuzhuyu tang (bureau additions): the same indications as the earlier Wuzhuyu tang (juan 9, no. 009); a modification omitting prepared rehmannia and adding moutan, cinnamon heart, poria, ophiopogon and pinellia.
