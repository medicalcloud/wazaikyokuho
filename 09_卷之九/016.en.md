# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 016 紫石英丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zishiying wan (Murasakisekieigan; Fluorite Pill)

It treats women who are childless from long-standing cold and those who have miscarried repeatedly. All this arises from deficiency damage of the *Chong* and *Ren* meridians and old cold illness in the uterus, with menses at irregular times, sudden unceasing bleeding, two periods in a month, early or late; and accumulated cold in the womb, the hundred illnesses of deficiency and weakness, flooding, metrostaxis and the thirty-six disorders of discharge, accumulations, gatherings and conglomerations, cold pain below the navel, cramping heaviness of the lower abdomen and white turbid urine. The above disorders all prevent pregnancy and birth, to the point of ending the line without conception; this medicine can govern and treat them all. Taken regularly it removes static blood, warms the womb and brings pregnancy; at delivery the birth is easy and the child is born robust and free of illness.

wuzeiyu gu (cuttlefish bone; burned to ash)　shanyu (Chinese yam)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried; 1½ liang each)　tianmendong (tenmondo; asparagus root; core removed, dried over a fire)　zishiying (murasakisekiei; fluorite; ground; 3 liang each)　ziwei (campsis flower)　xinyi (shini) ren (magnolia flower bud kernel)　shu gan dihuang (kanjio; prepared rehmannia)　juanbai (selaginella; roots removed)　yuyuliang (uyoryo; limonite; burned, quenched in vinegar seven times, ground)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　shihu (sekkoku; dendrobium; roots removed)　xiongqiong (Sichuan lovage)　mumeng (mumeng; 2 liang each)　shizhuyu (ailanthus prickly-ash fruit)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng)　xuduan (zokudan; dipsacus)　danggui (toki; Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed, lightly stir-fried)　chuanwu (Sichuan aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　mudanpi (moutan bark)　sangjisheng (mulberry mistletoe)　xixin (saishin; asarum; shoots removed)　houpo (koboku; magnolia bark; coarse bark removed, roasted with ginger juice)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried)　niuxi (goshitsu; achyranthes; shoots removed; 1 liang 1 *fen* each)　baiziren (arborvitae seed; lightly stir-fried, ground separately; 1½ liang)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with refined honey. Take thirty pills each time with warm wine or warm rice water, on an empty stomach before meals, twice a day.

## Notes

Zishiying wan: for infertility from long-standing cold and habitual miscarriage, attributed to deficient *Chong* and *Ren* and old cold in the uterus manifesting as irregular or frequent menses, accumulated cold of the womb, flooding, discharge, conglomerations, cold pain below the navel, lower abdominal heaviness and turbid urine; regular use is said to bring conception, easy delivery and a healthy child.
- Fluorite, limonite, cuttlefish bone, campsis, selaginella and mumeng, with prepared rehmannia, angelica, lovage, ginseng, asparagus root, dendrobium, dipsacus, mistletoe and achyranthes to supplement; Sichuan aconite, cinnamon, dried ginger, ailanthus prickly-ash and asarum warm; moutan moves blood; magnolia bud, magnolia bark, arborvitae seed, Chinese yam and licorice are added.
