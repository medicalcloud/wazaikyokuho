# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 069 橘皮煎丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jupi jian wan (Tangerine Peel Syrup Pill)

It treats long-standing deficiency and accumulated cold with pain of the heart and abdomen, vomiting of phlegm and water, reduced eating, deficiency fullness of the flanks and ribs, bowstring tension of the navel and abdomen, deficient, slippery large intestine, copious frequent urination, emaciated haggard skin, a withered yellow complexion, weary limbs, and slack, weak lower back and knees. It also treats strings, aggregations, abdominal masses, qi ascent and cough, long-standing malaria and dysentery, intestinal wind, piles and fistulas. Women with a deficient, cold sea of blood, red and white vaginal discharge and long childlessness should all take it.

danggui (toki) (Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed, first dried over a fire)　bixie (dioscorea hypoglauca)　houpo (koboku; magnolia bark; coarse bark removed, processed with ginger juice)　roucongrong (cistanche; soaked in wine, lightly roasted, sliced, dried over a fire)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　fuzi (bushi; aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　baji (morinda; core removed)　yangqishi (yokiseki; actinolite; soaked in wine, dried over a fire, ground to powder)　shihu (sekkoku; dendrobium; roots removed)　niuxi (goshitsu; achyranthes; rhizome head removed, soaked in wine)　duzhong (tochu; eucommia; bark removed, roasted with ginger juice)　wuzhuyu (goshuyu; evodia; rinsed in water, floating ones removed, dried over a fire)　lurong (rokujo; deer antler velvet; eggplant-shaped pieces, hair singed off, split, soaked in wine, roasted dry)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried)　tusizi (toshishi; dodder seed; soaked in wine, dried over a fire, pounded)　sanleng (sparganium; roasted in embers until cooked, pounded while hot; 3 liang each)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried; 1 liang)　chen jupi (kippi; aged tangerine peel; washed clean, dried over a fire, powdered; 15 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder. Using 5 sheng of wine, in a silver or stone vessel decoct the tangerine peel powder down to the consistency of malt syrup, then add the other medicine powders, stir and knead together evenly, put into a mortar, pound five hundred times and form pills the size of wutong seeds. Take twenty pills each time on an empty stomach, swallowed with warm wine; salt water is also fine.

## Notes

Jupi jian wan: for abdominal pain, vomiting of phlegm and water, poor appetite, flank fullness, umbilical tension, diarrhea, frequent urination, wasting, sallow complexion and weak back and knees from long-standing deficiency and accumulated cold; for strings, accumulations, cough, chronic malaria and dysentery, intestinal wind, piles and fistulas; and for women's cold sea of blood, discharge and infertility.
- Aged tangerine peel is boiled down in wine to a syrup, then warming and supplementing drugs (deer antler velvet, aconite, cinnamon, morinda, cistanche, actinolite and others) plus magnolia bark, sparganium and others are added and made into pills.
