# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 049 四倍散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Sibei san (Fourfold Doubling Powder)

It treats adults and children whose spleen qi is not smooth, supplementing deficiency and improving eating.

bai fuling (bukuryo; white poria; skin removed; 2 liang)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng; rhizome head removed; 1 liang)　hezi (chebule; roasted, stone removed; half a liang)　baizhu (byakujutsu; white atractylodes; 4 liang)

Powder the above. For each dose take one large *qian* with one *zhan* of water, three slices of ginger and one jujube, decoct down to six-tenths and take it warm on an empty stomach.

## Notes

Sibei san (Shaoxing-era additions): supplements unsmooth spleen qi and improves eating.
- Chebule half a liang, ginseng 1, poria 2 and atractylodes 4—each double the last (hence "fourfold doubling").
