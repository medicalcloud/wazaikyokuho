# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 008 定吐救生丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Dingtu jiusheng dan (Vomiting-Settling Life-Saving Elixir)

It treats latent heat in children generating drool, vexation and agitation of the heart and diaphragm, vigorous fever with sudden turmoil, inability to get milk and food down, retching and nausea, or vomiting counterflow.

shan daji (mountain euphorbia; boiled in sour rice water, sliced, dried over a fire, powdered; 15 liang)　ruxiang (frankincense; ground separately)　dingxiang (clove; powdered; 5 liang each)　fenshuang (mercuric chloride; ground)　nifen (calomel; ground; 7½ liang each)　longnao (borneol; ground; 2½ liang)　shuiyin (mercury)　huangla (yellow beeswax)　heiqian (black lead; made into granules with the mercury; 10½ liang each)

Grind the above together evenly; for each liang of melted wax add 2½ *qian* of honey, knead and form pills the size of glutinous millet. Give one pill per year of age. For vexation and agitation, take it with water in which raw sesame and purslane have been ground; for vomiting counterflow, with decocted clove and purslane decoction; adjust for deficiency or repletion; after meals and at bedtime. This medicine removes heat, transforms drool, brings down the diaphragm and stops vomiting. If the stomach is deficient and damaged by cold with unceasing vomiting, it must not be given. Vomiting in children should be treated quickly; if it continues long it becomes chronic fright, so this medicine should always be kept for emergencies.

## Notes

Dingtu jiusheng dan: for drool, agitation, high fever, sudden turmoil and vomiting from latent heat in children; not for vomiting from deficient, cold stomach. It is recommended to keep it on hand, since persistent vomiting can turn into chronic fright.
- Euphorbia purges; mercuric chloride, calomel, mercury and lead bring down drool; clove, frankincense and borneol are added; made into wax pills. It contains mercury and lead.
