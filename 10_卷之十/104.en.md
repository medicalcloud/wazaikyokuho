# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 104 五味湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wuwei tang (Gomito; Schisandra Drink)

It warms the middle and augments qi. It treats stuffiness and fullness of the chest and diaphragm, stabbing pain of the heart and abdomen, shortness of breath with choking oppression, cough with spitting of phlegm, vomiting counterflow and nausea, and no desire for food.

wuweizi (gomishi; schisandra; washed; 9 jin)　liangjiang (lesser galangal; stir-fried)　chenpi (chinpi; aged tangerine peel; white pith removed)　huixiang (fennel; stir-fried; 1½ jin each)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; stir-fried; 17½ jin)　yan (salt; stir-fried; 22 jin)

Powder the above. Take 2 *qian* each time mixed with boiling water, before meals.

## Notes

Wuwei tang ("Various drinks"): warms the middle and augments qi; for chest stuffiness, stabbing abdominal pain, breathlessness, choking, cough with phlegm, vomiting and poor appetite.
- Mainly schisandra, with lesser galangal and fennel to warm, tangerine peel, licorice and salt.
