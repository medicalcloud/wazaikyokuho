# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 029 葫芦巴丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huluba wan (Fenugreek Pill)

It treats, in adults and children, small-intestine qi, coiled-intestine qi, running-piglet qi and mounting qi with one-sided sagging and genital swelling, an egg-shaped mass in the lower abdomen moving up and down with unbearable pain, or gripping binding with attacking, stabbing pain around the navel, nausea, oppression and confusion.

huluba (fenugreek; stir-fried; 1 jin)　wuzhuyu (goshuyu; evodia; washed ten times in hot water, stir-fried; 10 liang)　chuanlianzi (toosendan fruit; stir-fried; 1 jin 2 liang)　da baji (large morinda; core removed, stir-fried)　chuanwu (Sichuan aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed; 6 liang each)　huixiang (fennel; rinsed free of earth, stir-fried; 12 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with flour paste boiled in wine. Take fifteen pills each time on an empty stomach, swallowed with warm wine; children take five pills with fennel decoction.

## Notes

Huluba wan (bureau additions): for one-sided scrotal sagging and swelling, an egg-like mass moving in the lower abdomen with severe pain, and gripping periumbilical pain with nausea and oppression, from small-intestine qi, coiled-intestine qi, running piglet and mounting.
- Fenugreek, evodia, morinda, Sichuan aconite and fennel warm; toosendan moves qi.
