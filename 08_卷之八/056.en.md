# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 056 槐白皮膏 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huaibaipi gao (Sophora White Bark Paste)

It treats the various internal and external piles with swollen, hard, bound nodes, itching at any time, or unbearable pain, or sores around the anus that are red, festering and ulcerating, or mouse-nipple warts attached to the nodes that have long failed to disperse.

huaibaipi (sophora root bark)　lianshi (renjitsu; chinaberry fruit; 5 liang each)　gancao (kanzo; licorice)　baizhi (byakushi; angelica dahurica; 2 liang each)　chixiaodou (sekishozu; rice bean; 2 *ge*)　taoren (tonin; peach kernel; 60)　danggui (toki; Chinese angelica; 3 liang)

Cut these seven ingredients into pieces and decoct over a gentle fire in 1 jin of rendered lard until the angelica dahurica turns yellow; the medicine is then ready. Each time rub it on the sore, twice a day.

## Notes

Huaibaipi gao (Wu Zhige's additions): an ointment for internal and external piles with hard nodes, itching, severe pain, red ulcerating perianal sores and persistent warty growths.
- Sophora root bark, chinaberry fruit, rice bean, peach kernel, angelica, angelica dahurica and licorice boiled in lard.
