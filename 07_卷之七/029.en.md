# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之七 029 玉屑無憂散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yuxie wuyou san (Jade Crumbs Carefree Powder)

It treats all of the following: swollen, painful throat, sores of the tongue and cheeks, congestion of wind toxin, and throat blockage from exuberant heat; or accidental swallowing of a hard object or the various bones stuck in the throat, with drool filling the mouth, hurried breathing, or even oppression, confusion and loss of consciousness.

xuanshen (scrophularia; rhizome head removed)　jingjie sui (schizonepeta spikes)　huashi (kasseki; talc; ground)　huanglian (oren; coptis; hairs removed)　suosha (amomum; husks removed)　bai fuling (bukuryo; white poria; stir-fried yellow)　guanzhong (kanju; cyrtomium rhizome; rhizome head removed)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried)　shandougen (sophora root; 1 liang each)　hanshuishi (kansuiseki; calcite; ground and water-ground; 2 liang)　pengsha (borax; 2 *qian*)

Make the above into a fine powder. Sprinkle 1 *qian* each time dry on the tongue, then swallow it down with fresh water, at any time.

## Notes

Yuxie wuyou san: for swollen, painful throat, sores of the tongue and cheeks, wind-toxin congestion and throat blockage from exuberant heat, and for hard objects or bones lodged in the throat (with drooling, hurried breathing or loss of consciousness).
- Sophora root, scrophularia, coptis, calcite, talc and borax clear heat; schizonepeta, cyrtomium, amomum, poria and licorice are added. Sprinkled on the tongue and swallowed.
