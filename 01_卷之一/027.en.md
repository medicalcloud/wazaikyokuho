# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 027 天南星丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Tiannanxing wan (Arisaema Pill)

It treats wind and transforms phlegm, clears the spirit and refreshes the qi, frees the chest and diaphragm, dispels wine toxin, and stops phlegm counterflow with nausea and vomiting from drunkenness.

tiannanxing (arisaema; 1 jin, pieces weighing about 1 liang each, soaked and washed in warm water, the loose skin inside and out and any hollow, soft parts scraped away clean; soaked overnight in prepared wine and steamed over mulberry firewood, adding hot water continuously to keep the cauldron full and the steam in the steamer strong, and sprinkling wine on it continually to keep the medicine moist; after seven full days and nights take it out, cut open one large piece with a copper knife and chew a little: if it does not numb the tongue it is done, otherwise steam again; when done, slice it fine with a copper knife and dry over a fire)　chensha (cinnabar; ground and water-ground; 2 liang, half for coating)　dingxiang (clove)　shexiang (musk; ground; 1 liang each)　longnao (borneol; ground; 1½ liang)

Make the above into a fine powder, add the ground medicines and mix evenly, knead with refined honey and wine into pills, fifty pills to each liang, coated with cinnabar powder. Take one pill each time, chewed well, with strongly decocted ginger decoction, at any time. Held in the mouth to dissolve after drinking, it removes vexing thirst and stops vomiting.

## Notes

Tiannanxing wan: transforms wind-phlegm and stops nausea and vomiting after drinking.
- It describes in detail the processing of arisaema: soaking in wine and steaming for seven days and nights until it no longer numbs the tongue when chewed.
