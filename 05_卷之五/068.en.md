# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 068 金液丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jinye dan (Golden Fluid Elixir)

It secures genuine qi, warms the cinnabar field, firms the sinews and bones, strengthens the yang way, removes long-standing cold and chronic chill, and supplements taxation damage and deficiency. It treats men with long-standing cold of the lower back and kidneys, abdominal masses in the heart and abdomen, cold aggregations below the flanks, various worms in the abdomen, loss of essence and enuresis, an emaciated form and inferior strength, painful, weak legs and knees, stubborn numbness from cold wind, qi ascent and nosebleed, cough with counterflow and chills and fever, sudden turmoil with cramps, and deficiency slippage with unceasing diarrhea. It also treats piles and fistulas, damp *ni*-worm sores, and unceasing bleeding from below; and women's blood binding with chills and fever, genital erosion, flat-abscesses and piles.

liuhuang (sulfur; 10 liang, carefully picked free of sand and stones, ground fine and water-ground, placed in a porcelain box whose mouth is sealed with red halloysite mixed in water, sealed with salt clay and dried in the sun. First bury a small jar in the ground and fill it with water, set the box on top, seal it with clay, nourish it over a gentle fire for seven days and seven nights; when the time is complete, add 1 jin of top fire and calcine; when it has cooled, take it out and grind into a fine powder)

For 1 liang of the above medicine powder use 1 liang of steamed cake soaked in hot water with the water squeezed out, knead and form pills the size of wutong seeds. Take thirty pills each time, up to a hundred, with warm rice water on an empty stomach. It also treats yin patterns of cold damage with a cold body, feeble pulse and cold hands and feet, with vomiting or diarrhea, or spontaneous sweating that stops by itself, or urinary incontinence; regardless of the number of pills, it should be taken continuously, until the body becomes warm and the pulse emerges.

## Notes

Jinye dan: said to secure genuine qi and warm the lower origin; for long-standing cold of the back and kidneys, accumulations, worms, seminal emission, enuresis, painful legs, numbness, nosebleed, cough, sudden turmoil, diarrhea, piles, fistulas, rectal bleeding, women's genital diseases, and yin-pattern cold damage with cold limbs.
- An elixir of sulfur alone, sealed in a box and heated for seven days and nights.
