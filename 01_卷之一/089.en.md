# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 089 苦参丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Kushen wan (Kujingan; Sophora Root Pill)

It treats accumulated heat of the heart and lungs and wind toxin of the kidneys attacking the skin, with recurrent scabies and lepra-like eruptions, unbearable itching and recurrent yellow discharge; as well as great wind with ulceration and destruction of the hands and feet and loss of the eyebrows; and all wind diseases.

kushen (kujin; sophora root; 32 liang)　jingjie (schizonepeta; stalks removed; 16 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with water paste. Take thirty pills each time, swallowed with good tea or with schizonepeta decoction, after meals.

## Notes

Kushen wan (bureau additions): for itchy scabies-like eruptions with discharge from heart-lung heat and kidney wind toxin, and for ulcerated limbs and loss of eyebrows in "great wind" (a disease usually identified with leprosy).
- Two ingredients: sophora root clears damp-heat and schizonepeta dispels wind.
