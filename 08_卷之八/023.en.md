# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 023 青解毒丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qing jiedu wan (Blue Toxin-Resolving Pill)

It treats adults and children with accumulated heat of the five zang organs and toxic qi attacking upward, vexation and oppression of the chest and diaphragm, swollen, painful throat, red eyes and abscess swellings, fever of the head and face, dry lips and mouth, sores on both cheeks, an abstracted spirit, palpitation with oppression and confusion, and restlessness whether sitting or lying; as well as damage by summer heat toxin with red face and hot body, agitation of the heart with vexing thirst and inability to eat.

hanshuishi (kansuiseki; calcite; ground)　shigao (sekko; gypsum; ground; 16 liang each)　qingdai (indigo; 8 liang)

Grind the above fine as powder, mix in the indigo evenly, and with seven steamed cakes mixed in water form pills the size of euryale seeds. Take one pill each time after meals dissolved in freshly drawn water; or chew it thoroughly and take it with ginger water. It is also suitable for all kinds of poisoning. For children's fright wind and tidal fever with congested phlegm and drool, restlessness of the heart and chest, red cheeks and much thirst, and restless sleep, a three-year-old child may take half a pill, adjusted further according to age.

## Notes

Qing jiedu wan (Wu Zhige's additions): for chest oppression, sore throat, red eyes, abscesses, facial heat, dry mouth, cheek sores, abstraction, palpitation and restlessness from accumulated heat of the five zang organs with toxic qi attacking upward; for red face, fever, vexing thirst and inability to eat from summer heat toxin; for poisonings; and for children's fright wind, tidal fever, phlegm, agitation, red cheeks, thirst and restless sleep.
- Three ingredients (calcite, gypsum, indigo) to clear heat and resolve toxin.
- Emendation: "restless" (*bu wen*) reads *bu yin* in the base text; emended following the Siku quanshu and Executive Yuan editions.
