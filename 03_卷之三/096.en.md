# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 096 塩煎散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yanjian san (Salt-Decocted Powder)

The patterns it treats are the same as for the earlier Yanjian san.

liangjiang (galangal; stir-fried)　cangzhu (black atractylodes; skin removed; 12 liang each)　suosha (amomum; skin removed)　huixiang (fennel; stir-fried; 5 liang each)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed, not exposed to fire)　dingpi (clove bark; 2 liang each)　juhong (red tangerine peel; 10 liang)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; stir-fried; 6 liang)　qingpi (green tangerine peel; white removed; 4 liang)　shanyao (Chinese yam; half a jin)

Make the above into a fine powder. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water and one *zi* of salt, decoct down to eight-tenths and [take it] on an empty stomach before meals.

## Notes

Yanjian san (bureau additions): the same indications as the earlier Yanjian san (juan 3, no. 067), but a different ten-ingredient formula (galangal, black atractylodes, amomum, fennel, cinnamon, clove bark, red and green tangerine peel, Chinese yam, licorice).
