# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 033 大通聖白花蛇散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Da tongsheng baihuashe san (Great Sage-Penetrating Pit Viper Powder)

It greatly treats all kinds of wind, whether new or old: limp, dragging hands and feet, slack and weak lower back and legs, an uneven gait, a clouded and covered spirit, deviation of the mouth and face, halting and sluggish speech, and exuberant congested phlegm and drool; or cramping of the sinews and vessels, stubborn numbness of the flesh, itching skin, vexing pain of the joints, or pain with no fixed location, wandering and unsettled; as well as wind qi attacking upward with a puffy face, tinnitus, headache and dizziness, or pouring down into the lower back and legs with lumbar pain, heavy thighs, swelling, itching and sores. It is suitable for all these.

haitongpi (erythrina bark; coarse bark removed)　duzhong (tochu; eucommia; chopped and stir-fried)　tianma (gastrodia; sprouts removed)　ganxie (dried scorpion; stir-fried)　yuliren (ikurinin; bush cherry kernel)　chijian (sekisen; gastrodia stem)　danggui (toki) (Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed, soaked in wine)　houpo (koboku; magnolia bark; processed with ginger juice)　manjingzi (vitex fruit; white skin removed)　muxiang (mokko; costus root)　fangfeng (bofu; saposhnikovia; sprouts removed)　gaoben (kohon) (Chinese lovage; earth removed)　baifuzi (typhonium; blast-fried)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　qianghuo (notopterygium; rhizome head removed)　bixie (dioscorea hypoglauca; soaked overnight in wine)　tiger bone (roasted with vinegar)　baizhi (byakushi; angelica dahurica)　shanyao (Chinese yam)　baihuashe (pit viper; soaked in wine and roasted, skin and bones removed, flesh used)　juhua (chrysanthemum; twigs and stalks removed)　niuxi (goshitsu; achyranthes; sprouts removed)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried)　weilingxian (clematis root; earth removed; 1 liang each)

Powder the above in equal parts. Take 1–2 *qian* each time mixed in warm wine; schizonepeta decoction is also fine; take on an empty stomach. Taken regularly it drives out wind qi and circulates the ying-nutrient and wei-defense qi. Those long ill with wind should take it regularly; a mild wind stroke recovers within twenty doses.

## Notes

Da tongsheng baihuashe san: a 24-ingredient powder for paralysis, gait and speech disorders, numbness and wandering pain from wind, new or old, and for wind qi attacking up or down.
- Pit viper and scorpion dispel wind from the meridians; eucommia, achyranthes, tiger bone and dioscorea strengthen the lower back and legs; many wind-dispelling and interior-warming drugs are added.
- The base text writes 赤箭当帰 without a space, but these are two ingredients, chijian and danggui.
