# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 070 枳実理中丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zhishi lizhong wan (Kijitsurichugan; Unripe Bitter Orange Middle-Regulating Pill)

It regulates the middle jiao, removes stuffiness and fullness, drives out phlegm-fluid retention and stops abdominal pain. It greatly treats chest bind in cold damage when the patient is near death, with the heart and diaphragm raised high, excess, full and painful, so that the hand cannot approach.

zhishi (kijitsu; unripe bitter orange; stir-fried with bran; 1 liang)　baizhu (byakujutsu; white atractylodes)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng; rhizome head removed)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried)　bai fuling (bukuryo; white poria; skin removed)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried; 2 liang each)

Pound and sieve the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of an egg yolk with refined honey. Take one pill each time dissolved in hot water. After two or three doses in succession the chest opens up. At any time.

## Notes

Zhishi lizhong wan (from here the layer of Chunyou-era additions): regulates the middle jiao, for stuffiness, fullness, phlegm-fluid retention and abdominal pain, and for chest bind in cold damage (raised, painful epigastrium that cannot be touched).
- Lizhong wan (Richugan) with unripe bitter orange and poria added.
