# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 015 成煉鍾乳粉 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Chenglian zhongru fen (Refined Stalactite Powder)

It governs the five taxations and seven damages and coughing counterflow with rising qi; it treats cold cough, restores the voice, brightens the eyes and augments essence, calms the five zang organs, frees all the joints, benefits the nine orifices, brings down breast milk, augments qi and supplements deficiency and damage, treats weak, cold, painful legs and exhausted damage of the lower jiao, and strengthens yin. Taken for a long time it prolongs life, improves the complexion, prevents aging and enables one to have children.

zhongru (stalactite; any amount)

Take stalactite from Shaozhou, thick or thin, as long as its color is bright, clean and lustrous; such is fit to refine. Only the yellow and red kinds are unfit for use. Any amount may be refined. Put the stalactite in a gold or silver vessel, fill a large pot with water, sink the gold or silver vessel in the water of the pot and boil it, keeping it always at a simmer with bubbles like fish eyes, adding water as it decreases. Thin stalactite is done in three days and three nights; coarse, thick, heavy-tubed pieces take seven days and seven nights; when the color of the stalactite turns yellowish white it is cooked. If one suspects it is still raw, boil it longer; a full ten days is best. When finished, take out the gold or silver bowl; discard the yellow turbid water in the pot in which the stalactite was boiled, and let no one drink it: drinking it is sure to harm the throat and injure the liver and lungs, causing headache together with unceasing diarrhea. Anyone who has drunk it recovers at once by eating pork. When this yellow water has been discarded, add clear water and boil again as before; after about half a day take it out. If the water stays clear and unchanged, stop: the stalactite is now free of toxin. Then grind it with water in a porcelain mortar with a jade pestle. The mortar and pestle must be enclosed in a white glossed-silk bag, its mouth made rather long so that the pestle can turn and move up and down. Each day add water and stir evenly so that it does not stick to the pestle or mortar, then seal and tie the bag's mouth and mark it with one's own writing so that no one can open it—first to keep out fine dust, second to keep the grinder from stealing and eating it. When the grinding feels dry and rough, the water is used up; add more, keeping it always like thin rice-washing water. The fine particles float up and the coarse sink, so grind around all sides of the mortar; if any part is missed, coarse and fine will be uneven. Therefore open it once or twice a day, stir and scrape it evenly so that it does not stick to the pestle; then it will become evenly fine with no coarse and fine mixture. After four or five days of grinding it looks like milk; rub some and look at it: when it looks like the greasy film of silverfish in books, it is done. When it is naturally lustrous and white, wash it with water; what does not fall away with the water is ready; what falls away with the water is not yet done and must be ground further, until it no longer falls away. When finished, let it settle, take it and dry it in the sun. Each time weigh out half a liang, divide it into three doses and take it mixed in warm wine on an empty stomach, adjusting according to the severity of the illness. It may also be compounded into stalactite pills and powders.

## Notes

Chenglian zhongru fen: said to treat the five taxations and seven damages, coughing with rising qi, cold cough and loss of voice; to benefit the eyes, essence, zang organs, joints and orifices; to promote lactation; and to supplement deficiency, cold legs and the lower jiao and strengthen yin.
- It gives a detailed method: stalactite is boiled (the first yellow, turbid water is discarded as harmful) and ground for days with a jade pestle into a fine powder; it even advises sealing the bag to stop the grinder from stealing it. The formula belongs to the tradition, since Tang times, of taking stalactite (consuming minerals for longevity).
