# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 098 玉真湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yuzhen tang (Jade Truth Drink)

It treats all cold qi with phlegm counterflow and nausea, stuffiness and oppression of the chest and diaphragm, gripping pain of the navel and abdomen, a bitter mouth without taste, and food without relish.

awei (asafetida; wrapped in dough and roasted in embers)　huixiang (fennel; picked clean, stir-fried; 3 jin each)　tanxiang (sandalwood; 1½ jin)　hujiao (black pepper; 9 liang)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried; 1½ jin)　xingren (kyonin; apricot kernel; skin and tips removed, stir-fried with bran, pounded separately; 3 jin 12 liang)　bai jingmi (kobei; white non-glutinous rice; stir-fried; 1 *dou* 6 sheng)　baimian (white flour; stir-fried; 6 liang)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; stir-fried; 10 liang)　yan (salt; stir-fried; 23½ jin)

Powder the above. Take 1 *qian* each time mixed with boiling water, before meals.

## Notes

Yuzhen tang ("Various drinks"): a drink for phlegm counterflow, nausea, chest stuffiness, gripping umbilical pain, bitter mouth and tasteless food from all cold qi.
- Asafetida, fennel, sandalwood, black pepper and dried ginger warm and move qi; apricot kernel, rice, white flour, licorice and salt are added.
