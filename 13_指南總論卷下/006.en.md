# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷下 006 論脾胃諸疾 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On the various disorders of the spleen and stomach. For long-standing spleen diarrhea with slippery, uncontrollable intestines, without limit for many days, and deficient emaciation, give Pingwei san (Heiisan) taken on an empty stomach with fennel decoction, together with Helile wan (Karirokugan) and Dingxiang doukou san. If various medicines have failed, give much Renshen (ninjin) doukou san; it is effective. There is also a kind where the stools are normal but every day in the early morning there are one or two watery stools, and nothing during the day; Jinsuo zhengyuan dan may be taken, stopping as soon as the diarrhea stops.

## Notes

On spleen and stomach disorders: for long-standing spleen diarrhea with slippery intestines, incontinence and wasting, Pingwei san with fennel decoction plus Helile wan and Dingxiang doukou san, or Renshen doukou san if these fail; for watery stools only in the early morning (of the "fifth-watch diarrhea" type), Jinsuo zhengyuan dan.
