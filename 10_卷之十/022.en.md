# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 022 開胃丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Kaiwei wan (Stomach-Opening Pill)

It treats children with timid, weak viscera and bowels that have received wind-cold internally, with abdominal pain, distension and fullness, borborygmus and diarrhoea, green or white [stools] and undigested milk and food. It also treats night crying from cold viscera and abdominal pain from foetal cold.

bai shaoyao (white peony root)　shexiang (musk; finely ground; 1 *fen* each)　renshen (ginseng)　muxiang (costus root)　peng ezhu (zedoary; roasted in embers)　baizhu (white atractylodes)　danggui [Chinese angelica; shoots removed, lightly stir-fried; half a liang each (one version lacks white atractylodes)]

Pound and sift the above into a powder, grind all evenly, knead with steamed cake soaked in hot water and form pills the size of broomcorn millet. Take fifteen pills each time with warm rice water. For abdominal pain and night crying in newborns, five pills may be given. Always before feeding.

## Notes

Kaiwei wan: for abdominal pain, distension, borborygmus, green or white diarrhoea and undigested milk from timid viscera with wind-cold in children; for night crying from cold viscera; and for abdominal pain from foetal cold. Also for newborns.
- Ginseng, atractylodes, angelica and white peony supplement; costus, zedoary and musk move qi.
