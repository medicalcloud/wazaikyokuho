# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 087 黒神丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Heishen wan (Black Spirit Pill)

It treats all wind diseases and paralytic wind with trembling of the hands and feet, numbness all over, stiffness of the shoulders and back and pain in the joints; it also treats women's blood wind with spinning head, dizzy eyes and a weary spirit.

mudanpi (moutan bark)　bai shaoyao (shakuyaku; white peony root)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage)　mahuang (mao; ephedra; roots and nodes removed; 4 liang each)　chi shaoyao (red peony root)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; 10 liang each)　jingjie (schizonepeta)　caowu (wild aconite; blast-fried; 6 liang each)　wudou (black soybeans; 8 liang)　heshouwu (fleeceflower root; soaked in rice-washing water, sliced and dried over a fire; 12 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of a euryale seed with water paste. Take one pill each time, chewed thoroughly, with tea or wine as preferred, at any time. For women's flowing blood wind, take it with black soybean-steeped wine; for children's fright wind, with decocted gold-and-silver decoction; for cough from wind damage, with ephedra decocted in wine; for headache, with scallion tea.

## Notes

Heishen wan (bureau additions): a different formula from the earlier one of the same name (juan 1, no. 058), for trembling, numbness, stiff shoulders and joint pain in wind disease and paralysis, and for women's blood wind.
- Wild aconite, ephedra and schizonepeta dispel wind; moutan, red and white peony, lovage and fleeceflower root regulate the blood; the vehicle varies with the symptoms.
