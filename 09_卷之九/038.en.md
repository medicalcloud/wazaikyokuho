# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 038 滋血湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zixue tang (Blood-Enriching Decoction)

It can treat women whose excessive taxation damage has injured the viscera and bowels, so that the qi of the *chong* and *ren* is deficient and cannot restrain the menstrual blood—sudden discharge, which is called flooding, or passing of fresh blood or of static blood unceasing for days, dribbling without end, with an emaciated form, inferior qi, lassitude and exhaustion. (Another formula appears later.)

chi shizhi (red halloysite; calcined red)　haipiaoxiao (cuttlefish bone; shell removed)　cebaiye (arborvitae leaf; twigs removed; 5 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder. Take 2 *qian* each time mixed in hot rice water; three doses in succession in one day bring a cure; at any time. The efficacy of this medicine cannot be fully described.

## Notes

Zixue tang (from here the layer of Baoqing-era additions): for flooding (sudden heavy bleeding) and persistent bleeding of fresh or static blood, wasting and fatigue when deficient qi of the *chong* and *ren* from excessive taxation damage cannot restrain the menses.
- Three ingredients (red halloysite, cuttlefish bone, arborvitae leaf) to stop bleeding. Another Zixue tang appears later.
