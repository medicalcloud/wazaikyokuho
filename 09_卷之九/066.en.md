# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 066 烏金散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wujin san (Black Gold Powder)

It treats postpartum blood confusion and blood dizziness, unceasing failed blood dribbling without end, pain of the navel and abdomen, dizziness of the head and eyes, weakness and much sweating. It is also suitable for flooding with excessive, unceasing bleeding.

qilinjie (dragon's blood)　baicaoshuang (pot soot)　luanfa (human hair; must be a man's, burned to ash)　songmo (pine-soot ink; calcined, quenched in vinegar)　liyu lin (carp scales; burned and powdered)　yanhusuo (corydalis)　danggui (Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　chi shaoyao (red peony root)

Take equal parts of the above, pound and sift into a powder. Take 2 *qian* each time mixed in warm wine.

## Notes

Wujin san (bureau additions): for postpartum blood confusion and dizziness, persistent failed blood, umbilical pain, dizziness, weakness and sweating, and for heavy unceasing bleeding in flooding. A different formula from the earlier Wujin san (juan 9, no. 039).
- Black drugs—dragon's blood, pot soot, hair ash, pine-soot ink and carp-scale ash—stop bleeding; corydalis, angelica, red peony and cinnamon move blood.
