# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 082 阿膠枳殻丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Ejiao zhiqiao wan (Donkey-Hide Gelatin and Bitter Orange Pill)

It treats postpartum weakness with constipated, rough stools.

ejiao (donkey-hide gelatin; broken up, stir-fried)　zhiqiao (bitter orange; soaked, pulp removed, stir-fried with bran; 2 liang each)　huashi (talc; ground and water-ground, for coating; half a liang)

Powder the above and form pills the size of wutong seeds with refined honey. Take twenty pills each time with warm water; if the bowels have not moved after about half a day, take again.

## Notes

Ejiao zhiqiao wan (bureau additions): for constipation from postpartum weakness.
- Donkey-hide gelatin moistens, bitter orange moves qi, talc forms the coating.
