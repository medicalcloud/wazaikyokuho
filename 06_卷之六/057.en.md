# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 057 大断下丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Da duanxia wan (Major Diarrhoea-Stopping Pill)

It treats cold lodged in the viscera and bowels with weak, deficient intestines and stomach, abdominal pain and diarrhoea, and no desire whatever for food.

gao liangjiang (lesser galangal; rhizome head removed)　chi shizhi (red halloysite; ground)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried)　longgu (dragon bone; ground; 1½ liang each)　roudoukou (nutmeg; wrapped in dough and roasted in embers)　muli (oyster shell; calcined)　fuzi (aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　baifan (alum; dried)　hezi (chebule; roasted in embers, stone removed; 1 liang each)　xixin (asarum; earth and leaves removed; 7½ *qian*)　suan shiliu pi (sour pomegranate rind; pulp removed, soaked overnight in rice vinegar, taken out and roasted to a scorched yellow; 1 liang)

Powder the above and form pills the size of wutong seeds with flour paste boiled in vinegar. Take fifty pills each time on an empty stomach with warm rice water.

## Notes

Da duanxia wan (from here the layer of Chunyou-era additions): for abdominal pain, diarrhoea and loss of appetite from cold lodged in the viscera and weak intestines and stomach.
- Lesser galangal, dried ginger, aconite and asarum warm; red halloysite, dragon bone, oyster shell, dried alum, chebule, nutmeg and pomegranate rind astringe the intestines to stop the diarrhoea.
