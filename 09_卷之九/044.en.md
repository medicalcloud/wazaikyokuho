# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 044 詵詵丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shenshen wan (Numerous Progeny Pill)

It treats women with deficiency cold of the *chong* and *ren*, failure to conceive, or frequent miscarriage.

zelan ye (lycopus leaf)　baizhu (white atractylodes; 1½ liang each)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried; half a liang each)　shu dihuang (prepared rehmannia; washed, dried over a fire)　danggui (Chinese angelica; washed, dried over a fire; 2 liang each)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage)　shihu (dendrobium; soaked in wine, chopped, stir-fried)　bai shaoyao (white peony root)　mudanpi (moutan bark; core removed)　yanhusuo (corydalis; 1 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with flour paste boiled in vinegar. Take fifty pills each time with warm wine on an empty stomach.

## Notes

Shenshen wan (Chunyou-era additions): for infertility and habitual miscarriage from deficiency cold of the *chong* and *ren*. "Shenshen" means "numerous (offspring)", from the *Shijing*.
- Siwu tang (prepared rehmannia, angelica, lovage, white peony), atractylodes and dendrobium supplement; lycopus, moutan and corydalis move blood; cinnamon and dried ginger warm.
