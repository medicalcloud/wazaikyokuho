# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 083 異香散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yixiang san (Extraordinary Fragrance Powder)

It treats disharmony of kidney qi with distension of the abdomen and flanks, stuffiness, oppression and choking obstruction, dyspnea and fullness and discomfort, food hard to digest, belching and acid swallowing; and all qi stuffiness with stabbing pain in the abdomen. This medicine can break up concretions, conglomerations and bound gatherings and greatly disperse chronic cold and deep accumulations. Taken regularly it regulates the five zang organs and sanjiao, harmonizes the stomach and improves eating.

shilian rou (lotus fruit flesh; skin removed; 1 liang)　peng'ezhu (zedoary; roasted)　jing sanleng (sparganium; blast-fried)　yizhi ren (alpinia oxyphylla; blast-fried)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; scorched; 6 liang each)　qingpi (green tangerine peel; white removed)　chenpi (chinpi; aged tangerine peel; white pith removed; 3 *fen* each)　houpo (koboku; magnolia bark; coarse bark removed, roasted with ginger juice; 2 liang)

Make the above medicines into a fine powder. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one *zhan* of water, three slices of fresh ginger, one jujube and a pinch of salt, decoct down to seven-tenths and take it at a comfortable temperature, at any time. It may also be stirred into salt water or mixed with salt or wine.

## Notes

Yixiang san (Wu Zhige's additions): for abdominal and flank distension, stuffiness, dyspnea and fullness, indigestion and acid reflux from disharmony of kidney qi, and for qi stuffiness and stabbing abdominal pain; said to break up concretions, accumulations and chronic cold.
- Sparganium and zedoary break up accumulations; alpinia and lotus fruit warm and secure the spleen and kidneys; the tangerine peels and magnolia bark move qi.
- The base text writes 青皮（去白）陳皮 without a space, but these are two ingredients.
