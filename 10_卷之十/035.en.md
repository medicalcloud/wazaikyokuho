# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 035 天竺飲子 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Tianzhu yinzi (Tabasheer Drink)

It treats accumulated heat of the bowels and viscera in adults and children with vexation, agitation and much thirst, sores of the tongue and cheeks, swollen, painful throat, hot face and dry mouth, red eyes and nosebleed, cinnabar tumours and nodes, and swollen, painful abscess sores. It also treats latent summerheat with dry heat, residual toxin of sores and rashes, and bloody stools and red, rough urine.

chuan yujin (Sichuan curcuma tuber; boiled in gleditsia water, sliced, dried over a fire)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried; 20 liang each)　da zhizi ren (large gardenia kernel; lightly stir-fried)　lianqiao (forsythia; 20 liang each)　xionghuang (realgar; water-ground; 5 liang)　gualougen (trichosanthes root; 10 jin)

Make the above into a fine powder. Take one large *qian* each time after meals and at bedtime mixed in fresh water; for children half a *qian*, adjusted further at the time as judged for the child's size.

## Notes

Tianzhu yinzi: for agitation, thirst, sores of the tongue and cheeks, sore throat, hot face, dry mouth, red eyes, nosebleed, cinnabar tumours, nodes and abscesses from accumulated heat of the viscera in adults and children; for dry heat of latent summerheat; for residual toxin of eruptions; and for bloody stools and dark, rough urine.
- Curcuma, gardenia, forsythia, trichosanthes root and realgar clear heat toxin; licorice is added. Despite the name, the formula in the base text contains no tabasheer.
