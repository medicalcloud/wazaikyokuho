# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 010 抱竜丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Baolong wan (Horyugan; Dragon-Embracing Pill)

It treats wind congestion with phlegm excess, clouded, dizzy head and eyes, vexation and oppression of the chest and diaphragm, an unsettled heart-spirit, abstraction with fright palpitations, and congestion of phlegm and drool; as well as vexing thirst from summer heat strike and mania and agitation from yang toxin.

xionghuang (yuo; realgar; ground and water-ground; 4 liang)　baishiying (hakusekiei; quartz; ground and water-ground)　sheng xijiao (saikaku; raw rhinoceros horn)　shexiang (jako; musk; ground)　zhusha (cinnabar; ground and water-ground; 1 liang each)　huoxiang ye (agastache leaf; 2 liang)　tiannanxing (arisaema; processed with ox bile; 16 liang)　niuhuang (goo; bovine bezoar; ground; half a liang)　ejiao (akyo; donkey-hide gelatin; broken up and stir-fried into beads; 3 liang)　jinbo (gold leaf; ground)　yinbo (silver leaf; ground; 50 leaves each)

Make the above into a fine powder, mix in the ground medicines evenly, knead with warm water and form pills the size of euryale seeds. Take one pill each time, dissolved and broken up in freshly drawn water with a little salt added, after meals.

## Notes

Baolong wan: for dizziness, chest oppression, an unsettled heart-spirit, fright palpitations and phlegm congestion from wind congestion with phlegm excess, for vexing thirst from summer heat strike, and for mania from yang toxin. Later known mainly as a formula for children's fright wind.
- Mainly bile-processed arisaema; realgar, bovine bezoar, musk, cinnabar, rhinoceros horn and gold and silver leaf remove phlegm and settle; quartz, agastache and donkey-hide gelatin are added.
