# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 071 旋覆湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xuanfu tang (Inula Decoction)

It treats postpartum damage by wind and contraction of cold, summer heat and damp, with cough, dyspnea and fullness, congested phlegm and drool, and restlessness whether sitting or lying.

xuanfuhua (senpukuka; inula flower)　wuweizi (gomishi; schisandra)　qianhu (peucedanum)　mahuang (mao; ephedra; nodes removed)　chi shaoyao (shakuyaku; red peony root)　banxiaqu (pinellia leaven)　xingren (kyonin; apricot kernel; skin and tips removed, stir-fried with bran)　fuling (bukuryo; poria; skin removed)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried)　jingjie (schizonepeta; stalks removed)

Take equal parts of the above and make into a coarse powder. For each dose take four large *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water, five slices of ginger and one jujube, decoct down to seven-tenths, remove the dregs and take it before meals.

## Notes

Xuanfu tang (bureau additions): for cough, dyspnea, congested phlegm and restlessness when wind, cold, summer heat or damp are contracted after childbirth.
- Ephedra, schizonepeta and peucedanum release the exterior; inula, pinellia leaven, apricot kernel and poria remove phlegm and stop cough; schisandra astringes; red peony and licorice are added.
