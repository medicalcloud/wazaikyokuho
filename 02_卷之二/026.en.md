# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 026 加減三五七散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jiajian sanwuqi san (Modified Three-Five-Seven Powder)

The patterns treated and the formula appear under the various winds.

## Notes

Only the heading of Jiajian sanwuqi san, with a cross-reference to juan 1, "treating the various winds" (juan 1, no. 041).
