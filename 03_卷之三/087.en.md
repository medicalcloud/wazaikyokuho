# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 087 丁香脾積丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Dingxiang piji wan (Clove Spleen Accumulation Pill)

It treats all kinds of food damage and accumulations in men, women and children, with distension and fullness of the chest and diaphragm, distension of the heart and abdomen, belching and acid swallowing, undigested old food, spleen pain and stomach reflux. It is also suitable for women's stabbing blood-qi pain.

dingxiang (clove)　muxiang (mokko; costus root; half a liang each)　zaojia (sokyo; soap pods; three large ones, burned with their nature preserved)　qing jupi (kippi; green tangerine peel; washed; 1 liang)　ezhu (zedoary; 3 liang)　sanleng (sparganium; 2 liang)　gaoliangjiang (lesser galangal; 2 liang; the above boiled dry together with 1 *sheng* of rice vinegar in a porcelain jar; the zedoary, sparganium and galangal are chopped while hot and dried over a fire together)　badou (hazu; croton seeds; shells removed; half a liang)

Add three spoonfuls of pot soot to the above, mill together into a fine powder and form pills the size of hemp seeds with flour paste. Take five or seven pills each time, up to fifteen or twenty at most. For food damage, take them with [a decoction of] the food concerned; for spleen-accumulation qi, with aged tangerine-peel decoction; for vomiting of sour water, with weak ginger decoction; for regurgitation, with agastache and licorice decoction; for men's small-intestine qi, with stir-fried fennel in wine; for women's stabbing blood-qi pain, with weak vinegar decoction; for vomiting counterflow, with acorus decoction; for children's gan qi, with quisqualis decoction. Adjust further according to deficiency or excess. To purge, the number of pills may be increased: take them at the beginning of the fifth watch with cold clear tea, and after three to five bowel movements restore with white rice gruel. Pregnant women must not take it.

## Notes

Dingxiang piji wan (Wu Zhige's additions): for chest and abdominal distension, acid reflux, old food, spleen pain and stomach reflux from food damage and accumulations, and for women's blood-qi pain; the vehicle varies with the symptom, and for purging the dose is increased and taken before dawn with cold tea.
- Croton, soap pod, sparganium and zedoary attack and purge accumulations; clove, costus, galangal and green tangerine peel warm and move qi; pot soot is added. Contraindicated in pregnancy.
