# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 015 論諸風挫枕転筋 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On cricked neck and cramps in the various winds. When from qi deficiency the neck sinews cannot turn and the sinews and collaterals are not smooth and painful, give Tongguan san (Tsukansan), Xiaofeng san (Shofusan), Da sanwuqi san or Zhuifeng san. For abundant phlegm and drool, give Bai wanzi.

## Notes

On cricked neck and cramps in the various winds (inability to turn the neck and painful sinews from qi deficiency): Tongguan san, Xiaofeng san, Da sanwuqi san or Zhuifeng san; Bai wanzi for abundant phlegm.
