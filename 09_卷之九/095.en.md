# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 095 推婦人行年法 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Method of Reckoning a Woman's Age-Year

Direction of Vital Qi: the parturient woman should sit and lie facing it, and the door of the delivery curtain should open toward it; greatly auspicious.
Month of Fanzhi (Reverse Branch): in this month, spread ashes, cover them with ox hide or horse or donkey hide, then spread straw, so that the malign blood does not soil the ground; auspicious.
Month of Calamity: do not give birth on it, and do not urinate or defecate facing it; avoiding it is greatly auspicious.
Direction of Cut-Off Life: do not give birth on it, and do not urinate or defecate facing it; avoiding it is greatly auspicious.
Day of Hanging Corpse: when giving birth on this day, do not grasp a rope; hang up a horse's bridle and grasp that; greatly auspicious.
Day of Closed Belly: from the final month until the full month after birth, do not urinate or defecate facing it, nor discard unclean water toward it; being careful about this is auspicious.
Direction of the Eight Infixations: the door of the delivery curtain must not open toward it; avoiding it is greatly auspicious.

## Notes

Method of reckoning a woman's age-year: from the parturient woman's age-year, seven spirits are reckoned—the direction of Vital Qi, the month of Fanzhi, the month of Calamity, the direction of Cut-Off Life, the day of Hanging Corpse, the day of Closed Belly and the direction of the Eight Infixations—to judge the auspicious orientation, place and procedure for delivery. The four tables by age exist only as images in the base text and are not included here.
