# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 026 金屑辰砂膏 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jinxie chensha gao (Gold Flake and Cinnabar Paste)

It treats evil heat in the channels of children with red cheeks and much thirst, unquiet sleep, delirious and manic speech, obstructed phlegm and drool and an abstracted spirit; as well as pent-up, accumulated phlegm-heat in adults with vexation and agitation of the heart and diaphragm, swollen, painful throat, and sores of the mouth and tongue.

yaxiao (refined mirabilite; dehydrated, ground)　tiefen (iron powder; ground; half a liang each)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried; 2 liang)　longnao (borneol; ground; 2 *qian*)　chensha (cinnabar; ground and water-ground; 3 liang)　gefen (clamshell powder; ground and water-ground; 8 liang)　renshen (ginseng; 1 liang)　jinbo (gold leaf; thirty leaves, for coating)

Make the above into a fine powder, knead with refined honey, make twenty pills from each 1½ liang, pinch them flat and coat with gold leaf. Give a piece half the size of a gleditsia seed each time; for adults, one pill divided into two doses; always dissolved in mint decoction; after meals and at bedtime.

## Notes

Jinxie chensha gao: for red cheeks, thirst, insomnia, delirium, phlegm and abstraction from evil heat in children, and for agitation, sore throat and mouth sores from accumulated phlegm-heat in adults.
- Flat pills of cinnabar, gold leaf, iron powder, borneol and refined mirabilite to clear heat and settle the heart, clamshell powder to transform phlegm, ginseng and licorice.
