# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 013 牡蠣散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Muli san (Oyster Shell Powder)

It treats insufficiency from the various deficiencies, as well as sudden deficiency in a new illness, with fluids not secured, constant spontaneous sweating of the body, worse when lying down at night and unceasing for a long time, emaciation and withering, palpitation with fright and fear, and shortness of breath with vexation and fatigue.

huangqi (astragalus; shoots and earth removed)　mahuang gen (ephedra root; washed)　muli (oyster shell; soaked in rice-washing water, earth brushed off, burned red-hot; 1 liang each)

Make these three ingredients into a coarse powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water and a hundred-odd grains of wheat, decoct together down to eight-tenths, remove the dregs and take it hot, twice a day at any time.

## Notes

Muli san: for spontaneous sweating (worse at night), wasting, palpitation, fright, breathlessness and fatigue from the various deficiencies or sudden deficiency in a new illness. Still known today as a formula for spontaneous and night sweating.
- Oyster shell and ephedra root stop sweating; astragalus secures the exterior; decocted with wheat.
