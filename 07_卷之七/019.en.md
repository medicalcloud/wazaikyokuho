# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之七 019 洗眼紫金膏 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xiyan zijin gao (Purple Gold Eyewash Paste)

It can treat nebulae and membranes covering the eye, proliferating flesh climbing over the eye, dimness and much tearing, and unclear vision, of many years' or recent standing; or wind qi attacking and pouring in with wind-millet bumps on the lids, or red festering extending around the orbits, fear of the sun and aversion to light, and gritty roughness making the eyes hard to open.

zhusha (cinnabar; ground separately)　ruxiang (frankincense; ground separately)　pengsha (borax; ground separately)　chi shaoyao (shakuyaku; red peony root)　danggui (toki) (Chinese angelica; washed, dried over a fire; 1 *fen* each)　xionghuang (yuo; realgar; ground and water-ground; 2 *qian*)　shexiang (jako; musk; ground separately; half a *qian*)　huanglian (oren; coptis; fibrous roots removed; half a liang)

Pound and sift the above into a fine powder, mix in the ground medicines evenly, grind again, knead with refined honey and form pills the size of gleditsia seeds. Each time put one pill in a clean cup, steep it in boiling water to dissolve and wash the eyes in a place without wind. When the medicine cools, close the eyes for a while; after two or three *shichen*, warm it again until hot and wash as before. One pill can be used for three to five washings. Do not wash from a copper or iron vessel. For sudden red, swollen eyes it must not be used.

## Notes

Xiyan zijin gao (bureau additions): for nebulae, proliferating flesh, dimness, tearing and poor sight, old or new, and for bumps on the lids, festering around the orbits, photophobia and difficulty opening the eyes from wind qi. Not to be used for sudden red, swollen eyes.
- An external eyewash: coptis, red peony and angelica with cinnabar, realgar, borax, frankincense and musk made into honey pills and dissolved in hot water; one pill serves three to five washings; copper and iron vessels are avoided.
