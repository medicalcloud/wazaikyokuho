# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 048 青娥丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qing'e wan (Young Maiden Pill)

The patterns it treats are the same as for the earlier Qing'e wan.

hutao rou (walnut kernels; 30, skins and membranes removed, ground separately to a paste)　buguzhi (psoralea; stir-fried with sesame in a silver vessel until cooked)　duzhong bark (eucommia; coarse bark removed, chopped, stir-fried yellow with bran, bran removed, lightly pounded while hot, then sprinkled evenly with wine and stir-fried again; 6 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder, mix in the ground medicine evenly and form pills the size of wutong seeds with wine paste. Take thirty to fifty pills each time with warm wine or salt water, on an empty stomach before meals.

## Notes

Qing'e wan (bureau additions): the same indications as the earlier Qing'e wan (juan 5, no. 031); a different version made into pills with wine paste rather than garlic.
