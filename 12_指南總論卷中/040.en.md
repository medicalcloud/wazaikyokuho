# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 040 論傷寒腹痛 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On abdominal pain in cold damage. When in cold damage there is heat with abdominal pain, constipation for three or four days, pain around the navel, or fever without aversion to cold, or thirst, this is pain caused by dry stools in the stomach. Hot medicines must on no account be used: Zhengqi san, Lizhong tang and the various hot medicines that push out accumulations must not be used, or a life will be ruined. Simply give Xiao chaihu tang decocted with a little peony added; if one or two doses do not work, give three or four, until the bowels move. Abdominal pain in cold damage also has a cold pattern: from taking too many cold medicines, there is spontaneous diarrhoea, pain in the abdomen and cold hands and feet; give Lizhong wan, or in severe cases Fuzi lizhong wan or Lizhong tang. If these do not work, use Jiangfu tang decocted with much licorice added; with the various hot medicines it stops at once. For abdominal pain after cold damage in those with qi deficiency and the elderly, with normal stools and no fever, only abdominal pain, give Huangqi jianzhong tang, taken in quantity until effective.

## Notes

On abdominal pain in cold damage: the heat pattern (constipation for three or four days, periumbilical pain, fever without chills, thirst) is due to dry stools in the stomach—hot medicines are forbidden and Xiao chaihu tang with peony is given until the bowels move; the cold pattern (after excess cold medicines, diarrhoea, cold extremities) is treated with Lizhong wan, Fuzi lizhong wan, Lizhong tang or Jiangfu tang; afebrile abdominal pain after cold damage in the deficient or elderly is treated with Huangqi jianzhong tang.
