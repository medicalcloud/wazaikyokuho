# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 003 鍾乳沢蘭丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zhongru zelan wan (Stalactite and Lycopus Pill)

It supplements deficiency and weakness and augments blood and qi. It treats deficiency damage of the *chong* and *ren* vessels with irregular menses, gripping pain of the navel and abdomen, heaviness of the lower back and thighs, weary limbs, aching joints, palpitation and abstraction, worry, anger and joylessness, a lustreless face and tasteless food. It removes wind-cold of the lower viscera and treats the thirty-six disorders of vaginal discharge, five-coloured flooding and spotting, long-standing cold of the womb with childlessness, repeated miscarriage, and taxation damage from childbirth with deficient, weak blood and qi of the *chong* and *ren*, wasted flesh and a liking for lying down. Taken for a long time it supplements and warms the original viscus, moistens the skin, lengthens the hair and removes dark spots, removes head wind and enables conception.

zhongru fen (stalactite powder; 3 liang)　zelan (lycopus; 2 liang 2½ *qian*)　wuyi (elm seed paste; stir-fried; half a liang)　maimendong (ophiopogon; core removed, dried over a fire; 1½ liang)　shanzhuyu (cornus; 1 liang 2½ *qian*)　aiye (mugwort leaf; stir-fried with vinegar; 7½ *qian*)　fangfeng (saposhnikovia; 1 liang 7½ *qian*)　baiziren (arborvitae seed; stir-fried, pounded separately)　renshen (ginseng; rhizome head removed)　shigao (gypsum; ground and water-ground)　shihu (dendrobium; roots removed)　shu gan dihuang (prepared rehmannia; steamed with wine; 1½ liang each)　xiongqiong (Sichuan lovage)　gancao (licorice; lightly roasted until red)　niuxi (achyranthes; rhizome head removed, soaked in wine, dried over a fire)　baizhi (angelica dahurica)　shanyao (Chinese yam)　danggui (Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed, stir-fried)　gaoben (Chinese lovage)　xixin (asarum; shoots removed, not exposed to fire)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed; 1 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder, knead with refined honey and form pills the size of wutong seeds. Take thirty to fifty pills each time with warm wine or rice water, on an empty stomach before meals, twice a day.

## Notes

Zhongru zelan wan: for irregular menses, gripping umbilical pain, heavy back and thighs, lassitude, joint pain, palpitation, abstraction, low mood, poor complexion and poor appetite from deficiency of the *chong* and *ren* vessels; for wind-cold of the lower viscera, the thirty-six discharge disorders, five-coloured flooding, infertility from a cold womb, habitual miscarriage and postpartum taxation damage.
- Mainly stalactite and lycopus; prepared rehmannia, angelica, lovage, ginseng, cornus, ophiopogon, dendrobium and others supplement; mugwort and cinnamon warm; saposhnikovia, asarum, Chinese lovage and angelica dahurica dispel wind.
