# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 078 如神止瀉丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Rushen zhixie wan (Divine Diarrhoea-Stopping Pill)

It is suitable for deficiency cold of the viscera and bowels with the spleen and stomach having received damp, unceasing diarrhoea, borborygmus and abdominal pain, inability to eat and gradual emaciation.

banxia (pinellia; steeped seven times in hot water to remove the sliminess)　cangzhu (black atractylodes; soaked in rice-washing water, black skin removed, dried over a fire; half a jin each)　chuanwu (Sichuan aconite; soaked in rice-washing water until soft, skin removed, sliced, dried over a fire, stir-fried yellow with 4 liang of salt, the salt discarded; weighed at 4 liang net)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with ginger-juice paste. Take fifty pills each time on an empty stomach before meals, swallowed with rice water.

## Notes

Rushen zhixie wan (bureau additions): for unceasing diarrhoea, borborygmus, abdominal pain, poor appetite and wasting from deficiency cold of the viscera with damp in the spleen and stomach.
- Three ingredients: black atractylodes and pinellia remove damp; Sichuan aconite stir-fried with salt warms.
