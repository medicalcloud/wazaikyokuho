# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 057 大断下丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Da duanxia wan (Major Diarrhea-Stopping Pill)

It treats cold lodged in the zang-fu organs with weak, deficient intestines and stomach, abdominal pain and diarrhea, and no desire whatever for food.

gao liangjiang (lesser galangal; rhizome head removed)　chi shizhi (red halloysite; ground)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried)　longgu (ryukotsu; dragon bone; ground; 1½ liang each)　roudoukou (nutmeg; wrapped in dough and roasted in embers)　muli (borei; oyster shell; calcined)　fuzi (bushi; aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　baifan (alum; dried)　hezi (chebule; roasted in embers, stone removed; 1 liang each)　xixin (saishin; asarum; earth and leaves removed; 7½ *qian*)　suan shiliu pi (sour pomegranate rind; pulp removed, soaked overnight in rice vinegar, taken out and roasted to a scorched yellow; 1 liang)

Powder the above and form pills the size of wutong seeds with flour paste boiled in vinegar. Take fifty pills each time on an empty stomach with warm rice water.

## Notes

Da duanxia wan (from here the layer of Chunyou-era additions): for abdominal pain, diarrhea and loss of appetite from cold lodged in the zang organs and weak intestines and stomach.
- Lesser galangal, dried ginger, aconite and asarum warm; red halloysite, dragon bone, oyster shell, dried alum, chebule, nutmeg and pomegranate rind astringe the intestines to stop the diarrhea.
