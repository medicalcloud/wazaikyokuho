# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 073 清心蓮子飲 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qingxin lianzi yin (Heart-Clearing Lotus Seed Beverage)

It treats [heat] stored and accumulated in the heart with constant vexation and agitation, compounded by thought, anxiety and taxing exertion, worry, sorrow and pent-up depression, resulting in white turbid urine, sometimes with sand or membranes, leakage of essence in dreams at night, dribbling with rough, painful urination, and urine red as blood; or, from excess of wine and sex, exuberance above and deficiency below with heart fire flaming upward and lung metal restrained, dry mouth and tongue gradually becoming wasting-thirst, unquiet sleep and weary limbs; the five strangury disorders of men and red and white vaginal discharge of women; and after illness, qi failing to be gathered in, yang floating outside, with vexing heat of the five hearts. The nature of the medicine is warm and balanced, neither cold nor hot; taken regularly it clears the heart and nourishes the spirit, seals essence and supplements deficiency, moistens the intestines and stomach, and regulates and smooths blood and qi.

huangqin (scutellaria)　maimendong (ophiopogon; core removed)　digupi (lycium root bark)　cheqianzi (plantago seed)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried; half a liang each)　shilian rou (lotus fruit flesh; plumule removed)　bai fuling (white poria)　huangqi (astragalus; roasted with honey)　renshen (ginseng; 7½ liang each)

Cut the above into a powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with ten grains of ophiopogon and one and a half *zhan* of water, decoct down to eight-tenths, remove the dregs, cool it by immersion in water and take it on an empty stomach before meals. For fever add bupleurum and mint to the decoction.

## Notes

Qingxin lianzi yin (from here the layer of Baoqing-era additions): for turbid urine, dream emissions, painful urination and haematuria from heat stored in the heart with overthinking, overwork and depression; for dry mouth and wasting-thirst when excess of wine and sex leads to exuberance above and deficiency below with heart fire restraining the lungs; for male strangury, female discharge, and vexing heat of the five hearts with floating yang after illness. Still used today for frequent, painful or incomplete urination with dual deficiency of qi and yin.
- Lotus fruit, poria, astragalus and ginseng supplement qi; scutellaria, ophiopogon, lycium root bark and plantago seed clear heart fire; licorice is added. Taken cooled.
