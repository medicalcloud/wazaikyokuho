# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 029 芎藭湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xiongqiong tang (Sichuan Lovage Decoction)

It treats all cases of heavy blood loss after childbirth with dizziness, oppression and loss of consciousness; as well as heavy blood loss from damage to the fetus, from flooding, from metal wounds, or from tooth extraction that does not stop, with [blood] deficiency, vexation of the heart and dizziness, a heavy head and dim eyes, deafness with a sense of fullness and blockage, and a tendency to fall when lifting the head.

danggui (toki) (Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed, washed, dried over a fire)　xiongqiong (Sichuan lovage; equal parts)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water, decoct down to one *zhan*, remove the dregs and take it fairly hot, at any time.

## Notes

Xiongqiong tang: for dizziness, loss of consciousness, vexation, heavy head, dim vision, blocked ears and fainting on rising after heavy blood loss from childbirth, miscarriage, flooding, wounds or tooth extraction.
- Two ingredients, angelica and lovage (the later Foshou san or Xionggui tang).
