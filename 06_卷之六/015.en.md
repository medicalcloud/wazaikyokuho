# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 015 消毒麻仁丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xiaodu maren wan (Toxin-Dispersing Hemp Seed Pill)

It governs all of the following: the various wind qi congesting upward, long-accumulated heat toxin, phlegm and drool bound and excess, blockage of the chest and diaphragm, and dizziness with spinning vision; or damage from the toxins of wine, flour and roasted or grilled food lodging in the heart and lungs and soaking into the intestines and stomach, pent up and not dispersing, which over time inwardly becomes depressed blood heat with intestinal wind and the five kinds of piles, and outwardly produces sores, abscesses and flat-abscesses, red macules and wandering swellings, dryness and oppression of the whole body, red acne on the face, dry mouth and cracked tongue, rough, painful throat, and middle wasting with drinking; or cold damage and seasonal epidemics with bleeding from the mouth and nose and agitation; as well as wind toxin pouring downward with swollen, painful sores, leg qi surging into the heart with oppression and confusion, and all toxic qi of wind-heat.

xingren (kyonin; apricot kernel; raw, skin and tips removed; 2 liang)　dahuang (daio; rhubarb; raw; 5 liang)　shanzhizi ren (gardenia kernel; 10 liang)

Form these three ingredients into pills with refined honey. Take thirty to fifty pills each time at bedtime, swallowed with warm water; it is effective when red, toxic, glue-like drool is passed. Adjust the dose as one sees fit. This medicine is very mild and good and does not damage the zang organs; taken regularly it searches out wind, smooths qi and resolves toxin. For children's fright heat, dissolve three to five pills in honey water; extremely effective.

## Notes

Xiaodu maren wan (Baoqing-era additions): for phlegm, chest blockage and dizziness from upward congestion of wind qi and accumulated heat toxin; for intestinal wind, piles, sores, abscesses, macules, wandering swellings, facial acne, dry mouth, sore throat and middle wasting from the toxins of wine and roasted food; for bleeding and agitation in seasonal epidemics; for sores from wind toxin pouring downward; for leg qi surging into the heart; and for children's fright heat.
- Three ingredients: rhubarb and gardenia purge heat toxin, apricot kernel moistens. Although named after hemp seed, the formula in the base text contains no hemp seed.
