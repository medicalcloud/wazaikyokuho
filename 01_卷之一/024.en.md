# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 024 防風丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Fangfeng wan (Saposhnikovia Pill)

It treats all wind, and phlegm-heat attacking upward, with headache and nausea, stiffness of the neck and back, dizzy, spinning eyes, palpitation with vexation, weakness of the hands and feet, painful, numb joints, halting, sluggish speech, twitching of the mouth and eyes, abstraction of thought, congested phlegm and drool, clouding and forgetfulness, and deficiency vexation with little sleep.

fangfeng (saposhnikovia; washed)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage)　tianma (gastrodia; sprouts removed, soaked overnight in wine)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried; 2 liang each)　zhusha (cinnabar; ground, for coating; half a liang)

Powder the above and form pills with refined honey, ten pills to each liang, coated with cinnabar. Take one pill each time dissolved in schizonepeta decoction; it may also be chewed and taken with tea or wine. At any time.

## Notes

Fangfeng wan: a simple four-ingredient formula for headache, dizziness, neck stiffness, palpitation, speech disorder and insomnia from wind and phlegm-heat.
- Saposhnikovia, Sichuan lovage and gastrodia expel wind, licorice harmonizes, and the cinnabar coating calms the heart.
