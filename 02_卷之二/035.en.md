# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 035 辰砂五苓散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Chensha wuling san (Shinshagoreisan; Cinnabar Five-Ingredient Poria Powder)

It treats cold damage with exterior and interior not yet resolved, headache and fever, oppression of the heart and chest, dry, parched lips and mouth, a clouded, deep mind, wild talk and delirium as if seeing spirits and ghosts; and miasmic malaria with vexation and oppression before the patient has come round.

chensha (cinnabar; ground)　baizhu (byakujutsu; white atractylodes; rhizome head removed)　mu zhuling (chorei; polyporus; black skin removed)　zexie (takusha; alisma; washed and chopped)　chi fuling (bukuryo; red poria; skin removed; 12 liang each)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed; 8 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder. Take 2 *qian* each time stirred into boiling water, at any time. For summer heat stroke with thirst and red, rough urination, mix it in freshly drawn water. For children with vexing heat of the five hearts, fretfulness and much crying, gnashing of teeth and eyes rolling upward as if about to have fright [wind], take half a *qian* each time mixed in warm boiled water.

## Notes

Chensha wuling san (from here the layer of Baoqing-era additions): for unresolved cold damage with fever, oppression, parched lips, clouded mind and delirium, for miasmic malaria, summer heat thirst, and children's vexing heat with threatened fright wind.
- Wuling san (Goreisan) (juan 2, no. 017) with cinnabar added to calm the heart-spirit.
