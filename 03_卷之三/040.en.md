# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 040 小烏沈湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xiao wuchen tang (Minor Lindera and Aquilaria Decoction)

It regulates the middle and eases qi, and treats stabbing pain of the heart and abdomen.

wuyao (lindera; core removed; 10 liang)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; stir-fried; 1 liang)　xiangfuzi (cyperus; calcined in a sand basin to remove skin and hairs, dried over a fire; 20 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder. Take 1 *qian* each time with or without a little salt, stirred into boiling water, at any time.

## Notes

Xiao wuchen tang (Shaoxing-era additions): regulates the middle and moves qi, for stabbing heart and abdominal pain.
- Lindera, cyperus and licorice; it uses cyperus in place of the aquilaria and ginseng of the earlier Wuchen tang (juan 3, no. 014).
