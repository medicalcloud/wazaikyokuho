# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 019 論纏喉風証 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On the pattern of throat-entwining wind. When accumulated heat and phlegm and drool attack the throat upward, so that the mouth cannot open and water and liquids cannot go down, give Bixue, blown into the throat through a bamboo tube. For severe cases, give Xionghuang jiedu wan or Yuxie wuyou san.

On the signs of cold damage.

## Notes

On throat-entwining wind: when accumulated heat and phlegm attack the throat so that the mouth will not open and nothing can be swallowed, Bixue is blown in through a bamboo tube; for severe cases Xionghuang jiedu wan (Jiedu xionghuang wan) and Yuxie wuyou san.
- The final line "On the signs of cold damage" is the heading of the following discussions of cold damage (from no. 020), placed here as body text in the base text (kept as is).
