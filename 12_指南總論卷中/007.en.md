# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 007 論諸風大便不通 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On constipation in the various winds. It always arises from stagnant heat of pathogenic wind drying the fluids of the intestines and stomach, causing rough constipation. Give Maren wan (Maningan; Hemp Seed Pill) or Sanhe san (Sanwasan; Three Harmonies Powder). If the bowels do not move, give Zaojia wan (Sokyogan; Gleditsia Pill) at once, stopping as soon as they move; it must not be taken long. For those with qi deficiency and the elderly, Zaojia wan must not be given; give only Maren wan, Sanhe san, or Siwu tang (Shimotsuto) with an equal amount of green tangerine peel (white removed) added and decocted together. For severe constipation, Shengong wan may be given, but not to the deficient or elderly.

## Notes

On constipation in the various winds (from stagnant wind-heat drying the intestinal fluids): Maren wan and Sanhe san; if ineffective, Zaojia wan, stopped once the bowels move and not for the deficient or elderly; for the deficient and elderly, Maren wan, Sanhe san or Siwu tang with green tangerine peel; for severe cases Shengong wan (not for the deficient or elderly).
