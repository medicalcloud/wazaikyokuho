# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 075 人参養栄湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Renshen yangying tang (Ninjinyoeito; Ginseng Nutrition-Nourishing Decoction)

It treats deficiency and damage from accumulated taxation with heavy, sluggish limbs, aching pain of the bones and flesh, feeble breathing and scant qi, dyspnea and breathlessness on movement, cramping of the lower abdomen, stiff, painful lower back and spine, heart deficiency with fright palpitations, dry throat and parched lips, tasteless food, declining, weak yin and yang, sorrow, worry and grief, and much lying down and little getting up. In long-standing cases it lasts for years, in acute ones a hundred days, gradually wasting away until the qi of the five zang organs is exhausted and recovery is difficult. It also treats dual deficiency of the lungs and large intestine with cough and diarrhea, dyspnea exhaustion and scant qi, and vomiting of phlegm and drool.

bai shaoyao (shakuyaku; white peony root; 3 liang)　danggui (toki) (Chinese angelica)　chenpi (chinpi; aged tangerine peel)　huangqi (ogi; astragalus)　guixin (cinnamon heart; coarse bark removed)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng)　baizhu (byakujutsu; white atractylodes; roasted in embers)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried; 1 liang each)　shu dihuang (prepared rehmannia; processed)　wuweizi (gomishi; schisandra)　fuling (bukuryo; poria; 7½ *qian* each)　yuanzhi (onji; polygala; stir-fried, core removed; half a liang)

Cut the above into a powder. For each dose take 4 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water, three slices of fresh ginger and two jujubes, decoct down to seven-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm. For leakage of essence, add 1 liang of dragon bone; for cough, adding donkey-hide gelatin is excellent.

## Notes

Renshen yangying tang (from here the layer of Chunyou-era additions): for heavy limbs, aching bones and flesh, breathlessness, lower abdominal cramping, back pain, fright palpitations, dry throat and lips, poor appetite, low mood and a tendency to stay in bed from accumulated taxation, and for cough, diarrhea, dyspnea and vomiting of phlegm from dual deficiency of the lungs and large intestine. Still used today for weakness after illness or surgery and for chronic respiratory disease with dual deficiency of qi and blood.
- Shiquan dabu tang (Juzentaihoto) (juan 5, no. 041) without lovage, plus tangerine peel, schisandra and polygala, with a large amount of white peony.
