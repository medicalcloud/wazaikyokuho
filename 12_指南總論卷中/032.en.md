# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 032 論傷寒頭痛 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On headache in cold damage. When cold damage has subsided but headache does not stop, [the head being] where all the yang gather, the qi of heat toxin is attacking upward; for unceasing headache give Longnao xiongxi wan, Taiyang dan, Bailong wan, Chuanxiong wan (Senkyugan) and the like.

## Notes

On headache persisting after cold damage (heat toxin attacking the head, where the yang gather): Longnao xiongxi wan, Taiyang dan, Bailong wan and Chuanxiong wan.
