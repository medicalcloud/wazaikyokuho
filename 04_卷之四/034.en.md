# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之四 034 定喘瑞応丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Dingchuan ruiying dan (Dyspnea-Settling Auspicious Response Elixir)

It specifically treats men and women with long-standing cough, dyspnea and hurried lung qi, breathing leaning [on something] and unable to sleep lying down, unhealed for years, gradually with puffiness of the face and eyes.

chantui (cicada slough; washed, earth, legs and wings removed, stir-fried)　xingren (kyonin; apricot kernel; skin and tips removed, stir-fried)　madouling (aristolochia fruit; 2 liang each)　calcined arsenic (6 *qian*)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of mallow seeds with steamed jujube flesh. Take six or seven pills each time at bedtime with cooled clear scallion tea. After taking them, avoid hot things for half a day. (One version uses 6 liang of anemarrhena instead of aristolochia fruit.)

## Notes

Dingchuan ruiying dan (bureau additions): for long-standing cough and dyspnea with inability to lie down and facial puffiness.
- Tiny pills containing calcined arsenic, extremely toxic, with cicada slough, apricot kernel and aristolochia fruit for cough and dyspnea. Not used today.
