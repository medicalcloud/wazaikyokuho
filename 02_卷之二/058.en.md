# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 058 人参順気散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Renshen (ninjin) shunqi san (Ginseng Qi-Smoothing Powder)

The patterns treated and the method of taking it are both the same as for the earlier Renshen shunqi san.

renshen (ginseng)　jiegeng (kikyo; platycodon)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried)　gange (dried pueraria)　baizhu (byakujutsu; white atractylodes)　baizhi (byakushi; angelica dahurica; 1 liang each)　mahuang (mao; ephedra; roots and nodes removed; 1½ liang)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; half a liang)

For the method of taking it, see above.

## Notes

Renshen shunqi san (bureau additions): the same indications and administration as the earlier formula of the same name (juan 2, no. 040), but a different formula with fewer ingredients.
