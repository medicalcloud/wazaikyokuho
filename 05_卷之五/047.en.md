# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 047 平補鎮心丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Pingbu zhenxin dan (Balanced Supplementing Heart-Settling Elixir)

The patterns it treats are the same as for the earlier Pingbu zhenxin dan.

shu gan dihuang (kanjio; prepared rehmannia)　sheng gan dihuang (dried raw rehmannia)　dried shanyao (Chinese yam)　tianmendong (tenmondo; asparagus root)　maimendong (bakumondo; ophiopogon; core removed)　baiziren (arborvitae seed)　fushen [poria with root; 4 liang each (one version 7 liang)]　chensha (cinnabar; ground separately, for coating)　kugeng (bitter platycodon; stir-fried; 3 liang each)　shichangpu (acorus; pieces with close-set nodes; 16 liang)　yuanzhi (onji; polygala; core removed, boiled three or four times with licorice; 7 liang)　danggui (toki) (Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed; 6 liang)　longgu (ryukotsu; dragon bone; 1 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with refined honey. Take thirty pills each time, swallowed on an empty stomach with rice water; warm wine is also fine; gradually increase to fifty. It should be taken regularly: it augments essence and marrow, nourishes qi and blood, sharpens sight and hearing, and brightens and preserves the complexion.

## Notes

Pingbu zhenxin dan (bureau additions): the same indications as the earlier Pingbu zhenxin dan (juan 5, no. 025), but a different formula containing raw rehmannia, platycodon, arborvitae seed, acorus and angelica; this is the "*Ju fang*" version compared in Liu Huo'an's comment in juan 5, no. 025.
