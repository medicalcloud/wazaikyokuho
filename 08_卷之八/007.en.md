# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 007 烏梅丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wumei wan (Mume Pill)

It governs visceral cold with roundworms stirring and rising into the diaphragm, vexation, oppression and vomiting, now starting and now stopping, vomiting as soon as one eats, and often spontaneously vomiting roundworms; these signs are called roundworm reversal. It also treats long-standing dysentery.

wumei (mume fruit; 300)　huangbo (phellodendron; roasted)　xixin (asarum; shoots removed)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　fuzi (aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　renshen (ginseng; rhizome head removed; 6 liang each)　shujiao (Sichuan pepper; seeds and closed fruits removed, lightly stir-fried to sweat out the oil)　danggui (Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed; 4 liang each)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried; 10 liang)　huanglian (coptis; fibrous roots removed; 16 liang)

Pound and sift the above separately and then combine. Soak the mume in vinegar overnight, remove the stones, steam under 5 *dou* of rice; when the rice is cooked, pound to a paste, combine with the medicines until well blended, put into a mortar with refined honey and pound two thousand times, and form pills the size of wutong seeds. Take fifteen pills each time with warm rice water, before meals.

## Notes

Wumei wan: for "roundworm reversal"—vexation, oppression and vomiting as roundworms rise into the diaphragm because of visceral cold, vomiting on eating and vomiting of worms—and for long-standing dysentery.
- The same composition and preparation as the *Shanghan lun* Wumei wan (mume, asarum, dried ginger, coptis, aconite, angelica, phellodendron, cinnamon, ginseng, Sichuan pepper).
