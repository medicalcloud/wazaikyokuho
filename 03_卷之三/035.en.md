# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 035 四柱散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Sizhu san (Four Pillars Powder)

It treats men with deficiency of the qi of the original viscus, depleted and failing true yang, constant ringing in both ears, cold pain of the navel and abdomen, spinning head and dizzy eyes, weary limbs, slippery frequent urination and unceasing diarrhoea. All with weak visceral qi should take it.

muxiang (costus root; wrapped in wet paper and roasted)　fuling (poria)　renshen (ginseng)　fuzi (aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed; 1 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one large *zhan* of water, two slices of ginger, one jujube and a little salt, decoct down to seven-tenths and take it on an empty stomach before meals.

## Notes

Sizhu san (Shaoxing-era additions): for tinnitus, cold umbilical and abdominal pain, dizziness, lassitude, frequent urination and diarrhoea from deficient qi of the original viscus (kidney) and declining true yang.
- Four ingredients ("four pillars"): costus, poria, ginseng and aconite.
