# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 072 遇仙立効散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yuxian lixiao san (Immortal-Encounter Immediate-Effect Powder)

It treats the various malign dysenteries, red or white, or thick and thin mixed, with tenesmus, wringing pain of the navel and abdomen, or passing five colors, or like fish brains, without limit day and night, or food-refusing with inability to eat. Regardless of whether adults, children, the weak, the elderly or women after childbirth, all should take it.

yumi qiao (poppy husk; stalk and cap removed, stir-fried yellow)　chuan danggui (toki; Sichuan angelica; washed)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; 2 liang each)　chi shaoyao (shakuyaku; red peony root)　suan liupi (sour pomegranate rind)　diyu (chiyu; sanguisorba; half a liang each)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water, decoct down to seven-tenths and take it warm on an empty stomach. For children adjust the dose according to age. Continue until healed. Avoid raw, cold, greasy and rank food and the like.

## Notes

Yuxian lixiao san (Wu Zhige's additions): for malign dysentery with red, white, five-colored or fish-brain stools, tenesmus, wringing umbilical pain, frequent stools day and night and food-refusing dysentery, regardless of age or constitution.
- Poppy husk and pomegranate rind astringe; sanguisorba stops bleeding; angelica and red peony harmonize the blood; licorice is added.
