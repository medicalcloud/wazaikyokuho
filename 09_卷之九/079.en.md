# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 079 交感地黄煎丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jiaogan dihuang jian wan (Mutual-Response Rehmannia Syrup Pill)

It treats, in women before and after childbirth, all of the following: black flowers seen before the eyes, or sudden mania as if seeing ghosts; retained placenta; loss of voice and inability to speak; distension and fullness of the heart and abdomen; undigested water and grain; dry mouth with vexing thirst; alternating chills and fever; sores in the mouth; swollen, painful throat; heart deficiency with fearful palpitation and sleeplessness at night; postpartum wind-strike with arched-back rigidity, red face and clenched jaw; flooding with bleeding like pig's liver; gripping pain of the navel and abdomen; blood too heavy or too scant binding into conglomerations; abstraction and stupor; swollen, full limbs; restlessness of the foetus before childbirth; and stabbing blood pain after childbirth.

sheng dihuang (raw rehmannia; washed clean, ground, juice squeezed out through cloth and the residue kept; stir-fry the rehmannia residue with the ginger juice and the ginger residue with the rehmannia juice, each until dry enough to powder)　shengjiang (fresh ginger; washed clean, ground to a pulp, juice squeezed out through cloth and the residue kept; 2 jin each)　yanhusuo (corydalis; mixed with glutinous rice, stir-fried red, rice removed)　danggui (Chinese angelica; shoots removed)　hupo (amber; ground separately; 1 liang each)　puhuang (typha pollen; stir-fried until fragrant; 4 liang)

Powder the above and form pills the size of a pellet with honey. Take one pill dissolved in angelica decoction, before meals.

## Notes

Jiaogan dihuang jian wan (bureau additions): for the many disorders before and after childbirth (black spots before the eyes, mania, hallucinations, retained placenta, loss of voice, abdominal fullness, indigestion, thirst, chills and fever, mouth sores, sore throat, palpitation, insomnia, postpartum convulsions, flooding like pig's liver, umbilical pain, conglomerations, abstraction, swollen limbs, restless foetus, postpartum stabbing pain).
- Raw rehmannia and fresh ginger, each residue stir-fried with the other's juice ("mutual response"), plus corydalis, angelica, amber and typha pollen.
