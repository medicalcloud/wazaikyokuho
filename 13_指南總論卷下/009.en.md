# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷下 009 論心脾腹痛 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On heart-spleen and abdominal pain. Heart-spleen and abdominal pain mostly involves accumulation. For cold accumulation, give Wenbai wan or Bao'an wan. The weak and elderly must not take much of these two medicines; give only Muxiang (mokko) tuiqi wan, or Ganying wan, Xiao dusheng wan or Litong wan to wash and disperse the accumulation, then give Zhengqi san, Jiahe san, Caopi san, Renshen (ninjin) zhusan, Bichengqie san, Pancong san, Yanjian san, Jishexiang san (Keizetsukosan), Zhuapi san or Jianzhong san. For cold, give Da chenxiang wan (Taijinkogan), two pills as one dose, then Pengjian wan, Wenzhong liangjiang wan, Dingxiang zhusan or Sizhu san (Shichusan).

## Notes

On heart-spleen and abdominal pain (mostly accumulations): Wenbai wan or Bao'an wan for cold accumulation (for the weak and elderly instead Muxiang tuiqi wan, Ganying wan, Xiao dusheng wan or Litong wan), followed by Zhengqi san, Jiahe san and others; Da chenxiang wan for cold, then Pengjian wan, Wenzhong liangjiang wan, Dingxiang zhusan or Sizhu san.
