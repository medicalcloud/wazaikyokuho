# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 004 柴胡石膏散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Chaihu shigao san (Bupleurum and Gypsum Powder)

It treats seasonal pestilence with vigorous fever and aversion to wind, headache and body pain, nasal congestion and dry throat, a feeling of fullness in the heart and chest, alternating chills and fever, phlegm-repletion cough and thick sticky mucus and spittle.

chi shaoyao (red peony root)　chaihu (bupleurum; sprouts removed)　qianhu (peucedanum; sprouts removed)　shigao (gypsum; calcined)　gange (dried pueraria; 50 liang each)　shengma (cimicifuga; 25 liang)　huangqin (scutellaria)　sangbaipi (mulberry root bark; 37½ liang each)　jingjie spikes (schizonepeta; earth removed; 37 liang)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one *zhan* of water, three slices of fresh ginger and a dozen or so fermented soybeans, decoct together down to seven-tenths, remove the dregs and take it fairly hot. Children divide it into three doses, adjusting further according to size. At any time.

## Notes

Chaihu shigao san: for seasonal pestilence with vigorous fever, aversion to wind, headache, body pain, alternating chills and fever and productive cough.
- Bupleurum, peucedanum, pueraria, cimicifuga and schizonepeta release the exterior; gypsum, scutellaria and mulberry bark clear lung and stomach heat; red peony cools the blood. It is decocted with fermented soybeans.
