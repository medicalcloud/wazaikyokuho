# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷下 017 論沈寒痼冷 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On deep-lying cold and chronic chill. For all cases of deficient, damaged original qi with cold below and exuberance above, water and fire not interacting, yin and yang out of order and reversal cold of the hands and feet, as well as yin patterns of cold damage, sudden turmoil with cramps, and dysentery and long-standing diarrhoea with a sunken, faint pulse, give Heixi dan, Laifu dan, Jinye dan, Fuzi lizhong wan, Jinsuo zhengyuan dan or Sishen dan.

## Notes

On deep-lying cold and chronic chill (deficient original qi, cold below and exuberance above, cold extremities, yin-pattern cold damage, cramps of sudden turmoil, chronic diarrhoea with a faint sunken pulse): Heixi dan, Laifu dan, Jinye dan, Fuzi lizhong wan, Jinsuo zhengyuan dan and Sishen dan.
