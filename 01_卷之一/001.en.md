# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 001 至宝丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zhibao dan (Supreme Jewel Elixir)

It treats sudden wind-strike with loss of speech, malign-qi strike with cessation of breath, poisoning by various substances and dark wind, heat-epidemic toxin, yin and yang toxins, toxic miasmic qi of mountain mists, gu toxin and water toxin, postpartum blood dizziness, bleeding from the mouth and nose, malign blood attacking the heart, vexation and agitation with panting, vomiting, difficult labour with oppression and distress (one version reads 乱, "chaos"), and a dead fetus that will not come down. For all the above illnesses, warm one *ge* of a boy's urine with three to five drops of fresh ginger juice and dissolve three to five pills in it; the effect is divine. It also treats accumulated heat in the heart and lungs, vomiting from latent heat, evil qi attacking the heart, wind constipation of the large intestine, abstraction of the spirit, dizziness of the head and eyes, restless sleep, dryness of the lips and mouth, and delirious speech in cold damage; it treats all of these.

raw rhinoceros horn shavings (ground)　zhusha (cinnabar; ground and water-ground)　xionghuang (realgar; ground and water-ground)　raw hawksbill shell shavings (ground)　hupo (amber; ground; 1 liang each)　shexiang (musk; ground)　longnao (borneol; ground; 1 *fen* each)　gold leaf (half put into the medicine, half used as coating)　silver leaf (ground; 50 leaves each)　niuhuang (bezoar; ground; half a liang)　anxixiang (benzoin; 1½ liang, powdered, stirred in ash-free wine, settled and levigated, the sand and earth strained off to give about 1 liang net, simmered over a gentle fire into a paste). Grind the raw rhinoceros horn and hawksbill shell to a fine powder, add the remaining medicines and grind them evenly; heat the benzoin paste in a double boiler until it sets, add it to the medicines and knead into a mass; store in an airtight container and form pills the size of tung seeds as needed. Dissolve three to five pills in ginseng decoction. It also treats children's epilepsies, acute fright and heart heat, sudden visiting-hostility (kewu) with inability to sleep, vexation and agitation, and wind-drool with convulsions: a child of two takes two pills dissolved in ginseng decoction.

## Notes

Zhibao dan: a representative orifice-opening formula for acute loss of consciousness and speech due to wind-strike, malign-qi strike or poisoning.
- It combines aromatic, heat-clearing, orifice-opening substances such as rhinoceros horn, hawksbill shell, musk, borneol, bezoar and benzoin; it was later counted among the "three treasures for cool opening" (with Angong niuhuang wan and Zixue).
- The method of preparation and administration follows directly at the end of the list of ingredients, as in the base text.
- The Siku quanshu edition orders the ingredients differently and words the processing differently (e.g. "ground" vs "water-ground"; see the variants).
