# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 012 論諸風頭痛目暈 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On headache and dizziness in the various winds. They always arise from wind-deficiency qi attacking the head and eyes upward. Give Taiyang dan, Bailong wan, Chatiao san (Tea-Mixed Powder) or Chuanxiong wan (Senkyugan). For pain at the temples, apply Jifeng san to the painful place. For dim vision with headache, give Xiaofeng san (Shofusan) or Zhuifeng san. For phlegm-heat headache, give Fangfeng wan (Bofugan). For the elderly and weak with wind-cold entering the brain, headache and dizziness, give Zhufu tang, Qianghuo wan or Sanwuqi san. For hemilateral and frontal head wind with pain at both temples and the eyebrow ridges drawing into both eyes with dimness, give Yuxian san.

## Notes

On headache and dizziness in the various winds (wind-deficiency qi attacking the head): Taiyang dan, Bailong wan, Chatiao san and Chuanxiong wan; Jifeng san applied for temple pain; Xiaofeng san and Zhuifeng san for dim vision with headache; Fangfeng wan for phlegm-heat headache; Zhufu tang, Qianghuo wan and Sanwuqi san for wind-cold headache in the elderly and weak; Yuxian san for head wind with temple and brow pain and dim vision.
