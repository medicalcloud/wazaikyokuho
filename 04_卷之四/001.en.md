# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之四 001 倍術丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Beizhu wan (Doubled Atractylodes Pill)

It treats the five rheums and wine aggregation. The first is called lodged rheum: water lodged below the heart. The second is called flank rheum: water collected below both flanks. The third is called phlegm-rheum: water in the stomach. The fourth is called spillage rheum: water overflowing above the diaphragm and among the five viscera. The fifth is called flowing rheum: water in the intestines that sloshes with a sound when one moves. All are caused by drinking wine and being exposed to cold, or by drinking too much water. This medicine treats them all.

ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed; half a jin each)　baizhu (white atractylodes; 1 jin)

Pound and sieve these three ingredients and form pills the size of wutong seeds with honey. Take twenty pills each time with warm rice gruel, increasing to thirty, before meals, twice a day.

## Notes

Beizhu wan (the first formula of juan 4, "treating phlegm-rheum"): for the five rheums (lodged, flank, phlegm, spillage, flowing) and wine aggregation.
- White atractylodes is used at double the amount of dried ginger and cinnamon (hence "doubled atractylodes"); it warms and dries water-rheum arising from cold after drinking or from excessive water intake.
