# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之七 026 如聖湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Rusheng tang (Nyoseito; Sage-Like Decoction)

It treats wind-heat toxic qi attacking the throat upward with sore throat and throat impediment, swelling, blockage, obstruction and oppression; and lung abscess with cough, spitting of pus and blood, chest fullness with shivering cold, a dry throat without thirst, occasional turbid froth, a fishy breath, and after a long time vomiting of pus like rice gruel. It also treats sore throat in cold damage.

ku jiegeng (kikyo; bitter platycodon; stir-fried; 1 liang)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; stir-fried; 2 liang)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one *zhan* of water, decoct down to seven-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm; children sip it from time to time; after meals and at bedtime.

## Notes

Rusheng tang: for sore throat, throat impediment and swelling from wind-heat toxin; for cough, pus and blood, chest fullness, chills, fishy breath and gruel-like pus in lung abscess; and for sore throat in cold damage.
- Two ingredients, platycodon and licorice: the same composition as the Jiegeng tang (Kikyoto) of the *Shanghan lun* and *Jingui yaolüe*.
