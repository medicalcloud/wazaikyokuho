# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 043 沈香鼈甲散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Chenxiang biejia san (Jinkobekkosan; Aquilaria and Turtle Shell Powder)

It can treat men and women with the five taxations and seven damages, deficient and damaged qi and blood, stiffness of the lower back and spine, heavy hands and feet, aching pain in all the joints, a black-yellow complexion, weary limbs, dyspnea and exhaustion on movement, discomfort of the chest and diaphragm, cough with phlegm and drool, many strange dreams at night, night sweats and loss of essence, a liking for lying down with little strength, wasted haggard flesh, no desire for food and daily emaciation; all taxation damage and the myriad deficiencies and hundred damages.

ganxie (dried scorpion; 2½ *qian*)　chenxiang (aquilaria; not exposed to fire)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng; rhizome head removed)　muxiang (mokko; costus root; not exposed to fire)　baji (morinda; core removed)　niuxi (goshitsu; achyranthes; rhizome head removed, soaked in wine)　huangqi (ogi; astragalus; rhizome head removed)　bai fuling (bukuryo; white poria; dried over a fire)　chaihu (saiko; bupleurum)　jingjie (schizonepeta; stalks removed)　banxia (hange; pinellia; soaked in ginger juice for two nights, stir-fried)　chuan danggui (toki; Sichuan angelica; rhizome head removed)　qinjiao (large-leaf gentian; rhizome head removed), half a liang each　fuzi (bushi; aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　biejia (bekko; turtle shell; soaked in vinegar, rim removed, roasted yellow; 1 liang each)　qianghuo (notopterygium)　shu gan dihuang (kanjio; prepared rehmannia; washed clean, sprinkled with wine, steamed, dried over a fire; 7½ *qian* each)　roudoukou (nutmeg; 4)

Make the above into a fine powder. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one *zhan* of water, two *cun* of scallion white, three slices of fresh ginger and two jujubes broken open, decoct together down to seven-tenths and [take it] on an empty stomach before meals.

## Notes

Chenxiang biejia san (Wu Zhige's additions): for stiff back, heavy limbs, joint pain, dark complexion, lassitude, breathlessness, cough, nightmares, night sweats, loss of essence, wasting and poor appetite from the five taxations and seven damages, and for all taxation damage and deficiencies.
- Ginseng, astragalus, poria, rehmannia and angelica supplement; aconite, cinnamon, morinda and achyranthes warm the kidneys; turtle shell, bupleurum and gentian clear deficiency heat; aquilaria, costus and nutmeg move qi; scorpion, schizonepeta, notopterygium and pinellia are added.
