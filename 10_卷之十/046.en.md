# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 046 使君子丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shijunzi wan (Shikunshigan; Quisqualis Pill)

It is suitable for the five gan disorders in children with disharmony of the spleen and stomach, swelling distension of the heart and abdomen, recurring gripping pain, inability to eat and gradual emaciation.

houpo (koboku; magnolia bark; bark removed, roasted with ginger juice)　chenpi (chinpi; aged tangerine peel; white pith removed)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage; 1 *fen* each)　shijunzi ren (quisqualis kernel; soaked, black skin removed; 1 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of gleditsia seeds with refined honey. Children three and over take one pill, younger ones half a pill, dissolved in old-rice water. It is especially good for children's abdominal pain.

## Notes

Shijunzi wan (Shaoxing-era additions): for abdominal distension, gripping pain, poor appetite and wasting from the five gan disorders with disharmony of the spleen and stomach; said to be excellent for children's abdominal pain.
- Quisqualis kills worms; magnolia bark, tangerine peel and lovage move qi.
