# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 062 妙応丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Miaoying dan (Marvellously Responsive Elixir)

(Also called Yanling dan, Life-Prolonging Elixir.) It treats the many illnesses of women; there is nothing it does not treat.

wan cansha (late silkworm droppings; stir-fried)　liyu lin (carp scales; burned and powdered)　danggui (toki; Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed)　shigao (sekko; gypsum; calcined, ground)　zelan (takuran; lycopus; stalks removed)　fuzi (bushi; aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　muxiang (mokko; costus root; blast-fried; 2 liang each)　shu gan dihuang (kanjio; prepared rehmannia; washed, soaked in wine, steamed, dried over a fire)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage)　fangfeng (bofu; saposhnikovia; rhizome head and forks removed)　wuyi (elm seed paste; stir-fried)　ma yaxiao (horse-tooth mirabilite; burned)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng)　huangqi (ogi; astragalus)　chuanjiao (Sichuan pepper; lightly stir-fried)　baiziren (arborvitae seed; lightly stir-fried, ground separately)　chantui (cicada slough; legs removed, washed, dried over a fire)　baiwei (byakubi; cynanchum root)　binglang (areca; not exposed to fire; 1 liang each)　houpo (koboku; magnolia bark; coarse bark removed, processed with ginger)　gaoben (kohon; Chinese lovage; shoots removed)　baijiang (haisho; dried ginger; blast-fried)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried until red; 3 liang each)　wuzhuyu (goshuyu; evodia; washed seven times in hot water)　honghua (safflower; stir-fried; half a liang each)

Powder the above, knead with refined honey, pound several thousand times and form pills the size of a pellet. Take one pill each time. For painful blood-conglomeration lumps, with silk-floss-ash wine. To hasten delivery, swallow it finely broken with warm wine. For blood taxation and blood deficiency, with platycodon wine. For blood flooding, with palm-fiber-ash wine. For blood-qi pain, [with] stir-fried dried-ginger wine. For blood wind, with schizonepeta wine. For blood dizziness with fainting, a fetus dead in the abdomen or retained placenta, decoct raw rehmannia juice, a boy's urine and wine, one *zhan* each, through two boils and take it mixed in. For regular use, dissolve it in vinegar decoction or warm wine, always on an empty stomach before meals.

## Notes

Miaoying dan (Yanling dan; bureau additions): for all women's illnesses, with different wines as vehicles for blood conglomerations, hastening labor, blood taxation and deficiency, flooding, blood-qi pain, blood wind, blood dizziness, dead fetus and retained placenta.
- Late silkworm droppings, carp-scale ash, lycopus, safflower and cynanchum move blood; prepared rehmannia, angelica, lovage, ginseng and astragalus supplement; aconite, dried ginger, Sichuan pepper and evodia warm; saposhnikovia, Chinese lovage and cicada slough dispel wind; gypsum, horse-tooth mirabilite, areca, magnolia bark, costus, arborvitae seed, elm seed paste and licorice are added.
- After "stir-fried dried-ginger wine" the word "take" (*xia*) is missing; it is supplied in brackets in the translation.
