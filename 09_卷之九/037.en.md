# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 037 油煎散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Youjian san (Oil-Decoction Powder)

It treats women with blood-wind taxation, a haggard appearance, weary, tired limbs and joints, panting fullness with deficiency vexation, faint breathing and scant qi, fever with much sweating, dry mouth and rough tongue, and no desire for food.

wujiapi (acanthopanax root bark)　mudanpi (moutan bark)　chi shaoyao (red peony root)　danggui (Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed; 1 liang each)

Powder the above. For each dose take 1 *qian* with one *zhan* of water, dip one bronze coin in oil and put it in with the medicine, decoct down to seven-tenths and take it warm. Do not stir while decocting, and do not blow on it while drinking. Three doses a day. Taken regularly it can make women plumper; its effect is most marvellous.

## Notes

Youjian san (Shaoxing-era additions): for haggardness, lassitude, panting, deficiency vexation, breathlessness, fever, sweating, dry mouth and poor appetite from blood-wind taxation in women.
- Four ingredients (acanthopanax, moutan, red peony, angelica) decocted with an oil-dipped bronze coin (hence "oil decoction").
