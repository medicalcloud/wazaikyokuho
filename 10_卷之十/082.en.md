# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 082 鎮心至宝丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zhenxin zhibao dan (Heart-Settling Supreme Jewel Elixir)

It treats all fright wind with convulsions in children, vigorous fever with much drool, a fish-like mouth and crow-like voice, and a fixed stare.

tiannanxing (arisaema; roasted in embers)　bai fuzi (typhonium tuber; blast-fried)　xionghuang (yuo; realgar; ground)　ganxie (dried scorpion; half a liang each)　bai jiangcan (silkworm; silk and mouth removed, stir-fried)　yujin (curcuma tuber; 1 liang each)　longnao (borneol; ground)　shexiang (jako; musk; ground; 2½ *qian* each)　chensha (cinnabar; ground; 1 *fen*)　nifen (calomel; 2 *qian*)　huashi (kasseki; talc; powdered; 2 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder, form pills the size of gleditsia seeds with refined honey and coat with gold and silver leaf. Take one pill each time after meals and at bedtime with mint decoction. Taken regularly it settles the heart-spirit and cools the throat and diaphragm.

## Notes

Zhenxin zhibao dan (bureau additions): for convulsions of fright wind with high fever, much drool, gaping "fish mouth", hoarse "crow voice" and fixed stare in children.
- Arisaema, typhonium, scorpion and silkworm dispel wind and phlegm; borneol, musk, cinnabar, realgar, curcuma and calomel settle and open the orifices; talc is added; coated with gold and silver leaf.
