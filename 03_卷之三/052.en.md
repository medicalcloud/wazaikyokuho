# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 052 温中良薑丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wenzhong liangjiang wan (Middle-Warming Galangal Pill)

It warms the spleen and stomach and smooths the triple burner. It treats cold phlegm gathering and binding so that qi is congested and blocked, vomiting as soon as one eats, choking oppression of the throat and diaphragm, wringing, stabbing pain of both flanks and ribs, vomiting and hiccough, belching of sourness and nausea, fullness of the middle with shortness of breath, and choking with the smell of food on belching; as well as lodged rheum with borborygmus, and damp diarrhoea and cold diarrhoea pouring down without stopping. Taken regularly it strengthens the spleen and stomach, makes food taste good, wards off cold evil and nourishes the upright qi.

gaoliangjiang (lesser galangal; stir-fried; 4 jin)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried)　baizhu (white atractylodes; 2 jin 4 liang each)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed; 28 liang)　gancao (licorice; scorched; 1 jin)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills with refined honey, twelve pills to each liang. Take one pill each time, chewed thoroughly, with ginger and tangerine-peel decoction; rice gruel is also fine; on an empty stomach before meals.

## Notes

Wenzhong liangjiang wan (Shaoxing-era additions): for vomiting, chest obstruction, wringing flank pain, nausea and fullness from bound cold phlegm, and for borborygmus of lodged rheum and damp or cold diarrhoea.
- Galangal, dried ginger and cinnamon warm; atractylodes and licorice supplement the spleen.
