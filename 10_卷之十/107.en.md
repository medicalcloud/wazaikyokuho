# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 107 生薑湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shengjiang tang (Shokyoto; Ginger Drink)

It treats damage by wine and food with vexing fullness of the heart and chest, spitting of sour fluid, unsettled vomiting counterflow, tasteless food and discomfort of the chest and diaphragm.

gan shengjiang (shokyo; dried fresh ginger; 2 jin)　baimian (white flour; stir-fried; 3 jin)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; stir-fried; 13 jin)　xingren (kyonin; apricot kernel; skin and tips removed, stir-fried with bran, ground separately; 10 jin)

Powder the above together with 22 jin of stir-fried salt. Take half a *qian* each time mixed like tea. For regular use one *zi*; it disperses food, transforms phlegm, and loosens and frees the chest and diaphragm; at any time.

## Notes

Shengjiang tang ("Various drinks"): a drink for chest fullness, sour regurgitation, vomiting, poor appetite and chest discomfort after damage by wine and food.
- Dried ginger, white flour, licorice, apricot kernel and salt.
