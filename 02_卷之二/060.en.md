# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 060 三拗湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Sanao tang (Sanyoto; Three Contrary Ingredients Decoction)

It treats colds from pathogenic wind with nasal congestion, a heavy voice and loss of voice; or damage by wind and cold with headache and dizziness, cramped and weary limbs, cough with much phlegm, fullness of the chest and shortness of breath.

gancao (kanzo; licorice; not mix-fried)　mahuang (mao; ephedra; roots and nodes not removed)　xingren (kyonin; apricot kernel; skin and tips not removed)

Take equal parts of the above and cut them into a coarse powder. For each dose take 5 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water and five coin-slices of ginger, decoct together down to one *zhan*, remove the dregs and take it at a comfortable warmth; cover with clothes and bedding and sleep until there is a slight sweat.

## Notes

Sanao tang (bureau additions): for nasal congestion, hoarseness, headache, cough, phlegm, chest fullness and shortness of breath in wind-cold colds.
- Mahuang tang (Maoto) without cinnamon twig: ephedra, apricot kernel and licorice. It is called "three contrary" because each ingredient deliberately goes against the usual processing (licorice not mix-fried, ephedra roots and nodes not removed, apricot skin and tips not removed).
