# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 046 白竜丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bailong wan (White Dragon Pill)

It treats all wind in men and women with sores and tinea all over the body, stubborn numbness of the hands and feet, one-sided and general headache, and nasal congestion with oppression of the head. It greatly resolves cold damage and treats head wind.

gaoben (kohon) (Chinese lovage; earth removed)　xixin (saishin; asarum)　baizhi (byakushi; angelica dahurica)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage)　gancao (kanzo; licorice)

Make the above into a fine powder in equal parts. To 4 liang of the medicine add 1 jin of powdered gypsum (calcined), knead with water into pills, eight to each liang. Chew one pill each time with mint tea, after meals. For wind-caused decayed teeth, divide one pill into three doses: rub it on dry, then rinse the mouth with salt water, then chew [the rest] and take it with scallion tea.

## Notes

Bailong wan (Shaoxing-era additions): for sores and tinea, numbness, headache and nasal congestion from wind, for cold damage and head wind, and for toothache from wind.
- Five ingredients (Chinese lovage, asarum, angelica dahurica, Sichuan lovage, licorice) with four times their weight of calcined gypsum; for toothache it is also rubbed on the teeth.
