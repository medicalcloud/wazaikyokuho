# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 052 三建丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Sanjian dan (Three Strengthening Elixir)

It strengthens original yang and supplements genuine qi. It treats deficiency and damage from taxation with the lower meridians declining and exhausted, kidney qi not secured, loss of essence and urine, spontaneous diarrhea, cold hands and feet, or a pulse thin as a thread, emaciation of the form and flesh, or aversion to the smell of food, hoarseness and loss of voice.

yangqishi (yokiseki; actinolite; calcined red-hot)　fuzi (bushi; aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　zhongru fen (stalactite powder; equal parts)

Make the above into a fine powder, mix evenly and form pills the size of wutong seeds with glutinous rice paste. Take twenty to thirty pills each time, swallowed with rice water before meals. Avoid fermented soybean juice and sheep's blood.

## Notes

Sanjian dan (bureau additions): for loss of essence and urine, diarrhea, cold limbs, a thready pulse, wasting, aversion to food and loss of voice from taxation damage with exhausted lower meridians.
- Three ingredients (actinolite, aconite, stalactite) to strengthen original yang.
