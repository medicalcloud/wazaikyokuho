# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 079 震霊丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zhenling dan (Thunder Spirit Elixir)

(A formula of the Primordial Lady of the Purple Palace, Lady Wei of the Southern Peak, from the *Daozang*; also called Zijin dan, Purple Gold Elixir.) This elixir does not involve metal-and-stone medicines of volatile nature; it is neither overbearing nor drying, and it seizes the harmonizing power of creation. It greatly treats men with declining, exhausted true origin, the five taxations and seven damages, cold pain of the navel and abdomen, aching limbs, exuberance above and deficiency below, dizziness of the head and eyes, an abstracted heart-spirit, and declining, faint blood and qi; as well as paralysis from wind-strike with loss of use of the hands and feet, hypertonicity of the sinews and bones, heaviness of the lower back and knees, a withered appearance and wasted flesh, dim eyes and deafness, a bitter mouth and dry tongue, tasteless food, insufficiency of heart and kidneys, slippery essence and dream emissions, bladder mounting with sagging, small-intestine strangury, much night sweating, long-standing diarrhoea and dysentery, vomiting and inability to eat, the eight winds and five impediments, and all deep-lying cold and chronic chill; taken, it works like magic. It also treats women with insufficient blood and qi, deficiency and damage from flooding and spotting, long-standing cold vaginal discharge and a childless womb; none fail to recover on taking it.

yuyuliang (limonite; calcined, quenched in vinegar any number of times until it crumbles when twisted in the hand)　zishiying (fluorite)　chi shizhi (red halloysite)　dingtou daizheshi (nail-head haematite; prepared like the limonite; 4 liang each)

All four of the above are made into small lumps, put into a crucible, sealed with salt clay, left to dry, calcined red-hot with 10 jin of charcoal until the fire burns out, and buried in a pit in the ground for two nights to draw out the fire toxin.

di ruxiang (drop frankincense; ground separately)　wulingzhi (flying squirrel droppings; sand and stones removed, ground)　moyao (myrrh; sand and stones removed, ground; 2 liang each)　zhusha (cinnabar; water-ground; 1 liang)

Combine all eight ingredients of the above two groups into a fine powder, form pills the size of small euryale seeds with paste boiled from glutinous rice flour, and dry them in the sun until they shine. Take one pill each time on an empty stomach with warm wine; cold water is also fine. Taken regularly it settles the heart-spirit, preserves the complexion, warms the spleen and kidneys, regulates the lower back and knees, removes corpse infixation and gu toxin, and wards off ghosts, goblins and evil pestilence. Taken for a long time it lightens the body and gradually leads into the way of the immortals. Avoid pig's and sheep's blood, which may reduce the force of the medicine. Women take it with vinegar decoction. Pregnant women must not take it. Its divine efficacy is extreme and cannot be fully described.

## Notes

Zhenling dan (Zijin dan; from here the layer of Wu Zhige's additions): attributed to Lady Wei in the *Daozang*; for declining true origin, the five taxations and seven damages, umbilical cold pain, exuberance above and deficiency below, dizziness, paralysis from wind-strike, hypertonicity, seminal emission, mounting, strangury, night sweats, chronic diarrhoea and dysentery and deep-lying cold in men, and for insufficient blood and qi, flooding, discharge and infertility in women.
- Four minerals (limonite, fluorite, red halloysite, haematite) calcined and freed of fire toxin, plus frankincense, flying squirrel droppings, myrrh and cinnabar. The text stresses that it avoids "volatile" metal-and-stone drugs (mercury, sulphur and the like).
