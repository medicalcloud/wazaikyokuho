# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 089 小駐車丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xiao zhuche wan (Shochushagan; Minor Cart-Halting Pill)

It is suitable for all diarrhea and dysentery in children from disharmony of cold and heat or irregular nursing, with unceasing diarrhea, or dysentery of fresh blood, or more red than white, abdominal pain and tenesmus, deficient, slippery intestines and stomach, very frequent stools, reduced eating and fatigue.

danggui (toki) (Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed; 2 liang)　hezi (chebule; blast-fried, stone removed; 1 liang)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried)　huanglian (oren; coptis; fibrous roots removed; 3 *fen* each)

Make the above into a fine powder, knead with the liquid of 1 liang 3 *fen* of donkey-hide gelatin boiled in water, and form pills the size of millet grains. For each year of age give ten to twenty or thirty pills with warm rice water, or with milk; adjust further for age.

## Notes

Xiao zhuche wan (bureau additions): for unceasing diarrhea, dysentery of fresh blood or more red than white, abdominal pain, tenesmus, slippery intestines, frequent stools, poor appetite and fatigue from disharmony of cold and heat or irregular feeding in children.
- The Zhuche wan (Chushagan) of juan 6, no. 028 (donkey-hide gelatin, angelica, coptis, dried ginger) plus chebule, adapted for children.
