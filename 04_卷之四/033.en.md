# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之四 033 人参潤肺丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Renshen runfei wan (Ginseng Lung-Moistening Pill)

It treats insufficient lung qi with cough and urgent dyspnea, phlegm and drool that will not clear, vexation and oppression of the chest and diaphragm, thick sticky mucus and spittle, and dry lips and mouth. It also treats wind congestion with phlegm excess, clouding and dizziness of the head and eyes and an unclear mind; or deficiency of both lungs and stomach with long-standing cough that will not stop, gradually becoming deficiency taxation, with emaciated limbs, chest fullness with shortness of breath, dyspnea fatigue on movement and reduced eating; and all kinds of cough, of many years or recent.

renshen (ginseng)　kuandonghua (kantoka; coltsfoot flower; stalks removed)　xixin (saishin; asarum; leaves removed, washed)　xingren (kyonin; apricot kernel; skin and tips removed, stir-fried with bran)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; scorched; 4 liang each)　zhimu (chimo; anemarrhena; 6 liang)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　jiegeng (kikyo; platycodon; 5 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of a euryale seed with refined honey. Chew one pill thoroughly each time after meals and wash it down with weak ginger decoction; it may also be dissolved in the mouth.

## Notes

Renshen runfei wan (from here the layer of bureau additions): for cough, dyspnea, phlegm, chest oppression, sticky sputum and dry lips from insufficient lung qi, dizziness from wind-phlegm, chronic cough progressing to deficiency taxation, and all coughs old or new.
- Ginseng and licorice supplement; coltsfoot, apricot kernel and platycodon treat cough and phlegm; anemarrhena moistens and clears; asarum and cinnamon warm.
