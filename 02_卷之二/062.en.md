# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 062 香薷湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xiangru tang (Elsholtzia Decoction)

It loosens the middle, harmonizes qi and regulates the nutritive and defensive qi. It treats irregular eating and drinking, hunger and satiety at the wrong times, or too many cold things, or hard foods obstructing and lodging, or sleeping right after eating, or fright, worry, resentment and anger, or wanting to eat right after exertion has stirred the qi, so that the spleen and stomach are in disharmony and the three ducts are blocked and stagnant; internal contraction of wind-cold with external reception of cold evil, with aversion to cold and vigorous fever, pain all over, fullness and oppression of the chest and diaphragm, sudden turmoil with vomiting, spleen pain and stomach reflux; drunkenness without sobering; and headache in seasonal cold damage, for which three doses are given in succession and recovery follows once sweat is obtained. Taken regularly it benefits the spleen and warms the stomach, disperses old phlegm and lodged rheum, improves eating, and wards off the qi of wind, cold, summerheat, damp, fog and dew.

bai biandou (hyacinth bean; stir-fried)　fushen (poria with root)　houpo (magnolia bark; coarse bark removed, chopped, stir-fried with ginger juice; 1 liang each)　xiangru (elsholtzia; earth removed; 2 liang)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried; half a liang)

Make the above into a fine powder. Take 2 *qian* each time stirred into boiling water; it may also be taken with salt added. At any time.

## Notes

Xiangru tang (bureau additions): for spleen-stomach disharmony and blocked ducts from irregular diet etc., chills and fever, body pain, sudden turmoil and stomach reflux from internal and external cold, hangover, and headache in seasonal cold damage.
- Xiangru san (juan 2, no. 022) with poria with root and licorice added, taken stirred into hot water.
