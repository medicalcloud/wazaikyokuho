# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 019 勝金丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shengjin wan (Gold-Surpassing Pill)

It treats all kinds of malaria with attacks at set times. These arise from external pathogen lodging in the zang-fu organs and raw and cold food damaging the spleen and stomach internally; sometimes cold first and then heat, sometimes heat first and then cold, sometimes more cold than heat, sometimes less cold than heat, sometimes heat only without cold, sometimes cold only without heat, sometimes attacks on consecutive days, sometimes every other day, sometimes another attack three to five days after one. In the cold stage the limbs tremble; in the hot stage the whole body is like fire, with headache and nausea, vexing thirst with drinking, dyspnea and hurried breath, a bitter mouth and dry throat, aching pain of the spine, borborygmus and abdominal pain, or phlegm gathered in the chest with vexing fullness and nausea. It treats them all.

binglang (areca; 4 liang)　changshan (dichroa root; soaked in wine, steamed, dried over a fire; 1 jin)

Powder the above and form pills the size of wutong seeds with flour paste made with water. On the evening before the attack, at bedtime, swallow thirty pills with cold wine and go to sleep at once. Nothing hot, tea or hot water may be taken. At the end of the fourth watch (1–3 a.m.), swallow another fifteen pills with cold wine. Avoid all hot soups, broths and gruel; at noon warm gruel may be eaten, and only in the evening may hot food be eaten. Avoid all raw, cold and fishy things. One formula uses 16 liang of Sichuan dichroa root powdered and made into pills with the whites of fifteen hen's eggs; the patterns treated and the manner of taking are entirely as above.

## Notes

Shengjin wan (Baoqing-era additions): for all types of malaria (any order or proportion of cold and heat; daily, alternate-day or periodic attacks; shivering, high fever, headache, nausea, thirst, dyspnea, back pain, abdominal pain, phlegm in the chest).
- Two ingredients, dichroa root and areca, taken with cold wine on the night before the attack and at the fourth watch, avoiding hot things. A variant makes the pills of dichroa with egg white.
