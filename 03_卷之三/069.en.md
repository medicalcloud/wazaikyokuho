# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 069 治中湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zhizhong tang (Middle-Governing Decoction)

It is suitable for disharmony of the spleen and stomach with reduced eating, shortness of breath, deficiency and emaciation, with vomiting counterflow besides; vomiting and diarrhoea of sudden turmoil, chest impediment with heart pain, counterflow qi and shortness of breath, fullness of the middle with deficiency glomus, and separation and obstruction; or cold in the chest after recovery from a major illness with occasional coughing and spitting.

renshen (ginseng)　gancao (licorice; stir-fried)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried)　baizhu (white atractylodes; chopped)　qingpi (green tangerine peel; stir-fried)　chenpi (aged tangerine peel; washed, white pith removed; 1 liang each)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water, decoct down to one medium *zhan*, remove the dregs and take it fairly hot, on an empty stomach before meals. It is especially suitable for qi deficiency after sudden turmoil when hot medicines have not yet been forbidden.

## Notes

Zhizhong tang (Baoqing-era additions): Lizhong tang (ginseng, atractylodes, dried ginger, licorice) plus green and aged tangerine peel, for spleen-stomach disharmony, poor appetite, vomiting, sudden turmoil, chest impediment and glomus, and for cough and spitting from cold in the chest after a major illness.
