# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 053 神効当帰膏 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shenxiao danggui gao (Divinely Effective Angelica Paste)

It treats scalds and burns with blisters at the outset, heat toxin spreading, red, hot pain, congested, exuberant toxic qi, and rotting into pus. It closes the openings of sores, generates flesh, draws out heat toxin and stops pain.

danggui (toki) (Chinese angelica)　huangla (yellow beeswax; 1 liang each)　mayou (sesame oil; 4 liang)

First decoct the angelica in the oil until it is scorched black and remove the dregs; then add the wax and stir quickly, let it cool and put it in a porcelain box. Each time spread it on a piece of old cloth and apply.

## Notes

Shenxiao danggui gao (Wu Zhige's additions): an ointment for scalds and burns with blisters, spreading heat toxin, red pain and suppuration; it closes the wound and generates flesh.
- Three ingredients: angelica boiled in sesame oil until scorched, set with yellow wax.
