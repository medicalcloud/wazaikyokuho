# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 045 小七香丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xiao qixiang wan (Minor Seven Fragrances Pill)

It can warm the middle and ease the diaphragm, transform accumulations and harmonize qi. It treats drunkenness with vomiting of wine, retching and acid swallowing, qi diaphragm and food choking with inability to get food down, cold-drool stomach reflux, abdominal distension and spleen pain, years-old accumulations of tea, wine and food with yellowing of both eyes and eyelids, red and white dysentery, and diarrhea from spleen toxin. It is also suitable for women's spleen blood qi and children's gan qi.

gansong (nardostachys; stir-fried; 80 liang)　yizhi ren (alpinia oxyphylla; stir-fried; 60 liang)　xiangfuzi (cyperus; stir-fried, hairs removed)　dingxiangpi (clove bark)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; stir-fried; 120 liang each)　peng'ezhu (zedoary; roasted, crushed while hot)　suosharen (amomum; 20 liang each)

Powder the above and form pills the size of mung beans with steamed bread soaked in water. Take twenty pills each time with warm wine, ginger decoction or boiled water as preferred. For distension and fullness of qi, take them with a decoction of water in which lindera has been ground. For excess of wine and food with dizziness, nausea and chest fullness and oppression, first chew twenty pills, then swallow twenty more with ginger and perilla decoction. This medicine is warm and mild in nature and does not stir the zang-fu organs.

## Notes

Xiao qixiang wan (Shaoxing-era additions): for vomiting after drinking, acid reflux, diaphragm qi, stomach reflux, abdominal distension, jaundice-like symptoms from years of accumulated tea, wine and food, dysentery and diarrhea, women's blood qi and children's gan qi.
- Nardostachys, alpinia, cyperus, clove bark, amomum, zedoary and licorice warm, move qi and transform accumulations; it is said not to stir (purge) the bowels.
