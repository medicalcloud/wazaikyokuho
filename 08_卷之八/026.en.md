# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 026 茱萸内消丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zhuyu neixiao wan (Evodia Internal-Dispersal Pill)

It treats weakness of the kidney meridian with the bladder struck by pathogenic qi, binding into cold mounting and scrotal swelling, one-sided fire attacking upward, pain of the navel and abdomen, swelling and distension of the scrotum, or sores and ulcers with occasional yellow fluid, heaviness of the lower back and legs, swelling and fullness of the shins and difficulty walking. Taking it disperses [the swelling] internally without disturbing the zang-fu organs. (One formula lacks unripe bitter orange, tangerine peel, peach kernel, corydalis, toosendan and costus.)

shanzhuyu (sanshuyu; cornus; pounded, stones removed, flesh taken and lightly stir-fried)　jiegeng (kikyo; platycodon; soaked in water for a full day and night, strained out, stir-fried dry over a gentle fire)　bai jili (tribulus; stir-fried, spines removed)　chuanwu (Sichuan aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　huixiang (fennel; imported by ship, rinsed free of sand and stones, dried over a fire)　shizhuyu (ailanthus prickly-ash fruit)　wuzhuyu (goshuyu; evodia; lightly stir-fried)　qingpi (green tangerine peel; white removed; 2 liang each)　haizao (kaiso; sargassum; washed, dried over a fire)　wuweizi (gomishi; schisandra; picked clean)　dafupi (areca husk; washed in wine, dried over a fire)　xuanhusuo (corydalis; 2½ liang each)　taoren (tonin; peach kernel; skin, tips and twin kernels removed, stir-fried with bran, ground separately)　zhishi (kijitsu; unripe bitter orange; pulp removed, stir-fried with bran)　chenpi (chinpi; aged tangerine peel; white pith removed; 1 liang each)　chuanlianzi (toosendan fruit; chopped, stir-fried; 3 liang)　muxiang (mokko; costus root; 1½ liang)

Powder the above and form pills the size of wutong seeds with wine paste. Take thirty pills with warm wine, before meals.

## Notes

Zhuyu neixiao wan (from here the layer of bureau additions): for cold mounting and scrotal swelling from a weak kidney meridian with the bladder struck by pathogen, umbilical pain, scrotal distension, sores with yellow fluid, heavy back and legs, swollen shins and difficulty walking; said to disperse the swelling internally.
- Evodia, ailanthus prickly-ash fruit, cornus, Sichuan aconite, cinnamon and fennel warm; toosendan, green and aged tangerine peel, costus, unripe bitter orange, areca husk, corydalis and peach kernel move qi and blood; sargassum softens hardness; platycodon, tribulus and schisandra are added.
