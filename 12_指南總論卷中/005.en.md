# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 005 論諸風骨節疼痛 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On joint pain in the various winds. It always arises from wind qi entering the sinews and network vessels with pain of the joints. For attacking and pouring pain in the legs and hands, or hypertonicity with inability to bend and stretch, Ruxiang chentong san, Zhuifeng yingtong wan, Huoluo dan, Ruxiang wan, Moyao wan and Taiyue huoxue dan may all be taken. It is good first to wash by pouring with Wuxiang san and then to apply Huoxue dan.

## Notes

On joint pain in the various winds: for pain from wind qi in the sinews and joints, pain shooting to the limbs and hypertonicity, it lists Ruxiang chentong san, Zhuifeng yingtong wan, Huoluo dan, Ruxiang wan, Moyao wan and Taiyue huoxue dan, with external washing with Wuxiang san followed by application of Huoxue dan.
