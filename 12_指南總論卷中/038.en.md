# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 038 論傷寒吐血発衄 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On hematemesis and nosebleed in cold damage. When on the fifth to seventh day of cold damage there is nosebleed or vomiting of blood, it is because exuberant heat and congested qi produce nosebleed or hematemesis; one must on no account give hot medicines recklessly. Give Jisu wan or Bohe jian, taken cold with decocted imperata root decoction or imperata flower juice; for severe cases give Sanhuang wan. If there is thirst, also give Wuling san (Goreisan) or Qingxin san decocted with genuine typha pollen added.

## Notes

On nosebleed and hematemesis in cold damage (exuberant heat and congested qi): no hot medicines; Jisu wan or Bohe jian taken cold with imperata root decoction or imperata flower juice; Sanhuang wan for severe cases; Wuling san or Qingxin san with typha pollen for thirst.
