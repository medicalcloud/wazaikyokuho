# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 104 人参木香散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Renshen muxiang san (Ginseng and Costus Powder)

It smooths qi and loosens the middle. It treats glomus and blockage of the chest and diaphragm, stabbing pain of the heart and abdomen, distension and fullness of the flanks and ribs, reduced eating, belching and acid swallowing, vomiting counterflow with choking oppression, and all qi disorders.

muxiang (costus root; not exposed to fire)　qingpi (green tangerine peel; white not removed; 3 jin each)　jianghuang (turmeric)　mainie (barley sprouts; earth removed, stir-fried; 5 jin each)　gancao (licorice; chopped and stir-fried; 11 jin)　peng'ezhu (zedoary; scrubbed; 4 jin)　salt (stir-fried; 11 jin)

Powder the above. Take 1 *qian* each time stirred into boiling water, at any time.

## Notes

Renshen muxiang san (bureau additions): for chest glomus, stabbing heart and abdominal pain, flank distension, poor appetite, acid reflux, vomiting and all qi disorders.
- Despite the name, ginseng is not in the list of ingredients in the base text. Costus, green tangerine peel, turmeric and zedoary move qi; barley sprouts digest food; large amounts of stir-fried salt and licorice are added, and it is stirred into hot water.
