# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 065 二気丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Erqi dan (Two Qi Elixir)

It assists yang and disperses yin, rectifies qi and warms the middle. It treats internal deficiency and interior cold with cold qi attacking, distension, fullness and stabbing pain of the heart, flanks, navel and abdomen, unceasing diarrhoea, unstoppable vomiting, intermittent spontaneous sweating, urinary incontinence, gradually waning yang qi and reversal cold of the hands and feet; as well as yin patterns of cold damage, sudden turmoil with cramps, long-standing cold dysentery, and shortness of qi with emaciation and fatigue. All deficiency cold and chronic chill are suitably treated with it.

liuhuang (sulphur; finely ground)　rougui (cinnamon bark; bark removed, powdered; 1 *fen* each)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried, powdered)　zhusha (cinnabar; ground, for coating; 2 *qian* each)　fuzi (aconite; one large piece, blast-fried, skin and navel removed, powdered; half a liang)

Grind all the above together evenly and form pills the size of wutong seeds with fine flour paste. Take thirty pills each time, swallowed with decocted mugwort and salt decoction that has been cooled, on an empty stomach before meals.

## Notes

Erqi dan (no layer marked): for distension, stabbing pain, unceasing diarrhoea and vomiting, sweating, incontinence and cold limbs from internal deficiency and interior cold, for yin-pattern cold damage, sudden turmoil, chronic cold dysentery, and all deficiency cold and chronic chill.
- Sulphur, aconite, cinnamon and dried ginger assist yang and dispel cold; cinnabar forms the coating.
