# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 035 論傷寒吐逆 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On vomiting in cold damage. Vomiting in cold damage is from stomach cold. When there is vomiting with a cold body, or from taking too many cold medicines, without thirst, with normal stools or spontaneous diarrhea, or vomiting of roundworms, this is cold in the stomach; give Renshen dingxiang san (Ninjinchokosan), Shen ling baizhu san (Jinryobyakujutsusan), Si junzi tang (Shikunshito), Lizhong wan (Richugan), Renshen wan (Ninjingan) or Jiahe san.

## Notes

On vomiting in cold damage from stomach cold (vomiting with a cold body, after excess cold medicines, no thirst, diarrhea, vomiting worms): Renshen dingxiang san, Shen ling baizhu san, Si junzi tang, Lizhong wan, Renshen wan and Jiahe san.
