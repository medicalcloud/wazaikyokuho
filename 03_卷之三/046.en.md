# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 046 連翹丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Lianqiao wan (Rengyogan; Forsythia Pill)

It treats disharmony of the spleen and stomach in men and women with qi stagnating into abdominal masses, distension and fullness of the heart and abdomen, dry retching and heartburn, inability to get food down, choking obstruction of the chest and diaphragm, pain of the flanks and ribs, a yellow face from wine accumulation, deficiency swelling of the limbs and inability to walk. It is suitable for all spleen and stomach disorders.

lianqiao (rengyo; forsythia; washed)　chenpi (chinpi; aged tangerine peel; 240 liang each)　qingpi (green tangerine peel; washed)　peng'ezhu (zedoary; blast-fried)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed, not exposed to fire)　good ink (calcined; 160 liang each)　binglang (areca; 80 liang)　qianniuzi (morning-glory seed; milled, powder taken; 220 liang)　sanleng (sparganium; blast-fried; 249 liang)　roudoukou (nutmeg; 25 liang)

Powder the above and form pills the size of wutong seeds with flour paste. Take thirty pills each time with ginger decoction. For long-standing red and white dysentery, wind constipation of the large intestine and bleeding diarrhea from spleen toxin, take them with coptis decoction. For women's illnesses, with ginger and vinegar decoction. At any time. Pregnant women must not take it.

## Notes

Lianqiao wan (Shaoxing-era additions): for qi stagnation and accumulations, abdominal fullness, nausea, chest obstruction and flank pain from spleen-stomach disharmony, and for jaundice-like symptoms and swelling from wine accumulation.
- Sparganium, zedoary, morning-glory seed, areca and tangerine peels break up accumulations and send qi down; forsythia scatters heat; cinnamon and nutmeg warm. Contraindicated in pregnancy.
