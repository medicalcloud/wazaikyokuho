# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 027 大已寒丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Da yihan wan (Major Cold-Stopping Pill)

It is suitable for long-standing cold and accumulated chill with weakness of the zang-fu organs, wringing pain of the heart and abdomen, distension and fullness of the flanks and ribs, borborygmus and diarrhea, spontaneous diarrhea and spontaneous sweating, and undigested rice and grain; for sudden decline of yang qi with yin qi alone prevailing and cold hands and feet; and for cold damage with exuberant yin, clouded spirit, short pulse and weary limbs.

biba (long pepper)　rougui (cinnamon bark; 4 jin each)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried)　gaoliangjiang (lesser galangal; 6 jin each)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with flour paste boiled in water. Take twenty pills each time with rice-gruel water, before meals.

## Notes

Da yihan wan (Shaoxing-era additions): for abdominal pain, flank distension, diarrhea and indigestion from long-standing cold, cold limbs from declining yang, and yin-exuberant cold damage.
- Four acrid-hot drugs—long pepper, cinnamon, dried ginger and lesser galangal—warm interior cold.
