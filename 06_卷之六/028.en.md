# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 028 駐車丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zhuche wan (Cart-Halting Pill)

It treats all diarrhoea and dysentery, whether recent or long-standing, as well as dysentery with pus and blood from cold and heat, slippery intestines with tenesmus, stools without limit day and night, and unbearable wringing pain of the navel and abdomen.

ejiao (donkey-hide gelatin; pounded, stir-fried into beads, powdered and boiled down with 4 sheng of vinegar into a paste)　danggui (Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed; 15 liang each)　huanglian (coptis; hairs removed; 30 liang)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried; 10 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder, knead with the gelatin paste and roll by hand into pills the size of wutong seeds. Take thirty pills each time before meals with warm rice water, three times a day. For children, make pills the size of hemp seeds and adjust further according to age.

## Notes

Zhuche wan: for all diarrhoea and dysentery, recent or long-standing, and for dysentery with pus and blood from cold and heat with slippery intestines, tenesmus, frequent stools and wringing umbilical pain.
- Coptis clears heat and dried ginger warms cold (used together); angelica and donkey-hide gelatin nourish the blood and stop bleeding. In the lineage of the Zhuche wan of the *Qianjin fang*.
