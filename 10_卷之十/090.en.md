# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 090 銀白散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yinbai san (Silver-White Powder)

It treats the hundred illnesses of children. For chronic fright with convulsions, give it mixed in rice water with musk. After acute fright has settled, mixed in old-rice water. For fright with unceasing vomiting, mixed in clove decoction. For "collapse of the heavenly pillar" (inability to hold up the head) and soft legs, mixed in thick rice water. For cold damage accompanied by fright, mixed in mint and scallion-white decoction. For gan qi with abdominal distension, hurried breathing and much thirst, mixed in lily-bulb decoction. For vigorous fever of the whole body with red face and startled screaming, mixed in gold-and-silver mint decoction. For red and white dysentery with no desire for milk or food, mixed in a decoction of three coin-sized slices of ginger and three jujubes. For eating without knowing hunger or fullness and failure to grow flesh, mixed in a decoction of a pinch of stir-fried barley sprout with fresh ginger. For sudden diarrhea, mixed in perilla and chaenomeles decoction. For a spirit and form as if departed, with incoherent speech, and for vomiting and diarrhea in adults, mixed in agastache decoction. After the various illnesses, for lack of vitality, little strength and no desire for food, mixed in decocted ginger-jujube decoction. For children with a timid constitution, one dose every day is best.

shengma (shoma; cimicifuga)　zhimu (chimo; anemarrhena)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried)　bai biandou (hyacinth bean; stir-fried)　shanyao (Chinese yam)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng)　fuling (bukuryo; poria; skin removed)　baizhu (byakujutsu; white atractylodes; equal parts)

Make the above into a fine powder. Take 1 *qian* each time with the vehicles above; for regular use mix with boiling water, at any time.

## Notes

Yinbai san (bureau additions): for the hundred illnesses of children, with vehicles varying by condition (chronic fright, after acute fright, fright with vomiting, weak neck and legs, cold damage with fright, gan distension, high fever with screaming, dysentery, failure to thrive, sudden diarrhea, collapse, convalescence); a daily dose is recommended for constitutionally weak children.
- Si junzi tang (Shikunshito) (ginseng, atractylodes, poria, licorice) with Chinese yam and hyacinth bean to supplement the spleen, plus cimicifuga and anemarrhena.
