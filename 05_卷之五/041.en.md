# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 041 十全大補湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shiquan dabu tang (Perfect Major Supplementation Decoction)

It treats men and women with insufficiency from the various deficiencies, the five taxations and seven damages, inability to eat, deficiency and damage from long illness, occasional tidal fever, qi attacking the bones and spine with cramping pain, seminal emission in dreams at night, a withered yellow complexion and weakness of the legs and knees; all cases where after illness the qi is not as before; injury and disturbance of blood and qi by worry, sorrow, thought and anxiety; panting, cough and fullness of the middle; weak spleen and kidney qi; and vexation and oppression of the five hearts. This medicine is warm but not hot in nature and effective in balanced supplementation; it nourishes qi and nurtures the spirit, awakens the spleen and stops thirst, smooths the upright and wards off evil, and warms the spleen and kidneys. Its effects cannot be fully described.

renshen (ginseng)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed, not exposed to fire)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage)　dihuang (rehmannia; washed, steamed with wine, dried over a fire)　fuling (poria; dried over a fire)　baizhu (white atractylodes; dried over a fire)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried)　huangqi (astragalus; rhizome head removed)　chuan danggui (Sichuan angelica; washed, rhizome head removed)　bai shaoyao (white peony root; equal parts)

Chop these ten ingredients into a coarse powder. For each dose take two large *qian* with one *zhan* of water, three slices of fresh ginger and two jujubes, decoct together down to seven-tenths and take it warm, at any time.

## Notes

Shiquan dabu tang (Wu Zhige's additions): for poor appetite, tidal fever, cramping pain of the bones and spine, seminal emission, sallow complexion and weak legs from insufficiency, the five taxations and seven damages and chronic illness; for poor recovery after illness, damage to blood and qi from worry, panting and cough, weak spleen and kidney qi, and vexation of the five hearts. It is still widely used today for dual deficiency of qi and blood (weakness after illness or surgery, anaemia, fatigue).
- Bazhen tang—Si junzi tang (ginseng, atractylodes, poria, licorice) combined with Siwu tang (angelica, lovage, peony, rehmannia)—plus astragalus and cinnamon, ten ingredients in all.
