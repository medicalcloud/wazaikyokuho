# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之七 038 荊芥湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jingjie tang (Schizonepeta Decoction)

It treats wind-heat congesting the lungs with swollen, painful throat, loss of voice, or a sensation as if something were stuck.

jingjie sui (schizonepeta spikes; half a liang)　jiegeng (platycodon; 2 liang)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried; 1 liang)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 4 *qian* with one *zhan* of water and three slices of ginger, decoct down to six-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm after meals.

## Notes

Jingjie tang (bureau additions): for swollen, painful throat, loss of voice or a sensation of something stuck from wind-heat congesting the lungs.
- Jiegeng tang (platycodon, licorice) plus schizonepeta.
