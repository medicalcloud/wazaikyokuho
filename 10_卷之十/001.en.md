# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 001 返魂丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Fanhun dan (Soul-Returning Elixir)

It should be given quickly to children with the various winds and epilepsy, with tidal attacks of convulsions, mouth and eyes drawn together, rigid neck and back, clenched jaw and eyes turned upward; and with deficiency wind arising after long deficiency from the various illnesses, with much sleeping, stupor and lassitude dragging on without resolving.

## Notes

Fanhun dan (the first formula of juan 10, "Treating children's diseases"): for the various winds and epilepsy in children (convulsions, mouth and eyes drawn together, rigid neck and back, clenched jaw, upward gaze), and for deficiency wind after long illness with somnolence and stupor.
- A large formula of over sixty ingredients: rhinoceros horn, antelope horn, bovine bezoar, borneol, musk, cinnabar, realgar, tabasheer and gold leaf clear heat and settle; agkistrodon, black-striped snake, scorpion, silkworm, gastrodia, arisaema, typhonium, notopterygium, pubescent angelica, saposhnikovia, ephedra and others dispel wind and phlegm; ginseng, atractylodes, poria, angelica and donkey-hide gelatin supplement; aconite, Sichuan aconite, dried ginger, cinnamon and others warm. It contains mercury, calomel and sulfur. One pill per year of age.
