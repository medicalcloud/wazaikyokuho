# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 097 参苓壮脾丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shen ling zhuangpi wan (Ginseng and Poria Spleen-Strengthening Pill)

It is suitable for weak spleen and stomach with stuffiness and oppression of the chest and diaphragm, distension and fullness of the flanks and ribs, stabbing pain of the heart and abdomen, stomach reflux with vomiting of food, bitter mouth and acid swallowing, chest fullness with shortness of breath, weary limbs and a withered yellow complexion; for a blocked middle jiao that cannot bear attack and deficiency cold of the zang organs that cannot accept drastic supplementation; for weakened qi after illness so that eating does not return to normal; for those born weak who cannot eat; and for long-standing diarrhea and dysentery with deficient, slippery stomach and intestines.

renshen (ninjin; ginseng)　baizhu (byakujutsu; white atractylodes)　fuling (bukuryo; poria; skin removed)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed, not exposed to fire)　suosha (amomum; skin removed)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger)　hujiao (black pepper)　mainie (barley sprouts; lightly stir-fried)　shenqu (medicated leaven)　shanyao (Chinese yam)　bai biandou (hyacinth bean; stir-fried)

Powder the above in equal parts and form pills the size of a pellet with refined honey. Chew one pill thoroughly each time and wash it down with plain hot water; warm wine is also fine; on an empty stomach before meals. Taken regularly it nurtures the spirit and nourishes qi, harmonizes and supplements the spleen and stomach, and makes eating better.

## Notes

Shen ling zhuangpi wan (bureau additions): for chest stuffiness, abdominal pain, stomach reflux, acid reflux, lassitude and sallow complexion from weak spleen and stomach, deficiency that can bear neither attack nor drastic supplementation, poor appetite after illness or from birth, and chronic diarrhea.
- Ginseng, atractylodes, poria, Chinese yam and hyacinth bean supplement the spleen; cinnamon, dried ginger and black pepper warm; amomum moves qi; barley sprouts and medicated leaven digest food. Its indications resemble those of Jiahe san (juan 3, no. 010).
