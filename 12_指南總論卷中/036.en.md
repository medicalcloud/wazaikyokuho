# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 036 論傷寒発喘 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On dyspnea in cold damage. Dyspnea with hurried breathing in cold damage always arises from wind-cold, the pathogen riding on the lungs so that meridian qi is exuberant above and dyspnea occurs; Mahuang tang (Maoto; Ephedra Decoction), Huagai san (Kagaisan; Canopy Powder), Kuandonghua san (Kantokasan), Renshen runfei wan, Yangfei wan, or Runjin san and Kuanfei [san] may all be taken.

## Notes

On dyspnea in cold damage (wind-pathogenic cold riding on the lungs, meridian qi exuberant above): Mahuang tang, Huagai san, Kuandonghua san, Renshen runfei wan, Yangfei wan, Runjin san and Kuanfei san.
