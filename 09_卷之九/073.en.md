# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 073 人参当帰散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Renshen danggui san (Ginseng and Angelica Powder)

It should be taken urgently after heavy blood loss in childbirth: when blood is deficient yin is deficient; yin deficiency generates internal heat, and internal heat is called vexation. The signs are vexing fullness of the heart and chest, faint, short breathing, headache with oppression and confusion, and painful joints, always worse in the late afternoon, resembling the deficiency vexation that follows a major illness.

gan dihuang (dried rehmannia)　renshen (ginseng)　danggui (Chinese angelica)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　maimendong (ophiopogon; core removed; 1 liang each)　bai shaoyao (white peony root; 2 liang)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose of four large *qian*: with two *zhan* of water first decoct 1 *ge* of non-glutinous rice and ten lophatherum leaves down to one *zhan*, remove the rice and leaves, add the medicine with three jujubes, decoct down to seven-tenths, remove the dregs and take it before meals. The rehmannia should be the raw dried kind; if the deficiency is severe, use the prepared kind.

## Notes

Renshen danggui san (bureau additions): for vexing fullness of the chest, breathlessness, headache, oppression and joint pain worse in the late afternoon—internal heat ("vexation") from blood and yin deficiency after heavy postpartum bleeding.
- Rehmannia, angelica, white peony and ophiopogon nourish yin and blood; ginseng supplements; cinnamon is added; decocted in a broth of rice and lophatherum. Prepared rehmannia is used if deficiency is severe.
