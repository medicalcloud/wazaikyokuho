# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 032 人参散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Renshen san (Ginseng Powder)

It regulates the middle, harmonizes qi, stops vomiting counterflow and removes vexing thirst. It treats stupor and fatigue with much sleeping and reduced intake of milk and food, as well as cold damage and seasonal qi with stomach qi not flowing smoothly, and agitated thirst that persists after vomiting and diarrhoea have stopped.

gange (dried pueraria; 2 liang)　renshen (ginseng)　bai fuling (white poria; skin removed; 1 liang each)　muxiang (costus root)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried)　huoxiang ye (agastache leaf; 1 *fen* each)

Powder the above. For each dose take 1 *qian* with one medium *zhan* of water, decoct down to seven-tenths, remove the dregs, let it cool to warm and take it, at any time.

## Notes

Renshen san: regulates the middle and harmonizes qi, stops vomiting and vexing thirst; for stupor, somnolence and poor feeding in children, and for agitated thirst after vomiting and diarrhoea have stopped in cold damage or seasonal qi.
- Pueraria stops thirst; ginseng, poria and licorice supplement; costus and agastache move qi and stop vomiting (close to Qian Yi's Baizhu san without atractylodes).
