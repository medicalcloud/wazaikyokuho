# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 079 神効参香散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shenxiao shenxiang san (Divinely Effective Ginseng and Costus Powder)

It can treat, whether recent or long-standing, deficient, timid visceral qi in adults and children with disharmony of cold and heat accumulating in the zang-fu organs and producing dysentery, passing fresh blood, or matter like bean juice or fish brains, or static blood, or purple-black blood, or red and white mixed, or five colors, with tenesmus, very frequent day and night, and extremely unbearable wringing pain of the navel and abdomen; as well as food-refusing, gan-worm and seasonal epidemic dysenteries.

bai biandou (hyacinth bean; stir-fried)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng)　muxiang (mokko; costus root; 2 liang each)　fuling (bukuryo; poria; skin removed)　roudoukou (nutmeg; skin removed; 4 liang each)　chenpi (chinpi; aged tangerine peel; white pith removed)　yingsuqiao (poppy husk; stalk removed; 12 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder. Take three large *qian* each time mixed in warm rice water, at any time, with immediate divine effect.

## Notes

Shenxiao shenxiang san (bureau additions): for all kinds of dysentery (fresh blood, bean-juice, fish-brain, static or purple-black blood, red and white, five-colored stools) with tenesmus, frequent stools and severe wringing pain from deficient visceral qi and disharmony of cold and heat, and for food-refusing, gan-worm and seasonal epidemic dysentery, recent or long-standing.
- Ginseng, poria and hyacinth bean supplement the spleen; costus and tangerine peel move qi; poppy husk and nutmeg astringe. A different formula from the Shenxiang san (Jinkosan) of juan 5, no. 078.
