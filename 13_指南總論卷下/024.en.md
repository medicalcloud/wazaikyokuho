# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷下 024 論霍乱吐瀉証 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On the pattern of vomiting and diarrhea of sudden turmoil. Vomiting and diarrhea of sudden turmoil have two patterns, cold and hot. For much cold without thirst, give Lizhong wan (Richugan), Jiangfu tang or Laifu dan. For vomiting and diarrhea of sudden turmoil with heat, vexation, agitation and thirst, take Xiangru san, Wuling san (Goreisan), Jiahe san, Shen ling baizhu san (Jinryobyakujutsusan) or Si junzi tang (Shikunshito). To regulate the spleen and stomach after vomiting and diarrhea of sudden turmoil, give Shen ling baizhu san, Jiahe san, Wuling san, Si junzi tang, Tiaoqi san and the like. For thirst, give Shen ling baizhu san to stop it; taking much is even better. For cold sweats with soft hands and feet, add Jinye dan, Erqi dan or Chaozhen dan. If ineffective, apply moxa at Qihai. Once the vomiting and diarrhea have settled, stop all hot medicines and regulate the spleen with warm medicines only.

## Notes

On vomiting and diarrhea of sudden turmoil: the cold pattern (no thirst) with Lizhong wan, Jiangfu tang or Laifu dan; the hot pattern (agitation, thirst) with Xiangru san, Wuling san and others; convalescence with Shen ling baizhu san and others; cold sweats and weak limbs with Jinye dan, Erqi dan or Chaozhen dan; moxa at Qihai if ineffective; once settled, stop hot medicines and use warm ones for the spleen.
