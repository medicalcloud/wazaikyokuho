# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之七 016 明睛散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Mingjing san (Eye-Brightening Powder)

It can treat external obstructions and clear nebulae and membranes, and it treats wind toxin attacking upward with painful pupils and red swelling, or itching of the lids and canthi, and frequent hot tears with dimness and roughness.

chi shaoyao (shakuyaku; red peony root)　danggui (toki) (Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed, washed, dried over a fire)　huanglian (oren; coptis; fibrous roots removed)　huashi (kasseki; talc; finely ground)

Take 5 liang of each of the above, chop and mill into a fine powder, and mix in the ground talc evenly. Each time steep half a *qian* in boiling water, let it settle, remove the dregs and wash the eyes while hot. Avoid all salted and preserved foods, pickled fish, wine, flour and other toxic things.

## Notes

Mingjing san (Wu Zhige's additions): for external obstructions and nebulae, and for painful pupils, red swelling, itchy lids and canthi, hot tears and dimness from wind toxin attacking upward.
- An external eyewash of red peony, angelica, coptis and talc steeped in hot water and decanted.
