# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 014 烏沈湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wuchen tang (Lindera and Aquilaria Decoction)

It harmonizes all qi, removes all cold, regulates the middle and supplements the five viscera, augments essence and strengthens the yang way, warms the lower back and knees and dispels evil qi. It treats vomiting, diarrhoea and cramps, painful concretions and aggregations, toxic swelling of wind-water, and cold-wind numbness. It also governs heart and abdominal pain from malign-qi strike, gu toxin, infixation and visiting-hostility, ghost qi, undispersed old food, seasonal miasmic epidemics, cold qi between the bladder and kidneys attacking and surging so that the spine cannot bend forward and back freely, and women's blood qi attacking with griping pain of the heart and abdomen. It is suitable for all of these.

Tiantai wu (lindera from Tiantai; 100 liang)　chenxiang (aquilaria; 50 liang)　renshen (ginseng; 3 liang)　gancao (licorice; scorched; 4½ liang)

Powder the above. Take half a *qian* each time with three slices of ginger and a little salt, stirred into boiling water, on an empty stomach before meals.

## Notes

Wuchen tang: harmonizes all qi and removes all cold; for vomiting, diarrhoea and cramps, painful concretions, swelling, cold numbness, malign-qi strike, gu toxin, old food, epidemics, back pain from lower-burner cold, and women's blood-qi abdominal pain.
- Mainly lindera and aquilaria, with ginseng and licorice; stirred into hot water.
