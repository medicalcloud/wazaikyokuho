# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 084 蛇頭丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shetou wan (Snake Head Pill)

It treats acute and chronic fright wind in children with twitching of the hands and feet, a fixed stare and arched-back rigidity, when the signs are critical.

shehanshi (limonite nodule; ten, calcined three times, quenched in vinegar, then boiled in licorice decoction to drive out the sour qi, ground and water-ground into a fine powder)　tie nifen (iron powder)　wulingzhi (flying squirrel droppings; soaked in wine, sand removed)　shensha (cinnabar; ground)　xieshao (scorpion tail)　bai fuzi (typhonium tuber; blast-fried)　yujin (curcuma tuber; blast-fried; 2 liang each)　longnao (borneol; ground separately; half a liang)　shexiang (musk; ground; 1 liang)　huashe tou (agkistrodon head; ten, soaked in wine, bones removed, teeth and flesh used)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of euryale seeds with flour paste. For each dose grind one pill in natural mint juice, dissolve with well-flower water and give it, adjusted for the child's size.

## Notes

Shetou wan (bureau additions): for critical acute or chronic fright wind (twitching limbs, fixed stare, opisthotonus) in children.
- Agkistrodon head, scorpion tail and typhonium dispel wind; limonite nodule, iron powder, cinnabar, borneol, musk and curcuma settle and open the orifices; flying squirrel droppings are added.
