# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之七 003 密蒙花散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Mimenghua san (Buddleja Flower Powder)

It treats all of the following: wind qi attacking and pouring in, with both eyes dim and dark, discharge and tears with aversion to light, wind-millet bumps on the eyelids, gritty roughness making them hard to open, itching or pain, gradual formation of nebulae and unclear vision; long-standing hemilateral headache drawing into both eyes, which gradually feel narrowed and small, dim, rough and dully painful; and sudden red swelling and pain.

mimenghua (buddleja flower; clean)　shijueming (abalone shell; boiled with salt in east-flowing water for a full day and night, strained out and ground to powder)　muzei (scouring rush)　du jili (tribulus; stir-fried, spines removed)　qianghuo (notopterygium; rhizome head removed)　juhua (chrysanthemum flower; earth removed; equal parts)

Make the above into a fine powder. Take 1 *qian* each time mixed in clear *la* tea, after meals, twice a day.

## Notes

Mimenghua san: for dim vision, discharge, tearing, photophobia, bumps on the eyelids, difficulty opening the eyes, itching, pain, nebulae and poor sight from wind qi attacking; for long-standing hemilateral headache drawing into the eyes; and for sudden red swelling and pain.
- Buddleja flower, abalone shell, scouring rush, tribulus and chrysanthemum brighten the eyes and clear nebulae; notopterygium disperses wind. Taken with *la* tea.
