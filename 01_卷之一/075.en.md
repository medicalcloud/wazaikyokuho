# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 075 四生丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Sisheng wan (Shiseigan; Four Raw Ingredients Pill)

It specifically treats paralysis of the left or right side, deviation of the mouth and eyes, wind stroke with urgent drool, and hemiplegia with inability to raise [the limbs]; it treats all of these.

wulingzhi (flying-squirrel droppings; stones removed)　gusuibu (drynaria)　chuan wutou (uzu) (Sichuan aconite; skin and tips removed)　danggui (toki) (Chinese angelica; equal parts)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with flour paste made with ash-free wine. Take seven pills each time, gradually increasing to ten and then fifteen, with warm wine. While taking this medicine do not take Lingbao dan, lest the medicine lose its effect.

## Notes

Sisheng wan (bureau additions): for paralysis, facial deviation, drool in wind stroke and hemiplegia.
- Four ingredients: flying-squirrel droppings, drynaria, Sichuan aconite and angelica. It is said to lose effect if combined with Lingbao dan (juan 1, no. 002).
