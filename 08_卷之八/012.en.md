# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 012 石葦散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shiwei san (Pyrrosia Powder)

It treats all cases of insufficient kidney qi with heat in the bladder, blocked waterways, dribbling strangury that does not flow, passing little but getting up often, cramping pain of the navel and abdomen, accumulating with attacks at intervals and arising at once with fatigue, with urine like bean juice or passing sand and stones.

shaoyao (peony root)　baizhu (white atractylodes)　huashi (talc)　kuizi (mallow seed)　qumai (dianthus; 3 liang each)　shiwei (pyrrosia leaf; hairs removed)　mutong (akebia; 2 liang each)　wangbuliuxing (vaccaria seed)　danggui (Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried; 1 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder. Take 2 *qian* each time mixed in decocted wheat decoction, before meals, two or three times a day.

## Notes

Shiwei san: for difficult, dribbling and frequent urination, cramping umbilical pain, urine like bean juice and sand-and-stone strangury (urinary stones) from insufficient kidney qi and bladder heat. The indications closely match those of the earlier Wulin san (juan 6, no. 014).
- Pyrrosia, talc, mallow seed, dianthus, akebia and vaccaria promote urination and relieve strangury; peony, angelica, atractylodes and licorice are added.
