# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 002 安息香丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Anxixiang wan (Ansokkogan; Benzoin Pill)

It treats all cold qi with pain of the heart and abdomen, choking obstruction of the chest and diaphragm, distension of the flanks and ribs, hard stuffiness below the heart, empty rumbling in the abdomen, hiccough and nausea, belching and acid swallowing, cold counterflow in the stomach with incessant vomiting, undispersed old retained fluid, stabbing pain in the chest and diaphragm, occasional vomiting of clear water, and no desire for food.

rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed; 2½ liang)　hezi (chebule; blast-fried, skin taken; 2 liang)　awei (asafoetida; finely ground, kneaded with a little white flour into a cake and roasted until fragrant and cooked; 1 *fen*)　fuling (bukuryo; poria; the white part)　danggui (toki) (Chinese angelica; washed in hot water, sliced and dried over a fire)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried, skin removed)　roudoukou (nutmeg; shell removed)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage)　dingxiang (clove; skin)　suoshaner (amomum)　wuweizi (gomishi; schisandra; lightly stir-fried)　baji (morinda; core removed, stir-fried with flour)　yizhi (alpinia oxyphylla; seeds, skin removed)　bai doukou (white cardamom; skin removed; 1½ liang each)　naosha (sal ammoniac; dissolved in half a *zhan* of wine, stones removed, put into the honey)　binglang (areca; blast-fried)　bichengqie (cubeb)　shaoyao (shakuyaku; peony root)　ezhu (zedoary)　sanleng (sparganium; blast-fried)　anxixiang (benzoin; dissolved in half a *zhan* of wine, sand removed, put into the honey)　xiangfu (cyperus; hairs removed)　huixiang (fennel; lightly stir-fried; 1½ liang each)　hujiao (black pepper)　gaoliangjiang (lesser galangal)　muxiang (mokko; costus root)　chenxiang (aquilaria)　ruxiang (frankincense; ground separately)　dingxiang (clove; 1 liang each)

Except for the benzoin and sal ammoniac, pound all the above medicines together and sieve into a fine powder. Put the benzoin and sal ammoniac into 30 liang of honey and refine it, make the medicines into a mass with it, pound one or two thousand times and form pills the size of a euryale kernel. Take one pill each time, chewed thoroughly, with warm wine; strongly decocted ginger decoction is also fine. Take before meals.

## Notes

Anxixiang wan: for pain of the heart and abdomen, chest obstruction, flank distension, nausea, acid reflux, vomiting and vomiting of clear water from all cold qi.
- Cinnamon, dried ginger, clove, black pepper, galangal, fennel and cubeb warm; costus, aquilaria, cyperus, areca, amomum and cardamoms move qi; sparganium, zedoary, asafoetida and sal ammoniac disperse accumulations; angelica, lovage and peony regulate the blood.
