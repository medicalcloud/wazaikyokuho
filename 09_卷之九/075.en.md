# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 075 四神散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Sishen san (Four Spirits Powder)

It treats postpartum retained blood that does not disperse, accumulating and gathering into lumps with urgent, acute pain like hidden-corpse disorder, as well as gripping pain of the heart and abdomen and diarrhoea.

danggui (Chinese angelica)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage)　chi shaoyao (red peony root)

Take equal parts of the above and pound into a powder. Take one square-inch spoonful each time mixed in warm wine.

## Notes

Sishen san (bureau additions): for acute severe pain (like hidden-corpse disorder) from retained postpartum blood forming lumps, and for gripping abdominal pain and diarrhoea.
- Four ingredients: angelica, lovage, red peony and dried ginger.
