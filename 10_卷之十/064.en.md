# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 064 柴胡散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Chaihu san (Saikosan; Bupleurum Powder)

It treats cold damage in children with vigorous fever, headache and body pain, dry mouth and vexing thirst.

shigao (sekko; gypsum)　huangqin (ogon; scutellaria)　gancao (kanzo; licorice)　chi shaoyao (shakuyaku; red peony root)　gegen (kakkon; pueraria; 1 liang each)　mahuang (mao; ephedra; roots and nodes removed)　chaihu (saiko; bupleurum; shoots removed; half a liang each)

Pound and sift the above into a powder. For a three-year-old child, take 1 *qian* each time with a small *zhan* of water, a little fresh ginger, three *cun* of scallion white and twenty fermented soybeans, decoct together down to five-tenths, strain off the dregs and take it warm, at any time; sweating is the sign of effect; adjust for the child's size.

## Notes

Chaihu san (Wu Zhige's additions): for high fever, headache, body pain, dry mouth and vexing thirst in cold damage in children.
- Ephedra, pueraria and bupleurum release the exterior; gypsum and scutellaria clear heat; red peony and licorice are added; decocted with ginger, scallion white and fermented soybean to induce sweating.
