# 太平惠民和劑局方 進表 p002 将仕郎措置薬局検閲方書陳承 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Chen Cheng, Gentleman for Service, Examiner of Formularies for the Management of the Pharmacies

Pei Zongyuan, Gentleman for Discussion, Acting Imperial Physician, concurrently Examiner of Formularies for the Management of the Pharmacies

Chen Shiwen, Gentleman for Court Service, Acting Director of the Treasury Bureau of the Department of State Affairs, Superintendent of the Management of the Pharmacies — respectfully presented

## Notes

The signatures of the memorial: the official titles of Chen Cheng, Pei Zongyuan and Chen Shiwen, who revised the formulary.
- Jiangshi lang, Fengyi lang and Chaofeng lang are civil-service ranks; the other titles denote posts for examining the formularies and supervising the state pharmacies.
