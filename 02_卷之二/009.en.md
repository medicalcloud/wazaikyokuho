# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 009 升麻葛根湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shengma gegen tang (Cimicifuga and Pueraria Decoction)

It is suitable for adults and children with seasonal warm epidemics, with headache, fever and vexing pain of the limbs; and for eruptive rashes, whether they have already erupted or not yet, and when it is doubtful which.

shengma (cimicifuga)　bai shaoyao (white peony root)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried; 10 liang each)　gegen (pueraria; 15 liang)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water, decoct to obtain one medium *zhan*, remove the dregs and take it fairly hot, at any time, two or three doses a day, until the illness departs and the body is clear and cool. Children take amounts according to their strength.

## Notes

Shengma gegen tang: for headache, fever and body pain in seasonal warm epidemics, and for eruptive diseases (measles, smallpox, etc.) before or as the rash comes out. It is still used in the early stage of colds and eruptive diseases.
- Four ingredients: cimicifuga and pueraria release the flesh and exterior and bring out the rash; peony and licorice harmonize.
- The base text reads 巳発 for 已発 ("already erupted"); since the Siku quanshu and Executive Yuan editions both read 已発, it has been emended (see the emendation).
