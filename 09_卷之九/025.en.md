# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 025 大通真丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Da tongzhen wan (Major True-Freeing Pill)

It is suitable for taxation damage of qi and blood with insufficient nutritive and defensive qi, cold lodged in the channels and network vessels invading and damaging the bowels and viscera, irregular menses, pain of the navel and abdomen, a haggard appearance, an emaciated weak body, deficiency distension of the flanks and ribs, dizzy, heavy head and eyes, palpitation and shortness of breath, reduced eating and a liking for lying down; as well as taxation damage from childbirth with weakness not recovered, wind-cold evil qi taking advantage of the deficiency to lodge and contend, intermittent pain of the abdomen and flanks, painful, weary limbs, getting up and lying down by turns, gradually becoming taxation damage. Taken regularly every day within a hundred days after childbirth, it removes old blood, nourishes new blood, augments qi, supplements deficiency, regulates and harmonizes the *chong* and *ren*, and prevents the various illnesses.

cangzhu (black atractylodes; soaked overnight in rice-washing water, lightly stir-fried)　chanqiao (cicada slough; mouthparts and legs removed, lightly stir-fried)　gancao (licorice; lightly roasted until red)　bai wuyi (white elm seed paste; lightly stir-fried)　baizhu (white atractylodes)　baiwei (cynanchum root)　xiongqiong (Sichuan lovage)　gaoben (Chinese lovage; lightly stir-fried)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried; half a liang each)　canzhi (silkworm egg-paper; burned to ash; 2½ liang)　renshen (ginseng; shoots removed)　chuanjiao (Sichuan pepper; seeds and closed fruits removed, lightly stir-fried to sweat out the oil)　fangfeng (saposhnikovia; shoots and forks removed)　shigao (gypsum; ground and water-ground)　danggui (Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed, lightly stir-fried)　fuzi (aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　zelan (lycopus; leaves)　jiegeng (platycodon; shoots removed)　baiziren (arborvitae seed; lightly stir-fried, ground separately; 1 liang each)　baizhi (angelica dahurica)　bai shaoyao (white peony root)　shizhuyu (ailanthus prickly-ash fruit)　houpo (magnolia bark; coarse bark removed, roasted with ginger juice; 3 *fen* each)

Pound and sift the above into a powder and form pills with refined honey, ten pills to each 1 liang 2 *qian*. Take one pill each time before meals, ground into angelica wine.

## Notes

Da tongzhen wan: for irregular menses, umbilical pain, haggardness, wasting, flank distension, dizziness, palpitation, breathlessness and poor appetite when cold lodges in the channels with taxation damage of qi and blood; and for abdominal and flank pain, limb pain and progressive taxation when wind-cold rides on postpartum damage. Daily use for the hundred days after childbirth is recommended.
- Silkworm egg-paper ash, cicada slough, cynanchum and lycopus, with ginseng, atractylodes, angelica, lovage and white peony to supplement; aconite, dried ginger, Sichuan pepper and ailanthus prickly-ash warm; saposhnikovia, Chinese lovage and angelica dahurica dispel wind; gypsum, black atractylodes, magnolia bark, platycodon, arborvitae seed, elm seed paste and licorice are added.
