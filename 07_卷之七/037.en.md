# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之七 037 玉池散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yuchi san (Jade Pool Powder)

It treats wind and worm toothache with swelling, itching and loosening, ulcerated, festering gums, receding gums with bleeding, bad breath and similar disorders.

danggui (toki; Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed)　gaoben (kohon; Chinese lovage)　digupi (lycium root bark)　fangfeng (bofu; saposhnikovia)　baizhi (byakushi; angelica dahurica)　huaihua (sophora flower; stir-fried)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried)　shengma (shoma; cimicifuga)　xixin (saishin; asarum; shoots removed; equal parts)

Powder the above. Each time rub a little on the teeth. If the pain is severe, take 2 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water, half a *ge* of black soybeans and three slices of fresh ginger, decoct down to one *zhan*, rinse the mouth with it while slightly warm and spit it out when it cools.

## Notes

Yuchi san (bureau additions): for toothache from wind or worms, swelling, itching and loosening, ulcerated, receding and bleeding gums, and bad breath.
- Cimicifuga, angelica dahurica, Chinese lovage, saposhnikovia, asarum and lovage disperse wind and stop pain; lycium root bark and sophora flower clear heat and stop bleeding; angelica and licorice are added. Used as a tooth powder, or decocted and held in the mouth.
