# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 018 四逆湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Sini tang (Frigid Extremities Decoction)

It is suitable for cold damage with spontaneous diarrhoea without thirst, incessant retching, or vomiting and diarrhoea together, urination sometimes rough and sometimes free, or excessive sweating, a faint pulse on the verge of expiring, abdominal pain with distension and fullness, and counterflow cold of the hands and feet; and for all deficiency-cold reversal cold. Whenever cold damage shows these signs, it is because yang qi is deficient and there is cold in the interior. Even if headache, body pain, fever, aversion to cold and cramping of the limbs are also felt, and the exterior pattern is complete, one must not yet attack the exterior; first take this medicine to assist yang and rescue the interior.

gancao (licorice; mix-fried; 2 liang)　ganjiang (dried ginger; 1½ liang)　fuzi (aconite; raw, skin and navel removed, finely sliced; half a liang)

Make the licorice and dried ginger into a coarse powder and mix in the aconite evenly. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water, decoct down to one medium *zhan*, remove the dregs and take it warm, at any time. Taken regularly it dispels summerheat qi and separates water and grain.

## Notes

Sini tang: for deficiency cold of the shaoyin and taiyin in cold damage with diarrhoea, vomiting, faint pulse and cold limbs, and all deficiency-cold reversal; it corresponds to Sini tang of the *Shanghan lun* (e.g. clauses 91, 323).
- Licorice, dried ginger and raw aconite. It states the *Shanghan lun* principle (clauses 91, 372) that when interior cold is present one first rescues the interior with Sini tang even if the exterior pattern is complete.
- The final sentence ("taken regularly it dispels summerheat and separates water and grain") is unusual for Sini tang and may have strayed in from the following Dashun san; this has not been verified.
