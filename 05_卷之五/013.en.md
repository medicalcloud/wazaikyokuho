# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 013 黄耆建中湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangqi jianzhong tang (Ogikenchuto; Astragalus Middle-Fortifying Decoction)

It is suitable for men and women with insufficiency from the various deficiencies, with cramping pain of the lower abdomen, distension of the flanks and ribs, deficiency fullness below the navel, vexing palpitation in the chest, a withered yellow complexion, dry lips and mouth, little strength and a heavy body, chest fullness with shortness of breath, stiff painful lower back and spine, aching bones and flesh, dyspnea exhaustion on movement and inability to eat; whether from excessive overwork or from failure to recover after illness.

huangqi (ogi; astragalus)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed; 3 liang each)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried; 2 liang)　bai shaoyao (shakuyaku; white peony root; 6 liang)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water, three or four slices of fresh ginger and one large jujube, decoct together down to one medium *zhan*, strain off the dregs, add a little malt syrup and heat again until it dissolves; take it fairly hot on an empty stomach before meals.

## Notes

Huangqi jianzhong tang: for lower abdominal cramping pain, flank distension, palpitation, sallow complexion, dry mouth, lassitude, shortness of breath, back pain, aching body and poor appetite from insufficiency, overwork or poor recovery after illness. It corresponds to Huangqi jianzhong tang of the *Jingui yaolüe* (blood impediment and deficiency taxation) and is still used for weak constitutions, night sweats and chronic abdominal pain.
- Xiao jianzhong tang (peony, cinnamon, licorice, ginger, jujube, malt syrup) plus astragalus; cinnamon appears here as cinnamon bark.
