# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 055 神仙百解散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shenxian baijie san (Immortals' Hundred-Resolutions Powder)

(Also called Shenxian jie shanghan siji jiajian baijie san, Immortals' Hundred-Resolutions Powder for Cutting Short Cold Damage, Modified for the Four Seasons.) It treats cold damage with pain all over the body, stiffness of all the joints, clouding and pain of the head and eyes, fatigue and weariness of the limbs, vigorous fever with aversion to cold and an unclear mind; and colds, pestilence and miasmic qi. Taken regularly it wards off pestilence and treats fatigue.

shan yinchen (capillaris)　chaihu (saiko; bupleurum; rhizome head removed)　qianhu (peucedanum; processed with ginger, stir-fried)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng)　qianghuo (notopterygium)　duhuo (dokukatsu; angelica pubescens root)　gancao (kanzo; licorice)　cangzhu (black atractylodes; soaked in rice-washing water, chopped, stir-fried)　gange (dried pueraria)　bai shaoyao (shakuyaku; white peony root)　shengma (shoma; cimicifuga)　fangfeng (bofu; saposhnikovia; sprouts removed)　gaoben (kohon) (Chinese lovage; rhizome head removed)　huoxiang (agastache; stalks removed)　baizhu (byakujutsu; white atractylodes)　banxia (hange; pinellia; roasted with ginger juice; 1 liang each)

From the Beginning of Spring there are no modifications. From the Beginning of Summer add to one batch:

chaihu (bupleurum; 1 *fen*)　chi fuling (bukuryo; red poria)　danggui (toki) (Chinese angelica; half a liang each)

From the Beginning of Autumn reduce the bupleurum by 1 *fen*, do not use the angelica and poria, and add only:

ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed; 1 *fen* each)　mahuang (mao; ephedra; nodes removed; half a liang)

From the Beginning of Winter there are no modifications. [One version lacks angelica and has scutellaria (rhizome head removed, half a liang).]

Make the above into a fine powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water, three slices of fresh ginger and two jujubes, decoct down to one *zhan* and take it hot, at any time, two doses in succession. To release the exterior, decoct it with three *cun* of scallion white and thirty prepared fermented soybeans, cover with clothes and bedding, and sweating brings recovery.

## Notes

Shenxian baijie san (from here the layer of secret formulas from the bureaus): for general pain, joint stiffness, headache, lassitude and chills and fever in cold damage, and for colds, pestilence and miasma.
- Bupleurum, peucedanum, notopterygium, duhuo, pueraria, cimicifuga, saposhnikovia and Chinese lovage release the exterior; ginseng, white atractylodes, licorice and peony supplement; black atractylodes, agastache, pinellia and capillaris remove damp. The additions vary with the season (red poria and angelica after the Beginning of Summer; dried ginger, cinnamon and ephedra after the Beginning of Autumn).
