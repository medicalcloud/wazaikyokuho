# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 055 紅丸子 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Hong wanzi (Red Pills)

It treats men with spleen accumulation and qi stagnation, fullness and oppression of the chest and diaphragm, a yellow face and distended abdomen, weak limbs, wine accumulation with inability to eat, unceasing dry retching, and pain from the shoulder blades through the heart and chest to both breasts; women with spleen blood accumulation and qi accumulation and all kinds of blood concretions and qi lumps; and children with food accumulation, bony emaciation, a yellow face, distended belly with hurried breathing and no liking for food, gradually becoming spleen taxation. It is suitable for old and young alike.

jing sanleng (sparganium; soaked until soft, sliced)　peng'ezhu (zedoary)　qing jupi (green tangerine peel)　chenpi (aged tangerine peel; white pith removed; 5 jin each)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried)　hujiao (black pepper; 3 jin each)

Make the above into a fine powder, form pills the size of wutong seeds with vinegar-and-flour paste and coat them with fanhong (red iron oxide). Take thirty pills each time after meals with ginger decoction. For children adjust the amount at the time.

## Notes

Hong wanzi (Shaoxing-era additions): for chest oppression, sallow complexion, abdominal distension, wine accumulation, vomiting and back and chest pain from spleen accumulation and qi stagnation in men, women's blood and qi accumulations, and children's food accumulation (spleen taxation).
- Sparganium and zedoary break up accumulations, the tangerine peels break up qi, dried ginger and black pepper warm; the red coating gives the name.
