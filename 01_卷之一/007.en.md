# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 007 透氷丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Toubing dan (Ice-Penetrating Elixir)

It treats all wind toxin attacking upward, with swelling and itching of the head and face, congestion of phlegm and drool, discomfort of the heart and chest, and dryness and roughness of the mouth and tongue; and wind toxin pouring downward, with heaviness of the lower back and legs, swelling, pain and sores, frequent constipation, and red, rough urine. It also treats wind-strike with paralysis and all wind diseases.

manjingzi (vitex fruit; white skin removed)　bai fuling (white poria; skin removed)　chuan dahuang (Sichuan rhubarb; coarse bark removed)　shanzhizi (gardenia; skin removed)　yizhizi (alpinia oxyphylla; skin removed)　weilingxian (clematis root; rhizome head removed, washed, dried over a fire)　baizhi (angelica dahurica; half a liang each)　fragrant ink (burned, quenched in wine, finely ground)　shexiang (musk; ground; 1 *qian* each)　fushen (poria with root; wood removed; half a liang)　chuanwu, 2 liang (Sichuan aconite; soaked in river water for half a month, sliced, dried over a fire and stir-fried with salt)　tianma (gastrodia; sprouts removed)　xianlingpi leaves (epimedium; washed and dried over a fire; half a liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder, add the [ground] medicines and grind evenly, and knead with refined honey to the consistency of cooked wheat. Smear the pestle and mortar with genuine ghee and pound ten thousand times; if it is dry, add honey as needed until right, and knead into a mass. For each dose roll pills the size of wutong seeds and dissolve two pills in fresh mint juice with warm wine. For sudden wind-strike with surging drool and clouded obstruction, decoct soap-pod and alum decoction, let it cool to lukewarm, dissolve four pills and pour them in. For paralytic wind take three to five pills daily, and the effect will gradually be felt; for regular use one pill. To disperse phlegm and free the diaphragm, take with warm wine after meals. For children's fright wind, add a little calomel and dissolve half a pill in mint juice; the effect is immediate. To treat scrofula, take one pill with scallion decoction. Avoid things that stir wind and toxic foods.

## Notes

Toubing dan: for wind toxin attacking upward (swelling and itching of the head and face) or pouring downward (heavy, swollen, ulcerated legs and constipation), and for paralysis after wind-strike.
- Rhubarb and gardenia drain heat downward; aconite, clematis, gastrodia and angelica dahurica dispel wind; musk opens the orifices.
- The Siku quanshu edition reads 口苦 (bitter mouth) for 口舌 and has the ink quenched in vinegar (see the variants).
