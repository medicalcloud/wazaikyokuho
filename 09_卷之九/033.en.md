# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 033 四順理中丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Sishun lizhong wan (Four Agreements Center-Regulating Pill)

It treats just-delivered women with both blood and qi damaged, sudden deficiency of the five zang organs, emaciated, deficient limbs, scant qi and much sweating. Taken regularly every day from delivery until the hundredth day, it strengthens qi, supplements deficiency, regulates and nourishes the qi of the zang organs, removes residual illness, aids digestion and improves the appetite.

gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried until slightly red; 2 liang)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng; rhizome head removed)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried)　baizhu (byakujutsu; white atractylodes; 1 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with refined honey. Take thirty pills each time with warm rice water, on an empty stomach before meals.

## Notes

Sishun lizhong wan: for damaged blood and qi, sudden deficiency of the five zang organs, wasting, breathlessness and sweating just after childbirth; regular use for the first hundred days is recommended.
- Lizhong wan (Richugan) (ginseng, atractylodes, dried ginger, licorice) with doubled licorice.
