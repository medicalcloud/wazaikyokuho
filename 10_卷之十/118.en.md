# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 118 鉄刷湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Tieshua tang (Iron Brush Decoction)

It treats disharmony of stomach qi with pain of the heart and abdomen; excessive drinking with retching and nausea; spleen pain and stomach reflux; wind-cold contracted internally with borborygmus and diarrhea; and stabbing pain of blood and qi in women.

xiangfuzi (cyperus; 6 liang)　jiegeng (kikyo; platycodon; 1½ jin)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; 1 jin)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; half a jin)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed; 4 liang)　huixiang (fennel; half a jin)　liangjiang (lesser galangal)　chenpi (chinpi; aged tangerine peel; 12 liang each)

Except for the cinnamon, stir-fry all together and make into a fine powder. Take 1 *qian* each time with a little salt, mixed with boiling water. Taken regularly it refreshes qi; at any time.

## Notes

Tieshua tang (from here the layer of bureau additions): a drink for abdominal pain from disharmonious stomach qi, retching and nausea after excess drinking, spleen pain and stomach reflux, borborygmus and diarrhea from wind-cold, and stabbing blood-qi pain in women.
- Mainly platycodon; cyperus and tangerine peel move qi; dried ginger, cinnamon, fennel and lesser galangal warm; licorice is added.
