# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 042 安胎飲 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Antai yin (Fetus-Calming Beverage)

It treats all of the following: morning sickness from the third or fourth month to the ninth month of pregnancy, with confused oppression in the chest, a heavy head and blurred vision, heavy limbs, lassitude and unwillingness to work, aversion to the smell of food, a craving for salty and sour things, much sleeping and little getting up, and vomiting with inability to eat; or a restless fetus moving out of season, pain of the lower back and abdomen, or occasional bleeding; and all illnesses of pregnancy. (Another formula appears later.)

diyu (chiyu; sanguisorba)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; lightly roasted until red)　fuling (bukuryo; poria; skin removed)　shu gan dihuang (kanjio; prepared rehmannia; washed, sprinkled with wine, steamed, dried over a fire)　danggui (toki; Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed, washed, soaked in wine)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage)　baizhu (byakujutsu; white atractylodes)　banxia (hange; pinellia; washed seven times in hot water)　ejiao (akyo; donkey-hide gelatin; pounded, stir-fried with bran)　huangqi (ogi; astragalus; shoots removed)　bai shaoyao (shakuyaku; white peony root; equal parts)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water, decoct down to eight-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm, at any time. If there is aversion to food, simply give whatever she craves as she pleases, and she will certainly recover. According to the prohibitions in pregnancy: do not eat chicken or duck eggs, raw carp slices, rabbit, dog, donkey, mule or goat meat, fish roe, turtle eggs, pheasant, sparrow or mulberries. Further, the *Taijiao lun* (Treatise on Prenatal Education) says: "Let the mother always dwell in a quiet room, hear many fine words, and listen to people expounding the *Odes* and *Documents* and discoursing on rites and music. If her ears hear no improper words, her eyes see no pathogen things and her heart raises no wicked thoughts, the child born will be generous and sturdy, blessed with fortune and longevity, loyal, filial, humane and righteous, intelligent and free of illness." This is a teaching left by the sages, and so it is written down following the formula.

## Notes

Antai yin (Baoqing-era additions): for morning sickness from the third to the ninth month (chest oppression, heavy head, dizziness, lassitude, aversion to food smells, cravings for salty and sour things, vomiting), for a restless fetus with back and abdominal pain or bleeding, and for all illnesses of pregnancy. It appends a list of foods forbidden in pregnancy and the teaching of the *Taijiao lun* on prenatal education.
- Siwu tang (Shimotsuto) (prepared rehmannia, angelica, lovage, white peony), donkey-hide gelatin and sanguisorba nourish blood and stop bleeding; astragalus, atractylodes, poria and licorice supplement; pinellia stops vomiting. A formula of the same name appears later.
