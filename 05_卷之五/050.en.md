# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 050 遠志丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yuanzhi wan (Polygala Pill)

It governs all cases in men and women of insufficient heart qi and deficient, damaged kidney channel from excessive thought and anxiety, with an abstracted spirit, forgetfulness and frequent fright, restless sleep, depleted, failing blood and qi, dribbling and seminal emission, white turbid urine, deficiency sweating and night sweats, and deafness or ringing in the ears.

yuanzhi (polygala; core removed, stir-fried with ginger juice)　muli (oyster shell; calcined, powder taken; 2 liang each)　bai fuling (white poria; skin removed)　renshen (ginseng)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried)　chensha (cinnabar; ground separately; 1 liang each)　roucongrong (cistanche; washed clean, sliced, dried over a fire; 4 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with refined honey. Take thirty pills each time on an empty stomach before meals with decocted rush-pith and salt decoction; warm wine is also fine. This medicine is warm and non-toxic; taken regularly it supplements and benefits the heart and kidneys, sharpens the ears and eyes, settles the will, calms the spirit and nourishes qi and blood.

## Notes

Yuanzhi wan (bureau additions): for abstraction, forgetfulness, fright, insomnia, seminal emission, turbid urine, sweating, night sweats, tinnitus and deafness from insufficient heart qi and damaged kidney channel through excessive thought.
- Polygala and cinnabar calm the heart-spirit; ginseng and poria supplement; cistanche and dried ginger warm the kidneys; oyster shell astringes essence and sweat.
