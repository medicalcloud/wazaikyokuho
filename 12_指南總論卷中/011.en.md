# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 011 論諸風瘙癢癮疹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On itching and hives in the various winds. They always arise from blood and qi not flowing smoothly, with twitching of the face as if insects were crawling. For congealed, stagnant blood and qi, give Paifeng tang, Huma san (Sesame Powder), Xiaofeng san (Wind-Dispersing Powder) or Sisheng wan. For severe cases, give much Zaojia jian wan or Heshouwu wan.

## Notes

On itching and hives in the various winds (blood and qi not flowing smoothly): Paifeng tang, Huma san, Xiaofeng san and Sisheng wan; for severe cases Zaojia jian wan and Heshouwu wan.
