# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 027 茯苓丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Fuling wan (Bukuryogan; Poria Pill)

It treats obstruction illness (morning sickness) in pregnancy with vexing confusion in the chest, dizzy, heavy head and eyes, aversion to the smell of food, vomiting counterflow with oppression, tossing about restlessly, and weary, weak limbs unable to support oneself. Taking this medicine regularly disperses phlegm and water, enables eating, strengthens and nourishes the fetus. One should first take Banxia fuling tang (Hangebukuryoto) and then this medicine.

gegen (kakkon; pueraria)　zhishi (kijitsu; unripe bitter orange; pulp removed, stir-fried yellow with bran)　baizhu (byakujutsu; white atractylodes)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried; 2 liang each)　chi fuling (bukuryo; red poria; skin removed)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　chenpi (chinpi; aged tangerine peel)　banxia (hange; pinellia; washed ten times in hot water to remove the sliminess, sliced, dried over a fire; 1 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder, knead with refined honey and form pills the size of wutong seeds. Take thirty pills each time with warm rice water on an empty stomach, before meals.

## Notes

Fuling wan: for morning sickness with chest vexation, dizziness, aversion to food smells, vomiting, restlessness and weakness of the limbs; taken after Banxia fuling tang.
- Pinellia, red poria, tangerine peel and unripe bitter orange remove phlegm and water; ginseng, atractylodes, licorice, dried ginger and cinnamon warm and supplement the spleen and stomach; pueraria is added.
