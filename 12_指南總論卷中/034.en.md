# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 034 論傷寒嘔逆 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On vomiting counterflow in cold damage. Vomiting counterflow in cold damage has three patterns: for vomiting counterflow from cold in the stomach, give Shen ling baizhu san, Wuling san or Si junzi tang; when there is also heat in the stomach, with thirst and dry mouth or red, rough urine, give Xiao chaihu tang or Wuling san—Xiao chaihu tang is extremely good at stopping heat vomiting; for shortness of qi with vomiting, Zhuye tang is immediately effective.

## Notes

On vomiting in cold damage, three patterns: stomach cold (Shen ling baizhu san, Wuling san, Si junzi tang); stomach heat with thirst or dark rough urine (Xiao chaihu tang or Wuling san; Xiao chaihu tang is excellent for heat vomiting); scant qi with vomiting (Zhuye tang).
