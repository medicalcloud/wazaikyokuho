# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 080 烏薬順気散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wuyao shunqi san (Lindera Qi-Smoothing Powder)

It treats all wind qi in men and women attacking and pouring into the four limbs, with pain in the joints, stubborn numbness all over the body and spinning dizziness of the head and eyes; it also treats paralysis with halting, sluggish speech and cramped sinews and vessels. It also treats leg qi with difficulty walking and weak legs and knees; and women's blood wind, and cold qi in the elderly rising to attack the chest, with stabbing pain in both flanks, distension of the heart and abdomen, and vomiting, diarrhea and borborygmus.

mahuang (mao; ephedra; roots and nodes removed)　chenpi (chinpi; aged tangerine peel; pulp removed)　wuyao (lindera; wood removed; 2 liang each)　bai jiangcan (silkworm; silk and mouth removed, stir-fried)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage)　zhiqiao (bitter orange; pulp removed, stir-fried with bran)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; stir-fried)　baizhi (byakushi; angelica dahurica)　jiegeng (kikyo; platycodon; 1 liang each)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried; half a liang)

Make the above into a fine powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one *zhan* of water, three slices of ginger and one jujube, decoct down to seven-tenths and take it warm. For seasonal cold damage with aversion to cold, vigorous fever, headache and weary limbs, add three *cun* of scallion white, decoct together and take it; sweating shows the effect. For body pain from a sprain, mix it in warm wine. For itching all over that turns into sores when scratched, decoct it with three mint leaves. Pregnant women must not take it. Taken regularly it disperses wind and smooths qi.

## Notes

Wuyao shunqi san (bureau additions): for joint pain, numbness and dizziness from wind qi in the limbs, paralysis and speech disorder, leg qi, women's blood wind, and chest and flank pain, abdominal distension and vomiting and diarrhea from cold qi in the elderly. It is known as a qi-smoothing formula for the early stage of wind stroke.
- Lindera, tangerine peel, bitter orange and platycodon smooth qi; ephedra, angelica dahurica, lovage and silkworm scatter wind; dried ginger warms. Contraindicated in pregnancy.
