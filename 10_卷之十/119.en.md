# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 119 快湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Kuai tang (Refreshing Drink)

It is greatly suitable for deficient, cold spleen and stomach and damage by wine and food with discomfort of the chest and diaphragm, vomiting counterflow and nausea, acid swallowing and spitting of water, a bland mouth and rough tongue, and no desire for food.

gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried; 18 liang)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried; 2½ jin)　sumi (awabei; millet; stir-fried; 30 liang)　jiegeng (kikyo; platycodon; stir-fried; 3 jin)

Powder the above finely together with 120 *qian* by weight of stir-fried salt. Take 1 *qian* each time mixed with boiling water, before meals.

## Notes

Kuai tang (bureau additions): a drink for chest discomfort, vomiting, nausea, acid reflux, watery regurgitation, bland taste and poor appetite from deficient, cold spleen and stomach or damage by wine and food.
- Platycodon, millet, dried ginger, licorice and salt.
