# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 033 桃花丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taohua wan (Tokagan; Peach Blossom Pill)

It treats weak, deficient intestines and stomach ridden by cold qi with churning pain of the navel and abdomen and dysentery of pure white matter; or cold and heat contending with red and white mixed, slippery, uncontrollable intestines, without limit day and night.

chi shizhi (red halloysite)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried; equal parts)

Powder the above and form pills the size of wutong seeds with flour paste made with water. Take thirty pills each time, swallowed with warm rice water, on an empty stomach before meals, three times a day.

## Notes

Taohua wan: for umbilical pain and white dysentery when cold qi rides on weak intestines and stomach, and for red and white dysentery with slippery, uncontrollable intestines from cold and heat.
- Two ingredients, red halloysite and dried ginger (the *Shanghan lun* Taohua tang (Tokato) without the rice, made into pills).
