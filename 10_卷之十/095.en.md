# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 095 木香湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Muxiang tang (Costus Root Drink)

It treats all qi disorders: glomus and blockage of the chest and diaphragm, stabbing pain of the heart and abdomen, distension and fullness of the flanks and ribs, reduced eating, belching and acid swallowing, and vomiting counterflow with choking oppression.

muxiang (costus root)　qingpi (green tangerine peel; 3 jin each)　jianghuang (turmeric)　maibai (barley sprout [?]; stir-fried; 5 jin each)　gancao (licorice; stir-fried)　yan (salt; stir-fried; 11 jin each)　pengzhu (zedoary; 4 jin)

Powder the above. Take 1 *qian* each time mixed with boiling water, at any time.

## Notes

Muxiang tang ("Various drinks"): a drink for chest glomus, stabbing abdominal pain, flank distension, poor appetite, belching, acid reflux, vomiting and choking—all qi disorders.
- Costus, green tangerine peel, turmeric and zedoary move qi; "maibai" (probably barley sprout), licorice and salt are added.
