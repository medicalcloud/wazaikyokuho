# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 025 霊液丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Lingye dan (Numinous Fluid Elixir)

It treats all wind-heat and accumulated heat of the viscera and bowels with toxic qi attacking upward, vexation and agitation of the chest and diaphragm, dry, rough mouth and tongue, congestion and oppression of the heart-spirit, blockage of the throat and tasteless food.

wumei (mume fruit; stones removed, stir-fried)　hanshuishi (calcite; calcined, ground and water-ground)　gualougen (trichosanthes root)　shigao (gypsum; ground)　gegen (pueraria)　chi fuling (red poria; 1 liang each)　maimendong (ophiopogon; core removed, dried over a fire; 1½ liang)　longnao (borneol; ground separately; 1 *qian*)

Pound and sift the above into a powder, mix in the ground medicine evenly and form pills the size of a pellet with refined honey. Wrap one pill in thin silk floss, hold it in the mouth to dissolve and swallow the saliva.

## Notes

Lingye dan (bureau additions): for chest agitation, dry mouth, oppression of the heart-spirit, throat blockage and tasteless food from wind-heat and accumulated heat of the viscera.
- Gypsum, calcite, trichosanthes root, pueraria and ophiopogon clear heat and generate fluids; mume stops thirst; red poria and borneol are added. Dissolved in the mouth.
