# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 106 麝香蘇合香丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shexiang suhexiang wan (Jakosokokogan; Musk Storax Pill)

The formula is the same as the earlier Suhexiang wan (Sokokogan), only without the naozi (borneol).

## Notes

Shexiang suhexiang wan (bureau additions): Suhexiang wan (juan 3, no. 001) without borneol; this is the entry referred to in juan 3, no. 001 ("the formula for Shexiang suhexiang wan appears later").
