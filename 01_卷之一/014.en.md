# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 014 銀液丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yinye dan (Silver Liquid Elixir)

It is suitable for all kinds of wind with phlegm and drool accumulating and binding, fullness and oppression of the heart and diaphragm, headache and dizziness, a hot face and palpitation, thick sticky phlegm and spittle and a clouded, confused spirit; as well as wind epilepsy with paroxysmal convulsions and surging drool with clouded obstruction.

heiqian (black lead; refined ten times, weigh out 3 liang, fuse with the mercury into "sand", divide into small lumps, boil for half a day in water with 10 liang of licorice, let cool, take out and grind for use)　tiefen (iron powder)　shuiyin (suigin; mercury; fused into "sand"; 3 liang each)　zhusha (cinnabar; ground and water-ground; half a liang)　tiannanxing (arisaema; blast-fried and powdered; 3 *fen*)　nifen (calomel; ground; 1 liang)

Grind the above together evenly and form pills the size of wutong seeds with flour paste. Take two pills each time with mint-honey decoction; ginger decoction is also fine. A mild purge is the measure; take after meals. For wind epilepsy, take at any time.

## Notes

Yinye dan: a heavy-mineral formula for sinking phlegm, for bound phlegm with chest oppression, dizziness and headache, and for attacks of wind epilepsy.
- It contains lead fused with mercury, iron powder, cinnabar and calomel—much mercury and lead—and is highly toxic; a mild purge carries the phlegm down.
