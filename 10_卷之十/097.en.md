# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 097 破気湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Poqi tang (Qi-Breaking Drink)

It treats all cold qi attacking the heart, abdomen, flanks and ribs with distension, fullness and stabbing pain, belching and acid swallowing, vomiting counterflow and nausea, blockage of the chest and diaphragm by belching, and reduced eating.

qingpi (green tangerine peel; white not removed)　chenpi (chinpi; aged tangerine peel; white not removed)　huixiang (fennel; picked over, stir-fried; 12 liang each)　xingren (kyonin; apricot kernel; skin and tips removed, stir-fried with bran, pounded separately)　guixin (cinnamon heart; 1 jin each)　liangjiang (lesser galangal; stir-fried)　jianghuang (turmeric)　bichengqie (cubeb)　muxiang (mokko; costus root; 6 liang each)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; stir-fried; 8½ jin)　yan (salt; stir-fried; 14 jin)　dingxiang pi (clove bark; 9 liang)

Powder the above. Take 1 *qian* each time mixed with boiling water, before meals.

## Notes

Poqi tang ("Various drinks"): a drink for distension, stabbing pain, belching, acid reflux, vomiting, chest blockage and poor appetite when cold qi attacks the heart, abdomen and flanks.
- Green and aged tangerine peel, costus and turmeric move qi; fennel, lesser galangal, cubeb, cinnamon heart and clove bark warm; apricot kernel, licorice and salt are added.
- Emendation: "huixiang" (fennel) reads "huixiang" with a different character in the base text; emended following the Siku quanshu and Executive Yuan editions.
