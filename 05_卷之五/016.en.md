# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 016 玉霜丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yushuang wan (Jade Frost Pill)

It treats deficient, exhausted true qi with damaged, depleted lower burner, bowstring tension of the navel and abdomen, soft painful lower back and legs, a weary spirit, a withered haggard complexion, or blood loss and night sweats, dribbling and loss of essence, spontaneous diarrhoea, slippery frequent urination, wasted flesh and impotence. Taken for a long time it rejoins bones and connects sinews, locks in essence and hardens marrow, prolongs life and preserves it, drives off old age and restores youth, calms the ethereal and corporeal souls, renews the skin and retains qi, lightens the body and strengthens yang, adds to one's years and keeps one in the world.

tianxiong (aconite tuber; 10 liang, long large ones; after soaking in wine for seven days, dig a pit in the ground and burn it red-hot with half a *cheng* of charcoal; quickly remove the charcoal and clean it, pour 2 *sheng* of vinegar into the pit and wait until it soaks in; while still hot, put the aconite tubers in, cover with a basin banked with earth, take them out the next day and remove skin and navel)　cishi (magnetite; quenched in vinegar seven times, more is better)　zhusha (cinnabar; water-ground)　zexie (alisma; washed, soaked overnight in wine, roasted)　niuxi (achyranthes; sprouts removed, soaked in wine, dried over a fire)　shihu (dendrobium; roots removed, roasted)　congrong (cistanche; skin removed, soaked overnight in wine, roasted dry)　baji (morinda; pieces with the core pierced through; 2 liang each)　huixiang (fennel; stir-fried)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed; 1 liang each)　jiajiuzi (garden chive seed; lightly stir-fried)　tusizi (dodder seed; soaked in wine for one day and night, steamed, sun-dried, pounded and sieved to powder, the light floating parts removed; 5 liang each)　muli (oyster shell; strongly calcined, pounded to powder)　zishaohua (freshwater sponge; if lacking, use scouring rush instead; 3 liang each)　lurong (deer antler velvet; use velvet still attached to the skull bone, first singe off the hair cleanly, cut into lengths of about 3 *cun*, soak in wine for one day and night and roast over a gentle fire until brittle; half a liang)　bai longgu (white dragon bone; 1 jin, the kind that sticks to the tongue, ground fine as powder, water-ground three times and sun-dried, steamed for one day and night with 1 *dou* of black soybeans, put in a double silk bag and sun-dried)

Pound and sieve these sixteen ingredients into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with refined wine and honey in equal parts. Take thirty pills each time on an empty stomach and before the evening meal with warm wine. Taken regularly it supplements the true qi and strengthens the yang way.

## Notes

Yushuang wan: a kidney-supplementing, yang-strengthening formula for tension of the navel and abdomen, soft painful legs, fatigue, haggard complexion, blood loss, night sweats, seminal emission, diarrhoea, frequent urination, wasting and impotence from deficient true qi and an exhausted lower burner.
- Aconite tuber (processed in a vinegar-soaked fire pit), deer antler velvet, cistanche, morinda, dodder, chive seed, freshwater sponge, fennel and cinnamon supplement kidney yang; dragon bone, oyster shell, magnetite and cinnabar secure essence and calm the spirit; achyranthes, dendrobium and alisma are added. The claimed effects show a strong flavour of longevity practices.
