# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 046 油調立効散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Youtiao lixiao san (Oil-Mixed Immediate-Effect Powder)

It treats damp scabies spreading and soaking, flowing and ulcerating over the whole body, the large ones forming blisters that release fluid when scratched, the small ones like millet grains, with unbearable itching and pain, damp, moist skin, unhealed over a long time.

nifen (calomel)　lüfan (melanterite)　huangbo (phellodendron; lightly roasted, finely ground)　liuhuang (sulphur; finely ground; equal parts)

Make the above into a fine powder, grind evenly, mix with raw oil and apply.

## Notes

Youtiao lixiao san (Wu Zhige's additions): for spreading damp scabies over the body (blisters, oozing, severe itching and pain), long unhealed.
- Calomel, melanterite, phellodendron and sulphur mixed with oil and applied externally.
