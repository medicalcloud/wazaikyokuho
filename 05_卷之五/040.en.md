# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 040 四神丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Sishen dan (Four Spirits Elixir)

It treats the hundred illnesses, supplements the five zang organs, keeps epidemics at a distance, drives off miasma, removes corpse infixation and gu toxin, and wards off ghosts, goblins and evil qi. It greatly treats men and women with deficient, damaged true origin, deficient, injured essence and marrow, an emaciated form and scant qi, fullness in the middle with deficiency below, so that water and fire do not interact and yin and yang lose their order, with a weary spirit, a withered haggard complexion, blood loss and night sweats, dribbling and loss of essence, spontaneous diarrhea, slippery frequent urination, fright and fear in dreams, impotence, heaviness of the lower back and thighs and cramping of the sinews and vessels; and it treats all deep-lying cold and chronic chill, strings, aggregations, mounting and conglomerations, wringing pain of the navel and abdomen, long-standing diarrhea and dysentery, and yin patterns of cold damage with a deep, feeble pulse, cool body with spontaneous sweating and cold limbs. For women's hundred illnesses—long-standing cold of the womb, cessation of pregnancy and childlessness, red and white vaginal discharge and irregular menses unhealed by long use of other medicines—it governs them all. This elixir borrows the creative power of yin and yang and obtains the balanced qi of heaven and earth; its effect differs from that of ordinary overbearing, drying elixirs calcined and refined only once. This elixir quickens the blood and fills the marrow, calms the ethereal and corporeal souls, gladdens and moistens the complexion, lightens the body and preserves life. If one does not rely on the force of the medicine to indulge one's passions, taking it for a long time can lead to the way of the immortals.

xionghuang (yuo; realgar)　cihuang (shio; orpiment)　liuhuang (sulfur)　zhusha (cinnabar; 5 liang each)

Grind the above medicines finely, put them into a porcelain box, seal it with powdered verbena and salt clay, and calcine it surrounded on all sides by a gentle fire for one day and one night; take it out, grind it again into a fine powder, grind glutinous rice dumplings into a paste and form pills the size of beans. Take one pill each time very early on an empty stomach, swallowed with freshly drawn water. Pregnant women must not take it. Avoid sheep's blood and mallow.

## Notes

Sishen dan (Wu Zhige's additions): claimed to treat the hundred illnesses—deficiency of the true origin with deficient essence and marrow, deep-lying cold, mounting, chronic diarrhea and dysentery, yin-pattern cold damage with cold limbs, and women's infertility, discharge and irregular menses—and even to lead to immortality with long use.
- An alchemical elixir of four minerals (realgar, orpiment, sulfur, cinnabar—compounds of arsenic, mercury and sulfur) sealed and calcined for a day and night; extremely toxic. Contraindicated in pregnancy. Not used today.
