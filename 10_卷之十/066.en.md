# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 066 人参散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Renshen san (Ninjinsan; Ginseng Powder)

It treats fever in cold damage in children. Taken regularly it regulates and smooths yin and yang, harmonizes and nourishes the spleen and stomach, settles vomiting and stops vexing thirst. The ingredients are the same as in the earlier Renshen san.

Make the above into a powder. For a three-year-old, take 1 *qian* each time with a small *zhan* of water, decoct down to five-tenths and take it warm, adjusted for the child's size.

## Notes

Renshen san (Wu Zhige's additions): for fever in cold damage in children; regular use harmonizes the spleen and stomach and stops vomiting and thirst. The ingredients are said to be the same as the earlier Renshen san (juan 10, no. 032), with a different dosage given.
