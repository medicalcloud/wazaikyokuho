# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 058 高良薑散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Gao liangjiang san (Koryokyosan; Lesser Galangal Powder)

It treats children damaged by cold with disharmony of the spleen and stomach, abdominal distension with qi oppression, and no desire for food.

gao liangjiang (lesser galangal)　cao doukou (katsumada galangal seed; husk removed)　chenpi (chinpi; aged tangerine peel; white pith removed)　danggui (toki) (Chinese angelica; lightly stir-fried)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed; 1 *fen* each)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng; rhizome head removed; half a liang)

Pound and sift the above into a powder. For a three-year-old, take 1 *qian* each time with one *zhan* of water, decoct down to five-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm, at any time, adjusted for the child's size.

## Notes

Gao liangjiang san (Wu Zhige's additions): for disharmony of the spleen and stomach, abdominal distension, qi oppression and poor appetite from cold damage in children.
- Lesser galangal, katsumada galangal seed and cinnamon warm; tangerine peel moves qi; ginseng and angelica supplement.
