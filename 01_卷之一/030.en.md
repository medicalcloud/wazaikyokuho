# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 030 小続命湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xiao xuming tang (Shozokumeito; Minor Life-Prolonging Decoction)

It treats sudden wind stroke with loss of consciousness, gradually noticed loss of control of half the body, deviation of the mouth and eyes, trembling of the hands and feet, halting and sluggish speech, numbness of the limbs, a disordered spirit, dizziness and heaviness of the head and eyes, much phlegm and drool, cramping of the sinews and vessels with inability to bend and stretch, vexing pain of the joints and inability to turn over; it also treats all kinds of wind, and taking it always shows effect. For leg qi with slackness and weakness [of the legs], taking it for a long time brings recovery. Those long ill with wind should take it in advance whenever the weather turns dark and overcast or the season changes, to prevent loss of voice.

fangji (boi; stephania)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　huangqin (ogon; scutellaria)　xingren (kyonin; apricot kernel; skin and tips removed, stir-fried yellow)　shaoyao (shakuyaku; peony root; the white kind)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; scorched)　xiongqiong (ligusticum)　mahuang (mao; ephedra; roots and nodes removed)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng; rhizome head removed; 1 liang each)　fangfeng (bofu; saposhnikovia; rhizome head removed; 1½ liang)　fuzi (bushi; aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed; half a liang)

Except for the aconite and apricot kernels, pound the above into a coarse powder, then add those two and mix evenly. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water and five slices of fresh ginger, decoct to obtain one *zhan*, remove the dregs and take it fairly hot, before meals. Adding one jujube is even better.

## Notes

Xiao xuming tang: the classic formula for wind stroke with hemiplegia, facial deviation, speech disorder, trembling and numbness, also used for leg qi.
- Like the Xiao xuming tang of the *Qianjin fang*, it scatters exterior wind with ephedra, saposhnikovia, stephania and apricot kernel, warms with aconite and cinnamon, regulates qi and blood with ginseng, peony, lovage and licorice, and clears heat with scutellaria.
- It is to be taken in advance at changes of weather to prevent loss of voice.
