# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 033 論傷寒発渇 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On thirst in cold damage. When by the fifth to seventh day of cold damage there is severe thirst, or fever without aversion to cold, normal stools and red urine, this is deficient agitation in the stomach; give Wuling san (Goreisan), or in severe cases Zhuye tang (Chikuyoto; Bamboo Leaf Decoction). For fever with thirst, give Chaihu tang (Saikoto) with trichosanthes added to the decoction.

## Notes

On thirst in cold damage: severe thirst on days five to seven (fever without chills, normal stools, dark urine) is deficient agitation of the stomach, treated with Wuling san or, if severe, Zhuye tang; Chaihu tang with trichosanthes for fever with thirst.
