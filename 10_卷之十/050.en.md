# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 050 至聖保命丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zhisheng baoming dan (Supreme Sage Life-Protecting Elixir)

It treats fetal fright and internal hanging in children, with a hard, firm abdomen, eyes turned upward, twitching of the hands and feet and arched-back rigidity. It treats without exception all cases of exuberant congestion of drool and phlegm and all acute and chronic fright wind.

quanxie (whole scorpion; 14)　bai fuzi (bushi; typhonium tuber)　tiannanxing (arisaema; blast-fried)　bai jiangcan (silkworm; straight, green ones, stir-fried)　zhusha (cinnabar; ground)　shexiang (jako; musk; ground; 1 *qian* each)　fangfeng (bofu; saposhnikovia; rhizome head and forks removed)　tianma (gastrodia; 2 *qian* each)　jinbo (gold leaf; ten leaves)　chantui (cicada slough; mud removed; 1 *qian*)

Make the above into a fine powder, mix in the ground medicines evenly, cook non-glutinous rice, take the soft center of the rice and knead into pills, forty to each liang. For a newborn, half a pill dissolved in breast milk; for a one-year-old, one pill dissolved in gold-and-silver mint decoction; for children over ten with acute signs, two pills dissolved in mint decoction. Taken regularly it settles the heart, calms the spirit, transforms phlegm and removes all signs of fright wind.

## Notes

Zhisheng baoming dan (Baoqing-era additions): for fetal fright and internal hanging in children (hard abdomen, upward gaze, twitching limbs, opisthotonus), exuberant phlegm and all acute and chronic fright wind; usable from birth.
- Scorpion, typhonium, arisaema, silkworm, saposhnikovia, gastrodia and cicada slough dispel wind and phlegm; cinnabar, musk and gold leaf settle.
