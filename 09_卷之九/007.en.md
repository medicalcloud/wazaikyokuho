# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 007 熟乾地黄散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shu gan dihuang san (Prepared Rehmannia Powder)

It treats women with taxation damage of blood and qi and deficient, damaged bowels and zang organs, wind-cold pathogenic qi taking advantage of deficiency to lodge and contend, vexing pain of the limbs, a clouded, heavy head and eyes, frequent fright palpitations, chills and fever with night sweats, emaciation with little strength, and inability to eat.

danshen (tanjin; salvia root; rhizome head removed)　fangfeng (bofu; saposhnikovia; rhizome head and forks removed)　danggui (toki; Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed, lightly stir-fried)　xixin (saishin; asarum; shoots removed)　gaoben (kohon; Chinese lovage; rhizome head removed, washed)　xiongqiong (Sichuan lovage; half a liang each)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng)　shu gan dihuang (kanjio; prepared rehmannia; sprinkled with wine, steamed, dried over a fire)　bai fuling (bukuryo; white poria; skin removed)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　baizhu (byakujutsu; white atractylodes; 1 liang each)　xuduan (zokudan; dipsacus)　fuzi (bushi; aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　huangqi (ogi; astragalus; rhizome head removed; 3 *fen* each)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 4 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water, half a *fen* of fresh ginger and three jujubes broken open, decoct down to one *zhan*, strain off the dregs and take it warm before meals.

## Notes

Shu gan dihuang san: for limb pain, heavy head and eyes, fright palpitations, chills and fever, night sweats, wasting, weakness and poor appetite when wind-cold rides on taxation damage of blood and qi and deficient zang organs in women.
- Prepared rehmannia, angelica, lovage, ginseng, atractylodes, poria, astragalus and dipsacus supplement qi and blood; salvia moves blood; aconite and cinnamon warm; saposhnikovia, asarum and Chinese lovage dispel wind.
