# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 081 熟乾地黄湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shu gan dihuang tang (Prepared Rehmannia Decoction)

It treats unceasing thirst from postpartum deficiency, with scant qi and weak legs, dim eyes and dizziness, and tasteless food.

shu gan dihuang (kanjio; prepared rehmannia; washed clean, soaked in wine, steamed, dried over a fire; 1 liang)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng; 3 liang)　maimendong (bakumondo; ophiopogon; core removed; 2 liang)　gualougen (karokon; trichosanthes root; 1 liang)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried; half a liang)

Cut the above into a powder. For each dose take 4 *qian* with two *zhan* of water, a pinch of glutinous rice, three slices of ginger and three jujubes, decoct down to seven-tenths, remove the dregs and take it before meals.

## Notes

Shu gan dihuang tang (bureau additions): for unceasing thirst, breathlessness, weak legs, dim vision, dizziness and poor appetite from postpartum deficiency.
- Ginseng and prepared rehmannia supplement; ophiopogon and trichosanthes root generate fluids and stop thirst; licorice is added; decocted with glutinous rice.
