# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 004 烏犀丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wuxi wan (Black Rhinoceros Horn Pill)

It treats men and women suddenly struck by any kind of wind, with clenched jaws, much phlegm above the diaphragm, or halting, sluggish speech and deviation of the mouth and eyes. Dissolve three pills in a little mint juice and wine each and take them; take again after a while, and there will be an immediate great effect. It also treats paralysis, dark wind and epilepsy, paroxysmal convulsions of the hands and feet, unrest of the heart-spirit, vexing numbness all over the body, intestinal wind and haemorrhoidal fistula, and wind toxin of the kidneys attacking upward and pouring downward; and in women, blood wind with spinning head and vomiting, swelling and itching of the skin, and pain all over the body.

All the above fifty-eight ingredients must be processed according to method, pounded and ground fine, combined with refined white honey, ghee added, pounded another five thousand times, and formed into pills the size of wutong seeds. For regular use take one pill at any time, chewed, with mint decoction or tea.

## Notes

Wuxi wan: a large 58-ingredient formula mainly for acute wind-strike (clenched jaws, phlegm, speech disorder, facial deviation), also used for paralysis, epilepsy, intestinal wind and haemorrhoids, and women's blood wind.
- Based on black rhinoceros horn, it combines a wide range of wind-dispelling, phlegm-transforming, orifice-opening and yang-warming drugs, including calcined animal products such as fox liver and crow sealed and burned in a jar.
- The list includes a procedure for turning mercury into "blue mud" with sulphur.
