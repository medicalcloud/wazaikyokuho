# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 051 小安腎丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xiao anshen wan (Minor Kidney-Calming Pill)

It treats all cases of deficient, depleted kidney qi and cold, exhausted lower origin with frequent urination at night, weary limbs, a sense of gradually wasting, heavy lower back and knees, a liking for lying down with little strength, a clouded spirit, cicada-like ringing in the ears, a colourless face, diarrhoea with borborygmus, dim eyes, and decayed, painful teeth.

xiangfuzi (cyperus)　chuanwu (Sichuan aconite)　chuanlianzi (toosendan fruit; 1 jin each of the above, boiled together with 4 liang of salt and 4 sheng of water until dry, then chopped and dried over a fire)　shu gan dihuang (prepared rehmannia; 8 liang)　huixiang (fennel; 12 liang)　chuanjiao (Sichuan pepper; seeds and closed fruits removed, lightly stir-fried to sweat out the oil; 4 liang)

Make these six ingredients into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with wine paste. Take twenty to thirty pills each time on an empty stomach and at bedtime, with salt water or salted wine as preferred. Taken regularly it supplements deficiency and damage and augments the lower origin.

## Notes

Xiao anshen wan (bureau additions): for nocturia, lassitude, wasting, heavy back and knees, tinnitus, poor complexion, diarrhoea, dim vision and toothache from depleted kidney qi and cold lower origin.
- Cyperus, aconite and toosendan boiled in salt water, plus rehmannia, fennel and Sichuan pepper; simpler than the earlier Anshen wan (juan 5, no. 005).
