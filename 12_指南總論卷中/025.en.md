# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 025 論和解証候 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On the signs of harmonizing and resolving. For cold damage or wind damage with alternating fever and chills, pain between the chest and flanks, dry retching and constipation, one packet of Xiao chaihu tang (Shosaikoto) may be used; in severe cases a further half packet brings effect. For those who say they are thirsty or whose urine is rough, give Wuling san (Goreisan) as well. For wind damage on the fourth or fifth day with fever, aversion to cold and a stiff neck but warm hands and feet, and constipation, mostly use Xiao chaihu tang, Baidu san, Qinjiao biejia san (Jingyobekkosan) and the like. When a woman with cold damage, from the third or fifth to the seventh or eighth day, at the time her menses should come or just after they have ended, develops chills and fever and suddenly speaks deliriously as if seeing ghosts and spirits, mild by day and worse at night, this is heat entering the blood chamber; use equal parts of Siwu tang (Shimotsuto) with bupleurum added, decocted and taken. If it does not subside, use Xiao chaihu tang with crushed raw rehmannia added to the decoction. For yang patterns of cold damage with vomiting counterflow and fever, also [use] Shen ling baizhu san (Jinryobyakujutsusan), Hejie san and the like. These are harmonizing and resolving patterns.

## Notes

On harmonizing and resolving patterns: Xiao chaihu tang for alternating fever and chills, chest and flank pain, dry retching and constipation (with Wuling san for thirst or rough urination); Xiao chaihu tang, Baidu san or Qinjiao biejia san for wind damage on days four to five with fever, chills, stiff neck, warm extremities and constipation; for "heat entering the blood chamber" (chills and fever and nocturnal delirium in a woman around menstruation) Siwu tang with bupleurum, or if unresolved Xiao chaihu tang with raw rehmannia; Shen ling baizhu san and Hejie san for yang-pattern vomiting and fever.
- Heat entering the blood chamber and its treatment with Xiao chaihu tang follow the *Shanghan lun*.
