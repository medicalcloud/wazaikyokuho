# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 043 小理中丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xiao lizhong wan (Shorichugan; Minor Middle-Regulating Pill)

It treats weak qi of the three ducts, accumulated cold in the middle jiao, a deficient spleen that does not grind food, slow digestion, frequent damage from eating, fullness and oppression of the chest and diaphragm, wringing, stabbing pain of the flanks and ribs, vomiting and hiccough, belching of sourness and nausea, abdominal distension and borborygmus, pain of the heart and abdomen, choking obstruction of diaphragm qi, stomach reflux with vomiting of food, and reduced eating.

hongdou (galangal fruit)　ezhu (zedoary; roasted, crushed while hot)　suoshaner (amomum; 1 liang each)　caodoukou (Katsumada galangal seed; roasted)　qingpi (green tangerine peel; white pulp removed)　chenpi (chinpi; aged tangerine peel; white pith removed)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried)　jing sanleng (sparganium; roasted, crushed while hot)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed; 2 liang each)　liangjiang (galangal)　qianniu (morning-glory seed; stir-fried until fragrant and cooked; 3 liang each)　awei (asafoetida; dissolved in vinegar, sand and grit removed, ground; 3 liang)

Powder the above and form pills the size of wutong seeds with flour paste boiled in water. Take thirty pills each time with ginger and tangerine-peel decoction; warm water is also fine; at any time. This medicine has no purgative nature and does not harm qi; it is best for those whose spleen and stomach lean to deficiency cold.

## Notes

Xiao lizhong wan (Shaoxing-era additions): for indigestion, chest oppression, flank pain, vomiting, acid reflux, abdominal fullness and pain, diaphragm qi and stomach reflux from accumulated cold in the middle jiao and spleen deficiency.
- Its composition differs from Lizhong wan (Richugan): galangal fruit, Katsumada galangal, galangal, dried ginger and cinnamon warm; sparganium, zedoary, asafoetida and morning-glory seed disperse accumulations; tangerine peels and amomum move qi. It is said to have no purgative action.
