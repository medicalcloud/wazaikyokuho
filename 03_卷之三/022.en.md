# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 022 藿香半夏散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huoxiang banxia san (Agastache and Pinellia Powder)

It treats stomach deficiency with cold in the middle, lodged phlegm and retained fluid, hiccough and vomiting, chest fullness with choking stuffiness, shortness of breath and lassitude, and inability to take food.

dingxiangpi (clove bark; half a liang)　huoxiang leaves (agastache; 1 liang)　banxia (hange; pinellia; soaked and washed seven times in hot water, lightly stir-fried yellow; 2 liang)

Make the above into a powder. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one *zhan* of water and seven slices of fresh ginger, decoct down to seven-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm, before meals.

## Notes

Huoxiang banxia san: for hiccough, vomiting, chest stuffiness, shortness of breath and poor appetite from stomach deficiency with middle cold and lodged phlegm-fluid retention.
- Three ingredients—pinellia, agastache and clove bark—warm the stomach and send phlegm-fluid retention down.
