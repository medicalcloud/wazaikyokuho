# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 074 大聖保命丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Dasheng baoming dan (Great Sage Life-Protecting Elixir)

It treats all wind diseases in men and women: both qi and blood deficient, yin and yang breaking out one-sidedly, sudden wind stroke with stiff prostration and clouded obstruction, surging drool and convulsions, trembling of the feet and hands, loss of consciousness, stiff tongue and loss of voice, limp, dragging hands and feet, deviation of the mouth and eyes, or paralysis with one side withered, hemiplegia, halting and sluggish speech, confused behavior and numbness of the four limbs. It also treats epilepsy with collapse, eyes closed and unable to open, exuberant drool with gurgling, or arched-back rigidity, a fixed staring gaze, clenched mouth with fainting, and clenched jaws. It also treats wind contending with the yang meridians, with dizziness and headache, cicada sounds in the ears, twitching of the skin, frequent yawning and sleepiness, stiff and cramped neck unable to turn; as well as kidney wind deficiency with painful legs and knees and difficulty walking, and one-sided wind flowing into one side so that it cannot bend or stretch—whether long-standing or new, it treats all of these.

The formula is the same as the earlier Dasheng yili jindan.

Make [the ingredients] into a fine powder and mix evenly, knead the medicine with the ink liquid as above, divide each liang into six pills, dry them in a cellar and coat them with gold leaf. For each dose take one pill: grate half a liang of fresh ginger with its skin to obtain its juice and dissolve the pill completely in the ginger juice; warm half a *zhan* of ash-free wine until hot, soak and dissolve [the pill] in it together, and take it warm. Judging the patient's capacity for wine, have him drink a further one or two *sheng* of warm wine to assist the force of the medicine. Then cover him with clothes and bedding and have him lie down at once, until sweat appears. For mild cases take half a pill each time, at any time. Those with wind disease do especially well to take it regularly: it supplements the five zang organs, secures the true origin, frees the joints, drives out pathogenic wind, strengthens the sinews and bones, quickens the blood and preserves the complexion.

## Notes

Dasheng baoming dan (from here the layer "secret formulas of proven experience added from the bureaus"): for all wind diseases—wind stroke, epilepsy, wind in the yang meridians, kidney wind deficiency and one-sided wind.
- Its ingredients are the same as the earlier Dasheng yili jindan (juan 1, no. 053); the pill size and administration differ (dissolved in ginger juice and wine, followed by plenty of wine to induce sweating).
- The base text reads 一、一升 ("one, one *sheng*"); since the Siku quanshu and Executive Yuan editions both read 一、二升, it has been emended (see the emendation).
