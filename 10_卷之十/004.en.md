# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 004 太一銀硃丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyi yinzhu dan (Supreme Unity Silver-Cinnabar Elixir)

It treats fright wind with vigorous fever in children, copious drool with epileptic attacks, convulsions of the hands and feet and eyes turned upward; as well as wind congestion with phlegm excess, fullness and oppression of the heart and diaphragm, vomiting of phlegm and drool, and constipated, rough stools.

heiqian (black lead; refined ten times, 3 liang weighed, made into granules with the mercury, divided into small lumps and boiled half a day in licorice water; when cool, taken out and ground, the licorice discarded)　shuiyin (suigin; mercury; made into granules)　tiefen (iron powder; 3 liang each)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; boiled with the lead; 10 liang)　tiannanxing (arisaema; blast-fried, powdered; 3 *fen*)　zhusha (cinnabar; water-ground; half a liang)　nifen (calomel; ground; 1 liang)

Grind the above together evenly and form pills the size of hemp seeds with flour paste. Give one pill per year of age with mint-honey decoction, aiming for a slight purge; if there is none, give again; after feeding.

## Notes

Taiyi yinzhu dan: for fright wind with high fever, drooling epileptic attacks, convulsions and upward gaze in children, and for wind congestion with phlegm excess, chest oppression, vomiting of phlegm and constipation.
- Lead, mercury, calomel, iron powder, cinnabar and arisaema: a strongly settling and sinking medicine containing lead and mercury; toxic.
