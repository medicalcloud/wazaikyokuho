# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之七 027 硼砂丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Pengsha wan (Hoshagan; Borax Pill)

It treats wind congestion and diaphragm heat in adults and children, with swollen, painful throat, sores of the tongue and cheeks, and dry mouth with vexing thirst.

shexiang (jako; musk; 1 liang, ground)　pengsha (borax; ground)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; steeped for juice and boiled down to a paste; 10 liang each)　yaxiao (refined mirabilite; dehydrated, ground; 2 liang)　meihua nao (plum-blossom borneol; ground separately; 3 *fen*)　hanshuishi (kansuiseki; calcite; burned red-hot; 50 liang)

Powder the above, knead with the licorice paste, and make four hundred pills to each liang. Hold one pill in the mouth to dissolve and swallow the saliva. Taken regularly it transforms phlegm, frees the diaphragm, generates fluids and stops thirst.

## Notes

Pengsha wan: for swollen, painful throat, sores of the tongue and cheeks, dry mouth and thirst from wind congestion and diaphragm heat.
- Calcite, borax and refined mirabilite clear heat; musk and plum-blossom borneol are added; made into tiny pills with licorice paste and dissolved in the mouth.
