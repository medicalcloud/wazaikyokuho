# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 024 真珠散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zhenzhu san (Shinjusan; Pearl Powder)

It is suitable for men and women with accumulated heat in the five zang organs, toxic qi attacking upward, vexation and oppression of the heart and chest, dry mouth and parched tongue, an abstracted spirit, palpitation with oppression and confusion, and restlessness whether sitting or lying.

gualougen (karokon) powder (trichosanthes root)　hupo (amber)　zhenzhu fen (pearl powder)　hanshuishi (kansuiseki; calcite; calcined, quenched in vinegar, ground)　tiefen (iron powder)　zhusha (cinnabar; ground and water-ground)　gancao (kanzo) powder (licorice; raw)　chuan dahuang (daio; Sichuan rhubarb)　yaxiao (refined mirabilite; dehydrated, ground)

Take equal parts of the above, pound each into powder and mix evenly. Take 1 *qian* each time mixed warm in bamboo-leaf decoction, at any time.

## Notes

Zhenzhu san (bureau additions): for vexation of the chest, dry mouth and tongue, abstraction, palpitation and restlessness from accumulated heat in the five zang organs with toxic qi attacking upward.
- Pearl, amber, iron powder and cinnabar settle the heart-spirit; calcite, refined mirabilite, rhubarb and trichosanthes root clear and purge heat; licorice is added.
