# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 057 乳香丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Ruxiang wan (Nyukogan; Frankincense Pill)

It treats all wind diseases with paralysis of the left or right side, deviation of the mouth and eyes, hemiplegia, halting and sluggish speech, abstraction of the spirit, congested phlegm and drool, limp, dragging hands and feet and cramping of the sinews and vessels; or stubborn numbness all over with running pain, slack, weak legs and knees and difficulty walking. It also treats injuries from blows with static blood that will not disperse and unbearable pain; or fatigue and injury from travelling on foot with swollen painful legs and knees; or wind toxin of the kidneys attacking upward with a swollen face and tinnitus, or pouring downward with heavy legs and knees. It treats all of these.

nuomi (glutinous rice; stir-fried)　chuan wutou (uzu) (Sichuan aconite; blast-fried, skin and tips removed)　wulingzhi (flying-squirrel droppings; sand and earth removed; 2 liang each)　ruxiang (frankincense; ground)　baizhi (byakushi; angelica dahurica; chopped)　huoxiang leaves (agastache; washed)　tiannanxing (arisaema; blast-fried)　moyao (myrrh; ground)　jingjie (schizonepeta; twigs and stalks removed)　chixiaodou (sekishozu; rice bean; raw)　gusuibu (drynaria; hairs removed)　baifuzi (typhonium; blast-fried; 1 liang each)　songzhi (matsuyani; pine resin; ground; half a liang)　fragrant ink (calcined)　caowutou (wild aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed; 5 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with flour paste boiled in wine. Take ten to fifteen pills each time, swallowed with cold wine; clear tea is also fine; at any time. Avoid hot things for one double-hour [afterwards].

## Notes

Ruxiang wan (Baoqing-era additions): for nearly the same patterns as the preceding Ruxiang yingtong wan (paralysis and wandering pain from wind, static blood after injury, swollen legs, kidney wind toxin).
- Wild and Sichuan aconite, arisaema and typhonium dispel wind; frankincense, myrrh, flying-squirrel droppings, pine resin and drynaria move the blood and stop pain. It is taken with cold wine, avoiding hot things.
