# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 062 痢聖散子 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Lisheng sanzi (Dysentery Sage Powder)

It treats red and white, intermittent and other dysenteries in men and women, whether of many years' or recent standing. (Another formula appears later.)

danggui (toki; Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried; 2 liang each)　huangbo (obaku) pi (phellodendron bark; coarse bark removed)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; scorched)　zhiqiao (bitter orange; pulp removed)　yumi (that is, poppy seed, of the same nature as the husk)　yingsuqiao (poppy husk; stalk and cap removed; 4 liang each)

Chop the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water and two Chinese chive bulbs broken up, decoct together down to eight-tenths, remove the dregs and take it slightly warm before meals. For the elderly and children adjust the dose. Avoid raw, cold and greasy things.

## Notes

Lisheng sanzi (Wu Zhige's additions): for red and white and intermittent dysentery, recent or of many years' standing.
- Poppy husk and seed astringe; phellodendron clears heat; dried ginger warms; angelica, bitter orange and licorice are added; decocted with Chinese chive bulb. Another Lisheng sanzi follows later (juan 6, no. 081).
