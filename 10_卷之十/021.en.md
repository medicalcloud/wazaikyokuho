# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 021 紫霜丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zishuang wan (Purple Frost Pill)

It treats loss of regularity in nursing and feeding with undigested lodged stagnation, stuffiness and fullness of the chest and abdomen, vomiting and nausea, irregular bowel movements and reduced intake of milk and food. It also treats cold damage with warm, vigorous fever and cold excess harbored within, sour, smelly stools and undigested milk and food, or fever that persists after sweating; as well as fever of the "changes and steamings" (developmental fevers) unresolved for many days, and epilepsy brought on by food with cold first and then heat.

daizheshi (hematite; quenched in vinegar, finely ground; 1 liang)　chi shizhi (red halloysite; powdered; 1 liang)　xingren (kyonin; apricot kernel; skin and tips removed, stir-fried with bran, ground separately; 50)　badou (hazu; croton seed; skin and germ removed, oil expressed, stir-fried and ground; 30 seeds)

Grind the above together evenly and form pills the size of glutinous millet with steamed cake soaked in hot water. Children over thirty days old may take one pill; from one to three years, two or three pills, with breast milk; rice water is also fine. Aim for a slight purge; it does not deplete the child. If there is no purge, give again, adjusting for deficiency or excess; after feeding.

## Notes

Zishuang wan: for lodged stagnation, chest and abdominal stuffiness, vomiting, irregular stools and poor feeding from irregular nursing; for high fever in cold damage with internal cold excess (sour, smelly stools, indigestion, fever persisting after sweating); for "change and steaming" fevers; and for food-induced epilepsy. Usable from thirty days of age.
- Croton seed and apricot kernel purge accumulations; hematite brings down; red halloysite astringes. The same composition as Qian Yi's Zishuang wan.
