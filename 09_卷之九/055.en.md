# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 055 内灸散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Neijiu san (Internal Moxibustion Powder)

It is suitable for all blood disorders of women before and after childbirth: blood flooding with deficiency and exhaustion; gripping pain of the abdomen and flanks; counterflow qi with vomiting; cold blood and cold qi congealing and accumulating into hard lumps with stabbing pain; discharge of green-white or five-coloured matter; deficiency rumbling in the abdomen; qi fullness with hard distension; dripping blood with lumbar pain; spitting of clear water; declining blood from frequent childbirth with a green-yellow complexion; inferiority and weakness from taxation damage; irregular menses; bleeding and miscarriage; blood confusion, blood dizziness and blood conglomerations with intermittent pain; dizziness of the head and eyes; malign blood rising to the heart with fainting and stupor; lochia that does not dry up; a deficient body with much sweating; and counterflow cold of the hands and feet.

huixiang (fennel)　huoxiang (agastache)　dingxiang pi (clove bark)　shu gan dihuang (prepared rehmannia; washed, dried over a fire)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed; 1½ liang each)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried until red)　shanyao (Chinese yam)　danggui (Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed, washed)　baizhu (white atractylodes)　baizhi (angelica dahurica; 8 liang each)　gaoben (Chinese lovage; rhizome head removed)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage)　huangqi (astragalus; shoots removed; 2 liang each)　muxiang (costus root; 1 liang)　chenpi (aged tangerine peel; white pith removed; 4 liang)　bai shaoyao (white peony root; 10 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one large *zhan* of water, add five slices of fresh ginger and a ball of mugwort, decoct together down to seven-tenths and take it hot on an empty stomach before meals; it may also be taken mixed in warm wine. For excessive bleeding after childbirth, decoct with typha pollen. For lochia not flowing freely, add angelica and safflower to the decoction. For watery diarrhoea, add powdered nutmeg to the decoction. For vomiting, add agastache and fresh ginger. For heat above and cold below, add schizonepeta. In general, for deficiency cold in the abdomen with disharmony of blood and qi, it is always suitable. One dose daily after childbirth prevents the hundred illnesses. It is especially suitable for men with deficiency cold, stabbing qi and pain of the heart and abdomen.

## Notes

Neijiu san (bureau additions): for all blood disorders before and after childbirth (flooding, abdominal and flank pain, vomiting, hard lumps of cold blood and qi, discharge, borborygmus, back pain, blood depletion from frequent births, taxation, irregular menses, miscarriage, blood dizziness, conglomerations, malign blood rising to the heart, persistent lochia, sweating, cold limbs); daily postpartum use prevents illness; also for deficiency-cold abdominal pain in men. Modifications are given.
- Angelica, lovage, white peony, prepared rehmannia, astragalus, atractylodes, Chinese yam and licorice supplement qi and blood; dried ginger, cinnamon, fennel and clove bark warm; agastache, costus and tangerine peel move qi; angelica dahurica and Chinese lovage are added; decocted with mugwort ("internal moxibustion").
