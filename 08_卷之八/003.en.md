# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 003 集効丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jixiao wan (Gathered Efficacy Pill)

It treats weak, deficient viscera and bowels, or much eating of sweet and fatty food, leading roundworms to stir, with churning pain of the heart and abdomen, swelling and gathering during attacks, moving up and down, pain that has intervals of rest, vexing heat in the abdomen and spitting of drool and froth from the mouth: this is roundworms biting, and this medicine should be taken. Even if it has not healed for years, taking this cures it. It also treats worms in the lower parts producing itchy, painful piles.

dahuang (rhubarb; chopped, stir-fried) 15 liang　muxiang (costus root; not exposed to fire)　binglang (areca)　helile (chebule; roasted in embers, stone removed, soaked in wine, dried over a fire)　fuzi (aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　qianghuo [notopterygium; stir-fried, ground (one version has wuyi, elm seed paste)]　heshi (carpesium fruit; stir-fried)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried; 10½ liang each)

Powder the above and form pills the size of wutong seeds with refined honey. Take thirty pills each time before meals with tangerine peel decoction; women take them with vinegar decoction.

## Notes

Jixiao wan: for churning abdominal pain when roundworms stir because of weak viscera or rich sweet food (swelling, moving up and down, intermittent, with vexing heat and spitting of froth); also for itchy, painful piles from worms in the lower parts.
- Carpesium and areca (and elm seed paste in one version) kill worms; rhubarb purges; costus, chebule, aconite and dried ginger are added. The note "one version has wuyi" under notopterygium is in the base text.
