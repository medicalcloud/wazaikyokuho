# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 032 神効胡粉丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shenxiao hufen wan (Divinely Effective Lead White Pill)

It is suitable for deficient, slippery intestines and stomach with dysentery without limit, red and white mixed, gripping pain of the navel and abdomen, tenesmus, reduced eating and emaciation, or failure to heal over a long time.

hufen (lead white)　wuzeiyu gu (cuttlefish bone)　ejiao (akyo; donkey-hide gelatin; stir-fried until scorched into beads; 40 liang each)　baifan (alum; calcined)　longgu (ryukotsu; dragon bone; washed; 80 liang each)　mituoseng (litharge; 20 liang)

Powder the above and form pills the size of wutong seeds with cooked millet. Take twenty to thirty pills each time with warm millet water on an empty stomach.

## Notes

Shenxiao hufen wan: for unceasing red and white dysentery, gripping umbilical pain, tenesmus, poor appetite and wasting from deficient, slippery intestines and stomach, including long-unhealed cases.
- Lead white, litharge (lead oxide), alum, dragon bone and cuttlefish bone astringe the intestines; donkey-hide gelatin stops bleeding. It contains lead.
