# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 084 追風応痛丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zhuifeng yingtong wan (Wind-Chasing Pain-Answering Pill)

[It treats] all wind diseases with paralysis of the left or right side, hemiplegia, deviation of the mouth and eyes, clenched jaws, halting and sluggish speech, cramping of the sinews and vessels, pain in all the bones and joints attacking upward and pouring downward, wandering and unsettled, heavy lower back and thighs, tinnitus and hardness of hearing, slack, weak legs and knees with inability to bend and stretch, difficulty walking, numbness all over the body and thickened, stubborn skin. It also treats women's blood wind attacking and pouring, with body pain, puffy face and wasted flesh, bitter mouth and dry tongue, spinning head and dizzy eyes, drowsiness with much sleep; or itchy skin with dormant papules turning into sores; and dark wind combined with head-clamping wind, with one-sided or general headache.

weilingxian (clematis root)　gouji (kuseki; cibotium; hairs removed; 4 liang each)　heshouwu (fleeceflower root)　chuanwu (Sichuan aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed; 6 liang each)　ruxiang (frankincense; ground; 1 liang)　wulingzhi (flying-squirrel droppings; soaked in wine, rinsed free of sand and grit; 5½ liang)

Powder the above and form pills with wine paste. Take fifteen pills each time, increasing to twenty, swallowed with warm wine containing musk; plain warm wine is also fine. Take when the stomach is fairly empty after a meal. Taken regularly it lightens the body, strengthens sinews and bones, frees the meridians and collaterals, and removes damp and wind. Pregnant women must not take it.

## Notes

Zhuifeng yingtong wan (bureau additions): for paralysis, convulsions, joint and wandering pain and weak legs in all wind diseases, and for women's blood wind, urticaria and headache.
- Clematis and Sichuan aconite dispel wind-damp; cibotium and fleeceflower root supplement the lower back, liver and kidneys; frankincense and flying-squirrel droppings move the blood. Contraindicated in pregnancy.
