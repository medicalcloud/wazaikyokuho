# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 013 熊胆丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xiongdan wan (Yutangan; Bear Bile Pill)

It kills gan and drives off fright. It treats vigorous fever with a clouded mind, vomiting of phlegm and drool, red cheeks and yellow face, dry nose and rough eyes, occasional night sweats, or deficiency fright, dragging on without resolving, and inability to nurse or eat.

xiongdan (bear bile; ground)　hu huanglian (picrorhiza; powdered; 2 *qian* each)　shijunzi (quisqualis fruit; stir-fried with bran, powdered)　tianjiangzi (cnidocampa cocoon; stir-fried with bran; seven each)　qingdai (indigo; ground; 1 *qian*)　hanshi mian (Cold Food Festival flour; 3 *qian*)　shexiang (jako; musk; ground; 1 *fen*)　ximo (fine ink; burned and quenched; half a *qian*)

Grind the above together evenly, knead with white flour paste and form pills the size of broomcorn millet. Take five to seven pills each time with rice water, at any time.

## Notes

Xiongdan wan: kills gan and drives off fright; for high fever, clouded mind, vomiting of phlegm, red cheeks, sallow face, dry nose and eyes, night sweats, deficiency fright and poor appetite in children.
- Bear bile, picrorhiza and indigo clear gan heat; quisqualis and cnidocampa cocoon kill worms; musk and ink are added.
- The note under picrorhiza reads "mu" (wood) in the base text, taken as an error for "mo" (powdered).
