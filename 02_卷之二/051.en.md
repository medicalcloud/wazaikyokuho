# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 051 不換金正気散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Buhuanjin zhengqi san (Fukankinshokisan; Priceless Qi-Righting Powder)

It is suitable for cold damage in any season, miasmic epidemics and seasonal qi with headache, vigorous fever and stiffness of the lower back and back; the five taxations and seven damages and miasmic qi of mountain mists with alternating fever and chills, the five diaphragm disorders with choking qi, cough with phlegm and drool and dyspnea and fatigue on walking; or sudden turmoil with vomiting and diarrhea, deficiency cold of the zang-fu organs and red and white dysentery.

houpo (koboku; magnolia bark; skin removed, processed with ginger juice)　huoxiang (agastache; twigs and earth removed)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; scorched)　banxia (hange; pinellia; boiled)　cangzhu (black atractylodes; soaked in rice-washing water)　chenpi (chinpi; aged tangerine peel; white pith removed)

Take equal parts of the above and make a chopped powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water, three slices of fresh ginger and two jujubes, decoct down to eight-tenths, remove the dregs and take it fairly hot before meals. Avoid raw and cold, greasy and toxic foods. People from elsewhere who do not adapt to the local water and soil should take it. Taken regularly it wards off miasmic qi, harmonizes the spleen and stomach and makes food taste good.

## Notes

Buhuanjin zhengqi san (Wu Zhige's additions): for seasonal cold damage and miasmic epidemics, the five taxations and seven damages, miasmic chills and fever, diaphragm qi, cough and phlegm, vomiting and diarrhea in sudden turmoil, and red and white dysentery; also for travelers who do not adapt to local water and soil.
- Pingwei san (Heiisan) (black atractylodes, magnolia bark, tangerine peel, licorice) plus agastache and pinellia.
