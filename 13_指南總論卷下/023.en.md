# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷下 023 論瀉疾証候 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On the signs of diarrhea and dysentery.

On the signs of diarrhea. Sudden diarrhea and watery diarrhea—these two patterns are common in autumn and summer, all arising from damage by food and drink and eating raw and cold things, with sudden unceasing diarrhea. One must inquire carefully. If there is belching with acid swallowing, dry retching and foul breath, this is food damage: first give one or two doses of Ganying wan, then Lizhong wan (Richugan), Renshen (ninjin) doukou san, Shouzhong jin wan, Laifu dan or Wenwei wan (all the above are warm in nature); for severe cases give Fuzi lizhong wan (Bushirichugan), Lizhong wan, Sizhu san (Shichusan), Yihan wan, Wenzhong liangjiang wan, Erjiang wan, Huolun san, Chaozhen dan, Zhengyuan san, Jinye dan or Erqi dan (all the above are hot in nature), or Dingxiang doukou san (slightly hot, astringent and securing). One must examine carefully to be sure: where there is no food damage, Ganying wan must not be given, and diarrhea-stopping medicines are used at once. For vomiting and diarrhea with abdominal pain, take Muxiang (mokko) tuiqi wan, Chenxiang wan, Dingxiang wan (Chokogan) or Ganying wan. For those weak after long illness, the aged and those with weak qi, with long unceasing diarrhea of the zang-fu organs, give Renshen doukou san, Houchang wan, Shen ling baizhu san (Jinryobyakujutsusan), Buhuanjin zhengqi san (Fukankinshokisan), Si junzi tang (Shikunshito) and the like.

## Notes

The first discussion under "On the signs of diarrhea and dysentery": diarrhea. Sudden diarrhea of summer and autumn arises from food and raw, cold things; if there is food damage (belching, acid reflux, dry retching, foul breath), first purge with Ganying wan, then use warm (Lizhong wan and others) or hot (Fuzi lizhong wan and others) medicines; without food damage, do not give Ganying wan but stop the diarrhea at once. Muxiang tuiqi wan and others for vomiting and diarrhea with abdominal pain; Renshen doukou san, Houchang wan, Shen ling baizhu san and others for chronic diarrhea in the weak and elderly. The warm or hot nature of the drugs is noted.
