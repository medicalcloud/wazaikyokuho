# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 011 金沸草散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jinfeicao san (Inula Powder)

It treats wind and transforms phlegm, removing clouding and pain of the head and eyes, stiffness of the neck and nape, alternating chills and fever, vexing pain of the limbs, fullness and oppression of the chest and diaphragm, phlegm and drool that will not clear, cough with panting and fullness, and thick sticky mucus and spittle; it also treats seasonal cold epidemics with vigorous fever and aversion to wind.

xuanfuhua (inula flower; stalks removed)　mahuang (ephedra; nodes removed)　qianhu (peucedanum; rhizome head removed; 3 liang each)　jingjie spikes (schizonepeta; 4 liang)　gancao (licorice; stir-fried)　banxia (pinellia; washed seven times in hot water, soaked in ginger juice)　chi shaoyao (red peony root; 1 liang each)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water, three slices of fresh ginger and one jujube, decoct together down to eight-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm, at any time. If there is cold evil, sweat comes out; if wind is exuberant, it is resolved and cleared.

## Notes

Jinfeicao san: for headache, neck stiffness, chills and fever and body pain from wind-cold, with productive cough and panting.
- Inula, peucedanum and pinellia send phlegm down; ephedra and schizonepeta release the exterior; red peony and licorice harmonize.
