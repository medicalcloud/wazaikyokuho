# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 061 白豆蔻散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bai doukou san (White Cardamom Powder)

It treats children with disharmony of the spleen and stomach, aversion to cold with vigorous fever, abdominal pain and vomiting, and inability to take milk or food.

pipaye (loquat leaf; hairs removed, lightly roasted)　bai doukou (white cardamom; husk removed)　chenpi (aged tangerine peel; white pith removed)　xiongqiong (Sichuan lovage)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried; 1 *fen* each)　gan mugua (dried chaenomeles)　renshen (ginseng)　huangqi (astragalus; half a liang each)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For a three-year-old child, take 1 *qian* each time with a small *zhan* of water, three coin-sized slices of fresh ginger and one jujube, decoct together down to seven-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm, at any time, adjusted for the child's size.

## Notes

Bai doukou san (Wu Zhige's additions): for aversion to cold, high fever, abdominal pain, vomiting and refusal of feeds from disharmony of the spleen and stomach in children.
- White cardamom, tangerine peel and loquat leaf stop vomiting; ginseng, astragalus and licorice supplement; chaenomeles and lovage are added.
