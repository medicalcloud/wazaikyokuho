# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 034 消風散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xiaofeng san (Wind-Dispersing Powder)

It treats all kinds of wind attacking upward, with clouding and pain of the head and eyes, stiffness of the neck and back, vexing pain of the limbs, twitching of the flesh, dizzy, spinning eyes, cicada-like ringing in the ears, rough eyes and sleepiness, nasal congestion with much sneezing, stubborn numbness of the skin, and itching with dormant papules. It also treats women's blood wind with swelling and itching of the scalp, pain of the supraorbital ridge, spinning with a tendency to fall, and phlegm counterflow with nausea.

jingjie spikes (schizonepeta)　gancao (licorice; stir-fried)　xiongqiong (ligusticum)　qianghuo (notopterygium)　bai jiangcan (silkworm; stir-fried)　fangfeng (saposhnikovia; rhizome head removed)　fuling (poria; skin removed, the white part used)　chanke (cicada slough; earth removed, lightly stir-fried)　huoxiang leaves (agastache; stalks removed)　renshen (ginseng; rhizome head removed), 2 liang each　houpo (magnolia bark; coarse bark removed, smeared with ginger juice and roasted until cooked)　chenpi (aged tangerine peel; pulp removed, washed and dried over a fire; half a liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder. Take 2 *qian* each time mixed in clear tea. For long-standing one-sided wind, take three doses a day and relief will soon be felt. For sudden contraction of wind-cold after changing clothes or bathing, with headache, heavy body, chills and fever, weariness and pain, mix it in clear schizonepeta tea; warm wine is also fine, and both may be taken. For children's deficiency wind with rough eyes and drowsiness, and for acute or chronic fright wind, mix half a *qian* in frankincense and schizonepeta decoction. All at any time.

## Notes

Xiaofeng san: for headache, dizziness, tinnitus, nasal congestion, numbness and itching of the skin and urticaria from wind attacking upward, and for women's blood wind, colds and children's fright wind.
- Schizonepeta, saposhnikovia, notopterygium, lovage, silkworm and cicada slough scatter wind; ginseng, poria, licorice, tangerine peel, magnolia bark and agastache regulate the middle. Its composition differs from the later Xiaofeng san of the *Waike zhengzong*.
