# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 073 赤石脂散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Chishizhi san (Red Halloysite Powder)

It treats children who after dysentery strain so that qi descends and the anus is pushed out and does not go back in.

fulonggan (oven earth)　chi shizhi (red halloysite; equal parts)

Grind the above finely into a powder. Each time spread half a *qian* on the head of the prolapsed intestine, three times a day.

## Notes

Chishizhi san (Wu Zhige's additions): an external powder for rectal prolapse from straining after dysentery in children.
- Oven earth and red halloysite dusted on the prolapsed bowel.
