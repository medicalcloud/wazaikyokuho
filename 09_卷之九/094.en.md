# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 094 催生符 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cuisheng fu (Labor-Hastening Talisman)

[Figure: the labor-hastening talisman. The figure in the base text is not included.]

Write the above talisman in water-ground cinnabar and paste it on the north wall of the room. When the time comes to mount the straw (the birthing place), hang it on a needle, burn it over a lamp without letting [the ash] fly away, and give it to drink mixed in warm water.

## Notes

Cuisheng fu: a talisman to hasten labor, written in cinnabar and pasted on the north wall; when the mother takes the birthing position it is burned over a lamp and the ash drunk in warm water. The base text includes a drawing of the talisman, which is not reproduced here.
