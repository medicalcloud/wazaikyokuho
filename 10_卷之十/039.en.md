# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 039 人参半夏丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Renshen banxia wan (Ninjinhangegan; Ginseng and Pinellia Pill)

It treats the lungs and stomach having received cold, with cough and hurried breathing, stuffiness and fullness of the chest and diaphragm, wheezing in the throat, vomiting of drool and froth, and inability to get milk and food down.

banxia (hange; pinellia; washed seven times in hot water, sliced, dried over a fire)　houpo (koboku; magnolia bark; coarse bark removed, roasted with ginger juice)　dingxiang (clove; 4 liang each)　chenpi (chinpi; aged tangerine peel; pulp removed)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng; rhizome head removed)　xixin (saishin; asarum; shoots removed; 2 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of hemp seeds with flour paste made with ginger juice. A three-year-old takes twenty pills each time with ginger decoction, after meals, adjusted for the child's size.

## Notes

Renshen banxia wan: for cough, hurried breathing, chest stuffiness, wheezing, vomiting of drool and froth and inability to feed when the lungs and stomach have received cold in children.
- Pinellia, tangerine peel and magnolia bark remove phlegm and bring qi down; clove and asarum warm; ginseng supplements.
