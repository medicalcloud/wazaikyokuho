# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 070 黒竜丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Heilong wan (Black Dragon Pill)

It treats headache in all wind stroke.

baizhi (byakushi; angelica dahurica; chopped)　gaoben (kohon) (Chinese lovage; washed; 2 liang each)　soft shigao (sekko; gypsum; finely ground)　chuanwu (Sichuan aconite; skin and tips removed, steamed three times with black soybeans)　nanxing (arisaema; washed; half a jin each)　mahuang (mao; ephedra; roots and nodes removed)　dried bohe leaves (mint; 4 liang each)　jingmo (capital ink; unburned; 1½ liang)

Make the above into a fine powder, pound with refined honey and form pills the size of a pellet. Take one pill each time, chewed, with mint decoction.

## Notes

Heilong wan (Wu Zhige's additions): for headache in wind stroke.
- Angelica dahurica, Chinese lovage, ephedra and mint scatter wind from the head; aconite and arisaema expel wind-phlegm; gypsum restrains heat. The ink gives the "black" of the name.
