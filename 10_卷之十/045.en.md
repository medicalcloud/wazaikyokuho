# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 045 睡驚丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shuijing dan (Sleep-Fright Elixir)

It treats fright evil in children, wind-heat and phlegm congestion, blockage of the throat and diaphragm, unquiet sleep at night, crying and screaming in sleep, and fright wind with convulsions.

shehuang (limonite nodule; calcined red, quenched five times in rice vinegar, then boiled dry in vinegar)　tiannanxing (arisaema; milled to powder, kneaded with mint juice into cakes, roasted until cooked)　fuling (poria; skin removed)　tiefen (iron powder; sifted repeatedly)　shijunzi ren (quisqualis kernel; the above five pounded and sifted into powder, half a jin of each weighed)

naozi (borneol; ground separately; half a liang)　shexiang (musk; ground separately; 1 liang)　yinbo (silver leaf; ground)　jinbo (gold leaf; ground; 100 leaves each)

Mix the ground medicines of the second group evenly into the powder of the first five, form pills the size of gleditsia seeds with glutinous rice paste and coat with cinnabar. Give it ground into mint decoction: for a five-year-old, one pill divided into two doses; for children three and under, one pill divided into three or four doses; adjust further for age. Taken regularly it calms the spirit, settles the heart, steadies fright and restrains phlegm.

## Notes

Shuijing dan (from here the layer of Shaoxing-era additions): for throat blockage, insomnia, crying in sleep and convulsions of fright wind from fright evil, wind-heat and phlegm congestion in children.
- Limonite nodule, iron powder, gold and silver leaf, borneol and musk settle; arisaema processed with mint juice removes phlegm; poria and quisqualis are added; coated with cinnabar.
