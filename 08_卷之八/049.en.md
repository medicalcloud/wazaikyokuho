# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 049 檳榔散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Binglang san (Areca Powder)

It treats abscesses, flat-abscesses, sores and boils after the pus has broken, when external exposure to wind-cold makes the swelling red, hot, bound and hard, the pus thin and watery and unceasing, the interstices within empty, the foul fluid smelly and putrid, the edges of the sore dry and tight so that good flesh does not grow; as well as gan fistulas and malign sores dragging on without healing, and shin ulcers from downward pouring that soak and fester without closing.

binglang (areca)　huanglian (coptis; fibrous roots removed, sliced)　muxiang (costus root; equal parts)

Make the above into a fine powder. Each time apply it dry on the sore.

## Notes

Binglang san (Wu Zhige's additions): for hard swelling, thin persistent pus, foul putrid discharge and failure of flesh to grow when an opened abscess or sore is exposed to wind-cold; also for gan fistulas, persistent malign sores and shin ulcers.
- A powder of areca, coptis and costus applied dry to the sore.
