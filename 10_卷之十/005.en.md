# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 005 軟金丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Ruanjin dan (Soft Gold Elixir)

It treats fright wind with vigorous fever in children, much sleeping with startled twitching, a clouded spirit, congested phlegm and drool, convulsions of the hands and feet, eyes turned upward, stiff neck and back, and clenched jaw.

shijunzi (quisqualis fruit; stir-fried, powdered)　yanmo (Yan ink; burned, ground)　qingdai (indigo; finely ground)　shexiang (jako; musk; finely ground)　nifen (calomel; ground; 1 *fen* each)　hu huanglian (picrorhiza; powdered; 1 *fen*)　hanshi mian (Cold Food Festival flour; 7½ *qian*)　tianjiangzi (cnidocampa cocoon; seven, stir-fried, powdered)

Grind the above together evenly and form pills the size of adzuki beans with white flour paste. Take one pill each time dissolved in decocted gold-and-silver mint decoction. Children of five and over may take two pills, adjusted further for size and deficiency or excess; at any time.

## Notes

Ruanjin dan: for fright wind with high fever, somnolence, startled twitching, clouded spirit, phlegm congestion, convulsions, upward gaze, rigid neck and back and clenched jaw in children.
- Quisqualis and picrorhiza treat gan; indigo, ink, cnidocampa cocoon, musk and calomel clear heat and settle fright; made into pills with Cold Food Festival flour.
