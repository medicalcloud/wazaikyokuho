# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 015 五膈寛中散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wuge kuanzhong san (Five Diaphragm Disorders Middle-Loosening Powder)

It treats the five diaphragm disorders that arise when worry, resentment, cold and heat stir the qi and injure the spirit, so that yin and yang are in disharmony, illness arises in the bowels and viscera and binds between the chest and diaphragm. The first is called worry diaphragm: qi is bound in the chest, fluids do not pass, food does not go down, with emaciation and shortness of breath. The second is resentment diaphragm: repletion and fullness below the heart, heartburn with every belch, undigested food and difficult urination and defecation. The third is qi diaphragm: counterflow fullness of the chest and flanks, choking obstruction, and belching that smells of food. The fourth is cold diaphragm: distension and fullness of the heart and abdomen, cough with qi counterflow, suffering from cold over the abdomen with thunderous rumbling, pain around the navel, and inability to eat fatty food. The fifth is heat diaphragm: heat in the five hearts, ulcers and sores in the mouth, vexing heaviness of the limbs, dry lips and mouth, sometimes a hot body, pain of the lower back and spine, chest impediment drawing into the back, and inability to eat much. It treats these and all qi disorders.

bai doukou (white cardamom; skin removed; 2 liang)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried; 5 liang)　muxiang (costus root; 3 liang)　houpo (magnolia bark; skin removed, roasted with ginger juice until cooked; 1 jin)　suoshaner (amomum)　dingxiang (clove)　qingpi (green tangerine peel; white removed)　chenpi (aged tangerine peel; white pith removed; 4 liang each)　xiangfuzi (cyperus; stir-fried, hairs removed; 16 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder. Take 2 *qian* each time with two slices of ginger and a little salt, stirred into boiling water, at any time.

## Notes

Wuge kuanzhong san: describes the symptoms of each of the five diaphragm disorders (worry, resentment, qi, cold, heat) and is used for all qi disorders.
- Its names for the five disorders differ in part from those of the earlier Wuge wan (juan 3, no. 009). Based on cyperus and magnolia bark, with amomum, cardamom, clove, costus and the tangerine peels to move qi and loosen the middle.
