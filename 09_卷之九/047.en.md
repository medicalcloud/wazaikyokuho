# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 047 琥珀黒竜丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Hupo heilong dan (Amber Black Dragon Elixir)

For all postpartum blood disorders—lochia not flowing freely, an undispersed "child's pillow", hard gathered accumulations and conglomerations that seem to grasp the hand when pressed, pain attacking the heart, exhaustion near death—merely pour the medicine in and it never fails; its efficacy is beyond words.

wulingzhi (flying squirrel droppings; sand and stones removed)　danggui (Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage)　gan dihuang (dried rehmannia; raw)　liangjiang (lesser galangal)

Take equal parts of the above, put into an earthenware box, seal the seams with red halloysite, seal with salt clay mixed with paper fibre, calcine red-hot with 10 jin of charcoal, remove from the fire and let cool; open the box and, when the contents are seen to have become black dregs, take them out, grind fine and add the following medicines. (One version says to use a rafter-head earthenware box.)

huaruishi (ophicalcite; calcined)　hupo (amber; ground; 1 *fen* each)　ruxiang (frankincense; ground separately)　liuhuang (sulphur; ground; 1½ *qian* each)　baicaoshuang (pot soot; ground separately; 5 liang)

Make all the above together into a fine powder and form pills the size of a pellet with paste boiled in rice vinegar. For each dose burn one pill red-hot over charcoal, drop it into 1 *ge* each of natural fresh-ginger juice and wine without lime and half a *zhan* of urine, grind it to dissolve and drink it all at once; it works immediately.

## Notes

Hupo heilong dan (Wu Zhige's additions): for all postpartum blood disorders—retained lochia, "child's pillow", hard accumulations, pain attacking the heart and near-fatal exhaustion.
- Flying squirrel droppings, angelica, lovage, raw rehmannia and lesser galangal calcined black in a sealed box, plus ophicalcite, amber, frankincense, sulphur and pot soot, made into black pills; each pill is burned red-hot and dropped into ginger juice, wine and urine before drinking.
