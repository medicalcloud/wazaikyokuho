# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 018 槐角丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huaijiao wan (Kaikakugan; Sophora Fruit Pill)

It treats the five kinds of intestinal wind with bleeding: blood before the stool is called external piles; blood after the stool is called internal piles; failure of the large intestine to retract is called prolapse of the anus; nipple-like proliferating flesh around the anus is called raised piles; a nipple on the top is called fistula. It treats all of these.

huaijiao (sophora fruit; twigs and stalks removed, stir-fried; 1 jin)　diyu (chiyu; sanguisorba)　danggui (toki) (Chinese angelica; soaked in wine overnight, dried over a fire)　fangfeng (bofu; saposhnikovia; rhizome head removed)　huangqin (ogon; scutellaria)　zhiqiao (bitter orange; pulp removed, stir-fried with bran; half a jin each)

Powder the above and form pills the size of wutong seeds with wine paste. Take thirty pills each time with rice water, at any time. This medicine treats intestinal wind and small worms in sores, tenesmus with pus and blood; it stops itching and pain, disperses gathered swellings and drives out damp toxin; long use removes the root of the illness for good.

## Notes

Huaijiao wan (from here the layer of Baoqing-era additions): for the five kinds of intestinal wind with bleeding (external piles, internal piles, prolapse, raised piles, fistula). Still known today as a formula for bleeding piles.
- Sophora fruit and sanguisorba stop bleeding; scutellaria clears heat; saposhnikovia dispels wind; angelica and bitter orange are added.
