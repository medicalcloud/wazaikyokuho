# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 054 氷黄散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Binghuang san (Ice-Yellow Powder)

It treats being affected by summer heat with latent heat, clouding and dizziness of the head and eyes, vomiting and diarrhea, dry mouth with vexing thirst, cold back and a grimy face.

chi fuling (bukuryo; red poria; skin removed)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; raw; 4 liang each)　hanshi mian (Cold Food Festival flour)　shengjiang (shokyo; fresh ginger; chopped fine, kneaded evenly with the flour and dried in the sun; 1 jin each)

Make the above into a fine powder. Take 2 *qian* each time mixed in freshly drawn water or cooled boiled water, at any time.

## Notes

Binghuang san (Wu Zhige's additions): for dizziness, vomiting, diarrhea and thirst from summer heat, with a cold back and grimy face (close to the summer heat-stroke pattern of the *Jingui yaolüe*).
- Cold Food Festival flour kneaded with fresh ginger and dried, combined with red poria and raw licorice, taken in cold water.
