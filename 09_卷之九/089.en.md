# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 089 禁草法 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Method of Spellbinding the Straw

When the straw and the felt and bedding have been spread, at once recite: "Iron, iron; hot water, hot water—this is not where you, sir, should be; this is the king's. With one word it becomes copper, with one word it becomes iron; mother and child give life to each other, both bamboo and iron. Quickly, quickly, as the law commands!"

## Notes

Method of spellbinding the straw: an incantation recited when the straw and bedding for delivery have been laid down. The sense is obscure; the translation follows the wording.
