# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之七 030 如聖勝金鋌 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Rusheng shengjin ding (Sage-Like Gold-Surpassing Ingot)

It treats acute throat blockage, throat-entwining wind, flying-up [swelling], single moth, double moth (tonsillar swellings), bound throat, double tongue and wooden tongue, with swollen, painful cheeks and jaw, where repeated use of medicines has failed and the patient cannot swallow water or gruel. (Another formula appears later.)

liuhuang (sulfur; finely ground)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage)　lacha (*la* tea)　bohe (mint; twigs and stalks removed)　chuanwu (Sichuan aconite; blast-fried)　xiaoshi (saltpeter; ground)　sheng dihuang (raw rehmannia; 2 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder, knead with the natural juice of split raw scallions and form into ingots. To take it, first rinse the mouth with freshly drawn water and spit it out; then chew five to seven fresh mint leaves until slightly soft, add one ingot of the medicine, chew together until very soft and swallow with well water. In severe cases three doses in succession bring a cure. For double tongue and swollen cheeks, first take one ingot, then place one ingot on the affected place, and the illness dissolves with the medicine. It also treats summer heat exposure with latent heat and loss of consciousness: mix and grind one ingot in fresh mint water and pour it in, and the patient revives at once. If, when traveling, one constantly holds an ingot in the mouth, there is no trouble from latent heat. For sores of the mouth and tongue with inability to close the mouth or eat hot food: after taking it as above, rinse with water, chew ten mint leaves to a mush and spit it out, rinse again, chew one ingot and hold it in the mouth, wrapping it in the gathered saliva; when the saliva is felt to be full, spit it out. After three ingots taken this way one can take wine and vinegar. When eating salty or sour food, pickled fish, dried meat or roasted and grilled food produces blisters in the throat, pinch them open and let the blood be spat out, and consciousness returns; chew several mint leaves with one ingot and swallow with well water. For sand strangury and heat strangury with blood in the urine, grind seven plantain leaves and a small piece of fresh ginger to a pulp, mix in water and remove the dregs, chew one ingot and wash it down with this water. This medicine separates yin and yang, removes wind-heat, transforms blood into drool and drool into water. It should always be carried on one's person for emergencies; one ingot can save a person's life. Children take only half an ingot.

## Notes

Rusheng shengjin ding (from here the layer of Baoqing-era additions): for acute diseases of the throat, mouth and tongue (acute throat blockage, throat-entwining wind, single and double moth, double and wooden tongue) with inability to swallow even water; for loss of consciousness from summer heat; for mouth sores; for blisters in the throat; and for sand and heat strangury with hematuria. To be carried for emergencies.
- Sulfur, saltpeter, Sichuan aconite, lovage, *la* tea, mint and raw rehmannia formed into ingots with scallion juice and chewed with mint. Another Rusheng shengjin ding follows (juan 7, no. 034).
