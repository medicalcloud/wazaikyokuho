# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 046 劫労散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jielao san (Taxation-Seizing Powder)

It treats the five taxations and seven damages, seasonal cold damage, miasmic malaria of mountain mists, and seasonal pestilence, with restlessness of the heart-spirit, bitter mouth and dry tongue, aversion to cold with vigorous fever, headache and nasal congestion, aching lower back and legs, stiffness of the spine and pain all over the body.

digupi (lycium root bark; 2½ liang)　qianhu (peucedanum; rhizome head removed)　jingjie (schizonepeta; 2 liang 7 *qian* each)　xiangfuzi (cyperus; stir-fried, hairs removed)　cangzhu (black atractylodes; soaked, skin removed, dried over a fire)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; scorched; 3 liang 6 *qian* each)　mahuang (mao; ephedra; roots and nodes removed)　baizhi (byakushi; angelica dahurica; 4½ *qian* each)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage; 2 liang 2½ *qian*)　jiegeng (kikyo; platycodon; rhizome head removed; 7 liang 2 *qian*)　danggui (toki; Chinese angelica; 7 liang 3½ *qian*)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed; 1 liang 3½ *qian*)　shigao (sekko; gypsum; 9 *qian*)　chenpi (chinpi; aged tangerine peel; white pith removed; 1 liang 3 *qian*)　tianxianteng (aristolochia stem; 2½ liang)

Make the above into a fine powder. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one *zhan* of water and half a smoked plum, add salt, decoct together and take it. To induce sweating, decoct it with scallion white and coin-slices of ginger and give three doses in succession. For regular use, it may be mixed in warm salted wine or stirred into hot salt water.

## Notes

Jielao san (Wu Zhige's additions): for the five taxations and seven damages, seasonal cold damage, miasmic malaria and epidemics with agitation, bitter mouth, chills and fever, headache, nasal congestion, aching lower back and legs and general pain.
- Ephedra, schizonepeta, angelica dahurica, peucedanum and lovage release the exterior; lycium bark and gypsum clear heat; angelica and cinnamon warm and supplement the blood; cyperus, black atractylodes, tangerine peel and platycodon regulate qi and damp.
