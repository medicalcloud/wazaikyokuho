# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 061 三生飲 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Sansheng yin (Three Raw Ingredients Drink)

It treats sudden strike with unconsciousness, deviation of the mouth and eyes, hemiplegia, rattling in the throat and phlegm qi congesting upward. Whether from external contraction of wind-cold or internal damage by joy and anger, whether the six pulses are deep and hidden or floating and exuberant under the fingers, it is suitable in all cases. It also treats phlegm syncope and qi syncope, and dizziness from qi deficiency, with great divine effect.

nanxing (arisaema; used raw; 1 liang)　muxiang (mokko; costus root; 1 *fen*)　chuanwu (Sichuan aconite; raw, skin removed)　fuzi (bushi; aconite lateral root; raw, skin removed; half a liang each)

Cut the above into pieces. For each dose take half a liang with two large *zhan* of water and fifteen slices of ginger, decoct down to eight-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm, at any time.

## Notes

Sansheng yin (from here the layer of formulas newly added in the Chunyou era): one of the representative formulas for sudden wind stroke with unconsciousness, facial deviation, hemiplegia and rattling phlegm, and for phlegm syncope, qi syncope and qi-deficiency dizziness.
- Arisaema, Sichuan aconite and fuzi are used raw ("three raw"); a large amount of ginger tempers their toxicity, and costus moves qi.
