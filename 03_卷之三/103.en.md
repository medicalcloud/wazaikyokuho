# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 103 五香散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wuxiang san (Five Fragrances Powder)

It makes all qi rise and descend, diffuses and frees the triple burner, disperses and guides congestion and stagnation, and scatters evil heat. It treats qi of yin and yang bound and pent up without dispersing, toxin smouldering from all kinds of heat, with swelling, pain and nodules, or [lesions] resembling abscesses and boils but not actually so, causing headache, nausea, chills and fever and urgent breathing.

muxiang (costus root)　dingxiang (clove)　chenxiang (aquilaria)　ruxiang (frankincense)　huoxiang (agastache; equal parts)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water, decoct down to eight-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm after meals.

## Notes

Wuxiang san (bureau additions): for swelling, pain and nodules from bound yin-yang qi and heat toxin, resembling but not actually abscesses or boils, with headache, nausea, chills and fever and urgent breathing.
- Five aromatics: costus, clove, aquilaria, frankincense and agastache (hence the name).
