# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 058 狗頭骨丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Goutougu wan (Dog Skull Pill)

It can treat long-standing diarrhoea with gripping pain of the navel and abdomen, discharges of mixed colours, unceasing day and night; or persons long deficient who frequently pass intestinal scum, which is called malign dysentery.

chi shizhi (red halloysite)　baigui (old tortoise shell; burned with nature preserved)　ganjiang (dried ginger; half a liang each)　roudoukou (nutmeg; wrapped in dough and roasted in embers)　fuzi (aconite; blast-fried, skin removed; 1 liang each)　goutougu (dog skull; one, burned with nature preserved, powdered; 1 liang)

Powder the above and form pills the size of wutong seeds with vinegar paste. Take fifty to seventy pills each time with rice water on an empty stomach.

## Notes

Goutougu wan (Chunyou-era additions): for long-standing diarrhoea with gripping umbilical pain and mixed-coloured stools day and night, and for malign dysentery with frequent passing of intestinal mucus in long-deficient persons.
- Dog skull and tortoise shell burned with nature preserved; red halloysite and nutmeg astringe; aconite and dried ginger warm.
