# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 086 豬蹄湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zhuti tang (Pig's Trotter Decoction)

It treats nursing women with scant qi and declining blood, rough, obstructed vessels and no milk at all.

zhuti (pig's trotter; one)　tongcao (rice-paper plant pith; 5 liang)

Wash the pig's trotter clean and prepare it as for eating; then, with 1 *dou* of water, soak and boil it together with the rice-paper plant pith until 4 or 5 sheng remain, and drink the liquid. If the milk does not come down, taking it again is excellent.

## Notes

Zhuti tang (bureau additions): for absence of milk from declining qi and blood in nursing women.
- The broth of a pig's trotter boiled with rice-paper plant pith.
