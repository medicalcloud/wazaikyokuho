# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 032 排風湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Paifeng tang (Wind-Expelling Decoction)

[It treats] men and women affected by wind deficiency and cold-damp, with pathogenic qi entering the zang organs, wild and absurd talk and a disordered spirit. When liver wind breaks out, the face is blue-green and the heart oppressed, there is vomiting of froth, fullness of the flanks with dizziness and heaviness of the head, the ears do not hear people's voices, one side withers with cramped sinews, and the patient lies with fists clenched. When heart wind breaks out, the face is red and suddenly hot, there is grief and anger, and the patient stares and shouts. When spleen wind breaks out, the face is yellow, the body numb, the patient cannot walk, food loses its taste, and dreams are confused, following the dead. When lung wind breaks out, the face is white, there is coughing counterflow with spitting of pus and blood, and qi rises until it is suddenly exhausted. When kidney wind breaks out, the face is black, the hands and feet cannot be controlled, the lower back aches so that one cannot bend forward or back, and there is numbness, cold and bone pain. Such signs alarm the heart, unsettle the will and make one abstracted and very forgetful. Taking this decoction calms the heart, settles the will, sharpens the hearing, brightens the eyes and clears all wind diseases of the zang-fu organs.

baixianpi (dictamnus root bark)　danggui (toki) (Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed, soaked overnight in wine)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　shaoyao (shakuyaku; peony root; the white kind)　xingren (kyonin; apricot kernel; skin and tips removed, stir-fried with bran)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; stir-fried)　fangfeng (bofu; saposhnikovia; rhizome head removed)　xiongqiong (ligusticum)　baizhu (byakujutsu; white atractylodes; 2 liang each)　duhuo (dokukatsu; angelica pubescens root; rhizome head removed)　mahuang (mao; ephedra; roots and nodes removed)　fuling (bukuryo; poria; skin removed, the white kind; 3 liang each)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water and four slices of fresh ginger, decoct together down to eight-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm, at any time.

## Notes

Paifeng tang: for mental disorder when wind-deficiency and cold-pathogenic damp enter the zang organs, describing the signs of wind in each of the five zang organs (liver, heart, spleen, lung, kidney).
- It belongs to the same lineage as the Paifeng tang of the *Qianjin fang*: dictamnus, duhuo, ephedra and saposhnikovia dispel wind, while angelica, peony, ligusticum, atractylodes, poria and licorice regulate qi and blood.
