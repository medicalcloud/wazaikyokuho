# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 113 胡椒湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Hujiao tang (Koshoto; Black Pepper Drink)

It treats the spleen and stomach having received cold, with blockage of the chest and diaphragm, pain of the heart and abdomen, vomiting counterflow and nausea. Taken regularly it warms the spleen and stomach, dispels cold and smooths qi.

hongdou (galangal fruit)　rougui (cinnamon bark; not exposed to fire; 1 liang each)　hujiao (black pepper; 6 liang)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; dried over a fire; 3 liang)　jiegeng (kikyo; platycodon; dried over a fire; 30 liang)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; stir-fried; 7 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder. Take one large *qian* each time with a little salt, mixed with boiling water, at any time.

## Notes

Hujiao tang (Baoqing-era additions): a drink for chest blockage, abdominal pain, vomiting and nausea when the spleen and stomach receive cold.
- Mainly platycodon, with black pepper, dried ginger, cinnamon and galangal fruit to warm, and licorice.
