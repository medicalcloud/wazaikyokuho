# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之四 003 化痰玉壺丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huatan yuhu wan (Phlegm-Transforming Jade Pot Pill)

It treats wind-phlegm with vomiting, headache and dizziness, vexing fullness of the chest and diaphragm and inability to get food down, and cough with exuberant phlegm and vomiting of drool and froth.

tiannanxing (arisaema; raw)　banxia (pinellia; raw; 1 liang each)　tianma (gastrodia; half a liang)　finest white flour (3 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with drops of water. For each dose take thirty pills: first bring one large *zhan* of water to the boil, put in the medicine and boil it five to seven times; when the pills float they are cooked. Take them out, let them cool to warm, and take them with ginger decoction, at any time.

## Notes

Huatan yuhu wan: for vomiting, headache, dizziness, chest fullness and poor appetite from wind-phlegm, and for productive cough with vomiting of frothy drool.
- Raw arisaema and pinellia transform phlegm, gastrodia dispels wind; the pills are made with white flour and boiled before being taken.
