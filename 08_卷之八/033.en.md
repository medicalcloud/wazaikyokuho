# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 033 樺皮散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huapi san (Birch Bark Powder)

It treats wind toxin of the lung viscus with sores and scabies over the whole body, and itching hives that become sores when scratched; it also treats wind pimples on the face and women's powder pimples (acne).

xingren (apricot kernel; skin and tips removed, boiled in a bowl of water in a silver pot until the water is reduced by about half, taken out and left to dry)　jingjie sui (schizonepeta spikes; 2 liang each)　zhiqiao (bitter orange; pulp removed, burned over charcoal with nature preserved, taken out and cooled on wet paper)　huapi (birch bark; burned to ash; 4 liang each)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried; half a liang)

Except for the apricot kernel, pound and sift all the medicines into a powder; grind the apricot kernel separately extremely fine, then gradually add the other powders and grind evenly. Take 2 *qian* each time after meals mixed in warm wine, three times a day. For severe sores and scabies, take frequently every day.

## Notes

Huapi san: for sores and scabies over the body, itchy hives and scratch sores from wind toxin of the lungs, and for facial pimples and acne.
- Birch bark and bitter orange burned with nature preserved, plus schizonepeta, apricot kernel and licorice.
