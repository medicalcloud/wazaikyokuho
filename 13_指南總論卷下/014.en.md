# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷下 014 論痰飲証候 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On phlegm-rheum and cough.

On the signs of phlegm-rheum. When the various phlegm-rheums are not transformed and linger in the chest and stomach duct, causing dizziness of the head and eyes, nausea and discomfort and gurgling sounds in the abdomen, give Xiaoyin wan, Beizhu wan or Wuling san. For cold, give Lizhong wan, Qingzhou bai wanzi or Yu shanren jiangqi tang. For thirst from drunkenness, retained fluid with vomiting counterflow and nausea, headache, or back pain extending to the lower back after excessive drinking, with no desire for food, give Xinfa banxia tang, Xiaoyin wan, Beizhu wan, Chensha huatan wan, Shengqi tang, Kuaiqi tang, Banxia wan, Tiannanxing wan, Da yangqi wan, Jupi banxia tang or Xiao jiangqi tang.

## Notes

The first discussion under the heading "On phlegm-rheum and cough": the signs of phlegm-rheum. Xiaoyin wan, Beizhu wan and Wuling san for dizziness, nausea and gurgling from lingering phlegm-rheum; Lizhong wan, Qingzhou bai wanzi and Yu shanren jiangqi tang for cold; Xinfa banxia tang, Xiaoyin wan and others for drunken thirst, retained fluid with vomiting, headache, back pain after drinking and poor appetite.
