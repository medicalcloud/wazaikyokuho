# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 111 檀香湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Tanxiang tang (Dankoto; Sandalwood Drink)

It treats an unrefreshed spirit, a clouded, dizzy head and eyes, palpitation with restlessness, and an unsettled will.

chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage; not exposed to fire)　baizhi (byakushi; angelica dahurica; not exposed to fire; 2 liang each)　jiegeng (kikyo; platycodon; dried over a fire; 30 liang)　tanxiang (sandalwood; not exposed to fire; 3 liang)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; stir-fried; 6 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder. Take 1 *qian* each time with a little salt, mixed with boiling water. It regulates the middle, smooths qi, calms the spirit, settles the will and clears the head and eyes.

## Notes

Tanxiang tang (Baoqing-era additions): a drink for an unrefreshed spirit, clouded head, dizziness, palpitation, agitation and an unsettled mind.
- Mainly platycodon, with sandalwood, lovage, angelica dahurica and licorice.
