# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 027 麝香大戟丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shexiang daji wan (Musk and Euphorbia Pill)

It treats swelling and distension of scrotal hernia, or small-intestine qi pain.

huluba (fenugreek; stir-fried; 4 liang)　daji (euphorbia root; skin removed, stir-fried yellow; half a liang)　shexiang (musk; ground separately; 1 *qian*)　huixiang (fennel; imported by ship)　chuanlianzi (toosendan fruit; 6 liang each)　binglang (areca; base scraped off, finely sliced, not exposed to fire)　hezi (chebule; blast-fried, stone removed, soaked in wine, steamed, dried over a fire)　fuzi (aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　muxiang (costus root; 1 liang each)

Powder the above, keeping the toosendan aside: boil it in 1–2 sheng of good wine with seven scallion whites three or four *cun* long until soft, remove the stones and take the flesh, pound it with the medicines and form pills the size of wutong seeds. Take five to seven, up to ten, pills on an empty stomach with warm wine; ginger decoction is also fine. For pain in tidal attacks, take fifteen pills with stir-fried ginger in hot wine.

## Notes

Shexiang daji wan (bureau additions): for swelling of scrotal hernia and small-intestine qi pain.
- Fenugreek, fennel and aconite warm; toosendan, costus, areca and musk move qi; euphorbia expels water; chebule is added. The toosendan is boiled in wine with scallion and its flesh used as the pill base.
