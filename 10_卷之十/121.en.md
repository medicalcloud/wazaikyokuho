# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 121 衙香 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Ya xiang (Official Hall Incense)

jiaxiang (operculum; prepared as above)　chenxiang (aquilaria; chopped)　jianxiang (jian aloeswood; chopped; 6 liang each)　naozi (borneol; ground)　shexiang (jako; musk; ground; 9 liang each)　yaxiao (refined mirabilite; ground; 12 liang)　tanxiang (sandalwood; chopped; 12 jin, stir-fried with clear *la* tea)　mi (honey; double the weight of the incense ingredients, refined, kneaded with the incense)

Powder the above, add the ground ingredients, knead evenly with the honey, and burn in the usual way.

## Notes

Ya xiang ("Various incenses"): a recipe for incense burned in official halls.
- Mainly sandalwood, with operculum, aquilaria, jian aloeswood, borneol, musk and refined mirabilite, kneaded with honey.
