# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 028 惺惺散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xingxing san (Awakening Powder)

It treats wind-heat, sores and rashes, cold damage and seasonal qi in children, with headache and vigorous fever, rough eyes and much sleeping, cough with coarse breathing, and nasal congestion with clear snivel.

gualougen (trichosanthes root)　renshen (ginseng)　xixin (asarum; leaves removed)　fuling (poria; skin removed)　baizhu (white atractylodes)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried)　jiegeng (platycodon; 1½ liang each)

Pound and sift the above together into a powder. For each dose take 1 *qian* with a small *zhan* of water and three mint leaves, decoct together down to four-tenths and take it warm. To harmonize qi, add fresh ginger to the decoction. At any time.

## Notes

Xingxing san: for headache, high fever, rough eyes, somnolence, cough, coarse breathing and nasal congestion from wind-heat, eruptions, cold damage or seasonal qi in children.
- Si junzi tang (ginseng, atractylodes, poria, licorice) plus trichosanthes root, asarum and platycodon, decocted with mint.
