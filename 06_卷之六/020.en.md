# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 020 勝氷丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shengbing dan (Ice-Surpassing Elixir)

It treats all cases of congestion and exuberance of the sanjiao surging up into the head and eyes with red, hot pain, sores of the mouth and tongue, blockage of the throat, obstruction on swallowing, and a clouded, oppressed mind.

baiyaozi (stephania cepharantha tuber; 1½ liang)　shandougen (sophora root)　hongneixiao (hongneixiao root)　huangyaozi (air potato tuber)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried)　huanglian (oren; coptis; 2 liang each)　shexiang (jako; musk; ground)　longnao (borneol; ground; 2 *qian* each)

Powder the above, put it in a Jian-ware bowl and steam it on top of the rice; when cool, add the borneol and musk and mix evenly, and form pills the size of euryale seeds with refined honey. Hold one pill in the mouth to dissolve. Also, grind a little on a fingernail with saliva and apply it to red eyes, with immediate effect.

## Notes

Shengbing dan (bureau additions): for red, hot pain of the head and eyes, mouth sores, throat blockage, difficulty swallowing and clouded mind from congestion and exuberance of the sanjiao; also applied to red eyes.
- Sophora root, stephania tuber, air potato tuber, hongneixiao and coptis clear heat toxin; borneol and musk are added. Held in the mouth to dissolve.
