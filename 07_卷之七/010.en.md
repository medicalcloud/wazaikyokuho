# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之七 010 草竜胆散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cao longdan san (Gentian Powder)

It treats all cases of the upper burner having received wind-heat, with qi toxin attacking and surging, sudden redness of the eyes, gritty roughness and aversion to light, swelling, pain and much discharge, tearing against the wind, nebulae and membranes climbing over the eye, and proliferating flesh with dull pain. (Another formula appears later.)

chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage; not exposed to fire)　xiangfu (cyperus; stir-fried, hairs removed; 4 liang each)　longdan cao (gentian; washed, rhizome head removed)　cao juemingzi (cassia seed; lightly stir-fried)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried)　muzei (scouring rush; washed clean, nodes removed)　juhua (chrysanthemum flower; stalks removed; 2 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder. Take 2 *qian* each time mixed in boiled ophiopogon water with a little sugar, after meals; it may also be mixed in rice-washing water; take after meals or at bedtime.

## Notes

Cao longdan san (from here the layer of Baoqing-era additions): for sudden red eyes, grittiness, photophobia, swelling, pain, discharge, tearing, nebulae and proliferating flesh (pterygium) from wind-heat in the upper burner. Another Cao longdan san follows (juan 7, no. 020).
- Gentian clears liver heat; cassia seed, scouring rush and chrysanthemum brighten the eyes; lovage and cyperus move qi and blood; licorice is added.
