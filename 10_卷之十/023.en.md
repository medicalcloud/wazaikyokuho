# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 023 没食子丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Moshizi wan (Gallnut Pill)

It treats children whose deficient intestines have received heat, with dysentery of fresh blood or stools of red liquid, abdominal pain with tenesmus, unceasing day and night, very frequent.

moshizi (oak gall)　diyu (sanguisorba; half a liang each)　huangbo (phellodendron; chopped, stir-fried with honey; 2 liang)　huanglian (coptis; chopped, stir-fried; 1½ liang)　suan shiliu pi (sour pomegranate rind; 1 liang)

Pound and sift the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of hemp seeds with flour paste boiled in vinegar. Take ten to twenty pills each time with warm rice water, before meals.

## Notes

Moshizi wan: for dysentery of fresh blood or red liquid, abdominal pain, tenesmus and frequent stools day and night when heat enters deficient intestines in children.
- Coptis and phellodendron clear heat; oak gall, pomegranate rind and sanguisorba astringe and stop bleeding.
