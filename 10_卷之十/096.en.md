# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 096 桂花湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Guihua tang (Cinnamon Flower Drink)

It treats all cold qi with stabbing pain of the heart and abdomen, glomus and oppression of the chest and diaphragm, distension and fullness of the flanks and ribs, vomiting counterflow and nausea, and tasteless food.

ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried; 9 liang)　guixin (cinnamon heart)　gancao (licorice; stir-fried; 9 jin each)　suoshaliren (amomum seed; 3 jin 14 liang)

Powder the above together with 14 jin of stir-fried salt. Take 1 *qian* each time mixed with boiling water, before meals.

## Notes

Guihua tang ("Various drinks"): a drink for stabbing abdominal pain, chest oppression, flank distension, vomiting, nausea and tasteless food from all cold qi.
- Cinnamon heart, dried ginger, amomum, licorice and salt.
