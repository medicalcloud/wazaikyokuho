# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷下 010 論胸膈不快 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On discomfort of the chest and diaphragm. For discomfort of the chest and diaphragm with stagnant qi, give Chenxiang jiangqi tang, Wuchen tang, Qing muxiang wan (Seimokkogan), Qiqi tang or Heqi san, or Renshen wan (Ninjingan), Hong wanzi, Xiao lizhong wan (Shorichugan), Zhuangqi chenxiang wan, Dingchen wan, Shunqi wan, Sanleng san (Sanryosan), Ruyi wan or Pengjian wan. For severe vexing oppression with constipation, give Qing muxiang wan. For spleen pain, give Pengjian wan and the like.

## Notes

On chest and diaphragm discomfort from stagnant qi: Chenxiang jiangqi tang, Wuchen tang, Qing muxiang wan, Qiqi tang, Heqi san and others; Qing muxiang wan for oppression with constipation; Pengjian wan for spleen pain.
