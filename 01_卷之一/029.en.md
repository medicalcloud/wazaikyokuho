# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 029 皂角丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zaojiao wan (Soap-Pod Pill)

It is suitable for wind qi attacking and pouring, with swelling and itching of the head and face, stiffness of the whole body, congested phlegm and drool, vexation and oppression of the chest and diaphragm, headache and dizziness, nasal congestion and dry mouth, itching skin, heavy painful lower back and legs, wind constipation, and red, rough urine; as well as cough with panting and fullness, thick turbid phlegm and spittle, sluggish speech with much drool, numbness of the hands and feet, dark wind and epilepsy, one-sided and general headache and head-clamping wind; and women's blood wind attacking and pouring, with pain all over, palpitation with vexation and agitation, and itchy dormant papules.

zaojiao (soap pod; crushed, kneaded in 18 liang 6 *qian* of water to extract the juice, simmered with 1 jin of honey into a paste)　dried bohe leaves (mint)　huaijiao (sophora fruit; scorched; 5 liang each)　qing jupi (green tangerine peel; pulp removed)　zhimu (anemarrhena)　beimu (fritillaria; core removed, stir-fried yellow)　banxia (pinellia; washed seven times in hot water)　weilingxian (clematis root; washed)　baifan (alum; calcined)　ganju (chrysanthemum; twigs removed; 1 liang each)　qianniuzi (morning-glory seed; scorched; 2 liang)

Powder the above and knead with the soap-pod paste into pills the size of wutong seeds. Take twenty pills each time after meals with ginger decoction. For phlegm repletion with cough, take with clamshell powder in pickled-vegetable juice; for numbness of the hands and feet, with ginger and mint decoction; for sluggish speech with exuberant drool, with schizonepeta decoction; for one-sided or general headache and head-clamping wind, with mint decoction.

## Notes

Zaojiao wan: for swelling and itching of the head and face, headache, cough, constipation, numbness and epilepsy from wind qi and phlegm, and for women's blood wind.
- Soap-pod paste is the base; morning-glory seed and sophora fruit purge, and fritillaria, pinellia and alum transform phlegm; the vehicle changes with the symptoms.
