# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 026 鍾乳健脾丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zhongru jianpi wan (Stalactite Spleen-Fortifying Pill)

It treats men and women with deficiency and damage and emaciation, a heavy body, cold, weak spleen and stomach, undigested food, abdominal distension with thunderous rumbling and unceasing diarrhoea. It also treats deficiency of the intestines with accumulated cold, diarrhoea of clear grain or of pure white matter, and gripping pain in the abdomen; and long-standing red and white dysentery with slippery, uncontrollable intestines, shortness of qi, emaciation and fatigue, and no desire for food. All should take it.

rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　renshen (ginseng)　huanglian (coptis; fibrous roots removed)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried)　longgu (dragon bone)　danggui (Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed)　shihu (dendrobium; roots removed)　damai nie (barley sprout; stir-fried)　fuling (poria; skin removed)　xixin (asarum; shoots and earth removed)　shenqu (medicated leaven; broken up, stir-fried)　chi shizhi (red halloysite; calcined; 2 liang each)　shujiao (Sichuan pepper; seeds and closed fruits removed, lightly stir-fried to sweat out the oil; 6 liang)　fuzi (aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed; 1 liang)　zhongru fen (stalactite powder; 3 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder, mix in the stalactite powder evenly, knead with refined honey and form pills the size of wutong seeds. Take thirty pills each time with warm rice water, before meals, three times a day.

## Notes

Zhongru jianpi wan (the first formula of juan 6, "Treating diarrhoea and dysentery"): for wasting from deficiency and damage, indigestion, borborygmus and unceasing diarrhoea from cold, weak spleen and stomach; for diarrhoea of undigested food, white dysentery and abdominal pain from deficient, cold intestines; and for incontinence, weakness and poor appetite in long-standing red and white dysentery.
- Sichuan pepper, aconite, dried ginger, cinnamon and asarum warm; ginseng, poria, dendrobium, angelica and stalactite supplement; dragon bone and red halloysite secure the intestines; medicated leaven and barley sprout aid digestion; a little coptis is added.
