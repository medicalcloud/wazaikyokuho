# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 103 厚朴湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Houpo tang (Magnolia Bark Drink)

It treats deficient, cold spleen and stomach with abdominal pain and diarrhoea, glomus and oppression of the chest and diaphragm, distension and fullness of the flanks and ribs, vomiting counterflow and nausea, and no desire for food.

houpo (magnolia bark; coarse bark removed; 10 jin, processed with 2 jin of fresh ginger)　zao (jujube; 1 *dou* 6 sheng)　dingxiang pi (clove bark; 8 liang)　gancao (licorice; stir-fried; 11 jin)　dingxiang zhizhang (clove twigs; 12 liang)　yan (salt; stir-fried; 15 jin)

Powder the above. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one *zhan* of water, three slices of ginger and two jujubes broken open, decoct together down to seven-tenths and take it hot. Taken regularly it warms the middle, smooths qi and improves eating. [Alternatively] take 1 *qian* each time mixed with boiling water, before meals.

## Notes

Houpo tang ("Various drinks"): a drink for abdominal pain, diarrhoea, chest glomus, flank distension, vomiting and poor appetite from deficient, cold spleen and stomach; it may be decocted or simply mixed with hot water.
- Magnolia bark, jujube, clove bark, clove twigs, licorice and salt.
