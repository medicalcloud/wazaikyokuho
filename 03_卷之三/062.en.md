# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 062 木香分気丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Muxiang fenqi wan (Costus Qi-Separating Pill)

It is suitable for all qi counterflow with fullness and oppression of the heart and chest, deficiency distension of the abdomen and flanks, undigested food, dry retching and vomiting, stuffiness and fullness of the chest and diaphragm, rising qi with cough and cold phlegm, and qi failing to rise and descend.

muxiang (mokko; costus root)　gansong (nardostachys; washed free of mud; 1 liang each)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried; 6 liang)　xiangfuzi (cyperus; 16 liang)　peng'ezhu (zedoary; roasted; 8 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills with water paste. Take twenty pills each time with decocted ginger and tangerine-peel decoction, at any time. It is best for those with weak spleen and stomach. Taken regularly it loosens the middle, smooths qi and improves eating.

## Notes

Muxiang fenqi wan (Baoqing-era additions): for chest oppression, abdominal and flank distension, indigestion, vomiting, stuffiness and cough with cold phlegm from qi counterflow.
- Cyperus, zedoary, costus and nardostachys move qi; licorice harmonizes.
