# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 077 神授散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shenshou san (Divinely Bestowed Powder)

It treats all postpartum illnesses, great or small, even those that have become critical.

qingpi (green tangerine peel; white removed)　guixin (cinnamon heart)　mudanpi (moutan bark)　chen jupi (kippi; aged tangerine peel; white pith removed)　bai shaoyao (shakuyaku; white peony root; 5 liang each)　honghua (safflower; 1½ liang)　baihe (byakugo; lily bulb; soaked in water and washed)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried)　danggui (toki) (Chinese angelica)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage; 2 liang each)　shenqu (medicated leaven; stir-fried)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng; rhizome head removed)　mainie (barley sprout; stir-fried; 3 liang each)

Powder the above. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one *zhan* of water, three slices of ginger and one jujube, decoct down to seven-tenths and take it on an empty stomach. Pregnant women must not take it. (One version does not use safflower.)

## Notes

Shenshou san (bureau additions): for all postpartum illnesses, mild or critical. Contraindicated in pregnancy.
- Angelica, lovage, white peony, moutan and safflower move blood; ginseng and licorice supplement; green and aged tangerine peel move qi; cinnamon heart and dried ginger warm; medicated leaven and barley sprout aid digestion; lily bulb is added.
