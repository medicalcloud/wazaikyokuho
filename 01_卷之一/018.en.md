# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 018 八風丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bafeng dan (Eight Winds Elixir)

It treats all kinds of wind and phlegm-heat attacking upward, with headache and a red face, dizzy, spinning eyes, nasal congestion and a dry throat, discomfort of the neck and nape, thick turbid phlegm and spittle, a drunken-feeling spirit, pain in all the joints, ringing in the ears like cicadas, wandering wind on the face, and twitching of the mouth and eyes.

huashi (talc; finely ground)　tianma (gastrodia; soaked in wine; 1 liang each)　longnao (borneol; ground)　shexiang (musk; ground; 1 *fen* each)　bai jiangcan (silkworm; lightly stir-fried)　baifuzi (typhonium; blast-fried; half a liang each)　banxia (pinellia; processed with alum; 2 liang)　hanshuishi (calcite; burned red-hot, finely ground and water-ground; half a jin)

Pound and sieve the above medicines into a fine powder, add the ground medicines, grind together evenly and form pills the size of cherries with refined honey. Take one pill each time, chewed thoroughly, with warm schizonepeta decoction; clear tea is also fine. Take after meals.

## Notes

Bafeng dan: for headache, red face, dizziness, tinnitus and facial twitching from wind and phlegm-heat attacking upward.
- Calcite and talc clear heat; gastrodia, silkworm, typhonium and pinellia expel wind-phlegm; borneol and musk are added.
