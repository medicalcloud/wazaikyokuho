# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 049 四生散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Sisheng san (Four Raw Ingredients Powder)

It treats wind toxin of the liver and kidneys in men and women which attacks upward, with red, itchy, painful eyes, photophobia and tearing at any time, or pours downward, with sores on the legs and knees; as well as wind tinea all over the body unresponsive to medicine, often with itching inside both ears. This is just the thing to take, and it never fails to work.

huangqi (astragalus)　chuan qianghuo (Sichuan notopterygium)　jili (sha-yuan tribulus)　baifuzi (typhonium; equal parts, used raw)

Make the above into a fine powder. Take 2 *qian* each time mixed in mint wine. For kidney wind toxin pouring down and producing sores, slit open a pig's kidney, put 2 *qian* of the powder inside and close it, wrap it and roast it until fragrant and cooked; chew it thoroughly on an empty stomach and wash it down with salted wine.

## Notes

Sisheng san (Shaoxing-era additions): for red, itchy eyes and photophobia, sores on the legs, wind tinea and itching in the ears from wind toxin of the liver and kidneys.
- Astragalus, notopterygium, sha-yuan tribulus and typhonium are all used raw ("four raw"); for sores from downward pouring, the powder is roasted inside a pig's kidney and eaten.
