# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 015 小白薇丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xiao baiwei wan (Shobyakubigan; Minor Cynanchum Pill)

It treats women with deficiency damage of the *Chong* and *Ren*, cold received by the womb, long childlessness, and births that have ceased. This arises from heat above and cold below, from which the hundred illnesses grow: or menses flooding down, five-colored discharge and metrostaxis, painful, heavy lower back and abdomen, a yellow face and wasted flesh; or failure to care for oneself during childbirth and nursing, going to the privy too soon; or sitting long on damp ground so that cold wind enters from below, the blood zang organ being already deficient and pathogenic wind riding in; or, when the menses should come, failure of proper care, injuring and disturbing the uterine collaterals, so that yin and yang are not harmonious, deficient yang of the upper jiao is congested and dry and pathogenic cold of the lower zang organs is bound and latent. As a result, pregnancy does not form, cold reaches its extreme with damage and ruin, the menses are uneven, eating is reduced, there are copious night sweats, dark spots and freckles appear on the face, the teeth loosen and the hair falls, the legs and knees are painful and heavy and there is little strength for movement. All should take it.

fupenzi (rubus fruit; stalks removed)　changpu (shobu; acorus; lightly stir-fried; 3 *fen* each)　bai longgu (ryukotsu; white dragon bone)　shu gan dihuang (kanjio; prepared rehmannia)　chuanjiao (Sichuan pepper; seeds and closed fruits removed, lightly stir-fried to sweat out the oil)　baiwei (byakubi; cynanchum root; shoots removed; 1 liang each)　shechuangzi (jashoshi; cnidium seed; stir-fried)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried)　xixin (saishin; asarum; shoots removed)　danggui (toki; Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed, lightly stir-fried)　cheqianzi (shazenshi; plantago seed)　xiongqiong (Sichuan lovage; half a liang each)　yuanzhi (onji; polygala; core removed)　taoren (tonin; peach kernel; skin and tips removed, stir-fried yellow with bran)　bai fuling (bukuryo; white poria; skin removed)　gaoben (kohon; Chinese lovage; shoots removed)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng)　juanbai (selaginella; roots removed)　baizhi (byakushi; angelica dahurica)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed; 3 liang each)　maimendong (bakumondo; ophiopogon; core removed, dried over a fire; 1½ liang)

Make the above into a fine powder, knead with refined honey and form pills the size of wutong seeds. Take thirty pills each time with warm wine or rice water, on an empty stomach before meals. Taken regularly it strengthens the sinews and bones, augments blood and qi, warms the lower zang organs, removes wind-cold and enables conception.

## Notes

Xiao baiwei wan: for infertility and cessation of births from deficient *Chong* and *Ren* and a cold womb; for the illnesses arising from heat above and cold below (flooding, five-colored discharge, back and abdominal pain, sallowness, wasting); and for infertility, irregular menses, poor appetite, night sweats, facial spots, loose teeth, hair loss, leg pain and weakness after poor postpartum care, sitting on damp ground or neglect during menstruation.
- Cynanchum, rubus fruit, cnidium, selaginella and dragon bone, with prepared rehmannia, angelica, lovage, ginseng, poria, polygala and ophiopogon to supplement; Sichuan pepper, dried ginger, asarum and cinnamon warm; Chinese lovage and angelica dahurica dispel wind; peach kernel, plantago seed and acorus are added.
