# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 026 十四味建中湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shisi wei jianzhong tang (Fourteen-Ingredient Middle-Fortifying Decoction)

It treats insufficiency of the ying-nutrient and wei-defense qi, damage to both bowels and zang organs, deficiency and damage from accumulated taxation, an emaciated body, shortness of breath with a liking for lying down, chills and fever with headache, cough with hurried dyspnea, spitting and vomiting of phlegm and froth, frequently cold hands and feet, a white, colorless face, cramping of the lower abdomen, pain in all the joints, much sweating when lying at night, fright and palpitation in dreams, slippery free urination, frequent stools, extreme deficiency from blood loss, palpitation with a dark face, and long-standing spleen-kidney deficiency with deficient eating.

danggui (toki; Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed, soaked in wine, dried over a fire)　bai shaoyao (shakuyaku; white peony root; chopped)　baizhu (byakujutsu; white atractylodes; chopped, washed)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng; rhizome head removed)　maimendong (bakumondo; ophiopogon; core removed)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage; washed clean)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　fuzi (bushi; aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　roucongrong (cistanche; soaked overnight in wine)　banxia (hange; pinellia; washed seven times in hot water)　huangqi (ogi; astragalus; mix-fried)　fuling (bukuryo; poria; skin removed)　shu dihuang (prepared rehmannia; washed free of earth, steamed overnight with wine, dried over a fire; equal parts)

Cut the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water, three slices of ginger and one jujube, decoct down to one *zhan*, remove the dregs and take it warm before meals.

## Notes

Shisi wei jianzhong tang (Baoqing-era additions): for wasting, shortness of breath, chills and fever, cough, cold limbs, pale face, lower abdominal cramping, joint pain, night sweats, fright, frequent urination, diarrhea, blood loss and chronic spleen-kidney deficiency from insufficiency of the ying-nutrient and wei-defense qi and accumulated taxation.
- Shiquan dabu tang (Juzentaihoto) (ginseng, atractylodes, poria, licorice, angelica, lovage, peony, rehmannia, astragalus, cinnamon) plus aconite, cistanche, ophiopogon and pinellia—fourteen ingredients.
