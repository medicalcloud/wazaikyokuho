## Translation of the text

Directions the Mother Must Avoid Facing, by Month

(Avoid giving birth facing the inauspicious direction of the month.)

## Notes

Directions the mother must avoid facing, by month: tables of the directions in which the mother must not face when giving birth in each month. The tables exist only as images in the base text and are not included here.
