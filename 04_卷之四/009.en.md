# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之四 009 胡椒理中丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Hujiao lizhong wan (Koshorichugan; Black Pepper Middle-Regulating Pill)

It treats deficiency cold of the lungs and stomach with qi not diffusing, cough with urgent dyspnea, counterflow qi and deficiency stuffiness, choking oppression of the chest and diaphragm, fullness and pain of the abdomen and flanks, pressing obstruction with shortness of breath, inability to eat, and incessant vomiting of phlegm and water.

kuandonghua (kantoka; coltsfoot flower; stalks removed)　hujiao (black pepper)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried)　biba (long pepper)　liangjiang (galangal)　xixin (saishin; asarum; sprouts removed)　chenpi (chinpi; aged tangerine peel; white pith removed)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; 4 liang each)　baizhu (byakujutsu; white atractylodes; 5 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with refined honey. Take thirty to fifty pills each time with warm water; warm wine or rice gruel is also fine; at any time, twice a day.

## Notes

Hujiao lizhong wan: for cough, dyspnea, stuffiness, chest oppression, abdominal and flank pain, shortness of breath, poor appetite and vomiting of phlegm and water from deficiency cold of the lungs and stomach.
- Lizhong wan (Richugan) (atractylodes, dried ginger, licorice) warmed further with black pepper, long pepper, galangal and asarum, with coltsfoot and tangerine peel for cough and phlegm; there is no ginseng.
