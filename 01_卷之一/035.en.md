# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 035 羌活散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qianghuo san (Notopterygium Powder)

It treats disordered wind qi with clouding and dizziness of the head and eyes, congested phlegm and drool and stiffness of the whole body; and wind evil with cold congestion, with headache, stiff neck, nasal congestion with a heavy voice, vexing pain of the limbs, and unease felt in advance whenever the weather turns overcast or windy and rainy.

qianhu (peucedanum; rhizome head removed)　qianghuo (notopterygium; rhizome head removed)　mahuang (ephedra; roots and nodes removed)　bai fuling (white poria; skin removed)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage)　huangqin (scutellaria)　gancao (licorice; scorched)　manjingzi (vitex fruit; white skin removed)　zhiqiao (bitter orange; pulp removed, stir-fried with bran)　xixin (asarum; sprouts removed)　shigao (gypsum; ground separately)　juhua (chrysanthemum; stalks removed)　fangfeng (saposhnikovia; rhizome head removed; 1 liang each)

Powder the above, add the gypsum and grind evenly. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one large *zhan* of water, three or four slices of fresh ginger and two or three mint leaves, decoct together down to seven-tenths, and take it fairly hot, at any time.

## Notes

Qianghuo san: for headache, dizziness, stiff neck, nasal congestion and joint pain from wind qi and wind-cold, worsening with changes of weather.
- Notopterygium, ephedra, saposhnikovia, asarum and peucedanum release the exterior; lovage, vitex and chrysanthemum clear the head; gypsum and scutellaria clear heat.
