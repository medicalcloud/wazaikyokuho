# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 092 磨積丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Moji wan (Accumulation-Grinding Pill)

It can treat children with timid, weak zang-fu organs that have received accumulated cold internally, with distending pain of the flanks and ribs, vomiting of phlegm with counterflow, borborygmus and diarrhea very frequent day and night, weary limbs, a colorless face, wasted flesh and inability to eat; as well as gan qi with emaciation, a big belly with green veins, dry mouth with vexing thirst, white turbid urine, eating that does not become flesh, or deficiency swelling and alternating fever and chills; or worms stirring with pain after eating sweet and fatty food, with screaming, crying and closed eyes.

ganqi (kanshitsu; dried lacquer; stir-fried)　dingxiang (clove; 1 liang each)　qingpi (green tangerine peel; white removed)　jing sanleng (sparganium; blast-fried; 6 liang each)　peng ezhu (zedoary; half a jin)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of millet grains with water paste. A two-year-old may take five pills, swallowed with weak ginger decoction, at any time; adjust further for age and deficiency or excess.

## Notes

Moji wan (bureau additions): for flank pain, vomiting of phlegm, borborygmus, frequent diarrhea, lassitude, poor complexion, wasting and poor appetite from weak zang organs with accumulated cold in children; for gan wasting with a big belly and visible veins, thirst, turbid urine, deficiency swelling and chills and fever; and for worm pain after sweet, fatty foods.
- Sparganium, zedoary, dried lacquer and green tangerine peel grind away accumulations; clove warms.
