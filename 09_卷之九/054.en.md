# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 054 皺血丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zhouxue wan (Wrinkled Blood Pill)

There is nothing it does not treat among the following: women with a deficient, cold sea of blood from which the hundred illnesses arise in changing forms, disharmony of qi and blood, intermittent chills and fever, excessive bleeding, or long-blocked menses, unceasing flooding, red and white vaginal discharge, conglomerations and aggregation lumps with attacking, stabbing pain, tight fullness of the lower abdomen, distending pain of the flanks and ribs, heavy lower back and weak legs, a yellow face and deficient body, reduced eating and gradual onset of taxation; irregular menses with frequent damage to fetal qi; and all illnesses before and after childbirth.

juhua (chrysanthemum flower; stalks removed)　huixiang (fennel)　xiangfu (cyperus; stir-fried, soaked in wine overnight, dried over a fire)　shu gan dihuang (kanjio; prepared rehmannia)　danggui (toki; Chinese angelica)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　niuxi (goshitsu; achyranthes)　yanhusuo (corydalis; stir-fried)　shaoyao (shakuyaku; peony root)　puhuang (hoo; typha pollen)　peng ezhu (zedoary; 3 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder. Boil 1 sheng of black soybeans in vinegar until dry, dry over a fire and powder; add two bowls of vinegar and boil down to one bowl, keep this as paste and form pills the size of wutong seeds. Take twenty pills each time with warm wine or vinegar decoction. For blood and qi attacking and stabbing, take them with stir-fried ginger wine. For gripping pain of conglomeration lumps, take them with angelica wine. Avoid duck meat and sheep's blood. This medicine warms the womb and enables conception.

## Notes

Zhouxue wan (bureau additions): for the many disorders of a deficient, cold sea of blood in women (disharmony of qi and blood, chills and fever, heavy bleeding, amenorrhea, flooding, discharge, stabbing pain of conglomerations, lower abdominal fullness, flank pain, weak back and legs, sallowness, poor appetite, taxation), irregular menses, habitual miscarriage and all illnesses before and after childbirth; said to warm the womb and enable conception.
- Prepared rehmannia, angelica, peony and achyranthes supplement; corydalis, typha pollen, zedoary and cyperus move blood and qi; cinnamon and fennel warm; chrysanthemum is added; made into pills with a paste of black soybeans boiled in vinegar.
