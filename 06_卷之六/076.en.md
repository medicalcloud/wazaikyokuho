# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 076 丁香豆蔻散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Dingxiang doukou san (Clove and Nutmeg Powder)

It is suitable for weak, deficient spleen and stomach with old cold lodged and accumulated, or internal damage of the spleen and stomach from raw and cold food and drink, with pouring diarrhoea, undigested water and grain, chest fullness and shortness of breath, retching counterflow and nausea, gripping pain of the navel and abdomen, distension and fullness of the flanks and ribs, deficiency rumbling in the abdomen and reduced eating; as well as long-standing dysentery from accumulated cold, pure white or more white than red, without limit day and night; or deficiency cold of the spleen and stomach with diarrhoea of many days that recurs after healing.

jing sanleng (sparganium; blast-fried)　muxiang (costus root; not exposed to fire)　houpo (magnolia bark; coarse bark removed, processed with ginger juice)　shaoyao (peony root)　roudoukou (nutmeg; blast-fried)　renshen (ginseng)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried)　fuling (white poria; skin removed; 5 liang each)　wuzhuyu (evodia; washed seven times in hot water, dried over a fire)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried)　dingxiang (clove; 3½ liang each)　cangzhu (black atractylodes; skin removed; 7 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one *zhan* of water, three slices of fresh ginger and one jujube broken open, decoct together down to eight-tenths and take it warm on an empty stomach before meals. If there is no time to decoct it, add a little salt and take it mixed with hot water.

## Notes

Dingxiang doukou san (bureau additions): for pouring diarrhoea, indigestion, chest fullness, breathlessness, retching, gripping umbilical pain, flank distension, borborygmus and poor appetite from weak spleen and stomach with old cold or damage by raw and cold food; for long-standing white dysentery from accumulated cold; and for protracted or recurrent diarrhoea from deficiency cold of the spleen and stomach.
- Clove, nutmeg, evodia and dried ginger warm; ginseng, poria, black atractylodes and licorice supplement; magnolia bark, costus and sparganium move qi and disperse accumulations; peony is added.
