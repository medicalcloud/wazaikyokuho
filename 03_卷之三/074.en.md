# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 074 鶏舌香散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jishexiang san (Keizetsukosan; Clove Powder)

It treats men and women with disharmony of yin and yang, weak zang-fu organs, stagnant qi in the middle duct, chronic cold and prolonged fluid retention accumulating without dispersing, distension and fullness of the chest and diaphragm, drawing pain of the heart and spleen attacking and stabbing the abdomen and flanks, and interference with eating. It also treats drunkenness with vomiting of wine, and soaking of prolonged fluid retention with vomiting counterflow, nausea, belching and acid swallowing.

xiangfuzi (cyperus; stir-fried, hairs removed)　chi shaoyao (shakuyaku; red peony root)　Tiantai wu (lindera from Tiantai; wood removed)　liangjiang (galangal; rhizome head removed, stir-fried in sesame oil)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed; 1 liang each)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried; half a liang)

Make the above into a fine powder. Take 2 *qian* each time with a little salt stirred into boiling water, at any time.

## Notes

Jishexiang san (Wu Zhige's additions): for chest distension, heart and spleen pain and stabbing abdominal and flank pain from middle-duct qi stagnation, chronic cold and prolonged fluid retention, and for hangover and vomiting, acid reflux from prolonged fluid retention.
- Cyperus and lindera move qi, galangal and cinnamon warm, red peony eases pain. Although the name refers to "chicken-tongue fragrance" (clove), clove is not in the list of ingredients in the base text.
