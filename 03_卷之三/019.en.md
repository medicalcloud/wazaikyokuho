# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 019 三和散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Sanhe san (Sanwasan; Three Harmonies Powder)

It treats disordered five zang organs and disharmony of the sanjiao with stuffiness and oppression of the heart and abdomen, distension of the flanks and ribs, congested, stagnant wind qi, vexing pain of the limbs and joints, deficiency puffiness of the head and face, slight swelling of the hands and feet, dryness and roughness of the stomach and intestines and difficult constipation. Even the elderly and those with weak qi may take it. It also treats back pain and flank pain that interfere with eating, and leg qi attacking upward with fullness and oppression of the chest and abdomen and blocked bowels.

qianghuo (notopterygium; rhizome head removed)　zisu (perilla; stems and leaves, coarse stalks removed)　chenxiang (aquilaria)　Xuanzhou mugua (chaenomeles from Xuanzhou; thinly sliced, dried over a fire)　dafupi (areca husk; roasted until scorched yellow; 1 liang each)　xiongqiong (ligusticum)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; stir-fried)　chenpi (chinpi; aged tangerine peel; white pith removed)　muxiang (mokko; costus root)　binglang (areca; wrapped in flour, roasted until cooked, flour removed)　baizhu (byakujutsu; white atractylodes; 3 *fen* each)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take two large *qian* with one *zhan* of water, decoct down to six-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm, at any time.

## Notes

Sanhe san: for stuffiness of the heart and abdomen, flank distension, joint pain, facial puffiness, swollen limbs and constipation from disharmony of the zang organs and sanjiao, and for back and flank pain and leg qi rising upward; also suitable for the elderly and weak.
- Perilla, aquilaria, costus, areca husk, areca and tangerine peel move qi and free the bowels; chaenomeles and notopterygium remove damp; atractylodes, licorice and lovage regulate.
