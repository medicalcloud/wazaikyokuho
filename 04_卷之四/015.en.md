# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之四 015 華蓋散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huagai san (Kagaisan; Canopy Powder)

It treats the lungs affected by pathogenic cold with cough and rising qi, vexing fullness of the chest and diaphragm, stiffness of the neck and back, a heavy voice and nasal congestion, a clouded head and dizzy eyes, phlegm qi not clearing, and wheezing.

zisuzi (perilla seed; stir-fried)　chi fuling (bukuryo; red poria; skin removed)　sangbaipi (mulberry root bark; roasted)　chenpi (chinpi; aged tangerine peel; white pith removed)　xingren (kyonin; apricot kernel; skin and tips removed, stir-fried)　mahuang (mao; ephedra; roots and nodes removed; 1 liang each)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried; half a liang)

Powder these seven ingredients. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one *zhan* of water, decoct down to seven-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm, after meals.

## Notes

Huagai san: for cough, rising qi, chest fullness, neck and back stiffness, nasal congestion, dizziness, phlegm and wheezing when the lungs are affected by pathogenic cold.
- Ephedra, apricot kernel and licorice (Sanao tang [Sanyoto]) plus perilla seed, mulberry bark, tangerine peel and red poria to diffuse and lower the lungs. "Huagai" (canopy) is a name for the lungs.
