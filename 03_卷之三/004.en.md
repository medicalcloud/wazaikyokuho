# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 004 大沈香丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Da chenxiang wan (Major Aquilaria Pill)

It treats all cold qi attacking the heart and abdomen with stabbing pain, choking obstruction of the chest and diaphragm, vomiting of phlegm and water, belching and acid swallowing, bitter mouth and rough tongue and no desire for food; and cold qi between the bladder and kidneys attacking and surging, with stiffness of the lower back and spine, wringing pain of the navel and abdomen, counterflow cold of the hands and feet and slippery, frequent urination. It is also suitable for sudden heart pain, vomiting and diarrhoea of sudden turmoil, qi pain of mounting and conglomerations, and women's stabbing blood-qi pain.

Tiantai wuyao (lindera from Tiantai)　baizhi (angelica dahurica)　gansong (nardostachys; washed and sun-dried)　gancao (licorice; scorched; 2½ jin each)　jianghuang (turmeric; skin removed)　tanxiang (sandalwood)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed; 20 liang each)　bai doukou (white cardamom; skin removed; 10 liang)　chenxiang (aquilaria; 20 liang)　xiangfuzi (cyperus; hairs removed, scorched; 5 jin)

Powder the above. Knead with refined honey, twenty pills to each liang. Take one pill each time, chewed, with stir-fried ginger and salt decoction. When the original qi stirs [i.e. cold rises from below], take it with stir-fried fennel and hot wine. Take on an empty stomach before meals.

## Notes

Da chenxiang wan: for stabbing heart and abdominal pain, vomiting and acid reflux from cold qi, and for stiff back, umbilical pain, cold limbs and frequent urination from cold in the lower burner (bladder and kidneys), as well as sudden heart pain, sudden turmoil, mounting conglomerations and women's blood-qi pain.
- Aquilaria, lindera, cyperus, nardostachys, angelica dahurica, turmeric and sandalwood move qi; dried ginger, cinnamon and white cardamom warm.
