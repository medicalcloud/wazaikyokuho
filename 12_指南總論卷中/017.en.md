# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 017 論諸風脚膝緩弱 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On slack, weak legs and knees in the various winds. When from long-standing deficiency the blood and qi are declining and weak and there is no strength for walking or standing, give Huangqi wan, Mugua wan or Yu shanren jiangqi tang. For pain without redness or swelling, wash by pouring with Xifeng tang.

## Notes

On weak legs and knees in the various winds (long-standing deficiency and declining blood and qi): Huangqi wan, Mugua wan and Yu shanren jiangqi tang; external washing with Xifeng tang for pain without redness or swelling.
