# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 062 当帰丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Danggui wan (Tokigan; Chinese Angelica Pill)

It treats children with disharmony of cold and heat, green-yellow stools, frequent pain of the heart and abdomen, or qi fullness in the abdomen, or occasional vomiting counterflow, and no desire for milk or food.

bai shaoyao (shakuyaku; white peony root)　danggui (toki; Chinese angelica; lightly stir-fried)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng)　xiongqiong (Sichuan lovage; 3 *fen* each)　baizhu (byakujutsu; white atractylodes)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried; half a liang each)

Pound and sift the above into a powder and form pills the size of hemp seeds with flour paste boiled in water. For a three-year-old child, ten pills each time with gruel water, three times a day, adjusted further for the child's size.

## Notes

Danggui wan (Wu Zhige's additions): for green-yellow stools, abdominal pain, fullness, vomiting and poor feeding from disharmony of cold and heat in children. A different formula from the Danggui wan of juan 9, no. 023.
- Angelica, white peony and lovage harmonize the blood; ginseng, atractylodes and licorice supplement the spleen.
