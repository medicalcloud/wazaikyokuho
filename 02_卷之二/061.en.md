# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 061 来蘇散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Laisu san (Revival Powder)

It resolves and frees warm epidemics and cold damage in any season: vigorous fever of the body, headache with aversion to cold, stiffness of the neck and spine, pain all over the body, vexing thirst with oppression and confusion, difficult urination and defecation, a liking for lying down, little strength and complete lack of desire for food; and also all qi disorders, the five taxations and seven damages, and miasmic malaria of mountain mists with alternating chills and fever. It treats all of these.

chaihu (bupleurum; rhizome head removed)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried)　ganjiang (dried ginger; 2 liang each)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed, not exposed to fire)　jiegeng (platycodon)　fangfeng (saposhnikovia)　jingjie spikes (schizonepeta)　wujiapi (acanthopanax bark; 1 liang each)　shaoyao (peony root; half a liang)　mahuang (ephedra; nodes removed)　chenpi (aged tangerine peel; white pith removed; 1½ liang each)　huangqi (astragalus; soaked overnight in honey water and roasted; 1 *fen*)

Make the above into a fine powder. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one *zhan* of water and three slices of fresh ginger, decoct together down to eight-tenths and take it hot, at any time. Taken regularly it harmonizes and smooths the triple burner, wards off miasmic qi and improves eating.

## Notes

Laisu san (bureau additions): for vigorous fever, headache, chills, neck and back stiffness, general pain and vexing thirst in seasonal warm epidemics and cold damage, and for qi disorders, the five taxations and seven damages and miasmic malaria.
- Ephedra, bupleurum, saposhnikovia and schizonepeta release the exterior; dried ginger and cinnamon warm; astragalus, peony and licorice supplement; tangerine peel and platycodon regulate qi; acanthopanax dispels wind-damp.
- The base text writes 荊芥穂五加皮 without a space, but these are two ingredients.
