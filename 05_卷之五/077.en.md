# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 077 養腎散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yangshen san (Kidney-Nourishing Powder)

It treats deficient, damaged kidney qi with pain of the lower back, legs, joints and bones, inability of the knees and shins to bend and stretch, and slack, weak legs and knees from long illness. Take one *zi* each time on an empty stomach with bean-steeped wine. After taking it there is numbness for a little while, and shortly the illness suddenly heals along with the qi of the medicine. For pain within the bones, chew walnut kernels and take it mixed in wine; in severe cases three to five doses. It treats wind, cold and damp alike.

quanxie (whole scorpion; half a liang)　tianma (gastrodia; 3 *qian*)　cangzhu (black atractylodes; processed; 1 liang)　fuzi (bushi; aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　caowutou (wild aconite; raw, skin and navel removed; 2 *qian* each)

Make the above into a fine powder. Take it mixed in warm wine on an empty stomach.

## Notes

Yangshen san (Chunyou-era additions): for pain of the back, legs, joints and bones, inability to flex the knees and shins, and weak legs after long illness from damaged kidney qi; said to treat wind, cold and damp alike.
- Scorpion and gastrodia dispel wind; aconite and raw wild aconite disperse cold-damp; black atractylodes is added. The dose is one *zi* (a tiny amount); the numbness after taking it is due to the aconite.
