# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 017 進食丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jinshi wan (Appetite-Promoting Pill)

It treats all of the following: undigested milk and food, distension and fullness of the heart and abdomen, vigorous fever with coarse breathing, vomiting of phlegm with counterflow, borborygmus and diarrhoea with undigested rice and grain; or red and white dysentery with abdominal pain and tenesmus; as well as food conglomerations, milk aggregations and string-qi glomus.

daizheshi (haematite; burned, quenched in vinegar, ground)　danggui (Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed, lightly stir-fried)　zhusha (cinnabar; ground and water-ground)　zhiqiao (bitter orange; pulp removed, stir-fried slightly yellow with bran)　muxiang (costus root; half a liang each)　shexiang (musk; finely ground; 1 *fen*)　badou shuang (defatted croton seed powder; half a *fen*)

Pound and sift the above medicines into a powder, mix in the ground medicines evenly and form pills the size of hemp seeds with flour paste. Give one pill per year of age with warm rice water, adjusting for deficiency or repletion; after meals.

## Notes

Jinshi wan: for undigested milk and food, abdominal distension, high fever, coarse breathing, vomiting of phlegm, borborygmus and diarrhoea with undigested food; for red and white dysentery with pain and tenesmus; and for food and milk accumulations and string-qi glomus in children.
- Defatted croton seed purges accumulations; haematite, bitter orange and costus bring down and move qi; angelica, cinnabar and musk are added.
