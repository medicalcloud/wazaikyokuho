# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 050 黄竜丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huanglong wan (Yellow Dragon Pill)

[It treats] men and women with latent summerheat, fever with thirst, vomiting and nausea, and summerheat toxin that has not healed after many years.

huanglian (coptis; rootlets removed; 32 liang)　good wine (5 *sheng*)

Boil the coptis in the wine until dry, then grind to a fine powder. Knead with a paste of flour boiled in water and form pills the size of wutong seeds. Take thirty pills each time, swallowed with hot water. It also treats excessive damage by wine with strong toxin causing rectal bleeding and diarrhoea: swallow with warm rice gruel before meals, two doses a day.

## Notes

Huanglong wan (Wu Zhige's additions): for fever, thirst and vomiting from latent summerheat, long-standing summerheat toxin, and rectal bleeding and diarrhoea from excessive drinking.
- A single-ingredient pill of coptis boiled dry in wine (wine-boiled coptis pill).
