# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 052 丹参膏 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Danshen gao (Salvia Paste)

It treats breast swelling and breast abscess with toxic qi flaring red and hot, gradually becoming attacking, stabbing pain, as well as hard bound breast lumps that do not disperse. It frees and smooths the meridians and collaterals and guides out congestion and stagnation.

danshen (tanjin; salvia root)　chi shaoyao (shakuyaku; red peony root)　baizhi (byakushi; angelica dahurica; equal parts)

Chop the above finely, steep in wine for three nights, add half a jin of lard, decoct gently until the angelica dahurica turns yellow, strain off the dregs and add 1 liang of yellow wax. Each time apply a little, from time to time.

## Notes

Danshen gao (Wu Zhige's additions): an ointment for breast swelling and breast abscess with red heat and stabbing pain, and for hard breast lumps that do not disperse.
- Salvia, red peony and angelica dahurica steeped in wine, boiled in lard and set with yellow wax.
