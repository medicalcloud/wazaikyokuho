# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 002 沢蘭丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zelan wan (Lycopus Pill)

It treats postpartum taxation damage with deficiency and weakness of the viscera and bowels not yet recovered, disharmony of qi and blood, emaciated weak limbs, fatigue with little strength, a withered yellow complexion, constant fright palpitations, much sweating with a liking for lying down, and inability to eat. Taken regularly every day within a hundred days after childbirth, it strengthens qi, augments blood, warms the lower viscera and improves eating.

huangqi (astragalus)　zelan (lycopus; stalks removed)　niuxi (achyranthes; shoots removed, soaked in wine overnight)　renshen (ginseng; rhizome head removed)　chi shizhi (red halloysite; calcined; 1 liang each)　fuzi (aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　muxiang (costus root)　bixie (dioscorea hypoglauca)　bai fuling (white poria; skin removed)　xuduan (dipsacus; 3 *fen* each)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　xiongqiong (Sichuan lovage)　baizhu (white atractylodes)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried)　danggui (Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed, chopped, lightly stir-fried)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried until slightly red; half a liang each)　shu gan dihuang (prepared rehmannia; washed clean, steamed with wine, dried over a fire; 1½ liang)

Powder the above and form pills the size of wutong seeds with refined honey. Take thirty pills each time with warm rice water, on an empty stomach before meals.

## Notes

Zelan wan: for postpartum taxation damage with weak viscera, disharmony of qi and blood, wasting, weakness, sallow complexion, fright palpitations, sweating, a tendency to stay in bed and poor appetite; regular use for the hundred days after childbirth is recommended.
- Astragalus, ginseng, atractylodes, poria, licorice, prepared rehmannia, angelica and lovage supplement qi and blood; lycopus and achyranthes move blood; aconite, cinnamon and dried ginger warm the lower body; red halloysite, dioscorea, dipsacus and costus are added.
