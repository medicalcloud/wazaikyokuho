# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 056 鉤藤膏 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Gouteng gao (Uncaria Paste)

It treats fetal cold and stomach cold in children with gripping pain of the belly, crying at night, vomiting of milk and food, green diarrhea, an appearance like fright convulsions, and occasional cold sweat.

jianghuang (turmeric; 2 *qian*)　moyao (myrrh; ground separately)　muxiang (mokko; costus root)　ruxiang [frankincense; ground separately; 4 *qian* each (one version has twenty momordica seeds, oil removed, ground)]

Make the above into a fine powder and knead with refined honey into a paste. For each dose of 3 *qian*, give the child one pill the size of a euryale seed dissolved in decocted uncaria decoction, adjusting for size, at any time.

## Notes

Gouteng gao (Chunyou-era additions): for gripping abdominal pain, night crying, vomiting of milk, green diarrhea, an appearance like convulsions and cold sweats from fetal and stomach cold in children.
- Turmeric, myrrh, frankincense and costus move qi and blood and stop pain; taken with uncaria decoction.
- Emendation: the name "Gouteng" reads "Diaoteng" in the base text; emended following the Siku quanshu and Executive Yuan editions.
- "For each dose of 3 *qian*, the child one pill" is hard to construe; it is translated literally.
