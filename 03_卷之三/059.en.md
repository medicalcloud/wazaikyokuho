# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 059 奪命抽刀散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Duoming choudao san (Life-Seizing Knife-Withdrawing Powder)

It treats men and women with accumulated cold of the spleen and stomach, disharmony of the middle burner, deficiency glomus below the heart, abdominal pain, counterflow fullness of the chest and flanks, choking obstruction, vomiting of cold phlegm, inability to get food down, belching and acid swallowing, a bitter mouth without taste and no desire for food; and women with long-standing, unbearable stabbing blood-qi pain.

ganjiang (dried ginger; chopped, stir-fried black together with half a liang of croton seeds, the croton then removed)　liangjiang (galangal; stir-fried with 100 blister beetles, the beetles then removed; 20 liang each)　nuomi (glutinous rice; stir-fried; 25 liang)　shichangpu (acorus; not exposed to fire; 22 liang)

Process the above clean and make a fine powder. Take 2 *qian* each time with a little salt, stirred into boiling water, at any time. Taken regularly it awakens the spleen and stomach and improves eating. This medicine greatly resolves wine toxin; it is best taken on an empty stomach before meals, or mixed in warm wine.

## Notes

Duoming choudao san (Baoqing-era additions): for glomus below the heart, abdominal pain, counterflow fullness, vomiting of cold phlegm and poor appetite from accumulated spleen-stomach cold, and for women's unbearable stabbing blood-qi pain.
- Dried ginger is stir-fried with croton and galangal with blister beetles to transfer their force (the croton and beetles are discarded), and glutinous rice and acorus are added. The name refers to removing pain as if withdrawing a knife.
