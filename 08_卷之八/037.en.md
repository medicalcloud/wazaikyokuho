# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 037 化膿排膿内補十宣散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huanong painong neibu shixuan san (Pus-Transforming, Pus-Expelling, Internally Supplementing Ten Dispersals Powder)

(Also called Zheli shibu san.) It treats all abscesses, flat-abscesses, sores and boils. Those not yet formed are quickly dispersed; those already formed quickly burst, the spoiled pus comes out by itself without being squeezed by hand, and the malign flesh goes away by itself without recourse to the knife. After taking the medicine the pain is suddenly reduced; its effect is divine.

huangqi (astragalus; that from Mianshang is best: pieces half like arrow shafts, two or three *chi* long, with unforked heads; wash clean, cut into one-*cun* lengths, hammer and tear into threads, moisten thoroughly with salt water, put in a cup and steam over a pot of boiling water for as long as it takes to cook rice, dry over a fire, and mill with the other medicines into a fine powder; 1 liang)　renshen (ginseng; that from Silla is best; choose compact, heavy, solid, moist pieces, wash clean, remove the rhizome head, slice thinly, dry over a fire and pound for use)　danggui (Chinese angelica; take that from Sichuan, choosing large pieces shaped like a horse's tail, moist, sweet, pungent and fragrant; wash in warm water, slice thinly and dry over a fire; 2 liang each)　houpo (magnolia bark; use that from Zijian, thick, purple, oozing oil when pressed with a fingernail; remove the coarse bark, slice, cover overnight with ginger juice, scorch until cooked and dry over a fire; do not use Guipu)　jiegeng (platycodon; the genuine kind has a core and a bitter taste; the kind without a core and with a sweet taste is jining (adenophora), which governs the resolution of drug toxins—never use it by mistake; wash clean, remove head and tail, slice thinly and dry over a fire)　guixin (cinnamon heart; use the thin rolled kind; by the ancient method only 2½ *qian* is taken from each liang of cinnamon with its bark, so to use 1 liang one should buy 4 liang; when the other medicines are done, grind it separately and then add it; it must not be exposed to fire)　xiongqiong (Sichuan lovage; that from Sichuan is best; nowadays large lumps of Fu lovage are mostly used; wash clean, slice and dry over a fire)　fangfeng (saposhnikovia; choose fresh, fragrant pieces, wash clean, slice and dry over a fire)　gancao (licorice; used raw)　baizhi (angelica dahurica; 1 liang each)

For these ten ingredients, the choice of drugs must be of the finest quality; all are cleaned, sun-dried and dried over a fire until extremely dry before weighing; except for the cinnamon heart, pound and sift them together into a fine powder, and add the cinnamon evenly. For each dose take from 3 *qian*, increasing to 5 or 6 *qian*, mixed in hot wine, several doses each day and night; the more the better. Continue until the opening of the sore has closed; taking it further is even better, for it supplements the earlier damage and prevents later trouble. Those who do not drink wine take it with strongly decocted costus decoction, though this is not as good as the force of wine. Those who cannot drink much may make an effort to mix it in wine from time to time and resolve the wine with costus decoction; the effect should then be no less than with wine. In general, abscesses and flat-abscesses arise from congealed, stagnant blood and qi and congested, bound wind toxin; if not treated early, they destroy the flesh outwardly and attack the viscera and bowels inwardly, and the harm is very great. As soon as it is noticed, take the medicine, doubling the number of doses; taking it to the point of intoxication makes the effect even faster. It disperses wind toxin, moves the channels and network vessels, expels pus and stops pain, and generates and grows flesh. The nature of the medicine is mild and balanced; the elderly, children, women and unmarried girls may all take it.

## Notes

Huanong painong neibu shixuan san (Zheli shibu san; Shaoxing-era additions): for all abscesses, sores and boils; said to disperse those not yet formed and to bring formed ones to burst and drain without lancing.
- Ten ingredients: astragalus, ginseng and angelica supplement from within; magnolia bark, platycodon, cinnamon heart, lovage, saposhnikovia, angelica dahurica and licorice expel pus and disperse wind toxin. It details how to choose and prepare each drug (e.g., distinguishing platycodon from adenophora). Taken in large amounts in hot wine. Later known as "Shixuan san" or "Tuoli shibu san".
- "Zheli" follows the base text's character (an exception in charfix).
