# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 059 滋血湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zixue tang (Blood-Enriching Decoction)

It treats women with hot blood and deficient qi, rough, stagnant, blocked menses causing blood to gather, numb limbs, hot flesh with sores, painful weariness of the whole body, on the verge of consumptive taxation. Other medicines must not be taken recklessly; only this should be used to nourish and free. (The patterns treated are also the same as for the earlier Zixue tang; the two may be compared.)

mabiancao (verbena)　jingjie sui (schizonepeta spikes; 4 liang each)　mudanpi (moutan bark; 1 liang)　chi shaoyao (shakuyaku; red peony root)　zhiqiao (bitter orange; core removed, stir-fried with bran)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　danggui (toki) (Chinese angelica; shoots removed, stir-fried)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage; 2 liang each)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 4 *qian* with one mume fruit and two *zhan* of water, decoct down to one *zhan*, remove the dregs and take it before meals on an empty stomach, four or five times a day. Those with this pattern, after half a month or a month, find the menses flow naturally and the hundred illnesses are all removed; divine effect.

## Notes

Zixue tang (bureau additions): for rough, stagnant or blocked menses from hot blood and deficient qi in women, with numb limbs, hot skin with sores, generalized aching and incipient consumption. A different formula from the earlier Zixue tang (juan 9, no. 038).
- Verbena, moutan, red peony, angelica and lovage move blood; schizonepeta, bitter orange and cinnamon are added; decocted with mume.
