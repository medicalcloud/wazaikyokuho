# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 061 十四友丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shisi you wan (Fourteen Friends Pill)

It supplements deficiency of the heart and kidneys, [for] fearful throbbing and confusion, unsettled spirit and will, and unquiet sleep. Therefore the *Classic* says: "When a zang organ has been damaged and the emotions lean to one side, a person cannot know their illness, and lying down is not peaceful."

shu dihuang (prepared rehmannia)　bai fuling (bukuryo; white poria)　bai fushen (white poria with root; wood removed)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng)　suanzaoren (sansonin; jujube seed; stir-fried)　baiziren (arborvitae seed; ground separately)　zishiying (murasakisekiei; fluorite; ground separately)　rougui (cinnamon bark)　ejiao (akyo; donkey-hide gelatin; stir-fried with clamshell powder)　danggui (toki) (Chinese angelica)　huangqi (ogi; astragalus)　yuanzhi (onji; polygala; soaked in hot water, core removed, sprinkled with wine and steamed; 1 liang each)　chensha (cinnabar; ground separately; 1 *fen*)　longchi (dragon tooth; ground separately; 2 liang)

Powder the above, combine with the four separately ground ingredients and form pills the size of wutong seeds with refined honey. Take thirty pills each time after meals, swallowed with jujube decoction.

## Notes

Shisi you wan (bureau additions): for fearful throbbing, confusion, an unsettled spirit and insomnia from deficiency of the heart and kidneys.
- Fourteen ingredients (hence the name): ginseng, astragalus, rehmannia, angelica and donkey-hide gelatin supplement qi and blood; poria, poria with root, jujube seed, arborvitae seed and polygala nourish the heart; fluorite, cinnabar and dragon tooth settle; cinnamon is added.
- The quotation is from *Suwen*, "Bingneng lun".
