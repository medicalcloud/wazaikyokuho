# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之四 032 橘皮半夏湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jupi banxia tang (Tangerine Peel and Pinellia Decoction)

It treats weak lungs and stomach with a liking for sour and cold foods, cold phlegm lodged and accumulated, retching and nausea, and thick sticky drool and spittle; or accumulated vomiting so that neither gruel nor medicine will stay down, counterflow cold of the hands and feet, dizziness and heaviness of the body. It also treats cold damage and seasonal qi with a desire to vomit but inability to vomit, a desire to retch but inability to retch, clouding, confusion, oppression and disorder; or excessive drinking with cold in the middle and lodged rheum, the sound of drool in the throat and incessant dry retching.

chenpi (aged tangerine peel; white pith removed)　banxia (pinellia; boiled; 7 liang each)

Chop these two ingredients into a coarse powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with ten slices of fresh ginger and two *zhan* of water, decoct down to one medium *zhan*, remove the dregs and take it warm, at any time. Keep the dregs of two doses, combine them into one dose and decoct again.

## Notes

Jupi banxia tang (Wu Zhige's additions): for retching, nausea and sticky saliva from weak lungs and stomach with cold phlegm, persistent vomiting with cold limbs and dizziness, nausea in cold damage, and gurgling phlegm and dry retching from rheum after excessive drinking.
- Tangerine peel and pinellia with plenty of fresh ginger (in the lineage of Xiao banxia tang and Jupi tang of the *Jingui yaolüe*).
