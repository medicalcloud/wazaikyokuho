# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 013 論諸風熱上攻面生熱瘡者 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On wind-heat attacking upward in the various winds with hot sores on the face. Give Qufeng wan, Longhu dan, Paifeng tang, Huma san (Gomasan), Heshouwu san (Kashusan), Qianghuo wan, Chuanxiong wan (Senkyugan), Bailong wan or Xiongxi wan. For a sensation as of crawling insects, give Zhuifeng san.

## Notes

On hot facial sores from wind-heat attacking upward: Qufeng wan, Longhu dan, Paifeng tang, Huma san, Heshouwu san, Qianghuo wan, Chuanxiong wan, Bailong wan and Xiongxi wan; Zhuifeng san for a crawling sensation.
