# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 031 当帰散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Danggui san (Tokisan; Chinese Angelica Powder)

It treats postpartum failed blood that does not disperse, a hard "child's pillow" (postpartum abdominal lump) with pain that comes and goes, as well as wind-cold contending within, taking advantage of the deficiency just after delivery, with lochia not flowing freely and hard distension of the navel and abdomen (one version reads "hard pain").

honglanhua (safflower)　guijian (euonymus twig; central wood removed)　danggui (toki) (Chinese angelica; shoots removed, stir-fried; 1 liang each)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one large *zhan* of wine, decoct down to seven-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm before eating gruel.

## Notes

Danggui san: for postpartum stagnant failed blood and "child's pillow" pain (a hard, painful postpartum abdominal lump), and for retained lochia with hard umbilical distension when wind-cold enters through postpartum deficiency.
- Three ingredients (safflower, euonymus twig, angelica) decocted in wine.
