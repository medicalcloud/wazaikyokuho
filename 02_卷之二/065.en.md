# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 065 荊芥散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jingjie san (Schizonepeta Powder)

It treats cold damage with headache, nasal congestion with running nose, a heavy voice and dry throat, fullness and oppression of the chest and diaphragm, and a splitting headache.

tiannanxing (arisaema; soaked and washed, boiled soft in fresh ginger juice, sliced, dried over a fire)　caowutou (wild aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　jingjie spikes (schizonepeta; half a liang each)　shigao (gypsum; ground; 1 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder. For each dose take 2 *qian* with 1 *qian* of old tea powder, half a mouthful of fresh ginger juice, three mint leaves and two *zhan* of water, decoct together down to eight-tenths and take it at a comfortable warmth.

## Notes

Jingjie san (bureau additions): for colds with nasal congestion, running nose, heavy voice, dry throat, chest oppression and a splitting headache.
- Schizonepeta scatters wind, arisaema and wild aconite expel wind-phlegm, gypsum restrains heat; decocted with tea.
