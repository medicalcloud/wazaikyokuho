# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 110 茴香湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huixiang tang (Fennel Drink)

Its uses are the same as the earlier Huixiang tang.

baizhi (angelica dahurica; not exposed to fire)　rougui (cinnamon bark; not exposed to fire; 2 liang each)　jiegeng (platycodon; dried over a fire; 30 liang)　huixiang (fennel)　gancao (licorice; both stir-fried; 6 liang each)

Powder the above. Take 1 *qian* each time with a little salt, mixed with boiling water, before meals. Taken regularly it loosens the middle, augments qi and warms the stomach.

## Notes

Huixiang tang (from here the layer of Baoqing-era additions): the same uses as the earlier Huixiang tang (juan 10, no. 109); a different formula, mainly platycodon with angelica dahurica, cinnamon, fennel and licorice.
