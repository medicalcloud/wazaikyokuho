# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 050 木香餅子 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Muxiang (mokko) bingzi (Costus Cakes)

It treats deficiency cold of the spleen meridian and cold phlegm in the stomach duct, choking stuffiness of the chest and diaphragm, a bland mouth and rough tongue, griping pain of the heart and abdomen, vomiting of old water, pain and oppression below the flanks, dyspnea fullness with urgent breathing, lassitude with little strength and complete lack of desire for food. Taken regularly it loosens the chest and diaphragm, scatters stagnant qi, disperses lodged cold and makes food taste good.

suoshaner (amomum; 12 liang)　tanxiang (sandalwood; 4 liang)　gansong (nardostachys; washed; 5 liang)　dingxiang (clove; 4½ liang)　peng'ezhu (zedoary; 10 liang)　muxiang (costus root; 2½ liang)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills with a separately prepared licorice paste, 250 pills to each liang, pinched into small cakes. Take three to five cakes each time, chewed thoroughly, with ginger decoction; warm wine is also fine; at any time.

## Notes

Muxiang bingzi (Shaoxing-era additions): for chest stuffiness, bland mouth, abdominal pain, vomiting, flank pain, dyspnea fullness, lassitude and poor appetite from spleen deficiency cold and cold phlegm.
- Amomum, sandalwood, nardostachys, clove, zedoary and costus made into tiny cakes with licorice paste and chewed.
