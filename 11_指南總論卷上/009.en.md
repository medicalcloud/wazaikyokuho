# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷上 009 論炮炙三品薬石類例・木部 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wood Section

Cinnamon bark: to use, do not expose to fire; first remove the coarse bark so that the flavorful inner part shows, chop, then use in medicines. In medicines for pregnant women it is better lightly stir-fried.
Poria and polyporus: to use, first remove the black skin, chop and dry over a fire.
Poria with root: to use, first remove the coarse skin and the wood held in its center, chop, dry over a fire and use in medicines.
Jujube seed: to use, first stir-fry over a gentle fire until thoroughly fragrant and cooked, then grind and break open for use.
Phellodendron: to use, first remove the coarse bark, smear with honey and roast before using in medicines.
Dried lacquer: to use, pound and stir-fry until cooked before using in medicines; otherwise it damages the intestines and stomach.
Vitex fruit: to use, soak in wine, steam for a full day, take out and dry over a fire.
Eucommia: to use, first remove the coarse outer bark cleanly, smear with ginger juice and roast until fragrant and cooked, until there are no threads; or simply chop, mix with ginger juice and stir-fry until the threads break.
Aquilaria and sandalwood: to use, first chop separately, pound and sift into a fine powder, then use in medicines.
Mulberry root bark: to use, first chop and lightly stir-fry before using in medicines.
Evodia: to use, first soak in boiling water and wash seven times, dry over a fire and lightly stir-fry before using in medicines. If used for external disorders and not taken by mouth, it need not be washed.
Areca: to use, choose ones that sit squarely and are firm and solid, first scrape off the base with a knife and slice finely; do not expose to fire, lest it lose its strength and effect; if used cooked, it is better not to use it at all.
Gardenia: to use, first remove the husk and seed hairs, soak overnight in licorice water, strain out, dry over a fire and use in medicines.
Unripe and ripe bitter orange: to use, old pieces are required; first soak in hot water, rub off the pulp, dry over a fire and stir-fry with bran until scorched, until fragrant and cooked.
Magnolia bark: to use, first scrape off the coarse bark until the red heart shows, roast three times with ginger juice until fragrant and cooked; or simply chop and stir-fry with ginger juice.
Cornus: to use, first pound and dry over a fire; or simply use with the stones.
Areca husk: to use, first wash with wine, then again with soybean juice, chop and dry over a fire; then it may be used.
Croton seed: to use, first remove the shell, germ and membrane, pound soft, wrap in paper and press out the oil, taking the "frost" for use in medicines. Another method: after removing shell, germ and membrane, boil in water, changing the water five times with one boil each, and grind; otherwise it causes oppression.
Sichuan pepper: to use, first remove twigs, stalks, seeds and closed fruits, stir-fry lightly, spread on the ground on paper, cover with a cup to make it "sweat", then use in medicines.
Gleditsia pod: to use, choose fat, long, large pods not eaten by worms, scrape off the skin, strings and seeds, smear with ghee and roast to a scorched yellow before using in medicines.
Chebule: to use, first blast-fry in hot ashes, remove the stone and take the flesh, soak in wine and steam for a full day, take out and dry over a fire before using in medicines.
Chinaberry fruit: to use, first soak in wine to moisten, wait until the skin and stone [can be removed], peel off the empty skin, dry over a fire, stir-fry with flour, pound coarsely in a wooden mortar, sift to remove the stones, then use in medicines.
Elm seed paste: to use, first lightly stir-fry; then it may be used.
Borneol, dragon's blood, frankincense, pine resin and the like: to use, grind each separately extremely fine before using in medicines.

## Notes

The Wood section of the processing examples: methods for each wood drug (cinnamon not exposed to fire, coarse bark removed; dried lacquer fried or it harms the intestines; eucommia roasted with ginger juice until the threads break; areca never heated; croton seed defatted to "frost"; Sichuan pepper fried to "sweat"; etc.).
