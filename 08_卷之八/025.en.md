# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 025 奪命丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Duoming dan (Life-Snatching Elixir)

It treats small-intestine mounting qi, of many years' or recent standing, with one-sided sagging and cramping pain, gripping pain below the navel leading to oppression and confusion; as well as swollen, hard external kidney (scrotum) growing larger day by day, and damp itching of the genitals that turns into sores with scratching.

wuzhuyu (evodia; twigs and stalks removed; 1 jin: 4 liang soaked in wine, 4 liang in vinegar, 4 liang in hot water and 4 liang in a boy's urine, each soaked overnight, then dried together over a fire)　zexie (alisma; ash and earth removed; 2 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with flour paste boiled in wine. Take fifty pills each time on an empty stomach before meals, swallowed with salt water or wine.

## Notes

Duoming dan (Wu Zhige's additions): for small-intestine mounting (one-sided scrotal sagging with cramping pain, pain below the navel, oppression), old or new, and for swollen, hard scrotum and damp, itchy genital sores.
- Evodia soaked four ways (wine, vinegar, hot water, boy's urine) and dried, plus alisma.
