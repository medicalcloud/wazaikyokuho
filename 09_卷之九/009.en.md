# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 009 呉茱萸湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wuzhuyu tang (Goshuyuto; Evodia Decoction)

It treats all cases in women of fundamentally deficient visceral qi harboring old wind-cold, with fullness and pain of the chest and diaphragm, gripping, stabbing pain of the abdomen and flanks, vomiting and nausea, reduced eating, deficiency puffiness of the body and face, aversion to cold with shivering, or unceasing diarrhea, shortness of qi, emaciation and fatigue; as well as cases where childbirth in this state causes sudden deficiency of visceral qi, pathogenic cold prevails within and the old illness grows worse.

jiegeng (kikyo; platycodon; shoots removed)　fangfeng (bofu; saposhnikovia; shoots and forks removed)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried)　danggui (toki; Chinese angelica; shoots removed, lightly stir-fried)　xixin (saishin; asarum; shoots removed; half a liang each)　shu gan dihuang (kanjio; prepared rehmannia; 3 *fen*)　wuzhuyu (goshuyu; evodia; washed seven times in hot water, lightly stir-fried; 2 liang)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one *zhan* of water, decoct down to eight-tenths, strain finely to remove the dregs and take it hot, on an empty stomach before meals.

## Notes

Wuzhuyu tang: for fullness and pain of the chest, stabbing pain of the abdomen and flanks, vomiting, poor appetite, puffiness, chills with shivering, diarrhea, breathlessness and wasting from deficient visceral qi with old wind-cold in women, and for worsening of old illness from sudden visceral deficiency after childbirth.
- Mainly evodia; dried ginger and asarum warm; angelica and prepared rehmannia nourish blood; platycodon, saposhnikovia and licorice are added. A different formula from the *Shanghan lun* Wuzhuyu tang.
