# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 022 備急丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Beiji wan (Bikyugan; Emergency Pill)

It treats all the various sudden illnesses of the heart and abdomen, malign strike and visiting hostility, with distension and fullness of the heart and abdomen, sudden pain as if stabbed by a knife, hurried breathing and clenched jaw.

ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried; 1 liang)　badou (hazu; croton seed; skin and oil removed, ground)　dahuang (daio; rhubarb; 2 liang each)

Powder the above and form pills the size of wutong seeds with refined honey. Take three pills each time with warm water, at any time.

## Notes

Beiji wan (from here the layer of Wu Zhige's additions): for sudden illnesses of the heart and abdomen, and malign strike and visiting hostility with distension, sudden knife-like pain, hurried breathing and clenched jaw.
- Three ingredients (croton seed, rhubarb, dried ginger) for urgent purging; the same composition as the *Jingui yaolüe* Sanwu beiji wan.
