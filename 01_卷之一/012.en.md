# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 012 麝香天麻丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shexiang tianma wan (Musk and Gastrodia Pill)

It treats wind impediment with loss of control of the hands and feet, or weakness and trembling, congealed and rough blood and vessels, stubborn numbness of the flesh, pain all over the body, difficulty turning over, and cramping of the sinews and vessels with inability to bend and stretch.

purple-backed dried fupingcao (duckweed; earth removed; 4 liang)　mahuang (ephedra; roots and nodes removed; 2 liang)　fangfeng (saposhnikovia; rhizome head and forks removed)　tianma (gastrodia; rhizome head removed; that from Yunzhou is best; 1 liang each)

When the above four ingredients have been processed according to method, mill them to a fine powder.

moyao (myrrh; ground separately extremely fine)　zhusha (cinnabar; ground and water-ground; 2 liang each)　anxixiang (benzoin; ground separately fine)　ruxiang (frankincense; ground)　shexiang (musk; ground; 1 liang each)　xuejie (dragon's blood; ground separately extremely fine; 3 liang)　huaijiao (sophora gum; ground separately fine; 1½ liang)

Except for the ground medicines, grind the milled medicines together with them and mix evenly; simmer refined and strained white granulated honey with the benzoin, knead into a mass, pound it well in a mortar and form pills the size of a pellet. Take one pill each time dissolved in warm wine or schizonepeta decoction, on an empty stomach; a slight sweat at the affected place shows the effect. If one does not wish to dissolve it, make pills the size of wutong seeds and take thirty pills each time with the same decoction as before.

## Notes

Shexiang tianma wan: for loss of control of the limbs, trembling, numbness, general pain and cramping from wind impediment.
- Duckweed, ephedra, saposhnikovia and gastrodia scatter wind; myrrh, frankincense, dragon's blood, benzoin and musk move the blood and stop pain.
- The base text reads 安心服 for 空心服 ("on an empty stomach"); since the Siku quanshu and Executive Yuan editions both read 空心服, it has been emended (see the emendation).
