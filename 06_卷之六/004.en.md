# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 004 洗心散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xixin san (Heart-Washing Powder)

It is suitable for wind congestion with vigorous fever, clouded, painful head and eyes, stiffness of the shoulders and back, vexing pain of the limbs and joints, hot qi surging upward, a bitter mouth and parched lips, swollen, painful throat, congestion and stagnation of phlegm and drool, thick sticky snivel and spittle, vexation and agitation of the heart-spirit, and rough eyes with painful pupils; as well as cold congestion with disharmony, nasal congestion with a heavy voice, dry throat with much thirst, vexing heat of the five hearts, red, rough urine and constipated, stagnant stools.

baizhu (white atractylodes; 1½ liang)　mahuang (ephedra; with nodes)　danggui (Chinese angelica; shoots removed, washed)　jingjie sui (schizonepeta spikes)　shaoyao (peony root)　gancao (licorice; scorched)　dahuang (rhubarb; wrapped in dough and roasted in embers, dough removed, sliced, dried over a fire; 6 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one *zhan* of water, add a little each of fresh ginger and mint, decoct together down to seven-tenths and take it warm. For children when bran-bean sores and rashes (pox and measles) are about to erupt, preceded by manic speech and much thirst, and for fright wind and accumulated heat, 1 *qian* may be given; in all cases at bedtime. For adults with congestion and repletion of the five viscera who need loose stools, increase to 4 or 5 *qian* and take it while hot.

## Notes

Xixin san: for vigorous fever, headache, stiff shoulders and back, joint pain, bitter mouth, sore throat, phlegm, agitation and eye pain from wind congestion; for nasal congestion, thirst, vexing heat of the five hearts, dark urine and constipation from cold congestion; and for children's incipient eruptions, fright wind and accumulated heat.
- Ephedra and schizonepeta release the exterior; rhubarb purges; angelica, peony, atractylodes and licorice are added.
