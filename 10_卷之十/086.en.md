# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 086 麦煎散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Maijian san (Wheat Decoction Powder)

It treats all of the following in children: cold damage accompanied by fright, with vomiting counterflow and vigorous fever, exterior and interior unresolved, coarse breathing with dyspnea and urgency, red face and spontaneous sweating, or manic speech with startled screaming, or no speech and no sweat; hives over the whole body, red and itchy, coming and going; tidal fever and seasonal illness; residual toxin of measles and pox not yet exhausted, with puffy swelling of the whole body and phlegm cough; transformation into acute or chronic fright wind with convulsions and eyes turned upward; and wind damage with drool, dyspnea and headache.

zhimu (chimo; anemarrhena)　digupi (lycium root bark; picked clean)　chi shaoyao (shakuyaku; red peony root)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried)　shigao (sekko; gypsum)　tinglizi (lepidium seed)　bai fuling (bukuryo; white poria; skin removed)　xingren (kyonin; apricot kernel; skin and tips removed, stir-fried with bran)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng)　huashi (kasseki; talc; half a liang each)　mahuang (mao; ephedra; roots and nodes removed; 1½ liang)

Make the above into a fine powder. Take 1 *qian* each time mixed in wheat decoction. For newborns who have caught wind-cold, with nasal congestion, fever, sneezing and much crying, give about one *zi* each time, likewise with wheat decoction.

## Notes

Maijian san (bureau additions): for cold damage with fright (vomiting, high fever, unresolved exterior and interior, dyspnea, red face, sweating, delirious screaming or mute without sweat), generalized hives, tidal and seasonal fevers, residual toxin of measles and pox with edema and phlegm cough, progression to fright wind, wind damage with phlegm, dyspnea and headache, and colds in newborns.
- Ephedra, apricot kernel and gypsum (Ma xing gan shi tang) plus anemarrhena, lycium root bark, red peony, lepidium, poria, talc and ginseng, taken with wheat decoction.
