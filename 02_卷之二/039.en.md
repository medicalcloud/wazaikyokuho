# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 039 保真湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Baozhen tang (True-Preserving Decoction)

For cold damage in any season, regardless of whether the pattern is yin or yang, take this medicine quickly as soon as one feels the illness starting, and it works at once.

gaoben (Chinese lovage; rhizome head removed)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage; 4 liang each)　gancao (licorice; stir-fried; 2 liang)　cangzhu (black atractylodes; washed, chopped, stir-fried with bran; 16 liang)

Cut the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water and three slices of fresh ginger, decoct together down to seven-tenths, remove the dregs and take it hot, at any time. Its divine effect cannot be fully described.

## Notes

Baozhen tang (Baoqing-era additions): for the very onset of seasonal cold damage (colds).
- Four ingredients: black atractylodes, with Chinese lovage and Sichuan lovage to scatter exterior wind-cold-damp.
