# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 094 豆蔻湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Doukou tang (Nutmeg Drink)

It treats all cold qi with distension and fullness of the heart and abdomen, glomus and stagnation of the chest and diaphragm, hiccup and vomiting, deficient, slippery diarrhoea, undigested water and grain, fatigue with little strength and no desire for food.

dingxiang zhizhang (clove twigs; 7 jin)　gancao (licorice; stir-fried; 11 jin)　baimian (white flour; stir-fried; 6 jin)　roudoukou (nutmeg; wrapped in dough and roasted in embers; 8 jin)

Powder the above together with 13 jin of stir-fried salt. Take 1 *qian* each time mixed with boiling water, before meals.

## Notes

Doukou tang (the first entry of juan 10, "Various drinks"): for abdominal distension, chest glomus, hiccup, vomiting, slippery diarrhoea, indigestion, fatigue and poor appetite from all cold qi.
- Nutmeg, clove twigs, licorice, white flour and salt powdered and taken mixed with hot water (these *tang* are prepared drinks of a tonic or beverage kind). The quantities in this "Various drinks" section are for large batches.
