# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 043 参蘇飲 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shensu yin (Ginseng and Perilla Drink)

It is suitable for colds with fever and headache, or for phlegm-rheum congealed and bound and thereby producing heat. If fever arises from a cold, follow the method for taking Yangwei tang: have the patient lie covered with bedding and give several doses in succession; a slight sweat brings recovery at once. If residual heat remains in the face, it should be taken further, slowly, and recovery will come naturally. For fever due to phlegm-rheum, simply give this medicine frequently day after day until the fever subsides; do not stop it prematurely. Although it contains peucedanum and pueraria, they merely release the flesh; since it also contains bitter orange and red tangerine peel, it naturally loosens the middle and eases the diaphragm without harming the spleen. It also greatly treats glomus and fullness of the middle duct with vomiting counterflow and nausea; for opening the stomach and improving appetite, nothing surpasses it. Do not hesitate because of its cool nature: it is effective for all fevers, regardless of cause. It is also suitable for children and unmarried women.

muxiang (costus root; half a liang)　zisuye (perilla leaf)　gange (dried pueraria; washed)　banxia (pinellia; washed seven times in hot water, processed with ginger juice, stir-fried)　qianhu (peucedanum; sprouts removed)　renshen (ginseng)　fuling (poria; skin removed; 3 *fen* each)　zhiqiao (bitter orange; pulp removed, stir-fried with bran)　jiegeng (platycodon; rhizome head removed)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried)　chenpi (aged tangerine peel; white pith removed; half a liang each)

Cut the above into pieces. For each dose take 4 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water, seven slices of ginger and one jujube, decoct down to six-tenths, remove the dregs and take it slightly hot, at any time. The *Yijian fang* omits the costus and has only ten ingredients.

## Notes

Shensu yin (Chunyou-era additions): for fever and headache in colds, fever from phlegm-rheum, and stomach glomus and vomiting. It is still used for colds and coughs in those with weak digestion.
- Perilla leaf, pueraria and peucedanum release the exterior; pinellia, poria, tangerine peel, bitter orange, platycodon and costus regulate phlegm and qi; ginseng and licorice supplement. It is also used in the after-care for the preceding Renshen yangwei tang. The *Yijian fang* version lacks costus.
