# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 029 木瓜丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Mugua wan (Chaenomeles Pill)

It is suitable, whether new or long-standing, for weakness of the kidney channel with heavy lower back and knees, swelling and itching of the thighs and legs that pour and break open into sores, dull pain in the soles and cramping of the sinews and vessels; or slack, weak lower back and knees, difficulty walking, hurried panting on movement, a dark sallow complexion, constipation and difficult urination, and reduced eating.

gouji (cibotium; hairs removed; 6 liang)　large ai (mugwort; stalks removed; 4 liang, kneaded into cakes with glutinous rice paste, dried over a fire and powdered)　mugua (chaenomeles; pulp removed; 4 liang)　tianma (gastrodia; rhizome head removed)　danggui (Chinese angelica; processed by soaking in wine)　bixie (dioscorea hypoglauca)　congrong (cistanche; rhizome head removed, soaked in wine)　niuxi (achyranthes; washed free of earth, soaked overnight in wine; 2 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with refined honey. Take twenty pills each time, gradually increasing to thirty, swallowed with warm wine on an empty stomach before meals; salt water is also fine.

## Notes

Mugua wan (Baoqing-era additions): a leg-qi formula for heavy lower back and knees, swollen itchy legs, sores, pain in the soles, cramping, difficulty walking, panting, dark complexion, constipation and poor appetite from weakness of the kidney channel; a different formula from the Mugua wan of juan 1, no. 083.
- Cibotium, achyranthes and cistanche supplement the lower back and legs; chaenomeles and dioscorea remove damp; gastrodia dispels wind; angelica and mugwort warm and move the blood.
