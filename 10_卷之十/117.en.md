# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 117 三倍湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Sanbei tang (Threefold Drink)

It treats disharmony of the spleen and stomach with oppression and fullness of the chest and diaphragm, undigested food, vomiting counterflow and nausea, or sudden turmoil with vomiting, stabbing pain of the heart and abdomen, borborygmus and diarrhoea, and water and grain not separating.

cao doukou ren (katsumada galangal seed; 2 liang)　gancao (licorice; 1 liang)　shengjiang (fresh ginger)　yan (salt; stir-fried; 5 liang each)

Mix the above evenly, put into a porcelain vessel to steep overnight, dry over a fire and powder. Take it mixed with boiling water.

## Notes

Sanbei tang (Wu Zhige's additions): a drink for chest oppression, indigestion, vomiting and nausea from disharmony of the spleen and stomach, and for vomiting, stabbing abdominal pain, borborygmus and diarrhoea of sudden turmoil.
- Katsumada galangal seed, licorice, fresh ginger and salt.
- Emendation: "disharmony of the spleen and stomach" (*bu he*) reads *zhong he* in the base text; emended following the Siku quanshu and Executive Yuan editions.
