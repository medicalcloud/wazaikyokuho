# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之四 013 款冬花散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Kuandonghua san (Coltsfoot Flower Powder)

It treats cold and congestion meeting so that the lung qi is not free, with cough, panting and fullness, vexation and oppression of the chest and diaphragm, phlegm repletion with exuberant drool, wheezing in the throat, nasal congestion with clear discharge, headache with dizziness as if covered, weary aching limbs, and swelling and pain of the throat.

kuandonghua (coltsfoot flower; stalks removed)　zhimu (anemarrhena)　sangye (mulberry leaf; washed, dried over a fire; 10 liang each)　banxia (pinellia; washed seven times in hot water, processed with ginger juice)　gancao (licorice; scorched; 20 liang each)　mahuang (ephedra; roots and nodes removed; 40 liang)　ejiao (donkey-hide gelatin; crushed and stir-fried into beads)　xingren (apricot kernel; skin and tips removed, stir-fried with bran)　beimu (fritillaria; core removed, stir-fried with bran; 20 liang each)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one *zhan* of water and three slices of fresh ginger, decoct together down to seven-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm after meals.

## Notes

Kuandonghua san: for cough, panting fullness, chest oppression, profuse phlegm, wheezing, nasal congestion, headache, body aches and sore throat when cold and congestion block the lung qi.
- Ephedra and apricot kernel diffuse the lungs; coltsfoot, fritillaria and pinellia treat cough and phlegm; anemarrhena, mulberry leaf and gelatin moisten and clear.
