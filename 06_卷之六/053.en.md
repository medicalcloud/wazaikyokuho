# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 053 育腸丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yuchang wan (Intestine-Nurturing Pill)

It treats weak, deficient intestines and stomach harboring raw and cold matter within, with abdominal distension and diarrhea, intermittent stabbing pain, tenesmus, red and white dysentery, sometimes turning to pus and blood, very frequent day and night, and failing to heal over a long time.

wumei rou (mume flesh)　huanglian (oren; coptis; fibrous roots removed; 1 *fen* each)　hezi pi (chebule fruit rind)　yingsuqiao (poppy husk; cap and fibers removed, roasted with honey)　roudoukou (nutmeg; wrapped in wet paper and roasted in embers; half a liang each)　danggui (toki; Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed, soaked in wine overnight, dried over a fire; 1 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with refined honey. Take thirty to fifty pills each time on an empty stomach before meals with rice water. For children make small pills and give them with decocted licorice-ginger decoction.

## Notes

Yuchang wan (Baoqing-era additions): for abdominal distension, diarrhea, stabbing pain, tenesmus, red and white dysentery and stools with pus and blood, frequent and long unhealed, from weak intestines and stomach harboring raw and cold matter.
- Mume, chebule, poppy husk and nutmeg astringe; coptis clears heat; angelica harmonizes the blood.
