# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 052 竹茹湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zhuru tang (Chikujoto; Bamboo Shavings Decoction)

It is suitable for pregnancy with picky eating, vomiting and headache, dizziness with staggering, phlegm counterflow with vexation and oppression, and disharmony of the limbs.

juhong (red tangerine peel; white thoroughly removed)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng)　baizhu (byakujutsu; white atractylodes)　maimendong zi (ophiopogon; core removed; 1 liang each)　bai fuling (bukuryo; white poria)　houpo (koboku; magnolia bark; processed with ginger juice; half a liang each)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; 1 *fen*)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one *zhan* of water and five slices of fresh ginger, add a lump of bamboo shavings the size of a pellet, decoct together down to seven-tenths, remove the dregs and take it.

## Notes

Zhuru tang (Wu Zhige's additions): for picky eating, vomiting, headache, dizziness, staggering, phlegm counterflow, vexation and disharmony of the limbs in pregnancy (morning sickness).
- Bamboo shavings, red tangerine peel, poria and magnolia bark remove phlegm and stop vomiting; ginseng, atractylodes, ophiopogon and licorice supplement.
