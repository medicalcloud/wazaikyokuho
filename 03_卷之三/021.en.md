# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 021 益智散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yizhi san (Yakuchisan; Alpinia Powder)

It treats cold damage with exuberant yin, stuffiness and fullness of the heart and abdomen, vomiting and diarrhea and cold hands and feet; and all cold qi rushing and surging with distension, fullness and wringing pain of the heart, flanks, navel and abdomen.

chuanwu (Sichuan aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed; 4 liang)　yizhi (alpinia oxyphylla; skin removed; 2 liang)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried; half a liang)　qingpi (green tangerine peel; white removed; 3 liang)

Make the above medicines into a powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with two *zhan* of water, a pinch of salt, five slices of fresh ginger and two jujubes broken open, decoct together down to eight-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm, before meals.

## Notes

Yizhi san: for abdominal fullness, vomiting, diarrhea and cold limbs in yin-exuberant cold damage, and for distension and wringing pain of the chest, flanks, navel and abdomen from rushing cold qi.
- Aconite and dried ginger warm, alpinia warms the spleen and kidneys, green tangerine peel breaks up qi.
