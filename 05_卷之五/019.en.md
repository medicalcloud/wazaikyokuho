# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 019 麝香鹿茸丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shexiang lurong wan (Musk and Deer Antler Velvet Pill)

It augments the true qi and supplements deficiency and exhaustion. It is suitable for damaged, depleted lower burner with wringing pain of the navel and abdomen, distension and fullness of both flanks, reduced eating, vexing pain of the limbs and joints, numbness of the hands and feet, heaviness of the lower back and thighs, difficulty walking, blurred vision, intercourse with ghosts in dreams at night, emissions and loss of essence, an unclear mind, impotence, slippery frequent urination, borborygmus from qi deficiency, spontaneous diarrhoea, deficiency vexation with night sweats, and internal dryness of the fluids.

lurong (deer antler velvet; hair singed off, soaked in wine and roasted; 70 liang)　shu gan dihuang (prepared rehmannia; washed clean, soaked in wine, steamed, dried over a fire; 10 jin)　fuzi (aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed; 140)　niuxi (achyranthes; sprouts removed, soaked overnight in wine, dried over a fire; 1 jin 4 liang)　duzhong (eucommia; coarse bark removed, stir-fried until the threads are gone; 3½ jin)　wuweizi (schisandra; 2 jin)　shanyao (Chinese yam; 4 jin)　roucongrong (cistanche; soaked overnight in wine; 3 jin)

Powder the above, form pills the size of wutong seeds with refined honey, and for each jin of pills use 1 *qian* of powdered musk for coating. Take twenty pills each time with warm wine; salt water is also fine; before meals. (Corrected by declaration in the twelfth month of the tenth year of Jiading.)

## Notes

Shexiang lurong wan (Shaoxing-era additions): for wringing umbilical pain, flank distension, poor appetite, joint pain, numbness, difficulty walking, blurred vision, dream intercourse, seminal emission, impotence, frequent urination, diarrhoea and night sweats from exhaustion of the lower burner.
- Deer antler velvet, aconite, cistanche, eucommia and achyranthes supplement kidney yang; rehmannia, Chinese yam and schisandra nourish yin; musk coating. A note at the end says it was corrected by declaration in the twelfth month of Jiading 10 (1217).
