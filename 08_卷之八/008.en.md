# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 008 神助散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shenzhu san (Divine Aid Powder)

(Formerly called Tingli san, Lepidium Powder.) It treats the ten kinds of water qi with swelling of the face, eyes, limbs and whole body which, when pressed with the hand, rises again as the hand is lifted, cough with dyspnea and hurried breathing, inability to lie in peace, a large swollen, distended abdomen, a bitter mouth and dry tongue, red, rough urine and constipation.

zexie (takusha; alisma; 2 liang)　jiaomu (shomoku) (Sichuan pepper seed; 1½ liang)　zhuling (chorei; polyporus; black skin removed; 2 liang)　hei qianniu (black morning-glory seed; lightly stir-fried, powdered; 2½ liang)　tingli (lepidium seed; stir-fried until fragrant, ground separately; 3 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder. For each dose decoct three scallion whites in one *zhan* of sour rice water down to half a *zhan*, add half a *zhan* of wine, mix in 3 *qian* of the medicine and take it very early in the morning facing east. After about as long as it takes a person to walk ten *li*, boil a thin gruel with sour rice water and scallion whites until the scallions are soft, add 5 *ge* of wine and sip it hot; the amount depends on the person, but about 1 sheng must be taken. Salt and flour must not be eaten. Between early morning and noon, 3 or 4 sheng of urine should be passed or the bowels should move, the dyspnea settles and the swelling is reduced by seven-tenths; take it again after a day's interval. After recovery, one must rest well and abstain from salt and sexual activity for three years.

## Notes

Shenzhu san (formerly Tingli san): for generalized edema of the ten water qi, cough, dyspnea, inability to lie down, swollen distended abdomen, bitter mouth, dark rough urine and constipation.
- Lepidium and black morning-glory seed expel water; alisma, polyporus and Sichuan pepper seed promote urination. Taken with scallion white, sour rice water and wine, followed by scallion gruel. After recovery salt and sex are to be avoided for three years.
