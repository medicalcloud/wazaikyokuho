# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 016 竹葉石膏湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zhuye shigao tang (Bamboo Leaf and Gypsum Decoction)

It is suitable for cold damage and seasonal qi with deficiency of both exterior and interior, fever over the whole body and vexation and oppression of the heart and chest; or when the illness has resolved with sweating but there is no fluid within, with deficiency, emaciation and shortage of qi, vexing fullness in the chest and qi counterflow with a desire to vomit; and for all deficiency-vexation heat. Deficiency-vexation heat resembles cold damage, but there is no aversion to cold and no body pain; the head does not ache either, and the pulse is not tight and rapid. One must not then sweat or purge; this medicine should be taken.

renshen (ginseng; rhizome head removed)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried; 2 liang each)　shigao (gypsum; 1 jin)　banxia (pinellia; washed seven times in hot water; 2½ liang)　maimendong (ophiopogon; core removed; 5½ liang)

Make the above into a coarse powder and mix in the pinellia evenly. For each dose take 3 *qian* with two *zhan* of water and five or six pieces each of green bamboo leaf and fresh ginger, decoct down to one and a half *zhan*, strain off the dregs, add a hundred-odd grains of non-glutinous rice and decoct again; when the rice is cooked remove it and take [the liquid] warm, at any time.

## Notes

Zhuye shigao tang: for depleted fluids after cold damage has resolved, with deficiency, emaciation, shortage of qi, vexing fullness in the chest and nausea (*Shanghan lun* clause 397), and for all deficiency-vexation heat.
- The same ingredients as the *Shanghan lun* formula (bamboo leaf, gypsum, ginseng, ophiopogon, pinellia, licorice, rice) plus fresh ginger. It distinguishes deficiency vexation from cold damage (no chills, body pain or headache, pulse not tight and rapid), for which sweating and purging are forbidden.
