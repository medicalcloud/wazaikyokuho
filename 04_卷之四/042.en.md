# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之四 042 杏参散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xingshen san (Apricot and Ginseng Powder)

It removes phlegm and sends qi down. It is suitable for distension and fullness of the chest and flanks, rising qi with urgent panting, breathing leaning [on something] and inability to sleep lying down, and a clouded, confused mind.

taoren (peach kernel; skin and tips removed, stir-fried with bran)　renshen (ginseng; rhizome head removed)　xingren (apricot kernel; skin and tips removed, stir-fried with bran)　sangbaipi (mulberry root bark; stir-fried with honey until slightly red, then soaked overnight in rice-washing water and dried over a fire)

Make the above into a fine powder in equal parts. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water, three slices of ginger and one jujube, decoct down to seven-tenths and take it warm, at any time.

## Notes

Xingshen san (bureau additions): for chest and flank distension, panting, inability to lie down and clouded mind from phlegm and qi rising.
- Apricot kernel, peach kernel and mulberry bark lower lung qi; ginseng supplements.
