# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之七 004 羚羊角散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Lingyangjiao san (Reiyokakusan; Antelope Horn Powder)

It treats adults and children with all wind-heat toxin attacking the eyes upward, sudden red swelling, or sores with pain, gritty roughness and aversion to light.

lingyangjiao (reiyokaku; antelope horn; shaved)　huangqin (ogon; scutellaria)　shengma (shoma; cimicifuga)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried)　cheqianzi (shazenshi; plantago seed; 10 liang each)　zhizi ren (gardenia kernel)　cao longdan (ryutan; gentian; 5 liang each)　juemingzi (ketsumeishi; cassia seed; 20 liang)

Powder the above. Take 1 *qian* each time after meals mixed in warm water, three times a day; children may take half a *qian*.

## Notes

Lingyangjiao san: for sudden red swelling, sores, pain, grittiness and photophobia from wind-heat toxin attacking the eyes.
- Antelope horn, gentian, gardenia, scutellaria and cimicifuga clear liver heat; cassia seed and plantago seed brighten the eyes; licorice is added.
