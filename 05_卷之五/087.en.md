# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 087 十補丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shibu wan (Juhogan; Ten Supplements Pill)

It treats deficient, damaged genuine qi and injured, exhausted lower jiao with stiffness and cramping of the navel and abdomen, pain of the lower back and legs, blood loss and night sweats, emissions and white turbidity, spontaneous diarrhea and slippery frequent urination; or the thirst of the three wastings with eating double the usual amount, wasting flesh, impotence and a withered, haggard complexion. Taken for a long time it supplements the five zang organs, moves the ying-nutrient and wei-defense qi, augments essence and marrow and improves eating.

fuzi (bushi; aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　baji (morinda; core removed)　poguzhi (psoralea; stir-fried)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried)　yuanzhi (onji; polygala; core removed, soaked in ginger juice, stir-fried)　tusizi (toshishi; dodder seed; soaked in wine, ground separately)　chi shizhi (red halloysite; calcined)　houpo (koboku; magnolia bark; coarse bark removed, roasted with ginger juice; 1 liang each)　chuanjiao (Sichuan pepper; seeds and closed fruits removed, stir-fried to sweat out the oil; 2 liang)

Powder the above and form pills the size of wutong seeds with wine paste. Take thirty to fifty pills each time with warm wine or salt water as preferred.

## Notes

Shibu wan (bureau additions): for umbilical cramping, back and leg pain, blood loss, night sweats, seminal emission, turbid urine, diarrhea and frequent urination from damaged genuine qi and exhausted lower jiao, and for the overeating and wasting of the three wastings, impotence and haggard complexion.
- Ten ingredients (hence the name): aconite, cinnamon, dried ginger, Sichuan pepper, morinda, psoralea and dodder warm and supplement; red halloysite secures; polygala and magnolia bark are added.
