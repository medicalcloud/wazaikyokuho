# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 114 撾脾湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zhuapi tang (Spleen-Rousing Drink)

It is suitable for discomfort of the spleen and stomach with a lingering hangover, vomiting of sour fluid, distending pain of the heart and abdomen and no desire for food, and for diarrhea from cold damage.

mayou (sesame oil; 4 liang)　liangjiang (lesser galangal; 15 liang)　huixiang (fennel; stir-fried; 7½ liang)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; 11 liang 7½ *qian*)

Stir-fry the medicines together with 1 jin of stir-fried salt and make into a fine powder. Take 1 *qian* each time mixed with plain hot water. Taken regularly it refreshes qi, greatly relieves drunkenness and makes eating relishable.

## Notes

Zhuapi tang (from here the layer of Wu Zhige's additions): a drink for spleen-stomach discomfort, hangover, sour vomiting, abdominal distending pain, poor appetite and diarrhea from cold; said to relieve drunkenness.
- Lesser galangal and fennel stir-fried with sesame oil and salt, plus licorice.
