# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 083 玉華白丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yuhua bai dan (Jade Flower White Elixir)

(A formula of three-grade preparation by Master Chongxu of the Tang, once presented to the throne in the Xuanhe–Zhenghe period and classed as a superior-grade elixir.) It clears the upper and fills the lower, assists and nourishes the root origin, supports the declining and rescues the weak, and supplements and benefits the zang-fu organs. It treats the five taxations and seven damages, copious night sweats, deficiency damage of atrophy of the lung lobes, long-standing cough and dyspnea upward, sudden turmoil with cramps, all six pulses deep and hidden, green-black lips and mouth, stabbing pain of the abdomen and flanks, an insecure large intestine, slippery frequent urination, emissions in dreams, emaciated, haggard flesh, dim eyes and tinnitus, stomach deficiency with reduced eating, long-standing malaria and dysentery, and accumulated cold and chronic chill that various medicines have failed to cure; taken, it works like magic.

bai shizhi (white halloysite; set on a clean tile, calcined red, finely ground, water-ground)　zuogu muli (borei; left-facing oyster shell; 7 *qian*, washed, pounded with chive leaves, sealed with salt clay, calcined and the white part taken)　yangqishi (yokiseki; actinolite; calcined red-hot in a crucible in a strong fire, taken out, quenched in wine and dried in a shady place; half a liang each)　zhongru fen (stalactite powder; the refined kind; 1 liang)

Grind each of these four ingredients extremely fine like powder, then mix them together evenly and grind for one or two days; form pills the size of euryale seeds with paste boiled from glutinous rice flour, and put them in a pit in the ground for one night to draw out the fire toxin. Take one pill each time on an empty stomach, swallowed with strongly decocted ginseng decoction that has been cooled; boiled water is also fine. Taken regularly it is warm and balanced, neither overbearing nor drying; it moistens the skin, gladdens the complexion and removes old ailments. For women who have long failed to conceive, take it with wine in which angelica and prepared rehmannia have been steeped, and it has the marvel of according with creation. For long-standing cold, flooding and discharge, deficiency damage and gripping pain of the navel and abdomen, take it with mugwort-vinegar decoction. After taking it, press it down with a little plain rice gruel. Avoid pig's and sheep's blood and mung bean flour, which may neutralize the force of the medicine. It is especially good for long-standing intestinal wind and visceral toxin.

## Notes

Yuhua bai dan (Wu Zhige's additions): for the five taxations and seven damages, night sweats, atrophy of the lung lobes, chronic cough, dyspnea, sudden turmoil, hidden pulses, stabbing pain, diarrhea, frequent urination, dream emissions, wasting, dim vision, tinnitus, poor appetite, chronic malaria and dysentery and accumulated cold; for women's infertility, flooding and discharge; and for intestinal wind and visceral toxin.
- Four mineral drugs (white halloysite, oyster shell, actinolite, stalactite) calcined and combined.
- "Chongxu" follows the base text's character (an exception to the character normalization). "Xuan-Zheng" refers to the Xuanhe and Zhenghe eras (reign of Emperor Huizong of the Northern Song).
