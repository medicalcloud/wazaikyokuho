# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 010 論諸風頭目昏眩 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On dizziness of the head and eyes in the various winds. It always arises from phlegm congestion with exuberance above. Give Qingzhou bai wanzi. For dizziness with much phlegm, give Chensha huatan wan (Cinnabar Phlegm-Transforming Pill). For dizziness with abundant phlegm, give Banxia wan or Tiannanxing wan. For heat in the heart and lungs, give Longnao xiongxi wan. For abundant phlegm with a stiff neck, give Jinfeicao san urgently. For much phlegm with diaphragm heat, give Chuanxiong wan. For blockage of the heart and chest with a bitter mouth and dry tongue, give Toubing dan, Qianghuo wan or Fangfeng wan. For severe phlegm with palpitation and floating, give Niuhuang qingxin wan (Bovine Bezoar Heart-Clearing Pill). For abundant phlegm with thirst and vomiting, give Tiannanxing wan.

## Notes

On dizziness in the various winds (phlegm congestion with exuberance above): Qingzhou bai wanzi, Chensha huatan wan, Banxia wan and Tiannanxing wan for phlegm; Longnao xiongxi wan for heart-lung heat; Jinfeicao san for phlegm with stiff neck; Chuanxiong wan for phlegm with diaphragm heat; Toubing dan, Qianghuo wan and Fangfeng wan for chest blockage and bitter mouth; Niuhuang qingxin wan for phlegm with palpitation.
