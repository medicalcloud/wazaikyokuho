# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 004 乳香丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Ruxiang wan (Nyukogan; Frankincense Pill)

It treats the various piles with bleeding, fleshy growths beside the anus, or swollen, painful nodes, or itchy, painful sores, or difficult defecation and prolapse of the rectum. It also treats intestinal wind with bleeding, recent or long-standing, and can treat all fistulas whose roots lie in the zang-fu organs.

zhiqiao (bitter orange; pulp removed, stir-fried with bran)　muli (borei; oyster shell; calcined)　bichengqie (cubeb)　yuanqing (blister beetle; head, wings and legs removed, stir-fried with glutinous rice until the rice turns yellow)　dahuang (daio; rhubarb; steamed, dried over a fire)　heshi (carpesium fruit; stir-fried; half a liang each)　bai dingxiang (sparrow droppings)　ruxiang (frankincense; ground; 1 *fen* each)

Powder the above and form pills the size of wutong seeds with millet paste. Take ten to fifteen pills each time: for intestinal wind with clear *la* tea; for the various piles with decocted Chinese chive bulb decoction; for the various fistulas with decocted iron-filings decoction; in all cases before meals.

## Notes

Ruxiang wan: for bleeding piles, anal growths, swollen painful nodes, itchy painful sores, difficult defecation and rectal prolapse; for intestinal wind with bleeding, old or new; and for fistulas rooted in the zang organs.
- Frankincense, sparrow droppings, blister beetle, carpesium, cubeb, oyster shell, bitter orange and rhubarb; the vehicle changes with the condition.
