# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 044 没薬降聖丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Moyao jiangsheng dan (Myrrh Sage-Descending Elixir)

(The formula appears under sores, swellings, injuries and fractures.)

## Notes

Only the heading of Moyao jiangsheng dan, with a cross-reference: the formula is in juan 8, "treating sores, swellings, injuries and fractures".
