# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 120 芬積香 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Fenji xiang (Fragrant Accumulation Incense)

chenxiang (aquilaria; chopped; 25 liang)　jianxiang (jian aloeswood)　tanxiang (sandalwood; chopped, soaked in green tea liquid, stir-fried yellow)　jiaxiang (operculum; boiled over charcoal for two days, then boiled until cooked with honey and wine)　shamu tan (fir charcoal; 20 liang each)　dingxiang (clove)　huoxiang (agastache leaves)　shexiang (musk; ground)　linglingxiang (lysimachia leaves)　yaxiao (refined mirabilite; ground; 10 liang each)　naozi (borneol; ground; 3 liang)　meihua nao (plum-blossom borneol; ground; 2 liang)

Except for the ingredients to be ground, make the above into a fine powder; refine 10 liang of honey and combine with the ground ingredients; burn in the usual way.

## Notes

Fenji xiang (the first entry of juan 10, "Various incenses"): a recipe for burning incense, not a medical formula; no indications are given.
- Aquilaria, jian aloeswood, sandalwood, operculum, clove, agastache, musk, lysimachia, borneol and others combined with charcoal powder and refined mirabilite, kneaded with honey and burned.
