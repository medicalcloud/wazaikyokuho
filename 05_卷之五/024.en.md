# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 024 双和湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shuanghe tang (Double Harmony Decoction)

It treats men and women with the five taxations, six extremes and seven damages, deficiency of both heart and kidneys, little essence, blood and qi, resulting in deficiency taxation. All the bones are withered and haggard, the limbs weary, chills and fever alternate, there is cough with a dry throat, dyspnea exhaustion on movement and a withered yellow complexion, and the slightest exposure easily leads to other illnesses. If injured by cold, old food is not dispersed, with spleen pain, abdominal pain, diarrhea and vomiting; if injured by heat, there is spinning head and dizziness, phlegm and drool with hurried breathing and vexing heat of the five hearts; or through hunger, overeating, exertion, joy, anger, fright and fear, illness follows; or there is deficiency distension with no desire for food, or eating much without putting on flesh, and with vexation of the heart, deficiency sweating and night sweats. It is suitable for all deficiency taxation in which drying medicines are not to be taken. Taken regularly it regulates the middle and nourishes qi, augments blood and nurtures the spirit, harmonizes the stomach, improves eating and supplements deficiency and damage.

bai shaoyao (shakuyaku; white peony root; 7½ liang)　danggui (toki; Chinese angelica; washed, soaked in wine)　huangqi (ogi; astragalus; roasted with honey)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage)　shu dihuang (prepared rehmannia; washed clean, steamed with wine; 3 liang each)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried)　rougui (cinnamon bark; skin removed, not exposed to fire; 2 liang 2½ *qian* each)

Make the above into a fine powder. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water, three slices of ginger and one jujube, decoct down to six-tenths and take it on an empty stomach before meals. Avoid raw and cold foods and fruit.

## Notes

Shuanghe tang (Baoqing-era additions): for deficiency taxation with deficiency of both heart and kidneys and scant essence, blood and qi (wasting, lassitude, chills and fever, cough, shortness of breath, sallow complexion, susceptibility to illness, sensitivity to cold and heat, night sweats), especially when drying medicines cannot be used.
- A combination of Siwu tang (Shimotsuto) (angelica, lovage, peony, rehmannia) and Huangqi jianzhong tang (Ogikenchuto) (astragalus, peony, cinnamon, licorice), supplementing both qi and blood (hence "double harmony").
