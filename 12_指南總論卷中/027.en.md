# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 027 論傷寒陰証 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On yin patterns of cold damage. For cold damage on the second or third, or fifth to seventh day, with body pain and inability to turn over, spontaneous sweating with cold limbs, diarrhea without thirst, or vomiting and diarrhea, pain of the navel and abdomen, or sore throat, give Lizhong wan (Richugan) or Lizhong tang (Richuto). For severe cold of the limbs with abdominal pain and hurried breathing, give Jiangfu tang (Ginger and Aconite Decoction) decocted with much licorice added, together with Fuzi lizhong wan (Bushirichugan). For still more severe cases, use properly refined Heixi dan (Kokushakutan), Jinye dan, Erqi dan and the like according to severity. When the diarrhea has stopped and the limbs are warm but chills and fever remain, then use Wuji san (Goshakusan), Sheng sanzi, Shihua san and the like; with slight sweating it resolves; otherwise the toxic qi returns and it becomes hard to treat.

## Notes

On yin patterns of cold damage: Lizhong wan or Lizhong tang for body pain, inability to turn, sweating, cold limbs, diarrhea without thirst, vomiting, umbilical pain or sore throat; Jiangfu tang (with much licorice) and Fuzi lizhong wan for severe cold, abdominal pain and breathlessness; Heixi dan, Jinye dan and Erqi dan for graver cases; once diarrhea stops and the limbs warm but chills and fever remain, Wuji san, Sheng sanzi or Shihua san to induce slight sweating.
