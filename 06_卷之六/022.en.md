# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 022 五淋散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wulin san (Gorinsan; Five Strangury Powder)

The patterns treated are the same as for the earlier Wulin san.

mutong (akebia; nodes removed)　huashi (kasseki; talc)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried; 6 liang each)　shanzhi ren (gardenia kernel; stir-fried; 14 liang)　chi shaoyao (shakuyaku; red peony root)　fuling (bukuryo; poria; skin removed; half a jin each)　danzhuye (lophatherum; 4 liang)　shan yinchen (capillaris; roots removed, sun-dried; 2 liang)

Pound and sift the above into a powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one *zhan* of water, decoct down to eight-tenths and take it on an empty stomach.

## Notes

Wulin san (bureau additions): the same indications as the earlier Wulin san (juan 6, no. 014); a different formula adding akebia, talc, lophatherum and capillaris and omitting angelica.
