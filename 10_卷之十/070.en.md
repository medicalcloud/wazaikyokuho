# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 070 烏梅散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wumei san (Mume Powder)

It treats children who after dysentery have reduced fluids and deficient, dry viscera and bowels, with vexing thirst and drinking; and vexing thirst in the various illnesses with drinking without limit.

wumei rou (mume flesh; lightly stir-fried; half a liang)　bai fuling (white poria)　gan mugua (dried chaenomeles; 1 liang each)

Pound and sift the above into a coarse powder. For a three-year-old, take 1 *qian* each time with a small *zhan* of water and one coin-sized slice of fresh ginger, decoct down to five-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm, at any time, adjusted for the child's size.

## Notes

Wumei san (Wu Zhige's additions): for vexing thirst and excessive drinking from depleted fluids and dry viscera after dysentery in children, and for vexing thirst in the various illnesses.
- Mume and chaenomeles (sour) generate fluids and stop thirst; poria is added.
