# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 017 四物湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Siwu tang (Shimotsuto; Four Substances Decoction)

It regulates and augments the ying-nutrient and wei-defense qi and nourishes qi and blood. It treats deficiency damage of the *Chong* and *Ren* with irregular menses, gripping pain of the navel and abdomen, metrorrhagia and metrostaxis, hard blood-conglomeration lumps with pain that comes and goes; old cold in pregnancy with improper care, a restless fetus and unceasing bleeding; and, after childbirth, wind-cold taking advantage of the deficiency to contend within, the lochia failing to come down and binding into conglomerations and gatherings, hard pain of the lower abdomen and intermittent chills and fever.

danggui (toki; Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed, soaked in wine, stir-fried)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage)　bai shaoyao (shakuyaku; white peony root)　shu gan dihuang (kanjio; prepared rehmannia; sprinkled with wine, steamed; equal parts)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water, decoct down to eight-tenths, remove the dregs and take it hot, on an empty stomach before meals. For a restless fetus in pregnancy with unceasing bleeding, add ten mugwort leaves and one piece of donkey-hide gelatin and decoct together as above. For a deficient, cold blood zang organ with flooding and excessive loss of blood, likewise add gelatin and mugwort to the decoction.

## Notes

Siwu tang: regulates the ying-nutrient and wei-defense qi and nourishes qi and blood; for irregular menses, gripping umbilical pain, flooding and hard blood conglomerations from deficient *Chong* and *Ren*; for a restless fetus with bleeding in pregnancy; and for retained lochia, conglomerations, hard lower abdominal pain and chills and fever after childbirth. Still widely used today as the basic blood-supplementing formula (irregular menses, anemia) and the base of many others.
- Four ingredients in equal parts: angelica, lovage, white peony, prepared rehmannia. For a restless fetus or flooding, mugwort and donkey-hide gelatin are added (the form of Jiao'ai tang).
