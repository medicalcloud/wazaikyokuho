# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之七 023 曽青散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zengqing san (Azurite Powder)

It treats, without exception, all wind-heat toxic qi attacking both eyes upward, with much discharge and tearing, fear of the sun and aversion to light, gritty roughness making the eyes hard to open, festering, red, swollen orbits, itching or pain; as well as seasonal sudden red eyes with dim, rough, painful eyes.

baijiang (dried ginger; blast-fried)　fangfeng (saposhnikovia; rhizome head removed; 1 liang each)　zengqing (azurite; 4 liang)　manjingzi (vitex fruit; skin removed; 2 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder. Each time sniff a little of the powder into the nose, with immediate effect.

## Notes

Zengqing san (bureau additions): for discharge, tearing, photophobia, difficulty opening, festering red swollen orbits, itching and pain from wind-heat toxic qi attacking both eyes, and for epidemic sudden red eyes.
- Mainly azurite (a copper mineral), with vitex, saposhnikovia and dried ginger, the powder sniffed into the nose.
