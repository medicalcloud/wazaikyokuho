# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷下 035 論小児雀目証 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On the pattern of sparrow eyes in children. Sparrow eyes means that there is nothing wrong during the day, but at night things cannot be seen: this is sparrow eyes. Give Wufu huadu dan from time to time, mixed in millet water at bedtime.

## Notes

On sparrow eyes (night blindness) in children (the last discussion of the General Guide, lower volume): seeing normally by day but not at night; Wufu huadu dan taken in millet water at bedtime.
