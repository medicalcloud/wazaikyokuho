# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之四 031 人参款花膏 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Renshen kuanhua gao (Ginseng and Coltsfoot Paste)

It treats deficiency cold of the lungs and stomach with long-standing cough that does not stop, fullness and oppression of the throat and diaphragm, cough with phlegm and drool, retching and nausea, distension and fullness of the abdomen and flanks, and weary pain of the lower back and spine; or cold cough of deficiency taxation, and all coughs of many years or recent that have not responded to other medicines.

kuandonghua (kantoka; coltsfoot flower; stalks removed)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng; rhizome head removed)　wuweizi (gomishi; schisandra; stalks removed, stir-fried)　ziwan (aster; rhizome head removed, washed)　sangbaipi (mulberry root bark; red skin removed; 1 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of a euryale seed with refined honey. Chew one pill thoroughly each time after meals and wash it down with weak ginger decoction; or divide each large pill into four small ones and hold them in the mouth to dissolve.

## Notes

Renshen kuanhua gao (Wu Zhige's additions): for long-standing cough, throat oppression, phlegm, vomiting, abdominal fullness and back pain from deficiency cold of the lungs and stomach, cold cough of deficiency taxation, and coughs old or new unresponsive to other medicines.
- Coltsfoot, aster and mulberry bark stop cough; ginseng and schisandra supplement and astringe lung qi.
