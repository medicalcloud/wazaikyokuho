# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷下 028 論悪瘡、疥癬 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On malign sores, scabies and tinea. For all nameless malign sores, leaking sores, shin ulcers and cold sores unhealed for years, give Taoren san or Qilin san and apply Bao'an gao. For wind-toxin hot sores, also called kidney-zang organ wind, with sore fluid and pustules that are damp, festering and spreading, give Heshouwu san (Kashusan), Sisheng wan (Shiseigan), Huma san (Gomasan) or Sishun yin. For constipation, give Zaojia jian wan or Taoren san and apply Huaji san. For itchy sores over the whole body or hives, first give medicines to open the bowels: Zaojia jian wan, Shengong wan, Maren wan (Maningan), Heshouwu san, Sishun yin, Sisheng wan, Xiaofeng san (Shofusan), Huma san, Huaji san, Huangqi wan (Ogigan) or Bailong wan. For dry, painful sores, give Yulong mofeng gao. For sores from scalds and burns, give Foshou san. For scabies, flat-abscesses and stubborn tinea, give Mofeng gao, Bailong wan, Huaji san or Qingxin wan.

## Notes

On malign sores, scabies and tinea: Taoren san or Qilin san with Bao'an gao for chronic nameless sores, leaking sores, shin ulcers and cold sores; Heshouwu san, Sisheng wan, Huma san and Sishun yin for damp, spreading wind-toxin sores ("kidney-zang organ wind"); Zaojia jian wan or Taoren san with Huaji san for constipation; laxatives first and then various formulas for generalized itchy sores and hives; Yulong mofeng gao for dry painful sores; Foshou san for burns; Mofeng gao, Bailong wan, Huaji san and Qingxin wan for scabies and stubborn tinea.
