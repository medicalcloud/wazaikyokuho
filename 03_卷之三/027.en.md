# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 027 三稜煎丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Sanleng jian wan (Sparganium Syrup Pill)

It smooths qi and loosens the middle, disperses accumulated stagnation and transforms phlegm-rheum. It treats glomus of qi in the middle duct, hard distension of the heart and abdomen, tightness and hardness below the flanks, blockage in the chest, panting fullness with shortness of breath, blocked belching, vomiting with phlegm counterflow, inability to get food down, and irregular bowels, sometimes loose and sometimes constipated.

xingren (apricot kernel; soaked in hot water, skin and tips removed, stir-fried yellow with bran)　naosha (sal ammoniac; water-ground; 1 liang each)　shenqu (medicated leaven; crushed, stir-fried)　mainie (barley sprouts; stir-fried; 3 liang each)　qingpi (green tangerine peel; white removed)　ganqi (dried lacquer; stir-fried)　luobozi (radish seed; lightly stir-fried; 2 liang each)　sanleng (sparganium; raw, finely chopped, pounded and sieved to powder; 8 liang, simmered with 3 *sheng* of wine in a stone vessel into a paste)

Powder the above medicines, knead evenly with the sparganium paste and form pills the size of wutong seeds. Take fifteen to twenty pills each time with warm rice gruel, after meals.

## Notes

Sanleng jian wan: for qi glomus in the middle duct, hard abdominal distension, hardness below the flanks, panting fullness, vomiting, poor appetite and irregular bowels.
- A paste of sparganium simmered in wine is the base; sal ammoniac and dried lacquer break up accumulations; medicated leaven, barley sprouts and radish seed digest food; green tangerine peel and apricot kernel send qi down.
