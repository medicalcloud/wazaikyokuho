# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 085 磁石丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cishi wan (Magnetite Pill)

It is suitable for wind toxin of the kidneys attacking upward, with puffiness of the head and face, tinnitus and dim eyes, swelling and itching of the scalp, pain at the temples, nasal congestion with oppression of the head, loose teeth, stiffness of the neck and back, itching all over with dormant papules turning into sores, pain in all the joints and numbness of the skin; and pouring down into the legs and knees with cramping of the sinews and vessels, inability to bend and stretch, dull pain under the feet and difficulty walking. Taken regularly it supplements, dispels wind, brightens the eyes, quickens the blood and preserves the complexion.

cishi (magnetite; burned and quenched in vinegar twenty times, pounded and sieved to powder; 10 liang)　niuxi (goshitsu; achyranthes; soaked in wine and dried over a fire; 6 liang)　huang zhizhu (yellow rhododendron flower; stir-fried; 8 liang)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　chi shaoyao (shakuyaku; red peony root)　hei qianniu (black morning-glory seed; stir-fried; 4 liang each)　caowu (wild aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed; 14 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills with wine paste. Take thirty pills each time, swallowed with roasted scallion and salted wine; roasted scallion tea is also fine. For one-sided and general headache take them with fresh scallion tea. For women's blood wind with pain all over, dizziness, puffy face and wasted body, take them with weak vinegar decoction, three doses a day, with great divine effect.

## Notes

Cishi wan (bureau additions): for facial puffiness, tinnitus, dim eyes, loose teeth and itching from kidney wind toxin attacking upward, and for cramping and pain of the legs when it pours downward.
- Magnetite settles the kidneys and brightens the ears and eyes; wild aconite and yellow rhododendron dispel wind; achyranthes, lovage, red peony and cinnamon move the blood; morning-glory seed purges.
