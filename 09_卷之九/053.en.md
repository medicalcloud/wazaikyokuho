# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 053 琥珀丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Hupo wan (Kohakugan; Amber Pill)

It treats the hundred illnesses before and after childbirth in women old or young, as well as the thirty-six kinds of blood cold, the seven mountings and eight aggregations, stabbing pain of the heart and abdomen, sudden paralysis, hemiplegia, the eight winds and twelve impediments with aching pain of the hands and feet, toxin bound as static blood in the breasts, fright and disturbance in pregnancy, injury and violation causing restlessness, a dead fetus that will not come out, and retained placenta. All should take it.

hupo (amber; ground)　chensha (cinnabar; ground separately)　chenxiang (aquilaria)　ejiao (akyo; donkey-hide gelatin; broken up, stir-fried)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　shihu (sekkoku; dendrobium; roots removed)　fuzi (bushi; aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　wuweizi (gomishi; schisandra; picked clean)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage; half a liang each)　niuxi (goshitsu; achyranthes; shoots removed, soaked in wine overnight)　danggui (toki; Chinese angelica; shoots removed, stir-fried)　roucongrong (cistanche; sliced, soaked in wine overnight, dried over a fire)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng)　xuduan (zokudan; dipsacus)　moyao (myrrh; ground; 3 *fen* each)　shu gan dihuang (kanjio; prepared rehmannia)　muxiang (mokko; costus root; 1 *fen* each)

Make the above into a fine powder, knead with refined honey and form pills the size of a pellet. Take one pill each time on an empty stomach dissolved in warmed wine, and again before the midday and evening meals; it generates essence and blood and removes malign blood. For pain of the abdomen and flanks, as if stabbed by a knife around the navel, and vomiting counterflow with qi rising to pound the heart, and phlegm toxin with no desire for food, take it in wine mixed with a little ginger juice. For the various dysenteries and red and white discharge, for flooding with bleeding from blood cold and for bleeding from a leaking fetus, chop fresh ginger and mugwort, stir-fry until red, put them into wine and boil several times, remove the dregs and take the pill dissolved in it. For unceasing diarrhea, take it with old-rice water. For rough urination and the various stranguries, take it with decocted rice-paper-plant and rush-pith decoction. For blood dizziness with loss of consciousness, take it dissolved in decocted angelica wine. For heat above and cold below, take it with strongly decocted ginseng decoction. For deficiency swelling and water qi over the whole body, take it with decocted rice bean decoction. For "double toxin" cold damage during childbirth and for wind stroke with arched-back rigidity, the body stiff as a board, take it with decocted Mahuang tang (Maoto) and cover with a quilt to induce sweating. For blocked menses, or five colors mixed and discharged frequently, stopping and starting without end, tasteless food, wasted skin, red face and parched lips, alternating fever and chills, vexing pain of the limbs, dry heat of the five hearts, dark spots, blood spots over the body and wandering red swellings, and for blood-wind taxation damage with weakness, take it dissolved in a boy's urine with a little ginger juice. For regular use urine is best; if nausea is feared, use half wine. For a pregnant woman, one dose a day in the difficult (last) month brings delivery without feeling pain. When a patient has taken five or ten doses and food intake doubles day by day, that is the effect of the medicine. Its virtues cannot all be recorded; the vehicles for urgent use have been briefly set out above.

## Notes

Hupo wan (from here the layer of bureau additions): for the hundred illnesses before and after childbirth, the thirty-six blood-cold disorders, mounting and conglomerations, stabbing abdominal pain, paralysis, impediment, breast stasis, a restless fetus, dead fetus and retained placenta; with detailed vehicles for many conditions (abdominal pain, dysentery, discharge, flooding, leaking fetus, diarrhea, strangury, blood dizziness, heat above and cold below, edema, postpartum cold damage and convulsions, amenorrhoea, blood-wind taxation).
- Amber, cinnabar and myrrh move static blood and settle; prepared rehmannia, angelica, lovage, donkey-hide gelatin, ginseng, cistanche, dipsacus, achyranthes, dendrobium and schisandra supplement; aconite and cinnamon warm; aquilaria and costus move qi.
