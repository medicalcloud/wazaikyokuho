# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 055 上丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shang dan (Superior Elixir)

It nourishes the five viscera, supplements insufficiency, secures the true origin, regulates the two qi, harmonizes the nutritive and defensive qi, preserves the spirit and guards the middle. Taken for a long time it lightens the body and withstands ageing, invigorates strength, improves the appetite and brightens the eyes, brings down heart fire to interact with kidney water, and augments essential qi. For men whose yang is cut off and who are impotent; for women whose yin is cut off and who cannot conceive; for heavy, painful lower back and knees, declining, failing sinews and bones, a dark sallow complexion, a weary heart and clouded will, abstraction asleep and awake, vexation, oppression and easy fatigue, dribbling and dream emissions, evil heat in the bladder, the five taxations and seven damages, emaciated flesh, and heat above with cold below such that supplementing medicines cannot be tolerated: after half a month of taking it, yin and yang naturally harmonize and the complexion and flesh become lustrous, pleasant and moist. It opens the heart and mind, calms the ethereal and corporeal souls, disperses food and nourishes stomach qi.

wuweizi (schisandra; half a jin)　shechuangzi (cnidium seed)　baibu gen (stemona root; soaked in wine overnight)　tusizi (dodder seed; soaked in wine, ground separately)　bai fuling (white poria)　roucongrong (cistanche; soaked in wine)　gouqizi (lycium fruit)　baiziren (arborvitae seed; ground separately)　duzhong (eucommia; stir-fried until the threads break)　fangfeng (saposhnikovia; forks removed)　baji (morinda; core removed)　shanyao (Chinese yam)　yuanzhi (polygala; core removed; 2 liang each)

Powder the above and form pills the size of wutong seeds with honey. Take thirty pills before meals with warm wine or salt water as preferred. In spring, [take them with] decocted dried-jujube decoction; in summer add 4 liang of schisandra; in the last month of each season add 6 liang of cistanche; in autumn add 6 liang of lycium fruit; in winter add 6 liang of polygala.

## Notes

Shang dan (bureau additions): said to nourish the five viscera and secure the true origin; for male impotence, female infertility, painful back and knees, failing sinews and bones, dark complexion, abstraction, dribbling, dream emissions, bladder heat and wasting, and for heat above and cold below that cannot tolerate supplementing medicines.
- Mainly schisandra; dodder, cistanche, lycium, eucommia, morinda and cnidium supplement the kidneys; poria, Chinese yam, arborvitae seed and polygala nourish heart and spleen; stemona and saposhnikovia are added. The added drug changes with the season.
