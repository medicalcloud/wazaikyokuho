# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 063 豆附丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Doufu wan (Nutmeg and Aconite Pill)

This medicine governs weak, deficient intestines and stomach in men and women that have received wind-cold internally, with undigested water and grain, pouring diarrhea, abdominal pain and borborygmus, counterflow cold of the hands and feet, unresponsive to various medicines.

roudoukou (nutmeg; blast-fried)　bai fuling (bukuryo; white poria; dried over a fire)　fuzi (bushi; aconite; blast-fried, navel removed; 4 liang each)　muxiang (mokko; costus root; not exposed to fire)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed; 2 liang each)　dingxiang (clove; not exposed to fire; 1 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with flour paste made with ginger juice. Take fifty to a hundred pills each time, swallowed with ginger decoction; rice gruel water is also fine; on an empty stomach before meals.

## Notes

Doufu wan (Wu Zhige's additions): for indigestion, pouring diarrhea, abdominal pain, borborygmus and cold limbs from weak intestines and stomach with wind-cold, unresponsive to other medicines.
- Mainly nutmeg and aconite (the "dou" and "fu" of the name); dried ginger, cinnamon and clove warm; costus moves qi; poria is added.
