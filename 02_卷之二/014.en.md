# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 014 防己黄耆湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Fangji huangqi tang (Boiogito; Stephania and Astragalus Decoction)

It treats wind and damp contending and lodging in the skin, with heaviness of the whole body, little strength in the four limbs, vexing pain of the joints, occasional spontaneous sweating, shivering aversion to wind and reluctance to take off clothes; and wind edema lodging and contending, with swelling of the lower back and legs, the upper body light and the lower heavy, and inability to bend and stretch.

fangji (boi; stephania; 4 liang)　huangqi (ogi; astragalus; 5 liang)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried; 2 liang)　baizhu (byakujutsu; white atractylodes; 3 liang)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water, three slices of fresh ginger and one jujube, decoct together down to one *zhan*, remove the dregs and take it fairly hot, at any time. After taking it, cover up and lie warm so as to sweat slightly, and the patient recovers.

## Notes

Fangji huangqi tang: for heaviness, sweating with aversion to wind and joint pain from wind-damp in the skin, and for swelling of the lower back and legs in wind edema. It corresponds to Fangji huangqi tang of the *Jingui yaolüe* (chapters on tetany-damp-heatstroke and water qi) and is still used for sweating, joint pain and edema in those with "watery" obesity.
- Stephania, astragalus, atractylodes and licorice with ginger and jujube. The Siku quanshu edition reads 風冷 (wind-cold) for 風水 (see the variants).
