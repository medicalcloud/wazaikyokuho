# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 060 蓯蓉大補丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Congrong dabu wan (Cistanche Major Supplementation Pill)

It governs all cases of deficient, exhausted original zang organ and insufficient blood and qi with white turbidity and emissions, spontaneous sweating and spontaneous diarrhea, a bitter mouth and dry tongue and emaciated limbs, and the various deficiencies of women.

muxiang (mokko; costus root; blast-fried)　fuzi (bushi; aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　huixiang (fennel; stir-fried)　roucongrong (cistanche; soaked in wine)　chuanjiao (Sichuan pepper; stir-fried to remove the oil; 10 liang each)　baji (morinda; core removed)　niuxi (goshitsu; achyranthes; soaked in wine)　bai jili (tribulus; stir-fried, spines removed)　taoren (tonin; peach kernel; stir-fried, skin and tips removed)　huangqi (ogi; astragalus)　zexie (takusha; alisma)　huluba (fenugreek)　wuweizi (gomishi; schisandra; 5 liang each)　binglang (areca)　tianma (gastrodia)　guixin (cinnamon heart)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage)　qianghuo (notopterygium; 2 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with honey. Take thirty to fifty pills on an empty stomach with salted wine or salt water as preferred.

## Notes

Congrong dabu wan (bureau additions): for turbid urine, emissions, spontaneous sweating, diarrhea, bitter mouth and wasting from exhausted original zang organ and insufficient blood and qi, and for women's various deficiencies.
- Cistanche, aconite, Sichuan pepper, fennel, morinda, achyranthes and fenugreek warm and supplement the kidneys; astragalus and schisandra supplement and astringe; costus and areca move qi; tribulus, gastrodia, notopterygium, lovage, peach kernel, alisma and cinnamon heart are added.
