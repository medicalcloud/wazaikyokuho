# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之四 011 大阿膠丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Da ejiao wan (Major Donkey-Hide Gelatin Pill)

It treats lung deficiency with visiting heat, cough with urgent breathing, vexing palpitation in the chest, weary aching limbs, dry throat and mouth, thirst with a desire for cold drinks, much spitting of drool and froth, sometimes with fresh blood, emaciation with fever, reduced eating and a liking for lying down. It is also suitable for spitting and vomiting of blood when the lungs and stomach have been injured by shouting in anger or by sexual exhaustion.

maimendong (ophiopogon; core removed)　danshen (salvia)　beimu (fritillaria; stir-fried)　fangfeng (saposhnikovia; rhizome head, forks and top removed)　baiziren (arborvitae seed)　fushen (poria with root; wood removed)　duzhong (eucommia; coarse bark removed, stir-fried)　baibu root (stemona; half a liang each)　dried shanyao (Chinese yam)　ejiao (donkey-hide gelatin; stir-fried)　fuling (poria; skin removed)　shu gan dihuang (prepared rehmannia)　wuweizi (schisandra; 1 liang each)　yuanzhi (polygala; core removed)　renshen (ginseng; 1 *fen* each)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills with refined honey, twenty-four to each liang. For each dose decoct one pill in one medium *zhan* of water down to six-tenths and take it warm with the dregs, sipping a little at a time frequently, at any time.

## Notes

Da ejiao wan: for cough, shortness of breath, chest palpitation, thirst, frothy sputum, blood-streaked sputum, emaciation and fever from visiting heat with lung deficiency, and for spitting and vomiting of blood after lung-stomach injury from shouting in anger or sexual exhaustion.
- Gelatin, rehmannia, ophiopogon, Chinese yam and schisandra moisten and supplement lungs and kidneys; fritillaria and stemona stop cough; salvia, arborvitae seed, poria with root and polygala calm the heart; ginseng supplements qi. The pill is decocted in water and sipped with the dregs.
