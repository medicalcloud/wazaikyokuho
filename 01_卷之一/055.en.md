# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 055 省風湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xingfeng tang (Wind-Reducing Decoction)

It treats sudden acute wind stroke with clenched mouth and complete inability to speak, deviation of the mouth and eyes, cramping of the sinews and vessels with pulling pain, exuberant wind and phlegm excess, spinning and stiff falling, dizziness and heaviness of the head and eyes, vexing fullness of the chest and diaphragm, paralysis of the left or right side, numbness of the hands and feet, vexing pain of the joints, difficulty walking, unsettled abstraction and a clouded, confused spirit. It may be taken in advance for any wind pattern.

fangfeng (bofu; saposhnikovia; rhizome head removed)　nanxing (arisaema; used raw; 4 liang each)　banxia (hange; pinellia; good white pieces soaked and washed in water, used raw)　huangqin (ogon; scutellaria; coarse bark removed)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; used raw; 2 liang each)

Cut the above into pieces. For each dose take four large *qian* with two large *zhan* of water and ten slices of fresh ginger, decoct down to one medium *zhan*, remove the dregs and take it warm, at any time.

## Notes

Xingfeng tang (Baoqing-era additions): for clenched mouth, loss of speech, facial deviation, cramping, exuberant phlegm, dizziness and paralysis in acute wind stroke.
- Raw arisaema and pinellia expel wind-phlegm, saposhnikovia scatters wind, scutellaria clears heat and licorice harmonizes; much fresh ginger is added to temper the toxicity of arisaema and pinellia.
