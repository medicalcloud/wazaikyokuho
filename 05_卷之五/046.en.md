# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 046 椒附丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jiaofu wan (Sichuan Pepper and Aconite Pill)

It supplements deficiency and strengthens qi, and warms and harmonizes the five viscera. It is suitable for insufficiency of the lower channels with accumulated cold harboured within, bowstring tension of the navel and abdomen with pain drawing into the lower back and spine, weary limbs, a dark sallow complexion, dry lips and mouth, dim eyes and tinnitus, palpitation and shortness of breath, many strange dreams at night and little vitality by day, occasional night sweats, slippery frequent urination with dribbling and white turbidity, slack, weak legs and knees, lack of strength in movement, distension and fullness of the heart and abdomen and inability to eat.

fuzi (aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　chuanjiao (Sichuan pepper; seeds removed, stir-fried to sweat out the oil)　binglang (areca; half a liang each)　chenpi (aged tangerine peel; white pith removed)　qianniu (morning-glory seed; lightly stir-fried)　wuweizi (schisandra)　shichangpu (acorus)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried; 1 liang each)

Chop these eight ingredients, boil them dry in good rice vinegar in a porcelain vessel over a "civil and military" fire, dry them over a fire and make a fine powder, and form pills the size of wutong seeds with flour paste boiled in vinegar. Take thirty pills each time, swallowed on an empty stomach before meals with salted wine or salt water. For women's cold sea of blood, take them with angelica wine; for diarrhoea, with rice water; for cold dysentery, with ginger decoction; for red dysentery, with licorice decoction. It greatly warms the lower origin, treats depletion of kidney qi, and treats lumbar pain.

## Notes

Jiaofu wan (bureau additions): for umbilical tension, back pain, lassitude, dark complexion, dim eyes, tinnitus, palpitation, nightmares, night sweats, frequent urination, dribbling, turbid urine, weak legs, abdominal fullness and poor appetite from insufficiency of the lower channels with internal accumulated cold, and for women's cold sea of blood, diarrhoea, dysentery and lumbar pain.
- Sichuan pepper, aconite and dried ginger warm the lower origin; schisandra astringes; acorus is added; areca, morning-glory seed and tangerine peel send accumulated cold down. Boiled dry in vinegar.
