# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 003 林檎散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Linqin san (Crabapple Powder)

It treats cold damage and seasonal pestilence with headache and stiff neck, vigorous fever and aversion to cold, cramping and vexing pain of the lower back, spine and four limbs, a red face and dry throat, retching counterflow, vexation and thirst.

mahuang (mao; ephedra; nodes removed)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　cangzhu (black atractylodes; skin removed)　chuan dahuang (daio) (Sichuan rhubarb)　gange (dried pueraria)　shigao (sekko; gypsum)　shanzhizi (gardenia; skin removed; 1½ liang each)　mutong (akebia)　qumai (kubaku; dianthus)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried)　qianhu (peucedanum)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage; 1 liang each)　huoxiang (agastache; leaves used)　chuan wutou (uzu) (Sichuan aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed; half a liang each)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one *zhan* of water and a dozen or so pieces of dried crabapple (fresh ones are also fine), decoct down to seven-tenths, remove the dregs and take it fairly hot, at any time, and repeat in succession. Cover with clothes and bedding until sweat appears.

## Notes

Linqin san: for cold damage and epidemics with chills, vigorous fever, headache and body pain, a red face, dry throat, retching and thirst—heat in both exterior and interior.
- Ephedra, cinnamon, aconite and black atractylodes release the exterior; gypsum, gardenia, rhubarb, akebia and dianthus clear and purge interior heat. It is decocted with dried crabapple.
