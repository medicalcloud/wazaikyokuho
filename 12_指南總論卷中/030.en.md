# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 030 論傷寒陽厥証 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On the yang reversal pattern of cold damage. If at the onset of illness the body is hot at once, with headache and constipation, and by the sixth or seventh day there is severe thirst, manic speech, throwing off clothes and covers, restlessness and reversal cold of the legs and hands: this is heat reaching its extreme and producing reversal cold, and after a while it will become warm and hot again. One must on no account give hot medicines at once; give Xiao chaihu tang and the like for the time being. Formerly the state physician Sun Yonghe had a method for probing this; only after carefully examining the external signs may medicines be used. In all cases of cold damage with reversal cold of the limbs, one should investigate the cause of the illness. If the pattern is unclear and yin and yang cannot yet be distinguished, take the four-ingredient Lizhong wan, add licorice and blend evenly again to make Sishun lizhong wan, and give it as a probe. If it is yin reversal, after taking the medicine the patient becomes quiet, not hot and settled, and the Lizhong tang mentioned above should gradually be added. If after taking Sishun lizhong wan there is vexation and agitation, restlessness, vexing thirst and manic speech, and the patient throws off clothes and covers, this is yang reversal: hot medicines must not be given, and Xiao chaihu tang and the like mentioned above should be used.

## Notes

On yang reversal: fever, headache and constipation at onset, followed by severe thirst, manic speech, throwing off covers, restlessness and cold extremities by days six or seven, is extreme heat producing coldness (yang reversal); hot medicines are forbidden and Xiao chaihu tang is given. When yin and yang cannot be distinguished, following the method of the Song state physician Sun Yonghe, Sishun lizhong wan (Lizhong wan with extra licorice) is given as a probe: if the patient settles it is yin reversal and Lizhong tang is added; if agitation and delirium appear it is yang reversal and Xiao chaihu tang is used.
