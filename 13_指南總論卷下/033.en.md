# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷下 033 論小児疹痘証 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On the various illnesses of children.

On the pattern of rashes and pox in children. When a child has sores and rashes, the signs are alternating heat and coolness, yawning with vexation and oppression, cough and sneezing, cold ears and nose and cold feet, aversion only to heat and not to wind, severe heat of the whole body, with or without convulsions, and continuous fever: this is pox and rash. Give Shengma gegen tang (Shomakakkonto), Xingxing san or Xiaodu xijiao yin. If there is already diarrhea, do not give other medicines; give only Xingxing san.

## Notes

The first discussion under "On the various illnesses of children": pox and rashes in children (alternating heat and coolness, yawning, oppression, cough, sneezing, cold ears, nose and feet, aversion to heat, high fever, with or without convulsions): Shengma gegen tang, Xingxing san or Xiaodu xijiao yin; only Xingxing san if there is diarrhea.
