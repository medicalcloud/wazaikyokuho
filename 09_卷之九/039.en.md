# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 039 烏金散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wujin san (Black Gold Powder)

It treats women who have long been childless and those who have repeatedly miscarried. All arises from old illness harboured in the *chong* and *ren* vessels, with menses at irregular times, sudden unceasing bleeding, two periods in a month, early or late, or dribbling without end; and accumulated cold in the womb, flooding, spotting and discharge, cold pain below the navel, and cramping heaviness of the lower abdomen. All the above disorders prevent pregnancy and birth. It can also treat dizziness of the head and eyes, palpitation and shortness of breath. (Another formula appears later.)

baizong (old palm fibre)　wumei (mume fruit)　ganjiang (dried ginger; all three burned with nature preserved; 5 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder. Take 2 to 3 *qian* each time mixed in decocted mume decoction. For severe flooding and spotting, three or four doses a day; always on an empty stomach before meals.

## Notes

Wujin san (Baoqing-era additions): for infertility and habitual miscarriage arising from old disorders of the *chong* and *ren* (irregular menses, abnormal bleeding, cold womb, flooding, discharge, cold pain below the navel, lower abdominal heaviness), and for dizziness, palpitation and breathlessness.
- Old palm fibre, mume and dried ginger burned black with nature preserved (hence "black") to stop bleeding and warm.
