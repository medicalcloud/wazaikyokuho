# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 007 黄耆丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangqi wan (Ogigan; Astragalus Pill)

It treats men's wind toxin of the kidneys attacking upward with deficiency puffiness of the head and face, cicada sounds in the ears, clouding and dizziness of the head and eyes and stiffness of the neck and back; and pouring down into the lower back and legs with sores on the legs and knees, difficulty walking, dull pain under the feet with inability to stand on the ground, cramping of the sinews and vessels with inability to bend and stretch, weakness of the limbs, aching pain in all the joints, cold pain of the lower back and thighs and slippery frequent urination; and paralysis and wind impediment with stubborn numbness of the whole body. It also treats women's blood wind with itching and painful limbs, slack, weak legs and knees and difficulty rising and sitting. It is suitable for all of these.

huangqi (ogi; astragalus)　du jili (tribulus; round parts removed)　chuanlianzi (toosendan fruit)　huixiang (fennel; stir-fried)　chuanwu (Sichuan aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　chixiaodou (sekishozu; rice bean)　dilong (earthworm; earth removed, stir-fried)　fangfeng (bofu; saposhnikovia; rhizome head and forks removed; 1 liang each)　wuyao (lindera; 2 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with flour paste boiled in wine. Take fifteen pills each time with warm wine or salt water; women take them with vinegar decoction; on an empty stomach.

## Notes

Huangqi wan: for facial puffiness, tinnitus, dizziness and neck stiffness when kidney wind toxin attacks upward, and for leg sores, difficulty walking, cramping, cold lumbar and thigh pain and frequent urination when it pours downward; also for paralysis, numbness and women's blood wind.
- Astragalus supplements; aconite, saposhnikovia, tribulus and earthworm dispel wind; toosendan, fennel and lindera move qi; rice bean removes damp.
