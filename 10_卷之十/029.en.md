# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 029 人参羌活散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Renshen qianghuo san (Ninjinkyokatsusan; Ginseng and Notopterygium Powder)

It treats pathogenic cold and warm disease, seasonal epidemics, sores and rashes in children with headache and body pain, vigorous fever and much sleeping; as well as tidal fever with vexing thirst and phlegm excess with cough.

chaihu (saiko; bupleurum; shoots removed)　duhuo (dokukatsu; pubescent angelica; rhizome head removed)　qianghuo (notopterygium; shoots removed; 2 liang each)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng; rhizome head removed)　xiongqiong (Sichuan lovage)　zhiqiao (bitter orange; pulp removed, stir-fried with bran)　fuling (bukuryo; poria; skin removed)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried; 1 liang each)　jiegeng (kikyo; platycodon)　qianhu (peucedanum)　tianma (gastrodia; soaked in wine, roasted)　digupi (lycium root bark; earth removed; half a liang each)

Make the above into a powder. For each dose take 1 *qian* with seven-tenths of a *zhan* of water and a little mint, decoct down to five-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm, at any time.

## Notes

Renshen qianghuo san: for headache, body pain, high fever and somnolence from pathogenic cold, warm disease, epidemics or eruptions in children, and for tidal fever, vexing thirst and phlegm cough.
- Renshen baidu san (Ninjinhaidokusan) (bupleurum, peucedanum, notopterygium, pubescent angelica, lovage, bitter orange, platycodon, poria, ginseng, licorice) plus gastrodia and lycium root bark, decocted with mint.
