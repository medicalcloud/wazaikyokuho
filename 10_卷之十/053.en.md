# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 053 助胃膏 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zhuwei gao (Stomach-Assisting Paste)

It treats weak, deficient stomach qi in children with inability to nurse or eat, distension and fullness of the abdomen and flanks, borborygmus and diarrhoea, regurgitation of milk and green stools, or occasional night crying, and abdominal pain from foetal cold.

bai doukou ren (white cardamom seed)　roudoukou (nutmeg; roasted in embers)　dingxiang (clove)　renshen (ginseng)　muxiang (costus root; 1 liang each)　bai fuling (white poria; skin removed)　guangui (official cinnamon; coarse bark removed)　baizhu (white atractylodes)　huoxiang (agastache leaves)　suoshaliren (amomum seed)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried; 2 liang each)　juhong (red tangerine peel; white removed)　shanyao (Chinese yam; 4 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder and knead with refined honey into a paste. Give one pill the size of a euryale seed each time, adjusted for the child's size, dissolved in rice water, at any time.

## Notes

Zhuwei gao (from here the layer of Chunyou-era additions): for poor feeding, abdominal and flank distension, borborygmus, diarrhoea, milk regurgitation, green stools, night crying and abdominal pain from foetal cold with weak stomach qi in children.
- Ginseng, atractylodes, poria, Chinese yam and licorice supplement; white cardamom, nutmeg, clove and cinnamon warm; costus, agastache, amomum and red tangerine peel move qi.
