# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 085 五疳消食丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wugan xiaoshi wan (Five Gan Food-Dispersing Pill)

It treats the five gan and eight dysenteries in children, kills worms in the abdomen and zang organs, and treats gan taxation and "galloping horse" (rapidly spreading gan of the gums) with festering teeth and lips, and a big belly with green veins. This medicine greatly improves eating, gladdens the complexion and grows the flesh.

mainie (barley sprout)　shijunzi (quisqualis fruit; husk removed, stir-fried)　huanglian (oren; coptis; fibrous roots removed, lightly stir-fried)　juhong (red tangerine peel; dried over a fire)　cao longdan (ryutan; gentian)　wuyi (elm seed paste)

Make equal parts of the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of millet grains with millet paste. Take twenty or thirty pills each time on an empty stomach, swallowed with rice water, at any time, adjusted for the child's age.

## Notes

Wugan xiaoshi wan (bureau additions): for the five gan and eight dysenteries, intestinal worms, gan taxation and "galloping horse" gan (rapidly necrotizing gums) with festering teeth and lips, and a big belly with visible veins in children; improves appetite and grows flesh.
- Quisqualis and elm seed paste kill worms; coptis and gentian clear gan heat; barley sprout and red tangerine peel aid digestion.
