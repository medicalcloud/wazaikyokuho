# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 008 茴香丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huixiang wan (Uikyogan; Fennel Pill)

It treats men's long-standing deficiency of the original zang organ with cold qi attacking and surging, wringing pain of the navel and abdomen, stiffness of the lower back and spine, a withered yellow complexion and reduced eating; and qi pain of the bladder and small intestine; and wind toxin of the kidneys with deficiency puffiness of the head and face, dim eyes and tinnitus, weakness of the legs and knees, swelling, pain and sores. It is also suitable for women's deficiency cold of the blood zang organ with reduced eating, little strength and painful limbs. Taken for a long time it supplements deficiency and damage, removes wind and cold, strengthens sinews and bones and sharpens the ears and eyes.

weilingxian (clematis root; washed free of earth)　chuanwu (Sichuan aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　chenpi (chinpi; aged tangerine peel; white pith removed)　fangfeng (bofu; saposhnikovia; sprouts removed)　chuanlianzi (toosendan fruit; stir-fried with bran)　bixie (dioscorea hypoglauca; 3 liang each)　wuyao (lindera; earth removed; 5 liang)　chuanjiao (Sichuan pepper; seeds and unopened ones removed, stir-fried to sweat out the oil; 2 liang)　chixiaodou (sekishozu; rice bean)　huixiang (fennel; stir-fried; 8 liang each)　dilong (earthworm; earth removed, stir-fried; 7 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with flour paste boiled in wine. Take twenty pills each time on an empty stomach and before the evening meal with warm wine; salt water is also fine. For small-intestine qi pain, take them with stir-fried ginger and fennel in wine; for cramps of the legs, with chaenomeles decoction; for women's deficiency cold of the blood zang organ, with warm vinegar decoction; for wringing pain of the navel and abdomen and slippery diarrhea or cold dysentery, with strongly decocted mugwort decoction.

## Notes

Huixiang wan: for wringing umbilical pain, back stiffness, sallow complexion and poor appetite from long-standing deficiency of the original zang organ and cold qi, for bladder and small-intestine qi pain (mounting), for facial puffiness, dim eyes, tinnitus and weak, swollen, ulcerated legs from kidney wind toxin, and for women's cold blood zang organ.
- Fennel, Sichuan pepper and aconite warm; lindera, toosendan and tangerine peel move qi; clematis, saposhnikovia, dioscorea, earthworm and rice bean dispel wind-damp; the vehicle varies with the symptom.
