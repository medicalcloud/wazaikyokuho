# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 092 産後将護法 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Method of Care After Delivery

According to the classic: a woman should not worry only at the time of delivery; after delivery she must take great care of herself. She must on no account, because nothing went wrong at delivery, follow her whims and indulge herself without restraint; an offence slight as an autumn hair may bring an illness heavier than Mount Song or Mount Tai. As soon as she has given birth, above all do not ask whether it is a boy or a girl; observe how much blood is discharged and, according to the signs, give medicine to suppress blood dizziness. After a while give her gruel and Sishun lizhong wan, and have someone press from below the heart down to the navel and abdomen, five to seven times a day. If there are signs of illness, give medicine according to the signs, alternating gruel and medicine and taking them frequently. She should sit with eyes closed, her back leaning against something, watched over on both sides. Keep both knees always raised; even if she falls asleep at times, wake her frequently. Only after a full day and night may she get into bed, and still with knees raised. Prop the head of the bed high, spread thick bedding, screen all four sides and stop up the gaps against harmful wind. During the first seven days let her constantly smell vinegar smoke to prevent dizziness and oppression. After the first seven days gradually add rich food, or boil mutton or a hen to make a thick broth for gruel, continuing until the hundredth day. Regularly take Danggui wan, Danggui jianzhong tang and Sishun lizhong wan, one or two doses of each daily, to nourish the qi of the viscera and supplement the blood vessels. Only after the second seven days may she eat soft-cooked meat. Within the full month she must above all avoid eating and drinking at will, exposure to wind and cold, unrestrained joy and anger, exertion in combing her hair, loud voice, labour and fine handwork and the like, and going to the privy to relieve herself. If she restrains and cares for herself in this way until the hundredth day, her qi and blood will begin to be harmonized and her viscera and bowels restored to balance. If she does not follow this, residual postpartum illnesses will result.

## Notes

Method of care after delivery: guidance for the postpartum period—not asking the baby's sex immediately; giving medicine against blood dizziness according to the blood loss; gruel and Sishun lizhong wan with abdominal pressing; sitting with eyes closed and knees raised, not allowed to sleep deeply; going to bed only after a day and night, protected from drafts; smelling vinegar smoke for seven days; then nourishing broths and, after fourteen days, meat; regular Danggui wan, Danggui jianzhong tang and Sishun lizhong wan; prohibitions during the first month (free eating, wind and cold, emotion, combing hair, loud voice, work, the privy); recovery by the hundredth day.
