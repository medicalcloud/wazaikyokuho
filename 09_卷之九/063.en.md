# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 063 人参養血丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Renshen (ninjin) yangxue wan (Ginseng Blood-Nourishing Pill)

It treats women of timid, weak constitution with deficient, damaged blood and qi. Taken regularly it supplements the *chong* and *ren*, regulates the blood vessels, frees congestion and breaks accumulations, drives off pathogenic heat, removes cold impediment, relaxes hard distension of the middle and lower jiao, calms the spirit, moistens the complexion, frees qi and disperses oppression. It also treats women in pregnancy with gripping pain in the abdomen, dry mouth and inability to eat; dizziness from flooding damage; and emaciation that has not returned to normal a month after childbirth.

wumei (ubai) rou (mume flesh; 3 liang)　shu gan dihuang (kanjio; prepared rehmannia; 5 liang)　danggui (toki) (Chinese angelica; shoots removed; 2 liang)　renshen (ginseng)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage)　chi shaoyao (shakuyaku; red peony root)　changpu (shobu; acorus; lightly stir-fried; 1 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder, knead with honey, pound several thousand times and form pills the size of wutong seeds. Take fifty to a hundred pills each time with warm wine or rice broth, before meals.

## Notes

Renshen yangxue wan (bureau additions): for regular use by constitutionally weak women with deficient blood and qi; also for abdominal pain, dry mouth and poor appetite in pregnancy, dizziness after flooding, and wasting that persists a month after childbirth.
- Prepared rehmannia, angelica, lovage and red peony (the Siwu tang (Shimotsuto) pattern) with ginseng supplement; mume and acorus are added.
