# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 100 紫蘇湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zisu tang (Shisoto; Perilla Drink)

It regulates qi, frees the diaphragm, disperses phlegm and stops cough. It treats vexation and oppression of the heart and chest and dry mouth with much thirst.

zisu ye (perilla leaf; 6 jin)　wumei (ubai; mume fruit; stones removed, lightly stir-fried; 9 jin)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; stir-fried; 10 jin)　xingren (kyonin; apricot kernel; skin and tips removed, stir-fried with bran, pounded separately; 3 jin)

Powder the above together with 10 jin of stir-fried salt. Take 1 *qian* each time mixed with boiling water, at any time.

## Notes

Zisu tang ("Various drinks"): a drink that regulates qi, frees the diaphragm, disperses phlegm and stops cough; for chest oppression, dry mouth and thirst.
- Perilla leaf, mume, apricot kernel, licorice and salt.
