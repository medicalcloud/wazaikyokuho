# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 021 保生丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Baosheng wan (Life-Protecting Pill)

It nourishes the fetus, augments blood, and calms and harmonizes the womb. It treats improper care during pregnancy or exertion causing a restless fetus, painful, heavy lower back and abdomen, uterine obstruction and leaking fetus with occasional lochia-like discharge, illness harbored in the womb with long failure to conceive; or pregnancy that cannot be firmly nourished, with the fetus withered and dry and not growing, overdue past the year, or though the months are full, the fetus moves without strength; or miscarriage; and, at delivery, mismanagement with fright and disturbance too early, the lochia descending before the time of birth has come, the fetal sac dry and withered, leading to difficult labor; or transverse or breech presentation with extreme pain, oppression and confusion, no delivery for days, the child dead in the abdomen, the abdomen icy cold, the lips and mouth green-black and cold froth vomited. [It also treats] malign blood surging upward just after delivery with dizziness, oppression and loss of consciousness, dyspnea and sweating; static blood not fully discharged with gripping pain of the navel and abdomen and alternating fever and chills; or taxation damage from childbirth with weakness not yet recovered, a yellow face and wasted flesh, palpitation and night sweats, inability to eat, gradually becoming postpartum taxation. Taken regularly from the beginning of the last month, it strengthens qi, nourishes the fetus, rectifies and smooths the course of labor, and moistens the fetus for easy delivery. Taken regularly after childbirth, it nourishes blood and qi, harmonizes yin and yang, tightens the interstices, fills the bowels and zang organs, treats wind deficiency and removes chronic cold.

da maren (hemp seed; husks removed; 1½ liang)　beimu (baimo; fritillaria)　huangqin (ogon; scutellaria)　dadou huangjuan (daizuoken; soybean sprout)　jingmi (kobei; non-glutinous rice)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; lightly roasted until red)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　shihu (sekkoku; dendrobium; roots removed)　shigao (sekko; gypsum; finely ground; 1 liang each)　danggui (toki) (Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed, stir-fried; half a liang)　qinjiao (Qin pepper; lightly stir-fried to sweat out the oil; 1 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder, knead with refined honey and form pills the size of a pellet. Take one pill each time dissolved in warm wine or jujube decoction; it may also be chewed; on an empty stomach before meals.

## Notes

Baosheng wan: for a restless fetus, back and abdominal pain, bleeding in pregnancy, infertility, poor fetal growth, overdue pregnancy and miscarriage; for difficult labor (early rupture, transverse or breech presentation, dead fetus); and for postpartum surging of malign blood, abdominal pain from stasis, chills and fever and postpartum taxation. Taken in the last month for easy delivery and after birth to nourish blood and qi.
- Hemp seed, soybean sprout and rice moisten; fritillaria, scutellaria and gypsum clear heat; dried ginger, cinnamon and Qin pepper warm; dendrobium, angelica and licorice are added.
