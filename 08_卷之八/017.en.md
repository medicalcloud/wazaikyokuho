# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 017 清心蓮子飲 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qingxin lianzi yin (Seishinrenshiin; Heart-Clearing Lotus Seed Beverage)

The patterns treated and the formula appear in the section on chronic cold.

## Notes

Qingxin lianzi yin (Shaoxing-era additions): a cross-reference giving only the name; the patterns and formula are in the chronic-cold section (juan 5, no. 073).
