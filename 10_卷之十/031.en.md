# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 031 消毒散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xiaodu san (Toxin-Dispersing Powder)

It treats children whose sores and rashes have erupted but not yet come out evenly and fully, or whose toxic qi is congested and held back so that the eruption is not free, with vigorous fever and manic agitation, blockage of the throat and diaphragm, unquiet sleep and constipated, rough stools; and it treats congested heat of the upper diaphragm in adults and children, with swollen, painful throat and blockage of the chest and diaphragm.

niubangzi (arctium seed; scorched; 6 liang)　jingjie sui (schizonepeta spikes; 1 liang)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried; 2 liang)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 1 *qian* with one *zhan* of water, decoct down to seven-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm, after meals; for children give small amounts according to their strength. When treating sores and rashes, if the bowels are loose it should not be given.

## Notes

Xiaodu san: for eruptions that fail to come out fully, and for high fever, agitation, throat blockage, insomnia and constipation from obstructed toxin in children; also for upper-diaphragm heat and sore throat in adults and children. Not for use when there is diarrhoea.
- Three ingredients: arctium seed, schizonepeta and licorice (the Xiaodu xijiao yin of juan 6, no. 018, without saposhnikovia).
- Emendation: "1 *qian* per dose" reads "1 *zhan*" in the base text; emended following the Siku quanshu and Executive Yuan editions.
