# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 012 定志丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Dingzhi wan (Will-Settling Pill)

It is suitable for unsettled heart qi and insufficiency of the five zang organs, with abstraction, trembling and palpitation, worry, sorrow, grief and pain, mistakes and forgetfulness, fright and nightmares in dreams, fear and restlessness, joy and anger at any time, better in the morning and worse in the evening or better in the evening and worse in the morning, or outbreaks of mania and dizziness.

yuanzhi (onji; polygala; sprouts and core removed)　changpu (shobu; acorus; 2 liang each)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng)　bai fuling (bukuryo; white poria; skin removed; 3 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder, form pills the size of wutong seeds with refined honey and coat them with cinnabar. Take seven pills each time, increasing to twenty, with warm rice gruel, after meals and at bedtime, three times a day. Taken regularly it benefits the heart, strengthens the will and prevents forgetfulness.

## Notes

Dingzhi wan: for unsettled heart qi with abstraction, palpitation, worry, forgetfulness, nightmares, fear, mood swings, mania or dizziness.
- Polygala, acorus, ginseng and poria coated with cinnabar (the Dingzhi xiaowan of the *Qianjin fang*).
