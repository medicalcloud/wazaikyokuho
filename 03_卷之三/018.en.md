# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 018 平胃散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Pingwei san (Heiisan; Stomach-Calming Powder)

It is suitable for disharmony of the spleen and stomach with no desire for food, distension, fullness and stabbing pain of the heart, abdomen, flanks and ribs, a bitter mouth without taste, chest fullness with shortness of breath, retching and nausea, belching and acid swallowing, a withered yellow complexion, an emaciated, weak body, lassitude with a liking for lying down, a heavy body with painful joints, frequent spontaneous diarrhea, or outbreaks of sudden turmoil; and for the five chokings and eight glomuses, diaphragm qi and stomach reflux.

cangzhu (black atractylodes; coarse skin removed, soaked in rice-washing water for two days; 5 jin)　houpo (koboku; magnolia bark; coarse bark removed, processed with ginger juice and stir-fried until fragrant)　chenpi (chinpi; aged tangerine peel; white pith removed; 3 jin 2 liang each)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; stir-fried; 30 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one *zhan* of water, two slices of fresh ginger and two dried jujubes, decoct together down to seven-tenths, remove the ginger and jujubes and take it hot, on an empty stomach before meals. It may also be taken with a pinch of salt stirred into boiling water. Taken regularly it regulates qi and warms the stomach, transforms old food, disperses phlegm-fluid retention and wards off the unseasonable qi of wind, cold, chill and damp in any season.

## Notes

Pingwei san: for poor appetite, abdominal fullness and pain, bitter mouth, nausea, acid reflux, lassitude, heaviness and diarrhea from damp stagnation of the spleen and stomach, and for the five chokings, eight glomuses, diaphragm qi and stomach reflux. It is still widely used for indigestion and heaviness of the stomach.
- Black atractylodes, magnolia bark, tangerine peel and licorice; the foundation of many formulas (Buhuanjin zhengqi san [Fukankinshokisan], Duijin yinzi, Weiling tang, etc.).
