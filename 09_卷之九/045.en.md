# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 045 人参鼈甲丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Renshen biejia wan (Ginseng and Turtle Shell Pill)

It treats all deficiency damage in women with emaciated, haggard flesh, night sweats and palpitation, cough with qi ascent, irregular menses, or chills and fever, and no desire for food.

xingren (apricot kernel; soaked in hot water, skin and tips removed, stir-fried)　renshen (ginseng)　danggui (Chinese angelica; washed, dried over a fire)　chi shaoyao (red peony root)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried)　chaihu (bupleurum; shoots removed)　jiegeng (platycodon; rhizome head removed; 1 liang each)　digupi (lycium root bark)　xuan huanglian (Xuanzhou coptis; fibrous roots removed)　hu huanglian (picrorhiza; 1 *fen* each)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　muxiang (costus root; half a liang each)　shexiang (musk; ground separately; half a *fen*)　biejia (turtle shell; one, weighing about 2 liang, roasted with vinegar until yellow)

Make the above into a fine powder. Grind 1 jin of sweet wormwood to a pulp and squeeze out the juice; boil it down with 5 sheng of a boy's urine and 5 sheng of wine to about 2 sheng, then add 3 liang of genuine ghee and 3 liang of white granulated honey and boil down again to a paste; when cool, add the medicine powders, knead evenly and form pills the size of wutong seeds. Take fifty pills each time with warm wine, at any time.

## Notes

Renshen biejia wan (Chunyou-era additions): for wasting, night sweats, palpitation, cough, qi ascent, irregular menses, chills and fever and poor appetite from all deficiency damage in women.
- Turtle shell, bupleurum, lycium root bark, picrorhiza, coptis and sweet wormwood clear deficiency heat; ginseng, angelica, red peony and licorice supplement; apricot kernel and platycodon stop cough; cinnamon, costus and musk are added; made into pills with a paste of sweet wormwood juice, boy's urine, wine, ghee and honey.
