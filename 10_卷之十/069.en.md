# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 069 竜骨丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Longgu wan (Dragon Bone Pill)

It treats children with long-standing red and white dysentery, very frequent day and night, abdominal pain, emaciation and weakness, and no desire for food.

huanglian (coptis; fibrous roots removed, lightly stir-fried)　huangbo (phellodendron)　bai longgu (white dragon bone)　helile pi (chebule fruit rind; blast-fried, stone removed)　muxiang (costus root; 1 *fen* each)　danggui (Chinese angelica; lightly stir-fried)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried)　baifan (alum; dried, ground; half a liang each)　hufen (lead white; lightly stir-fried until yellow; 3 *fen*)

Pound and sift the above into a powder, knead with refined honey and form pills the size of mung beans. For a three-year-old, ten pills each time with warm gruel water, three times a day, adjusted at the time for the child's size.

## Notes

Longgu wan (Wu Zhige's additions): for frequent stools, abdominal pain, wasting and poor appetite in long-standing red and white dysentery in children.
- Dragon bone, chebule, alum and lead white astringe; coptis and phellodendron clear heat; dried ginger warms; angelica and costus are added. It contains lead.
