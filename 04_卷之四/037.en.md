# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之四 037 人参定喘湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Renshen dingchuan tang (Ginseng Panting-Settling Decoction)

It treats men and women with lung-qi cough, of many years or recent, with rising qi and urgent dyspnea, the sound of drool in the throat, chest fullness with qi counterflow, restlessness sitting or lying and inability to get food down; and the lungs affected by pathogenic cold with cough, a heavy voice, loss of voice, nasal congestion and a clouded head.

renshen (ninjin; ginseng; sliced)　mahuang (mao; ephedra; nodes removed)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried)　ejiao (akyo; donkey-hide gelatin; stir-fried)　banxia qu (pinellia leaven; 1 liang each)　sangbaipi (mulberry root bark)　wuweizi (gomishi; schisandra; 1½ liang each)　yingsuqiao (poppy husk; brushed with honey and roasted; 2 liang)

Make the above into a coarse powder and mix in the ginseng slices evenly. For each dose take three large *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water and three slices of fresh ginger, decoct together down to seven-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm after meals. It also treats children with long illness, urgent dyspnea of lung qi, the sound of drool in the throat, discomfort of the chest and diaphragm and vomiting of phlegm and froth; adjust further according to age.

## Notes

Renshen dingchuan tang (bureau additions): for old or recent lung-qi cough with dyspnea, gurgling in the throat, chest fullness, insomnia and poor appetite, for cough, loss of voice and nasal congestion when the lungs are affected by cold, and for chronic dyspnea in children.
- Ephedra and mulberry bark diffuse and lower the lungs; ginseng, gelatin and licorice supplement and moisten; schisandra and poppy husk astringe the lungs; pinellia leaven transforms phlegm.
