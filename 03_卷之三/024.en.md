# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 024 積気丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jiqi wan (Accumulated Qi Pill)

It treats disharmony of yin and yang with weakness of the viscera and bowels, cold qi lingering and stagnating within so that qi accumulates without dispersing, propping fullness of the chest and flanks, choking qi as soon as one eats, distension of the heart and abdomen, stabbing and urgent qi (one version reads "urgent qi with stabbing pain"), undigested old food, drawing pain of the heart and abdomen, belching and acid swallowing, soaking of lodged rheum, nausea and vomiting counterflow, painful aggregation lumps, disordered viscera and bowels, inability to eat, alternating chills and fever, gradual wasting until one is bedridden, a yellow face, hot skin and an exhausted spirit.

badou (croton seeds; 100, skin, core and membranes removed, oil pressed out to obtain the frost; 3 *qian*)　taoren (peach kernel; skin and tips removed, stir-fried with bran, ground separately; 1½ liang)　fuzi (aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed; 4 liang)　rice vinegar (5 *sheng*, used with the sal ammoniac and rhubarb and simmered over a gentle fire into a paste)　dahuang (rhubarb; wrapped in flour and roasted, flour removed, powdered)　ganqi (dried lacquer; stir-fried until scorched)　muxiang (costus root)　biejia (turtle shell; roasted yellow with vinegar; 1 liang each)　sanleng (sparganium; roasted, crushed while hot)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　naosha (sal ammoniac; ground; 2 liang each)　zhusha (cinnabar; ground and water-ground)　shexiang (musk; ground separately; 2½ *qian* each)

Make the above into a fine powder, add the ground medicines and mix evenly, and form pills the size of wutong seeds with the vinegar paste. Take two pills each time, warm, with stir-fried ginger decoction; costus decoction is also fine. Take after meals and at bedtime, adjusting further according to deficiency or repletion. Avoid raw, cold and hard foods.

## Notes

Jiqi wan: for propping fullness, distension, pain, nausea, chills and fever and wasting from qi accumulations, old food, lodged rheum and aggregation lumps formed when cold qi stagnates in weak viscera.
- A strongly purgative formula: croton frost, rhubarb, dried lacquer, sparganium, peach kernel, turtle shell and sal ammoniac attack and purge accumulations; aconite and cinnamon warm; costus and musk move qi.
