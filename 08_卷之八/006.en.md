# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 006 克効餅子 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Kexiao bingzi (Sure-Effect Cakes)

It can govern all kinds of malaria with attacks at set times: first yawning and stretching, then chills and shivering, chattering jaws, cold inside and out, pain of the lower back and spine; when the shivering ends, heat inside and out, headache as if splitting, thirst with desire for cold drinks; or phlegm accumulated in the chest with vexing fullness and nausea; whether heat first and then cold, or cold first and then heat, more cold than heat, more heat than cold, cold and heat equal, heat only without cold, cold only without heat; whether one attack a day, one every other day, or one attack followed by another six or seven days later.

gancao (licorice; scorched)　lüdou mo (mung bean powder)　heye (lotus leaf; scorched; 5 liang each)　dingfen (lead white; ground)　longnao (borneol; ground)　shexiang (musk; ground; half a liang each)　jinbo (gold leaf; 25 leaves, for coating)　xinpi (arsenic from Xinzhou; boiled in vinegar; 2½ liang)　zhusha (cinnabar; ground and water-ground; 1 liang 1 *fen*)

Powder the above, knead with refined honey, make twenty pills to each liang, pinch them flat and coat them with gold leaf. Take one cake each time, ground and dissolved in freshly drawn water. For daily attacks, take it before the attack; for alternate-day attacks, on the night of the day without an attack; for attacks every few days, on the night before; for consecutive daily attacks, at dawn.

## Notes

Kexiao bingzi: for all types of malaria (attacks of chills and shivering followed by high fever, headache, thirst, phlegm with nausea; any order or proportion of cold and heat; daily, alternate-day or periodic attacks).
- Mainly arsenic, with lead white, cinnabar, borneol and musk plus mung bean, lotus leaf and licorice, made into flat pills coated with gold leaf; the time of taking depends on the pattern of attacks. It contains arsenic and lead and is highly toxic.
