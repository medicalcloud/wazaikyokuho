# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之四 035 人参清肺湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Renshen qingfei tang (Ninjinseihaito; Ginseng Lung-Clearing Decoction)

It treats deficiency cold of the lungs and stomach with cough and urgent dyspnea, choking oppression of the chest and diaphragm, distension and fullness of the abdomen and ribs, pressing obstruction with shortness of breath, a liking for cold drinks, and choking with a dull pain in the throat. It also treats atrophy of the lung lobes and taxation cough with spitting of fishy-smelling blood, dry retching with vexing heat, loss of voice, wasted flesh, lassitude and reduced eating.

digupi (lycium root bark)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng; rhizome head removed)　ejiao (akyo; donkey-hide gelatin; stir-fried with bran)　xingren (kyonin; apricot kernel; skin and tips removed, stir-fried with bran)　sangbaipi (mulberry root bark; coarse bark removed)　zhimu (chimo; anemarrhena)　wumei (ubai; smoked plum; stone removed)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried)　yingsuqiao (poppy husk; stalk and cap removed, roasted with honey)

Take equal parts of the above and cut into a coarse powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water, one smoked plum and one jujube, decoct together down to one *zhan*, strain off the dregs and take it lukewarm after meals and at bedtime. Keep the dregs of two doses and decoct them together as one dose.

## Notes

Renshen qingfei tang (bureau additions): for cough, dyspnea, chest oppression, abdominal fullness, shortness of breath, a liking for cold drinks and throat pain from lung-stomach deficiency, and for blood-streaked sputum, vexing heat, loss of voice and wasting in atrophy of the lung lobes and taxation cough.
- Ginseng, gelatin and licorice supplement and moisten; lycium bark, anemarrhena and mulberry bark clear lung heat; apricot kernel stops cough; smoked plum and poppy husk astringe the lungs. Though the indications say "deficiency cold", the composition contains many heat-clearing drugs.
