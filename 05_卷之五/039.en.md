# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 039 黄耆鼈甲散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangqi biejia san (Astragalus and Turtle Shell Powder)

It treats visiting heat in deficiency taxation with wasted flesh, weary limbs, vexing heat of the five hearts, dry mouth and throat, red cheeks and palpitation, tidal fever in the evening, night sweats, discomfort of the chest and flanks, reduced eating with much thirst, thick sticky cough and spittle, and occasionally pus and blood.

renshen (ginseng)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　kugeng (bitter platycodon; 1 liang 6½ *qian* each)　sheng gan dihuang (dried raw rehmannia; washed, dried over a fire; 3 liang 3 *qian*)　banxia (pinellia; boiled)　ziwan (aster; rhizome head removed)　zhimu (anemarrhena)　chi shaoyao (red peony root)　huangqi (astragalus)　gancao (licorice; scorched)　sangbaipi (mulberry root bark; 2½ liang each)　tianmendong (asparagus root; core removed, dried over a fire)　biejia (turtle shell; rim removed, roasted with vinegar; 5 liang each)　qinjiao (large-leaf gentian; rhizome head removed)　bai fuling (white poria; dried over a fire)　digupi (lycium root bark; earth removed)　chaihu (bupleurum; rhizome head removed; 3 liang 3 *qian* each)

Chop the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take two large *qian* with one *zhan* of water, decoct down to seven-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm, after meals.

## Notes

Huangqi biejia san (Wu Zhige's additions): the same indications as the earlier Renshen huangqi san (juan 5, no. 014), with nearly the same ingredients (cinnamon added and more turtle shell).
