# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之四 012 百部丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Baibu wan (Stemona Pill)

It treats disordered lung qi with cough and urgent dyspnea, vexation and oppression of the chest and diaphragm, dry lips and mouth, puffiness of the face and eyes, discomfort of the throat, accumulated for a long time without healing. It is also suitable for coughing and spitting of pus and blood.

tianmendong (tenmondo; asparagus root; core removed; 1 jin)　xingren (kyonin; apricot kernel; skin and tips removed, stir-fried)　huangqi (ogi; astragalus)　baibu root (stemona; 6 liang each)　gualou root (trichosanthes root; 16 liang)　zisu (perilla)　ziwan (aster; sprouts removed, washed)　madouling (aristolochia fruit; 22 liang each)　heishen (scrophularia; 8 liang)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed; 4 liang)

Make the above together into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with refined honey. Take fifteen pills each time, warm, with decocted smoked plum and licorice decoction, after meals.

## Notes

Baibu wan: for chronic, unhealing cough, dyspnea, chest oppression, dry lips, facial puffiness and throat discomfort from disordered lung qi, and for coughing of pus and blood.
- Stemona, aster, aristolochia fruit and apricot kernel stop cough; asparagus root, trichosanthes root and scrophularia moisten and clear; astragalus supplements; perilla and cinnamon are added.
