# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 011 大天南星丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Da tiannanxing wan (Major Arisaema Pill)

It treats acute and chronic fright wind in children with tidal surging of drool and convulsions, eyes turned upward, mouth and eyes drawn together, clenched jaw, rigid spine, and a blocked spirit with loss of consciousness for days on end.

longnao (borneol; ground)　niuhuang (bovine bezoar; ground)　ruxiang (frankincense; ground; 1 *qian* each)　tiannanxing (arisaema; processed with ox bile; half a liang)　renshen (ginseng)　tianma (gastrodia; rhizome head removed)　fangfeng (saposhnikovia; rhizome head removed; 1 *fen* each)　zhusha (cinnabar; ground; 3 *qian*)　ganxie (dried scorpion; fourteen, moistened in hot water, earth removed, lightly stir-fried, powdered)　shexiang (musk; ground; 1½ *qian*)

Grind and pound the above evenly, knead with refined honey and form pills the size of a large euryale seed. Take one pill each time dissolved in schizonepeta-mint decoction; adjust as judged for the child's size; at any time.

## Notes

Da tiannanxing wan: for surging drool, convulsions, upward gaze, mouth and eyes drawn together, clenched jaw, rigid spine and prolonged loss of consciousness from acute or chronic fright wind in children.
- Bile-processed arisaema, gastrodia, scorpion and saposhnikovia dispel wind and phlegm; bovine bezoar, borneol, musk and cinnabar open the orifices and settle; ginseng and frankincense are added.
