# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 007 妙香丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Miaoxiang wan (Myokogan; Marvelous Fragrance Pill)

It treats seasonal illnesses and cold damage in men and women, resolves the five toxins, and treats tidal fever and accumulated heat, as well as children's fright epilepsy and the hundred illnesses.

badou (hazu; croton seed; 315 seeds, skin, germ and membrane removed, stir-fried until cooked, ground like oily flour)　niuhuang (goo; bovine bezoar; ground)　longnao (borneol; ground)　nifen (calomel; ground)　shexiang (jako; musk; ground; 3 liang each)　chensha (cinnabar; water-ground; 9 liang)　jinbo (gold leaf; ground; 90 leaves)

Grind the above together evenly; refine 6 liang of yellow wax, add 3 *fen* of white granulated honey, refine together evenly and form pills, thirty to each liang. For tidal fever, accumulated heat, chest bind and jaundice in cold damage, manic running with agitated heat, dry mouth and red face, and blockage of urine and stools, give one pill with decocted rhubarb and mix-fried licorice decoction. For toxic dysentery with bloody stools, decoct coptis decoction and dissolve a little calomel in it. For alcohol toxin, food toxin, tea toxin, qi toxin, latent wind-phlegm with stuffiness, vomiting and counterflow and the like, give it in all cases with calomel, borneol and rice water. For poisoning with vomiting of blood, oppression, restlessness near death, give it with fresh human blood and it heals at once. For children's hundred illnesses, fright epilepsy, acute and chronic fright wind with tidal surging of drool and convulsions, give two pills the size of mung beans with borneol, calomel and honey decoction. For heat from the various accumulations and food accumulation, red cheeks, agitation, unquiet sleep, crying with fright and diarrhea, give it in all cases with gold-and-silver mint decoction, adjusting further for age. If an adult or a woman suffering from cold damage or a seasonal illness has yin and yang qi interlocked and bound, toxic qi lying latent in the stomach, dyspnea, agitation and red eyes, tidal fever without fixed pattern, and after seven or eight days up to half a month is still not at ease, with the physician unable to understand and the signs and pulse in confusion, one pill may be given, or it may be divided into three; in all cases dissolve borneol and calomel in about half a *zhan* of rice water and give it. This single dose turns down and expels all foul toxic drool, and the pill is purged out along with it. If one wishes to recover it, wash it clean in water, wrap it in oiled paper, bury it in the ground and take it out after five days; it can be given again. When adults or children have taken one pill according to the method and it has saved three people, it can no longer be used. If one wants the medicine to act quickly, prick a hole in it with a needle and soak it in cold water for a short time before taking it; the effect will be even faster.

## Notes

Miaoxiang wan: for seasonal illnesses and cold damage, the five toxins, tidal fever and accumulated heat, and children's fright epilepsy and hundred illnesses. The vehicle changes with the condition: chest bind, jaundice, mania and constipation; toxic dysentery with bleeding; alcohol and food toxins; poisoning with hematemesis; children's fright wind and accumulated heat; and protracted, unclear cases of cold damage.
- Croton seed purges strongly; bovine bezoar, borneol, musk, cinnabar and gold leaf open the orifices and calm; calomel (a mercury compound) is added; the pills are made with yellow wax and honey. The expelled pill is washed and reused, said to serve three patients. It contains croton and mercury and is highly toxic.
