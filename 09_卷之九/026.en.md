# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 026 半夏茯苓湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Banxia fuling tang (Hangebukuryoto; Pinellia and Poria Decoction)

It treats morning sickness in pregnancy with confused oppression in the chest, dizziness of the head and eyes, weary limbs, vexing pain in all the joints, phlegm counterflow in the chest and diaphragm, vomiting and nausea, aversion to the smell of food, a liking for salty and sour things, much lying down and little getting up, and complete inability to eat.

xuanfuhua (senpukuka; inula flower)　chenpi (chinpi; aged tangerine peel; pulp removed, stir-fried with bran)　jiegeng (kikyo; platycodon)　bai shaoyao (shakuyaku; white peony root)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; lightly roasted until red)　xiongqiong (Sichuan lovage; half a liang each)　shu gan dihuang (kanjio; prepared rehmannia; soaked in wine)　chi fuling (bukuryo; red poria; skin removed; 3 *fen* each)　banxia (hange; pinellia; washed ten times in hot water, sliced, dried over a fire; 1 liang 2 *fen*)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water and four slices of fresh ginger, decoct together down to eight-tenths, remove the dregs and take it fairly hot, before meals. Then take Fuling wan (Bukuryogan), and the phlegm and water are dispersed and the patient can eat.

## Notes

Banxia fuling tang: for morning sickness with chest oppression, dizziness, lassitude, joint pain, phlegm, vomiting, nausea, aversion to food smells, cravings for salty and sour things, a tendency to stay in bed and inability to eat. Followed by Fuling wan (the next entry).
- Pinellia, red poria, tangerine peel and inula remove phlegm and stop vomiting; ginseng and licorice supplement; prepared rehmannia, lovage and white peony nourish blood and calm the fetus; platycodon is added.
