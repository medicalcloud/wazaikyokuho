# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 038 撞気阿魏丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zhuangqi awei wan (Qi-Ramming Asafoetida Pill)

It treats the five kinds of choking disease, the nine kinds of heart pain, strings, aggregations and qi lumps, cold qi attacking and stabbing, and cold lodged in the spleen and stomach with fullness and distension of the chest, abdominal pain and borborygmus and vomiting of sour water; men's small-intestine qi, and women's blood qi and blood stabbing.

huixiang (fennel; stir-fried)　qingpi (green tangerine peel; white removed)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; stir-fried)　peng'ezhu (zedoary; blast-fried)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage)　chenpi (chinpi; aged tangerine peel; white pith removed; 1 liang each)　baizhi (byakushi; angelica dahurica; half a liang)　dingxiangpi (clove bark; blast-fried; 1 liang)　suoshaner (amomum)　rougui (cinnamon bark; skin removed; half a liang each)　shengjiang (shokyo; fresh ginger; 4 liang, sliced, salted overnight with half a liang of salt, stir-fried black)　hujiao (black pepper)　awei (asafoetida; soaked overnight in vinegar and made into paste with flour; 2½ *qian* each)

Pound the above into a powder and form pills the size of a euryale seed with the asafoetida paste; for each jin of pills use 7 *qian* of cinnabar for coating. For men's qi pain take one or two pills with stir-fried ginger and salt decoction. For women's blood qi, with vinegar decoction. For regular use, chew one pill well and take it with tea or wine as preferred.

## Notes

Zhuangqi awei wan (Shaoxing-era additions): for the five chokings, nine heart pains, strings, aggregations and qi lumps, stabbing pain from cold qi, chest fullness, abdominal pain and vomiting from cold lodged in the spleen and stomach, men's small-intestine qi (mounting) and women's blood-qi pain.
- Asafoetida and zedoary break up accumulations; fennel, clove bark, cinnamon, black pepper and salted ginger warm; the tangerine peels, amomum, lovage and angelica dahurica move qi.
