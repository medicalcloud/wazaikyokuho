# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 064 焼脾散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaopi san (Spleen-Burning Powder)

It treats weak spleen and stomach with long-standing cold and accumulated chill, heart-qi and spleen pain, stomach reflux with cold phlegm, stabbing pain of the navel and abdomen, vomiting and nausea and no desire for food; and women's blood qi attacking and stabbing with griping pain of the abdomen and flanks. It works at once.

chi shaoyao (shakuyaku; red peony root)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried; 6½ liang each)　liangjiang (galangal; stir-fried in oil; 10 liang)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried; 4 liang)

Powder the above. Take two large *qian* each time stirred into plain hot water, at any time.

## Notes

Shaopi san (Baoqing-era additions): for heart and spleen pain, stomach reflux, stabbing abdominal pain, vomiting and poor appetite from weak spleen and stomach with long-standing cold, and for women's stabbing blood-qi pain.
- Four ingredients: galangal and dried ginger warm, red peony harmonizes the blood and stops pain, licorice harmonizes.
