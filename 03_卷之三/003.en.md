# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 003 丁沈丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Dingchen wan (Clove and Aquilaria Pill)

It treats all cold qi attacking the heart, abdomen, flanks and ribs with distension, fullness and stabbing pain, choking obstruction of the chest and diaphragm, phlegm counterflow and nausea, belching and acid swallowing, no desire for food, cold counterflow in the stomach with incessant vomiting; as well as stomach reflux and diaphragm qi, old food and prolonged fluid retention, heart pain and sudden turmoil; and women's blood-qi pain of the heart and abdomen.

gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried)　qingpi (green tangerine peel; pulp removed, chopped, stir-fried)　dingxiang (clove)　bai doukou ren (white cardamom)　chenxiang (aquilaria)　muxiang (mokko; costus root)　binglang (areca)　roudoukou ren (nutmeg; 5 liang each)　baizhu (byakujutsu; white atractylodes; chopped, lightly stir-fried; 40 liang)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng; rhizome head removed)　fuling (bukuryo; poria; skin removed)　helile (kariroku; chebule; roasted, skin taken; 10 liang each)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried until cracked; 2½ liang each)　shexiang (jako; musk; ground separately; 1 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder, add the musk and mix evenly, and form pills the size of wild jujubes with refined honey. Take one pill each time, chewed thoroughly, with stir-fried ginger and salt decoction; warm wine is also fine. Take on an empty stomach before meals.

## Notes

Dingchen wan: for distension and pain of the heart, abdomen and flanks from cold qi, chest obstruction, nausea, acid reflux and vomiting, stomach reflux, diaphragm qi, old food, prolonged fluid retention, heart pain and sudden turmoil, and women's blood-qi abdominal pain.
- Based on white atractylodes, with ginseng, poria and licorice (Si junzi tang (Shikunshito)) to supplement the spleen, clove, aquilaria, costus, cardamom, nutmeg, cinnamon and dried ginger to warm and move qi, plus areca, green tangerine peel, chebule and musk.
