# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之四 041 半夏丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Banxia wan (Hangegan; Pinellia Pill)

It treats disordered lung qi with cough, dyspnea and fullness, congestion and blockage of phlegm and drool, hard fullness below the heart, shortness of breath with vexation and oppression; and wind congestion with phlegm excess, clouding and dizziness of the head and eyes, discomfort of the throat and diaphragm, vomiting and nausea, a clouded, confused mind, palpitation with heat, and thick sticky mucus and spittle.

baifan (alum; calcined; 15 liang)　banxia (hange; pinellia; washed in hot water to remove the slipperiness, covered with ginger juice overnight; 3 jin)

Pound the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with fresh ginger juice. Take twenty pills each time, increasing to thirty, with ginger decoction after meals and at bedtime.

## Notes

Banxia wan (bureau additions): for cough, dyspnea and fullness, phlegm congestion, hard fullness below the heart and shortness of breath from disordered lung qi, and for dizziness, throat discomfort, vomiting, clouded mind, palpitation and sticky phlegm from wind-phlegm.
- Two ingredients, pinellia and calcined alum, made into pills with ginger juice.
