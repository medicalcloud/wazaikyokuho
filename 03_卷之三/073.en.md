# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 073 丁香煮散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Dingxiang zhusan (Clove Boiled Powder)

It treats latent cold in the spleen and cold received in the stomach duct, with glomus and oppression of the chest and diaphragm, stabbing pain of the heart and abdomen, phlegm counterflow and nausea, cold cough with fullness of the middle, deficient and slippery viscera and bowels, reduced eating, stomach reflux with vomiting, and counterflow cold of the limbs. For all deep-lying cold and chronic chill, old or new, its effect cannot be fully described.

dingxiang (clove; not exposed to fire)　hongdou (galangal fruit; skin removed)　qingpi (green tangerine peel; white removed)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried)　chuanwu (Sichuan aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　chenpi (aged tangerine peel; white pith removed)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried)　liangjiang (galangal; blast-fried, rhizome head removed; 4 liang each)　yizhi (alpinia oxyphylla; skin removed; 5½ liang)　hujiao (black pepper; 2 liang)

Chop the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one *zhan* of water, three slices of ginger and a pinch of salt, decoct down to seven-tenths and take it fairly hot on an empty stomach before meals; decoct the dregs again. Stop as soon as the illness recedes. Excellent.

## Notes

Dingxiang zhusan (from here the layer of Wu Zhige's additions): for chest glomus, stabbing heart and abdominal pain, nausea, cold cough, diarrhoea, poor appetite, stomach reflux and cold limbs from latent cold in the spleen and stomach, and for deep-lying chronic cold.
- Acrid-hot drugs (clove, galangal fruit, aconite, dried ginger, galangal, alpinia, black pepper) warm; the tangerine peels move qi. Stop when the illness recedes.
