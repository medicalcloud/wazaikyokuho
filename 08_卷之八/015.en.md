# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 015 常山飲 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Changshan yin (Jozanin) (Dichroa Beverage)

It treats malaria. Malaria in general arises from external pathogen lodging at Fengfu and from raw and cold food damaging the spleen and stomach internally; sometimes cold first and then heat, sometimes heat first and then cold, sometimes cold or heat alone, sometimes attacks on consecutive days, sometimes one attack every other day. In the cold stage the limbs tremble; in the hot stage the whole body seems to burn, with headache and nausea, vexing thirst with drinking, dyspnea and hurried breath, a bitter mouth and dry tongue, aching pain of the spine, borborygmus and abdominal pain. When various medicines fail and it gradually becomes taxation malaria, this medicine treats it.

zhimu (chimo; anemarrhena)　chuan changshan (Sichuan dichroa root)　caoguo (tsaoko fruit)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried; 2 jin each)　liangjiang (lesser galangal; 20 liang)　wumei (ubai; mume fruit; kernels removed; 1 jin)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one *zhan* of water, five slices of fresh ginger and one jujube, decoct down to seven-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm.

## Notes

Changshan yin (Shaoxing-era additions): for malaria arising from external pathogen lodging at Fengfu and raw, cold food damaging the spleen and stomach (varied patterns of cold and heat, shivering, high fever, headache, nausea, thirst, dyspnea, back pain, borborygmus, abdominal pain), unresponsive to other medicines and turning into taxation malaria.
- Dichroa root stops malaria; tsaoko and lesser galangal warm; anemarrhena clears heat; mume and licorice are added.
