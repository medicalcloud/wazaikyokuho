# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 050 論傷暑吐瀉 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On vomiting and diarrhea from summer heat damage. For summer heat strike with vomiting, fever and oppression and confusion, or sudden turmoil with vomiting and diarrhea, give Xiaoshu wan, Wuling san (Goreisan), Guiling wan (Keiryogan), Xiangru san and the like.

## Notes

On vomiting and diarrhea from summer heat: Xiaoshu wan, Wuling san, Guiling wan and Xiangru san for vomiting, fever and oppression in summer heat strike or vomiting and diarrhea of sudden turmoil.
