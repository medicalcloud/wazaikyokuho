# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 008 安息活血丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Anxi huoxue dan (Benzoin Blood-Quickening Elixir)

It is suitable for insufficiency of the *chong* and *ren* with long-standing cold of the lower jiao, gripping pain of the navel and abdomen, irregular menses—heavy and unceasing when they come, or failing to come past their time—or flooding with loss of blood, or unceasing vaginal discharge, a withered yellow complexion, emaciated, haggard flesh, heavy limbs, distension and fullness of the chest and flanks, declining, scant strength and reduced eating; and all deficiency cold of blood and qi.

wuzhuyu (goshuyu; evodia; soaked seven times in hot water, dried over a fire, lightly stir-fried)　anxixiang (benzoin; pounded, ground with good wine, settled and the dregs removed, boiled down to a paste over a gentle fire in a silver vessel)　baiziren (arborvitae seed; stir-fried)　shanzhuyu (sanshuyu; cornus; stones removed)　yanhusuo (corydalis)　taoren (tonin; peach kernel; skin and tips removed, stir-fried with bran until slightly yellow)　huzhang (kosho; bushy knotweed root)　danggui (toki; Chinese angelica)　duzhong (tochu; eucommia; coarse bark removed, chopped, stir-fried)　fuzi (bushi; aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　muxiang (mokko; costus root; 20 liang each)　zelan (takuran; lycopus; leaves)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　aiye (gaiyo; mugwort leaf; lightly stir-fried)　huangqi (ogi; astragalus; rhizome head removed)　mudanpi (moutan bark; 2½ jin each)　roucongrong (cistanche; soaked in wine, dried over a fire)　houpo (koboku; magnolia bark; coarse bark removed, roasted with ginger juice until cooked; 5 jin each)

Make the above into a fine powder; add white flour to the benzoin paste and boil together into a paste, knead and form pills the size of wutong seeds. Take thirty pills each time before meals with warm wine; vinegar decoction is also fine.

## Notes

Anxi huoxue dan: for gripping umbilical pain, irregular menses (heavy or delayed), flooding, discharge, sallow complexion, wasting, heavy limbs, chest and flank distension, weakness and poor appetite from insufficiency of the *chong* and *ren* with long-standing cold of the lower jiao, and for all deficiency cold of blood and qi.
- Made into pills with benzoin paste; corydalis, peach kernel, bushy knotweed, lycopus, moutan and angelica move blood; evodia, aconite, dried ginger, cinnamon and mugwort warm; eucommia, cistanche, cornus, arborvitae seed and astragalus supplement; costus and magnolia bark move qi.
