# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 041 論傷寒大小便秘 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On constipation and urinary blockage in cold damage. For constipation in cold damage, give Maren wan; if the bowels do not move, give Shengong wan or Sanhuang wan; if there is heat, give Sishun qingliang yin; afterward supplement with Shen ling baizhu san or Jiahe san. For urinary blockage, Wuling san, Daochi san and Wulin san may all be given.

## Notes

On constipation and urinary blockage in cold damage: Maren wan, then Shengong wan or Sanhuang wan, Sishun qingliang yin for heat, followed by supplementation with Shen ling baizhu san or Jiahe san; Wuling san, Daochi san and Wulin san for urinary blockage.
