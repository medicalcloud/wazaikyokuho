# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 052 論傷食証候 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On the signs of food damage. When an illness has headache, fever and aversion to cold, or abdominal fullness and vomiting, but no body pain, only lassitude of the limbs, the pattern is not cold damage. This is food damage, lying in the intestines and stomach; Ganying wan, Dusheng wan, Pengjian wan, Jiahe san, Sishi wan and the various medicines for food damage may all be taken. For those with qi deficiency and the elderly, take Wuji san (Goshakusan) and swallow Qing muxiang wan (Seimokkogan), Shunqi san and the like.

## Notes

On food damage: headache, fever, chills, abdominal fullness and vomiting without body pain, with only lassitude of the limbs, is food damage, not cold damage; Ganying wan, Dusheng wan, Pengjian wan, Jiahe san and Sishi wan; Wuji san with Qing muxiang wan or Shunqi san for the deficient and elderly.
