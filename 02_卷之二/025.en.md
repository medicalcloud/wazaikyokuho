# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 025 香蘇散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xiangsu san (Cyperus and Perilla Powder)

It treats seasonal pestilence and cold damage in all four seasons.

xiangfuzi (cyperus; stir-fried until fragrant, hairs removed)　zisuye (perilla leaf; 4 liang each)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried; 1 liang)　chenpi (aged tangerine peel; 2 liang, white pith not removed)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one *zhan* of water, decoct down to seven-tenths, remove the dregs and take it hot, at any time, three doses a day. If made into a fine powder, take only 2 *qian*, stirred with salt [into hot water]. (Once a white-haired old man gave this formula to a rich family, who compounded it and distributed it; when a great epidemic came, all the sick in the city recovered. Afterwards the epidemic ghost questioned the rich man, and he told the truth. The ghost said, "This old man has already taught three people," knocked its head on the ground and withdrew.)

## Notes

Xiangsu san (Shaoxing-era additions): for epidemics and cold damage (colds) in any season. It is still used for colds in those with weak digestion and the elderly, and for qi constraint.
- Cyperus, perilla leaf, tangerine peel and licorice move qi while releasing the exterior. The bracketed passage is an original note giving the legend of a white-haired old man who gave the formula and cured an epidemic.
