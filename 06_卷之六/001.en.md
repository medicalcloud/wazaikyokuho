# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 001 紫雪 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zixue (Purple Snow)

It treats leg qi with toxin spreading through inside and outside, unresolved vexing heat, sores in the mouth, and mania with shouting and running; miasmic epidemics and toxic pestilence; sudden death and warm malaria; the five corpse disorders and five infixations; the various disorders of the heart and abdomen with gripping, stabbing, cutting pain; the outbreak of toxicity from various hot medicines; and sudden jaundice from pathogenic heat; it also resolves gu toxin, ghosts and goblins, and the heat toxin of wild roads. It also treats children's fright epilepsy and the hundred illnesses.

shigao (sekko; gypsum)　huangjin (gold; 100 liang)　hanshuishi (kansuiseki; calcite)　cishi (magnetite)　huashi (kasseki; talc)

The four ingredients above [other than gold] are 3 jin each. Pound and crush them, boil in 1 *hu* of water down to 4 *dou*, remove the dregs and add the following:

xijiao (saikaku; rhinoceros horn shavings)　lingyangjiao (reiyokaku; antelope horn shavings)　qing muxiang (aristolochia root; pounded)　chenxiang (aquilaria; pounded; 5 liang each)　xuanshen (scrophularia; washed, dried over a fire, pounded)　shengma (shoma; cimicifuga; 1 jin each)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; chopped, stir-fried; 8 liang)　dingxiang (clove; 1 liang, pounded)

Put these eight ingredients into the preceding medicinal liquid and boil again to obtain 1 *dou* 5 sheng; remove the dregs and add the following:

poxiao (bokusho; crude mirabilite; refined; 10 jin)　xiaoshi (saltpeter; 4 sheng; if lacking, refined mirabilite will also do; each sheng weighs 7 liang 7½ *qian*)

Put these two ingredients into the preceding liquid, decoct over a gentle fire stirring ceaselessly with a willow spatula until 7 sheng remain, pour into a wooden basin, and after half a day when it is about to set, add the following:

shexiang (jako) dangmenzi (musk pods; 1 liang 2½ *qian*, ground)　zhusha (cinnabar; water-ground; 3 liang)

Put these two ingredients into the preceding medicine, stir evenly and leave to cool for two days.

The medicine forms purple frost-like snow. Take 1 or 2 *qian* each time mixed in cold water; for adults and children adjust the dose as judged at the time; take after meals.

## Notes

Zixue (the first formula of juan 6, "Treating accumulated heat"): for vexing heat with toxin spreading through the body in leg qi, mouth sores and mania; miasmic epidemics, warm malaria, corpse and infixation disorders and stabbing pain of the heart and abdomen; poisoning by hot medicines; sudden jaundice from pathogenic heat; gu toxin; and children's fright epilepsy. Later regarded as a representative "cool opening" formula for high fever, impaired consciousness and convulsions in warm diseases.
- Gypsum, calcite, magnetite and talc are boiled with gold; rhinoceros horn, antelope horn, aristolochia root, aquilaria, scrophularia, cimicifuga, licorice and clove are added and boiled; it is crystallized with mirabilite and saltpeter, and musk and cinnabar are added. It forms purple frost or snow, hence the name.
- Gold is given as "100 liang", the other four minerals 3 jin each; the gold is presumably only boiled.
