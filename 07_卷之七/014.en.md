# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之七 014 洗肝散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xigan san (Liver-Washing Powder)

It treats all cases of wind toxin attacking upward with sudden red eyes, swelling and pain making them hard to open, gritty roughness with discharge and tears, dimness and aversion to light, or the formation of nebulae and membranes.

danggui (toki; Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed)　bohe (mint; stalks removed)　qianghuo (notopterygium; rhizome head removed)　fangfeng (bofu; saposhnikovia; rhizome head removed)　shanzhizi ren (gardenia kernel)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried)　dahuang (daio; rhubarb; roasted in embers)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage; 2 liang each)

Powder the above. Take 2 *qian* each time mixed in cold or boiled water, after meals; effect is seen with daily use.

## Notes

Xigan san (Wu Zhige's additions): for sudden red eyes, swelling and pain, difficulty opening, discharge, tearing, dimness, photophobia and nebulae from wind toxin attacking upward.
- Notopterygium, saposhnikovia, mint and lovage disperse wind; gardenia and rhubarb clear and purge heat; angelica and licorice are added.
