# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 013 竜脳芎犀丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Longnao xiongxi wan (Borneol, Lovage and Rhinoceros Horn Pill)

It disperses wind and transforms phlegm, removes pathogenic heat from the heart and lungs and dispels all wind of the head and face. It treats one-sided and general headache, palpitation with vexation and oppression, a hot face and twitching eyelids, nasal congestion with a clouded head, phlegm-heat cough, and discomfort of the throat and diaphragm.

shigao (sekko; gypsum; finely ground)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage; 4 liang each)　raw longnao (borneol; ground separately)　raw xijiao (saikaku; rhinoceros horn)　shanzhizi (gardenia; skin removed; 1 liang each)　zhusha (cinnabar; ground and water-ground; 4 liang, 1 liang of it for coating)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng; rhizome head removed)　fuling (bukuryo; poria; skin removed, use the white kind)　xixin (saishin; asarum; sprouts removed)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried; 2 liang each)　ejiao (akyo; donkey-hide gelatin; crushed and stir-fried; 1½ liang)　maimendong (bakumondo; ophiopogon; core removed; 3 liang)

Except for what is ground separately and what is added later, pound and sieve everything into a fine powder and form pills with refined honey. Take one or two pills each time, chewed thoroughly, with tea or wine as preferred, after meals.

## Notes

Longnao xiongxi wan: for headache, facial heat, nasal congestion and phlegm-heat cough due to wind-heat and phlegm.
- Sichuan lovage and asarum dispel wind from the head; gypsum, gardenia, rhinoceros horn and borneol clear heat from the heart and lungs; ginseng, poria, ophiopogon and gelatin supplement.
