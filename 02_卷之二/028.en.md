# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 028 太陽丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang dan (Greater Yang Elixir)

It treats headache—all headache from cold damage, contracted wind or accumulated qi, one-sided headache and head-clamping wind. Take one pill each time, chewed, with mint tea. It is also suitable for wind congestion with exuberant phlegm and discomfort of the throat and diaphragm.

naozi (borneol; 2 liang, ground separately)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage)　gancao (kanzo; licorice)　baizhi (byakushi; angelica dahurica; 1 jin each)　shigao (sekko; gypsum; ground separately; 2 jin)　large chuanwu (Sichuan aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed; 1 jin)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills with honey and flour paste, eighteen pills to each liang, coated with vermilion.

## Notes

Taiyang dan (Shaoxing-era additions): for all headache, one-sided or head-clamping, from cold damage, colds or accumulated qi; this is the formula referred to in juan 1, no. 042.
- Lovage and angelica dahurica scatter wind from the head, aconite dispels cold, gypsum restrains heat and borneol opens. The dosage is given within the indications, as in the base text.
