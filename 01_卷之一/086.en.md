# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 086 胡麻散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huma san (Sesame Powder)

It is suitable for wind toxin of the spleen and lungs attacking and surging, with itching of the skin all over the body, or sores and scabies, or dormant papules which, when scratched by hand, spread and weep into sores that do not heal for a long time, or heal and break out again; wandering wind on the face, or a sensation of crawling insects; purple patches, white patches, stubborn numbness and similar wind; or kidney wind attacking and pouring with sores on the legs and knees.

huma (sesame; 12 liang)　jingjie (schizonepeta)　kushen (sophora root; 8 liang each)　heshouwu (fleeceflower root; washed and dried over a fire; 10 liang)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried)　weilingxian (clematis root; 6 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder. Take 2 *qian* each time stirred into mint tea, after meals; it may also be mixed with wine or stirred into honey water. After taking this medicine, bathe frequently; the important thing is to sweat, and then it works at once.

## Notes

Huma san (bureau additions): for generalized itching, sores and scabies, urticaria and eczema-like eruptions from spleen and lung wind toxin, wandering facial wind, purple and white patches, and leg sores from kidney wind.
- Sesame and fleeceflower root moisten the blood, schizonepeta and clematis dispel wind, and sophora root clears damp-heat; bathing to induce sweating follows the dose.
