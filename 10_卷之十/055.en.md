# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 055 小抱竜丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xiao baolong wan (Minor Dragon-Embracing Pill)

It may be given for wind damage and pestilence with fever and stupor, coarse breathing with panting and fullness, phlegm repletion with congested cough, as well as fright wind with tidal convulsions, gu toxin and summerheat strike. It is suitable for robust, replete children.

tianzhuhuang (tabasheer; 1 liang)　xionghuang (realgar; ground and water-ground; 2 *fen*)　chensha (cinnabar; ground separately)　shexiang (musk; ground separately; half a liang each)　tiannanxing (arisaema; fermented in yellow ox bile in the twelfth month and dried in the shade for a hundred days; if unavailable, use raw arisaema with skin and navel removed, chopped and stir-fried until cooked; 4 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder, knead with water in which licorice has been boiled and form pills the size of gleditsia seeds. Take one pill each time dissolved in warm water; for infants within a hundred days, divide into three doses. Boiling the licorice in snow water from the twelfth month to knead the medicine is even better.

## Notes

Xiao baolong wan (Chunyou-era additions): for fever, stupor, panting and phlegm cough in wind damage and pestilence, for convulsions of fright wind, and for gu toxin and summerheat strike; suited to robust children. Later widely known as a formula for children's fright wind and phlegm-heat.
- Bile-processed arisaema and tabasheer remove phlegm-heat; realgar, cinnabar and musk settle; made into pills with licorice water. A simplified form of the Baolong wan of juan 6, no. 010.
