# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷下 034 論小児瘡癬証 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On the pattern of sores and tinea in children. For scabies sores over the whole body and damp infant eczema, give Qingxin yin; for head sores and similar disorders, Xiaodu yin and Huadu dan are in general use; then wash with scallion-salt decoction, wipe dry and sprinkle Wubi san on.

## Notes

On sores and tinea in children: Qingxin yin for generalized scabies and damp infant eczema; Xiaodu yin and Huadu dan (Wufu huadu dan) for head sores, followed by washing with scallion-salt decoction and dusting with Wubi san.
