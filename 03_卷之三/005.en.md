# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 005 理中丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Lizhong wan (Richugan; Center-Regulating Pill)

It regulates disharmony of the middle jiao and chronic cold of the spleen and stomach, with deficiency stuffiness below the heart, abdominal pain, counterflow fullness of the chest and flanks, choking obstruction, vomiting of cold phlegm, inability to get food down, belching of sourness and acid swallowing, a bitter mouth with loss of taste, lassitude with a liking for lying down, and complete lack of desire for food. It also treats cold damage and seasonal qi with interior cold and exterior heat, vomiting and diarrhea of sudden turmoil, wringing pain of the heart and abdomen, disharmony of the hands and feet, a hot body without thirst, and borborygmus with spontaneous diarrhea and undigested grain.

baizhu (byakujutsu; white atractylodes)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; scorched; 20 liang each)

Powder the above and form pills with refined honey, ten pills to each liang. Take one pill each time before meals dissolved in boiling water; it may also be chewed, or made into pills the size of wutong seeds. It may also be taken by those just recovered from a major illness who sleep excessively, and by those deficient within soon after childbirth. Taken regularly it warms the spleen and stomach, disperses phlegm and drives out retained fluid, smooths the sanjiao, improves eating and wards off pathogenic qi of wind, cold, damp and chill.

## Notes

Lizhong wan: the pill form of Renshen tang (Ninjinto) (Lizhong tang [Richuto]) for stuffiness, abdominal pain, vomiting and poor appetite from spleen-stomach deficiency cold, for Taiyin cold damage and vomiting and diarrhea of sudden turmoil (*Shanghan lun* clause 386), and for deficiency after illness or childbirth.
- Ginseng, white atractylodes, dried ginger and licorice; still used today as Renshen tang for cold digestion and diarrhea. The later Lizhong tang (juan 3, no. 011) is the decoction of the same ingredients.
