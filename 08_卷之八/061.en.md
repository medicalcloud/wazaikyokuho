# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 061 升麻和気飲 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shengma (shoma) heqi yin (Cimicifuga Qi-Harmonizing Beverage)

It treats scabies sores breaking out on the limbs, irregular pain and itching of the buttocks and thighs, in severe cases aversion to cold with fever, attacking, stabbing pain and spreading puffy swelling. It also treats leprous wind entering the zang organs with damp itching below the genitals, tinnitus and eye pain.

ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger)　shu zhiqiao (processed bitter orange; half a *qian* each)　gange (dried pueraria)　shu cangzhu (processed black atractylodes)　jiegeng (kikyo; platycodon)　shengma (cimicifuga; 1 liang each)　danggui (toki; Chinese angelica)　shu banxia (hange; processed pinellia)　fuling (bukuryo; poria)　baizhi (byakushi; angelica dahurica; 2 *qian* each)　chenpi (chinpi; aged tangerine peel)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; 1½ liang each)　shaoyao (shakuyaku; peony root; 7½ *qian*)　dahuang (daio; rhubarb; steamed; half a liang)

Cut the above into a powder. For each dose take four large *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water, three slices of ginger and fifteen pieces of rush pith, decoct down to seven-tenths, remove the dregs and take it before meals.

## Notes

Shengma heqi yin (bureau additions): for scabies sores on the limbs, pain and itching of the buttocks and thighs, chills, fever, stabbing pain and swelling, and for damp genital itching, tinnitus and eye pain when leprous wind enters the zang organs.
- Cimicifuga, pueraria and angelica dahurica disperse; black atractylodes, pinellia, poria and tangerine peel remove damp; rhubarb and bitter orange purge; angelica, peony, platycodon, dried ginger and licorice are added.
