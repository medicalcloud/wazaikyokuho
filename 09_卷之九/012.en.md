# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 012 禹餘糧丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yuyuliang wan (Limonite Pill)

It treats women with long-standing deficiency from vaginal discharge, damaged and ruined uterine network vessels, irregular menses gradually becoming flooding and spotting, deficient, exhausted qi and blood, a yellow face and thin body, tension of the navel and abdomen, painful, heavy lower back and knees, vexing pain of the limbs, palpitation and dizziness, alternating cold and heat of the hands and feet, and no desire for food.

sangjisheng (mulberry mistletoe)　baiye (arborvitae leaf; lightly stir-fried)　danggui (Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed, lightly stir-fried)　houpo (magnolia bark; coarse bark removed, smeared with ginger juice and roasted)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried)　baizhu (white atractylodes)　biejia (turtle shell; soaked in vinegar, rim removed, roasted yellow)　fuzi (aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed; 1 liang each)　yuyuliang (limonite; burned, quenched in vinegar seven times, water-ground)　bai shizhi (white halloysite; 2 liang each)　gouji (cibotium; hairs removed)　bai shaoyao (white peony root; 3 *fen* each)　wuzhuyu (evodia; washed seven times in hot water, lightly stir-fried; half a liang)

Make the above into a fine powder, knead with refined honey and form pills the size of wutong seeds. Take thirty pills each time with warm wine or rice water, on an empty stomach before meals.

## Notes

Yuyuliang wan: for deficiency from long-standing discharge, damaged uterine vessels, irregular menses progressing to flooding, and sallowness, wasting, umbilical tension, back and knee pain, limb pain, palpitation, dizziness, alternating cold and heat of the extremities and poor appetite from exhausted qi and blood.
- Limonite, white halloysite and arborvitae leaf stop bleeding and discharge; aconite, dried ginger and evodia warm; mistletoe and cibotium supplement the kidneys; angelica, white peony, atractylodes, turtle shell and magnolia bark are added.
