# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 015 薑附湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jiangfu tang (Ginger and Aconite Decoction)

It is suitable for cold damage that has already been purged and then made to sweat, so that both interior and exterior are deficient while the pathogenic qi has not resolved; no exterior pattern is seen, there is no great heat in the body, there is restlessness with inability to sleep by day but quiet at night, no vomiting and no thirst, and a deep, feeble pulse. It also treats sudden strike by wind-cold, long-accumulated phlegm-water, cold pain of the heart and abdomen, cramps in sudden turmoil (huoluan), and all deficiency cold.

ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; 1 liang)　fuzi (bushi; aconite; raw, skin and navel removed, finely sliced; 1 piece)

Combine the above evenly. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water, decoct down to one *zhan*, remove the dregs and take it warm, before meals.

## Notes

Jiangfu tang: for deficiency of interior and exterior after purging and sweating, with daytime vexation and nighttime quiet and a deep, feeble pulse (the pattern of Ganjiang fuzi tang, *Shanghan lun* clause 61), and for wind-cold strike, phlegm-water, cold pain of the heart and abdomen, cramps in sudden turmoil and all deficiency cold.
- Two ingredients, dried ginger and raw aconite, corresponding to Ganjiang fuzi tang of the *Shanghan lun*.
