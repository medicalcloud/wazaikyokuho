# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之四 005 金珠化痰丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jinzhu huatan wan (Gold and Pearl Phlegm-Transforming Pill)

It treats phlegm-heat, calms the spirit and will, and removes headache and dizziness, palpitation and abstraction, vexation and oppression of the chest and diaphragm, thick sticky mucus and spittle, phlegm-excess cough and discomfort of the throat.

zaojia ren (soap-pod seeds; stir-fried)　tianzhuhuang (tabasheer)　baifan (alum; clear, bright pieces, put in a stone or iron vessel and simmered until the liquid is gone, cooled and ground)　qianbaishuang (lead acetate frost; finely ground), 1 liang each　banxia (hange; pinellia; washed seven times in hot water, pounded fine together with 2 liang of fresh ginger washed and scraped of its skin, made into cakes and roasted slightly yellow; 4 liang)　raw white longnao (borneol; finely ground; half a liang)　chensha (cinnabar; ground and water-ground; 2 liang)　gold leaf (for coating; 20 leaves)

Powder the pinellia and soap-pod seeds, mix and grind evenly with the other medicines, and form pills the size of wutong seeds with flour paste boiled in ginger juice. Take ten to fifteen pills each time with ginger decoction, after meals and at bedtime.

## Notes

Jinzhu huatan wan: for headache, dizziness, palpitation, abstraction, chest oppression, sticky phlegm, cough and throat discomfort from phlegm-heat.
- Pinellia, soap-pod seed, alum and tabasheer transform phlegm; lead frost, cinnabar, borneol and gold leaf calm the spirit and clear heat.
