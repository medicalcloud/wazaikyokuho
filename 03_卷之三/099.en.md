# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 099 人参煮散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Renshen (ninjin) zhusan (Ginseng Boiled Powder)

It treats disharmony of the spleen and stomach with stagnant qi in the middle duct, distending pain of the heart and abdomen, no desire for food, and chronic cold and prolonged fluid retention accumulating without dispersing; or excessive cold drinking injuring the spleen qi within, with vomiting of phlegm and counterflow, alternating fever and chills, or occasional sweating. It also treats cold-damp of the stomach and intestines with pouring diarrhea, water and grain not separated, thunderous rumbling in the abdomen and deficiency fullness of the flanks and ribs. It also treats cold damage with exuberant yin and counterflow cold of the limbs.

renshen (ginseng; 4 liang)　qingpi (green tangerine peel; white removed; 12 liang)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried; 10 liang)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried; 6 liang)　sanleng (sparganium; roasted, crushed; 12 liang)　shaoyao (shakuyaku; peony root; 1 jin)　dingpi (clove bark; 6 liang)　fuling (bukuryo; poria; skin removed)　cangzhu (black atractylodes; skin removed; half a jin each)

Powder the above. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one *zhan* of water, five slices of fresh ginger and three jujubes, decoct together down to seven-tenths and take it warm before meals on an empty stomach.

## Notes

Renshen zhusan (bureau additions): for abdominal pain and poor appetite from spleen-stomach disharmony and middle-duct qi stagnation, chronic cold and prolonged fluid retention, vomiting and chills and fever after excessive cold drinking, cold-damp diarrhea with borborygmus, and cold limbs in yin-exuberant cold damage.
- Ginseng, poria and licorice supplement; dried ginger and clove bark warm; black atractylodes removes damp; green tangerine peel and sparganium break up qi and accumulations; a large amount of peony is used.
