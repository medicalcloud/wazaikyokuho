# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 009 牛黄涼膈丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Niuhuang liangge wan (Gooryokakugan; Bovine Bezoar Diaphragm-Cooling Pill)

It treats wind congestion with phlegm excess pent up, accumulated and not dispersing, headache and red face, a vexed heart with tidal fever and agitation, congestion of phlegm and drool, blockage of the throat and diaphragm, an abstracted spirit, unquiet sleep, dry mouth with much thirst, parched lips and sore throat, red swelling of the jaw and cheeks, and sores of the mouth and tongue.

niuhuang (goo; bovine bezoar; ground; 1 liang 1 *fen*)　nanxing (arisaema; processed with ox bile; 7½ liang)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; scorched; 10 liang)　chaishiying (fluorite [?]; ground and water-ground)　shexiang (jako; musk; ground)　longnao (borneol; ground; 5 liang each)　yaxiao (refined mirabilite; dehydrated, finely ground)　hanshuishi fen (calcite powder; calcined)　shigao (sekko; gypsum; finely ground; 20 liang each)

Powder the above and form pills with refined honey, thirty to each liang. Take one pill each time, chewed with warm mint and ginseng decoction, after meals. Children regularly take half a pill; for acute fright, one pill as treatment; in both cases dissolved in mint water.

## Notes

Niuhuang liangge wan: for headache, red face, agitation, tidal fever, phlegm congestion, abstraction, insomnia, thirst, sore throat, swollen cheeks and mouth sores from wind congestion with phlegm excess, and for acute fright in children.
- Bovine bezoar, musk and borneol open the orifices; bile-processed arisaema removes phlegm; gypsum, calcite and refined mirabilite clear heat; fluorite and licorice are added.
- The base text writes "chaishiying", probably an error for zishiying (murasakisekiei; fluorite); it has not been emended.
