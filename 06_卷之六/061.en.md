# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 061 戊己丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wuji wan (Bokigan; Wu-Ji [Earth] Pill)

It treats the spleen having received damp qi, with unceasing diarrhea, slow digestion of rice and grain, and stabbing pain of the navel and abdomen. It can also treat diarrhea from gan qi in children.

huanglian (oren; coptis; fibrous roots removed)　wuzhuyu (goshuyu; evodia; stalks removed, stir-fried)　bai shaoyao (shakuyaku; white peony root; 5 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with flour paste. Take twenty pills each time with strongly decocted rice water, on an empty stomach, three times a day.

## Notes

Wuji wan (Wu Zhige's additions): for unceasing diarrhea, slow digestion and stabbing umbilical pain when the spleen has received damp, and for diarrhea from gan qi in children.
- Three ingredients: coptis, evodia and white peony (Zuojin wan plus peony). Wu and ji are the earth stems of the five phases, referring to the spleen and stomach.
