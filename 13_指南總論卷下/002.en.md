# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷下 002 論気虚腫満 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On swelling and fullness from qi deficiency. Swelling and fullness from qi deficiency arise from stagnation of spleen qi, the spleen meridian receiving damp and qi not flowing, causing puffy swelling of the head and face, swelling and fullness of the limbs, and a belly distended like a drum. For dyspnea upward with hurried breathing, give Fuling san, Wuling san (Goreisan), Sanhe san (Sanwasan), Fenqi zisu yin, Muxiang (mokko) fenqi wan, Yu shanren jiangqi tang, Xiao jiangqi tang, Suzi jiangqi tang (Soshikokito), Caopi san or Jiahe san. For severe dyspnea, give Runjin san or Kuanfei san. For constipation, give Sanhe san; for urinary blockage, Wuling san.

## Notes

On swelling from qi deficiency (stagnant spleen qi and damp: facial puffiness, limb swelling, drum-like abdomen, dyspnea): Fuling san, Wuling san, Sanhe san, Fenqi zisu yin, Muxiang fenqi wan, Suzi jiangqi tang and others; Runjin san and Kuanfei san for dyspnea; Sanhe san for constipation; Wuling san for urinary blockage.
