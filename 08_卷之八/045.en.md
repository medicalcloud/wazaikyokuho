# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 045 急風散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jifeng san (Acute Wind Powder)

It treats the various sores, long-standing or recent, with wind strike through a wound (tetanus), stiff neck and rigid back, the lower back arched backward, clenched jaw and inability to speak, convulsions of the hands and feet, eyes staring upward and a bubbling sound in the throat.

dansha (cinnabar; 1 liang)　cao wutou (wild aconite; 3 liang, half used raw, half burned with nature preserved and quenched in rice vinegar to cool)　shexiang (musk; ground)　sheng wudou (raw black soybean; powdered together with the wild aconite; 1 *fen* each)

Make the above into a fine powder and mix evenly. For tetanus, mix half a *qian* in a small *zhan* of wine and take it; divine effect. To extract an arrowhead, first take half a *qian* mixed in a *zhan* of wine, then apply the medicine on the arrow wound.

## Notes

Jifeng san (Wu Zhige's additions): for tetanus from wounds (stiff neck and back, opisthotonus, clenched jaw, convulsions, upward gaze, rattling in the throat); also taken and applied externally to extract arrowheads.
- Cinnabar, wild aconite (raw and burned), musk and black soybean.
