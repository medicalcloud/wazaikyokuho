# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之四 028 破飲丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Poyin wan (Rheum-Breaking Pill)

It treats all prolonged fluid retention that will not disperse, with occasional vomiting of phlegm and froth, dizziness with a tendency to fall, and discomfort of the diaphragm and stomach duct.

xuanfuhua (senpukuka; inula flower; 8 liang)　baizhu (byakujutsu; white atractylodes; 1 jin 1 liang)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried; 6 liang each)　chi fuling (bukuryo; red poria; skin removed; 7 liang)　zhishi (kijitsu; unripe bitter orange; stir-fried with bran; 2 liang)

Powder the above and form pills the size of wutong seeds with flour paste. Take fifty pills each time with boiled water.

## Notes

Poyin wan (Chunyou-era additions): for vomiting of frothy phlegm, dizziness and epigastric discomfort from prolonged fluid retention.
- Atractylodes and red poria drain water; cinnamon and dried ginger warm; inula and unripe bitter orange send phlegm and qi down.
