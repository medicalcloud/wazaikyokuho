# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之七 012 春雪膏 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Chunxue gao (Spring Snow Paste)

It treats all cases of insufficiency of the liver channel with wind-heat received internally attacking the eyes upward, dim, dark, itchy and painful eyes, gritty roughness making them hard to open, dimness, dizziness, red swelling, fear of the sun and aversion to light, inability to see far, tearing against the wind and frequent sight of black flowers.

naozi (borneol; ground; 2½ *qian*)　ruiren (prinsepia kernel; skins and shells removed, pressed to remove the oil; 2 liang)

Knead the borneol and prinsepia kernel together with 6 *qian* by weight of raw honey. Each time, with a copper chopstick or the leg of a gold or silver hairpin, apply a little to the inner and outer canthi from time to time. For redness and festering extending around the orbits, spread the medicine on oiled paper and apply it.

## Notes

Chunxue gao (from here the layer of Chunyou-era additions): for dim vision, itching, pain, difficulty opening the eyes, red swelling, photophobia, poor distance vision, tearing and floaters from an insufficient liver channel with wind-heat, and for redness and festering around the orbits.
- An eye paste of borneol and prinsepia kernel kneaded with honey, applied to the canthi; for festering, spread on oiled paper and applied.
