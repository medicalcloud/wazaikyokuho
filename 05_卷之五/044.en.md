# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 044 小菟糸子丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xiao tusizi wan (Shotoshishigan; Minor Dodder Seed Pill)

It is suitable for deficient and damaged kidney qi with the five taxations and seven damages, cramping of the lower abdomen, aching limbs, a dark sallow complexion, dry lips and mouth, dim eyes and tinnitus, palpitation and shortness of breath, fright and fear in dreams at night, a weary spirit, irregular joy and anger, sorrow and joylessness, tasteless food, lack of strength in movement, distension and fullness of the heart and abdomen, withered, slack legs and knees, slippery frequent urination, impotence, damp itching of the inner thighs, rough painful urination, blood in the urine and occasional dribbling. Taken for a long time it fills the bone marrow, rejoins severed injuries, supplements the five zang organs, removes the myriad illnesses, sharpens sight and hearing, improves the complexion, lightens the body and prolongs life.

shilian rou (lotus fruit flesh; 2 liang)　tusizi (toshishi; dodder seed; soaked in wine and ground; 5 liang)　bai fuling (bukuryo; white poria; dried over a fire; 1 liang)　shanyao (Chinese yam; 2 liang, of which 7½ *qian* is made into paste)

Make the above into a fine powder, knead with the Chinese-yam paste and form pills the size of wutong seeds. Take fifty pills each time with warm wine or salt water, on an empty stomach. If the legs and knees are weak, take them with chaenomeles decoction, and again before the evening meal.

## Notes

Xiao tusizi wan (Wu Zhige's additions): the same indications as the earlier Tusizi wan (Toshishigan) (juan 5, no. 002), simplified to four ingredients (dodder seed, lotus fruit flesh, poria, Chinese yam).
