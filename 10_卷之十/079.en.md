# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 079 益黄散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yihuang san (Yellow-Augmenting Powder)

It treats weak, deficient spleen and stomach in children with abdominal pain and diarrhoea, no desire for milk or food, unceasing vomiting, fatigue and listlessness of spirit, swelling distension of the heart and flanks, a green-yellow complexion, and drowsiness without waking.

dingxiang (clove; 4 *qian*, not exposed to fire)　chenpi (aged tangerine peel; white pith removed; 2 liang)　gancao (licorice; scorched)　hezi (chebule; blast-fried, stone removed)　qingpi (green tangerine peel; white removed; 1 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder. For each dose take one large *qian* with seven-tenths of a *zhan* of water, decoct down to five or six tenths and give it before meals, adjusted for the child's size. This medicine has the most divine effect, beyond description.

## Notes

Yihuang san (Wu Zhige's additions): for abdominal pain, diarrhoea, poor feeding, unceasing vomiting, fatigue, listlessness, chest and flank distension, poor complexion and drowsiness from weak spleen and stomach in children.
- Tangerine peel, green tangerine peel, clove, chebule and licorice (the Yihuang san of Qian Yi's *Xiaoer yaozheng zhijue*).
