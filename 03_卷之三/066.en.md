# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 066 白朮六一湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Baizhu liuyi tang (White Atractylodes Six-to-One Decoction)

It treats disharmony of the spleen and stomach with glomus and oppression of the heart and abdomen, distension of the flanks and ribs, a bitter mouth without taste, retching and nausea, no desire for food, a withered yellow complexion, intestinal deficiency with spontaneous diarrhoea, an emaciated, weak body, and diaphragm qi and stomach reflux.

baizhu (white atractylodes; rhizome head removed; 6 liang)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried; 1 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one *zhan* of water, decoct down to eight-tenths and take it on an empty stomach before meals; it may also be stirred into boiling water. Taken regularly it nurtures the spirit, warms the stomach, drives out damp and disperses phlegm. It is suitable in every season.

## Notes

Baizhu liuyi tang (Baoqing-era additions): for glomus, flank distension, bitter mouth, nausea, poor appetite, sallow complexion, diarrhoea and wasting from spleen-stomach disharmony, and for diaphragm qi and stomach reflux.
- Two ingredients, white atractylodes and licorice in the ratio 6:1 (hence the name).
