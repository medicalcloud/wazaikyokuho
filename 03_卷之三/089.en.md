# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 089 木香分気丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Muxiang fenqi wan (Costus Qi-Separating Pill)

The patterns it treats are the same as for the earlier Muxiang fenqi wan.

muxiang (costus root)　dingxiangpi (clove bark)　xiangfuzi (cyperus; stir-fried, hairs removed)　peng'ezhu (zedoary; roasted)　suoshaner (amomum)　gancao (licorice; 4 liang each)　huoxiang leaves (agastache)　chuan jianghuang (Sichuan turmeric)　tanxiang (sandalwood)　gansong (nardostachys; washed; 1 liang each)

Dry these ten ingredients in the sun without exposing them to fire, pound and sieve them into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with thin paste. Take twenty to thirty pills each time, swallowed with ginger and tangerine-peel decoction, at any time. Best for those with weak spleen and stomach. Taken regularly it loosens the middle, smooths qi and improves eating.

## Notes

Muxiang fenqi wan (bureau additions): the same indications as the earlier Muxiang fenqi wan (juan 3, no. 062), but a different ten-ingredient formula adding clove bark, amomum, agastache, turmeric and sandalwood.
