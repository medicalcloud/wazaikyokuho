# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之七 018 明睛地黄丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Mingjing dihuang wan (Eye-Brightening Rehmannia Pill)

It treats all of the following in men and women: accumulated heat of the liver zang organ, liver deficiency with dim eyes, membranes entering the water wheel (pupil), leaking eye with discharge and tears, sight of black flowers, unclear vision, a turbid eye with cold tears, and nebulae and membranes covering the eye; deficient, exhausted kidneys with the liver having received deficiency heat; and red eyes from sudden heat, wind-toxin and qi eyes, of many years' or recent standing. It also treats dry and damp leg qi, middle wasting and wasting-thirst, and the various wind-qi disorders arising from deficient, failing kidney qi. Simply taking this can supplement the liver, augment the kidneys, drive out wind and brighten the eyes; its effects cannot be fully described.

sheng gan dihuang (kanjio; dried raw rehmannia; dried over a fire, washed)　shu gan dihuang (prepared rehmannia; washed, dried over a fire; 1 jin each)　niuxi (goshitsu; achyranthes; rhizome head removed, soaked in wine; 3 liang)　shihu (sekkoku; dendrobium; shoots removed)　zhiqiao (bitter orange; pulp removed, stir-fried with bran)　fangfeng (bofu; saposhnikovia; rhizome head and forks removed; 4 liang each)　xingren (kyonin; apricot kernel; skin and tips removed, stir-fried yellow with bran, finely ground, oil removed; 2 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with refined honey. Take thirty pills each time on an empty stomach before meals, swallowed with warm wine; rice water or salt water is also fine. Avoid all wind-stirring and toxic things.

## Notes

Mingjing dihuang wan (bureau additions): for dim vision from accumulated liver heat or liver deficiency, membranes over the pupil, leaking eye, discharge, tearing, floaters, poor sight, a turbid eye, cold tears and nebulae; for kidney deficiency with deficiency heat in the liver; for red eyes from heat and wind-toxin and qi eyes, old or new; and for dry and damp leg qi, wasting-thirst and wind-qi disorders from deficient kidney qi.
- Mainly raw and prepared rehmannia; achyranthes and dendrobium supplement the liver and kidneys; saposhnikovia dispels wind; bitter orange and apricot kernel are added.
