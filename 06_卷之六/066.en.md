# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 066 神応黒玉丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shenying hei yu dan (Divinely Responsive Black Jade Elixir)

It treats men and women with intestinal wind, piles and fistulas, new or long-standing, bedridden with unbearable pain; taking this medicine shows effect within three or four doses. At the onset of this illness there is itching or pain, and many hard nodes form around the anus: these are piles; if they burst, it is a fistula; if there is only bleeding, it is [intestinal] wind. All arise from excess in five matters: wine, sex, temper, wind and food. People mostly treat it externally by applying ointments, but the illness lies in the intestines, which have their own worms, and if the root is not removed the illness will not be eliminated. This medicine truly has effect.

ciweipi (hedgehog skin; chopped; 16 liang)　zhu xuanti (pig's dewclaws; 100)　niujiaosai (ox horn core; chopped; 12 liang)　huaijiao (sophora fruit; 6 liang)　leiwan (omphalia)　zhima (sesame; 4 liang each)　luanfa (human hair; washed clean in gleditsia water, dried over a fire)　baizong (old palm fibre; chopped; 8 liang each)　kulian gen (chinaberry root; 5 liang)

Chop and crush the above, burn them in an earthenware jar with nature preserved, mill into a fine powder, add 2 liang of frankincense and 8 *qian* of musk, grind evenly and form pills the size of wutong seeds with flour paste made with wine. Take eight pills each time, first chewing one walnut thoroughly, then swallowing them with warm wine, on an empty stomach and before the evening meal, two doses a day; if the illness is severe, three a day. Strictly avoid other medicines. Within two or three days the root is removed for good.

## Notes

Shenying hei yu dan (Wu Zhige's additions): for severe pain from intestinal wind, piles and fistulas, new or old. It distinguishes piles (nodes around the anus), fistulas (burst nodes) and intestinal wind (bleeding only), attributes them to excess of wine, sex, temper, wind and food, and says that since there are worms in the intestines external treatment alone cannot remove the root.
- Hedgehog skin, pig's dewclaws, ox horn core, human hair, old palm fibre and others are burned black with nature preserved; sophora fruit, omphalia, chinaberry root and sesame are added, with frankincense and musk.
