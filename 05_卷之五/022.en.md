# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 022 樸附丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Pufu wan (Magnolia Bark and Aconite Pill)

It treats weak original qi of the spleen with slow digestion, frequent injury whenever one eats, abdominal pain and borborygmus, slippery diarrhoea day and night without limit, deficient and damaged stomach qi, food without relish, and retching of foul drool. This medicine is warm in nature; it also treats stomach reflux and nausea, and is most suitable for those long suffering from spleen diarrhoea and cold diarrhoea.

houpo (magnolia bark; coarse bark removed, processed with ginger juice)　fuzi (aconite; blast-fried, skin removed; 1 jin each)　shenqu (medicated leaven; stir-fried; 8 liang)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried; 3 jin)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with flour paste boiled in wine. Take thirty pills each time on an empty stomach before meals, with rice gruel or salt water.

## Notes

Pufu wan (Baoqing-era additions): for indigestion, abdominal pain, borborygmus, unceasing diarrhoea, poor appetite and nausea from weak spleen qi, and for stomach reflux and chronic spleen and cold diarrhoea.
- Magnolia bark, aconite and dried ginger warm the spleen and dry damp; medicated leaven digests food.
