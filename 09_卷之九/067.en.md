# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 067 艾煎丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Aijian wan (Mugwort Syrup Pill)

It treats flooding damage with dribbling bleeding and fullness and pain of the small intestine (lower abdomen).

renshen (ninjin; ginseng)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage)　changpu (shobu; acorus; nodes, stir-fried with honey; 1 liang each)　shu'ai (processed mugwort; kneaded into cakes with glutinous rice water, dried over a fire; 4 liang)　shizhuyu (ailanthus prickly-ash fruit; washed in hot water)　danggui (toki) (Chinese angelica; 7½ *qian* each)　bai shaoyao (shakuyaku; white peony root)　shu gan dihuang (kanjio; prepared rehmannia; 1½ liang each)

Powder the above and form pills the size of wutong seeds with paste boiled in wine. Take fifty pills each time with wine or rice water as preferred. Taken regularly it supplements the ying-nutrient and wei-defense qi and secures the meridians.

## Notes

Aijian wan (bureau additions): for dribbling bleeding after flooding with lower abdominal fullness and pain.
- Siwu tang (Shimotsuto) (prepared rehmannia, angelica, lovage, white peony) and ginseng supplement; mugwort and ailanthus prickly-ash warm and stop bleeding; acorus is added.
