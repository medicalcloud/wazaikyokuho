# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之四 014 鍾乳補肺湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zhongru bufei tang (Stalactite Lung-Supplementing Decoction)

It treats insufficient lung qi with cough and rising qi, chest fullness pressing upward, obstruction of the throat, shortness of breath with dyspnea exhaustion, continuous spitting, cold rising from the back, a mouth as if holding frost and snow, speaking without voice, and in severe cases spitting of fishy-smelling blood, dry retching with heart vexation, hearing the sound of wind and rain in the ears, withered skin and hair and a white complexion.

zhongru (stalactite; crushed to the size of rice grains)　sangbaipi (mulberry root bark)　maimendong (bakumondo; ophiopogon; core removed; 3 liang each)　baishiying (hakusekiei; white quartz; crushed to the size of rice grains)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng; rhizome head removed)　wuweizi (gomishi; schisandra; picked)　kuandonghua (kantoka; coltsfoot flower; stalks removed)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　ziwan (aster; washed free of earth; 2 liang each)

Except for the white quartz and stalactite, make the above together into a coarse powder and mix evenly with the quartz and stalactite. For each dose take 4 *qian* with two *zhan* of water, five slices of fresh ginger, one large jujube broken open and thirty-odd grains of non-glutinous rice, decoct together down to one *zhan*, strain through floss to remove the dregs and take it warm, after meals.

## Notes

Zhongru bufei tang: for cough, rising qi, pressure in the chest, throat obstruction, shortness of breath, much spitting, cold back, cold mouth, loss of voice, blood-streaked sputum, tinnitus, withered skin and white complexion from insufficient lung qi.
- Stalactite and white quartz warm and supplement the lungs; ginseng, schisandra and ophiopogon supplement qi and yin; coltsfoot, aster and mulberry bark stop cough; cinnamon warms. In the lineage of the Bufei tang of the *Qianjin fang*.
