# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之四 039 茯苓半夏湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Fuling banxia tang (Poria and Pinellia Decoction)

It is suitable for lodged phlegm and retained rheum with fullness and oppression of the chest and diaphragm, cough and vomiting, shortness of breath and nausea, so that food cannot be kept down.

fuling (poria; skin removed; 3 liang)　banxia (pinellia; soaked seven times in hot water; 5 liang)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take four large *qian* with one large *zhan* of water and seven slices of fresh ginger, decoct down to seven-tenths, remove the dregs and take it on an empty stomach.

## Notes

Fuling banxia tang (bureau additions): for chest oppression, cough, vomiting, shortness of breath, nausea and inability to eat from lodged phlegm and retained rheum.
- Poria and pinellia with fresh ginger (corresponding to Xiao banxia jia fuling tang of the *Jingui yaolüe*).
