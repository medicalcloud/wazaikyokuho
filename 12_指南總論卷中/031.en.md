# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 031 論傷寒潮熱 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On tidal fever in cold damage. In cold damage from the fifth to seventh day up to more than ten days, when the patient is somewhat clear-headed in the early morning, but around the *shen* and *you* hours (3–7 p.m.) has fever without aversion to cold, slight sweating, constipation for two or three days, or delirious speech with thirst, give three to five doses of Xiao chaihu tang, or five to seven in severe cases; with a slight bowel movement the fever will surely subside. For thirst, give Wuling san. If vexation and agitation persist and the fever has not subsided, add Qibao xixin san or give Qinjiao biejia san to regulate.

## Notes

On tidal fever in cold damage: for afternoon fever (*shen–you* hours) without chills, slight sweating, constipation, delirium and thirst after five to ten or more days, several doses of Xiao chaihu tang to produce a slight purge; Wuling san for thirst; Qibao xixin san or Qinjiao biejia san if agitation and fever persist.
