# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 083 失笑散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shixiao san (Shisshosan; Sudden Smile Powder)

It treats postpartum pain of the heart and abdomen near death, where a hundred medicines have failed; taking this brings sudden recovery.

puhuang (hoo; typha pollen; stir-fried until fragrant)　wulingzhi (flying squirrel droppings; ground with wine, rinsed free of sand and earth; equal parts, powdered)

First mix 2 *qian* in strong vinegar and boil down to a paste, add one *zhan* of water, decoct down to seven-tenths and take it hot before meals.

## Notes

Shixiao san (bureau additions): for near-fatal postpartum pain of the heart and abdomen unresponsive to other medicines. Still known today as a representative formula for abdominal pain, dysmenorrhoea and postpartum pain from static blood.
- Two ingredients, typha pollen and flying squirrel droppings, boiled to a paste in vinegar and then decocted in water.
