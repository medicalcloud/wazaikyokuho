# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 080 来復丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Laifu dan (Return Elixir)

(A formula of Mister Du of Bajiao in Tieweng city; also called Zhengyi dan, Orthodox Unity Elixir.) This medicine pairs the two qi and evenly regulates yin and yang, seizing the harmonizing qi of heaven and earth; it is a formula of water and fire already in balance, which can be cold or hot, gentle or urgent. It is good at treating failure of the ying-nutrient and wei-defense qi to interact and nourish each other, failure of the heart and kidneys to rise and fall, excess above and deficiency below, qi blockage and phlegm syncope, cold pain of the heart and abdomen, and deficiency slippage of the zang-fu organs. Regardless of men or women, old or young, even in critical patterns, so long as stomach qi remains, none fail to be set at ease. It supplements damage and supports deficiency, rescues yin and assists yang, and its effect is outstanding.

xiaoshi (saltpetre; 1 liang, made into a fine powder together with the sulfur, put into a fixed pot and slowly stir-fried over a gentle fire, stirring ceaselessly with a willow spatula so that the yin and yang qi enter each other; the fire must not be too strong, lest it damage the force of the medicine; then ground extremely fine again: this is called the "two qi powder")　taiyin xuanjingshi (selenite; ground and water-ground)　shipshang liuhuang (imported sulfur; use clear pieces free of sand and stones; 1 liang each)　wulingzhi (flying squirrel droppings; choose those from Mount Wutai, settled in water to remove sand and stones, sun-dried)　qingpi (green tangerine peel; white removed)　chenpi (chinpi; aged tangerine peel; white pith removed; 2 liang each)

Make the flying squirrel droppings and the two tangerine peels into a fine powder, then add the selenite powder and the "two qi powder" above, mix evenly and form pills the size of peas with paste made from good drip vinegar. Take thirty pills each time on an empty stomach, swallowed with rice gruel water; in severe cases fifty; for children three to five; for newborns one. For children's chronic fright wind, or unceasing vomiting and diarrhea turning into deficiency-wind convulsions—not wind, but because stomach qi is about to expire—grind five pills and give them with rice water. For old people stupefied by latent summer heat, give them with perilla decoction. For women's postpartum blood counterflow surging upward with fainting, unceasing lochia, and red and white vaginal discharge, give them in all cases with vinegar decoction. Taken regularly it harmonizes yin and yang, augments essence and spirit, disperses yin damp of the lower back and kidneys and stops cold pain of the abdomen and ribs, with immediate divine effect. For all illnesses where yin and yang patterns cannot be distinguished, it is suitable. Its numinous wonders cannot be fully recorded.

## Notes

Laifu dan (Zhengyi dan; Wu Zhige's additions): for qi blockage, phlegm syncope, cold pain and diarrhea when the heart and kidneys fail to rise and fall with excess above and deficiency below; said to rescue even critical cases as long as stomach qi remains; also for children's chronic fright wind, latent summer heat in the elderly, and postpartum blood counterflow, lochia and discharge.
- A "two qi powder" of saltpetre and sulfur stir-fried together (cold saltpetre and hot sulfur, "water and fire in balance"), plus selenite, flying squirrel droppings and green and aged tangerine peel.
