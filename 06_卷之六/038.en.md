# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 038 神功丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shengong wan (Divine Merit Pill)

It treats congested qi of the triple burner with glomus and oppression of the heart and abdomen, wind-heat of the six bowels with constipation, pain of the lower back and thighs, heavy pain of the shoulders and back, a clouded head and hot face, a bitter mouth and dry throat, vexation and agitation of the heart and chest and unquiet sleep; and it treats leg qi and dry, bound stools in people who habitually have wind.

da maren (hemp seed; pounded separately into a paste)　renshen (ginseng; 2 liang each)　helile pi (chebule fruit rind)　dahuang (rhubarb; brocade-patterned pieces, wrapped in dough and roasted in embers; 4 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder, add the hemp seed, pound and grind evenly and form pills the size of wutong seeds with refined honey. Take twenty pills each time with warm water; warm wine or rice water are also fine; after meals and at bedtime. If the bowels do not move, the number of pills may be doubled, until they move.

## Notes

Shengong wan (this and the following formulas appear to belong to the appended section on constipation): for chest and abdominal glomus from congested qi of the triple burner, constipation from wind-heat of the six bowels, pain of the back, thighs, shoulders and back, clouded head, hot face, bitter mouth, agitation and insomnia, for leg qi, and for constipation in people with wind.
- Rhubarb purges, hemp seed moistens, chebule and ginseng are added.
