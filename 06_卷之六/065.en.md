# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 065 肉豆蔻散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Roudoukou san (Nutmeg Powder)

It is suitable for deficient spleen and stomach qi with distension and fullness of the abdomen and flanks, undigested water and grain, slippery diarrhoea, deficiency rumbling in the abdomen, fatigue with little strength, a bitter mouth and dry tongue, no desire for food, and daily increasing emaciation and weakness.

cangzhu (black atractylodes; soaked overnight in rice-washing water, skin removed, dried over a fire; 8 liang)　huixiang (fennel; stir-fried)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　chuanwu (Sichuan aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　hezi pi (chebule fruit rind; 2 liang each)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried)　houpo (magnolia bark; coarse bark removed, stir-fried with ginger)　chenpi (aged tangerine peel; white pith removed)　roudoukou (nutmeg; wrapped in dough and roasted in embers)　gancao (licorice; scorched; 4 liang each)

Powder the above. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one *zhan* of water, two slices of fresh ginger and one jujube, decoct down to seven-tenths and take it warm.

## Notes

Roudoukou san (Wu Zhige's additions): for abdominal and flank distension, indigestion, diarrhoea, borborygmus, fatigue, bitter mouth, poor appetite and wasting from deficient spleen and stomach qi.
- Pingwei san (black atractylodes, magnolia bark, tangerine peel, licorice) with nutmeg and chebule to astringe, and dried ginger, Sichuan aconite, cinnamon and fennel to warm.
