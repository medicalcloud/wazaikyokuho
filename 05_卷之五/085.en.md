# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 085 秘伝玉鎖丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Michuan yusuo dan (Secretly Transmitted Jade Lock Elixir)

It treats insufficient heart qi, excessive thought and anxiety, deficient, damaged kidney meridian and insecure true yang with dribbling after urination, white turbid urine like grease, frequent leakage in dreams, and in severe cases a cramped, weary body, aching joints, inability to eat, a dark sallow complexion, a withered appearance and wasted flesh, dry lips and mouth, deficiency vexation with night sweats, and lack of strength in movement. (One version further has the ten characters: "This medicine is warm, not hot, in nature and has great divine efficacy.")

fuling (bukuryo; poria; skin removed; 4 liang)　longgu (ryukotsu; dragon bone; 2 liang)　wubeizi (gallnut; 6 liang)

Powder the above and form pills with water paste. Take forty pills each time on an empty stomach, swallowed with salt water, three doses a day. This medicine is warm, not hot, in nature and has extreme divine efficacy.

## Notes

Michuan yusuo dan (bureau additions): for dribbling, greasy white turbid urine and dream emissions from insufficient heart qi, overthinking, damaged kidney meridian and insecure true yang, and in severe cases for bodily stiffness, joint pain, poor appetite, dark complexion, wasting, dry mouth, night sweats and weakness.
- Three ingredients (poria, dragon bone, gallnut) to secure essence; corresponds to the securing part of the preceding Jinsuo zhengyuan dan.
- The small note at the end of the indications is as in the base text.
