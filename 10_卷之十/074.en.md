# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 074 柏墨散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Baimo san (Phellodendron and Soot Powder)

It treats children who after the umbilical cord has been cut are damaged by water and damp, or whose navel is injured by the damp of swaddling cloths, or, when unwrapped, are attacked by wind-cold, so that the child's limbs are not at ease, the navel swells and the child cries and cannot nurse; this should be treated quickly.

luanfa (human hair; washed clean, burned to ash)　fu xia heimei (black soot from under a cauldron)　huangbo (obaku) mo (powdered phellodendron; equal parts)

Grind the above medicines together finely. Each time apply a little.

## Notes

Baimo san (Wu Zhige's additions): an external powder for umbilical infection after the cord is cut (from water, damp swaddling or wind-cold), with a swollen navel, crying and inability to nurse in newborns.
- Hair ash, pot soot and powdered phellodendron applied to the navel.
