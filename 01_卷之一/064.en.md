# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 064 寿星丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shouxing wan (Star of Longevity Pill)

It is suitable when the heart and abdomen [are disturbed] by fright, the spirit does not keep to its abode, wind-drool surges in attacks, the hands and feet convulse, the patient is very forgetful, behaves abnormally and has a clouded, obstructed mind.

tiannanxing (arisaema; 1 jin. First heat a pit in the ground red-hot with 30 jin of charcoal, remove the charcoal, pour 5 *sheng* of wine into the pit, wait until the wine has soaked in, put the arisaema into the pit, cover the pit with a basin and pack ash around it so that no vapour escapes; the next day take it out and powder it)　zhusha (cinnabar; ground separately; 2 liang)　hupo (amber; ground separately; 1 liang)

Grind the above evenly and form pills the size of wutong seeds with flour paste boiled in ginger juice. Take thirty pills each time, increasing to fifty, washed down with decocted acorus and ginseng decoction, after meals and at bedtime.

## Notes

Shouxing wan (Chunyou-era additions): for disturbance of the spirit by fright with surging wind-drool, convulsions, forgetfulness, abnormal behaviour and clouded consciousness.
- Arisaema baked in a wine-soaked fire pit transforms phlegm; cinnabar and amber calm the heart-spirit; it is taken with acorus and ginseng decoction.
