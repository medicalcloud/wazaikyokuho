# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 020 妙香散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Miaoxiang san (Wondrous Fragrance Powder)

It treats men and women with insufficient heart qi, an unsettled will, fright, palpitation, fear and terror, grief, worry and sorrow, deficiency vexation with little sleep, irregular joy and anger, much night sweating, tasteless food and clouding and dizziness of the head and eyes. Taken regularly it supplements and augments qi and blood, calms the spirit and settles the heart.

shexiang (musk; ground separately; 1 *qian*)　muxiang (costus root; roasted; 2½ liang)　shanyao (Chinese yam; roasted with ginger juice)　fushen (poria with root; skin and wood removed)　fuling (poria; skin removed, not dried over a fire)　huangqi (astragalus)　yuanzhi (polygala; core removed, stir-fried; 1 liang each)　renshen (ginseng)　jiegeng (platycodon)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried; half a liang each)　chensha (cinnabar; ground separately; 3 *qian*)

Make the above into a fine powder. Take 2 *qian* each time mixed in warm wine, at any time.

## Notes

Miaoxiang san (Shaoxing-era additions): for anxiety, fright and palpitation, fear, worry, insomnia, emotional instability, night sweats, poor appetite and dizziness from insufficient heart qi.
- Ginseng, astragalus, Chinese yam, poria, poria with root and licorice supplement qi; polygala, cinnabar and musk calm the heart-spirit; costus and platycodon regulate qi.
