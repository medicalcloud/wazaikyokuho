# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 101 盧氏異方感応丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Lu shi yifang ganying wan (Mr Lu's Variant Responsive Pill)

It differs greatly from the formula in the *Hezi ju fang*. But in using it, the preparation must follow the method and the proportions must above all be evenly balanced; it only warms and transforms and must not let any one force prevail. This medicine [transforms] accumulated stagnation without stirring the viscera and bowels. The marvel of its action lies in the large amount of wax, which must on no account be reduced. Taken regularly it strengthens the spleen and improves eating, and one will never suffer from cold and heat, diarrhoea or dysentery; for it gradually disperses and grinds down accumulated stagnation, and so naturally there is no illness. When drunk or overfed with wine and food, it is especially good to take more. Its divine effect cannot be described.

huangla (yellow beeswax; genuine; 10 liang)　badou (croton seeds; 100, skins removed, ground to powder, wrapped in several layers of paper and pounded, changing the paper when the oil soaks through, until the oil is exhausted and it becomes white frost—this is best)　ruxiang (frankincense; chopped and ground; 3 *qian*)　xingren (apricot kernels; 70, skin and tips removed, finely ground, oil removed as with the croton)　dingxiang (clove; dried in the bosom)　muxiang (costus root; wrapped in wet paper and roasted)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried)　roudoukou (nutmeg; wrapped in flour and roasted)　bichengqie (cubeb)　binglang (areca)　qingpi (green tangerine peel; washed in hot water, pulp removed, stir-fried)　baicaoshuang (pot soot; finely sieved)　pianzi jianghuang (sliced turmeric; 1 liang each)

Except for the croton powder, pot soot, apricot kernels and frankincense, make the rest together into a fine powder, then mix with those four and grind evenly. First melt the 10 liang of yellow wax into liquid in a silver or stone vessel and strain through a double layer of floss to remove the dregs; boil the wax in 1 *sheng* of good ash-free wine in a silver or stone vessel until melted, bring it to the boil several times, take it out and let it cool; the wax will float on the wine of its own accord; discard the wine. For compounding in spring and summer use 1 liang of clear sesame oil, in autumn and winter 1½ liang; heat it in a large silver vessel until fragrant and cooked, then add the wine-boiled wax and melt them together into liquid; while hot, mix in the powdered medicines until perfectly even; when it has set a little, divide it into portions and store in a jar; it may be taken only after half a month. To take it, roll pills the size of radish seeds as needed and take as many as desired; increasing from twenty or thirty to fifty pills does no harm. Because there is so much wax this medicine is hard to roll into pills, but the finer the pills, the broader their effect. It should always be taken at bedtime; to treat illness, take it at any time.

## Notes

Lu shi yifang ganying wan (bureau additions): Mr Lu's variant of Ganying wan, which it states differs greatly from the earlier Ganying wan (juan 3, no. 042). It gradually disperses stagnation without stirring the bowels, is taken regularly to strengthen the spleen and improve appetite, and in larger amounts after drinking and eating.
- Croton frost and apricot-kernel frost transform accumulations, with frankincense, clove, costus, dried ginger, nutmeg, cubeb, areca, green tangerine peel, turmeric and pot soot, all encased in a large amount of beeswax, which must not be reduced.
