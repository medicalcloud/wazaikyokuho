# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 013 匀気散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yunqi san (Qi-Evening Powder)

It treats stagnant, uneven qi, deficiency glomus of the chest and diaphragm, undispersed chronic cold and stabbing pain of the heart and abdomen. It removes distension, fullness and choking obstruction and stops vomiting and nausea. Taken regularly it regulates and smooths the spleen and stomach and makes eating better.

dingxiang (clove)　tanxiang (sandalwood)　muxiang (costus root)　bai doukou ren (white cardamom; 2 liang each)　huoxiang leaves (agastache)　gancao (licorice; scorched; 8 liang each)　suoshaner (amomum; 4 liang)

Powder the above. Take 1 *qian* each time with one *zi* of powdered salt, stirred into boiling water, at any time.

## Notes

Yunqi san: for chest glomus, chronic cold, stabbing heart and abdominal pain, fullness, vomiting and nausea from qi stagnation.
- Clove, sandalwood, costus, white cardamom, amomum and agastache move qi and warm the stomach; licorice harmonizes; taken with salt in hot water.
