# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 023 枇杷葉散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Pipaye san (Biwayosan; Loquat Leaf Powder)

It treats being affected by summer heat with latent heat, drinking too much so that the spleen and stomach are damaged by cold, food not being digested, stuffiness and oppression of the chest and diaphragm, retching and nausea, clouding and dizziness of the head and eyes, dry mouth with vexing thirst, weary limbs and complete lack of desire to eat; or disharmony of yin and yang developing into sudden turmoil with vomiting, diarrhea and cramps, vexation, agitation and drinking.

pipaye (loquat leaf; hairs removed, roasted)　chenpi (chinpi; aged tangerine peel; pulp removed with hot water, dried over a fire)　dingxiang (clove), half a liang each　houpo (koboku; magnolia bark; skin removed, smeared with ginger juice and roasted; 4 liang)　baimaogen (imperata root)　maimendong (bakumondo; ophiopogon; core removed, dried over a fire)　dried mugua (chaenomeles)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried; 1 liang each)　xiangru (elsholtzia; 3 *fen*)

Pound and sieve the above medicines into a powder. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one *zhan* of water and two slices of fresh ginger, decoct down to seven-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm; it may also be mixed in warm water. If there is restlessness, mix it in freshly drawn water. At any time. Children under three may take half a *qian*, adjusted further according to size.

## Notes

Pipaye san: for chest stuffiness, nausea, thirst, lassitude and poor appetite after excessive cold drinking in summer has chilled the spleen and stomach, and for vomiting, diarrhea, cramps and thirst in sudden turmoil.
- Loquat leaf sends down counterflow stomach qi; elsholtzia, magnolia bark, tangerine peel and clove remove summer heat-damp and cold; imperata and ophiopogon stop thirst; chaenomeles treats cramps.
