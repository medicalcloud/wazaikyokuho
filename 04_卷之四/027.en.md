# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之四 027 渫白丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xiebai wan (Rinsed White Pill)

It treats phlegm and drool in the diaphragm and stomach duct that will not clear, clouding and dizziness of the head and eyes, and vomiting of drool and froth.

fuzi (aconite; one piece weighing 6 *qian*, raw, skin and navel removed)　raw liuhuang (sulphur; ground separately)　tiannanxing (arisaema; used raw)　banxia (pinellia; used raw; 1 liang each)　penxiao (mirabilite)　xuanjingshi (selenite; half a liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder, mix in 3 liang of fine flour evenly, knead with water and form pills the size of wutong seeds. For each dose boil thirty pills in boiling water until they float, take them out and wash them down with ginger decoction, after meals.

## Notes

Xiebai wan (Chunyou-era additions): for dizziness and vomiting of frothy drool from phlegm in the diaphragm and stomach duct.
- Raw aconite and sulphur warm; raw arisaema and pinellia transform phlegm; mirabilite and selenite send phlegm-heat down. The pills are boiled until they float before being taken.
