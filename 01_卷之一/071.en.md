# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 071 驚気丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jingqi wan (Fright-Qi Pill)

It is very suitable when fright and worry have accumulated qi and the heart has received pathogenic wind, so that in attacks the jaws clench and drool surges with clouded obstruction, and on waking the mind is as if demented.

zisuzi (perilla seed; stir-fried; 1 liang)　juhong (red tangerine peel)　southern muxiang (mokko; costus root)　fuzi (bushi; aconite; raw, skin and navel removed)　mahuang (mao; ephedra; roots and nodes removed)　huashe (pit viper; soaked in wine and roasted, skin and bones removed)　bai jiangcan (silkworm; lightly stir-fried)　nanxing (arisaema; washed and soaked, thinly sliced, soaked overnight in ginger juice)　tianma (gastrodia; sprouts removed; half a liang each)　zhusha (cinnabar; ground; 1½ *fen*, for coating)　ganxie (dried scorpion; tail stings removed, lightly stir-fried; 1 *fen*)

Powder the above, add a little ground borneol and musk, grind together until perfectly even, pound with refined honey and form pills the size of longan fruits. Take one pill each time dissolved in gold-and-silver mint decoction; warm wine is also fine. (Regarding this formula: in the wushen year a man in the army had broken the law; he had been stripped and was about to be executed when he was released, but his spirit was lost and he became as if demented. After taking one pill he fell asleep, and when he awoke the illness was gone. The wife of Zhang Zaiyang, Superintendent of Jiangdong, lost her mind for several years after fleeing from bandits; she received this formula and recovered before finishing one course. Again, the wife of the Police Inspector Huang Yan had manic reversal for more than a year; she was given this formula with the aconite removed and iron powder added, and recovered before finishing one course. Iron powder transforms phlegm, calms the heart and restrains liver pathogen; if there is much anger and liver pathogen is greatly exuberant, iron powder can subdue it. The *Suwen* says, "Heat syncope with manic anger is treated with iron powder"—the idea of metal overcoming wood.)

## Notes

Jingqi wan (Wu Zhige's additions): for accumulated qi from fright and worry, with pathogenic wind in the heart causing attacks of clenched jaws and clouding, and a dementia-like state on waking.
- Perilla seed, red tangerine peel and costus move qi; aconite, ephedra, pit viper, silkworm, arisaema, gastrodia and scorpion expel wind-phlegm; cinnabar, borneol and musk calm the heart and open the orifices.
- The bracketed passage is an original note recording three cases and a modification adding iron powder; the *Suwen* reference probably points to the iron-filings drink for heat syncope anger in the chapter "Bingneng lun".
