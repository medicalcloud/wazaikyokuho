# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 038 降心丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jiangxin dan (Heart-Lowering Elixir)

[It treats] insufficiency of the heart and kidneys with hot body and night sweats, forgetfulness and seminal emission; and excess of hot medicines with exuberance above and deficiency below, qi and blood not descending, and red or white, thick, turbid, unclear urine. Taken regularly it settles and benefits the heart-spirit, supplements deficiency and nourishes the blood, augments the cinnabar field and seals the essential qi.

shu gan dihuang (kanjio; prepared rehmannia; washed clean, soaked in wine, steamed, dried over a fire)　tianmendong (tenmondo; asparagus root; core removed)　maimendong (bakumondo; ophiopogon; core removed; 3 liang each)　fuling (bukuryo; poria; skin removed)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng)　yuanzhi (onji; polygala; boiled with licorice, rhizome head and woody core removed)　fushen (poria with root)　shanyao (Chinese yam; 2 liang each)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed, not exposed to fire)　zhusha (cinnabar; ground and water-ground; half a liang each)　danggui (toki; Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed, washed, dried over a fire; 3 liang)

Powder the above and form pills the size of wutong seeds with refined honey. Take thirty pills each time, swallowed with decocted ginseng decoction.

## Notes

Jiangxin dan (Wu Zhige's additions): for hot body, night sweats, forgetfulness and seminal emission from heart-kidney insufficiency, and for red or white turbid urine with exuberance above and deficiency below after excessive hot medicines.
- Rehmannia, asparagus root, ophiopogon and angelica nourish yin and blood; ginseng, poria and Chinese yam supplement; polygala, poria with root and cinnabar calm the heart-spirit; a little cinnamon draws the fire downward.
