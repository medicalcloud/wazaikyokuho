# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 021 論傷寒得病之由 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On how cold damage is contracted. In cold damage, at the onset of illness there is at once inability to eat, unremitting fever, headache or pain of the whole body, or spontaneous sweating with aversion to wind, aversion to cold with vigorous fever: these are the patterns of wind damage and cold damage. One must at once ask how many days the illness has lasted, whether there is sweating, whether there is aversion to wind, whether there is thirst, whether there is vomiting counterflow, and whether urination is free; only after careful questioning may medicines be used. The patterns of cold damage are unlike those of miscellaneous illnesses; if medicines are given recklessly without matching the pattern, the offense is not slight, and many people are harmed.

## Notes

On the onset of cold damage: early signs (poor appetite, persistent fever, headache, body pain, spontaneous sweating with aversion to wind, chills with high fever) indicate wind damage or cold damage; one must ask about the number of days, sweating, aversion to wind, thirst, vomiting and urination before prescribing.
