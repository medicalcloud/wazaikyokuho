# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之四 036 新法半夏湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xinfa banxia tang (New Method Pinellia Decoction)

It is suitable for disharmony of the spleen and stomach with stagnant qi in the middle duct, chronic cold and lodged rheum accumulating without dispersing, stabbing pain of the heart and abdomen, distension of the viscera and bowels, vomiting of phlegm and water, belching and acid swallowing; or drunkenness with vomiting of wine, hiccough and nausea, headache with vexing thirst, lassitude with a liking for lying down and no desire for food.

qingpi (green tangerine peel; white removed)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried), 6 liang each　jiegeng (platycodon; stir-fried)　chenpi (aged tangerine peel; white pith removed), 1 liang each　dingxiangpi (clove bark; 4 liang)　gancao (licorice; stir-fried; 12 liang)　banxia (pinellia; washed in hot water, processed with ginger juice; 2½ liang)

Make the above into a fine powder. Take 1 *qian* each time with a pinch of salt stirred into boiling water, at any time. Taken regularly it warms and harmonizes the triple burner, opens the stomach, strengthens the spleen, disperses hangover and improves eating.

## Notes

Xinfa banxia tang (bureau additions): nearly the same indications as the Xinfa banxia tang of juan 3, no. 065, but a different formula (green and aged tangerine peel, dried ginger, platycodon, clove bark, licorice, pinellia). This is the one referred to in juan 4, no. 024 ("another formula appears later").
