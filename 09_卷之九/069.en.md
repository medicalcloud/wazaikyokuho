# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 069 調経散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Tiaojing san (Menses-Regulating Powder)

It treats postpartum failed blood that, taking advantage of deficiency, lodges and accumulates in the five zang organs, flows along the meridians into the limbs, stagnating more deeply day by day, rotting and spoiling like water, gradually causing puffy swelling of the body, face and eyes. It is also suitable when, because of childbirth, failed blood rises to invade the heart, which cannot bear the contact, so that the heart is vexed and agitated, there is no peace lying or rising, the patient seems to see ghosts and spirits, and speech is confused.

danggui (toki) (Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　moyao (myrrh; ground separately)　hupo (amber; ground separately)　chi shaoyao (shakuyaku; red peony root; 1 liang each)　xixin (saishin; asarum; shoots removed)　shexiang (jako; musk; ground separately; half a liang each)

Pound the above into a fine powder and mix in the ground medicines evenly. Take 1 *qian* each time mixed evenly in warm wine with a little ginger juice. In general, physicians fail to recognize postpartum deficiency swelling and at once treat it as water qi. Water qi is mostly treated with water-guiding medicines, which greatly deplete a person. After childbirth the patient is already deficient; to deplete her further with medicine is called double deficiency, and it often causes needless early death. Simply take this medicine, and as the blood moves the swelling subsides and she recovers.

## Notes

Tiaojing san (bureau additions): for edema of the body, face and eyes when postpartum failed blood lodges in the zang organs and limbs and rots "like water", and for agitation, insomnia, hallucinations and confused speech when failed blood invades the heart. It warns against mistaking postpartum swelling for water qi and further depleting the patient with diuretics.
- Angelica, red peony, myrrh and amber move static blood; cinnamon, asarum and musk warm and open.
- Two emendations: "cannot bear the contact" (*bu shou chu*; base text *xia shou chu*) and "already deficient" (*ji xu*; base text *ji xu* with a different character), both following the Siku quanshu and Executive Yuan editions.
