# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 034 漏芦散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Loulu san (Rhaponticum Powder)

It treats nursing women with blocked qi vessels and failure of milk to flow, as well as congealed stagnation of the channels and network vessels with distending pain within the breast, evil toxin retained and stored, or abscess swelling. Taking this medicine makes it disperse internally of its own accord and the milk flows.

loulu (rhaponticum root; 2½ liang)　gualou (trichosanthes fruit; ten, burned until scorched over a fierce fire with nature preserved)　shetui (snake slough; ten, roasted)

Make the above into a fine powder. Take 2 *qian* each time mixed in warm wine, at any time; after a good while drink hot broth to assist it.

## Notes

Loulu san: for failure of lactation, distending breast pain, retained toxin and breast abscess in nursing women; disperses the swelling internally and lets the milk flow.
- Three ingredients (rhaponticum, burned trichosanthes fruit, snake slough) taken in wine, followed by hot broth.
