# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 123 清遠香 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qingyuan xiang (Pure and Distant Incense)

jiangzhenxiang (dalbergia; the purple-vine kind)　linglingxiang (lysimachia)　maoxiang (fragrant grass; 6 liang each)　dingxiang pi (clove bark)

Powder the above, knead with refined honey and use in the usual way.

## Notes

Qingyuan xiang ("Various incenses"; the last entry of juan 10 and of the main text): an incense recipe. No amount is given for clove bark, and the list at the end of the preceding Jiangzhen xiang entry (scrophularia, angelica dahurica, agastache, cyperus, nardostachys, musk) may belong to this recipe (not verified).
