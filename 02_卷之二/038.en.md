# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 038 解暑三白散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jieshu sanbai san (Summerheat-Resolving Three White Powder)

It treats being affected by summerheat with latent heat, drinking too much, counterflow of yin and yang qi, sudden turmoil with vomiting, difficult urination, disharmony of the viscera and bowels, nausea and dizziness.

zexie (alisma)　baizhu (white atractylodes)　bai fuling (white poria; equal parts)

Cut the above into pieces. For each dose take one packet with one *zhan* of water, five slices of ginger and ten rush pith stems, decoct down to eight-tenths, remove the dregs and take it, at any time. Each packet weighs half a liang.

## Notes

Jieshu sanbai san (Baoqing-era additions): for sudden turmoil, vomiting, difficult urination, nausea and dizziness from excessive drinking in summer heat.
- Three white ingredients (alisma, white atractylodes, white poria; hence "three white") drain water.
