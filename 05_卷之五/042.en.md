# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 042 秦艽鼈甲散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qinjiao biejia san (Large-Leaf Gentian and Turtle Shell Powder)

It can treat men and women with taxation damage of qi and blood, weary limbs, an emaciated weak body, vexing pain of the joints, a clouded head and red cheeks, withered haggard limbs, a withered yellow complexion, parched lips and dry mouth, vexing heat of the five hearts, cough with phlegm and drool, drawing pain of the lower back and spine, getting up and lying down restlessly, unquiet sleep and dreams, an abstracted mind, occasional night sweats, a bitter mouth without taste and food without relish; as well as miasmic qi of mountain mists with alternating chills and fever.

jingjie (schizonepeta; stalks removed)　beimu (fritillaria; core removed)　tianxianteng (aristolochia stem)　qianhu (peucedanum; rhizome head removed)　qingpi (green tangerine peel; white removed)　chaihu (bupleurum; rhizome head removed)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried)　chenpi (aged tangerine peel; white pith removed)　qinjiao (large-leaf gentian; rhizome head removed, washed)　biejia (turtle shell; rim removed, roasted with vinegar; 1 liang each)　gange (dried pueraria; 2 liang, dried over a fire)　baizhi (angelica dahurica)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　qianghuo (notopterygium; half a liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one *zhan* of water and three slices of fresh ginger, decoct together down to eight-tenths and take it fairly hot, at any time; it may also be mixed in wine. Taken regularly it nourishes qi and blood, regulates the nutritive and defensive qi and relieves weariness.

## Notes

Qinjiao biejia san (Wu Zhige's additions): for lassitude, wasting, joint pain, red cheeks, vexing heat of the five hearts, cough, back pain, insomnia, night sweats and poor appetite from taxation damage of qi and blood, and for chills and fever from miasma.
- Gentian, turtle shell and bupleurum clear deficiency heat; schizonepeta, peucedanum, pueraria, angelica dahurica and notopterygium release the exterior; fritillaria stops cough; tangerine peels and aristolochia stem move qi; cinnamon is added.
