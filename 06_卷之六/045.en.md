# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 045 半硫丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Banliu wan (Pinellia and Sulphur Pill)

It removes accumulated cold, warms the original viscus, warms the spleen and stomach and improves eating. It treats all strings, aggregations and cold qi of the heart and abdomen, and in the elderly wind constipation and cold constipation, or diarrhoea and the like.

banxia (pinellia; soaked seven times in hot water, dried over a fire, made into a fine powder)　liuhuang (sulphur; bright, clear, good pieces, ground extremely fine and treated with a willow-wood mallet)

Take equal parts of the above, boil them down together with the natural juice of fresh ginger, add dry steamed-cake powder and stir evenly, put into a mortar and pound several hundred times, and form pills the size of wutong seeds. Take fifteen to twenty pills each time on an empty stomach with warm wine or ginger decoction; women take them with vinegar decoction.

## Notes

Banliu wan: said to remove accumulated cold and warm the spleen and stomach; for strings, aggregations and cold qi of the heart and abdomen, cold constipation in the elderly, and diarrhoea. Still known today as a formula for constipation from deficiency cold in the elderly.
- Two ingredients, pinellia and sulphur, made into pills with ginger juice and steamed cake.
