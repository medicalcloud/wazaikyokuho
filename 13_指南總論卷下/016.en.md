# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷下 016 論諸虚不足 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On the signs of the various deficiencies.

(With chronic cold appended.)

On insufficiency from the various deficiencies. These all arise from deficient, exhausted kidney qi and accumulated cold in the lower origin, with pain of the lower back and spine, weary limbs, a lusterless complexion, an unrefreshed spirit, dry lips and mouth, dim eyes and tinnitus, slippery frequent urination, many strange dreams at night, night sweats and loss of essence, no desire for food and daily emaciation. Give Tusizi wan (Toshishigan), Anshen wan, Bawei wan, Wubi shanyao wan, Huangqi jianzhong tang (Ogikenchuto) or Huixiang wan (Uikyogan). For weak spleen and stomach, also give stomach-strengthening medicines. For severe deficiency and damage, give Shexiang lurong wan (Jakorokujogan), Chenxiang lurong wan (Jinkorokujogan), properly refined Heixi dan (Kokushakutan), Jinsuo zhengyuan wan, Zhang Zouma yushuang wan, Zhengyuan san, Chenxiang biejia san (Jinkobekkosan), Jiaofu wan or Sizhu san (Shichusan). For deficiency below with rushing diarrhea, [give] Sizhu san, Zhengyuan san or Dingxiang doukou san. To treat insufficient heart qi with an abstracted mind, confused speech, unsettled fright palpitations and many strange dreams at night, give Dingzhi wan, Jiangqi dan, Zhenxin wan, Renshen huangqi san (Ninjinogisan), Miaoxiang san (Myokosan), Wuchen tang or Shen ling baizhu san (Jinryobyakujutsusan). To treat white turbid urine, dream emissions and seminal loss, give Pingbu zhenxin dan, Jiangxin dan, Weixi wan or Dingzhi wan. For severe cold exhaustion and deficiency damage with cold, slippery, insecure essence, give Jinsuo zhengyuan dan. For pain of the lower back and hips, give Qing'e wan; in severe cases, Shenying wan, Huangqi wan (Ogigan), Ruxiang wan (Nyukogan), Huoluo dan (Katsurakutan), Anshen wan or Fanfeng yingtong wan. For tinnitus, Huangqi wan works at once. For fever of deficiency taxation that worsens in the afternoon, take Anshen wan, Bawei wan, Shanyao wan, Chenxiang biejia san, Shen ling baizhu san or Jiahe san on an empty stomach, and Renshen danggui san (Ninjintokisan) after meals. If the coughed drool is yellow or contains blood, take Renshen yangfei wan (Ninjinyohaigan) at bedtime. For deficiency taxation from overexertion with unceasing vomiting of blood, give Michuan jiangqi tang or Xiao jiangqi tang decocted with much ginseng added. For the above with severe fever, give Xiaoyao san (Shoyosan). For heat in the bones, give Renshen huangqi san. The weak and elderly may take Huangqi jianzhong tang regularly. For deficiency sweating and night sweats with palpitation and shortness of breath, take Muli san (Boreisan), Zhihan san, Huangqi jianzhong tang, Da shanyao wan, or Renshen danggui san decocted with wheat. For unceasing spontaneous sweating, Zhufu tang or Zhengyuan san. For night sweats from heart heat, give Chensha miaoxiang san.

## Notes

The first discussion under "On the signs of the various deficiencies (with chronic cold appended)": insufficiency from the various deficiencies (exhausted kidney qi and accumulated cold below: back pain, lassitude, poor complexion, dry mouth, dim vision, tinnitus, frequent urination, nightmares, night sweats, loss of essence, poor appetite, wasting) treated with Tusizi wan, Anshen wan, Bawei wan and others; with further formulas for weak spleen and stomach, severe deficiency, rushing diarrhea, insufficient heart qi (abstraction, confused speech, palpitations, nightmares), turbid urine and emissions, cold essence, back and hip pain, tinnitus (Huangqi wan), afternoon fever of deficiency taxation, blood-tinged sputum, hematemesis from overexertion, bone heat, and deficiency, night and spontaneous sweating.
- Most of the formulas are in juan 5, "Treating the various deficiencies". "Fanfeng yingtong wan" follows the base text (perhaps an error for Zhuifeng yingtong wan).
