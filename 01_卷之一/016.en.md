# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 016 碧霞丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bixia dan (Azure Cloud Elixir)

It treats sudden strike by acute wind with dizziness and stiff falling, congestion of phlegm and drool, confusion and oppression of the heart-spirit, clenched jaws and upturned eyes; as well as the five kinds of epilepsy with surging drool and convulsions.

shilü (malachite; ground and water-ground nine times; 10 liang)　tips of fuzi (bushi; aconite lateral root)　tips of wutou (uzu; aconite main root)　xieshao (scorpion tails; 70 of each)

Powder the three [latter] ingredients, add the malachite and mix evenly, and form pills the size of a euryale seed with flour paste. For each dose quickly dissolve one pill in half a *zhan* of mint juice, add half a *ge* of wine, warm it and take it. After a while the patient vomits phlegm and drool; then treat according to the pattern. If the jaws are clenched, pry them open and pour it in; it works at once.

## Notes

Bixia dan: an emetic for acute wind stroke or epileptic attacks with phlegm congestion, to bring up phlegm first.
- Based on malachite, with aconite tips and scorpion tails to move phlegm and wind; after vomiting, treatment continues according to the pattern.
