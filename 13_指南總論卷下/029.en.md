# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷下 029 論月経諸疾 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On the various illnesses of women.

On the various menstrual disorders. They all arise from irregular menses—early or late, heavy or scant, dribbling without stopping, or blocked without passage—with weary limbs, fatigue with little strength and tasteless food. For regular supplementation, take Siwu tang (Shimotsuto), Shu gan dihuang wan, Neibu danggui wan, Hupo wan (Kohakugan), Danggui jianzhong tang (Tokikenchuto), Chenxiang biejia san (Jinkobekkosan), Huoxue dan, Zelan wan (Takurangan) or Yiyin dan. For a cold, exhausted sea of blood, give Nuangong wan, Huixiang wan (Uikyogan), Dasheng san, Xiao baiwei wan (Shobyakubigan), Heixi dan (Kokushakutan), Shexiang lurong wan (Jakorokujogan) or Neibu danggui wan. For excessive menstrual blood dribbling without stopping, give Jiao'ai tang, Wenjing tang (Unkeito), Xiao baiwei wan, Wujin san, Yiyin dan, Zelan wan, Neibu danggui wan or Shu gan dihuang wan. For red and white vaginal discharge unresponsive to various medicines, take Zelan wan together with Pingbu zhenxin dan, Suxiao dasheng san, Wenjing tang, Zelan san, Xiao baiwei wan or Zishiying wan (Murasakisekieigan). For unceasing white discharge with constant cold pain of the abdomen, give Nuangong wan, Shexiang lurong wan, properly refined Heixi dan, Huixiang wan, Weixi wan, Zelan wan, Zhengyuan san or Laifu dan. For five-colored discharge, give Yiyin dan, Zixue tang or Fulonggan san (Bukuryukansan). For excessive bleeding from metrorrhagia and metrostaxis with dizziness of the head and eyes and a tendency to fall on lifting the head, give Xiongqiong tang, Jiao'ai tang, Wujin san, Hupo wan, Nuangong wan, Dasheng san or Neibu danggui wan. For failed blood of flooding not stopping for days, give Zixue tang. For deficient, exhausted, long-cold blood and qi with metrorrhagia and metrostaxis of red and white, five colors unsettled, or like fermented-soybean juice, give Wenjing tang, Fulonggan san or Siwu tang. For blocked menses and absent menses in unmarried girls, give Puhuang san (Hoosan), Xiaoyao san (Shoyosan), Dasheng san, Heishen san, Hupo zelan jian, Tongzhen wan, Huoxue dan, Siwu tang or Dihuang wan. For blood-wind taxation with blockage of the chest and diaphragm, rough menstrual flow, vexing pain of the limbs and palpitation, give Renshen jingjie san (Ninjinkeigaisan), Shu gan dihuang wan or Xiaoyao san. For dizziness from blood wind and qi deficiency, and dizziness before and after childbirth, give Baosheng wan, Siwu tang or Jiao'ai tang. For fever of deficiency taxation, and for chills and fever together, give Huangqi biejia san (Ogibekkosan), Xiaoyao san, Dihuang wan, Zelan wan, Jingjie san (Keigaisan), Jiahe san or Shen ling baizhu san (Jinryobyakujutsusan). For fever that has not yet subsided, Renshen danggui san (Ninjintokisan) is excellent. For those with strong qi, give Xiaoyao san. For phlegm cough, perhaps with blood, take Renshen yangpi wan at bedtime. For young women with strong qi and heat in the bones, take Shu gan dihuang wan before meals and Renshen huangqi san (Ninjinogisan) after meals. For mounting and conglomerations, cold pain within the vagina, or head wind entering the brain, cold impediment with cramping sinews, blocked blood with childlessness and red and white leakage, give Shu gan dihuang wan, Zhongru zelan wan or Mudan jian wan. For childless women, or those with the various deficiencies, heat above and cold below, from which the hundred illnesses arise, give Xiao baiwei wan, Yuyuliang wan (Uyoryogan) or Yangqishi wan (Yokisekigan). For those first with cold, stabbing pain of the heart and abdomen, swelling or chills, unceasing diarrhea and shortness of qi, give Wuzhuyu tang (Goshuyuto) or Huoxue tang. For blood wind attacking and pouring in, with vexing heat of the five hearts, itching of the whole body, or hives, or red swelling, give Renshen jingjie san, Renshen danggui san, Xiaofeng san (Shofusan), Siwu tang decocted with schizonepeta, or Xiaoyao san; in severe cases take Zaojia jian wan. For congealed, stagnant blood and qi, take Qing muxiang wan (Seimokkogan) to free, open and guide.

## Notes

The first discussion under "On the various illnesses of women": menstrual disorders, with formulas from juan 9 for regular supplementation in irregular menses (Siwu tang, Shu gan dihuang wan and others), a cold sea of blood, menorrhagia, red and white discharge, white discharge with cold abdominal pain, five-colored discharge, heavy flooding with dizziness, persistent failed blood, flooding of mixed or bean-juice-like color, amenorrhea (including in girls), blood-wind taxation, dizziness, deficiency fever and chills, unresolved fever, phlegm cough with blood, bone heat in young women, conglomerations, vaginal cold pain and infertility, infertility with heat above and cold below, cold abdominal pain with diarrhea, blood-wind itching and hives, and congealed blood and qi.
- "Renshen yangpi wan" follows the base text (elsewhere in the Guide Renshen yangfei wan [Ninjinyohaigan]).
