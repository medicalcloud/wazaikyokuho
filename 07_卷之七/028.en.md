# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之七 028 麝臍散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Sheqi san (Musk Pod Powder)

It treats loose teeth, painful wind and worm-eaten teeth, receding gums exposing the roots, and foul-smelling drool and blood. Used regularly it makes the teeth firm and resolves toxic qi of the tooth sockets.

niuxi (goshitsu; achyranthes; rhizome head removed; 10 jin)　mulü (populus euphratica resin; 44 liang)　huangqie (yellow eggplant; finely cut; 20)　yuliren (ikurinin; bush cherry kernel; 20 liang)　shexiang (jako) kong pizi (empty musk pods; finely chopped; 100)

Pound and crush these five ingredients and put them into an earthenware jar, cover the mouth with a tile leaving one small hole, seal with salt clay and burn red-hot; when the smoke turns white, stop the fire, take it out, cover it with fresh earth for a full day and night, take it out, and then add the following medicines:

shengma (shoma; cimicifuga)　xixin (saishin; asarum; shoots removed; 10 jin each)

Make the above into a fine powder. Each time rub a little on the affected place, and after a while rinse the mouth with warm water. At bedtime apply a little more; swallowing the saliva does no harm.

## Notes

Sheqi san: for loose teeth, wind and worm toothache, receding gums and foul-smelling drool and blood; said to firm the teeth and resolve toxin of the tooth sockets with regular use.
- A tooth powder of empty musk pods, achyranthes, populus resin, yellow eggplant and bush cherry kernel burned with nature preserved, plus cimicifuga and asarum.
