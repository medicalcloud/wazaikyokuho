# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 081 痢聖散子 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Lisheng sanzi (Dysentery Sage Powder)

The patterns treated are the same as above.

caoguo (tsaoko fruit; husk removed)　shichangpu (acorus; hairs removed)　bai fuling (bukuryo; white poria)　mahuang (mao; ephedra; roots and nodes removed)　houpo (koboku; magnolia bark; roasted with ginger juice)　duhuo (dokukatsu; pubescent angelica)　zhiqiao (bitter orange; stir-fried with bran)　huoxiang (agastache)　baizhu (byakujutsu; white atractylodes)　xixin (saishin; asarum; washed, leaves removed)　wuzhuyu (goshuyu; evodia; stalks removed)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; scorched)　mu zhuling (chorei; polyporus; skin removed)　cangzhu (black atractylodes; soaked)　liangjiang (lesser galangal; rhizome head removed)　chi shaoyao (shakuyaku; red peony root)　fuzi (bushi; aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　gaoben (kohon; Chinese lovage; rhizome head removed)　chaihu (saiko; bupleurum; rhizome head removed)　zexie (takusha; alisma)　fangfeng (bofu; saposhnikovia; rhizome head removed)　banxia (hange; pinellia; boiled; equal parts)

Chop the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water and two Chinese chive bulbs broken up, decoct together down to eight-tenths, remove the dregs and take it slightly warm before meals. For the elderly and children adjust the dose. Avoid raw, cold and greasy things.

## Notes

Lisheng sanzi (bureau additions; the last formula of juan 6): the same indications as the earlier Lisheng sanzi (juan 6, no. 062); a different formula of twenty-two ingredients.
- Ephedra, pubescent angelica, asarum, saposhnikovia, Chinese lovage and bupleurum disperse exterior pathogen; black and white atractylodes, agastache, tsaoko, magnolia bark and pinellia remove damp; poria, polyporus and alisma promote urination; aconite, evodia and lesser galangal warm; bitter orange, acorus, red peony and licorice are added. The composition belongs to the same lineage as the Song "Sheng sanzi" (known as an epidemic formula).
