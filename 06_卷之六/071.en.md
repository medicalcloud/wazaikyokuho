# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 071 縛虎丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Fuhu wan (Tiger-Binding Pill)

It treats intermittent dysentery that has not healed for one or two years, with emaciation, decline and weakness. It also treats spleen pain and lumbar pain.

pi (arsenic; good lumpy pieces, ground fine in a mortar)　huangla (yellow beeswax; half a liang each)

Melt the yellow wax, add the arsenic and stir with seven willow twigs one after another, changing as soon as the tip scorches; when all have been used, take it out and at once form pills the size of wutong seeds. Take one pill each time: for dysentery with cold water, likewise for spleen pain; for lumbar pain with cold wine; in all cases before meals. For children make pills the size of broomcorn millet and give one each time with the same vehicles as above.

## Notes

Fuhu wan (Wu Zhige's additions): for wasting and weakness from intermittent dysentery unhealed for one or two years, and for spleen pain and lumbar pain.
- Two ingredients: arsenic made into pills with yellow beeswax. Extremely toxic; not used today.
