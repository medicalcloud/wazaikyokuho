# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 047 導滞散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Daozhi san (Stagnation-Guiding Powder)

It treats crushing by heavy objects or falls from heights producing heat within the five zang organs, with hematemesis and bloody stools that do not stop; or static blood within with distension and fullness of the chest and abdomen and coarse, short, panting breath.

danggui (toki; Chinese angelica)　dahuang (daio; rhubarb)

Take equal parts of the above, stir-fry and powder. Take 2 *qian* each time mixed in warm wine, at any time.

## Notes

Daozhi san (Wu Zhige's additions): for unceasing hematemesis and bloody stools from internal heat after crushing or falls, and for chest and abdominal distension and dyspnea from internal static blood.
- Two ingredients, angelica and rhubarb, to purge static blood.
