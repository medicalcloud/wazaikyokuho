# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷下 011 論脾虚翻胃 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On stomach reflux from spleen deficiency. For stomach reflux from spleen deficiency with inability to take food or even decoctions, give Geqi san, Renshen muxiang san, Shen ling baizhu san or Wuge kuanzhong san. For belching, acid swallowing and spleen pain, give Ruyi wan, Sishi wan and the like.

## Notes

On stomach reflux from spleen deficiency: Geqi san, Renshen muxiang san, Shen ling baizhu san and Wuge kuanzhong san when neither food nor decoctions are retained; Ruyi wan and Sishi wan for belching, acid reflux and spleen pain.
