# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 032 何首烏散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Heshouwu san (Kashusan; Polygonum Multiflorum Powder)

It treats wind toxin of the spleen and lungs attacking and surging, with tinea and scabies over the whole body and itching, or hives that become sores when scratched, stiff, weary shoulders and back, numbness of the flesh, cracked hands and feet, and wind qi attacking upward with sores on the head and face; as well as purple patch wind, white patch wind, stubborn numbness and similar wind disorders.

jingjie sui (schizonepeta spikes)　manjingzi (vitex fruit; white skin removed)　kebocao (carpesium herb [?]; earth removed)　weilingxian (clematis root; washed clean)　heshouwu (polygonum multiflorum root)　fangfeng (bofu; saposhnikovia; rhizome head and forks removed)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried)

Take 5 jin of each of the above, pound and sift into a powder. Take 1 *qian* each time after meals mixed in warm wine; boiling water is also fine.

## Notes

Heshouwu san: for itching tinea and scabies, hives, scratch sores, stiff shoulders and back, numb flesh, cracked hands and feet, and sores of the head and face from wind toxin of the spleen and lungs, and for purple and white patch wind and stubborn numbness.
- Polygonum multiflorum, clematis, schizonepeta, saposhnikovia, vitex, kebocao (perhaps carpesium) and licorice dispel wind and stop itching.
