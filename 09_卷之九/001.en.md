# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 001 熟乾地黄丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shu gan dihuang wan (Prepared Rehmannia Pill)

It treats all disorders of wind deficiency and taxation cold in women: wind-cold pathogenic qi lodged in the meridians and collaterals, with blood and qi cold and rough, unable to warm and moisten the skin; or wind-cold lodging in the abdomen, so that the spleen and stomach are cold and weak and cannot digest water and grain; or the intestines deficient and receiving cold, with occasional diarrhea; or the womb harboring cold, long failing to conceive, menses irregular—sometimes heavy, sometimes scant, early or late, dribbling without stopping, or blocked—accumulations, gatherings and conglomerations, little color in the face and body, eating that waxes and wanes, wasting flesh, aching joints, intermittent chills and fever, gradual emaciation, five-colored vaginal discharge, cold pain within the vagina with occasional swelling and itching, pain of the navel and abdomen just before the menses, wrinkled, rough skin, itching hives, numbness and cramping sinews, dark spots and freckles on the face, yellowing hair that falls out, spontaneous tearing, palpitation and blurred vision; and after childbirth, taxation damage not yet recovered, wasted flesh with chills and fever, a withered dark complexion, tasteless food, gradually becoming postpartum taxation.

shu gan dihuang (kanjio; prepared rehmannia; soaked in wine)　wuweizi (gomishi; schisandra; picked clean)　baiziren (arborvitae seed; lightly stir-fried, ground separately)　xiongqiong (Sichuan lovage; 1½ liang each)　zelan (takuran; lycopus; stalks removed; 2 liang 1 *fen*)　yuyuliang (uyoryo; limonite; burned red, quenched in vinegar seven times, finely ground)　fangfeng (bofu; saposhnikovia; rhizome head and forks removed)　roucongrong (cistanche; soaked in wine overnight)　bai fuling (bukuryo; white poria; skin removed)　houpo (koboku; magnolia bark; coarse bark removed, roasted with ginger juice)　baizhi (byakushi; angelica dahurica)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried)　shanyao (Chinese yam)　xixin (saishin; asarum; shoots removed)　juanbai (selaginella; roots removed; 1 liang each)　danggui (toki) (Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed, soaked in wine, stir-fried)　gaoben (kohon) (Chinese lovage; rhizome head removed, washed)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried; 1 liang 3 *fen* each)　shujiao (shokusho) (Sichuan pepper; seeds and closed fruits removed, lightly stir-fried to remove the oil)　niuxi (goshitsu; achyranthes; shoots removed, soaked in wine overnight)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng)　xuduan (zokudan; dipsacus)　shechuangzi (jashoshi; cnidium seed; picked clean, lightly stir-fried)　wuyi (elm seed paste; stir-fried)　duzhong (tochu; eucommia; coarse bark removed, roasted yellow)　aiye (gaiyo; mugwort leaf; stir-fried; 3 *fen* each)　chi shizhi (red halloysite; calcined, quenched in vinegar)　shigao (sekko; gypsum; calcined, ground and water-ground; 2 liang each)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　shihu (sekkoku; dendrobium; roots removed)　baizhu (byakujutsu; white atractylodes; 1 liang 1 *fen* each)　zishiying (murasakisekiei; fluorite; calcined, quenched in vinegar, ground and water-ground; 3 liang)

Pound and sift the above medicines into a powder, knead with refined honey, pound five to seven hundred times and form pills the size of wutong seeds. Take thirty pills each time with warm wine or rice water, on an empty stomach before meals. Taken regularly it nourishes the blood and supplements qi, harmonizes and smooths the ying-nutrient and wei-defense qi, fills out the skin, regulates the menses, lengthens the hair and preserves the complexion, removes wind and dispels cold, and enables conception. It is warm and balanced, not hot and non-toxic. It is not suitable during pregnancy.

## Notes

Shu gan dihuang wan (the first formula of juan 9, "Treating women's diseases"): for all disorders of wind deficiency and taxation cold in women—cold in the meridians, cold spleen and stomach, diarrhea, a cold womb with infertility, irregular, dribbling or absent menses, conglomerations, wasting, joint pain, chills and fever, five-colored discharge, cold pain and itching of the vagina, premenstrual pain, dry skin, hives, numbness, facial spots, hair loss, tearing, palpitation and dizziness—and for postpartum exhaustion. Not for use in pregnancy.
- A large formula of thirty-three ingredients: prepared rehmannia, angelica, lovage, cistanche, dipsacus, eucommia, achyranthes and others supplement; fluorite, limonite, red halloysite, gypsum, dried ginger, Sichuan pepper, mugwort and cinnamon warm the womb; lycopus and selaginella move blood; saposhnikovia, asarum, Chinese lovage and angelica dahurica dispel wind.
