# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之四 008 桔梗湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jiegeng tang (Kikyoto; Platycodon Decoction)

It removes phlegm and sends qi down. It treats distension and fullness of the chest and flanks, chills and fever with retching, hard stuffiness below the heart, shortness of breath with vexation and oppression, phlegm counterflow and nausea, and inability to get food down.

jiegeng (kikyo; platycodon; finely chopped, lightly stir-fried)　banxia (hange; pinellia; washed seven times in hot water, processed with ginger juice)　chenpi (chinpi; aged tangerine peel; pulp removed; 10 liang each)　zhishi (kijitsu; unripe bitter orange; stir-fried reddish-yellow with bran; 5 liang)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one medium *zhan* of water and five slices of fresh ginger, decoct together down to seven-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm, at any time.

## Notes

Jiegeng tang: for chest and flank distension, chills and fever with retching, stuffiness below the heart, shortness of breath, nausea and poor appetite from phlegm and qi counterflow.
- Platycodon, pinellia, tangerine peel and unripe bitter orange remove phlegm and send qi down; a different formula from the Jiegeng tang of the *Shanghan lun* (platycodon and licorice).
