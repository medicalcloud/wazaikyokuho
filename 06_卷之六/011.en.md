# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 011 甘露丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Ganlu wan (Sweet Dew Pill)

It treats adults and children with wind congestion and phlegm-heat, restlessness of the heart and diaphragm, unquiet sleep at night, delirious and manic speech, red eyes and nosebleed, and dry mouth and throat; it treats summer heat strike and resolves heat toxin.

qianbaishuang (lead acetate)　longnao (borneol; 3 *fen* each)　yaxiao (refined mirabilite; dehydrated; 3 liang)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried; 1 liang)　hanshuishi (kansuiseki; calcite; powdered; 32 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of a pellet with glutinous rice paste. Take half a pill each time, ground in ginger-honey water; freshly drawn water is also fine. For children divide one pill into five doses. After meals.

## Notes

Ganlu wan: for agitation, insomnia, delirium, red eyes, nosebleed and dry mouth and throat from wind congestion and phlegm-heat, for summer heat strike and heat toxin.
- Mainly calcite, with refined mirabilite, borneol, lead acetate and licorice. It contains lead.
