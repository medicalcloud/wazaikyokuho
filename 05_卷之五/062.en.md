# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 062 鍾乳白沢丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zhongru baize wan (Stalactite White Marsh Pill)

It treats men with the various deficiencies and hundred damages, the five taxations and seven damages, insufficient genuine qi, the original zang organ not secure, spirit and will both deficient, sudden decline of the strength of the sinews, dizziness of the head and eyes, deficiency ringing in the ears, acute pain of the heart and abdomen, counterflow qi and vomiting, phlegm cough with dyspnea and hurried breathing, distension and oppression of the chest and diaphragm, spleen diarrhea and dysentery, seminal emission and turbid urine, cold limbs with spontaneous sweating, and a feeble pulse about to expire; and women with deficient, cold sea of blood, unceasing metrorrhagia and metrostaxis, red and white vaginal discharge, irregular menses, intermittent pain of the navel and abdomen, a colorless face and inability to eat. Any kind of deficiency taxation illness is suitably treated with it.

bai tanxiang (white sandalwood; powdered)　di ruxiang (drop frankincense; ground separately; 1 liang each)　yangqishi (yokiseki; actinolite; calcined red-hot, ground)　fuzi (bushi; aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed; 1½ liang each)　zhongru fen (stalactite powder; 2 liang)　shexiang (jako; musk; ground separately; 1 *qian*)

Mix the above evenly, knead into a mass by dripping in water and divide into sixty pills. For each dose decoct one pill in one *zhan* of water until dissolved, down to seven-tenths of a *zhan*, and take it hot on an empty stomach; in acute illness, at any time. Taken for a long time it supplements and augments essence and blood, assists yang and disperses yin, calms the heart-spirit, settles the ethereal and corporeal souls, prolongs life and increases longevity, and raises the dying and returns them to life.

## Notes

Zhongru baize wan (bureau additions): for dizziness, tinnitus, abdominal pain, vomiting, dyspnea cough, diarrhea, seminal emission, cold limbs, sweating and a feeble pulse from the various deficiencies and insufficient genuine qi in men, for flooding, discharge, irregular menses, abdominal pain and poor appetite from a deficient, cold sea of blood in women, and for all deficiency taxation.
- Stalactite, actinolite and aconite supplement yang; white sandalwood, frankincense and musk move qi. The pill is dissolved by decoction and drunk.
