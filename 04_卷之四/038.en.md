# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之四 038 細辛五味子湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xixin wuweizi tang (Saishingomishito; Asarum and Schisandra Decoction)

It is suitable, whether new or long-standing, for insufficiency of the lung meridian with timid, weak stomach qi, either affected by pathogenic wind or with lodged cold and retained fluid, with cough and breathing leaning [on something], inability to lie in peace, chest fullness with pressing obstruction, shortness of breath and reduced eating, dry retching with heat, coughing up bound phlegm or spitting drool and froth, clouding and dizziness of the head and eyes, pain and heaviness of the body, loss of voice, nasal congestion with clear discharge, occasional deficiency puffiness of the head, face, legs and knees, incessant phlegm-cough and pain drawing into the chest and flanks.

northern xixin (saishin; asarum; sprouts removed)　banxia (hange; pinellia; washed seven times; 1 liang each)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried)　wumei (ubai; smoked plum; stone removed; 1½ liang each)　wuweizi (gomishi; schisandra)　yingsuqiao (poppy husk; stalk and cap removed; 3 liang each)　sangbaipi (mulberry root bark; stir-fried; 2 liang)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with two and a half *zhan* of water and ten slices of fresh ginger, decoct down to one *zhan*, strain through gauze to remove the dregs and take it warm. Keep the dregs of two doses, combine them into one dose and decoct again.

## Notes

Xixin wuweizi tang (bureau additions): for cough, dyspnea, chest fullness, shortness of breath, phlegm, frothy drool, dizziness, body pain, loss of voice, nasal congestion, puffiness and chest and flank pain when pathogenic wind or cold fluid retention affects insufficient lungs and weak stomach qi.
- Asarum, pinellia and ginger warm and transform cold fluid retention; schisandra, smoked plum and poppy husk astringe the lungs; mulberry bark lowers; licorice harmonizes.
