# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 002 定命丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Dingming dan (Life-Settling Elixir)

It treats acute and chronic fright wind in children, upward-hanging gaze and puckered mouth, tidal attacks of convulsions, infant epilepsy with vigorous fever, and clouded, blocked consciousness.

qingdai (indigo; ground; half a *qian*)　chansu (toad venom; dried, soaked in wine overnight; 1 *qian*)　ganxie (dried scorpion; seven whole ones, lightly stir-fried)　shexiang (musk; ground; 1 *zi*)　bai fuzi (typhonium tuber; blast-fried, powdered; half a *fen*)　tiannanxing (arisaema; blast-fried, powdered; 1 *fen*)

Grind the above finely and evenly, form pills the size of mung beans with millet gruel, and coat them separately with indigo. Take one pill each time with schizonepeta-mint decoction; there is no doubt that sleepy drowsiness follows. For any patient, first dissolve half a pill and drip it into the nose; one who sneezes will surely recover. (One version does not use arisaema.)

## Notes

Dingming dan: for acute and chronic fright wind, upward gaze, puckered mouth, convulsions and infant epilepsy with high fever and loss of consciousness in children.
- Small pills of toad venom, scorpion, typhonium, arisaema, musk and indigo. Sneezing after dripping it into the nose is taken as a sign of recovery.
