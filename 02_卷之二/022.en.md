# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 022 香薷散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xiangru san (Elsholtzia Powder)

It treats disharmony of cold and heat in the zang-fu organs, from irregular eating and drinking, or excess of raw fish, raw and cold foods, or irregular daily life, or lying on damp ground by the road, or seeking coolness in the wind, so that wind-cold qi returns to the sanjiao and passes to the spleen and stomach; the spleen and stomach, chilled, cannot digest water and grain, true and pathogenic qi interfere with each other, the stomach and intestines become weak, and food is thrown into disorder between them, resulting in vomiting and diarrhea, pain of the heart and abdomen, and qi counterflow of sudden turmoil. Some have heart pain and vomit first; some have abdominal pain and purge first; some vomit and purge together; some have fever, headache and body pain and then vomit and purge with deficiency vexation; some only vomit and purge with stabbing pain of the heart and abdomen; some have cramps with contracture and pain; some only retch without bringing anything up; some have counterflow cold of the limbs with a pulse about to expire; some are vexed, oppressed, clouded and obstructed and near death. This medicine can govern all of these.

bai biandou (hyacinth bean; lightly stir-fried)　houpo (koboku; magnolia bark; coarse bark removed, roasted with ginger juice until cooked; half a jin each)　xiangru (elsholtzia; earth removed; 1 jin)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one *zhan* of water and one *fen* of wine, decoct down to seven-tenths, remove the dregs, chill by immersing in water, and drink two doses in succession: there is an immediate divine effect. At any time, according to the illness. The formula in the *Huoren shu* omits the hyacinth bean and adds 4 liang of coptis, chopped and ground evenly with ginger juice and stir-fried yellow; it is called Huanglian (oren) xiangru san.

## Notes

Xiangru san: the representative summer formula for summer heat-damp, for vomiting, diarrhea, abdominal pain and sudden turmoil when wind-cold enters the spleen and stomach through irregular diet, raw and cold food, lying on damp ground or cooling off in the wind.
- Three ingredients: elsholtzia, magnolia bark and hyacinth bean, taken chilled. It records the variant of the *Huoren shu* (Zhu Gong), which replaces hyacinth bean with coptis (Huanglian xiangru san).
