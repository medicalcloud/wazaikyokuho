# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 060 温脾散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wenpi san (Spleen-Warming Powder)

It treats all cases of disharmony of spleen and stomach qi, deficiency distension of the abdomen and flanks, no desire for milk or food, fatigue and lack of strength, and vigorous fever with aversion to cold.

helile pi (chebule fruit rind; blast-fried)　renshen (ginseng; 3 *fen* each)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried; 1 *fen*)　baizhu (white atractylodes)　muxiang (costus root)　fuling (poria; skin removed)　huoxiang (agastache; stalks removed)　chenpi (aged tangerine peel; white pith removed)　huangqi (astragalus)　jiegeng (platycodon; half a liang each)

Pound and sift the above into a powder. For a three-year-old, take 1 *qian* each time with one *zhan* of water, a coin-sized slice of fresh ginger and one Huai jujube, decoct together down to five-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm, at any time, adjusted for the child's size.

## Notes

Wenpi san (Wu Zhige's additions): for deficiency distension of the abdomen and flanks, poor feeding, fatigue, weakness, high fever and aversion to cold from disharmony of spleen and stomach qi.
- Si junzi tang (ginseng, atractylodes, poria, licorice) and astragalus supplement; costus, agastache and tangerine peel move qi; chebule and platycodon are added.
