# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 037 清神散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qingshen san (Spirit-Clearing Powder)

It disperses wind congestion and transforms phlegm and drool. It treats a clouded head and dizzy eyes, palpitation and a hot face, headache and tinnitus, nasal congestion with a heavy voice, twitching of the mouth and eyes, a clouded, confused spirit, painful and weary limbs, stiffness of the neck and nape, vexation and oppression of the heart and diaphragm, and discomfort of the throat.

tanxiang (sandalwood; chopped)　renshen (ginseng; rhizome head removed)　qianghuo (notopterygium; sprouts removed)　fangfeng (saposhnikovia; sprouts removed; 10 liang each)　bohe (mint; earth removed)　jingjie spikes (schizonepeta)　gancao (licorice; scorched; 20 liang each)　shigao (gypsum; ground; 40 liang)　xixin (asarum; sprouts removed, washed and dried over a fire; 5 liang)

Powder the above. Take 2 *qian* each time stirred into boiling water, or with powdered tea added. Take after meals.

## Notes

Qingshen san: for a clouded head, dizziness, palpitation, headache, tinnitus, nasal congestion and throat discomfort from wind congestion and phlegm.
- A large amount of gypsum clears heat; notopterygium, saposhnikovia, schizonepeta, mint and asarum scatter wind; sandalwood and ginseng regulate qi.
