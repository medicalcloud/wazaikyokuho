# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 037 論傷寒咳嗽 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On cough in cold damage. Cough in cold damage arises from cold evil entering the lung channel through deficiency, or from drinking too much water so that fluid is retained, with cough and slight panting, fever with or without thirst, or chills and fever and pain below the flanks; give two or three doses of Xiao qinglong tang (Minor Green-Blue Dragon Decoction). If the pain below the flanks is severe, there must be retained fluid; add a little genkwa stir-fried with vinegar to Xiao qinglong tang and decoct together—do not use much, or it will cause diarrhoea. If chills and fever arise and the cough has not ended, go on to Xiao chaihu tang, adding twenty grains of schisandra to each dose, or give Qinjiao biejia san.

## Notes

On cough in cold damage (cold evil entering the lungs, or retained fluid after excessive drinking): Xiao qinglong tang for cough, panting, fever, thirst or chills and flank pain; a little genkwa added for severe flank pain from retained fluid; Xiao chaihu tang with schisandra or Qinjiao biejia san if chills, fever and cough persist.
