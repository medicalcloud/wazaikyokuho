# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 042 六神丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Liushen dan (Six Spirits Elixir)

It treats gan qi in children with emaciation, timid, weak viscera and bowels, deficient, slippery diarrhoea, reduced intake of milk and food, drinking without limit, and distension and fullness of the heart and abdomen.

dingxiang (clove)　muxiang (costus root)　roudoukou (nutmeg; shell removed; half a liang each)

Wrap these three ingredients together in dough and roast them in gentle ashes until the dough is cooked; take out and let cool.

hezi (chebule; roasted in embers, stone removed)　shijunzi ren (quisqualis kernel; half a liang each)　luhui (aloe; finely ground and added to the medicines; 1 liang)

Pound and sift all the above together into a fine powder, knead with jujube flesh and form pills the size of hemp seeds. Take five to seven pills each time with warm rice water, before feeding.

## Notes

Liushen dan: for wasting, weak viscera, slippery diarrhoea, poor feeding, excessive drinking and abdominal fullness from gan qi in children.
- Six ingredients: clove, costus and nutmeg (roasted in dough) and chebule warm and astringe; quisqualis and aloe kill gan worms.
