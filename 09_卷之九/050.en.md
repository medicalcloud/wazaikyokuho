# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 050 滑胎枳殻散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huatai zhiqiao san (Fetus-Lubricating Bitter Orange Powder)

It treats insufficient fetal qi in women and makes the fetus slippery for easy delivery. Taken regularly it nourishes the fetus, augments qi, calms and harmonizes the womb and treats all malign illnesses within the fetus.

zhiqiao (bitter orange; pulp removed, stir-fried; 24 liang)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; scorched; 6 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder. Take 1 *qian* each time on an empty stomach mixed with boiling water. In the last month, take three doses a day.

## Notes

Huatai zhiqiao san (Wu Zhige's additions): for insufficient fetal qi; taken in the last month to make the fetus slippery for easy delivery.
- Two ingredients: bitter orange and licorice.
