# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 066 六和湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Liuhe tang (Six Harmonies Decoction)

It treats disharmony of the heart and spleen with qi failing to rise and descend, sudden turmoil with cramps, vomiting and diarrhoea, alternating chills and fever, phlegm with panting and cough, glomus and fullness of the chest and diaphragm, clouding and pain of the head and eyes, swelling of the limbs, a liking for lying down with lassitude, and red, rough urine; also cold damage in which yin and yang cannot be distinguished, being affected by summerheat with latent heat and vexation and oppression, or its turning into dysentery, and drunkenness with vexing thirst and aversion to food. It is also suitable for women before and after childbirth.

suoshaner (amomum)　banxia (pinellia; scalded seven times in hot water)　xingren (apricot kernel; skin and tips removed)　renshen (ginseng)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried; 1 liang each)　chi fuling (red poria; skin removed)　huoxiang leaves (agastache; dust brushed off)　bai biandou (hyacinth bean; lightly stir-fried with ginger juice)　mugua (chaenomeles; 2 liang each)　xiangru (elsholtzia)　houpo (magnolia bark; processed with ginger juice; 4 liang each)

Chop the above. For each dose take 4 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water, three slices of fresh ginger and one jujube, decoct down to eight-tenths, remove the dregs and take it, at any time.

## Notes

Liuhe tang (bureau additions; the last formula of juan 2): for cramps, vomiting and diarrhoea of sudden turmoil, chills and fever, cough, chest glomus, oedema and lassitude from heart-spleen disharmony and disordered rising and descending of qi, for summerheat oppression, dysentery and hangover, and for women before and after childbirth. It is known as a summerheat-damp formula.
- Xiangru san (elsholtzia, magnolia bark, hyacinth bean) with agastache, amomum, pinellia, apricot kernel, ginseng, licorice, red poria and chaenomeles added.
