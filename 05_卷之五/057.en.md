# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 057 玄兎丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xuantu dan (Dark Hare Elixir)

A divine medicine for the thirst and copious urination of the three wastings; taken regularly it checks seminal emission, stops white turbidity and prolongs life.

tusizi (toshishi; dodder seed; soaked in wine until soft, ground while moist, dried over a fire, powdered separately; 10 liang)　wuweizi (gomishi; schisandra; soaked in wine, powdered separately, weighed at 7 liang)　bai fuling (bukuryo; white poria)　dried lian rou (lotus seed flesh; 3 liang each)

Powder the above; separately mill 6 liang of dried Chinese yam powder, add wine to the wine left over from soaking and boil a paste, knead to a suitable consistency, pound several thousand times and form pills the size of wutong seeds. Take fifty pills each time, swallowed with rice broth on an empty stomach before meals.

## Notes

Xuantu dan (bureau additions): for the thirst and polyuria of the three wastings (upper, middle and lower wasting and thirst); taken regularly it is said to stop seminal emission and turbid urine.
- Mainly dodder seed and schisandra, with poria, lotus seed and Chinese yam.
