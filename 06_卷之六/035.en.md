# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 035 不二丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bu'er wan (Unparalleled Pill)

It is suitable for adults and children with all kinds of diarrhoea and dysentery, whether cold or hot, red or white, dragging on without healing or recurring after healing, with pain in the abdomen.

badou (croton seed; skin, germ and membrane removed, oil removed)　xingren (apricot kernel; soaked, skin and tips removed, ground; 70 each)　huangla (yellow beeswax; 1 liang 3 *qian*)　pishuang (arsenic trioxide; ground, put into a porcelain jar whose seams are sealed tight with red halloysite and sealed with salt clay, burned red-hot, taken out when cool; 1 liang 6 *qian*)　baijiaoxiang (liquidambar resin; finely ground; 4 *qian*)　huangdan (red lead; stir-fried; 2½ liang)　ruxiang (frankincense; ground; 6½ *qian*)　zhusha (cinnabar; ground and water-ground; half a liang)　mubiezi (momordica seed; burned until scorched; 10)

Grind the above together evenly, knead with melted wax and form pills the size of glutinous millet, 120 pills to each *qian*. Take one pill each time; children half a pill. For watery diarrhoea give it with freshly drawn water; for red dysentery with licorice decoction; for white dysentery with dried-ginger decoction; for red and white dysentery with licorice and dried-ginger decoction. In all cases give it cooled at bedtime, and avoid hot things for one or two *shichen* (two to four hours).

## Notes

Bu'er wan: for all diarrhoea and dysentery, cold or hot, red or white, that drags on or keeps recurring, with abdominal pain.
- Highly toxic drugs (croton seed, arsenic trioxide, red lead, momordica seed and others) made into tiny wax pills; the vehicle changes with the kind of stool. Not used today.
