# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 023 麦門冬散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Maimendong san (Bakumondosan; Ophiopogon Powder)

It treats all cases in men and women of pent-up, accumulated pathogenic heat with vexation and oppression of the heart and chest, dry throat and parched mouth, and unquiet sleep; or blockage of the large and small intestines and sores of the mouth and tongue.

xiaocao (polygala sprout; core removed)　huanglian (oren; coptis; fibrous roots removed)　shengma (shoma; cimicifuga; coarse bark removed)　xijiao (saikaku; rhinoceros horn shavings)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried)　zhiqiao (bitter orange; pulp removed, stir-fried yellow)　huangqin (ogon; scutellaria)　daqing (isatis leaf; roots removed; half a liang each)　mangxiao (bosho; refined mirabilite; 1 liang)　maimendong (bakumondo; ophiopogon; core removed; 3 *fen*)

Make the above into a fine powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one *zhan* of water, decoct down to seven-tenths and take it warm after meals.

## Notes

Maimendong san (bureau additions): for vexation of the chest, dry mouth and throat and insomnia from accumulated pathogenic heat, and for blocked bowels and urine and mouth sores.
- Coptis, scutellaria, cimicifuga, rhinoceros horn and isatis clear heat; mirabilite and bitter orange purge; ophiopogon, polygala sprout and licorice are added.
