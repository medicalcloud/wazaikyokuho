# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷下 027 論癰疽発背 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On the various patterns of abscesses and flat-abscesses.

(With sores and tinea appended.)

On abscesses, flat-abscesses and effusions of the back. For the various red, swollen toxins, whether on the limbs, hands, feet, head or face, as soon as they arise give Loulu tang, Sanxian san, Tuoli san, Bao'an gao, Ruxiang neixiao gao, Daochi wan, Sishun yin or Tongqi san. For abscesses with constipation, give Maren wan or Sanhuang wan; for the robust, give Shengong wan; only after three to five bowel movements does the toxic qi recede. For those already burst with pus and blood not flowing freely, give Sanxian san, Loulu tang or Tuoli san, and apply Bao'an gao or Ruxiang neixiao gao. For sore openings that long fail to close, give Taoren san or Qilin san.

## Notes

The first discussion under "On the various patterns of abscesses (with sores and tinea appended)": abscesses and effusions of the back. For early red swelling, Loulu tang, Sanxian san, Tuoli san, Bao'an gao and others; for constipation, Maren wan, Sanhuang wan or (in the robust) Shengong wan, purging several times to drive out the toxin; for burst abscesses with poor drainage, Sanxian san, Loulu tang and Tuoli san with plasters; Taoren san or Qilin san for wounds that will not close.
