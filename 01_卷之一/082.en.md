# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 082 左経丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zuojing wan (Meridian-Assisting Pill)

It treats paralysis of the left or right side, trembling of the hands and feet, halting and sluggish speech, pain all over the body, cramping of the sinews and vessels with inability to bend and stretch, rigidity of the neck and back, [pathogen] pouring down into the legs and knees with difficulty walking, and vexing pain of the joints with inability to turn over; as well as falls, blows and sprains with external wounds and internal injury. Taken regularly it frees the meridians, quickens the blood, disperses wind and smooths qi, strengthens the bones and lightens the body.

raw black soybeans (1 jin, boiled with 21 blister beetles, heads and legs removed, until the beans swell; discard the beetles and use the beans, dried over a fire)　chuanwu (Sichuan aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed; 2 liang)　ruxiang (frankincense; ground; 2 liang)　moyao (myrrh; 1½ liang)　caowu (wild aconite; blast-fried; 4 liang)

Powder the above and form pills the size of wutong seeds with vinegar paste. Take thirty pills each time with warm wine, at any time.

## Notes

Zuojing wan (bureau additions): a different formula from the earlier one of the same name (juan 1, no. 065), for paralysis, trembling, general pain, cramping and difficulty walking, and for blows and sprains.
- Black soybeans boiled with blister beetles (which are discarded) are combined with Sichuan and wild aconite to dispel wind-cold and frankincense and myrrh to move the blood.
