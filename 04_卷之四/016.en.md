# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之四 016 丁香半夏丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Dingxiang banxia wan (Clove and Pinellia Pill)

It treats chronic cold of the spleen and stomach with phlegm lodged in the chest and diaphragm, vomiting and nausea, acid swallowing and sour belching, glomus and fullness of the heart and abdomen, stabbing pain of the flanks and ribs, shortness of breath with choking oppression, and no desire for food.

roudoukou ren (nutmeg)　muxiang (costus root)　dingxiang (clove)　renshen (ginseng)　chenpi (aged tangerine peel; white pith removed; 1 *fen* each)　huoxiang (agastache; leaves; half a liang)　banxia (pinellia; soaked seven times in hot water, stir-fried with ginger juice; 3 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of adzuki beans with flour paste boiled in ginger juice. Take twenty pills each time with ginger decoction, at any time.

## Notes

Dingxiang banxia wan: for vomiting, nausea, acid reflux, glomus, stabbing flank pain, shortness of breath and poor appetite from chronic spleen-stomach cold with phlegm lodged in the chest.
- Based on pinellia, with clove, nutmeg, costus, agastache and tangerine peel to warm and move qi, and ginseng to supplement.
