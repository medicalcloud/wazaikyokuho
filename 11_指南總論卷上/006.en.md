# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷上 006 論服薬食忌 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On food prohibitions when taking medicines.

With atractylodes, do not eat peaches, plums, sparrow meat, coriander, garlic, pickled black carp and the like.
With veratrum, do not eat raccoon-dog meat.
With croton seed, do not eat reed-shoot soup or wild boar meat.
With coptis or platycodon, do not eat pork.
With pinellia or acorus, do not eat malt sugar or mutton.
With rehmannia, do not eat elm seed paste.
With asarum, do not eat raw vegetables.
With asparagus root, do not eat carp.
With licorice, do not eat Chinese cabbage or seaweed.
With moutan, do not eat raw coriander.
With pokeweed root, do not eat dog meat.
With dichroa, do not eat raw scallion or raw vegetables.
With azurite or cinnabar, do not eat raw blood.
With poria, do not eat vinegared things.
With turtle shell, do not eat amaranth.
When taking medicine, do not eat much raw coriander, garlic or mixed raw vegetables; nor slippery things or fruits; nor much fatty pork, dog meat, oily, greasy soups, raw fish slices or rank-smelling things.
When taking medicine, in general avoid seeing corpses or the defilements of women in childbirth.

## Notes

On food prohibitions when taking medicines: a list of foods forbidden with particular drugs (atractylodes with peaches, plums, sparrow, coriander and garlic; licorice with Chinese cabbage and seaweed; poria with vinegar; etc.) and general prohibitions during medication (raw coriander, garlic, raw vegetables, slippery foods, fruit, fatty meats and fish; seeing corpses or postpartum defilement).
- Based on the "food prohibitions when taking medicines" section of the preface of the *Bencao jing jizhu* and similar sources.
