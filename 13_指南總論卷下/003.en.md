# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷下 003 論乾湿脚気 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On dry and damp leg qi. Leg qi is of several kinds: that with unbearable pain is called cold; that with vexing oppression and thirst is called heat; that with swelling and heaviness is called damp. Treat according to the pattern; it cannot be discussed as one. For damp swelling, give Huangqi jianzhong tang or Xiao xuming tang. For wind-damp, give Zhufu tang. For heat with thirst, give Zixue. For surging into the heart with oppression, give Sanhe san, Maren wan or Jiangqi tang. For pain thrusting into the lower back, give Da wuchen tang.

## Notes

On dry and damp leg qi: unbearable pain is cold, vexing oppression with thirst is heat, swelling with heaviness is damp. Huangqi jianzhong tang and Xiao xuming tang for damp swelling; Zhufu tang for wind-damp; Zixue for heat with thirst; Sanhe san, Maren wan and Jiangqi tang for surging into the heart; Da wuchen tang for lumbar pain.
