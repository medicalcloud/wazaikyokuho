# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 038 虎骨散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Hugu san (Kokotsusan; Tiger Bone Powder)

It treats wind-toxin pathogenic qi that takes advantage of deficiency to attack and pour into the space between the skin and the bone marrow, contending with the blood and qi and striking back and forth, so that the pain has no fixed place but wanders unsettled, is quiet by day and severe at night, allowing little sleep, with cramping of the sinews and vessels and inability to bend and stretch. Also called Ruxiang chentong san (Frankincense Pain-Chasing Powder).

cang'erzi (xanthium fruit; lightly stir-fried)　gusuibu (drynaria)　zirantong (pyrite; quenched in wine, finely ground)　qilinjie (dragon's blood; finely ground)　baifuzi (typhonium; blast-fried)　chi shaoyao (shakuyaku; red peony root; 3 liang each)　danggui (toki; Chinese angelica; sprouts removed)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　baizhi (byakushi; angelica dahurica)　moyao (myrrh)　fangfeng (bofu; saposhnikovia; sprouts removed; 3 *fen* each)　niuxi (goshitsu; achyranthes; sprouts removed, soaked overnight in wine)　wujiapi (gokahi; acanthopanax bark)　tianma (gastrodia; rhizome head removed)　binglang (areca)　qianghuo (notopterygium; rhizome head removed; 1 liang each)　tiger shin bone (roasted with ghee)　baigui (old tortoise shell; roasted with ghee; 2 liang each)

Pound and sieve the above medicines into a powder and mix in the ground medicines evenly. Take 1 *qian* each time mixed in warm wine, at any time.

## Notes

Hugu san (also Ruxiang chentong san): for wind toxin entering the sinews and bones through deficiency, with wandering pain worse at night and cramping (close to so-called "white tiger joint wind wind").
- Tiger shin bone, tortoise shell, achyranthes, acanthopanax and drynaria strengthen sinews and bones; dragon's blood, myrrh, pyrite, red peony and angelica move the blood; saposhnikovia, notopterygium, gastrodia and xanthium dispel wind.
