# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 067 七生丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qisheng wan (Seven Raw Ingredients Pill)

It treats men and women with the thirty-six kinds of wind, the five kinds of lumbar pain, injuries from blows with pain penetrating the bones, stiffness of the back and shoulders, stubborn numbness of the hands and feet that runs about unsettled, cramping and contraction of the sinews and vessels, and long-standing wind disease. It treats all of these.

dilong (earthworm; earth removed)　wulingzhi (flying-squirrel droppings; stones removed)　songzhi (pine resin; wood removed)　jingjie (schizonepeta; twigs and stalks removed)　chuanwu (Sichuan aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　tiannanxing (arisaema; blast-fried; 1 liang each)　caowu (wild aconite; blast-fried, skin and tips removed; 2 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with flour paste boiled in vinegar. Take five to seven pills each time with tea or wine as preferred. Pregnant women must not take it.

## Notes

Qisheng wan (Wu Zhige's additions): for many kinds of wind, lumbar pain, bone-deep pain after injury, stiff back and shoulders, numbness, wandering pain and cramping.
- Seven ingredients, close to the preceding Huoluo dan: wild and Sichuan aconite, arisaema and earthworm with flying-squirrel droppings, pine resin and schizonepeta. Contraindicated in pregnancy.
