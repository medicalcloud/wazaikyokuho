# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之七 020 草竜胆散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cao longdan san (Gentian Powder)

It treats all of the following: sudden red swelling and pain of the eyes, heat of wind qi surging upward, pain of the eye extending to the orbit, red festering of the lids and canthi, static flesh invading the eye and frequent hot tears; counterflow damage to liver qi from shouting and anger; damage to the strength of the eyes from long staring; rough pain from wind, sand, dust and earth entering the eyes, resulting in internal or external obstructions and nebulae; and all eye diseases.

jilizi (tribulus; stir-fried, spines removed)　cao longdan (gentian; 6 liang each)　chi shaoyao (red peony root; half a jin)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried)　qianghuo (notopterygium)　fangfeng (saposhnikovia; forks and branches removed; 3 liang each)　juhua (chrysanthemum flower; twigs removed; half a liang)　fuling (poria; skin removed; 4 liang)

Pound the above into a powder. Take 2 *qian* each time after meals and at bedtime mixed in warm wine.

## Notes

Cao longdan san (bureau additions): for sudden red swelling and pain, pain spreading from the eye to the orbit, festering lids, static flesh and hot tears from wind-heat surging up; for damage to liver qi from anger; for eye strain; for internal and external obstructions after sand or dust enters the eye; and for all eye diseases. A different formula from the earlier Cao longdan san (juan 7, no. 010).
- Gentian and red peony clear liver heat; tribulus and chrysanthemum brighten the eyes; notopterygium and saposhnikovia disperse wind; poria and licorice are added.
