# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 040 脾約麻仁丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Piyue maren wan (Spleen-Constraint Hemp Seed Pill)

It treats dry, rough intestines and stomach, depleted, scant fluids, hard stools or constipation without movement, distension and fullness of the navel and abdomen and stiffness of the lower back and spine, as well as dry, bound stools in people with wind. It also treats copious frequent urination with stools hardened as a result but no thirst; this is called spleen constraint, and this medicine governs it.

houpo (magnolia bark; coarse bark removed, stir-fried with ginger juice)　shaoyao (peony root)　zhishi (unripe bitter orange; stir-fried with bran; half a jin each)　dahuang (rhubarb; steamed, dried over a fire; 1 jin)　xingren (apricot kernel; skin and tips removed, stir-fried, ground)　maren (hemp seed; ground separately; 5 liang each)

Pound and sift the above, knead with honey and form pills the size of wutong seeds. Take twenty pills each time at bedtime with warm water, until the bowels move; if they do not, take again.

## Notes

Piyue maren wan: for hard stools, constipation, umbilical distension and stiff back from dry intestines and stomach with depleted fluids, for constipation in people with wind, and for "spleen constraint" (copious urination with hard stools as a result). Still used today as Maziren wan for habitual constipation, especially in the elderly.
- The same composition as the *Shanghan lun* Maziren wan (hemp seed, peony, unripe bitter orange, rhubarb, magnolia bark, apricot kernel).
