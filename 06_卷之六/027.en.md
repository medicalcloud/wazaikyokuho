# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 027 朝真丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Chaozhen dan (Audience-with-the-Perfected Elixir)

It treats weak, deficient intestines and stomach that have received wind-cold internally, or internal damage of the spleen and stomach from raw or cold food and drink, with sudden pouring diarrhea without limit day and night, borborygmus and abdominal pain, and cold hands and feet.

liuhuang (sulfur; raw, finely ground; 30 liang)　zhusha (cinnabar; ground, for coating; 3 liang 1 *qian*)　baifan (alum; calcined; 7½ liang)

Grind the above evenly, form pills the size of wutong seeds with steamed cake soaked in water, and coat them with the cinnabar. Take thirty pills each time with warm rice water, at any time. In summer it should be kept ready for emergencies.

## Notes

Chaozhen dan: for sudden heavy diarrhea, borborygmus, abdominal pain and cold limbs when weak intestines and stomach receive wind-cold or the spleen and stomach are damaged by raw or cold food.
- Sulfur warms, calcined alum astringes, cinnabar forms the coating. To be kept ready for sudden diarrhea in summer.
