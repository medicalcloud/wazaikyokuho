# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 028 黄耆六一湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangqi liuyi tang (Astragalus Six-to-One Decoction)

It greatly treats men and women with insufficiency from the various deficiencies, with weary limbs, vexing palpitation in the chest, constant parching thirst, dry lips and mouth, a withered yellow complexion and inability to eat. It is especially suitable for those who first become thirsty and then are about to develop sores and boils, or who become thirsty after suffering abscesses. Taken regularly it supplements qi and blood evenly and calms and harmonizes the zang-fu organs.

huangqi (astragalus; rhizome head removed, roasted with honey; 6 liang)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried; 1 liang)

Cut the above into pieces. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one *zhan* of water and one jujube, decoct down to seven-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm, at any time.

## Notes

Huangqi liuyi tang (Baoqing-era additions): for lassitude, palpitation, thirst, dry lips, sallow complexion and poor appetite from insufficiency, and for thirst before sores and boils break out or after abscesses.
- Two ingredients, astragalus and licorice, in the ratio 6:1 (hence the name).
