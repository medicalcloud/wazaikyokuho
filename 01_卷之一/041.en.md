# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 041 加減三五七散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jiajian sanwuqi san (Modified Three-Five-Seven Powder)

It treats the eight winds and five impediments with paralysis and limp, dragging limbs, deviation of the mouth and eyes, pulling at the corners of the eyebrows, stiffness of the neck and back, clenched jaws, clouded oppression in the heart, a drunken look, fever over the whole body, vexing pain of the joints, numbness of the flesh, insensibility of the lower back and knees, and twitching of the skin or a feeling of crawling insects. It also treats headache from yang deficiency, with wind-cold entering the brain, the eyes spinning as if on a boat, and ringing in the ears like cicadas or like wind and rain. It can treat all wind-cold-damp impediment, leg qi with slackness and weakness, and the like. (This is Da sanwuqi san.)

shanzhuyu (sanshuyu; cornus)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried)　fuling (bukuryo; poria; skin removed; 3 jin each)　fuzi (bushi; aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed; 35 pieces)　xixin (saishin; asarum; 1 jin 8 liang)　fangfeng (bofu; saposhnikovia; rhizome head removed; 4 jin)

Make the above into a fine powder. Take 2 *qian* each time mixed in warm wine, before meals.

## Notes

Jiajian sanwuqi san (= Da sanwuqi san), in the layer of formulas added in the Shaoxing era: for paralysis, facial deviation and neck stiffness from wind, headache, dizziness and tinnitus from yang deficiency, wind-cold-damp impediment and leg qi.
- Aconite, dried ginger, asarum and saposhnikovia warm and scatter wind-cold; cornus supplements the liver and kidneys; poria removes damp.
