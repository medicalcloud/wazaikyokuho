# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 059 復元通気散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Fuyuan tongqi san (Origin-Restoring Qi-Freeing Powder)

It treats sores, boils, abscesses and flat-abscesses just starting to flare red and hot, with initial pain, whether the pus has already broken or not; small-intestine qi, kidney abscess and inguinal toxin (*biandu*); lumbar pain with stabbing qi; sores of the thighs and knees; and women's breast inflammation (*chuinai*).

boshang huixiang (imported fennel; stir-fried)　chuanshanjia (pangolin scales; chopped, stir-fried with clamshell powder, powder removed; 2 liang each)　nan muxiang (mokko; southern costus root; not exposed to fire; 1½ liang)　yanhusuo (corydalis; skin rubbed off)　bai qianniu (white morning-glory seed; stir-fried, powdered)　chenpi (chinpi; aged tangerine peel; white pith removed)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; stir-fried; 1 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder. Take one large *qian* each time mixed in hot wine; if the illness is in the upper body, after meals; if in the lower body, before meals. Those who do not drink wine take it mixed in decocted southern costus decoction.

## Notes

Fuyuan tongqi san (bureau additions): for early red, painful sores, boils and abscesses, whether burst or not; small-intestine qi, kidney abscess and inguinal swellings; lumbar pain with stabbing qi; sores of the thighs and knees; and breast inflammation.
- Pangolin scales open swellings; fennel, costus, corydalis and tangerine peel move qi and stop pain; white morning-glory seed purges; licorice is added.
