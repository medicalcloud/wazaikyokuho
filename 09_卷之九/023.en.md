# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 023 当帰丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Danggui wan (Chinese Angelica Pill)

It treats postpartum weakness and excessive blood loss with deficiency, exhaustion and scant qi, cramping of the navel and abdomen with pain drawing into the lower back and spine, a pale face without colour, a liking for lying down but inability to sleep, dry lips and mouth, palpitation with vexation and fatigue, alternating cold and heat of the hands and feet, a heavy head and dizzy vision, and no desire for food; or taxation damaging the *chong* and *ren* with wind-cold accumulated within, flooding and spotting dribbling without end, and, as the menses are about to come, heavy pain of the lower back and thighs and cramping pain of the navel and abdomen. It also treats men and women who have fallen from heights with static blood within, haematemesis, bloody stools and similar illnesses.

zhen puhuang (genuine typha pollen; stir-fried; 3½ *fen*)　shu gan dihuang (prepared rehmannia; 10 liang)　ejiao (donkey-hide gelatin; pounded, stir-fried dry)　danggui (Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed, lightly stir-fried)　xuduan (dipsacus)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried)　gancao (licorice; lightly roasted until red)　xiongqiong (Sichuan lovage; 4 liang each)　fuzi (aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　baizhi (angelica dahurica)　baizhu (white atractylodes)　wuzhuyu (evodia; washed seven times in hot water, lightly stir-fried; 3 liang each)　rougui (cinnamon bark; bark removed)　bai shaoyao (white peony root; 2 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder, knead with refined honey and form pills the size of wutong seeds. Take twenty pills each time before meals with warm wine, gradually increasing to fifty.

## Notes

Danggui wan: for postpartum weakness and heavy blood loss with breathlessness, umbilical cramping with back pain, pallor, insomnia, dry mouth, palpitation, alternating cold and heat of the extremities, heavy head, dizziness and poor appetite; for flooding from taxation damage and wind-cold; for premenstrual back, thigh and umbilical pain; and for static blood, haematemesis and bloody stools after falls.
- Mainly prepared rehmannia; angelica, lovage, white peony, donkey-hide gelatin, typha pollen and dipsacus supplement and stop bleeding; aconite, dried ginger, cinnamon and evodia warm; atractylodes, angelica dahurica and licorice are added.
