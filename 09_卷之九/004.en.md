# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 004 人参荊芥散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Renshen jingjie san (Ninjinkeigaisan; Ginseng and Schizonepeta Powder)

It treats women with blood wind and taxation qi, body pain, a clouded head and rough eyes, palpitation with vexation and fatigue, chills and fever with night sweats, red cheeks and dry mouth, phlegm cough with chest fullness and an unrefreshed spirit; or irregular menses, gripping pain of the navel and abdomen, hard string and aggregation lumps with pain that comes and goes; or occasional vomiting counterflow and inability to eat; or improper care after childbirth with prolonged emaciation and haggardness, getting up and lying down by turns, and in severe cases bedridden.

jingjie sui (schizonepeta spikes)　lingyangjiao (reiyokaku; antelope horn; shaved)　suanzaoren (sansonin; jujube seed; lightly stir-fried)　sheng gan dihuang (kanjio; dried raw rehmannia)　zhiqiao (bitter orange; stir-fried with bran, pulp removed, then weighed)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng)　biejia (bekko; turtle shell; soaked in vinegar, rim removed, roasted yellow)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　baizhu (byakujutsu; white atractylodes)　chaihu (saiko; bupleurum; 7½ liang each)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; chopped, scorched)　xiongqiong (Sichuan lovage)　chi shaoyao (shakuyaku; red peony root)　mudanpi (moutan bark)　danggui (toki; Chinese angelica)　fangfeng (bofu; saposhnikovia; shoots and forks removed; 5 liang each)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water and three slices of fresh ginger, decoct down to eight-tenths, remove the dregs and take it hot, at any time, twice a day. Taken regularly it removes all old illnesses of wind deficiency and taxation cold. It is not suitable during pregnancy.

## Notes

Renshen jingjie san: for body pain, clouded head, rough eyes, palpitation, fatigue, chills and fever, night sweats, red cheeks, dry mouth, phlegm cough and chest fullness from blood wind and taxation qi in women; for irregular menses, gripping umbilical pain and hard aggregation lumps; for vomiting and poor appetite; and for prolonged wasting after improper postpartum care. Not for use in pregnancy.
- Bupleurum, turtle shell, antelope horn, moutan and red peony clear deficiency heat; ginseng, atractylodes, angelica, lovage, raw rehmannia and jujube seed supplement; schizonepeta and saposhnikovia dispel wind; cinnamon, bitter orange and licorice are added.
