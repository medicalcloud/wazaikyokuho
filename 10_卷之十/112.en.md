# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 112 縮砂湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Suosha tang (Shukushato; Amomum Drink)

It treats all cold qi with stabbing pain of the heart and abdomen, stuffiness and oppression of the chest and diaphragm, distension and fullness of the flanks and abdomen, vomiting counterflow and nausea, tasteless food, disharmony of the spleen and stomach, and much damage by wine and food with unceasing vomiting.

suoshaliren (amomum seed; not exposed to fire)　gancao (kanzo) [licorice; stir-fried; 12 liang each (one version has 2 liang each)]　jiegeng (kikyo; platycodon; dried over a fire; 60 liang)　dingxiang pi (clove bark; not exposed to fire; 6 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder. Take 1 *qian* each time with a little salt, mixed with boiling water, before meals. Taken regularly it disperses stagnant qi, loosens the chest and diaphragm, strengthens the spleen and stomach, improves eating and stops vomiting.

## Notes

Suosha tang (Baoqing-era additions): a drink for stabbing abdominal pain, chest stuffiness, distension, vomiting, poor appetite and disharmony of the spleen and stomach from cold qi, and for unceasing vomiting after wine and food.
- Mainly platycodon, with amomum, clove bark and licorice.
