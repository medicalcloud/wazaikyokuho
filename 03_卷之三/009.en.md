# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 009 五膈丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wuge wan (Five Diaphragm Disorders Pill)

It treats the illnesses of worry diaphragm, qi diaphragm, food diaphragm, retained fluid diaphragm and taxation diaphragm that arise when sorrow, worry, thought and anxiety, together with irregular eating and drinking, stir the qi and injure the spirit, so that yin and yang are in disharmony, illness arises in the zang-fu organs and binds in the chest and diaphragm. Eating raw or cold things brings on an attack: the heart and chest are blocked and full, qi cannot pass, the pain is like stabbing and draws into the spine, food will not go down, there is hard pain below the heart, and pain brings a desire to vomit, which stops it once one vomits. In severe cases the hands and feet are counterflow cold, with rising qi, coughing counterflow, dyspnea and shortness of breath.

shujiao (shokusho) (Sichuan pepper; seeds and unopened ones removed, lightly stir-fried to remove the oil)　xixin (saishin; asarum; sprouts and earth removed)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　yuanzhi (onji; polygala; core removed; 3 liang each)　maimendong (bakumondo; ophiopogon; core removed, dried over a fire)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried; 5 liang each)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried; 2 liang)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng; rhizome head removed; 4 liang)　fuzi (bushi; aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed; 1½ liang)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of a pellet with refined honey. Hold one pill in the mouth to dissolve and swallow it; the chest, diaphragm and throat should feel hot. When the medicine's force has somewhat waned, take another pill; three doses by day and two by night; after seven days of taking it the patient recovers. They may also be made into pills the size of wutong seeds and taken with warm wine, after meals.

## Notes

Wuge wan: for the five diaphragm disorders (worry, qi, food, retained fluid, taxation) in which worry, anxiety and irregular diet bind qi in the chest, with attacks brought on by cold food—chest obstruction, stabbing pain radiating to the back, difficulty swallowing and vomiting.
- From the lineage of the Wuge wan of the *Qianjin fang*: Sichuan pepper, asarum, cinnamon, dried ginger and aconite warm; ginseng, ophiopogon, licorice and polygala supplement. It is dissolved in the mouth.
