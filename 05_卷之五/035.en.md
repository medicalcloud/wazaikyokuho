# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 035 楽令建中湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Leling jianzhong tang (Leling's Center-Fortifying Decoction)

It treats taxation damage of blood and qi, deficiency and damage of the five zang organs and six fu organs, borborygmus and a weary spirit, and disharmony of the ying-nutrient and wei-defense qi; it dispels deficiency heat and removes the hundred illnesses.

qianhu (peucedanum)　xixin (saishin; asarum)　huangqi (ogi; astragalus; smeared with honey and roasted)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng)　guixin (cinnamon heart)　jupi (kippi; tangerine peel; white pith removed)　danggui (toki; Chinese angelica; washed free of earth)　bai shaoyao (shakuyaku; white peony root)　fuling (bukuryo; poria; skin removed)　maimendong (bakumondo; ophiopogon; core removed)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried; 1 liang each)　banxia (hange; pinellia; washed seven times in hot water, sliced; 7½ *qian*)

Cut the above into pieces. For each dose take 4 *qian* with four slices of ginger, one jujube and one *zhan* of water, decoct down to seven-tenths, remove the dregs and take it slightly hot, at any time.

## Notes

Leling jianzhong tang (Chunyou-era additions): for borborygmus, fatigue, disharmony of the ying-nutrient and wei-defense qi and deficiency heat from taxation damage of blood and qi and deficiency of the zang organs.
- Huangqi jianzhong tang (Ogikenchuto) (astragalus, cinnamon, peony, licorice) plus ginseng, angelica, poria, ophiopogon, pinellia, tangerine peel, peucedanum and asarum (in the lineage of the Leling huangqi tang of the *Qianjin fang*).
