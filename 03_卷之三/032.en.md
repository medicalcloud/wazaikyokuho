# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 032 九痛丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jiutong wan (Nine Pains Pill)

It treats the nine kinds of heart pain: (1) worm heart pain; (2) infixation heart pain; (3) wind heart pain; (4) palpitation heart pain; (5) food heart pain; (6) retained fluid heart pain; (7) cold heart pain; (8) heat heart pain; (9) coming-and-going heart pain. It also treats heart and chest pain flowing and pouring for years, and also treats cold surging with rising qi, falls from horses and carriages, static blood and similar conditions.

langdu (rodoku; stellera root; roasted until fragrant; 1 liang)　fuzi (bushi; aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed; 3 liang)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried)　badou (hazu; croton seeds; skin, core and membranes removed, stir-fried dry, frost taken)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng)　wuzhuyu (goshuyu; evodia; washed seven times in hot water; 1 liang each)

Make these six ingredients into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with refined honey. Take one pill each time with warm wine on an empty stomach. For sudden malign-qi strike with distending pain of the heart and abdomen and inability to speak, two pills bring immediate recovery.

## Notes

Jiutong wan: for the nine kinds of heart pain (worm, infixation, wind, palpitation, food, retained fluid, cold, heat, coming-and-going), long-standing chest pain, rising cold, static blood after falls, and sudden malign-qi abdominal pain.
- The same six ingredients as the Jiutong wan appended to the chapter on chest bi-impediment in the *Jingui yaolüe* (stellera, aconite, dried ginger, croton, ginseng, evodia).
