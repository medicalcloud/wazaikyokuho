# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 007 紫蘇子丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zisuzi wan (Shisoshigan; Perilla Seed Pill)

It treats all qi counterflow with choking oppression of the chest and diaphragm, stabbing pain of the heart and abdomen, distension and fullness of the flanks and ribs, undigested food and retching with a desire to vomit; as well as cold damage to the lungs and stomach with cough, stuffiness and fullness, or qi rushing upward urgently so that one cannot lie down in peace.

zisuzi (perilla seed; picked clean)　chenpi (chinpi; aged tangerine peel; white pith removed; 2 liang each)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng; rhizome head removed)　gaoliangjiang (lesser galangal; stir-fried; 1 liang each)

Make these five ingredients into a fine powder and form pills the size of a pellet with refined honey. Take one pill each time, chewed thoroughly, with warm wine; rice gruel is also fine; at any time. They may also be made into small pills. If after eating melons, raw fish, or raw and cold foods one feels some injury, with belching smelling of half-digested food, as if about to develop sudden turmoil, hold one pill in the mouth to dissolve and swallow the juice little by little; when it is finished, recovery is immediate. Taking this medicine regularly, one never suffers sudden turmoil; it is excellent.

## Notes

Zisuzi wan: for chest obstruction, heart and abdominal pain, flank distension, indigestion and retching from qi counterflow, and for cough and dyspnea from cold damage to the lungs and stomach; it is also held in the mouth at the first signs of sudden turmoil from raw and cold food.
- Perilla seed and tangerine peel send qi down, cinnamon and galangal warm, and ginseng supplements.
