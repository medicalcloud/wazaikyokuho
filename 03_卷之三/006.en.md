# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 006 和胃丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Hewei wan (Stomach-Harmonizing Pill)

It treats disharmony of the spleen and stomach with glomus of qi in the middle duct, distension and oppression of the heart and abdomen, no desire for food, vomiting with phlegm counterflow, belching and acid swallowing, a withered yellow complexion, emaciated flesh, stabbing pain of the abdomen and flanks, irregular bowels, little strength with a liking for lying down, and a heavy body with painful joints; and deficiency taxation with weak spleen and stomach, undigested food and glomus and fullness of the heart and abdomen. It is suitable for all of these. Old and young and those with weak qi may all take this medicine regularly; it warms and harmonizes the spleen and stomach and regulates and improves eating.

houpo (magnolia bark; coarse bark removed, crushed, stir-fried together with 2 liang of fresh ginger ground to a pulp)　banxia (pinellia; half washed in hot water, sun-dried and lightly stir-fried; half processed with ginger juice into cakes and roasted yellow)　biejia (turtle shell; one large nine-ribbed shell, coated outside with yellow mud; dissolve 1 liang of sal ammoniac in two bowls of rice vinegar, put it inside the shell and simmer dry over a gentle fire; take 2 liang and grind fine as powder for use)　shenqu (medicated leaven; crushed, stir-fried)　mainie (barley sprouts; lightly stir-fried)　baizhu (white atractylodes; chopped, stir-fried)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed; 2 liang each)　zhiqiao (bitter orange; pulp removed, stir-fried with bran)　sanleng (sparganium; blast-fried)　qingpi (green tangerine peel; white removed, stir-fried)　renshen (ginseng; 3 liang each)　chenpi (aged tangerine peel; white pith removed)　hezi (chebule; blast-fried, stone removed; 4 liang each)　binglang (areca)　danggui (Chinese angelica; 1½ liang each)　shaoyao (peony root)　gancao (licorice; stir-fried; 1 liang each)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried)　chi fuling (red poria; skin removed; 3 *fen* each)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of adzuki beans with honey. Take twenty pills each time, increasing to thirty, slightly chewed, with warm water, at any time.

## Notes

Hewei wan: for glomus, abdominal fullness, poor appetite, vomiting, acid reflux, sallow complexion, emaciation, abdominal and flank pain and irregular bowels from spleen-stomach disharmony, and for spleen-stomach weakness in deficiency taxation; it may be taken regularly even by the old and young.
- Ginseng, atractylodes, licorice and red poria supplement; magnolia bark, pinellia, tangerine peels, bitter orange and areca send qi and phlegm down; medicated leaven and barley sprouts digest food; sparganium and turtle shell (calcined with sal ammoniac) disperse accumulations; cinnamon and dried ginger warm.
