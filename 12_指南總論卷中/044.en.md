# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 044 論傷寒後黄疸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On jaundice after cold damage. For cold damage with jaundice of the eyes and whole body, inhibited urination, or sweating on the head, give Wuling san (Goreisan); chop a little capillaris and gardenia to make 2 *qian*, decoct, and mix the powder in the decoction; taken in quantity it is effective.

## Notes

On jaundice after cold damage: for jaundice of the eyes and body with inhibited urination or head sweating, Wuling san taken in a decoction of capillaris and gardenia (the idea of Yinchen wuling san).
