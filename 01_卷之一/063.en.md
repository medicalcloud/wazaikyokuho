# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 063 五痺湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wubi tang (Five Impediments Decoction)

It treats wind-cold-damp pathogen lodging in the flesh and body, with slack, weak hands and feet and numbness; or disordered qi and blood with impeded, stagnant numbness.

pianzi jianghuang (sliced turmeric; washed free of ash and earth)　qianghuo (notopterygium)　baizhu (byakujutsu; white atractylodes)　fangji (boi; stephania; 1 liang each)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; lightly mix-fried; half a liang)

Cut the above into pieces. For each dose take 4 *qian* by weight with one and a half *zhan* of water and ten slices of fresh ginger, decoct down to eight-tenths and remove the dregs. If the illness is in the upper body take it after meals; if in the lower body, before meals.

## Notes

Wubi tang (Chunyou-era additions): for slackness and numbness of the limbs from wind-cold-damp, and numbness from disordered qi and blood.
- Turmeric moves the blood, notopterygium and stephania dispel wind-damp, and atractylodes dries damp; the timing depends on the location of the illness.
