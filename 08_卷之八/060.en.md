# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 060 排膿托裡散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Painong tuoli san (Pus-Expelling Interior-Supporting Powder)

It treats all sores, boils and abscess toxins, as well as intestinal abscess and flat-abscess of the back, red and swollen but not yet broken, or already broken with pus and blood not dispersing, fever of the whole body and unbearable pain. It also treats women's breast abscess and all toxic swellings; all should take it.

di wugong (goko; ground-centipede herb)　chi shaoyao (shakuyaku; red peony root)　danggui (toki; Chinese angelica)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; equal parts)

Make the above into a fine powder. Take 2 *qian* each time mixed in warm wine, at any time.

## Notes

Painong tuoli san (bureau additions): for all sores, boils, abscesses, intestinal abscess and flat-abscess of the back, whether broken or not, with undischarged pus and blood, generalized fever and severe pain; also for breast abscess and all toxic swellings.
- Four ingredients (ground-centipede herb, red peony, angelica, licorice) taken in wine.
