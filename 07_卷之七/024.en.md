# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之七 024 秘伝羊肝丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Michuan yanggan wan (Secretly Transmitted Sheep's Liver Pill)

It treats men and women with insufficiency of the liver channel and wind toxin attacking upward, dim, dark eyes with tearing, aversion to light and fear of the sun, gritty roughness making the eyes hard to open, itching or pain. It also treats internal and external obstructions of the eye of many years' or recent standing, and proliferating flesh climbing over the eye that cannot be cured by needling and scraping.

bai yangzi gan (white lamb's liver; one, washed clean, membranes removed)　huanglian (coptis; fibrous roots removed, pounded and sifted into powder)

First pound the sheep's liver in an earthenware bowl until soft, then gradually add the coptis powder, mixing and grinding until suitably moist, and form pills the size of wutong seeds. Take fourteen pills each time after meals, swallowed with warm sour rice water. After five successive prescriptions it heals. It governs all eye diseases, obstructions and nebulae, and green blindness (blindness without visible change). Pork and cold water are forbidden. (Many eye formulas use coptis, but this one is the most remarkable. Liu Yuxi says: There was a man named Cui Chengyuan who, in office, tried a prisoner condemned to death and saved his life; some years later the prisoner died of an eye disease. One day Cui was suddenly afflicted with internal obstruction and lost his sight for over a year. Later, sitting alone sighing in the middle of the night, he heard a rustling sound by the steps. Cui asked, "Who is it?" The answer came: "I am the prisoner whose life you once saved; I have come to repay your kindness." Having told him this formula, it vanished. Cui made up the formula and took it, and within a few months his sight was restored; he therefore passed the formula on to the world.)

## Notes

Michuan yanggan wan (bureau additions): for dim vision, tearing, photophobia, difficulty opening, itching and pain from an insufficient liver channel with wind toxin; for internal and external obstructions, old or new; for proliferating flesh not cured by needling and scraping; and for all eye diseases, obstructions and green blindness.
- Two ingredients, white lamb's liver and coptis, pounded together into pills. The note recounts Liu Yuxi's story of Cui Chengyuan, who received the formula from the spirit of a prisoner whose life he had saved and was cured of blindness.
