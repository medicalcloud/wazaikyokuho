# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 018 消毒犀角飲 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xiaodu xijiao yin (Toxin-Dispersing Rhinoceros Horn Beverage)

It treats all of the following in adults and children: evil heat pent up within with blockage of the throat and diaphragm, cough with congested phlegm and drool, red eyes and swollen lids, nodes in the cheeks and neck, abscess swellings with gathered toxin, wind rashes over the whole body, red papules of dan-toxin, and sores and rashes, whether erupted or not yet erupted, that fail to come out fully. When children's rashes and pox are about to erupt, or have erupted but the heat does not resolve, give three or four doses of this medicine urgently, and they come out fully and the toxin is dispersed, with divine effect as soon as it is applied.

fangfeng (saposhnikovia; shoots removed; 8 liang)　jingjie sui (schizonepeta spikes)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried; 16 liang each)　shunianzi (arctium seed; stir-fried; 64 liang)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one *zhan* of water, decoct down to seven-tenths, remove the dregs and take it pleasantly warm after meals.

## Notes

Xiaodu xijiao yin (Wu Zhige's additions): for throat blockage, phlegm cough, red eyes, swollen lids, neck nodes, abscesses and wind rashes from pent-up evil heat, and for eruptions (children's pox and rashes) that fail to come out fully.
- Mainly arctium seed (burdock seed), with saposhnikovia and schizonepeta to vent the rash and licorice. Although named after rhinoceros horn, the formula in the base text contains none.
