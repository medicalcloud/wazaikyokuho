# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之四 006 玉液丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yuye wan (Jade Liquid Pill)

It treats wind congestion, transforms phlegm and drool, frees the throat and diaphragm, clears the head and eyes, removes cough and stops vexing heat.

hanshuishi (kansuiseki; calcite; burned red to release its great toxin, water-ground; 30 liang)　baifan (alum; calcined and finely ground)　banxia (hange; pinellia; washed seven times in hot water and finely powdered; 10 liang each)

Grind the above together and form pills the size of wutong seeds with white flour paste. Take ten pills each time with warm ginger decoction, after meals and at bedtime; thirty pills per dose is also fine.

## Notes

Yuye wan: for wind congestion, phlegm, throat and diaphragm discomfort, heavy head, cough and vexing heat.
- Three ingredients: calcite clears heat; alum and pinellia transform phlegm.
