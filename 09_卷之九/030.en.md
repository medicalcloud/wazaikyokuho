# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 030 蒲黄散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Puhuang san (Hoosan; Typha Pollen Powder)

It treats postpartum lochia that does not flow freely, blood surging up against the heart, vexation, oppression, fullness and urgency, stupor and loss of consciousness, or manic, wild speech, and dyspnea breath about to expire.

gan heye (dried lotus leaf; roasted)　mudanpi (moutan bark)　yanhusuo (corydalis)　sheng gan dihuang (kanjio; dried raw rehmannia)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried; 3 *fen* each)　puhuang (hoo; typha pollen; raw; 2 liang)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one *zhan* of water and a little honey, decoct together down to seven-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm, at any time.

## Notes

Puhuang san: for vexation, stupor, loss of consciousness, delirium and dyspnea from retained lochia with blood surging upward after childbirth.
- Mainly raw typha pollen; moutan, corydalis and lotus leaf move static blood; raw rehmannia and licorice are added; decocted with honey.
