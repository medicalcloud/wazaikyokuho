# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 079 快気湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Kuaiqi tang (Qi-Easing Decoction)

It treats all qi disorders with distension and fullness of the heart and abdomen, choking obstruction of the chest and diaphragm, belching and acid swallowing, phlegm counterflow in the stomach with vomiting, unresolved hangover and no desire for food.

suoshaner (amomum; 8 liang)　xiangfuzi (cyperus; stir-fried, hairs removed; 32 liang)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; scorched; 4 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder. Take 1 *qian* each time stirred into salt water. Taken regularly it eases qi, makes food taste good and warms and nourishes the spleen and stomach. Chopped into a coarse powder and decocted with fresh ginger, it is called Xiao jiangqi tang (Minor Qi-Lowering Decoction).

## Notes

Kuaiqi tang (Wu Zhige's additions): for abdominal fullness, chest obstruction, acid reflux, vomiting, hangover and poor appetite in qi disorders.
- Cyperus, amomum and licorice; decocted with ginger it is called Xiao jiangqi tang.
