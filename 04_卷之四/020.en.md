# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之四 020 麻黄散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Mahuang san (Ephedra Powder)

It treats men and women with lung-qi cough, long-standing or recent, with urgent panting surging upward, restlessness sitting or lying, congested phlegm and drool, thick sticky cough and spittle, cold numb feet and hands, and pain and distension of the chest and flanks. It also treats cough and panting from wind damage with discomfort above the diaphragm.

hezi pi (chebule skin; stone removed)　kuandonghua (coltsfoot flower; rhizome heads, twigs and stalks removed)　gancao (licorice; scorched; 5 liang each)　mahuang (ephedra; roots and nodes removed; 10 liang)　rougui (cinnamon bark; 6 liang, skin removed, not exposed to fire)　xingren (apricot kernel; skin and tips removed, stir-fried with bran; 3 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one *zhan* of water and 1 *qian* of good tea, decoct together down to eight-tenths and take it at a comfortable warmth after meals and when lying down at night. If one cannot decoct it in the middle of the night, simply mix the powder evenly with tea and stir it into boiling water, or swallow it dry. Avoid fish, wine, roasted and fried foods, pork and fishy foods.

## Notes

Mahuang san (from here the layer of Baoqing-era additions): for old or recent lung-qi cough with panting surging upward, phlegm, sticky sputum, cold limbs and chest and flank pain, and for cough and panting from wind damage.
- Ephedra, apricot kernel, cinnamon and licorice diffuse and warm the lungs; coltsfoot stops cough; chebule astringes the lungs. Decocted with tea.
