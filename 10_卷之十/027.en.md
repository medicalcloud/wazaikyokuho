# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 027 潤肺散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Runfei san (Lung-Moistening Powder)

It treats children in whom cold and congestion interlock, with obstructed lung qi, cough with dyspnea and hurried breathing, loss of voice, congested phlegm and drool, vexing fullness of the chest and diaphragm, nasal congestion with clear snivel, and dry, painful throat.

beimu (baimo; fritillaria; core removed, stir-fried yellow with bran)　xingren (kyonin; apricot kernel; skin, tips and twin kernels removed with hot water, dried over a fire, stir-fried with flour; 2½ liang each)　mahuang (mao; ephedra; roots and nodes removed)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng; 2 liang each)　ejiao (akyo; donkey-hide gelatin; stir-fried yellow and dry)　jiegeng (kikyo; platycodon; half a liang each)　chenpi (chinpi; aged tangerine peel; white pith removed; 1 *fen*)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried; 1 liang)

Pound and sift the above together into a coarse powder. For each dose take 1 *qian* with eight-tenths of water, decoct down to six-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm, after meals.

## Notes

Runfei san: for cough, dyspnea, loss of voice, phlegm congestion, chest fullness, nasal congestion with clear discharge and dry sore throat from interlocking cold and congestion obstructing lung qi in children.
- Ephedra and apricot kernel diffuse the lungs; fritillaria, platycodon and tangerine peel remove phlegm; donkey-hide gelatin moistens; ginseng and licorice supplement.
