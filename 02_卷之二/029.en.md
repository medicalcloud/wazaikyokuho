# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 029 和解散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Hejie san (Harmonizing and Resolving Powder)

It treats cold damage in men and women in any season with headache, aversion to cold with vigorous fever, vexation, agitation and spontaneous sweating, cough, vomiting and diarrhea.

houpo (koboku; magnolia bark; coarse bark removed, roasted with ginger juice)　chenpi (chinpi; aged tangerine peel; washed; 4 liang each)　gaoben (kohon) (Chinese lovage)　jiegeng (kikyo; platycodon)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; half a jin each)　cangzhu (black atractylodes; skin removed; 1 jin)

Make the above together into a coarse powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water, three slices of fresh ginger and two jujubes, decoct down to seven-tenths and take it hot, at any time.

## Notes

Hejie san (Shaoxing-era additions): for headache, chills and vigorous fever, agitation, spontaneous sweating, cough, vomiting and diarrhea in seasonal cold damage (colds).
- Pingwei san (Heiisan) (black atractylodes, magnolia bark, tangerine peel, licorice) plus Chinese lovage and platycodon: it harmonizes the exterior while removing damp.
