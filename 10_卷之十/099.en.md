# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 099 薄荷湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bohe tang (Hakkato; Mint Drink)

It disperses wind congestion and transforms phlegm and drool. It treats a clouded head and blurred vision, nasal congestion and dry throat, vexation and oppression of the heart and chest, and an unrefreshed spirit.

jingjie sui (schizonepeta spikes)　yan (salt; stir-fried; 3 jin each)　jisu ye (borneol mint leaf; 7½ jin)　gualougen (karokon; trichosanthes root; 11 liang)　suoshaliren (amomum seed; 3 liang)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; chopped, stir-fried; 4 jin)

Powder the above. Take 1 *qian* each time mixed with boiling water, after meals.

## Notes

Bohe tang ("Various drinks"): a drink that disperses wind congestion and transforms phlegm; for clouded head, dizziness, nasal congestion, dry throat, chest oppression and an unrefreshed spirit.
- Borneol mint and schizonepeta disperse wind; trichosanthes root moistens; amomum, licorice and salt are added.
