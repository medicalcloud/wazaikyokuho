# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 062 大醒風湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Da xingfeng tang (Major Wind-Awakening Decoction)

It treats phlegm reversal from wind stroke with surging drool and clouded dizziness, convulsions of the hands and feet and hemiplegia, as well as joint wind painful wind with cramping of the sinews and vessels.

nanxing (arisaema; raw; 8 liang)　fangfeng (bofu; saposhnikovia; raw; 4 liang)　duhuo (dokukatsu; angelica pubescens root; raw)　fuzi (bushi; aconite; raw, skin and navel removed)　quanxie (whole scorpion; lightly stir-fried)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; raw; 2 liang each)

Cut the above into pieces. For each dose take 4 *qian* by weight with two large *zhan* of water and twenty slices of fresh ginger, decoct down to eight-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm, at any time, two doses a day.

## Notes

Da xingfeng tang (Chunyou-era additions): for phlegm reversal, convulsions and hemiplegia in wind stroke, and for cramping in joint wind painful wind.
- Raw arisaema, aconite, duhuo and saposhnikovia expel wind-phlegm and cold-damp; scorpion stops convulsions; a large amount of ginger is added.
