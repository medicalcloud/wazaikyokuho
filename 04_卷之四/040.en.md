# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之四 040 人参藿香湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Renshen huoxiang tang (Ginseng and Agastache Decoction)

It treats men and women with weak spleen and stomach qi, vomiting and hiccough, inability to get food down, counterflow cold of the hands and feet and thick sticky drool and phlegm. It also treats [a state] like dyspnea yet not dyspnea, wanting to retch yet not retching, confusion reaching the heart, oppression and disorder without rest, or miasmic malaria and other illnesses in which water, broth, gruel and medicine are vomited as soon as they enter the mouth; it works at once. Long-standing stomach reflux is cured after a hundred days of taking it. This medicine warms the spleen and stomach, transforms phlegm-fluid retention, disperses chronic cold and stops vomiting.

huoxiang (agastache; stalks removed)　renshen (ginseng; sliced; 6 liang each)　banxia (hange; pinellia; washed seven times in hot water, processed with ginger juice; 2½ liang)

Pound the above into a coarse powder and mix in the ginseng evenly. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water and ten slices of fresh ginger, decoct down to one *zhan*, remove the dregs and take it at a comfortable temperature. Avoid in pregnancy.

## Notes

Renshen huoxiang tang (bureau additions): for vomiting, hiccough, inability to eat, cold limbs and sticky phlegm from weak spleen-stomach qi, for agitation, for vomiting of everything taken in miasmic malaria, and for chronic stomach reflux; avoid in pregnancy.
- Agastache, ginseng and pinellia with plenty of fresh ginger.
