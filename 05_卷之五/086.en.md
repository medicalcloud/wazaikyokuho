# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 086 巴戟丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Baji wan (Morinda Pill)

It supplements the kidney zang organ, warms the cinnabar field, raises the yang way, reduces urination, fills the essence and augments the marrow, preserves the complexion and moistens the skin. It treats deficient, exhausted original qi with a dark face and eyes, dry mouth and rough tongue, deficiency fright in dreams, cold tears from the eyes, cicada-like ringing in the ears, heaviness of the lower back and hips, aching pain in all the joints, tension of the neck sinews, weariness of the back and shoulder blades, genital sweating and night sweats, and weakness of the limbs. It also treats women with long-standing cold of the womb, irregular menses, sometimes heavy and sometimes scant, and red and white vaginal discharge; all should take it.

liangjiang (lesser galangal; 6 liang)　zijinteng (zijinteng vine; 16 liang)　baji (morinda; 3 liang)　qingyan (halite; 2 liang)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　wuzhuyu (goshuyu; evodia; 4 liang each)

Powder the above and form pills with wine paste. Take twenty pills each time with warmed salted wine; salt water is also fine; one dose at noon and one at bedtime.

## Notes

Baji wan (bureau additions): for dark complexion, dry mouth, fright, cold tears, tinnitus, heavy back, joint pain, stiff neck and back, genital sweating, night sweats and weakness from exhausted original qi, and for women's cold womb, irregular menses and discharge.
- Mainly zijinteng vine, with morinda, lesser galangal, cinnamon, evodia and halite to warm the lower origin.
