# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 054 霊砂 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Lingsha (Numinous Cinnabar)

Warm in nature and non-toxic, it governs the hundred illnesses of the five viscera, augments essence and nourishes the spirit, supplements qi and brightens the eyes, calms the ethereal and corporeal souls, frees the blood vessels, stops vexation and fullness and kills evil goblins. It is good at treating failure of the nutritive and defensive qi to interact and nourish each other, failure of yin and yang to rise and fall, exuberance above and deficiency below with dizziness and hurried breathing, cold pain of the heart and abdomen, stomach reflux and vomiting, sudden turmoil with cramps, slippery diarrhoea of the viscera and bowels, and red and white dysentery. Taken for a long time it communicates with the spirits, lightens the body, prevents ageing and makes the heart numinous. This elixir follows the method of ingestion in the immortals' scriptures and unites the marvel of the correspondences of the five phases; its substance is light and clear and does not fly off with the smoke and flames, and men and women, old and young, may all take it.

shuiyin (mercury; 1 jin)　liuhuang (sulphur; 4 liang)

Stir-fry these two ingredients in a new iron pot into granules; if smoke or flames arise, sprinkle at once with vinegar. When it has been ground fine, put it into a water-and-fire cauldron, seal the mouth with red halloysite mixed in vinegar, bind it with iron wire and dry it in the sun, seal it with salt clay and calcine it with 20 jin of charcoal. If the cauldron cracks, repeatedly paint the place with red halloysite using a brush. Stop when the fire has burned out; take it out after a night, grind into a fine powder and form pills the size of hemp seeds with glutinous rice paste. Take three pills each time on an empty stomach with jujube decoction, rice water, well-flower water or ginseng decoction as preferred; according to the severity of the illness increase to five or seven. Avoid pig's and sheep's blood, mung bean flour, and cold and slippery things.

## Notes

Lingsha (bureau additions): claimed to govern the hundred illnesses of the five viscera—dizziness and hurried breathing with exuberance above and deficiency below, cold pain, stomach reflux, sudden turmoil, diarrhoea and dysentery—and with long use to bring communion with the spirits and agelessness.
- An alchemical elixir of mercury and sulphur calcined in a cauldron (mercuric sulphide, i.e. artificial cinnabar). Toxic from mercury; not used today.
