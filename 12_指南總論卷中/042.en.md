# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 042 論傷寒後自汗 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On spontaneous sweating after cold damage. For spontaneous sweating after cold damage, take Muli san (Boreisan) or Zhihan san, together with Jiahe san or Huangqi jianzhong tang (Ogikenchuto) decocted with ginseng added; or Renshen danggui san (Ninjintokisan) decocted with wheat added.

## Notes

On spontaneous sweating after cold damage: Muli san, Zhihan san, Jiahe san, Huangqi jianzhong tang with ginseng, or Renshen danggui san with wheat.
