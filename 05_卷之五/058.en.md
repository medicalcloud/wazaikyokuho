# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 058 竜歯鎮心丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Longchi zhenxin dan (Dragon Tooth Heart-Settling Elixir)

It treats insufficient heart and kidney qi with fright palpitations and forgetfulness, unquiet sleep and dreams, seminal emission with little colour in the face, and aching pain of the shins.

longchi (dragon tooth; water-ground)　yuanzhi (polygala; core removed, stir-fried)　tianmendong (asparagus root; core removed)　shu dihuang (prepared rehmannia)　shanyao (Chinese yam; 6 liang each, stir-fried)　fushen (poria with root)　maimendong (ophiopogon; core removed)　cheqianzi (plantago seed; stir-fried)　bai fuling (white poria)　guixin (cinnamon heart)　digupi (lycium root bark)　wuweizi (schisandra; 5 liang each)

Powder the above and form pills the size of wutong seeds with honey. Take thirty to fifty pills each time on an empty stomach with warm wine or rice broth as preferred.

## Notes

Longchi zhenxin dan (bureau additions): for fright palpitations, forgetfulness, insomnia, seminal emission, poor complexion and aching shins from insufficient heart and kidney qi.
- Dragon tooth, polygala and poria with root calm the heart; rehmannia, asparagus root, ophiopogon, Chinese yam and schisandra nourish yin; poria, plantago seed, lycium root bark and cinnamon heart are added.
