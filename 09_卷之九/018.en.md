# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 018 陽起石丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yangqishi wan (Actinolite Pill)

It treats women with a deficient, cold womb, excessive taxation damage and wind-cold bound and contending, long failure to conceive and finally childlessness. Taking this medicine greatly benefits the womb and disperses accumulated cold.

yangqishi (actinolite; soaked in wine for half a day, finely ground; 2 liang)　wuzhuyu (evodia; washed seven times in hot water, dried over a fire, lightly stir-fried; 3 *fen*)　shu dihuang (prepared rehmannia; 1 liang)　niuxi (achyranthes; shoots removed, soaked in wine, dried over a fire)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried)　baizhu (white atractylodes; 3 *fen* each)

Make the above into a fine powder, knead with refined honey, pound three hundred times and form pills the size of wutong seeds. Take twenty to thirty pills each time with warm wine or warm rice water, on an empty stomach before meals, twice a day. As soon as pregnancy is felt, stop taking it.

## Notes

Yangqishi wan: for long-standing infertility from a deficient, cold womb, taxation damage and bound wind-cold; said to benefit the womb and disperse accumulated cold. Stop once pregnancy is recognized.
- Mainly actinolite; evodia and dried ginger warm; prepared rehmannia, achyranthes and atractylodes supplement.
