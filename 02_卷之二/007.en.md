# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 007 聖散子 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Sheng sanzi (Sage Powder)

It is suitable for cold damage, seasonal pestilence, wind-warmth and damp-warmth, regardless of whether yin and yang are both affected, whether exterior and interior have yet been distinguished, or whether there is heat outside and cold inside or heat inside and cold outside: with cramping pain of the head, neck, lower back and spine, fever with aversion to cold, pain and heaviness of the limbs and joints, vomiting counterflow, dyspnea and coughing, nasal congestion and a heavy voice; as well as damage to the stomach duct from raw and cold food and drink, with fullness and oppression of the chest and diaphragm, distension and pain of the abdomen and flanks, binding and stuffiness below the heart, counterflow cold of the hands and feet, borborygmus and diarrhea with undigested food, occasional spontaneous sweating and difficult urination.

houpo (koboku; magnolia bark; coarse bark removed, roasted with ginger juice)　baizhu (byakujutsu; white atractylodes)　fangfeng (bofu; saposhnikovia; rhizome head removed)　wuzhuyu (goshuyu; evodia; washed seven times in hot water)　zexie (takusha; alisma)　fuzi (bushi; aconite; blast-fried until cracked, skin and navel removed)　gaoben (kohon) (Chinese lovage; earth removed)　gaoliangjiang (lesser galangal)　zhuling (chorei; polyporus; skin removed)　huoxiang (agastache; twigs and earth removed)　cangzhu (black atractylodes)　mahuang (mao; ephedra; roots and nodes removed)　xixin (saishin; asarum; sprouts removed)　shaoyao (shakuyaku; peony root)　duhuo (dokukatsu; angelica pubescens root; rhizome head removed)　banxia (hange; pinellia; washed seven times in hot water, processed with ginger juice)　fuling (bukuryo; poria; skin removed)　chaihu (saiko; bupleurum; rhizome head removed)　zhiqiao (bitter orange; pulp removed, stir-fried with bran; half a liang each)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried; 1 liang)　caodoukou ren (Katsumada galangal seed; 10, skin removed)　shichangpu (acorus; half a liang)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 4 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water, decoct to obtain one *zhan*, remove the dregs and take it hot, at any time; a slight sweat over the whole body brings recovery. When the seasonal qi is unharmonious, drinking it on an empty stomach wards off pathogen epidemics.

## Notes

Sheng sanzi: for cold damage, epidemics, wind-warmth and damp-warmth regardless of yin/yang or exterior/interior, with body pain, chills and fever, vomiting and cough, and for stomach damage from raw and cold foods with fullness, diarrhea and cold limbs.
- A strongly warm-drying formula: ephedra, duhuo, Chinese lovage, asarum, saposhnikovia and bupleurum release the exterior; aconite, evodia, galangal and Katsumada galangal warm the interior; the two atractylodes, magnolia bark, agastache, pinellia, poria, polyporus and alisma remove damp.
- It is known to have been popularized by Su Shi and was later criticized for harm when misused in warm-heat epidemics (the General Guide's discussion of exterior cold damage also warns against misuse in summer heat).
