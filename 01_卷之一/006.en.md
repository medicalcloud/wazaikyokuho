# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 006 摩挲丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Mosuo wan (Masagan; Stroking Pill)

It treats wind stroke with paralysis, loss of control of half the body, deviation of the mouth and eyes, halting and sluggish speech, a clouded and obstructed spirit and difficulty walking; or one-sided withering of the flesh with limp, dragging hands and feet; or cramping of the sinews and vessels with inability to bend and stretch, and qi impediment, as well as body pain from all kinds of wind.

heishen (scrophularia; moist pieces chosen, washed, dried over a fire)　diyu (chiyu; sanguisorba; sprouts removed)　chuanwu (Sichuan aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　muxiang (mokko; costus root)　dingxiang (clove; 8 liang each)　Tiantai wuyao (lindera from Tiantai)　xunluxiang (frankincense; use drop frankincense, ground separately)　xionghuang (yuo; realgar; ground and water-ground)　wuxi (black rhinoceros horn; shaved, ground separately fine)　longnao (borneol; ground separately)　chensha (cinnabar; ground and water-ground)　zirantong (pyrite; burned red and quenched in vinegar)　shexiang (jako; musk; ground separately; 4 liang each)　tianma (gastrodia; sprouts removed; 1 jin)　pearl powder (finely ground, 2 liang; if lacking, substitute dragon teeth)

Grind the above fifteen ingredients to powder and mix evenly, and form pills with refined honey the size of paper-mulberry fruits. Take one pill each time dissolved in warm wine, at any time. After taking it, cover with clothes and bedding in a place sheltered from wind to bring out sweat. Severe cases fully recover after a month, mild ones in half a month, and at the onset five to seven doses bring relief.

## Notes

Mosuo wan: for hemiplegia, facial deviation, speech disorders and difficulty walking after wind stroke, and for cramping of the sinews and body pain from wind.
- Based on gastrodia, it warms with aconite, lindera, costus and clove, opens the orifices with rhinoceros horn, borneol and musk, and mends sinews and bones with pyrite; sweating is induced after the dose.
- It gives a substitution: dragon teeth if pearl is lacking; the Siku quanshu edition lacks this note (see the variants).
