# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 072 附子散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Fuzi san (Aconite Powder)

It treats deficient cold of the large intestine in children with prolapse of the anus, mostly brought on by dysentery; the medicine should be applied.

fuzi (aconite; raw, skin and navel removed)　longgu (dragon bone; 1 liang each)

Pound and sift the above into a fine powder. Each time spread 1 *qian* on the prolapsed rectum and press it back in; apply frequently.

## Notes

Fuzi san (Wu Zhige's additions): an external powder for rectal prolapse from deficient cold of the large intestine in children, usually after dysentery.
- Raw aconite and dragon bone dusted on the prolapse, which is then pushed back.
