# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 065 紅花当帰散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Honghua danggui san (Kokatokisan; Safflower and Angelica Powder)

It is suitable for women with a deficient, exhausted blood zang organ, or accumulated static blood, with absent menses; or menses that stop and start irregularly, with intermittent abdominal pain, painful, heavy lower back and hips, and attacking, stabbing pain with tight, hard lower abdomen; and amenorrhea in unmarried girls.

liujinu cao (artemisia anomala; 5 liang)　danggui (toki; Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed)　niuxi (goshitsu; achyranthes; soaked in wine)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried)　ziwei (campsis flower)　honghua (safflower)　sumu [sappanwood (one version has zedoary); 2 liang each]　chi shaoyao (shakuyaku; red peony root; 9 liang)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　baizhi (byakushi; angelica dahurica; 1½ liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder. Take 3 *qian* each time mixed in hot wine, once on an empty stomach and once at bedtime. If the blood has long failed to move, take it mixed in strongly decocted safflower wine. It must not be taken during pregnancy.

## Notes

Honghua danggui san (bureau additions): for amenorrhea or irregular menses, abdominal pain, pain of the back and hips and a hard lower abdomen from an exhausted blood zang organ or static blood, and for amenorrhea in girls. Contraindicated in pregnancy.
- Safflower, artemisia anomala, campsis, sappanwood (zedoary in one version), red peony, achyranthes and angelica move static blood; cinnamon, angelica dahurica and licorice are added.
