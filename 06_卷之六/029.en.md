# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 029 訶黎勒丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Helile wan (Chebule Pill)

It treats weak, deficient intestines and stomach that have received wind-cold internally, with undigested water and grain, pouring diarrhoea, abdominal pain and borborygmus, and chest fullness with shortness of breath. It also treats accumulated cold of the intestines and stomach with long-standing dysentery of pure white or green-black matter without limit day and night; and damage of the spleen and stomach by cold with sudden unceasing diarrhoea, counterflow cold of the hands and feet and a faint pulse about to expire. All should take it.

helile pi (chebule fruit rind)　chuan wutou (Sichuan aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　suoshaliren (amomum seed)　baifan (alum; calcined; 40 liang each)　roudoukou (nutmeg; skin removed, blast-fried)　muxiang (costus root)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried; 20 liang each)　longgu (dragon bone; washed)　chi shizhi (red halloysite; 80 liang each)

Powder the above and form pills the size of wutong seeds with cooked millet. Take twenty to thirty pills each time with warm millet water, before meals. In severe cases the number of pills may be doubled.

## Notes

Helile wan: for indigestion, pouring diarrhoea, abdominal pain, borborygmus, chest fullness and breathlessness from weak intestines and stomach with wind-cold; for long-standing white or green-black dysentery from accumulated cold; and for sudden diarrhoea, cold limbs and a faint pulse from cold damage to the spleen and stomach.
- Chebule, dragon bone, red halloysite, alum and nutmeg astringe the intestines; Sichuan aconite and dried ginger warm; amomum and costus move qi.
