# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 021 青州白丸子 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qingzhou baiwanzi (Qingzhou White Pill)

It treats men and women with hemiplegia, stubborn numbness of the hands and feet, deviation of the mouth and eyes and congestion of phlegm and drool, and all wind that other medicines cannot treat. It is also suitable for children's fright wind, adults' head wind and hair-washing wind, and women's blood wind.

banxia (hange; pinellia; good white pieces, soaked and washed in water; 7 liang; used raw)　chuan wutou (uzu) (Sichuan aconite; skin and navel removed; used raw; half a liang)　nanxing (arisaema; raw; 3 liang)　baifuzi (typhonium; raw; 2 liang)

Pound and sieve the above into a fine powder, put it into a raw-silk bag and swish it in well-flower water (the first water drawn from the well in the morning); knead out with the hands whatever does not come out. If there are dregs, grind them again and swish them out of the silk bag until all is gone. Put [the liquid] into a porcelain basin, expose it to the sun by day and to the dew by night until dawn, pour off the water, stir in fresh well-flower water, expose it again, and the next morning change the water and stir again. Continue thus for five days in spring, three in summer, seven in autumn and ten in winter; then remove the water and dry it in the sun until it looks like pieces of jade, crush and grind it, and form pills the size of mung beans with the clear liquid of a gruel made from glutinous rice flour. Begin with five pills and increase to fifteen, taken with ginger decoction at any time. For paralytic wind, take twenty pills with warm wine three times a day; after three days, bathing will bring sweat and the limbs can stretch. Yawning after three to five days of taking it is the sign of response. Taking about ten pills regularly, one will never suffer from wind-phlegm obstructing the diaphragm. For children's fright wind, take two or three pills with mint decoction.

## Notes

Qingzhou baiwanzi: a representative wind-phlegm formula for hemiplegia, numbness, facial deviation and phlegm congestion, and for children's fright wind, head wind and women's blood wind.
- Its characteristic preparation uses raw pinellia, aconite, arisaema and typhonium, powdered and repeatedly steeped in water and sun-dried for days to reduce toxicity.
