# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 083 小黄連阿膠丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xiao huanglian ejiao wan (Shoorenakyogan; Minor Coptis and Donkey-Hide Gelatin Pill)

It treats children with unrestrained nursing and feeding, disharmony of cold and heat, red and white dysentery, or stools like fish brains, more white than red, tenesmus and abdominal pain, vexing thirst with drinking, inhibited urination, very frequent trips to the privy, and reduced eating with little strength.

roudoukou (nutmeg)　fuling (bukuryo; poria; skin removed)　hezi (chebule; blast-fried, stone removed; 1 liang each)　huanglian (oren; coptis; fibrous roots removed, lightly stir-fried; 2 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder, knead with 1 liang of donkey-hide gelatin melted in vinegar, and form pills the size of millet grains. For each year of age give ten to fifteen or twenty pills with warm water, or with milk; adjust further for age, at any time.

## Notes

Xiao huanglian ejiao wan (bureau additions): for red and white dysentery (more white than red, like fish brains), tenesmus, abdominal pain, vexing thirst, inhibited urination, frequent stools, poor appetite and weakness from unrestrained feeding and disharmony of cold and heat in children.
- The Huanglian ejiao wan (Orenakyogan) of juan 6, no. 031 (coptis, gelatin, poria) with nutmeg and chebule added to astringe, adapted for children.
