# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 046 論傷寒後患痢 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On dysentery after cold damage. Dysentery after cold damage is mostly a heat pattern, with discharge of pure pus or pure blood; give Huanglian ejiao wan (Orenakyogan) or Zhuche wan (Chushagan). For much blood, give Diyu san (Chiyusan) and Weifeng tang as well.

## Notes

On dysentery after cold damage (mostly heat, with pus or blood): Huanglian ejiao wan and Zhuche wan; Diyu san and Weifeng tang for much blood.
