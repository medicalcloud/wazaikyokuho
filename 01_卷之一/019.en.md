# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 019 牛黄生犀丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Niuhuang (goo) shengxi wan (Bezoar and Raw Rhinoceros Horn Pill)

It treats exuberant wind with congested phlegm, headache and dizziness, vexation and oppression of the throat and diaphragm, abstraction of thought, palpitation with a red face, a dry mouth with much thirst, restless sleep, red, rough urine and frequent constipation.

huangdan (minium; ground)　xionghuang (yuo; realgar; ground and water-ground)　nifen (calomel; ground)　lingyangjiao (reiyokaku; antelope horn; shaved; 5 liang each)　lead-mercury (fused with lead into "sand")　zhusha (cinnabar; ground and water-ground)　longchi (dragon teeth; ground and water-ground; 10 liang each)　tianma (gastrodia; sprouts removed)　yaxiao (saltpetre; ground)　banxia (hange; pinellia; processed with alum; 20 liang each)　raw xijiao (saikaku; rhinoceros horn; shaved)　longnao (borneol; ground; 2½ liang each)　niuhuang (bezoar; ground; 2½ *qian*)

Powder the above and form pills with refined honey, twenty pills to each liang. Take one pill each time dissolved in warm mint decoction. For wind stroke with surging drool, clenched jaws and unconsciousness, dissolve 1 *qian* of calomel, three pills of the medicine and seven drops of fresh ginger juice together in mint water and give it; once there is vomiting or purging and the phlegm and drool are expelled, the patient recovers. For children with wind-heat, congested phlegm and restless sleep, [heat] rising to the gums, half a pill each time. For acute fright wind with surging drool, convulsions, upturned eyes and clenched jaws, dissolve half a *qian* of calomel and three to five drops of fresh ginger juice in mint water together with one pill. Adjust further according to age.

## Notes

Niuhuang shengxi wan: for exuberant wind-phlegm with headache, dizziness, chest oppression, thirst, insomnia and constipation, for unconsciousness in wind stroke, and for children's acute fright wind.
- Lead and mercury minerals (minium, calomel, lead-mercury, cinnabar) sink phlegm; bezoar, rhinoceros horn, antelope horn and borneol clear heat and open the orifices; phlegm is expelled by vomiting or purging.
