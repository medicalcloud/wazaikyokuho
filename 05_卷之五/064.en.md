# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 064 十全飲 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shiquan yin (Perfect Beverage)

It treats the various deficiencies and hundred damages with disharmony of the ying-nutrient and wei-defense qi, an emaciated body, a withered yellow complexion, aching pain of the legs and knees, weary pain of the lower back and spine, dizziness and heavy ears, a bitter mouth and dry tongue, hot bones and inner vexation, palpitation with much sweating, eating that waxes and wanes, alternating fever and chills, dyspnea, cough, hematemesis and nosebleed, seminal emission and blood loss; and women's metrorrhagia and metrostaxis and irregular menses. In all cases where after illness one has not returned to normal, and where worry has injured and disturbed blood and qi, this medicine supplements in a balanced, effective way and is most suitable.

shu gan dihuang (kanjio; prepared rehmannia)　bai fuling (bukuryo; white poria)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng)　gui (cinnamon; coarse bark removed, not exposed to fire)　chuan danggui (toki; Sichuan angelica; rhizome head removed)　bai shaoyao (shakuyaku; white peony root)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage)　baizhu (byakujutsu; white atractylodes)　huangqi (ogi; astragalus; rhizome head removed)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried; equal parts)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water, three slices of fresh ginger and one jujube, decoct down to seven-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm, at any time.

## Notes

Shiquan yin (bureau additions): the same ten ingredients as the earlier Shiquan dabu tang (Juzentaihoto) (juan 5, no. 041); for wasting, sallow complexion, pain of the legs, knees and back, dizziness, bone heat, palpitation, sweating, alternating fever and chills, dyspnea cough, hematemesis, nosebleed and seminal emission from the various deficiencies, for women's flooding and irregular menses, and for poor recovery after illness.
