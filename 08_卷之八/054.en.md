# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 054 膩粉膏 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Nifen gao (Calomel Paste)

It treats pathogenic wind and heat toxin lodging and contending in the skin, with sores on the body itching and hurting at any time, and large scabies turning into sores, red, hot and painful, spreading and soaking with unceasing discharge from the flesh. It draws out heat toxin, stops pain, generates flesh and closes the openings of sores, with divine effect.

zhuzhi (lard; refined; 6 liang)　songzhi (matsuyani; pine resin; half a liang)　nifen (calomel)　hufen (lead white)　huanglian (oren; coptis; powdered)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; powdered; 1 liang each)

First decoct the pine resin in the lard, then add 2 liang of yellow wax, strain off the dregs, then add the calomel and the [other] four ingredients, stir evenly and pour into a porcelain vessel. Apply a little of the medicine each time, changing three or four times a day.

## Notes

Nifen gao (Wu Zhige's additions): an ointment for itchy, painful skin sores from pathogenic wind and heat toxin, and for large, red, painful, spreading, oozing scabies sores.
- Calomel, lead white, coptis and licorice made into an ointment with lard, pine resin and yellow wax; contains mercury and lead.
