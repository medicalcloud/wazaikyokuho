# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 078 犀角人参散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xijiao renshen san (Saikakuninjinsan; Rhinoceros Horn and Ginseng Powder)

It is suitable for deficiency heat in children, unceasing vexing thirst after vomiting and diarrhea, and after purgation.

shengxi (raw rhinoceros horn; shaved; 2 liang)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng; 15 liang)　fuling (bukuryo; poria; 25 liang)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; scorched; 5 liang)　jiegeng (kikyo; platycodon)　gange (dried pueraria; 2½ liang each)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take one large *qian* with one medium *zhan* of water and five pieces of rush pith, decoct together down to six-tenths, let cool to warm and take it, at any time. For vexing thirst, add fresh bamboo leaves to the decoction.

## Notes

Xijiao renshen san (Wu Zhige's additions): for deficiency heat, unceasing vexing thirst after vomiting and diarrhea, and after purging in children.
- Rhinoceros horn clears heat; ginseng, poria and licorice supplement; pueraria stops thirst; platycodon is added; decocted with rush pith.
