# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 088 秘伝神仙消痞丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Michuan shenxian xiaopi wan (Secretly Transmitted Divine Immortal Glomus-Dispersing Pill)

It treats all glomus disorders (abdominal masses) in children. These all arise from disharmony of cold and warmth, irregular nursing and feeding, or eating raw and cold things, fruit, sticky food and the like, so that the weak spleen and stomach cannot digest them, the five zang organs are blocked, the sanjiao is congested and stagnant, and lumps bind in the abdomen, hard as stone, sometimes with chills and fever like malaria, inability to eat and gradual emaciation; it should be given urgently.

banmao (blister beetle; twenty, heads, legs and wings removed, stir-fried with half a sheng of glutinous rice until the rice is scorched yellow, the rice discarded)　badou (hazu; croton seed; skins removed, defatted powder taken; twenty seeds)

First mill the blister beetles into a fine powder, then add the defatted croton powder and grind evenly; form pills the size of small mung beans with rice paste. For children under three, three pills each time at the beginning of the fifth watch (before dawn) with clear tea; adjust further for age and deficiency or excess. This medicine is divinely marvelous.

## Notes

Michuan shenxian xiaopi wan (bureau additions): for abdominal masses in children—stone-hard lumps with chills and fever, poor appetite and wasting—arising from irregular warmth and feeding or from raw, cold, fruit or sticky foods that weak spleen and stomach cannot digest.
- Two ingredients, blister beetle and defatted croton seed, both highly toxic.
