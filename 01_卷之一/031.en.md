# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 031 防風湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Fangfeng tang (Saposhnikovia Decoction)

It treats wind deficiency with fever, stiffness of the neck and back, loss of control of the limbs and joints, abstraction and wild talk that comes and goes at any time without the patient being aware of it. It is also very effective for leg qi with slackness and weakness. This medicine is warm and gentle and does not deplete the patient.

qinjiao (large-leaf gentian; sprouts and earth removed)　duhuo (angelica pubescens root; rhizome head removed)　mahuang (ephedra; nodes removed)　banxia (pinellia; washed seven times in hot water, sliced)　fangfeng (saposhnikovia; rhizome head removed; 2 liang each)　shengma (cimicifuga)　fangji (stephania)　baizhu (white atractylodes)　shigao (gypsum; calcined)　shaoyao (peony root; white)　huangqin (scutellaria)　gancao (licorice)　danggui (Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed)　yuanzhi (polygala; core removed)　renshen (ginseng; rhizome head removed; 1 liang each)

Make the above into a coarse powder and mix in the pinellia slices evenly. For each dose take 4 *qian* with two medium *zhan* of water and seven or eight slices of fresh ginger, decoct down to one *zhan*, remove the dregs, take six-tenths of the clear liquid, add a little powdered musk, and take it hot after meals and at bedtime.

## Notes

Fangfeng tang: for fever, stiffness of the neck and back and loss of limb control due to wind deficiency, intermittent delirious talk, and leg qi.
- Gentian, duhuo, ephedra, saposhnikovia, cimicifuga and stephania dispel wind-damp; atractylodes, ginseng, angelica, peony and polygala supplement; gypsum and scutellaria clear heat; a little musk is added when taking it.
