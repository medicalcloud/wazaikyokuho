# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 005 解毒雄黄丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jiedu xionghuang wan (Toxin-Resolving Realgar Pill)

It resolves toxin and treats throat-entwining wind and acute throat impediment with sudden collapse, loss of voice and inability to speak, or clenched jaw and loss of consciousness.

yujin (curcuma tuber)　xionghuang (realgar; ground and water-ground; 1 *fen* each)　badou (croton seed; skin removed, oil expressed; 14)

Powder the above and form pills the size of mung beans with flour paste boiled in vinegar. Take seven pills with hot clear tea; when stubborn drool is vomited, consciousness returns at once; if there is no vomiting, take again. Even if the patient seems dead, so long as the chest is still warm, if the medicine poured in does not go down, pry the mouth open at once with a knife, a ruler or an iron spoon and pour it in; once the medicine passes the throat, none fail to revive. A little vomiting or diarrhoea does no harm. It also treats congested heat of the upper diaphragm with obstructed phlegm and drool, swollen, painful throat, red eyes, abscess swellings and all toxic heat; all should take it. For children with red, swollen throat, or fright heat with congested phlegm and drool, take two or three pills, adjusted for the child's size.

## Notes

Jiedu xionghuang wan: for collapse, loss of voice, clenched jaw and unconsciousness from throat-entwining wind and acute throat impediment, restoring consciousness by making the patient vomit phlegm; also for upper-diaphragm heat, phlegm, sore throat, red eyes, abscesses and toxic heat, and for swollen throat and fright heat with phlegm in children.
- Three ingredients: realgar, curcuma tuber and croton seed, to vomit and purge phlegm.
