# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 074 当帰養血丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Danggui yangxue wan (Angelica Blood-Nourishing Pill)

It treats postpartum malign blood that does not disperse with pain that comes and goes, as well as lochia not flowing freely and hard distension of the navel and abdomen; it also treats irregular menses in unmarried girls, red and white vaginal discharge, and pain of the heart, abdomen, lower back and legs.

danggui (Chinese angelica)　mudanpi (moutan bark)　chi shaoyao (red peony root)　yanhusuo (corydalis; 2 liang each, stir-fried)　rougui (cinnamon bark; 1 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with honey. Take thirty pills with warm wine or rice water, warm, before meals. If the pain is severe, chew them thoroughly and swallow.

## Notes

Danggui yangxue wan (bureau additions): for undispersed postpartum malign blood with intermittent pain, retained lochia with hard umbilical distension, and irregular menses, discharge and pain of the heart, abdomen, back and legs in girls.
- Angelica, moutan, red peony and corydalis move static blood; cinnamon warms.
