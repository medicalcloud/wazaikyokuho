# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 079 伏虎丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Fuhu dan (Tiger-Subduing Elixir)

It specifically treats paralysis of the left or right side. (Zhang Huiyou's formula.)

sheng gan dihuang (kanjio; dried raw rehmannia)　manjingzi (vitex fruit; white removed)　bai jiangcan (silkworm; stir-fried, silk removed; 1 *fen* each)　wulingzhi (flying-squirrel droppings; skin removed; half a liang)　zhizhuhua (rhododendron flower; stir-fried)　tiannanxing (arisaema)　baijiaoxiang (liquidambar resin)　caowutou (wild aconite; blast-fried; 1 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder, boil powdered pinellia in wine to make a paste, and form pills the size of longan fruits. Divide each pill into four doses, swallowed with wine, two doses a day. (Regarding this formula: in Wuyi Lane in Jiankang prefecture there was an old man surnamed Zhong who all his life loved the Way and morning and evening gazed reverently toward Mount Mao. Because he drank much wine, he happened to contract a wind disease that a hundred treatments failed to cure. One day a Daoist suddenly appeared, told him he was suffering from too much wine and instructed him to take this medicine; the Daoist then vanished. Taking it proved effective, and so it was known to be an immortal's formula.)

## Notes

Fuhu dan (bureau additions): for paralysis of either side; attributed to Zhang Huiyou.
- Wild aconite, arisaema, rhododendron flower, silkworm and vitex dispel wind; flying-squirrel droppings and liquidambar resin move the blood; raw rehmannia cools the blood. The bracketed passage is an original note telling how a wine-loving old man received it from a Daoist.
