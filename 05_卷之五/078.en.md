# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 078 参香散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shenxiang san (Jinkosan; Ginseng and Aromatics Powder)

It treats an unsettled heart qi, the various deficiencies and hundred damages, heavy limbs, a joyless mood, many strange dreams at night, night sweats and loss of essence, fear, vexation and palpitation, joy and anger at any time, dry mouth and parched throat, thirst with desire to drink water, reduced eating, and emaciated, haggard flesh gradually becoming consumptive taxation. Taken regularly it supplements essence and blood, regulates heart qi, improves eating, calms the spirit and guards the middle; its effects cannot be fully described.

renshen (ninjin; ginseng)　shanyao (Chinese yam)　huangqi (ogi; astragalus; processed)　bai fuling (bukuryo; white poria; skin removed)　shilian rou (lotus fruit flesh; plumule removed)　baizhu (byakujutsu; white atractylodes; roasted in embers; 1 liang each)　wuyao (lindera)　suosharen (amomum seed)　juhong (red tangerine peel)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried; half a liang each)　dingxiang (clove)　nan muxiang (mokko; southern costus root)　tanxiang (sandalwood; 1 *fen* each)　chenxiang (aquilaria; 2 *qian*)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried; 3 *fen*)

Cut the above into a powder. For each dose take 4 *qian* with one large *zhan* of water, three slices of fresh ginger and one jujube, decoct down to seven-tenths, remove the dregs and take it on an empty stomach. (One method includes half a liang of blast-fried aconite.)

## Notes

Shenxiang san (Chunyou-era additions): for heaviness, low mood, nightmares, night sweats, loss of essence, fear, palpitation, emotional instability, thirst, poor appetite and wasting tending toward consumption from an unsettled heart qi and the various deficiencies.
- Ginseng, astragalus, atractylodes, poria, Chinese yam, lotus fruit and licorice supplement spleen and heart; aquilaria, clove, costus, sandalwood, lindera, amomum and red tangerine peel move qi; dried ginger warms (hence "ginseng" and "aromatics" in the name).
