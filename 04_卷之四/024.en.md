# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之四 024 新法半夏湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xinfa banxia tang (New Method Pinellia Decoction)

It treats deficient spleen and stomach qi with phlegm-fluid retention not dispersing, vomiting counterflow of sour water, distension and stuffiness of the abdomen and ribs, spinning head and nausea, and no desire for food. (Another formula appears later.)

suosharen (amomum)　shenqu (medicated leaven; stir-fried)　caoguo ren (tsaoko seed)　juhong (red tangerine peel; washed clean, white removed; 5 liang each)　bai doukou ren (white cardamom)　dingxiang (clove; half a liang each)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; raw and mix-fried; 2 liang)　large banxia (hange; pinellia; 4 liang: soaked and washed seven times in hot water, each piece cut in two, soaked a day and a night in boiling water with 1 liang of powdered alum, taken out, washed separately in hot water to remove the alum; when dry, each half cut in two again and soaked a day and a night in fresh ginger juice in a silver bowl, then heated in a water bath until the ginger juice has completely dried, dried over a gentle fire and finely powdered; then kneaded into cakes with fresh ginger juice, sun-dried or dried over a fire, and roasted yellow without scorching)

Make the above into a fine powder. For each dose, first mix 1 *qian* into a paste with fresh ginger juice, add a little stir-fried salt, and stir it into boiling water.

## Notes

Xinfa banxia tang (from here the layer of Chunyou-era additions): for vomiting, abdominal and flank stuffiness, dizziness, nausea and poor appetite when phlegm-fluid retention does not disperse because of spleen-stomach qi deficiency; a different formula from juan 3, no. 065.
- Pinellia processed repeatedly with alum and ginger juice (the "new method") is combined with amomum, tsaoko, white cardamom, clove, red tangerine peel, medicated leaven and licorice; it is made into a paste with ginger juice and stirred into hot water.
