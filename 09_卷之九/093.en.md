# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 093 胎神遊方 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wandering Locations of the Foetal Spirit

The location occupied [by the foetal spirit] must not be repaired or built upon; [doing so] damages the foetus:
1st month: the bed; 2nd: the door; 3rd: the gate; 4th: the stove; 5th: one's own body; 6th: the stove; 7th: the *zi* (north) direction; 8th: the privy; 9th: the gate; 10th: the door; 11th: the stove; 12th: the bed.

## Notes

Wandering locations of the foetal spirit: a monthly table of where the foetal spirit resides; repairing or building at that place is forbidden because it harms the foetus. Arranged as a table in the base text.
