# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 006 小青竜湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xiao qinglong tang (Minor Blue-Green Dragon Decoction)

It treats cold damage in which the exterior has not resolved and there is water qi below the heart, with dry retching, fever, cough and slight panting. It also treats spillage rheum with pain and heaviness of the body, and coughing counterflow so that one breathes leaning [on something] and cannot lie down at ease; or, from a cold body and drinking cold things, internal damage to the lung channel with cough, rapid panting and vomiting of drool and froth. It is suitable for all of these.

ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried)　xixin (asarum; leaves removed)　mahuang (ephedra; nodes and roots removed)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　shaoyao (peony root)　gancao (licorice; chopped and stir-fried; 3 liang each)　wuweizi (schisandra; 2 liang)　banxia (pinellia; washed seven times in hot water, sliced; 2½ liang)

Make the seven ingredients into a coarse powder and mix in the pinellia evenly. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water, decoct down to one *zhan*, remove the dregs and take it warm, after meals.

## Notes

Xiao qinglong tang: for cough and panting with water qi below the heart when the exterior has not resolved (*Shanghan lun* clause 40), for spillage rheum (*Jingui yaolüe*), and for cough, panting and frothy sputum from cold rheum.
- The same eight ingredients as in the *Shanghan lun*, with cinnamon bark for cinnamon twig. It is still widely used today for bronchitis, asthma and allergic rhinitis.
