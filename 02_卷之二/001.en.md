# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 001 人参敗毒散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Renshen baidu san (Ninjinhaidokusan; Ginseng Toxin-Vanquishing Powder)

It treats cold damage and seasonal qi with headache, stiff neck, vigorous fever and aversion to cold, and vexing pain of the body; as well as cough from cold congestion with nasal congestion and a heavy voice, headache from wind-phlegm, and retching with chills and fever.

chaihu (saiko; bupleurum; sprouts removed)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; scorched)　jiegeng (kikyo; platycodon)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng; rhizome head removed)　xiongqiong (ligusticum)　fuling (bukuryo; poria; skin removed)　zhiqiao (bitter orange; pulp removed, stir-fried with bran)　qianhu (peucedanum; sprouts removed, washed)　qianghuo (notopterygium; sprouts removed)　duhuo (dokukatsu; angelica pubescens root; sprouts removed)

Take 30 liang of each of these ten ingredients and make a coarse powder. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one *zhan* of water and a little fresh ginger and mint each, decoct together down to seven-tenths and remove the dregs; at any time. If cold predominates, take it hot; if heat predominates, take it warm.

## Notes

Renshen baidu san, the first formula of juan 2, "treating cold damage": for headache, stiff neck, chills and fever and body pain in cold damage and seasonal epidemics, for cough and nasal congestion from cold, and for wind-phlegm headache.
- Notopterygium, duhuo, bupleurum, peucedanum and lovage scatter exterior wind-cold-damp; bitter orange, platycodon and poria regulate qi and phlegm; ginseng supports the healthy qi to expel the pathogen. Later it was also used for colds in the weak and in the early stage of dysentery.
