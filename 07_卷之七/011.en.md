# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之七 011 蝉花散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Chanhua san (Cicada Flower Powder)

It treats pent-up heat in the liver channel contending internally with wind-toxin qi and attacking the eyes upward, with nebulae and membranes covering the eye, red swelling and pain, dim, dark, unclear vision, gritty roughness making the eyes hard to open, and much discharge and tearing; and internal and external obstructions of the eye.

chantui (cicada slough; washed clean of earth)　gujingcao (pipewort; washed free of earth)　bai jili (tribulus; stir-fried)　juhua (chrysanthemum flower; stalks removed)　fangfeng (saposhnikovia; not exposed to fire)　cao jueming (cassia seed; stir-fried)　mimenghua (buddleja flower; twigs removed)　qianghuo (notopterygium)　huangqin (scutellaria; earth removed)　manjingzi (vitex fruit; white skin removed)　shanzhizi (gardenia; skin removed)　gancao (licorice; stir-fried)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage; not exposed to fire)　muzei cao (scouring rush; washed clean)　jingjie sui (schizonepeta spikes; equal parts)

Powder the above. Take 2 *qian* each time mixed in clear tea, or in schizonepeta decoction with a little tea; take after meals and at bedtime.

## Notes

Chanhua san (Baoqing-era additions): for nebulae, red swelling and pain, dim vision, difficulty opening the eyes, discharge and tearing from pent-up heat in the liver channel with wind toxin, and for internal and external obstructions.
- Cicada slough, pipewort, buddleja, cassia seed, scouring rush, chrysanthemum, tribulus and vitex clear nebulae and brighten the eyes; saposhnikovia, notopterygium, schizonepeta and lovage disperse wind; scutellaria and gardenia clear heat; licorice is added.
