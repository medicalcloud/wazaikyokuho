# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 003 涼膈散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Liangge san (Diaphragm-Cooling Powder)

It is suitable for adults and children with accumulated heat in the bowels and viscera, vexation and agitation with much thirst, a hot face and clouded head, parched lips and dry throat, swollen tongue and throat blockage, red eyes and nosebleed, hard lumps of the jaw and cheeks, sores of the mouth and tongue, replete phlegm not flowing, thick sticky snivel and spittle, unquiet sleep, delirious and manic speech, dry, rough intestines and stomach, constipation and urinary retention, and all wind congestion.

chuan dahuang (Sichuan rhubarb)　poxiao (crude mirabilite)　gancao (licorice; scorched; 20 liang each)　shanzhizi ren (gardenia kernel)　bohe ye (mint leaf; stalks removed)　huangqin (scutellaria; 10 liang each)　lianqiao (forsythia; 2½ jin)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one *zhan* of water, add seven bamboo leaves and a little honey, decoct down to seven-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm after meals. Children may take half a *qian*, adjusted further according to age. Stop taking it once there is diarrhoea.

## Notes

Liangge san: for vexation, thirst, hot face, dry lips and throat, swollen tongue, throat blockage, red eyes, nosebleed, swollen cheeks, mouth sores, phlegm, insomnia, delirium and constipation from accumulated heat in the viscera and bowels. Still used today for repletion heat of the upper and middle burners (fever with sore throat, mouth ulcers, constipation).
- Mainly forsythia; scutellaria, gardenia and mint clear upper-burner heat; rhubarb, mirabilite and licorice (Tiaowei chengqi tang) purge the heat. Decocted with bamboo leaves and honey.
