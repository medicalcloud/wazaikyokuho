# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 026 論傷寒陽証 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On yang patterns of cold damage. For yang patterns of cold damage with a red face, fever and thirst not stopping by the fifth or sixth day, or excessive hot medicines causing severe heat with mania, vexation and agitation, or diarrhoea of red liquid or pus and blood, give Sanhuang wan, Sishun yin or Xixin san. For constipation, give Xiao chaihu tang and the like.

## Notes

On yang patterns of cold damage: Sanhuang wan, Sishun yin and Xixin san for red face, fever and thirst persisting past five or six days, mania from excessive hot medicines, or diarrhoea of red fluid or pus and blood; Xiao chaihu tang and the like for constipation.
