# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 040 辰砂半夏丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Chensha banxia wan (Cinnabar and Pinellia Pill)

It treats children with lung congestion and phlegm repletion, cough with panting and hurried breathing, glomus and fullness of the chest and diaphragm, palpitation with vexation and oppression, obstructed phlegm and drool, and audible wheezing.

wulingzhi (flying squirrel droppings; lightly stir-fried, ground and water-ground with wine, sand and earth removed)　zhusha (cinnabar; ground and water-ground; 1 liang each)　tingli (lepidium seed; rinsed clean in water, sun-dried, pounded separately to a paste)　xingren (apricot kernel; soaked in hot water, skin, tips and twin kernels removed, stir-fried with bran, pounded separately to a paste)　banxia (pinellia; soaked seven times in hot water to remove the sliminess, dried over a fire; half a liang each)

Powder the above, mix in the ground medicines evenly, knead with flour paste boiled in ginger juice and form pills the size of small hemp seeds. Take five to seven pills each time with weak ginger decoction, after meals.

## Notes

Chensha banxia wan: for cough, panting, chest glomus, palpitation, vexing oppression, phlegm and wheezing from lung congestion with phlegm repletion in children.
- Lepidium, apricot kernel and pinellia remove phlegm and calm panting; flying squirrel droppings and cinnabar are added.
