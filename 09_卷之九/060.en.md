# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 060 安胎飲 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Antai yin (Fetus-Calming Beverage)

The patterns treated and the ingredients are the same as for the earlier Antai yin. (One formula lacks pinellia and sanguisorba but has ginseng and mulberry mistletoe; another lacks atractylodes, astragalus, pinellia and sanguisorba but has mugwort leaf; all in equal parts.)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 4 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water, decoct down to eight-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm, at any time.

## Notes

Antai yin (bureau additions): the same indications and ingredients as the earlier Antai yin (juan 9, no. 042), with two variant formulas noted (one adding ginseng and mistletoe, one adding mugwort); the dose differs (4 *qian*).
