# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之七 015 菊花散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Juhua san (Kikukasan; Chrysanthemum Powder)

It regulates wind toxin of liver qi with red, swollen eyes, dimness and aversion to light, gritty roughness making the eyes hard to open, static flesh climbing over the eye, itching or pain, and gradual formation of nebulae; it also treats sudden red swelling and pain, all without exception.

bai jili (tribulus; stir-fried, spines removed)　qianghuo (notopterygium; rhizome head removed, not exposed to fire)　muzei (scouring rush; nodes removed)　chantui (cicada slough; head, legs and wings removed; 3 liang each)　juhua (chrysanthemum flower; stalks removed; 6 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder. Take 2 *qian* each time after meals and at bedtime mixed in clear tea. Taken regularly it clears the head and eyes, washes the liver and dispels wind. Avoid wind-provoking things, salted and preserved foods, and roasted and grilled food.

## Notes

Juhua san (Wu Zhige's additions): for red swollen eyes, dimness, photophobia, difficulty opening, static flesh over the eye, itching, pain and nebulae from wind toxin of liver qi, and for sudden red swelling and pain.
- Mainly chrysanthemum; tribulus, scouring rush and cicada slough clear nebulae; notopterygium disperses wind.
