# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之七 022 還睛丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huanjing wan (Sight-Restoring Pill)

For men and women with wind toxin attacking upward, red, swollen eyes, fear of the sun and aversion to light, abundant discharge and tears, gritty roughness making the eyes hard to open, itchy, red, painful orbits, red festering of the lids and canthi and static flesh invading the eye, or with sudden red eyes and unbearable pain of the eye, taking it works at once. It also treats hemilateral and frontal headache, all head wind and dizziness of the head and eyes.

baizhu (white atractylodes; used raw)　tusizi (dodder seed; soaked in wine, ground separately)　qingxiangzi (celosia seed; earth removed)　fangfeng (saposhnikovia; rhizome head removed)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried)　qianghuo (notopterygium; shoots removed)　bai jili (tribulus; stir-fried, spines removed)　mimenghua (buddleja flower)　muzei (scouring rush; nodes removed)

Take equal parts of each, make into a fine powder and form pills the size of a pellet with refined honey. Take one pill each time, chewed thoroughly and swallowed with plain hot water, on an empty stomach before meals, three times a day.

## Notes

Huanjing wan (bureau additions): for red swollen eyes, photophobia, discharge, tearing, difficulty opening, itchy orbits, festering lids and static flesh from wind toxin attacking upward; for sudden red eyes with severe pain; and for hemilateral and frontal headache, head wind and dizziness.
- Celosia seed, buddleja, scouring rush and tribulus brighten the eyes; saposhnikovia and notopterygium disperse wind; dodder, atractylodes and licorice supplement.
