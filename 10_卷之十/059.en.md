# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 059 人参丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Renshen wan (Ginseng Pill)

It treats children who in nursing and feeding have drunk too much cold, damaging the spleen and stomach with cold, with distension and fullness of the abdomen and flanks and much vomiting of phlegm and drool.

renshen (ginseng)　dingxiang (clove)　chenpi (aged tangerine peel; white pith removed)　ganjiang (dried ginger; dried over a fire)　baizhu (white atractylodes; 1 *fen* each)　banxia (pinellia; washed seven times in hot water; half a liang)

Pound and sift the above into a powder, knead with refined honey and form pills the size of hemp seeds. For each three-year-old child, give ten pills with warm water, at any time, twice a day, adjusted for the child's size.

## Notes

Renshen wan (Wu Zhige's additions): for cold damage of the spleen and stomach from excessive cold drinks in children, with abdominal and flank distension and much vomiting of phlegm.
- Ginseng, atractylodes and dried ginger (of the Lizhong type) plus clove, tangerine peel and pinellia.
