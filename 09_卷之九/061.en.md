# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 061 益陰丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yiyin dan (Yin-Augmenting Elixir)

It can treat women with long-standing deficiency of the sea of blood, timid, weak viscera and bowels, wind-cold evil qi taking advantage of deficiency to lodge and contend, cold pain of the knees and abdomen and occasional diarrhoea; or cold harboured in the womb with long failure to conceive, irregular menses, sometimes heavy, sometimes scant; or early or late, dribbling without stopping, with five-coloured discharge; or blocked menses, binding and gathering into mounting and conglomerations, little colour in the face and body, eating that waxes and wanes, wasting flesh, aching joints, intermittent chills and fever, pain of the navel and abdomen just before the menses, dry, rough skin, dark spots and freckles on the face, swollen, itchy scalp and hair falling with the comb; or postpartum taxation damage not yet recovered, a withered, haggard complexion, tasteless food, gradually becoming postpartum taxation.

The formula is the same as the earlier Nanyue Wei furen jiyin dan.

## Notes

Yiyin dan (bureau additions): for cold pain of the knees and abdomen and diarrhoea from long deficiency of the sea of blood with wind-cold; for infertility, irregular menses, spotting, five-coloured discharge, amenorrhoea, conglomerations, poor complexion, wasting, joint pain, chills and fever, premenstrual pain, dry skin, facial spots, scalp swelling and hair loss from a cold womb; and for postpartum taxation. The indications resemble those of the Shu gan dihuang wan (juan 9, no. 001).
- The formula is said to be the same as the Nanyue Wei furen jiyin dan (juan 9, no. 048).
