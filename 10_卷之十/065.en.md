# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 065 葛根散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Gegen san (Pueraria Powder)

It treats cold damage in children with vexing heat of the limbs, headache and body pain, agitation of the heart, dry mouth and thirst.

gegen (pueraria)　mahuang (ephedra; roots and nodes removed)　renshen (ginseng; half a liang each)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried; 1 *fen* each)

Pound the above into a coarse powder. For a three-year-old, take 1 *qian* each time with a small *zhan* of water, a little fresh ginger and one jujube, decoct together down to five-tenths, strain off the dregs and take it warm, adjusted for the child's size, at any time.

## Notes

Gegen san (Wu Zhige's additions): for vexing heat of the limbs, headache, body pain, agitation and thirst in cold damage in children.
- Pueraria, ephedra and cinnamon release the exterior; ginseng and licorice supplement (of the Gegen tang type).
