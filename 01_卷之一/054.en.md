# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 054 乳香応痛丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Ruxiang yingtong wan (Frankincense Pain-Answering Pill)

It treats all wind qi with paralysis of the left or right side, deviation of the mouth and eyes, hemiplegia, halting and sluggish speech, abstraction of the spirit, congestion of phlegm and drool, cramping of the sinews and vessels, or stubborn numbness all over the body with running pain, slack and weak legs and knees and difficulty walking. It also treats injuries from blows with static blood that will not disperse and unbearable pain; or fatigue and injury from travelling on foot with swollen, painful legs and knees; or wind toxin of the kidneys attacking upward with a swollen face and tinnitus, or pouring downward with heavy legs and knees; and one-sided or general headache attacking the eyes. It treats all of these.

longgu (ryukotsu; dragon bone; soaked overnight in wine, dried over a fire, ground to powder, water-ground three times and dried in the sun; 4½ liang)　wugong (goko; centipedes; six, tail stings removed, wrapped in mint leaves and roasted until cooked)　chixiaodou (sekishozu; rice bean; used raw)　tiger bone (roasted with ghee until scorched; 6 liang each)　bai jiangcan (silkworm; stir-fried, silk and mouth removed)　caowutou (wild aconite; blast-fried, skin and tips removed; 12 liang each)　baijiaoxiang (liquidambar resin; picked clean and refined)　tianma (gastrodia; rhizome head removed, washed)　chuan niuxi (goshitsu; Sichuan achyranthes; soaked in wine, rhizome head removed)　chuan danggui (toki; Sichuan angelica; rhizome head removed, soaked in wine; 3 liang each)　quanxie (whole scorpions; tail stings removed, lightly roasted; 70)　ruxiang (frankincense; ground; 6 *qian*)　mubie ren (momordica seed kernels; 72, ground separately)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with vinegar paste. Take five to seven pills each time, swallowed with cold wine or with cold clear tea, at any time; avoid all hot things for about one double-hour [afterwards]. This medicine is especially good taken at bedtime. Avoid damp things, noodles, roasted and fried food, pickled fish and dried meat, and anything that produces heat or stirs wind.

## Notes

Ruxiang yingtong wan (Baoqing-era additions): for paralysis, facial deviation, speech disorder, wandering pain and difficulty walking from wind qi, for pain from static blood after injury, swollen legs from walking, kidney wind toxin and headache.
- Wild aconite, centipede, scorpion and silkworm dispel wind; tiger bone, achyranthes and dragon bone strengthen sinews and bones; frankincense, liquidambar resin and angelica move the blood. It is taken with cold wine, and hot things are to be avoided.
