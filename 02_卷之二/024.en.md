# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 024 僧伽応夢人参散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Sengqie yingmeng renshen san (Ginseng Powder of Sangha's Dream)

It treats cold damage with fever of the body and headache, and conditions such as wind congestion with phlegm-cough and coughing of blood.

gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried; 6 liang)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng)　jiegeng (kikyo; platycodon; lightly stir-fried)　qingpi (green tangerine peel; pulp removed)　baizhi (byakushi; angelica dahurica)　gange (dried pueraria)　baizhu (byakujutsu; white atractylodes; 3 liang each)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried; 5½ *qian*)

Make the above into a fine powder. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one *zhan* of water, two slices of fresh ginger and two jujubes, decoct down to seven-tenths and drink it at a comfortable warmth. For cold damage, decoct it with fermented soybeans and drink it hot; there is great divine effect. At any time. (One version lacks licorice.)

## Notes

Sengqie yingmeng renshen san (Shaoxing-era additions): for fever and headache in cold damage and for phlegm-cough and coughing of blood from wind.
- Ginseng, atractylodes, licorice and dried ginger supplement and warm the middle; angelica dahurica and pueraria release the exterior; platycodon and green tangerine peel regulate qi and phlegm. The name seems to mean a formula revealed in a dream by the monk Sangha (Sengqie), but no account of its origin is given.
