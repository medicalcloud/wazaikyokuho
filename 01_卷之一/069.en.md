# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 069 乳香趁痛散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Ruxiang chentong san (Frankincense Pain-Chasing Powder)

(The formula is the same as Hugu san [Kokotsusan].) (One version says: the formula is the same as Longhu dan.)

## Notes

Only the heading of Ruxiang chentong san, with a note that the formula is the same as the earlier Hugu san (also called Ruxiang chentong san), and a variant note saying it is the same as Longhu dan.
