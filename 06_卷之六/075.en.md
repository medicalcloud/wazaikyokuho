# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 075 秘伝斗門散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Michuan doumen san (Secretly Transmitted Sluice-Gate Powder)

It treats the eight kinds of toxic dysentery with gripping pain of the viscera and bowels, pus and blood, red and white, or passing of static blood, or in flakes, or a mixture of five colours, very frequent day and night. It also treats food-refusing malign dysentery with tenesmus, long unceasing thirst and complete inability to eat that other medicines cannot cure, with immediate divine effect.

heidou (black soybean; stir-fried, skins removed; 12 liang)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried; 4 liang)　yingsuqiao (poppy husk; stir-fried with honey; half a jin)　diyu (sanguisorba; stir-fried)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried; 6 liang each)　bai shaoyao (white peony root; 3 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one *zhan* of water, decoct together down to seven-tenths and take it warm.

## Notes

Michuan doumen san (bureau additions): nearly the same indications as the earlier Doumen san (juan 6, no. 055)—the eight toxic dysenteries, stools of static blood or flakes, and food-refusing dysentery; a different formula omitting pueraria and angelica and adding white peony.
