# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 039 麻仁丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Maren wan (Maningan; Hemp Seed Pill)

It smooths the sanjiao, harmonizes the five zang organs, moistens the intestines and stomach and removes wind qi. It treats congestion and binding of cold and heat, deficient, scant fluids, and constipation with difficulty passing stools, or blockage without movement. It is especially suitable for the elderly with weak qi and for people with wind who have constipated, rough stools.

zhiqiao (bitter orange; pulp removed, stir-fried with bran)　bai binglang (white areca; roasted in embers half-cooked)　tusizi (toshishi; dodder seed; soaked in wine, powdered separately)　shanyu (Chinese yam)　fangfeng (bofu; saposhnikovia; forks and branches removed)　shanzhuyu (sanshuyu; cornus)　cheqianzi (shazenshi; plantago seed)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed; 1½ liang each)　muxiang (mokko; costus root)　qianghuo (notopterygium; 1 liang each)　yuliren (ikurinin; bush cherry kernel; skin removed, ground separately)　dahuang (daio; rhubarb; half steamed, half raw)　maren (hemp seed; pounded and ground separately; 4 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder, mix in the separately ground medicines evenly, knead with refined honey and form pills the size of wutong seeds. Take fifteen to twenty pills each time with warm water at bedtime.

## Notes

Maren wan: for constipation from congestion of cold and heat and deficient fluids, especially in the elderly with weak qi and in people with wind.
- Hemp seed and bush cherry kernel moisten; rhubarb purges; bitter orange, areca and costus move qi; dodder, Chinese yam, cornus and cinnamon supplement; saposhnikovia, notopterygium and plantago seed are added. A different formula from the *Shanghan lun* Maziren wan (Mashiningan) (the next entry, Piyue maren wan [Hiyakumaningan]).
