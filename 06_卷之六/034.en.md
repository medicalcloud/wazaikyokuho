# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 034 霊砂丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Lingsha dan (Numinous Cinnabar Elixir)

It treats timid, weak viscera and bowels with accumulated stagnation within, gripping pain of the navel and abdomen, dysentery with pus and blood without limit day and night, tenesmus, borborygmus and abdominal distension, undigested rice and grain, shortness of qi and fatigue, no desire for food, or episodes of chills and fever, and gradual emaciation.

xiaoshi (saltpetre; finely ground together with the arsenic, put into a porcelain jar with the mouth covered with lime, burned over charcoal for half a day, taken out and the fire toxin removed)　Xinzhou pishuang (arsenic trioxide from Xinzhou)　nifen (calomel)　fenshuang (mercuric chloride; ground; half a liang each)　huangdan (red lead; ground)　kufan (dried alum; ground; 1½ liang each)　zhusha (cinnabar; ground and water-ground; 1 liang)　ruxiang (frankincense; ground)　Guifu huashi (talc from Guifu; 1 liang each)

Grind and sift the above medicines into a powder, knead with 2 liang 4 *qian* of steamed cake and form pills the size of wutong seeds. Take five pills each time with warm millet water; if not healed, increase the number of pills and take again. Children may take one or two pills, adjusted at the time according to their size.

## Notes

Lingsha dan: for umbilical pain, dysentery with pus and blood, tenesmus, borborygmus, abdominal fullness, indigestion, weakness, poor appetite, chills and fever and wasting from timid, weak viscera with internal accumulated stagnation.
- Arsenic trioxide, calomel and mercuric chloride (mercury compounds), red lead, dried alum, cinnabar, frankincense, talc and saltpetre. It contains arsenic, mercury and lead, is extremely toxic and is not used today. A different formula from the Lingsha of juan 5, no. 054 (mercury and sulphur).
