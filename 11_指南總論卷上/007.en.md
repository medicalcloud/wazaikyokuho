# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷上 007 論炮炙三品薬石類例・玉石部 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On examples of the processing of drugs and minerals of the three grades.

Jade and Stone Section

Cinnabar, realgar and orpiment: to use, first break them up, grind fine and water-grind, spread paper in a bowl of ashes to absorb the water and dry them, then use them in medicines. If otherwise calcined and refined, follow the respective formula.
Stalactite: to use, first boil it according to method for the full number of days, then grind it fine in water until it is no longer gritty; only then may it be put into medicines and taken.
Alum: to use, take bright, clear pieces, first calcine them in a fire in an iron pot or on a knife, then grind fine for use in medicines. If used raw, follow the respective formula.
Red and white halloysite: to use, calcine red-hot in a charcoal fire, take out, cool, grind fine and water-grind, then use in medicines. In haste, grinding extremely fine without water-grinding will do.
Sulfur: to use, first grind fine and water-grind, then use in medicines. If otherwise calcined and refined, follow the respective formula.
Actinolite: to use, first burn red-hot over charcoal and quench seven times in good wine; boiling in good wine for half a day will also do; in either case grind fine and water-grind, then use in medicines.
Magnetite: to use, first burn red-hot over charcoal, quench nine times in strong vinegar, pound, sift, grind fine and water-grind, then use in medicines. For decoctions, pound and rinse with water to remove the red juice, then use.
Black lead: to use, first melt in an iron pot over charcoal, pour out onto a new tile and strain off the dross; do this once or twice, take the clean lead, weigh and use. If made into granules, calcine and refine according to the respective formula.
Red lead: to use, first stir-fry until the color changes, grind extremely fine and sift again, then use in medicines.
Saltpetre: to use, first grind extremely fine, put in a porcelain bottle and calcine red-hot in a charcoal fire, then use in medicines. In haste, merely stir-fry and grind fine.
Table salt: to use, first stir-fry, grind fine, then use in medicines.
Lime: to use, that which has weathered into powder is best. First soak it overnight in vinegar, strain out and let dry, calcine in a fire until the rank, foul smell is gone, cool, grind fine, then use in medicines. If otherwise calcined and refined, follow the respective formula.
Oven earth: this is the earth beneath the crescent facing the cauldron in the stove. To use, first burn red over a fire, grind fine and water-grind, then use in medicines. If needed urgently, simply burn and grind.
Pot soot: that from village houses is good. To use, grind extremely fine and sift again, then use in medicines.
Talc: to use, first scrape it off with a knife, boil it with moutan bark for a full day, take out, grind and water-grind with east-flowing water, dry in the midday sun, then use in medicines. If needed urgently, simply grind fine.
Limonite, fluorite, gypsum, calcite, hematite, stone swallow and the like: to use, calcine all in a fire, quench seven times in vinegar, pound, grind and water-grind extremely fine, then use in medicines.
Selenite: to use, pound, grind fine, water-grind and sun-dry, then use in medicines.
White chalk: this is white clay. To use, for each liang prepared, put 1 *fen* of salt into a basin of water and boil in a copper vessel through ten-odd boils, then water-grind with this boiled water before using in medicines; this prevents it from binding and roughening the intestines.
Pyrite: to use, burn red-hot, quench nine times in vinegar, grind fine and sift.
Ophicalcite: to use, calcine in a great fire; in haste, it may be used uncalcined.

## Notes

The beginning of the discussion of processing methods for drugs of the three grades: the Jade and Stone section lists processing of mineral drugs (water-grinding cinnabar, realgar and orpiment; boiling stalactite; calcining and quenching alum, halloysite, actinolite and magnetite; refining lead; treating lime, oven earth, talc, white chalk and others).
- Quick methods for emergencies are also given. It continues with the Herb section (008), Wood (009), Animal (010), Bird, Fish and Insect (011), and Fruit and Vegetable (012).
