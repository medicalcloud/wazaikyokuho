# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 091 産前将護法 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Method of Care Before Delivery

According to the treatises of various authorities on childbirth: once the parturient woman enters her final month, drinking wine is strictly forbidden, lest her heart-spirit be clouded and confused at delivery. At delivery, bystanders must not be allowed to make a commotion; if everyone rushes about, the mother may be frightened. Only an experienced midwife and two or three close, calm, careful, steady and mature attendants should assist. When the mother first feels abdominal pain, let her sit or lie as she pleases and encourage her to eat and drink, lest her strength be weak at delivery. If the abdominal pain gradually worsens, she should simply endure it well, and may take one or two doses of the foetus-lubricating Yubaipi san in due order. In taking the medicine, take care not to give it too early; the right time must be found. Bystanders must not hurry her. Have someone support her and walk her slowly. If walking becomes somewhat difficult, let her stand leaning on something, and after a while support her and walk again, until the abdominal pain extends to and draws on the lower back and the labour pains come frequently; then give one dose of Cuisheng dan and continue to support her walking with effort. When the labour pains grow more severe, standing becomes difficult, and it is recognized that the moment of birth is at hand, give the Cuisheng fu (labour-hastening talisman) and then let her calmly mount the straw (the birthing place). Take care not to mount the straw too early; if too early, the child will find it hard to turn in the abdomen. Also observe carefully and wait until the child presses to be born before having someone hold her waist. The person holding the waist must not lean to one side; then the child follows its proper course and delivery is naturally easy. Some deliver lying down; they too should wait until settled lying, the back flat on the mat and the body not bent, so that the child does not lose its way. If these rules are not followed, difficult labour will surely result; even if one is fortunate enough to escape it, various illnesses will surely arise.

## Notes

Method of care before delivery: guidance from treatises on childbirth—no wine in the final month; a quiet room with an experienced midwife and a few attendants; letting the mother sit, lie and eat freely in early labour; timing of Yubaipi san (juan 9, no. 022), Cuisheng dan (no. 028) and the Cuisheng fu talisman (no. 094); walking her with support; not mounting the birthing straw too early; the posture of the person holding the waist; and the posture for delivery lying down.
