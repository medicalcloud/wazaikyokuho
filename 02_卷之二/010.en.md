# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 010 葛根解肌湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Gegen jieji tang (Pueraria Flesh-Releasing Decoction)

It treats cold damage, warm disease and seasonal cold epidemics with headache and stiff neck, fever and aversion to cold, stiffness of the limbs, vexing pain of the joints, stiff, painful lower back and spine, and vexation and oppression of the chest and diaphragm.

gegen (kakkon; pueraria; 4 liang)　mahuang (mao; ephedra; nodes removed; 3 liang)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed; 1 liang)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried)　huangqin (ogon; scutellaria)　shaoyao (shakuyaku; peony root; 2 liang each)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water and one jujube broken open, decoct down to eight-tenths, remove the dregs and take it fairly hot, at any time, until sweat appears.

## Notes

Gegen jieji tang: for headache, stiff neck and back, chills and fever, joint pain, back pain and chest oppression in cold damage, warm disease and cold epidemics.
- Close to Gegen tang (Kakkonto) of the *Shanghan lun* (clause 31), with scutellaria in place of fresh ginger and cinnamon bark for cinnamon twig.
