# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 037 縮脾飲 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Suopi yin (Spleen-Restraining Drink)

It resolves latent heat, removes vexing thirst, disperses summer heat toxin and stops vomiting and diarrhea. It is also suitable for restlessness from taking too much hot medicine after sudden turmoil.

suosharen (amomum)　wumei (ubai) flesh (smoked plum; cleaned)　caoguo (tsaoko; roasted, skin removed)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried; 4 liang each)　gange (dried pueraria; chopped)　bai biandou (hyacinth bean; skin removed, stir-fried; 2 liang each)

Cut the above into pieces. For each dose take 4 *qian* with one large bowl of water, decoct down to eight-tenths, remove the dregs, chill by immersing in water and drink it to relieve vexation; or take it hot or warm as desired. It is excellent drunk in place of boiled water.

## Notes

Suopi yin (Baoqing-era additions): a summer drink for vexing thirst, vomiting and diarrhea from summer heat, and for agitation after overuse of hot medicines in sudden turmoil.
- Amomum and tsaoko warm the spleen, smoked plum and pueraria stop thirst, and hyacinth bean removes summer heat-damp; it may be drunk cold and used instead of boiled water.
