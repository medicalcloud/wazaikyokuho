# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 116 白梅湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Baimei tang (Hakubaito; White Plum Drink)

It treats heat strike with restlessness of the five hearts, vomiting of sudden turmoil, dry mouth with vexing thirst, and blocked fluids.

baimei (salted white plum; ground and broken; 29 jin)　tanxiang (sandalwood; 14 liang)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; 13½ jin)　yan (salt; stir-fried; 15 jin)

Powder the above. Take 1 *qian* each time with grated fresh ginger, in freshly drawn water. For dry retching after wine with nausea and a rough tongue, drink it like tea.

## Notes

Baimei tang (Wu Zhige's additions): a drink for vexing heat of the five hearts, vomiting of sudden turmoil, dry mouth and thirst from heat strike, and for dry retching and nausea after drinking.
- Salted white plum, sandalwood, licorice and salt, taken with ginger in cold water.
