# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 049 論傷暑証候 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On the signs of summer heat damage. Damage by summer heat in summer is also called latent summer heat. It refers to a person coming from the heat and immediately sitting or lying in a cool place, not realizing that he is closed in by the external cool, cold qi so that the summer heat qi lies latent in the interstices and cannot be discharged; hence "latent summer heat". Its signs are spontaneous sweating with aversion to cold, or aversion to cold in the back with thirst, or a grimy face like someone who has not washed, or dry front teeth, the two central teeth dry: this is latent summer heat. Vomiting counterflow with thirst, and inability to stay lying on the mat, are also summer heat damage; give Wuling san (Goreisan), Guihua wan, Dashun san or Xiangru san. For severe fever, give Xiangru san, Zhuye tang (Chikuyoto), Pipaye san (Biwayosan) or Xiao chaihu tang (Shosaikoto) taken cold, with immediate effect.

## Notes

On summer heat damage (latent summer heat): coming from heat into a cool place traps summer heat in the interstices; signs include spontaneous sweating with chills, chill in the back with thirst, a grimy face, dry front teeth, vomiting with thirst and restlessness when lying. Wuling san, Guihua wan, Dashun san and Xiangru san; for severe fever Xiangru san, Zhuye tang, Pipaye san or Xiao chaihu tang taken cold.
