# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 030 大温脾丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Da wenpi wan (Major Spleen-Warming Pill)

It treats all cases of weak, deficient spleen and stomach with cold qi attacking and surging, undigested food, distension and pain of the heart and abdomen, vomiting and acid swallowing, glomus and dysphagia with blockage, borborygmus and diarrhoea, water and grain not separating, a yellow face and wasted flesh, and reduced eating with a liking for lying down. Taken regularly it warms the spleen, augments the stomach, disperses grain and improves eating. For long-standing deficiency and chronic chill, eating little and being easily damaged [by food], it is especially suitable for regular use.

wuzhuyu (evodia; [washed] seven times in hot water, dried over a fire)　damai nie (barley sprout; stir-fried)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed; 5 liang each)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried)　jiegeng (platycodon)　renshen (ginseng)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried; 3 liang each)　fuzi (aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　xixin (asarum; shoots removed; 2 liang each)　shenqu (medicated leaven; broken up, stir-fried; 3 liang 1 *qian*)　zhishi (unripe bitter orange; stir-fried with bran; 1½ *fen*)

Make the above into a fine powder, knead with refined honey and form pills the size of wutong seeds. Take twenty pills each time with warm wine; rice water is also fine; three times a day, on an empty stomach before meals.

## Notes

Da wenpi wan: for indigestion, abdominal distension and pain, vomiting, acid reflux, glomus, borborygmus, diarrhoea, sallow complexion, wasting and poor appetite from weak spleen and stomach with cold qi; especially for regular use in long-standing deficiency and chronic chill with poor appetite.
- Evodia, aconite, dried ginger, cinnamon and asarum warm; ginseng and licorice supplement; barley sprout and medicated leaven aid digestion; platycodon and unripe bitter orange are added.
