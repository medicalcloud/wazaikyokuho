# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 053 大聖一粒金丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Dasheng yili jindan (Great Sage One-Grain Golden Elixir)

It treats acute wind stroke in men and women with paralysis of the left or right side, convulsions of the hands and feet, deviation of the mouth and eyes, surging drool and sluggish speech, pain all over the body, and one-sided or general head wind. Everything belonging to wind disease is treated by it.

large black fuzi (bushi; aconite lateral root; blast-fried, skin and tips removed)　large chuan wutou (uzu) (Sichuan aconite main root; blast-fried, skin and tips removed)　Silla baifuzi (typhonium from Silla; blast-fried; 2 liang each)　bai jili (tribulus; stir-fried, sharp spines removed)　bai jiangcan (silkworm; washed, silk removed, lightly stir-fried)　wulingzhi (flying-squirrel droppings; ground; 1 liang each)　moyao (myrrh; ground separately)　baifan (alum; calcined and ground separately)　pure musk (ground)　fine fragrant ink (ground into liquid)　zhusha (cinnabar; ground; half a liang each)　gold leaf (200 leaves, for coating)

Make the first six ingredients together into a fine powder and grind the last four evenly into it; grind the ink in one *zhan* of well-flower water until it is used up, knead [the powder] with the ink liquid, pound five hundred times with pestle and mortar, form pills the size of a pellet, coat with gold leaf and dry them in a cellar. Take one pill each time after meals and at bedtime, ground and dissolved in fresh ginger juice and added to hot wine; then drink as much hot wine as desired, lie down in a warm place free of wind, cover with clothes and bedding, and recovery follows as soon as sweat appears. For mild illness divide each pill into two doses. Avoid things that provoke wind; pregnant women must not take it.

## Notes

Dasheng yili jindan (from here the layer of formulas newly added in the Baoqing era): for paralysis, convulsions, facial deviation, speech disorder, general pain and head wind in acute wind stroke.
- Three aconite-type drugs (fuzi, wutou, baifuzi) with tribulus, silkworm, flying-squirrel droppings, myrrh, alum, musk, ink and cinnabar; sweating is induced with hot wine. Contraindicated in pregnancy.
