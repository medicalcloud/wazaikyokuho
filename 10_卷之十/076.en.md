# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 076 朱礬散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zhufan san (Cinnabar and Alum Powder)

It treats goose mouth (thrush) in newborns, with white flakes like rice bran on the tongue and also outside the nose, and inability to nurse.

zhusha (cinnabar; finely ground)　baifan (alum; dried; equal parts)

Grind the above medicines extremely fine. Each time apply a little on the child's tongue, three times a day; first wipe the coating off the tongue repeatedly with a wad of hair until clean, and it heals at once.

## Notes

Zhufan san (Wu Zhige's additions): an external powder for thrush in newborns (white flakes on the tongue and outside the nose, inability to nurse).
- Cinnabar and dried alum applied after wiping the tongue with hair.
