# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 012 五疳保童丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wugan baotong wan (Five Gan Child-Protecting Pill)

It treats the five gan disorders of children. A child's bones and flesh are light and soft and its intestines and stomach slight and delicate; if nursing and feeding are regulated, the zang-fu organs are in harmony. But if the wet nurse mismanages cold and warmth, eats and drinks irregularly, becomes drunk or overfull, joyful or angry, or if the child, after the hundredth day and before the age of five, takes more and more milk and food without discrimination of raw and cold things and likes to eat fatty, sweet and sour things, the five gan disorders result. The first is called liver gan: the signs are shaking the head and rubbing the eyes, white membranes covering the eyes, sweat flowing over the whole body, sleeping face down, rough, itchy eyes, a green-yellow flesh color, hair standing on end and a scorched-looking head, green veins and a hot brain, abdominal masses in the abdomen, frequent diarrhea, long unhealed, and increasing emaciation. The second is called heart gan: the signs are vigorous fever of the whole body, irregular vomiting and diarrhea, red cheeks and yellow face, vexing fullness of the chest and diaphragm, dry nose and agitated heart, sores of the mouth and tongue, long-unhealed dysentery with much pus and blood, occasional night sweats or deficiency fright. The third is called spleen gan: the signs are many veins visible on the abdomen, dyspnea and coarse breathing, little nursing or eating, distension and fullness of the heart and abdomen, much crying and coughing counterflow, a withered yellow complexion, protruding bones and scorched hair, a withered form and inferior strength, congestion and oppression of the chest and diaphragm, undigested water and grain, a constantly dry mouth and nose, a liking for eating mud and earth, a joyless mood, a liking for darkness and dislike of light, disharmony of the intestines and stomach, and much sour, smelly diarrhea. The fourth is called lung gan: the signs are cough with counterflow qi, dry, scorched skin and hair, much snivel and crying, blockage of the throat, rubbing the nose and biting the nails, vigorous fever with aversion to cold, sores of the mouth and nose, red, itchy lip margins, qi distension in the abdomen, gradually decreasing nursing and eating, irregular bowels with frequent diarrhea, grains of rice in the stools, and millet-like eruptions on the skin. The fifth is called kidney gan: the signs are wasted flesh, sores on the gums, intermittent chills and fever, dry mouth and nose, a brain hot as fire and legs cold as ice, increasing vomiting, decreased nursing and eating, frequent diarrhea, gaping lower orifice with the anus not retracting, and itchy, painful gan sores. All the above conditions are treated.

huanglian (oren; coptis; fibrous roots removed)　bai shan tou (white eel head; roasted until scorched yellow; if unavailable, substitute stir-fried white elm seed paste)　cao longdan (ryutan; gentian; rhizome head removed)　xionghuang (yuo; realgar; ground and water-ground)　qing jupi (kippi; green tangerine peel; pulp removed)　wubeizi (gallnut)　yemingsha (bat droppings; lightly stir-fried; 1 liang each)　chan tou (toad head; one, roasted yellow)　kulian gen (chinaberry root)　tianjiangzi (cnidocampa cocoon; lightly stir-fried)　hu huanglian (picrorhiza)　shexiang (jako; musk)　qingdai (indigo; ground)　xiongdan (bear bile; ground)　luhui [aloe; ground; 1 liang each (one version has toad ash and lightly stir-fried snail)]

Make the above into a fine powder, grind all evenly and form pills the size of hemp seeds with cooked glutinous rice. Give one pill per year of age with warm rice water, at any time; three doses a day is even better. One formula has 1 *fen* of lightly stir-fried snail.

## Notes

Wugan baotong wan: for the five gan disorders of children (liver, heart, spleen, lung and kidney gan), describing their causes (the wet nurse's indiscretions; irregular feeding between one hundred days and five years of age) and the signs of each in detail.
- Coptis, picrorhiza, gentian, aloe, bear bile and indigo clear gan heat; chinaberry root, gallnut, realgar, toad head, eel head and cnidocampa cocoon kill worms; bat droppings brighten the eyes; green tangerine peel and musk are added.
