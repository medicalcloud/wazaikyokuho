# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 058 暖宮丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Nuangong wan (Womb-Warming Pill)

The patterns treated are the same as for the earlier Nuangong wan.

shashen (shajin; adenophora root; washed clean)　diyu (chiyu; sanguisorba)　huangqi (ogi; astragalus)　jiegeng (kikyo; platycodon)　baiwei (byakubi; cynanchum root)　niuxi (goshitsu; achyranthes; soaked in wine overnight)　duzhong (tochu; eucommia; coarse bark removed, roasted with ginger juice)　houpo (koboku; magnolia bark; coarse bark removed, stir-fried with ginger juice)　baizhi (byakushi; angelica dahurica; half a liang each)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried)　xixin (saishin; asarum; shoots removed)　shujiao (shokusho) (Sichuan pepper; seeds and closed fruits removed, stir-fried to sweat out the oil; 1 *fen* each)　fuzi (bushi; aconite; one large piece, blast-fried, skin and navel removed)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with refined honey. Take twenty or thirty pills each time on an empty stomach, swallowed with warm wine or jujube decoction. It also treats long-standing cold of the womb in women with failure to conceive.

## Notes

Nuangong wan (bureau additions): the same indications as the earlier Nuangong wan (juan 9, no. 040); a different formula using adenophora, sanguisorba, astragalus, cynanchum, eucommia and others; also for infertility from a long-cold womb.
