# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷下 007 論心脾疼証 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On heart-spleen pain. For women's heart-spleen pain and stabbing blood-qi pain, give Pengjian wan or Niantong wan. Niantong wan is hot in nature and must not be used lightly; use it only when deep-lying cold and chronic chill are clearly seen. Pancong san, Jishexiang san (Keizetsukosan), Yanjian san and Ruyi wan may also be chosen. If the pain extends to the lower back and spine, or for stabbing pain of small-intestine qi, and for the weak and elderly, Da chenxiang wan (Taijinkogan) is suitable: two pills as one dose take effect at once—men with hot wine, women with vinegar decoction, or ginger decoction for those who do not drink wine. Heqi san may also be given to regulate and harmonize the spleen and stomach.

## Notes

On heart-spleen pain: Pengjian wan and Niantong wan for women's heart-spleen and blood-qi pain (Niantong wan only when deep cold is certain, being hot); Pancong san, Jishexiang san, Yanjian san and Ruyi wan; Da chenxiang wan for pain extending to the back, small-intestine qi pain and the weak or elderly; then Heqi san.
