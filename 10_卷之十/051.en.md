# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 051 挨積丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Aiji wan (Accumulation-Pushing Pill)

It treats disharmony of the spleen and stomach in children with undigested lodged stagnation, abdominal distension and borborygmus, vomiting counterflow and nausea, irregular bowel movements and reduced intake of milk and food, or gan diarrhoea and accumulation diarrhoea with sour, smelly stools. It also treats men and women with discomfort of the chest and diaphragm, wine accumulation and food accumulation, vomiting counterflow and nausea, vomiting and diarrhoea and spleen pain.

jing sanleng (sparganium; blast-fried)　dingxiang pi (clove bark; not exposed to fire; 3 liang each)　dingxiang (clove; not exposed to fire)　qingpi (green tangerine peel; white removed; 1 liang each)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried)　badou (croton seed; skin, membrane and oil removed; 2½ *qian* each)

Make the above into a fine powder, mix in the croton seed evenly, and form pills the size of millet grains with flour-and-vinegar paste. Take fifty to sixty pills each time; a two-year-old may take seven to ten pills; swallow with ginger decoction, or boiled water; at any time, adjusted further for the child's age. This medicine does not use rhubarb, sal ammoniac, mercury powder and the like; all its drugs are warm in nature. Taken regularly it disperses accumulated stagnation, improves feeding, removes yellowness and grows flesh.

## Notes

Aiji wan (Baoqing-era additions): for abdominal distension, borborygmus, vomiting, irregular stools, poor feeding and gan or accumulation diarrhoea with sour, smelly stools from disharmony of the spleen and stomach and lodged stagnation in children, and for chest discomfort, wine and food accumulation, vomiting, diarrhoea and spleen pain in adults.
- Sparganium and green tangerine peel break accumulations; croton seed purges; clove, clove bark and dried ginger warm. It notes that it avoids rhubarb, sal ammoniac and mercury and uses only warm drugs.
