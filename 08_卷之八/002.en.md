# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 002 神応丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shenying wan (Divinely Responsive Pill)

It treats all lumbar pain: insufficiency of the kidney meridian ridden by wind-cold with lumbar pain as if broken, drawing into the spine, difficulty bending forward and back and difficulty turning over; or taxation damage of the kidneys from excessive labor; or damage to the lower back by earth qi from sleeping in a cold, damp place; or injury from a fall; or wind-cold lodging and contending.

weilingxian (clematis root; earth removed; 20 liang)　danggui (toki; Chinese angelica)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed; 10 liang each)

Powder the above and form pills the size of wutong seeds with flour paste boiled in wine. Take fifteen pills each time with warm wine or decocted fennel decoction, before meals. Women take them with decocted cinnamon-heart decoction, increasing to twenty pills. Pregnant women must not take it. Avoid tea.

## Notes

Shenying wan: for lumbar pain as if broken, radiating to the back, with difficulty bending and turning, whether from wind-cold riding on an insufficient kidney meridian, overwork, sleeping on cold damp ground, a fall or wind-cold. Contraindicated in pregnancy.
- Mainly clematis root, with angelica and cinnamon.
