# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 024 当帰建中湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Danggui jianzhong tang (Tokikenchuto; Angelica Center-Fortifying Decoction)

It treats all deficiency damage of blood and qi in women and postpartum taxation damage with weakness and insufficiency, gripping pain in the abdomen, feeble breathing and scant qi, cramping of the lower abdomen with pain drawing into the lower back and spine, occasional spontaneous sweating and no desire for food.

danggui (toki; Chinese angelica; 4 liang)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed; 3 liang)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried; 2 liang)　bai shaoyao (shakuyaku; white peony root; 6 liang)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one and a half cups of water, five slices of ginger and one jujube broken up, decoct together down to one *zhan*, remove the dregs and take it hot on an empty stomach before meals. From delivery until the end of the first month, taking three doses a day makes one robust.

## Notes

Danggui jianzhong tang: for deficiency of blood and qi in women and postpartum taxation damage with weakness, gripping abdominal pain, breathlessness, lower abdominal cramping radiating to the back, spontaneous sweating and poor appetite; daily use through the first postpartum month is recommended. Still used today for postpartum abdominal pain and dysmenorrhea.
- Guizhi tang (Keishito) with doubled peony plus angelica (the *Jingui yaolüe* Neibu danggui jianzhong tang without malt sugar).
