# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 090 禁水法 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Method of Spellbinding the Water

When water is stored at the time of delivery, recite: "Namo Three Jewels water! Water in a well is well water, water in a river is river water, water in a vessel is pure water, water in the Dharma is true water. Knowing yourself not true, do not pose as true water. With purity hold the turbid; with uprightness subdue the pathogen. Day Roamer and Night Killer, General of the Five Earths, Green Dragon and White Tiger, Vermilion Bird and Dark Warrior, Zhaoyao and Heavenly Dog, Xuanyuan and Nüba, Heaven Swallower and Earth Swallower, Hanging Corpse and Closed Belly, the taboos of the Six Jia, the Twelve Divine Kings, Earth Talisman and Hidden Spirit—each keep to your place; you must not stir, you must not offend. If any stir, if any offend, I will apply the divine spell and seize your form. *Akhani abhira makutari bhatiri svāhā*."

## Notes

Method of spellbinding the water: an incantation recited when storing water for delivery, mixing Buddhist elements (the Three Jewels, a dhāraṇī) with Daoist malign spirits (Day Roamer, Night Killer, Green Dragon and White Tiger, Vermilion Bird and Dark Warrior, Hanging Corpse, Closed Belly and others). The closing dhāraṇī is transliterated, not translated.
