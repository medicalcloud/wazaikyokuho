# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 096 逐日産母生子宜向方 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Directions the Mother Should Face for Birth, by Day

On *zi*, *wu*, *mao* and *you* days she should face south; on *yin*, *shen*, *si* and *hai* days, northwest; on *chen*, *xu*, *chou* and *wei* days, southeast.

## Notes

Directions the mother should face for birth, by day: the favorable direction for the mother to face when giving birth, according to the earthly branch of the day.
