# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 060 急風散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jifeng san (Acute Wind Powder)

It treats men and women with one-sided and general headache, head-clamping wind, and pain at the temples (taiyang point) so that they cannot rest sitting or lying.

raw chuanwu (Sichuan aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　chensha (cinnabar; ground and water-ground; 2 liang each)　raw nanxing (arisaema; washed, skin removed; 4 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder; each time, mix it with wine and smear it on the painful place. It also treats children's wind damage with nasal congestion and clear nasal discharge: mix with wine and smear it on the fontanelle. It must not be taken internally.

## Notes

Jifeng san (Baoqing-era additions): an external application for one-sided and general headache and pain at the temples; for children's colds it is smeared on the fontanelle.
- Three ingredients (Sichuan aconite, arisaema, cinnabar); internal use is forbidden.
