# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 087 入月安産図 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Ruyue anchan tu (Chart for Safe Delivery in the Final Month)

For childbirth, on the first day of entering the final month, [this chart] is pasted on the due-north wall inside the bedchamber. The methods for safe delivery and for storing the placenta are all laid out within the bedchamber. For each month, the safe delivery and storage of the placenta and the avoidance of the directions of malign spirits are all changed with the solar terms; when the term of the next month begins, change at once to the next month's delivery chart. After delivery, the soiled, unclean water is discarded in the direction in which the placenta is stored, regardless of distance; above all, avoid the direction of "Closed Belly".

## Notes

Ruyue anchan tu (from here the section on rituals and prohibitions for childbirth at the end of juan 9): a chart for safe delivery is pasted on the north wall of the bedchamber at the start of the final month and replaced with the next month's chart at each solar term; soiled water is discarded toward the direction where the placenta is stored, avoiding the "Closed Belly" direction. The chart itself is not included in the base text.
