# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 029 論傷寒陰厥証 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On the yin reversal pattern of cold damage. If from the onset of illness the limbs are reversal-cold, gooseflesh rises on the body, the bowels are irregular with diarrhoea or vomiting, this is cold reversal; give Lizhong wan, Lizhong tang and the like.

## Notes

On yin reversal: cold limbs, gooseflesh and irregular bowels with diarrhoea or vomiting from the very onset indicate cold (yin) reversal, treated with Lizhong wan or Lizhong tang.
