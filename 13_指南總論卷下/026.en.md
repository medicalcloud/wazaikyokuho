# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷下 026 論腸風、痔瘻証 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On the patterns of intestinal wind, piles and fistulas. For bleeding from intestinal wind or visceral toxin, or constipation with heat, give Sanhuang wan or Huanglian ejiao wan swallowed with strongly decocted costus decoction. For heat, give Jisu wan, Changfeng hei san, Jinxie dan, Wanying wan, or Siwu tang decocted with schizonepeta and saposhnikovia added. For attacks of the various piles, give Diaochang wan. For those not yet burst, [give] Qudu san, and also apply pig's bile.

## Notes

On intestinal wind, piles and fistulas: Sanhuang wan or Huanglian ejiao wan with costus decoction for bleeding from intestinal wind or visceral toxin with constipation and heat; Jisu wan, Changfeng hei san, Jinxie dan, Wanying wan or Siwu tang with schizonepeta and saposhnikovia for heat; Diaochang wan for attacks of piles; Qudu san and pig's bile applied for unburst piles.
