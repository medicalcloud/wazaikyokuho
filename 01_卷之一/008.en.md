# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 008 竜脳天麻煎 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Longnao tianma jian (Borneol and Gastrodia Syrup)

It treats all wind and paralytic wind, with loss of control of half the body, deviation of the mouth and eyes, sluggish speech with much drool, and a clouded, confused spirit; or cramping of the sinews and vessels, numbness all over, pain in all the joints and trembling of the hands and feet; and wind toxin of the kidneys attacking upward, with deficiency swelling of the head and face, tinnitus and hardness of hearing, nasal congestion and a dry mouth, phlegm and drool that will not clear, pouring downward into the lower back and thighs, with slack, weak legs and knees, swelling, pain and sores. It also treats women's blood wind attacking and pouring, with body pain, a puffy face and emaciated flesh, a bitter mouth and dry tongue, spinning head and dizzy eyes, and drowsiness with much sleep; or itchy skin with dormant papules (urticaria) turning into sores; dark wind combined with brain wind, and one-sided or general headache. It treats all of these.

tianguazi (melon seeds; washed clean in hot water)　fupingcao (duckweed; sorted and washed clean)　chuanwu (Sichuan aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　diyu (sanguisorba; sprouts removed, scraped clean)　heishen (scrophularia; washed clean, dried over a fire; 50 liang each)　tianma (gastrodia; sprouts removed; 100 liang)

Make the above six ingredients into a fine powder, dissolve them together with snow water and white granulated honey, 15 jin 10 liang each, strain through a silk bag and simmer over a gentle fire in a silver or stone vessel to a thick paste.

raw longnao (borneol; ground; 8 liang)　shexiang (musk; ground; 4 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder. Except for the borneol and musk, knead [the powders] evenly with the gastrodia-aconite paste; when it has cooled, add the borneol and musk and knead evenly again. Pound a thousand-odd times in a mortar and roll into sticks. Each dose is a piece the size of a soap-pod seed, chewed together with mint and taken with tea or wine as preferred, at any time. For paralytic wind, taking it together [with other medicines] shows effect. For tetanus from wounds, take it with black soybean-steeped wine. To induce sweating, take three doses with roasted scallion and hot wine. It may also be taken regularly.

## Notes

Longnao tianma jian: for paralysis and speech disorders after wind-strike, cramping and numbness, wind toxin of the kidneys (puffy face, tinnitus, weak legs), women's blood wind, urticaria and headache.
- Based on gastrodia: melon seeds, duckweed, sanguisorba, scrophularia and aconite are made into a paste with snow water and honey, borneol and musk are added, and the mass is rolled into sticks that are chewed.
- The Siku quanshu edition reads 甜瓜仁 for 甜瓜子 and 錠子 for 挺子 (see the variants).
