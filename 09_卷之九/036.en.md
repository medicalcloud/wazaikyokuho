# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 036 黒神散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Heishen san (Black Spirit Powder)

It treats all postpartum disorders in women: lochia not fully discharged, retained placenta, attacking surges with stuffiness and fullness of the heart and chest, or hard distension of the navel and abdomen with gripping pain; blood dizziness with clouded spirit, darkening vision and clenched jaw; and the various illnesses of postpartum static blood.

heidou (black soybean; stir-fried; half a sheng, skins removed)　shu gan dihuang (kanjio; prepared rehmannia; soaked in wine)　danggui (toki; Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed, processed with wine)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried)　shaoyao (shakuyaku; peony root)　puhuang (hoo; typha pollen; 4 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder. For each dose decoct 2 *qian* together with half a *zhan* of wine and half a *zhan* of a boy's urine and take it mixed. In acute cases take two doses in succession, at any time.

## Notes

Heishen san (Shaoxing-era additions): for retained lochia and placenta, chest stuffiness, hard umbilical distension and pain, blood dizziness with clouded consciousness, darkened vision and clenched jaw, and all disorders of postpartum static blood.
- Black soybean, typha pollen, angelica, prepared rehmannia and peony move and nourish blood; cinnamon and dried ginger warm; licorice is added; taken with wine and boy's urine.
