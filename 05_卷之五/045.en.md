# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 045 沈香鹿茸丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Chenxiang lurong wan (Jinkorokujogan; Aquilaria and Deer Antler Velvet Pill)

It is suitable for insufficient genuine qi and cold, exhausted lower origin with wringing pain of the navel and abdomen, deficiency distension of the flanks and ribs, slack, weak legs and knees, stiffness of the lower back and spine, weary limbs, a dull complexion, dry lips and mouth, dim eyes and tinnitus, palpitation and shortness of breath, many strange dreams at night and little vitality by day, joy and anger at any time, sorrow and joylessness, deficiency vexation with night sweats, tasteless food, lack of strength in movement, intercourse with ghosts in dreams at night, emissions and loss of essence, slippery frequent urination with occasional dribbling, damp itching of the genitals and impotence.

chenxiang (aquilaria; 1 liang)　fuzi (bushi; aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed; 4 liang)　baji (morinda; core removed; 2 liang)　lurong (rokujo; deer antler velvet; hair singed off, soaked in wine and roasted; 3 liang)　shu gan dihuang (kanjio; prepared rehmannia; washed clean, sprinkled with wine, steamed, dried over a fire; 6 liang)　tusizi (toshishi; dodder seed; soaked in wine, ground, dried over a fire; 5 liang)

Make the above medicines into a fine powder, grind 1½ *qian* of musk separately and mix it in evenly, and form pills the size of wutong seeds with refined honey. Take forty to fifty pills each time, swallowed on an empty stomach with good wine or salt water. Taken regularly it nourishes the genuine qi, augments essence and marrow, sharpens sight and hearing, and brightens and preserves the complexion.

## Notes

Chenxiang lurong wan (from here the layer of bureau additions): for umbilical pain, weak legs, stiff back, lassitude, dim eyes, tinnitus, palpitation, nightmares, night sweats, dream intercourse, seminal emission, frequent urination, damp genital itching and impotence from insufficient genuine qi and cold lower origin.
- Deer antler velvet, aconite, morinda and dodder supplement kidney yang; rehmannia nourishes yin; aquilaria and musk move qi.
