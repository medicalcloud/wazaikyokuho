# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 009 五福化毒丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wufu huadu dan (Five Blessings Toxin-Transforming Elixir)

It treats pent-up, accumulated toxic heat in children with fright, fear and manic agitation, red cheeks and dry throat, sores of the mouth and tongue, unquiet sleep at night, delirium with vexing thirst, and many sores and boils on the head, face and body.

jiegeng (kikyo; platycodon; lightly stir-fried)　xuanshen (scrophularia; washed, dried over a fire; 6 liang each)　qingdai (indigo; ground)　yaxiao (refined mirabilite; dehydrated)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng; rhizome head removed; 2 liang each)　fuling (bukuryo; poria; skin removed; 5 liang)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; stir-fried; 1½ liang)　yinbo (silver leaf; eight leaves, for coating)　shexiang (jako; musk; ground; half a *qian*)　jinbo (gold leaf; eight leaves, for coating)

Make the above into a fine powder, mix in the ground medicines evenly and form pills with refined honey, twelve to each liang. For each year-old child, divide one pill into four doses, dissolved in mint water. After sores and rashes, when residual toxin attacks the mouth and teeth upward with foul-smelling drool and blood, dissolve one pill in natural raw rehmannia juice and brush it inside the mouth with a chicken feather. For heat gan with yellow, wasted flesh and sparrow eyes (night blindness), dissolve it in old millet rice-washing water. Take after meals and at bedtime.

## Notes

Wufu huadu dan: for fright, mania, red cheeks, dry throat, mouth sores, insomnia, delirium, thirst and boils from accumulated toxic heat in children; for residual toxin after eruptions affecting the mouth and teeth (applied with rehmannia juice); and for heat gan with wasting and night blindness. It is mentioned in the note to Longshi san (juan 7, no. 025).
- Scrophularia, platycodon, indigo and refined mirabilite clear heat toxin; ginseng, poria and licorice supplement; musk and gold and silver leaf are added.
