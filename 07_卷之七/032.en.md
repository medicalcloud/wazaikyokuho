# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之七 032 赴筵散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Fuyan san (Banquet-Going Powder)

It can treat wind toothache and worm-eaten teeth attacking with pain that does not stop day or night, unbearable pain, unquiet sleep, receding gums exposing the roots, teeth loose and about to fall out, or puffy swelling of the cheeks and jaw and festering gums with bleeding.

liangjiang (lesser galangal; rhizome head removed)　caowu (wild aconite; skin removed)　xixin (saishin; asarum; earth and leaves removed)　jingjie (schizonepeta; stalks removed)

Take 2 liang of each of these four ingredients and mill into a powder. Each time rub a little on the painful place. Spit out any drool; it must not be swallowed. After a good while rinse the mouth with warm salt water, and the pain stops at once. For regular use as a tooth powder, mix it half and half with powdered rotten charcoal; regular use stops receding gums and bad breath, and there will never be tooth disease.

## Notes

Fuyan san (from here the layer of Wu Zhige's additions): for severe toothache from wind or worms with insomnia, receding gums, loose teeth, swollen cheeks and bleeding, festering gums; also used as a tooth powder.
- A powder of wild aconite, lesser galangal, asarum and schizonepeta rubbed on the painful place; the drool is spat out.
