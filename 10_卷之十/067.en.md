# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 067 豆蔻香連丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Doukou xianglian wan (Nutmeg, Costus and Coptis Pill)

It treats children with irregular nursing and feeding, weak, deficient intestines and stomach, and qi of cold and heat lodged in the intestines, with red and white dysentery, gripping pain within the intestines, very frequent day and night, and no desire for food. Adjust the dose for the child's size.

huanglian (coptis; fibrous roots removed, lightly stir-fried; 3 *fen*)　roudoukou ren (nutmeg kernel; two)　dingxiang (clove; 1 *fen*)　muxiang (costus root)　helile (chebule; blast-fried, stone removed; half a liang each)

Pound and sift the above into a powder and form pills the size of broomcorn millet with millet gruel. A three-year-old takes ten pills with gruel water.

## Notes

Doukou xianglian wan (Wu Zhige's additions): for red and white dysentery, gripping intestinal pain, frequent stools and poor appetite when cold and heat lodge in the weak intestines of children with irregular feeding.
- Xianglian wan (coptis, costus) plus nutmeg, clove and chebule to astringe and warm.
