# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 047 七聖散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qisheng san (Seven Sages Powder)

It treats wind-damp flowing and pouring into the meridians and collaterals, with slackness and loss of control of the limbs and joints; or pain of the legs and knees with inability to walk.

xuduan (zokudan; dipsacus)　duhuo (dokukatsu; angelica pubescens root)　fangfeng (bofu; saposhnikovia)　duzhong (tochu; eucommia)　bixie (dioscorea hypoglauca)　niuxi (goshitsu; achyranthes; soaked overnight in wine)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; equal parts)

Process each of the above medicines clean, dry them over a fire, take half a liang of each and make a fine powder. Take 2 *qian* each time mixed in warm wine.

## Notes

Qisheng san (Shaoxing-era additions): for slackness and loss of control of the limbs and leg pain from wind-damp in the meridians.
- Dipsacus, eucommia and achyranthes strengthen the lower back and legs; duhuo, saposhnikovia and dioscorea dispel wind-damp.
