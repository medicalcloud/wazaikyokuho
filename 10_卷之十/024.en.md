# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 024 水銀扁丸子 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shuiyin bian wanzi (Flat Mercury Pills)

It is suitable for fright wind with vigorous fever in children, copious drool with coarse breathing, or convulsions with eyes turned upward; as well as irregular nursing with chest fullness and vomiting counterflow, a confused, oppressed spirit, epileptic attacks and convulsions.

huangming jiao (clear yellow glue; stir-fried yellow and dry; 1 *qian* 3 *zi*)　nifen (calomel)　ganxie (dried scorpion; whole)　baicaoshuang (pot soot; ground)　niuhuang (goo; bovine bezoar; ground)　qianshuang (lead acetate; ground)　qingdai (indigo; ground; 1 *fen* each)　badou (hazu; croton seed; skin, membrane and oil removed, boiled yellow)　heiqian (black lead; made into granules with the mercury)　shuiyin (suigin; suigin; mercury; 1 liang each)　xiangmo (fragrant ink; burned and quenched; 3 *qian*)

Make the above into a fine powder, mix in the ground medicines evenly, form pills the size of mung beans with old cooked millet and pinch them flat. A one-year-old takes one pill, a two-year-old two, a three-year-old three, and four years and above four, with dried-persimmon decoction; mint decoction is also fine. Adjust for deficiency or excess, aiming for the passing of green, sticky, slippery drool; after feeding. This medicine must not be dissolved or crushed.

## Notes

Shuiyin bian wanzi: for fright wind with high fever, drool, dyspnea, convulsions and upward gaze in children, and for chest fullness, vomiting, confusion and epileptic convulsions from irregular feeding.
- Mercury, lead, calomel, lead acetate and croton seed purge phlegm and drool; scorpion, bovine bezoar and indigo settle; pot soot, ink and glue are added; flattened pills, one per year of age. It contains mercury and lead.
