# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 006 八味丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bawei wan (Eight-Ingredient Pill)

It treats deficient, depleted kidney qi with cold, exhausted lower origin, pain of the navel and abdomen, frequent urination at night, slack, weak legs and knees, weary limbs, a dark sallow complexion and no desire for food. It is also suitable for leg qi surging upward with insensibility of the lower abdomen; for insufficiency in deficiency taxation with thirst and desire to drink water, heavy painful lower back, cramping of the lower abdomen and difficult urination; or for men's dispersion-thirst with, on the contrary, copious urination; and for women's shifted bladder with blocked urination.

mudanpi (moutan bark)　bai fuling (white poria)　zexie (alisma; 3 liang each)　shu gan dihuang (prepared rehmannia; 8 liang)　shanzhuyu (cornus)　shanyao (Chinese yam; 4 liang each)　fuzi (aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed; 2 liang each)

Powder the above and form pills the size of wutong seeds with refined honey. Take fifteen to twenty-five pills each time with warm wine, on an empty stomach before meals, twice a day. Taken for a long time it strengthens the original yang, augments essence and marrow, quickens the blood and preserves the complexion, strengthens the will and lightens the body.

## Notes

Bawei wan: for umbilical and abdominal pain, frequent nocturnal urination, weak legs, lassitude and dark complexion from deficient kidney qi and cold lower origin, and for leg qi rising with lower abdominal insensibility, thirst, back pain, lower abdominal cramping and difficult urination in deficiency taxation, men's dispersion-thirst with copious urine, and women's shifted bladder with urinary retention. It corresponds to Shenqi wan (Bawei dihuang wan) of the *Jingui yaolüe* and is still widely used for kidney-deficiency patterns.
- Prepared rehmannia, cornus, Chinese yam, alisma, poria and moutan (the later Liuwei dihuang wan) plus aconite and cinnamon. The indications combine the *Jingui yaolüe* clauses on leg qi rising, deficiency-taxation back pain, dispersion-thirst and shifted bladder; the *Jingui*'s cinnamon twig appears here as cinnamon bark.
