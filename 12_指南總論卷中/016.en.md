# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 016 論諸風痰逆嘔吐 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On phlegm counterflow and vomiting in the various winds. When phlegm counterflow and vomiting occur at the onset of the various winds, give Wenzhong huatan wan, Jupi banxia tang (Kippihangeto), Huoxiang tang or Banxia san (Hangesan); the vomiting stops at once. They may be taken regularly.

## Notes

On phlegm counterflow and vomiting at the onset of the various winds: Wenzhong huatan wan, Jupi banxia tang, Huoxiang tang and Banxia san.
