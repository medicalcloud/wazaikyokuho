# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 021 香薷丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xiangru wan (Elsholtzia Pill)

It is suitable for adults and children damaged by summer heat with latent heat, agitation and thirst, dimmed vision and oppression, clouding and dizziness of the head and eyes, vexing fullness of the chest and diaphragm, retching and nausea, a bitter mouth and dry tongue, weary limbs and no desire for food; or outbreaks of sudden turmoil with vomiting, diarrhea and cramps.

xiangru (elsholtzia; earth removed)　zisu (perilla; stems and leaves, coarse stalks removed)　dried mugua (chaenomeles; 1 liang each)　dingxiang (clove)　fushen (poria with root; wood removed)　tanxiang (sandalwood; chopped)　huoxiang leaves (agastache)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried; 5 *qian* each)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills with refined honey, thirty pills to each liang. Take one or two pills each time, chewed thoroughly, with warm water; they may also be dissolved in freshly drawn water. Children take half a pill. At any time.

## Notes

Xiangru wan: for summer heat damage with heat, thirst, dizziness, chest oppression, nausea, lassitude and poor appetite, and for vomiting, diarrhea and cramps in sudden turmoil.
- Elsholtzia, perilla and agastache scatter summer heat-damp; chaenomeles treats cramps; clove and sandalwood warm the stomach; poria with root calms the heart.
