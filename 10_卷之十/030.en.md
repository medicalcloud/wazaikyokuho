# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 030 辰砂金箔散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Chensha jinbo san (Cinnabar and Gold Leaf Powder)

It treats evil heat of the heart and diaphragm in children with an unsettled spirit and will, fright, fear and vexing thirst, abstraction and fearful palpitation, unquiet sleep at night, delirious and manic speech, and sores on the gums; as well as phlegm repletion with cough and blockage of the throat and diaphragm.

chensha (cinnabar; ground and water-ground; 70 liang)　renshen (ginseng; rhizome head removed)　fuling (poria; skin removed)　yaxiao (refined mirabilite; dehydrated; 30 liang each)　jiegeng (platycodon; 50 liang)　gefen (clamshell powder; ground and water-ground; 80 liang)　gancao (licorice; stir-fried; 25 liang)　jinbo (gold leaf; 200 leaves, mixed into the medicine)　sheng naozi (raw borneol; ground; 2 liang)

For adults and children with swollen, painful throat and sores of the mouth and tongue, sprinkle a little on the affected place each time and swallow the saliva, with immediate effect. For diaphragm heat in adults, take 1 *qian* each time mixed in fresh water, after meals and at bedtime.

## Notes

Chensha jinbo san: for an unsettled spirit, fright, vexing thirst, abstraction, palpitation, insomnia, delirium and gum sores from evil heat of the heart and diaphragm in children, and for phlegm cough and throat blockage. Sprinkled on for sore throat and mouth sores; taken in water for diaphragm heat in adults.
- Cinnabar, gold leaf, borneol and refined mirabilite clear heat and settle; clamshell powder and platycodon transform phlegm; ginseng, poria and licorice are added.
