# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 053 伏火二気丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Fuhuo erqi dan (Fire-Subduing Two Qi Elixir)

It treats deficient, damaged true origin with deficient, injured essence and marrow, insufficient kidney qi, a black face and scorched ears, deficiency below and exuberance above with dizziness of the head and eyes, stabbing pain of the heart and abdomen, stomach reflux and vomiting, night sweats from deficiency/consumption, dyspnea and fullness from water qi, and complete inability to take food; and women with long-standing cold of blood and qi, metrorrhagia and metrostaxis, and conglomerations, lumps and aggregations. This medicine seizes the creative power of yin and yang and aids the marvel of the heart and kidneys interacting and nourishing each other; it greatly supplements the various deficiencies.

liuhuang (sulfur; 4 liang)　heixi (black tin, i.e. lead)　shuiyin (suigin; mercury)　dingxiang (clove; not exposed to fire)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; half a liang each)

First melt the black tin, then add the mercury and form granules; combine with the sulfur and grind again to a black-gray color; next add the remaining medicines and grind evenly; form pills the size of wutong seeds with paste boiled in the natural juice of fresh ginger. Take ten to fifteen pills each time, swallowed with strongly decocted ginger decoction, on an empty stomach before meals.

## Notes

Fuhuo erqi dan (bureau additions): for a dark face, dizziness with exuberance above and deficiency below, stabbing pain, stomach reflux, night sweats, dyspnea from water qi and inability to eat from deficient true origin and insufficient kidney qi, and for women's cold, metrorrhagia and metrostaxis and conglomerations.
- A mineral elixir of sulfur, black tin (lead) and mercury with clove and dried ginger (of the type of the later Heixi dan [Kokushakutan]). It contains lead and mercury, is highly toxic and is not used today.
