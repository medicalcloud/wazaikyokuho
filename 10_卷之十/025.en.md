# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 025 牛黄膏 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Niuhuang gao (Bovine Bezoar Paste)

It treats fright, transforms drool, cools the diaphragm, settles the heart, removes evil heat and stops phlegm cough.

gefen (clamshell powder; ground and water-ground; 200 liang)　yaxiao (refined mirabilite; dehydrated, ground)　zhusha (cinnabar; ground and water-ground; 10 liang each)　renshen (ginseng; 25 liang)　xionghuang (realgar; ground and water-ground; 75 liang)　longnao (borneol; ground; 4 liang)　gancao (licorice; scorched; 50 liang)　jinbo, yinbo (gold and silver leaf; 200 leaves each, for coating)　niuhuang (bovine bezoar; 2 liang, ground separately)

Make the above into a fine powder, knead with refined honey, make twenty pills from each 1 liang 8 *qian*, and coat them with gold and silver leaf. For a one-year-old, give a piece the size of a mung bean each time dissolved in warm mint water, adjusting at the time according to age; after meals.

## Notes

Niuhuang gao: treats fright, transforms drool, cools the diaphragm, settles the heart, removes evil heat and stops phlegm cough in children.
- Bovine bezoar, borneol, cinnabar, realgar, refined mirabilite and gold and silver leaf clear heat and settle; clamshell powder transforms phlegm; ginseng and licorice are added.
