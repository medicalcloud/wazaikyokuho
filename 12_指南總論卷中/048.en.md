# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 048 論傷寒後調理 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On recuperation after cold damage. Cold damage fundamentally has no supplementing method; one must not supplement with overly warming medicines. If supplemented too much, the fever returns. Only slightly warm medicines should be used to recuperate; simply give Shen ling baizhu san. For the weak and elderly, recuperate with Jiahe san and the like.

## Notes

On recuperation after cold damage: there is basically no supplementing method in cold damage, and over-warming supplementation brings back the fever; use mildly warm Shen ling baizhu san, and Jiahe san for the weak and elderly.
