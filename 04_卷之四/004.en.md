# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之四 004 辰砂化痰丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Chensha huatan wan (Cinnabar Phlegm-Transforming Pill)

It treats wind and transforms phlegm, calms the spirit and settles the will, frees the throat and diaphragm, clears the head and eyes, stops cough and removes vexation and oppression.

baifan (alum; calcined, ground separately)　chensha (cinnabar; water-ground; half a liang each)　nanxing (arisaema; blast-fried; 1 liang)　banxia (hange; pinellia; washed seven times, pounded with ginger juice and made into leaven; 3 liang)

Powder the alum, pinellia leaven and arisaema, mix evenly, form pills the size of wutong seeds with flour paste boiled in ginger juice, and coat them separately with powdered cinnabar. Take ten pills each time with ginger decoction, after meals. It also treats children's phlegm-cough from wind congestion: a child of one year takes one pill, crushed, with ginger and mint decoction.

## Notes

Chensha huatan wan: transforms wind-phlegm, calms the spirit, clears the throat, head and eyes, and stops cough and vexation; also for children's phlegm-cough.
- Pinellia leaven, arisaema and alum transform phlegm; cinnabar calms the spirit.
