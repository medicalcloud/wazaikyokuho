# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 001 蘇合香丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Suhexiang wan (Sokokogan; Storax Pill)

It treats corpse transmission and bone steaming, lingering consumption and atrophy of the lung lobes, infixation and visiting-hostility, ghost qi and sudden heart pain, vomiting and diarrhea of sudden turmoil, seasonal qi, ghosts and goblins, miasmic malaria, sudden red and white diarrhea, static blood and amenorrhoea, strings and aggregations, clove sores and fright epilepsy, ghost hostility striking a person, children vomiting milk, and adults' fox and raccoon-dog [possession] and similar illnesses. (The formula for Shexiang suhexiang wan [Jakosokokogan] appears later.)

baizhu (byakujutsu; white atractylodes)　qing muxiang (aristolochia root)　wuxi shavings (black rhinoceros horn)　xiangfuzi (cyperus; stir-fried, hairs removed)　zhusha (cinnabar; ground and water-ground)　helile (kariroku; chebule; roasted, skin removed)　bai tanxiang (white sandalwood)　anxixiang (benzoin; powdered separately and simmered into a paste with 1 *sheng* of ash-free wine)　chenxiang (aquilaria)　shexiang (jako; musk; ground)　dingxiang (clove)　biba (long pepper; 2 liang each)　longnao (borneol; ground)　suhexiang oil (storax; put into the benzoin paste; 1 liang each)　xunluxiang (frankincense; ground separately; 1 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder, add the ground medicines and mix evenly, form a mass with the benzoin paste and refined white honey, and for each dose roll pills the size of wutong seeds. Early in the morning take well-flower water, warm or cold as preferred, and dissolve four pills in it. The elderly and children may take one pill. It may also be dissolved in warm wine; in all cases take it on an empty stomach. Wrap one pill the size of a pellet in waxed paper, put it in a scarlet silk bag and wear it over the heart; no evil spirit will dare approach.

## Notes

Suhexiang wan (the first formula of juan 3, "treating all qi disorders"): a representative warm orifice-opening formula for acute illnesses from pathogenic qi or qi blockage—corpse transmission (identified with tuberculosis), malign-qi strike, ghost qi, sudden heart pain, sudden turmoil, miasmic malaria, static blood and fright epilepsy.
- Many aromatics (storax, benzoin, musk, borneol, aquilaria, clove, sandalwood, frankincense, aristolochia, cyperus) with rhinoceros horn and cinnabar to calm and atractylodes and chebule to protect; it is also worn as an amulet against evil.
