# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 017 五苓散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wuling san (Goreisan; Five-Ingredient Poria Powder)

It is suitable for cold damage and warm-heat disease in which exterior and interior have not yet resolved, with headache and fever, dry mouth and throat, vexing thirst with drinking of water, or vomiting as soon as water goes in, or difficult urination; and when sweating has resolved the exterior but vexing thirst does not stop. It also treats vomiting and diarrhea in sudden turmoil (huoluan) with agitation, thirst and drinking.

zexie (takusha; alisma; 25 liang)　baizhu (byakujutsu; white atractylodes)　zhuling (chorei; polyporus; skin removed)　chi fuling (bukuryo; red poria; skin removed; 15 liang each)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed; 10 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder. Take 2 *qian* each time mixed in hot water, at any time; after taking it drink plenty of hot water, and recovery follows as soon as there is sweat. It also treats static heat in the interior with jaundice of the body: mix it in strongly decocted Yinchenhao tang (Inchinkoto; Virgate Wormwood Decoction) and take before meals. For jaundice with thirst, and for summer heat stroke with drinking, it may also be mixed in water. For children add a little powdered white atractylodes. If deficiency heat develops, add a little powdered silky astragalus and ginseng.

## Notes

Wuling san: for unresolved exterior and interior with vexing thirst, water counterflow (vomiting as soon as one drinks) and difficult urination (*Shanghan lun* clauses 71, 74, etc.), for vomiting, diarrhea and thirst in sudden turmoil, jaundice and thirst in summer heat. It is still widely used for thirst, reduced urine, edema, vomiting and diarrhea.
- Five ingredients: alisma, atractylodes, polyporus, poria (here red poria) and cinnamon (here cinnamon bark). Drinking plenty of hot water afterwards to sweat follows the *Shanghan lun*; for jaundice it is taken in Yinchenhao tang (close to Yinchen wuling san).
