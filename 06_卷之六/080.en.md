# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 080 黄耆湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangqi tang (Astragalus Decoction)

It treats constipated, rough stools in the elderly.

mian huangqi (cotton astragalus)　chenpi (aged tangerine peel; white pith removed; half a liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder. For each dose of 3 *qian*: grind 1 *ge* of hemp seed to a pulp, pour in water and obtain one *zhan* of milky liquid, strain off the dregs and decoct it in a silver or stone vessel; when it froths like milk, add one large spoonful of white honey, bring to a boil again, mix in the medicine powder and take it on an empty stomach before meals. Even severe constipation is cured within two doses, and with regular use there is no further trouble with constipation. This medicine is neither cold nor drying, and its effect is divine.

## Notes

Huangqi tang (bureau additions): for constipation in the elderly.
- Powdered astragalus and tangerine peel taken in hemp-seed milk with white honey; regarded as a formula for constipation from qi deficiency.
