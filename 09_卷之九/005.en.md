# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 005 牡丹煎丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Mudan jian wan (Moutan Syrup Pill)

It treats women whose *Chong* and *Ren* are fundamentally deficient with cold harbored in the lower abdomen, or with taxation damage from childbirth in which wind-cold in the womb contends with blood and qi and binds into conglomerations and gatherings, hard lumps that come and go, stabbing pain of the navel and abdomen, tension of the flanks and ribs, painful, heavy lower back and knees, cramping, swelling and fullness, stiffness of the back and neck, and numbness of the hands and feet; or irregular menses, or static blood stagnant, rough and blocked, or flooding, metrostaxis and vaginal discharge, cold pain of the lower abdomen, chills and fever with night sweats, aching limbs, a withered yellow complexion, many dark spots and freckles, emaciation with little strength, frequent fright palpitations and no desire for food.

yanhusuo (corydalis)　suosharen (amomum seed; half a liang each)　chi shaoyao (shakuyaku; red peony root)　mudanpi (moutan bark; 1 liang each)　shanzhuyu (sanshuyu; cornus)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried; half a liang each)　longgu (ryukotsu; dragon bone; finely ground and water-ground)　shu gan dihuang (kanjio; prepared rehmannia; soaked in wine)　binglang (areca)　qianghuo (notopterygium; 2 liang each)　gaoben (kohon; Chinese lovage; earth removed)　wuweizi (gomishi; schisandra)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng)　baizhi (byakushi; angelica dahurica)　danggui (toki; Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed, soaked in wine)　gan shanyao (dried Chinese yam)　zexie (takusha; alisma)　xuduan (zokudan; dipsacus; thin pieces)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　bai fuling (bukuryo; white poria)　baizhu (byakujutsu; white atractylodes)　fuzi (bushi; aconite; skin and navel removed)　muxiang (mokko; costus root)　niuxi (goshitsu; achyranthes; shoots removed, soaked in wine overnight, dried over a fire)　bixie (dioscorea hypoglauca; blast-fried, powdered, stir-fried until cooked; 1 liang each)　shihu (sekkoku; dendrobium; roots removed, soaked in wine; 3 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder, knead with refined honey and form pills the size of wutong seeds. Take twenty to thirty pills each time with warm wine or vinegar decoction, on an empty stomach before meals, twice a day. It is not suitable during pregnancy.

## Notes

Mudan jian wan: for conglomerations (hard lumps, stabbing umbilical pain, flank tension, back and knee pain, swelling, stiff back and neck, numb hands and feet) when wind-cold in the womb binds with blood and qi in women with deficient *Chong* and *Ren* or postpartum damage; also for irregular menses, amenorrhea from stasis, flooding, discharge, cold lower abdominal pain, chills and fever, night sweats, sallow complexion, facial spots, weakness, fright palpitations and poor appetite. Not for use in pregnancy.
- Moutan, red peony and corydalis move static blood; prepared rehmannia, angelica, ginseng, atractylodes, Chinese yam, cornus, dendrobium and others supplement; aconite, cinnamon and dried ginger warm; notopterygium, Chinese lovage and angelica dahurica dispel wind.
