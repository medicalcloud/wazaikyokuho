# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 058 黒神丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Heishen wan (Black Spirit Pill)

It is suitable for men and women with paralysis of the left or right side, stubborn numbness of the feet and hands, pain of the lower back and knees, and pain running through all the joints of the four limbs. (Another formula [of the same name] appears later.)

shu gan dihuang (prepared rehmannia; washed clean)　chixiaodou (rice bean; raw)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried)　gaoben (Chinese lovage; washed, rhizome head removed)　mahuang (ephedra; chopped, nodes removed, scum removed in hot water)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage; 6 liang each)　qianghuo (notopterygium; not exposed to fire)　gansong (nardostachys; washed free of earth)　danggui (Chinese angelica; washed, rhizome head removed; 3 liang each)　chuanwu (Sichuan aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　gancao (licorice; chopped; 18 liang each)　huoxiang (agastache; washed free of earth)　fragrant ink (burned and quenched in vinegar; half a jin each)　caowu (wild aconite; blast-fried, skin and tips removed; 1 jin)　baizhi (angelica dahurica; 12 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of longan fruits with flour paste boiled in water. Take one or two pills each time, chewed thoroughly, with tea or wine as preferred. It is also suitable for women's blood wind with pain of the feet and hands, and for injuries from blows.

## Notes

Heishen wan (Baoqing-era additions): for paralysis, stubborn numbness, lumbar and knee pain and wandering joint pain, women's blood wind and injuries.
- Wild and Sichuan aconite, ephedra, notopterygium, angelica dahurica and Chinese lovage dispel wind-cold; prepared rehmannia, angelica and lovage nourish the blood. The ink gives the "black" of the name.
- Another formula of the same name appears later.
