# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 009 牛黄小烏犀丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Niuhuang xiao wuxi wan (Bezoar Minor Black Rhinoceros Horn Pill)

It treats all kinds of wind with cramping of the sinews and vessels, numbness of the hands and feet, halting and sluggish speech, deviation of the mouth and face, palpitation and abstraction, congestion of phlegm and drool, dizziness of the head and eyes, and vexing pain of the limbs and joints; also wind-strike paralysis, dark wind and epilepsy; kidney wind attacking upward with a swollen face and tinnitus, or pouring downward into the lower back and legs with heaviness and pain; and women's blood wind with spinning head and vomiting, swollen, itchy skin and pain all over the body.

tianma (gastrodia; sprouts removed; 20 liang)　chuanwu (Sichuan aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　diyu (sanguisorba; sprouts removed, washed and dried over a fire)　xuanshen (scrophularia; washed and dried over a fire; 10 liang each)

Make the above four ingredients into a fine powder; dissolve honey in a little water, put them together in a stone pot and simmer over a gentle fire, stirring, into a thick paste; let it cool, then add the following medicines.

fupingcao (duckweed; washed clean and dried over a fire)　longnao bohe leaves (borneol mint; earth removed)　tianguazi (melon seeds; 10 liang each)　raw rhinoceros horn　zhusha (cinnabar; ground and water-ground; 5 liang each)　longnao (borneol; ground)　niuhuang (bezoar; ground)　shexiang (musk; ground; 1 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder, knead it together with the paste and form pills the size of a euryale seed. Take one pill each time, chewed thoroughly, with schizonepeta tea; warm wine is also fine. At any time.

## Notes

Niuhuang xiao wuxi wan: for cramping, numbness, speech disorders, facial deviation and dizziness from wind, paralysis and epilepsy after wind-strike, kidney wind and women's blood wind.
- Like the preceding Longnao tianma jian, it is built on a paste of gastrodia, aconite, sanguisorba and scrophularia, to which rhinoceros horn, bezoar, cinnabar, borneol and musk are added.
- "Borneol mint" is a strongly aromatic variety of mint.
