# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 005 麻黄湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Mahuang tang (Ephedra Decoction)

It treats cold damage with headache, fever and aversion to wind, pain of the joints, and panting and fullness without sweating.

mahuang (ephedra; nodes removed; 3 liang)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried; 1 liang)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed; 2 liang)　xingren (apricot kernels; 70, skin and tips removed, stir-fried and ground separately into a paste)

Make the above into a coarse powder and mix in the apricot-kernel paste evenly. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water, decoct down to eight-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm, until sweat appears. If the patient already sweats spontaneously, it must not be taken. At any time.

## Notes

Mahuang tang: for taiyang cold damage without sweating (headache, fever, aversion to wind, joint pain, panting), corresponding to Mahuang tang of the *Shanghan lun* (e.g. clause 35).
- Ephedra, cinnamon (here cinnamon bark instead of the *Shanghan lun*'s cinnamon twig), apricot kernel and licorice; it is given as a boiled powder, 3 *qian* per dose.
- The prohibition when there is spontaneous sweating corresponds to the *Shanghan lun* distinction from the Guizhi tang pattern.
