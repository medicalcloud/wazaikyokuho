# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 032 銼散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cuo san (Chopped Powder)

It treats men and women with the five taxations and seven damages, affected by chill and cold, with weak qi and a deficient body, much fatigue and little strength. Taken regularly it strengthens the sinews and bones, makes the limbs light and healthy and improves eating.

tianxianteng (aristolochia stem)　qinghaozi (sweet wormwood seed; stir-fried)　sangbaipi (mulberry root bark; stir-fried)　xiangfuzi (cyperus; stir-fried)　jingjie spikes (schizonepeta)　qianhu (peucedanum; processed with ginger juice and stir-fried)　chaihu (saiko; bupleurum)　jiegeng (kikyo; platycodon)　mahuang (mao; ephedra; roots and nodes removed)　cangzhu (black atractylodes; stir-fried)　gange (dried pueraria)　chenpi (chinpi; aged tangerine peel; 10 jin each)　huixiang (fennel; stir-fried)　qinjiao (large-leaf gentian)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage)　bai shaoyao (shakuyaku; white peony root)　gaoben (kohon) (Chinese lovage)　huangqi (ogi; astragalus)　banxia (hange; pinellia; made into coarse powder and roasted with ginger juice)　qianghuo (notopterygium; 2½ jin each)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; stir-fried)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　baizhi (byakushi; angelica dahurica)　houpo (koboku; magnolia bark; coarse bark removed, stir-fried with ginger juice; 5 jin each)

Make these twenty-four ingredients into a coarse powder. For each dose take three large *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water, adding fresh ginger, smoked plum and jujube, decoct down to seven-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm. The dregs of two doses are combined and decocted as one dose.

## Notes

Cuo san (Shaoxing-era additions): a large 24-ingredient powder for weakness and fatigue in the five taxations and seven damages when affected by chill and cold.
- Ephedra, schizonepeta, bupleurum, peucedanum, pueraria, notopterygium, Chinese lovage and angelica dahurica release the exterior; black atractylodes, magnolia bark, tangerine peel, pinellia and cyperus regulate the middle; astragalus, white peony, lovage and cinnamon supplement and warm. The dregs of two doses are decocted again together.
- The base text writes 柴胡桔梗 without a space, but these are two ingredients, chaihu and jiegeng.
