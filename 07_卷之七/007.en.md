# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之七 007 菊睛丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jujing wan (Chrysanthemum Eye Pill)

It treats insufficiency of the liver and kidneys with dim, dark eyes, unclear vision, a hazy blur, constant sight of black flowers and much cold tearing. Taken for a long time it supplements insufficiency and strengthens the eyes.

gouqizi (lycium fruit; 3 liang)　baji (morinda; core removed; 1 liang)　gan juhua (sweet chrysanthemum flower; picked over; 4 liang)　congrong (cistanche; soaked in wine, skin removed, stir-fried, sliced, dried over a fire; 2 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with refined honey. Take thirty to fifty pills each time with warm wine or salt water, on an empty stomach before meals.

## Notes

Jujing wan: for dim vision, poor sight, floaters and cold tearing from insufficiency of the liver and kidneys; said to strengthen vision with long use.
- Four ingredients: chrysanthemum and lycium fruit brighten the eyes; morinda and cistanche supplement the kidneys (the same idea as the later Qiju dihuang wan).
