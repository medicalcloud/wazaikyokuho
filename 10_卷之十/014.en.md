# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 014 虎睛丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Hujing wan (Tiger Eye Pill)

It treats fright wind with vigorous fever in children, congested, stagnant phlegm and drool, a clouded spirit, much sleeping with startled crying, or convulsions with a fixed stare.

fushen (poria with root; wood removed)　tianma (gastrodia; shoots removed)　nifen (calomel; ground)　tianzhuhuang (tabasheer; ground)　hu huanglian (picrorhiza; 5 liang each)　zhusha (cinnabar; ground and water-ground; 2 liang)　shexiang (jako; musk; ground)　bai fuzi (typhonium tuber; blast-fried)　tiannanxing (arisaema; blast-fried; 3 liang each)　qingdai (indigo; ground; 7 liang)　shijunzi (quisqualis fruit; 100)　tianjiangzi (cnidocampa cocoon; lightly stir-fried; 40)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with flour paste. Give one pill per year of age dissolved in mint decoction, adjusted for deficiency or excess; after feeding.

## Notes

Hujing wan: for fright wind with high fever, stagnant phlegm, clouded spirit, somnolence with startled crying, convulsions and fixed stare in children.
- Gastrodia, arisaema, typhonium and cnidocampa cocoon dispel wind and phlegm; tabasheer, indigo, picrorhiza and calomel clear heat; poria with root, cinnabar and musk settle; quisqualis is added. Although named after tiger eye, the formula in the base text contains none.
