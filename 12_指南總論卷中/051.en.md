# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 051 論停痰証候 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On the signs of retained phlegm. When phlegm lies between the chest and diaphragm, it too can cause aversion to cold with vigorous fever, aversion to wind with spontaneous sweating and blockage of the throat; but when there is no headache and no body pain, Jinfeicao san (Kinfutsusosan), Xiaoyin wan, Chensha huatan wan, Beizhu wan and the like may all be given.

## Notes

On retained phlegm (one of the patterns resembling cold damage): phlegm in the chest can cause chills, fever, aversion to wind, sweating and throat blockage, but without headache or body pain; Jinfeicao san, Xiaoyin wan, Chensha huatan wan and Beizhu wan.
