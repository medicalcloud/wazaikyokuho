# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 063 厚朴散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Houpo san (Magnolia Bark Powder)

It treats all cases of children who have contracted wind-cold externally, with vigorous fever and aversion to cold, headache and a heavy body, cold in the middle with counterflow qi, vomiting and nausea, or reversal cold of the hands and feet, and disharmony of the spleen and stomach.

cangzhu (black atractylodes; soaked overnight in rice-washing water, black skin removed, dried over a fire)　houpo (magnolia bark; bark removed, roasted with ginger juice)　chenpi (aged tangerine peel; white pith removed; 1 liang each)　ganjiang (dried ginger; blast-fried; 3 *fen*)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried; half a liang)

Make the above into a fine powder. For a three-year-old child, take 1 *qian* each time with a small *zhan* of water, two coin-sized slices of fresh ginger and one jujube, decoct together down to five-tenths, strain off the dregs and take it hot.

## Notes

Houpo san (Wu Zhige's additions): for high fever, chills, headache and heavy body from wind-cold contracted externally, for vomiting, nausea and cold limbs from cold in the middle, and for disharmony of the spleen and stomach in children.
- Pingwei san (black atractylodes, magnolia bark, tangerine peel, licorice) plus dried ginger.
