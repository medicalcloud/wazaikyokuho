# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 056 水煮木香丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shuizhu muxiang wan (Water-Boiled Costus Root Pill)

It treats all red and white dysentery with pus and blood mixed and tenesmus; or slippery leakage of the viscera and bowels without limit day and night; or accumulated, long-standing cold with pain of the navel and abdomen and no desire for food. (Another formula appears later.)

danggui (Chinese angelica; washed, rhizome head removed)　hezi (chebule; blast-fried, stone removed)　muxiang (costus root; not exposed to fire; 6 liang each)　qingpi (green tangerine peel; white removed)　gancao (licorice; scorched red; 2 liang 4 *qian* each)　yingsuqiao (poppy husk; pulp removed; 2 liang 8 *qian*)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of a pellet with refined honey. For each dose decoct one pill in eight-tenths of a *zhan* of water down to six-tenths and take it warm on an empty stomach before meals.

## Notes

Shuizhu muxiang wan (Baoqing-era additions): for all red and white dysentery with pus and blood and tenesmus, for frequent stools from slippery viscera, and for umbilical pain and poor appetite from accumulated, long-standing cold.
- Costus and green tangerine peel move qi; chebule and poppy husk astringe; angelica and licorice are added. The pill is boiled in water before taking (hence "water-boiled"). Another Shuizhu muxiang wan follows later (juan 6, no. 059).
