# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 003 論諸風気中 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On qi-strike among the various winds. This illness mostly arises in arrogant, high-ranking people who, thwarted by some event, become angry and cannot vent it, so that counterflow qi rises, and they suddenly fall down, stupefied and unconscious, with clenched jaw and cramped hands and feet. The appearance is no different from wind-strike, except that there is no sound of drool in the mouth. This pattern is simply qi-strike, and one must not recklessly give drool-extracting or sweat-inducing medicines, which would only produce other illnesses. Simply give Qiqi tang (Seven Qi Decoction) to separate and resolve the qi and disperse what is congested and bound, and the qi will settle of itself. Qiqi tang given in succession acts quickly; Suhexiang wan (Storax Pill) may also be given.

## Notes

On qi-strike (collapse from counterflow qi after anger and the like): indistinguishable from wind-strike except for the absence of rattling drool; drool-extracting and sweating medicines must not be used; Qiqi tang and Suhexiang wan move the qi.
