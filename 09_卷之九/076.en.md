# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 076 当帰黄耆湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Danggui huangqi tang (Tokiogito; Angelica and Astragalus Decoction)

It treats postpartum pain of the lower back and legs with inability to turn over, vigorous fever with spontaneous sweating, a stiff body and shortness of breath.

danggui (toki; Chinese angelica; shoots removed; 3 liang)　huangqi (ogi; astragalus)　shaoyao (shakuyaku; peony root; 2 liang each)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take four large *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water and five slices of ginger, decoct down to seven-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm before meals.

## Notes

Danggui huangqi tang (bureau additions): for postpartum pain of the back and legs with inability to turn over, high fever, spontaneous sweating, stiffness and breathlessness.
- Three ingredients: angelica, astragalus and peony.
