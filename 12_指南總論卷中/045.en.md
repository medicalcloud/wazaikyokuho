# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 045 論傷寒後腹満 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On abdominal fullness after cold damage. For abdominal fullness after cold damage, with no desire for food, or indigestion after eating, and distension and fullness of the abdomen and flanks, give Yunqi san, Chenxiang jiangqi tang, Pengjian wan, Sishi wan or Shen ling baizhu san (Jinryobyakujutsusan). For severe cases, give Qing muxiang wan (Seimokkogan) together with Jiahe san, Si junzi tang (Shikunshito), Muxiang (mokko) fenqi wan, Muxiang san (Mokkosan) or Muxiang liuqi yin, according to deficiency or excess.

## Notes

On abdominal fullness after cold damage: Yunqi san, Chenxiang jiangqi tang, Pengjian wan, Sishi wan and Shen ling baizhu san for poor appetite, indigestion and distension; for severe cases Qing muxiang wan with Jiahe san, Si junzi tang, Muxiang fenqi wan, Muxiang san or Muxiang liuqi yin according to deficiency or excess.
