# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 084 金鎖正元丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jinsuo zhengyuan dan (Golden Lock Origin-Rectifying Elixir)

It can treat insufficient genuine qi and deficient, weak original zang organ with weary limbs, aching pain in all the joints, a clouded head with dizziness and pain, dim eyes and tinnitus, a yellow-black complexion, falling hair at the temples, swollen, itchy scalp, a clouded, weary spirit, frequently cold hands and feet, stuffiness and oppression of the heart and chest, cutting pain around the navel, aching pain of the knees and shins with inability to stand for long; or weak legs with dull pain and difficulty walking, stiffness of the lower back and spine with inability to bend forward and back, abdominal pain with stabbing qi, deficiency distension of both flanks, undigested food, irregular stools, retching counterflow and nausea, reduced eating, abstraction and forgetfulness, hurried breathing and dyspnea exhaustion, many strange dreams at night, palpitation and night sweats, slippery frequent urination, seminal emission and white turbidity; and all illnesses of deficiency cold of the original zang organ.

wubeizi (gallnut)　fuling (bukuryo; poria; skin removed; 8 liang each)　zi baji (purple morinda; core removed; 16 liang)　buguzhi (psoralea; soaked in wine, stir-fried; 10 liang)　roucongrong (cistanche; washed clean, dried over a fire)　huluba (fenugreek; stir-fried; 1 jin each)　longgu (ryukotsu; dragon bone)　zhusha (cinnabar; ground separately; 3 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder, mix in the ground medicine evenly and form pills the size of wutong seeds with wine paste. Take fifteen to twenty pills each time on an empty stomach before meals, swallowed with warm wine; salt water is also fine.

## Notes

Jinsuo zhengyuan dan (from here the layer of bureau additions): for general lassitude, joint pain, dizziness, tinnitus, hair loss, cold limbs, periumbilical pain, weak legs, stiff back, abdominal pain, indigestion, forgetfulness, dyspnea, nightmares, night sweats, frequent urination, seminal emission and turbid urine from insufficient genuine qi and weak original zang organ, and for all deficiency cold of the original zang organ.
- Morinda, psoralea, cistanche and fenugreek warm the kidneys; gallnut and dragon bone secure essence (the "golden lock"); poria and cinnabar are added.
