# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 018 論破傷風証 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On the pattern of wind through wounds. For swelling where a blow has broken the skin and wind has entered, give Shangyuan san, mixed with natural fresh-ginger juice and applied to the affected place. For internal injury, give Taiyue huoxue dan. For unceasing bleeding, give Huaruishi san.

## Notes

On wind through wounds: Shangyuan san mixed with ginger juice and applied for swelling after wind enters a wound; Taiyue huoxue dan for internal injury; Huaruishi san for unceasing bleeding.
