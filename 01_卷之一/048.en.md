# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 048 活血応痛丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huoxue yingtong wan (Blood-Quickening Pain-Answering Pill)

It treats wind-damp lodging in the kidney meridian, with congealed, stagnant blood and vessels, heavy painful lower back and thighs, inability to turn over, insensible skin and numbness of the whole body. Attacking upward, it causes deficiency swelling of the head and face and constant ringing in the ears; pouring downward, heavy painful legs and knees with little strength and difficulty walking. It also treats cramping of the neck and back that will not relax. Taken regularly it quickens the blood and vessels, strengthens sinews and bones, and makes the qi and vessels flow freely.

gouji (kuseki; cibotium; hairs removed; 4 jin)　cangzhu (black atractylodes; soaked overnight in rice-washing water, skin removed; 6 jin)　xiangfuzi (cyperus; hairs removed, stir-fried; 7½ jin)　chenpi (chinpi; aged tangerine peel; washed, stems removed; 5½ jin)　moyao (myrrh; ground separately; 12 liang)　weilingxian (clematis root; washed; 2 jin)　caowutou (wild aconite; 1½ jin, half of it blast-fried)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with flour paste boiled in wine. Take fifteen to twenty pills each time with warm wine or boiled water as preferred, at any time. When taking it for a long time, avoid peaches, plums, sparrows, pigeons and all blood products.

## Notes

Huoxue yingtong wan (Shaoxing-era additions): for wind-damp in the kidney meridian with heavy painful lower back and thighs, numbness, facial puffiness, tinnitus, leg pain and difficulty walking.
- Cibotium strengthens the lower back; black atractylodes, clematis and wild aconite dispel wind-damp; cyperus, tangerine peel and myrrh move qi and blood.
