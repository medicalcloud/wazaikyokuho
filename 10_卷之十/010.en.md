# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 010 霊砂帰命丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Lingsha guiming dan (Numinous Cinnabar Life-Returning Elixir)

It treats pent-up, accumulated evil heat in children with unremitting tidal fever, red cheeks and dry mouth, vexation and agitation of the heart and diaphragm, obstructed phlegm and drool and unquiet sleep, or fright epilepsy with tidal surging of drool and convulsions. It may also be given for accumulated stagnation not dispersing, diarrhoea lasting many days, gripping pain in the abdomen, vexing thirst and retching, unresolved by regulating medicines.

badou (croton seed; germ, membrane and skin removed, stir-fried until cooked, ground like oily flour; 315 seeds)　niuhuang (bovine bezoar; ground)　longnao (borneol; ground)　shexiang (musk; ground)　nifen (calomel; ground; 3 liang each)　chensha (cinnabar; ground and water-ground; 9 liang)　jinbo (gold leaf; ground; ninety leaves)

Grind the above together evenly; refine 6 liang of yellow wax, add 3 *fen* of white granulated honey, refine together evenly and form pills the size of mung beans. Take two pills each time with gold-and-silver mint decoction, adjusted for age. For fright epilepsy with convulsions, take them with borneol, calomel and honey decoction. If the pills are first soaked in cold water for a short time before being taken, the effect is seen even faster.

## Notes

Lingsha guiming dan: for tidal fever, red cheeks, dry mouth, agitation, phlegm, insomnia and fright epilepsy with convulsions from accumulated evil heat in children, and for prolonged diarrhoea, abdominal pain, thirst and retching from accumulated stagnation.
- The same composition as the Miaoxiang wan of juan 6, no. 007 (croton seed, bovine bezoar, borneol, musk, calomel, cinnabar, gold leaf in wax and honey), made into small pills for children. Despite the name, it contains no lingsha.
