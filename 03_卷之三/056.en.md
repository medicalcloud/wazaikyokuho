# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 056 蘇子降気湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Suzi jiangqi tang (Soshikokito; Perilla Seed Qi-Lowering Decoction)

It treats men and women with deficient yang attacking upward, qi failing to rise and descend, exuberance above with deficiency below, congestion of the diaphragm with much phlegm, discomfort of the throat, cough, deficiency vexation with drinking, clouding and dizziness of the head and eyes, lumbar pain and weak legs, weary limbs, wringing, stabbing pain of the abdomen, qi diarrhea of cold or heat, wind constipation with rough, stagnant, blocked stools, swelling of the limbs, and interference with eating.

zisuzi (perilla seed)　banxia (hange; pinellia; washed seven times in hot water; 2½ liang each)　chuan danggui (toki; Sichuan angelica; rhizome head removed; 1½ liang)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; scorched; 2 liang)　qianhu (peucedanum; rhizome head removed)　houpo (koboku; magnolia bark; coarse bark removed, mixed with ginger juice and stir-fried; 1 liang each)　rougui [cinnamon bark; skin removed; 1½ liang (one version has aged tangerine peel, white pith removed, 1½ liang)]

Make the above into a fine powder. For each dose take two large *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water, two slices of fresh ginger, one jujube and five perilla leaves, decoct together down to eight-tenths, remove the dregs and take it hot, at any time. Taken regularly it clears the spirit and smooths qi, harmonizes the five zang organs, moves stagnant qi, improves eating and removes damp qi.

## Notes

Suzi jiangqi tang (from here the layer of Baoqing-era additions): for productive cough and dyspnea, throat discomfort, dizziness, lumbar pain, weak legs, constipation and edema with exuberance above and deficiency below and deficient yang attacking upward. It is still used for chronic cough and asthma of the "excess above, deficient below" type.
- Perilla seed, pinellia, peucedanum and magnolia bark (and tangerine peel) send phlegm and qi down above; cinnamon and angelica warm and supplement below. A simplified version of the earlier Yu shanren jiangqi tang (juan 3, no. 036).
