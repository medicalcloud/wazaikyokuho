# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 031 黄連阿膠丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huanglian ejiao wan (Coptis and Donkey-Hide Gelatin Pill)

It treats deficient qi of the intestines and stomach with disharmony of cold and heat, red and white dysentery resembling fish brains, tenesmus, pain of the navel and abdomen, dry mouth with vexing thirst, and inhibited urination.

ejiao (donkey-hide gelatin; broken up, stir-fried; 1 liang)　huanglian (coptis; hairs removed; 3 liang)　fuling (poria; skin removed; 2 liang)

Make the coptis and poria together into a fine powder, knead with the gelatin powder mixed in water and form pills the size of wutong seeds. Take twenty pills each time with warm rice water, before meals.

## Notes

Huanglian ejiao wan: for red and white dysentery like fish brains, tenesmus, abdominal pain, thirst and inhibited urination from deficient qi of the intestines and stomach with disharmony of cold and heat.
- Three ingredients: coptis, donkey-hide gelatin and poria. Distinct from the Huanglian ejiao tang of the *Shanghan lun*.
