# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 076 軽脚丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qingjiao wan (Light-Foot Pill)

It treats paralysis of the left or right side with weak legs and inability to walk.

mubiezi (momordica seed; ground separately)　baijiaoxiang (liquidambar resin; ground separately)　bai shaoyao (white peony root; 2 liang each)　caowu (wild aconite; skin and tips removed; 4 liang)　chixiaodou (rice bean; 1 liang, ground separately to powder and made into paste)

Powder the above and form pills the size of wutong seeds with the rice-bean paste. Take seven pills each time, increasing to ten as needed, with warm wine or chaenomeles decoction. If the illness is in the upper body, take after meals and at bedtime; if in the lower body, on an empty stomach. Avoid hot things for a while.

## Notes

Qingjiao wan (bureau additions): for weak legs and inability to walk after paralysis.
- Wild aconite and momordica seed open the channels, liquidambar resin moves the blood, and white peony relaxes the sinews; the pills are made with rice-bean paste.
