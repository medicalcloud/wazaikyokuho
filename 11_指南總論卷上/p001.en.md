# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷上 p001 勅授太医助教前差充四川総領所検察恵民局許… (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Compiled by Xu Hong, by imperial appointment Assistant Instructor of the Imperial Medical Service, formerly dispatched as Inspector of the Benevolent Pharmacy of the Sichuan General Command.

## Notes

The compiler's attribution at the head of the General Guide, upper volume. Xu Hong held by imperial appointment the post of Assistant Instructor of the Imperial Medical Service and had previously been sent to inspect the Benevolent Pharmacy of the Sichuan General Command.
- The Siku quanshu edition gives a shorter title (see the variants).
