# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 075 二薑丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Erjiang wan (Two Gingers Pill)

It nourishes the spleen and warms the stomach, dispels cold and disperses phlegm. It greatly treats pain of the heart and spleen, loosens the chest and sends qi down, and makes food taste good. It treats all damage from cold foods.

ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried)　liangjiang (galangal; rhizome head removed)

Make the above into a fine powder in equal parts and form pills the size of wutong seeds with flour paste. Take fifteen to twenty pills each time after meals with tangerine-peel decoction. Not suitable for pregnant women.

## Notes

Erjiang wan (Wu Zhige's additions): warms the spleen and stomach, dispels cold and phlegm; for heart and spleen pain and damage from cold foods.
- Two ingredients, dried ginger and galangal ("two gingers"); not suitable in pregnancy.
