# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 006 竜脳飲子 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Longnao yinzi (Borneol Drink)

It is suitable for adults and children with pent-up, accumulated pathogenic heat, swollen, painful throat, red eyes and mouth sores, a vexed heart and nosebleed, dry throat with much thirst and unquiet sleep; it also removes phlegm-heat cough, restlessness from summer heat strike, and all wind congestion.

suoshaliren (amomum seed)　gualougen (karokon; trichosanthes root; 3 liang each)　huoxiang ye (agastache leaf; 2 liang 4 *qian*)　shigao (sekko; gypsum; 4 liang)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; stir-fried with honey; 16 liang)　da zhizi (shishi) ren (large gardenia kernel; lightly stir-fried; 12 liang)

Powder the above. Take 1 to 2 *qian* each time mixed in fresh water with honey. It also treats residual toxin of cold damage with tidal fever and deficiency sweating: take 2 *qian* of the medicine with one *zhan* of water and five or six bamboo leaves, decoct down to seven-tenths and take it warm. Take after meals in all cases.

## Notes

Longnao yinzi: for sore throat, red eyes, mouth sores, vexation, nosebleed, thirst and insomnia from accumulated pathogenic heat, for phlegm-heat cough, agitation from summer heat strike, and for tidal fever and deficiency sweating from residual toxin of cold damage.
- Gypsum, gardenia and trichosanthes root clear heat; agastache and amomum are added; licorice is abundant. Although named after borneol, the formula in the base text contains no borneol.
