# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 006 論風湿証候 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On the signs of wind-damp. It always arises from deficient interstices injured by wind and cold-damp qi. When every night, from about the third or fourth watch, there is weary pain of the lower back and spine with inability to turn over, or weary pain of the body, there is cold-damp; give Xiao xuming tang (Shozokumeito). For constipation with copious urination and body pain, give Zhufu tang (Atractylodes and Aconite Decoction). For vexing pain of the joints, give Ruxiang chentong san. For numbness of the body and weak shins, give Zhuifeng yingtong wan or Huangqi wan (Ogigan). For severe lumbar pain, give Qing'e wan.

## Notes

On the signs of wind-damp (wind and cold-damp entering deficient interstices): Xiao xuming tang for night-time back pain with inability to turn over; Zhufu tang for constipation, copious urination and body pain; Ruxiang chentong san for joint pain; Zhuifeng yingtong wan and Huangqi wan for numbness and weak shins; Qing'e wan for severe lumbar pain.
