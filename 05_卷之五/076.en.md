# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之五 076 鹿茸大補湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Lurong (rokujo) dabu tang (Deer Antler Velvet Major Supplementation Decoction)

It treats insufficiency from the various deficiencies in men and women, deficiency and damage of blood and qi after childbirth, and all deficiency and damage.

lurong (deer antler velvet; processed)　huangqi (ogi; astragalus; roasted with honey)　danggui (toki) (Chinese angelica; soaked in wine)　bai fuling (bukuryo; white poria; skin removed)　congrong (cistanche; soaked in wine)　duzhong (tochu; eucommia; stir-fried, threads removed; 2 liang each)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng)　bai shaoyao (shakuyaku; white peony root)　rougui (cinnamon bark)　shihu (sekkoku; dendrobium; soaked in wine, steamed, dried over a fire)　fuzi (bushi; aconite; blast-fried)　wuweizi (gomishi; schisandra)　banxia (hange; pinellia)　baizhu (byakujutsu; white atractylodes; roasted in embers; 1½ liang each)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; half a liang)　shu gan dihuang (kanjio; prepared rehmannia; steamed with wine, dried over a fire; 3 liang)

Cut the above into pieces. For each dose take 4 *qian* with three slices of ginger, one jujube and one *zhan* of water, decoct down to seven-tenths and take it hot on an empty stomach.

## Notes

Lurong dabu tang (Chunyou-era additions): for insufficiency from the various deficiencies in men and women, postpartum deficiency of blood and qi, and all deficiency and damage.
- A warming, supplementing formula of the Shiquan dabu tang (Juzentaihoto) type (ginseng, astragalus, atractylodes, poria, licorice, angelica, peony, rehmannia, cinnamon) without lovage, plus deer antler velvet, cistanche, eucommia, aconite, dendrobium, schisandra and pinellia.
