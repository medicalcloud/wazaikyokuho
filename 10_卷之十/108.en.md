# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 108 益智湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yizhi tang (Yakuchito; Alpinia Oxyphylla Drink)

It treats all cold qi with vomiting counterflow and nausea, distension, fullness and stabbing pain of the navel, abdomen, flanks and ribs, stuffiness and oppression of the chest and diaphragm, and reduced eating.

yizhi ren (alpinia oxyphylla kernel; 4½ jin)　jing sanleng (sparganium; roasted in embers; 1½ jin)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried; 3 liang)　qingpi (green tangerine peel)　peng ezhu (zedoary)　chenpi (chinpi; aged tangerine peel; 12 liang each)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; stir-fried; 15 jin)　yan (salt; stir-fried; 16½ jin)

Make the above into a fine powder. Take 1 *qian* each time mixed with boiling water, at any time. Taken regularly it smooths qi, loosens the middle, disperses old cold and regulates the spleen and stomach.

## Notes

Yizhi tang ("Various drinks"): a drink for vomiting, nausea, distension and stabbing pain of the abdomen and flanks, chest stuffiness and poor appetite from all cold qi.
- Alpinia oxyphylla and dried ginger warm; sparganium, zedoary and green and aged tangerine peel move qi and disperse accumulations; licorice and salt are added.
