# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 050 論傷暑吐瀉 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On vomiting and diarrhoea from summerheat damage. For summerheat strike with vomiting, fever and oppression and confusion, or sudden turmoil with vomiting and diarrhoea, give Xiaoshu wan, Wuling san, Guiling wan, Xiangru san and the like.

## Notes

On vomiting and diarrhoea from summerheat: Xiaoshu wan, Wuling san, Guiling wan and Xiangru san for vomiting, fever and oppression in summerheat strike or vomiting and diarrhoea of sudden turmoil.
