# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 071 進食散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jinshi san (Appetite-Improving Powder)

It treats deficiency cold of the spleen and stomach with no desire for food, and long-standing patients whose spleen is deficient so that they do not eat at all. After only one or two doses they suddenly feel able to eat.

qing jupi (green tangerine peel; pulp removed)　chenpi (aged tangerine peel; white pith removed)　gaoliangjiang (lesser galangal; thinly sliced, stir-fried)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried; 1 *fen* each)　caoguo rou (tsaoko flesh)　chuan wutou (Sichuan aconite; blast-fried; 3 each)　hezi (chebule; roasted, stone removed; 5)

Make the above into a fine powder. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one large *zhan* of water and five slices of fresh ginger, decoct down to seven-tenths and take it before meals.

## Notes

Jinshi san (Chunyou-era additions): for poor appetite from spleen-stomach deficiency cold and for chronic patients who eat nothing because of spleen deficiency.
- Galangal, cinnamon, tsaoko and aconite warm; tangerine peels move qi; chebule astringes; licorice harmonizes.
