# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 011 温経湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wenjing tang (Unkeito; Menses-Warming Decoction)

It treats deficiency damage of the *chong* and *ren* with irregular menses, sometimes heavy and unceasing when they come, sometimes failing to come past their time, or flooding with excessive, unceasing loss of blood. It also treats a past miscarriage with static blood retained, cramping pain of the lower abdomen, fever and diarrhea, vexing heat of the palms, and dry lips and mouth. It also treats cold in the lower abdomen with long failure to conceive.

ejiao (akyo; donkey-hide gelatin; broken up and stir-fried with clamshell powder)　danggui (toki) (Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed)　xiongqiong (Sichuan lovage)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; stir-fried)　shaoyao (shakuyaku; peony root)　mudanpi (moutan bark; 2 liang each)　banxia (hange; pinellia; washed seven times in hot water; 2½ liang)　wuzhuyu (goshuyu; evodia; washed seven times in hot water, dried over a fire, stir-fried; 3 liang)　maimendong (bakumondo; ophiopogon; core removed; 5½ liang)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one and a half *zhan* of water and five slices of fresh ginger, decoct down to eight-tenths, remove the dregs and take it hot, on an empty stomach before meals.

## Notes

Wenjing tang: for irregular menses (heavy or delayed) and flooding from deficiency damage of the *chong* and *ren*; for lower abdominal pain, fever, diarrhea, hot palms and dry lips from static blood after miscarriage; and for infertility from cold in the lower abdomen. Still used today for irregular menses, infertility and menopausal hot palms.
- The same composition as the *Jingui yaolüe* Wenjing tang (evodia, angelica, lovage, peony, ginseng, cinnamon, donkey-hide gelatin, moutan, ginger, licorice, pinellia, ophiopogon).
- Emendation: "core removed" for ophiopogon reads "qu dong" in the base text; emended following the Siku quanshu and Executive Yuan editions.
