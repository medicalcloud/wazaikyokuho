# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 031 小犀角丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xiao xijiao wan (Shosaikakugan; Minor Rhinoceros Horn Pill)

It treats intestinal abscess, breast abscess, effusion of the back and all toxic swellings; taking it transforms [the swelling] into water.

badou (hazu; croton seed; 22, skin, membrane and germ removed, stir-fried to express the oil, finely ground)　dahuang (daio; rhubarb; steamed, dried over a fire; 1 liang 1 *fen*)　xijiao (saikaku; rhinoceros horn; 3 liang)　huanglian (oren; coptis; fibrous roots removed)　zhizi (shishi; gardenia; skin removed)　gan liaolan (dried indigo knotweed)　shengma (shoma; cimicifuga)　huangqin (ogon; scutellaria)　fangfeng (bofu; saposhnikovia; rhizome head removed)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng)　danggui (toki; Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed)　huangqi (ogi; astragalus; shoots removed)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried; 1 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder, mix in the croton seed evenly, knead with refined honey and form pills the size of wutong seeds. Take three pills each time with warm water. After two or three bowel movements, eat cold gruel to stop them. If there is no purging, increase to four or five pills. At first aim for a free purge, then gradually reduce the number of pills, aiming for slightly loose stools; for the elderly and children adjust as judged. Stop when the swelling subsides and [the skin] becomes soft and moist. When yellow water is passed and the swollen place is felt to wrinkle slightly and change color, that is the sign of its dispersing. All swelling toxins disperse internally; its divine efficacy is beyond discussion. Avoid hot noodles, garlic, pork, reed shoots, fish, seaweed, Chinese cabbage, raw and cold things and sticky food.

## Notes

Xiao xijiao wan (apparently in the section on sores, swellings, wounds and fractures of juan 8): said to disperse internally intestinal abscess, breast abscess, effusion of the back and all toxic swellings.
- Croton seed and rhubarb purge; rhinoceros horn, coptis, gardenia, scutellaria, indigo knotweed and cimicifuga clear heat toxin; ginseng, astragalus and angelica supplement; saposhnikovia and licorice are added. The number of pills is adjusted by the bowel movements.
