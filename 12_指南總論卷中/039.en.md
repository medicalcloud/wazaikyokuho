# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 039 論傷寒咽喉疼痛 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On sore throat in cold damage. Sore throat in cold damage always arises from internal heat with blocked qi, yang toxin fuming upward and making the throat sore. For abundant phlegm, give Rusheng tang (Nyoseito). For severe cases, Sishun yin, Xijiao yin and Xixin san (Saishinsan) may all be taken.

## Notes

On sore throat in cold damage (internal heat, blocked qi, yang toxin fuming upward): Rusheng tang for abundant phlegm; Sishun yin, Xijiao yin and Xixin san for severe cases.
