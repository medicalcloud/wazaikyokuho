# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之二 059 藿香正気散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huoxiang zhengqi san (Agastache Qi-Righting Powder)

It is suitable for treating cold damage with headache, aversion to cold with vigorous fever and rising qi with panting and cough; the five taxations and seven damages, the eight kinds of wind-phlegm, the five kinds of diaphragm qi, cold pain of the heart and abdomen, stomach reflux with retching and nausea, qi diarrhoea and sudden turmoil, empty rumbling of the viscera and bowels, miasmic malaria of mountain mists, and deficiency swelling of the whole body; women's stabbing pain of blood and qi before and after childbirth; and children's gan damage.

dafupi (areca husk)　baizhi (angelica dahurica)　zisu (perilla)　fuling (poria; skin removed; 1 liang each)　banxia qu (pinellia leaven)　baizhu (white atractylodes)　chenpi (aged tangerine peel; white pith removed)　houpo (magnolia bark; coarse bark removed, roasted with ginger juice)　kugeng (bitter platycodon; 2 liang each)　huoxiang (agastache; earth removed; 3 liang)　gancao (licorice; mix-fried; 2½ liang)

Make the above into a fine powder. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one *zhan* of water, three coin-slices of ginger and one jujube, decoct together down to seven-tenths and take it hot. To induce sweating, cover with clothes and bedding and decoct another dose and take them together.

## Notes

Huoxiang zhengqi san (bureau additions): for headache, chills and fever and cough from external wind-cold combined with internal damp stagnation, and for vomiting and diarrhoea (sudden turmoil), cold abdominal pain, miasmic malaria, oedema, women's pain before and after childbirth, and children's gan. It is still widely used today for summer colds and gastroenteritis.
- Agastache, perilla and angelica dahurica release the exterior; areca husk, magnolia bark, tangerine peel, pinellia leaven, atractylodes, poria, platycodon and licorice regulate damp and qi; a development of the earlier Zhengqi san (juan 2, no. 030).
