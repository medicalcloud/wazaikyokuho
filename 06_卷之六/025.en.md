# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 025 霊液丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Lingye dan (Numinous Fluid Elixir)

It treats all wind-heat and accumulated heat of the zang-fu organs with toxic qi attacking upward, restlessness of the chest and diaphragm, dry, rough mouth and tongue, congestion and oppression of the heart-spirit, blockage of the throat and tasteless food.

wumei (ubai; mume fruit; stones removed, stir-fried)　hanshuishi (kansuiseki; calcite; calcined, ground and water-ground)　gualougen (karokon; trichosanthes root)　shigao (sekko; gypsum; ground)　gegen (kakkon; pueraria)　chi fuling (bukuryo; red poria; 1 liang each)　maimendong (bakumondo; ophiopogon; core removed, dried over a fire; 1½ liang)　longnao (borneol; ground separately; 1 *qian*)

Pound and sift the above into a powder, mix in the ground medicine evenly and form pills the size of a pellet with refined honey. Wrap one pill in thin silk floss, hold it in the mouth to dissolve and swallow the saliva.

## Notes

Lingye dan (bureau additions): for chest agitation, dry mouth, oppression of the heart-spirit, throat blockage and tasteless food from wind-heat and accumulated heat of the zang organs.
- Gypsum, calcite, trichosanthes root, pueraria and ophiopogon clear heat and generate fluids; mume stops thirst; red poria and borneol are added. Dissolved in the mouth.
