# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 081 秘方換腿丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Mifang huantui wan (Secret Formula Leg-Changing Pill)

It treats weakness of the kidney channel with [evil] pouring down into the lower back and knees, or exposure to wind while seeking coolness so that cold qi takes advantage, with heaviness and little strength, slow steps, cramping pain of the sinews and vessels, inability to bend and stretch, and a dull pain in the soles that hinders standing on the ground. It greatly treats dry and damp leg qi with red, swollen, painful suffering, attacks at any time, groaning and unbearable pain, fullness of qi with rapid panting, difficulty moving, a dark sallow complexion and constipation. It treats all of these.

yiyiren (coix seed)　shinanye (photinia leaf)　tiannanxing (arisaema; washed, processed with ginger, stir-fried)　chuan niuxi (Sichuan achyranthes; soaked in wine and dried over a fire)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　danggui (Chinese angelica; rhizome head removed)　tianma (gastrodia; sprouts removed)　fuzi (aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　qianghuo (notopterygium)　fangfeng (saposhnikovia; forks removed)　shihu (dendrobium; roots removed)　bixie (dioscorea hypoglauca; lightly roasted)　huangqi (astragalus; roasted with honey)　xuduan (dipsacus; 1 liang each)　cangzhu (black atractylodes; soaked in rice-washing water; 1½ liang)　binglang (areca; half a liang)　dried mugua (chaenomeles; 4 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with flour paste. Take thirty to fifty pills each time, swallowed on an empty stomach with warm wine or chaenomeles decoction, two or three doses a day. Taken regularly it relaxes the sinews and lightens the legs, and one will never suffer from leg qi. (In the past a person with this illness took it for a month and the strength of his legs suddenly became vigorous; it truly has the power of changing the legs.)

## Notes

Mifang huantui wan (bureau additions): for heavy, cramped legs and pain in the soles from cold qi invading a weak kidney channel, and for swollen, painful dry or damp leg qi with panting and constipation.
- Its composition is close to the earlier Huantui wan (juan 1, no. 073) with cinnamon, aconite and black atractylodes added.
