# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 028 青木香丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qing muxiang wan (Green Costus Pill)

It loosens the middle and frees the diaphragm, moves stagnant qi and digests food. It treats choking obstruction of the chest and diaphragm, distending pain of the abdomen and flanks, hard glomus below the heart, sounds of water in the intestines, retching with phlegm counterflow and no desire for food.

buguzhi (psoralea; stir-fried until fragrant)　bichengqie (cubeb)　binglang (areca; wrapped in sour millet rice and then in wet paper, roasted in the fire until the paper scorches, rice removed; 40 liang each)　hei qianniu (black morning-glory seed; 240 liang, stir-fried until fragrant, pounded separately to obtain 120 liang of powder)　muxiang (costus root; 20 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder, mix in the morning-glory powder evenly, gradually add clear water to the right consistency and form pills the size of mung beans. Take twenty pills each time with tea, hot water or boiled water as preferred, after meals. After each meal with wine five to seven pills may be taken. Children of one year take one pill. Pregnant women must not take it.

## Notes

Qing muxiang wan: for chest obstruction, distending pain of the abdomen and flanks, glomus below the heart, borborygmus, vomiting and poor appetite from qi stagnation.
- A large amount of morning-glory seed purges; costus, areca and cubeb move qi; psoralea warms. Contraindicated in pregnancy. "Qing muxiang" in the name seems to be used as another name for costus.
