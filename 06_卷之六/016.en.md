# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之六 016 導赤散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Daochi san (Red-Guiding Powder)

It treats adults and children with internal deficiency of the heart channel ridden by evil heat, with vexation, agitation, oppression and confusion, [the heat] transmitted down into the lower channels with red, rough, dribbling urination and fullness and pain below the navel.

sheng gan dihuang (dried raw rehmannia)　mutong (akebia)　gancao (licorice; raw; equal parts)

Cut the above into pieces. For each dose take 3 *qian* with one *zhan* of water and a few bamboo leaves, decoct together down to six-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm, at any time.

## Notes

Daochi san (Chunyou-era additions): for vexation and agitation from evil heat riding on a deficient heart channel, and for dark, rough, dribbling urination and pain below the navel when the heat is transmitted downward. Still used today for mouth ulcers and painful urination from heart fire.
- Raw rehmannia, akebia and licorice decocted with bamboo leaves. Originally a formula from Qian Yi's *Xiaoer yaozheng zhijue*.
