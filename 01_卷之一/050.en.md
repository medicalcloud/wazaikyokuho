# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 050 通関散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Tongguan san (Tsukansan; Pass-Opening Powder)

It is suitable for wind stroke and cold damage with fever and aversion to wind, headache and dizziness, nasal congestion with a heavy voice, stiffness of the shoulders and back, aching pain of the body, twitching of the flesh and clenched jaws, for head wind old or new, and for phlegm attacking with dimmed vision.

fuxiong (lovage from Fuzhou; 2 liang)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage; 1 liang)　chuanwu (Sichuan aconite; 2 liang)　longnao bohe (borneol mint; 1½ liang)　baizhi (byakushi; angelica dahurica)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; 2 liang each)　xixin (saishin; asarum; half a liang)

Make the above into a fine powder. Take one large *qian* each time mixed in scallion white and clear tea; mint decoction is also fine. At any time.

## Notes

Tongguan san (Shaoxing-era additions): for fever, aversion to wind, headache, nasal congestion and stiff shoulders in wind stroke and cold damage, and for head wind.
- The two lovages, angelica dahurica, asarum and mint scatter wind from the head; aconite warms against cold.
