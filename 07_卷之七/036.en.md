# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之七 036 如神散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Rushen san (Divine Powder)

It treats, whether long-standing or recent, wind toothache and worm-eaten teeth attacking with pain that does not stop day or night and unquiet sleep; or loose teeth with puffy swelling extending to the cheeks.

chuanjiao (Sichuan pepper; seeds and closed fruits removed, lightly stir-fried to sweat out the oil)　lufengfang (wasp nest; lightly roasted)

Pound and sift the above into a fine powder. Each time take 1 *qian* with one *zhan* of water and a little salt, decoct together down to eight-tenths, rinse the mouth with it while hot and spit it out when it cools. One dose works at once.

## Notes

Rushen san (bureau additions): for toothache from wind or worms that does not stop day or night, insomnia, loose teeth and swollen cheeks.
- Two ingredients, Sichuan pepper and wasp nest, decocted in salt water, held in the mouth and spat out when cool.
