# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷下 005 論癥積気塊 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On conglomerations, accumulations and qi lumps. Conglomerations, accumulations and qi lumps all arise from qi deficiency and from cold qi, heat qi, anger, resentment, joy, worry and sorrow binding and accumulating within, hard and firm like a cup, with gripping pain of the heart and abdomen and inability to eat. Use medicines to dissolve and grind them away gradually; they cannot be purged out. Give Qiqi tang, Dingxiang wan, Qing muxiang wan, Muxiang tuiqi wan, Aiji wan or Pengjian wan. For accumulated qi that does not disperse, with swelling distension of the abdomen and flanks, give Jiqi wan or Sanleng jian wan. For hardness below the heart with bound lumps surging against the heart, give Wenbai wan. For distension and fullness with no desire for food, give Yangpi wan, Xiaoshi wan, Jiahe san or Si junzi tang.

## Notes

On conglomerations and qi lumps (qi deficiency and the seven emotional qi binding into cup-like hard masses with gripping pain and poor appetite): to be ground away gradually, not purged; Qiqi tang, Dingxiang wan, Qing muxiang wan, Muxiang tuiqi wan, Aiji wan and Pengjian wan; Jiqi wan and Sanleng jian wan for abdominal and flank distension; Wenbai wan for hard masses below the heart; Yangpi wan, Xiaoshi wan, Jiahe san and Si junzi tang for fullness with poor appetite.
