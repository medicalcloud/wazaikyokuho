# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 051 四斤丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Sijin wan (Four Jin Pill)

It is suitable for insufficiency of the kidney meridian with [pathogen] descending to attack the lower back and legs, swelling and itching of the thighs and knees, inability to bend and stretch, weak legs with little strength, inability to stand on the ground, a dull pain in the soles, dyspnea and fatigue on walking, cramping of the sinews and vessels, and stiffness of the lower back and knees; and for all wind-cold-damp impediment and leg qi with slackness and weakness.

Xuanzhou mugua (chaenomeles from Xuanzhou; pulp removed)　niuxi (goshitsu; achyranthes; rhizome head removed, chopped)　tianma (gastrodia; rhizome head removed, finely chopped)　congrong (cistanche; washed clean, sliced; each dried over a fire and weighed out at 1 jin)

When the above four ingredients have been processed as described, soak them in 5 *sheng* of ash-free wine—five days each in spring and autumn, three days in summer, ten full days in winter—then take them out and dry them over a fire, and add

fuzi (bushi; aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　tiger bone (smeared with ghee and roasted; 2 liang each)

Make all the above together into a fine powder, and form pills the size of wutong seeds with flour paste made with the wine in which the medicines were soaked. Take thirty to fifty pills each time on an empty stomach with decocted chaenomeles wine, or swallow them with salt water. Taken regularly, this medicine supplements deficiency, removes damp and greatly strengthens the sinews and bones.

## Notes

Sijin wan (Shaoxing-era additions): for swelling, itching and weakness of the lower back and legs, pain in the soles, breathlessness on walking and cramping due to insufficiency of the kidney meridian, and for wind-cold-damp impediment and leg qi.
- Chaenomeles, achyranthes, gastrodia and cistanche, 1 jin each ("four jin"), are soaked in wine, and aconite and tiger bone are added.
- In the base text the paragraph with the first four ingredients ends with "and add", leading into the next paragraph with aconite and tiger bone.
