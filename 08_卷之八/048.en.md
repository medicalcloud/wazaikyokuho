# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 048 如聖散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Rusheng san (Sage-Like Powder)

It treats wind toxin of the lung viscus breaking out in the skin, with blood and qi congealed and rough, turning into itchy scabies sores which, when scratched, lift the skin and form scabs, gradually spreading and soaking in, dragging on without healing. This medicine quickens the blood vessels, moistens the skin, disperses wind evil and stops itching.

shechuangzi (cnidium seed; half a liang)　huanglian (coptis; fibrous roots removed; 3 *fen*)　hufen (lead white; made into granules; 1 liang)　shuiyin (mercury; ground with the lead white and drops of water until black; 1 *fen*)

Mix the above medicines with raw sesame oil to a thin, smooth consistency. Each time, first wash the sore clean with salted sour rice water, then apply the medicine; replace it as soon as it dries. Within three to five applications it heals.

## Notes

Rusheng san (Wu Zhige's additions): for itchy scabies arising from wind toxin of the lungs in the skin, scabbing when scratched and spreading persistently.
- An external preparation of cnidium seed, coptis, lead white and mercury in sesame oil; contains mercury and lead. Distinct from the Rusheng tang of juan 7, no. 026.
