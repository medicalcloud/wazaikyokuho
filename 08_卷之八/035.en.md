# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 035 玉竜膏 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yulong gao (Jade Dragon Paste)

It rubs away wind and stops pain, disperses swelling and transforms toxin. It treats all wounds, fractures, sores and swellings.

gualou (trichosanthes fruit; one large, skin removed)　huangla (yellow beeswax; 1½ liang)　baizhi (angelica dahurica; picked clean, chopped; half a liang)　mayou (sesame oil; clear and genuine; 6 liang)　shexiang (musk; ground; 1 *qian*)　songzhi (pine resin; ground; 1½ *qian*)　lingxiang (lysimachia foenum-graecum)　huoxiang (agastache; 1 liang each)　xingren (apricot kernel; skin and tips removed)　shengma (cimicifuga)　huangqi (astragalus)　chi shaoyao (red peony root)　baiji (bletilla)　bailian (ampelopsis root)　gancao (licorice; picked clean, chopped; 1 *fen* each)

Soak the above in the oil for seven days; then pour off the oil, first refine it until fragrant and well cooked, let it cool, add the medicines, decoct over a gentle fire until yellow, and strain off the dregs through silk. Put it into a silver or stone pot, add the wax, musk and pine resin, boil down for a while and store in a porcelain box. Each time spread a little thinly on silk and apply. For itchy wind tinea of the head and face and painful sores and swellings, smear and rub it in until hot, using it frequently. For fleshy bells (polyps) in the ears or nose, apply it daily with a paper twist; in a month they heal. For moxa sores and children's lumpy sores, smearing it on also removes the scars, with divine effect.

## Notes

Yulong gao: a plaster for all wounds, fractures, sores and swellings; also for itchy wind tinea of the head and face, polyps in the ears or nose, moxa sores, children's sores and scars.
- Angelica dahurica, cimicifuga, astragalus, red peony, bletilla, ampelopsis, trichosanthes, apricot kernel, lysimachia, agastache and others boiled in sesame oil, made into a paste with yellow wax, musk and pine resin.
