# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 041 丁香散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Dingxiang san (Chokosan; Clove Powder)

It treats stomach deficiency with counterflow qi, unremitting vomiting, an emaciated, weary spirit, and sudden turmoil with restlessness.

renshen (ninjin; ginseng; half a liang)　dingxiang (clove)　huoxiang ye (agastache leaf; 1 *fen* each)

Pound and sift the above together into a powder. For each dose take 1 *qian* with half a *zhan* of water, boil five to seven times, add a little breast milk, remove the dregs and take it fairly hot, at any time.

## Notes

Dingxiang san: for unremitting vomiting, exhaustion and sudden turmoil from stomach deficiency with counterflow qi in children.
- Three ingredients (ginseng, clove, agastache), decocted with a little breast milk.
