# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 056 烏鶏煎丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wuji jian wan (Black Chicken Decoction Pill)

It treats all the various illnesses of women before and after childbirth.

wu xiongji (black-boned cockerel; one)　wuyao (lindera)　shichuang (stalagmite)　mudanpi (moutan bark)　renshen (ginseng; rhizome head removed)　baizhu (white atractylodes)　huangqi (astragalus; 1 liang each)　cangzhu (black atractylodes; soaked in rice-washing water, sliced, dried over a fire; 1½ liang)　haitongpi (erythrina bark)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　fuzi (aconite; blast-fried, skin and navel removed)　bai shaoyao (white peony root)　peng ezhu (zedoary)　chuanwu (Sichuan aconite; blast-fried)　honghua (safflower)　chenpi (aged tangerine peel; 2 liang each)　yanhusuo (corydalis)　muxiang (costus root)　hupo (amber)　shu gan dihuang (prepared rehmannia; washed, dried over a fire)　roudoukou (nutmeg)　caoguo (tsaoko fruit; half a liang each)

Chop the above finely. Take one black-boned cockerel, scald it and remove the feathers, intestines and stomach, put the above medicines into the bird's belly, and boil it in a new porcelain jar with 1 *dou* of good wine until dry; remove the chicken bones, place in an oiled bamboo tray, dry over a fire, make into a fine powder and form pills the size of wutong seeds with refined honey. Take thirty pills each time. For cold damage before or after childbirth, take them with honeyed wine. For qi oppression and vigorous fever before childbirth, with stir-fried ginger wine. For red and white discharge, with wine boiled with fresh ginger and rehmannia. For postpartum failed blood attacking the heart, swallow them with a boy's urine and stir-fried ginger wine. For postpartum blood lumps attacking and pounding with pain of the heart and abdomen, with corydalis wine. For vomiting counterflow before childbirth, with ginger decoction. To hasten delivery, with stir-fried hollyhock-seed wine. To calm the foetus, with salted wine. For unmarried girls whose menses should come but do not, with pain of the limbs, with decocted safflower wine. For blood and qi attacking and stabbing with pain of the heart and abdomen, with decocted angelica wine. For blood dizziness, swallow them with burned palm-fibre ash mixed in wine. For blood evil, with wine in which cinnabar and musk have been ground. For blood oppression, with decocted mume decoction in which cinnabar has been ground. For long-standing cold of the womb, with warm wine or jujube decoction on an empty stomach, once a day. For blood-wind taxation, swallow them with ginseng wine. For gripping pain of the lower abdomen, with stir-fried fennel in salted wine. For blood dispersed into the limbs with deficiency puffiness and yellow swelling of the whole body, with rice-bean wine. For regular use, take them with warm wine or vinegar decoction as preferred, always on an empty stomach before meals.

## Notes

Wuji jian wan (bureau additions): for all illnesses before and after childbirth, with detailed vehicles for each (cold damage, chest oppression, discharge, failed blood attacking the heart, blood lumps, vomiting, hastening labour, calming the foetus, amenorrhoea, stabbing blood-qi pain, blood dizziness, blood evil, blood oppression, cold womb, blood-wind taxation, lower abdominal pain, oedema).
- Twenty-one drugs stuffed into a black-boned cockerel and boiled down in wine, the bones removed and the rest dried and made into pills. Ginseng, astragalus, atractylodes, prepared rehmannia and white peony supplement; aconite, Sichuan aconite and cinnamon warm; zedoary, corydalis, safflower, moutan and amber move blood; lindera, costus, tangerine peel, nutmeg, tsaoko and black atractylodes move qi and remove damp; erythrina bark and stalagmite are added.
