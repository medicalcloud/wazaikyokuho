# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 013 逍遥散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Xiaoyao san (Shakuyakusan; Free and Easy Powder)

It treats blood deficiency and taxation fatigue with vexing heat of the five hearts, painful limbs, a clouded, heavy head and eyes, palpitation and red cheeks, dry mouth and throat, fever with night sweats, and reduced eating with a liking for lying down; as well as blood and heat contending with irregular menses, distending pain of the navel and abdomen, and chills and fever like malaria. It also treats unmarried girls with weak blood and deficient yin, disharmony of the ying-nutrient and wei-defense qi, phlegm cough and tidal fever, an emaciated body, gradually becoming steaming bone disorder.

gancao (kanzo; licorice; lightly roasted until red; half a liang)　danggui (toki) (Chinese angelica; shoots removed, chopped, lightly stir-fried)　fuling (bukuryo; white poria; skin removed)　shaoyao (shakuyaku; white peony root)　baizhu (byakujutsu; white atractylodes)　chaihu (saiko; bupleurum; shoots removed; 1 liang each)

Make the above into a coarse powder. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one large *zhan* of water, add one piece of roasted fresh ginger cut open and a little mint, decoct together down to seven-tenths, remove the dregs and take it hot, at any time.

## Notes

Xiaoyao san: for vexing heat of the five hearts, limb pain, heavy head, palpitation, red cheeks, dry mouth and throat, fever, night sweats, poor appetite and a tendency to stay in bed from blood deficiency and taxation fatigue; for irregular menses, umbilical distending pain and malaria-like chills and fever when blood and heat contend; and for phlegm cough, tidal fever, wasting and steaming bone disorder in unmarried girls with weak blood and yin deficiency. Still widely used today as the representative formula for liver depression with blood deficiency (irregular menses, premenstrual syndrome, menopausal symptoms, low mood).
- Six ingredients (bupleurum, angelica, white peony, atractylodes, poria, licorice), decocted with roasted ginger and mint.
