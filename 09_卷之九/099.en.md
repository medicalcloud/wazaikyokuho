# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之九 099 逐日日遊神 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Location of the Day-Roaming Spirit, by Day

On *guisi*, *jiawu*, *yiwei*, *bingshen* and *dingyou* days it is in the north of the room; on *gengzi*, *xinchou* and *renyin* days, in the south of the room; on *guimao* days, in the west of the room; on *jiachen*, *yisi*, *bingwu* and *dingwei* days, in the east of the room; on the six *wu* days and the six *ji* days, in the center of the room; on the remaining days it is outside the room, which is auspicious.

## Notes

Location of the Day-Roaming Spirit, by day (the last entry of juan 9): where in the room the Day-Roaming Spirit resides on each day of the sexagenary cycle (to be avoided). The base text's "six *xu*" probably means "six *wu*" (the six *wu* days and six *ji* days).
