# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之七 034 如聖勝金鋌 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Rusheng shengjin ding (Sage-Like Gold-Surpassing Ingot)

The patterns treated and the manner of taking are the same as for the earlier Rusheng shengjin ding; the ingredients differ slightly.

poxiao (crude mirabilite; 4 liang)　chuanxiong (Sichuan lovage; 1 liang)　liuhuang (sulphur; finely ground; 1½ liang)　guanzhong (cyrtomium rhizome; 2 liang)　bohe ye (mint leaf)　jingjie sui (schizonepeta spikes)　nencha (young tea; half a liang each)

Powder the above, knead with the natural juice of split raw scallions and form into ingots. The vehicles for taking it are as in the earlier formula.

## Notes

Rusheng shengjin ding (from here the layer of bureau additions): the same indications and administration as the earlier Rusheng shengjin ding (juan 7, no. 030); a different formula using crude mirabilite, cyrtomium and schizonepeta and omitting Sichuan aconite and raw rehmannia.
