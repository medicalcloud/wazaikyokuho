# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 047 加減四君子湯 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jiajian si junzi tang (Modified Four Gentlemen Decoction)

It treats unceasing vomiting and diarrhea in children with inability to nurse or eat. Taken regularly it regulates the stomach and improves eating.

bai biandou (hyacinth bean; steamed until cooked, dried over a fire)　huoxiang ye (agastache leaf)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried)　huangqi (ogi; astragalus; shoots removed; 1 liang each)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng)　fuling (bukuryo; poria; skin removed, dried over a fire)　baizhu (byakujutsu; white atractylodes; 4 liang each)

Make the above into a fine powder. Take 1 *qian* each time with a little salt, mixed with hot water; or decoct with seven-tenths of a *zhan* of water down to five-tenths and take it warm.

## Notes

Jiajian si junzi tang (Shaoxing-era additions): for unceasing vomiting and diarrhea and poor feeding in children; regular use regulates the stomach and improves appetite.
- Si junzi tang (Shikunshito) (ginseng, atractylodes, poria, licorice) plus astragalus, hyacinth bean and agastache.
