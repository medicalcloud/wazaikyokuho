# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之十 077 紫蘇子散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Zisuzi san (Shisoshisan; Perilla Seed Powder)

It treats children given milk to drink while crying before their breath has settled, so that the milk and qi counter each other and the qi cannot descend.

zisuzi (perilla seed; lightly stir-fried)　luobozi (radish seed; lightly stir-fried)　helile (kariroku) pi (chebule fruit rind)　xingren (kyonin; apricot kernel; skin and tips removed, stir-fried yellow with bran)　renshen (ninjin; ginseng; shoots removed)　muxiang (mokko; costus root; half a liang each)　qingpi (green tangerine peel; white removed)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; mix-fried until slightly red; 1 liang each)

Pound and sift the above into a fine powder. For each dose take 1 *qian* with one *zhan* of water and a little coin-sized fresh ginger, decoct together down to five-tenths, remove the dregs and take it warm, at any time, adjusted for the child's size.

## Notes

Zisuzi san (Wu Zhige's additions): for qi failing to descend (cough, dyspnea) when a crying child is fed before its breath has settled, so that milk and qi counter each other.
- Perilla seed, radish seed and apricot kernel bring qi down; green tangerine peel and costus move qi; chebule, ginseng and licorice are added.
