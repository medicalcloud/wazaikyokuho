# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之一 022 辰砂丸 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Chensha wan (Shinshagan; Cinnabar Pill)

It treats all kinds of wind with exuberant phlegm, headache and nausea, a clouded, confused spirit, dizziness and palpitation, vomiting of phlegm and drool, and vexation and oppression of the chest and diaphragm.

pengsha (borax; ground)　niuhuang (goo; bezoar; ground; 1 *qian* each)　baifuzi (typhonium; blast-fried)　bai jiangcan (silkworm; silk and mouth removed, scorched)　tiannanxing (arisaema; blast-fried until cracked, ground)　xieshao (scorpion tails; scorched; 1 *fen* each)　chensha (cinnabar; ground; half a liang)　banxia (hange; pinellia; washed seven times in hot water; 1 liang)

Make the above into a fine powder, grind together evenly and form pills the size of wutong seeds with flour paste boiled in water. Take twenty pills each time with ginger and schizonepeta decoction, at any time.

## Notes

Chensha wan: for headache, nausea, dizziness, palpitation and vomiting of phlegm from wind-phlegm.
- Pinellia, arisaema, typhonium, silkworm and scorpion tails expel wind-phlegm; cinnabar and bezoar calm the heart; borax transforms phlegm.
