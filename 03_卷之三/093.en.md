# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之三 093 蟠葱散 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Pancong san (Coiled Scallion Powder)

It treats deficiency cold of the spleen and stomach in men and women attacking and battering the heart and abdomen with stabbing pain reaching the flanks and ribs, stuffiness and oppression of the chest and diaphragm, cramping pain from the back and shoulders through the neck, no desire for food, occasional vomiting counterflow, cramps of sudden turmoil, cold abdomen with diarrhea, stabbing bladder qi, and swelling and pain of the small intestine and outer kidneys (scrotum). It also treats women's blood qi attacking and stabbing, hard concretion and conglomeration lumps, red and white vaginal discharge, sometimes with chills and fever, unceasing malign blood before or after childbirth, and pain of the navel and abdomen. It is suitable for all deficiency cold with no desire for food.

yanhusuo (corydalis; 3 liang)　cangzhu (black atractylodes; soaked overnight in rice-washing water, skin removed)　gancao (kanzo; licorice; scorched; half a jin each)　fuling (bukuryo; poria; the white kind, skin removed)　peng'ezhu (zedoary)　sanleng (sparganium; roasted)　qingpi (green tangerine peel; white removed; 6 liang each)　dingpi (clove bark)　suosha (amomum; skin removed)　binglang (areca; 4 liang each)　rougui (cinnamon bark; coarse bark removed)　ganjiang (kankyo; dried ginger; blast-fried; 2 liang each)

Pound and sieve the above into a powder. For each dose take 2 *qian* with one *zhan* of water and one scallion white with its roots, decoct down to seven-tenths and take it fairly hot on an empty stomach before meals.

## Notes

Pancong san (bureau additions): for stabbing heart, abdominal and flank pain, chest oppression, stiff back and neck, poor appetite, vomiting, sudden turmoil and diarrhea from spleen-stomach deficiency cold, for bladder qi, small-intestine qi and scrotal pain, and for women's blood qi, concretions, vaginal discharge, lochia before and after childbirth and abdominal pain.
- Corydalis, sparganium, zedoary, green tangerine peel and areca move qi, blood and accumulations; cinnamon, dried ginger, clove bark and amomum warm; black atractylodes, poria and licorice remove damp and regulate; decocted with scallion white.
