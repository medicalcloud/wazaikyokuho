# 太平惠民和劑局方 卷之八 021 神応黒玉丹 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shenying hei yu dan (Divinely Responsive Black Jade Elixir)

The patterns treated and the formula appear in the section on diarrhoea and dysentery.

## Notes

Shenying hei yu dan (Chunyou-era additions): a cross-reference giving only the name; the patterns and formula are in the diarrhoea section (juan 6, no. 066).
