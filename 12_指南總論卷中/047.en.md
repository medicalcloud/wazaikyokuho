# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷中 047 論傷寒中湿証 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On the damp-strike pattern of cold damage. When in cold damage the whole body aches, the patient cannot turn over, the joints have vexing pain, urination is inhibited while the stools are on the contrary free, and sweat comes out on the forehead, this is strike by damp qi; give much Wuling san (Goreisan) to promote urination, and then Zhufu tang as well.

## Notes

On damp-strike in cold damage (generalized pain, inability to turn, joint pain, inhibited urination with free stools, forehead sweating): Wuling san to promote urination, then Zhufu tang.
