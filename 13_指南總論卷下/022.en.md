# 太平惠民和劑局方 指南總論 卷下 022 論諸血熱妄行 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

On reckless movement of blood from heat. For unceasing hematemesis and nosebleed with dizziness and yellow eyes, give Longnao jisu wan, Bohe jian, or Siwu tang (Shimotsuto) decocted with schizonepeta added.

## Notes

On reckless movement of blood from heat: Longnao jisu wan, Bohe jian or Siwu tang with schizonepeta for unceasing hematemesis and nosebleed with dizziness and yellow eyes.
